座右铭Motto

学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。
是以罔则思，殆则学。
思学交修，方不罔不殆。

知而无行则耗，行而无知则迷。
是以耗则行，迷则知。
知行合一，乃无耗无迷。

吾所确知者，唯己之无知；
吾所渴求者，乃未知之未知。

To learn without reflection is to drift; to reflect without learning is to founder.
So: when drifting, reflect — when foundering, learn.
Reflection and study, mutually refined, leave one neither drifting nor foundering.

To know without acting is to waste; to act without knowing is to stray.
So: when wasting, act — when straying, know.
Knowledge and action, united as one, leave one neither wasting nor straying.

I know only that I know nothing;
I seek only what I know not that I know not.
