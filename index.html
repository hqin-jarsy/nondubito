On the name of this site

座右铭Motto

学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。
是以罔则思，殆则学。
思学交修，方不罔不殆。

知而无行则耗，行而无知则迷。
是以耗则行，迷则知。
知行合一，乃无耗无迷。

吾所确知者，唯己之无知；
吾所渴求者，乃未知之未知。

To learn without reflection is to drift; to reflect without learning is to founder.
So: when drifting, reflect — when foundering, learn.
Reflection and study, mutually refined, leave one neither drifting nor foundering.

To know without acting is to waste; to act without knowing is to stray.
So: when wasting, act — when straying, know.
Knowledge and action, united as one, leave one neither wasting nor straying.

I know only that I know nothing;
I seek only what I know not that I know not.

Has AGI Arrived? Notes on the Eve of a Subjectivity Renaissance

AI把工具理性推到极致，这件事的真正意义不是"AI造出了新能力"，而是把"工具"这一端从人类身上剥离下来。我们不是文明的顶点——我们是主体性黑暗时代的尾声，是复兴时代的前夜。

The real significance of AI pushing instrumental rationality to its limit is not "AI has new capabilities" — it is the separation of the instrumental side from the human being. We are not at the pinnacle of civilization. We are at the late dusk of the subjectivity dark age, on the eve of a renaissance.

Non Dubito is a space for philosophical writing that refuses the borders of any single language. Philosophy, like consciousness itself, is not the property of one tradition.

Essays here apply the Self-as-an-End framework to thinkers across East and West — reading them not as historical curiosities, but as structural analyses of what it means to exist as an end in itself.

Academic papers are archived on Zenodo. What you find here is writing meant to be read, shared, and thought about.
