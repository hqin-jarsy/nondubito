## Reading work restored

The uncertainty of the person opens onto the work of singers, listeners and scribes, not a declaration that Homer was fabricated. Oral formulas are both rhythmic resources and poetry; memory and invention are not opposites.

Alphabetic writing preserves words without capturing every sound and listener. Dictation remains a hypothesis, and oral performance and written transmission overlap. Confucius introduces selection within transmission, without sorting entire civilizations into collective East and individual West.

Achilles' withdrawal, Briseis, Patroclus, Hector and the inability of victory to repair loss prepare the encounter with Priam. The supplication takes time on the page; shared tears do not erase unequal power. Returning the body is a real, limited action, not the end of war.

Penelope's three-year stratagem gains room rather than freezing time forever. The bed test restores her independent judgment to the homecoming. Plato's critique and Socrates' wish for conversation remain in tension. The final bridge keeps distinct lives audible instead of using Gödel or the Buddha as universal shortcuts.
