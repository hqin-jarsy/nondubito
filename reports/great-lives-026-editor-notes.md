# Great Lives 026 — Dostoevsky: editorial record

Reviewed: 2026-09-20. Title and existing source URL retained.

## Scope and coverage

Revised the Chinese and English source essay and independently wrote complete Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, and Korean editions. Each language preserves eight narrative sections and 73 paragraphs: 8 / 10 / 10 / 8 / 8 / 11 / 8 / 10. Traditional Chinese preserves every source paragraph; the other languages use their own syntax and established names and book titles.

1. Execution ground: calendar distinction; letter to brother; power staging death; remembered experience transformed into The Idiot; Du Fu, Beethoven, Sima Qian comparison without ranking suffering.
2. Underground: Siberia and illness without genius diagnosis; unnamed narrator and self-contradiction; Liza's visit and the single banknote she leaves; interest calculation and cruelty; Socrates; Freud chronology; novelist/character/clinical evidence distinction.
3. Crime: Napoleonic exception theory; both Alyona and Lizaveta; hidden loot; bell and repetition; Porfiry and Sonya; surrender versus slow change in Siberia; Nietzsche distinction; the victim cannot be reduced to material.
4. Karamazov: Dmitri's wrongful conviction; Ivan's crisis; Alyosha and Zosima; Smerdyakov's confession; unequal legal and moral responsibilities; Freud's 1928 reading, not medical diagnosis.
5. God: conventional summary distinguished from fixed quotation; suffering children and returned ticket; words enter another person's choices; no claim that atheism entails murder; Nietzsche not simple optimism.
6. Grand Inquisitor: Ivan's imagined story; bread, miracles, authority; three temptations; prison kiss and departure; limited Qin Shi Huang/Washington comparison, slavery retained; Christian author not identical to every voice; Alyosha kisses Ivan.
7. Fiction: novel beside philosophy and psychoanalysis; atmosphere, time, consequences; reader may disagree, stop, and decline identification; force of literature is not coercion.
8. Death: dates and age; late recognition without invented interior verdict; no unsourced funeral count; unwritten Alyosha continuation; Bach and Leonardo distinctions; explicitly imagined bridge alongside Freud; trembling hands and invitation to continue.

## Editorial repairs

- Replaced the old cinematic galloping messenger and blanket claim that fiction was simply memory with the brother-letter testimony and an explicit distinction between experience and fictional form.
- Removed the unsupported first-seizure date, disease-as-genius explanation, epilepsy-as-cause-of-death chain, and Freud's speculative epilepsy explanation as accepted fact.
- Restored Lizaveta and Sonya, resisting both the one-victim calculation and a single-cause account of confession.
- Replaced the old identification of Raskolnikov with Nietzsche's Übermensch and the claim that Ivan went mad because his reasoning was too correct.
- Distinguished the four brothers' positions and the speaker's responsibility from the killer's. Retained Freud's interpretation as interpretation history.
- Preserved the critical problem of freedom and care without allowing the fictional Inquisitor to speak for all churches or Washington to become an unqualified Christ figure.
- Replaced the old celebration of readers being locked inside a murderer's mind with attentive but voluntary reading.
- Removed imagined knowledge of Du Fu's sense of failure and Dostoevsky's confidence in his greatness; corrected the obsolete claim that everything by Leonardo was unfinished.
- Editor's reread caught the banknote count and an awkward Chinese light metaphor; both fixed across editions. Main editor fully reread source ZH/EN and the DE/ES full drafts; DE title syntax and ES Mishkin / inacabamiento wording were polished.
