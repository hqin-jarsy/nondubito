### Batch 13 editorial work

- **61, hidden on dry land:** retain recognition without recruitment and the right to decline well-meant opportunities. - **62, sixty transformations:** retain sincere judgment followed by revision without asserting total certainty at every stage or caricaturing skepticism as never judging. - **63, useful ground:** preserve the dependence of a usable patch on surrounding space without inventing a necessary engineering collapse. Question-led teaching can be coercive, while an explicit explanation can remain open. The stranded fish already conveys timing and appropriate help; do not assert that most readers see only a complaint. The great-fish passage does contain explicit argument, and Guo's “小大各有所适” complicates a simple bigger-is-better reading. Patience is not a guarantee of return. - **64, webbed toes:** preserve the author's question about practice contradicting stated principles, but give the chapter and older interpretations a fair hearing. Repetition may open another entrance; length and style alone do not reveal private motives or prove historical authorship. Retain a reasoned question about the text's boundaries instead of claiming a settled self-refutation. Apply the same caution to the essayist's own preferred form.
- **65, borrowed voices:** distinguish introductory glosses from contested meanings of 寓言、重言、卮言. Record Guo's reading of the numbers as rates of belief rather than asserting a statistical composition of the book. Borrowed speech is not guaranteed neutrality, age is questioned in the text itself, and responsive-vessel imagery is not an established mechanical reconstruction. The opening does not require every utterance to be narrative and itself uses the matchmaking comparison. Restore Laozi's direct instruction and Yang Ziju's response before the final seating scene. The opening/ending juxtaposition can be a reader's interpretation, not proof of editorial intent. A story can manipulate without a stated moral; clear explanation can allow dissent. Explicitly answer the series' own responsibility now: checkable quotation, separation of additions from text, stated reasons, visible disagreements and revisability. Do not defer the whole question to essay 67 or declare all commentary inherently disqualified.

### Batch 10 editorial work

- **46, the wooden cock:** correct four “not yet” answers to three, followed by “几矣.” Preserve the trainer's actual reasons rather than claiming that he never explains. Four ten-day intervals are narrative form, not a universal training schedule. The text does not reveal the cock's private state, certify that other birds infallibly recognize hidden strength, or say it has abandoned readiness to fight. Fatigue does not prove bluffing; quietness does not prove ability; a room without objections may reflect power rather than trust. Reacting to danger or defending someone is not automatically dependence. Keep the right to assess the work rather than its display, acknowledge that earlier craft stories describe exercises and judgment, and distinguish the series' reading route from the chapter's actual order. The ancient fighting setting is not endorsed as animal practice or made the ideal for human relationships.
- **47, a new measure:** restore the transmitted order. The sea's small-stone, marsh, grain, and hair comparisons precede the question about heaven/earth and the hair's tip; they are not answers after “否.” The response after “否” actually discusses quantity, time, changing shares, beginnings/endings, knowledge and lifespan. It contains arguments and direction, not only an unexplained refusal. The argument is not empirical cosmology or proof of infinite divisibility. Seeking a new method can be thoughtful; limited standards remain useful. Distinguish a true local claim from a total explanation. A teacher's admission of limits does not erase every form of authority or have no possible benefit. Retitle the question without asserting a private motive or instantaneous reaction.
- **48, viewpoints and appraisals:** restore the third exchange about the most minute and greatest, and the fourth about worth and size. “What should I do?” belongs to the next exchange, not the introduction to the six viewpoints. Perspective does not manufacture facts; sincerity does not guarantee that both disputants are right. “自贵而相贱” is not a mathematical theorem or a claim that self-deprecation never occurs. Public judgment is not wholly within one's control, but can be influenced and corrected; inner and social assessments need not remain eternally separate. Do not create an infallible observer outside all perspectives. Preserve non-harm and other normative direction. Animal and political examples are literary arguments, not modern zoological findings or licenses for success-only legitimacy. Retain the outward/return contrast, five actions in the inn, the interval before a requested explanation, ordinary equality, meeting-room analogy, and the ending's effect on the reader. Do not invent a procession, a fixed length of travel, the identical individuals on both visits, or access to everyone's motives. Silence is read with the later request, not as automatic proof of insight or a demand to accept unjust criticism. Distinguish informality from abuse.
