### Batch 08 editorial work

- **36, the sheep butcher:** restore the refused audience and the admission that he fled danger, rather than deliberately following the king. Distinguish refusal of false credit from modesty. The symmetry of punishment and reward is not a universal rule that only someone previously liable to punishment can deserve a reward. Relief and basic support have other justifications. Keep “三旌之位” as a high office without an unsupported exact modern/historical equivalence. Acknowledge the king's explicit praise and the narrator's “遂不受也”; do not claim there is no evaluation or invent his return as a separately narrated event. Retain the insight that a valuable offer can be declined without disparaging it. No invented inner struggle or universal safety in refusing authority. Correct Su Shi's distinct objections and remove an unsupported reception-history claim that later readers stopped reading these chapters. Retitle “好处也可以拒绝——屠羊说” / “The Gift He Would Not Accept.”
- **37, Yuan Xian:** distinguish ancient “病” from a modern medical diagnosis and quote “学而不能行” without silently adding “道.” Keep material hardship real rather than turning poverty into superiority or requiring suffering as proof of independence. Do not infer that every route out of poverty requires wrongdoing or that Zigong committed every offense in Yuan Xian's list. Preserve the sharpness of the closing remarks and Zigong's shame. Remove the claim that inward commitments cannot be assessed at all; one external metric cannot replace relevant evidence. Do not invent Zigong's failure to hear the music or guarantee a person's inability to misread another's intention. Omit misleading cross-references about Huzi's first three displays being accurately diagnosed. Retitle around the distinction between poverty and the judgment placed on it.
- **38, three swords:** distinguish annual casualties from deaths. Restore the visitor's risk under sovereign power and the intervening seven-day selection with more than sixty casualties. Restore the explicit rebuke and appeal to rank; the speech is not simply a neutral arrangement whose conclusion is left entirely blank. Its large-scale political ideals are not automatically better or modern ideals. Treat the meeting as a literary scene, not a verified historical encounter; the imperial title inside the rhetoric does not establish actual status. Keep the swordsmen's death ending without inventing details or declaring it a later interpolation on stylistic dislike alone. Preserve the potential of entering another's interest while refusing to call the entire method harmless, non-coercive, or educationally validated. Protection and practical transitions cannot wait for persuasive success. Retitle “话说进去了，剑士们怎么办——三剑” / “Three Swords—and the People Left Behind.”
- **39, genuineness:** preserve the contrast between outward form and inward feeling without turning it into a lie detector. Loud grief is not evidence of falseness; quiet authority may be fear of power; moving someone does not certify truth, and lack of an immediate response does not disprove care. Restore the explanatory continuation and the examples about mourning, parents, and shared drinking. Acknowledge the text's strong critique of convention while allowing learned courtesy to help genuine concern. Sincerity does not excuse injury. Earlier funeral stories prescribe no new quota of tears. The repeated bows in the chapter complicate a simple ritual/false versus spontaneous/true dichotomy. Characterization and deference do not settle authorship. Retitle “真诚，不必交出规定的表情——真者，精诚之至也” / “No Required Shape for Sincerity.”
- **40, horse-tending boy:** restore the mythic account of illness, the elder, the chariot of the sun, the planned further travels, the refusal and the emperor's renewed questions. It is not only two short answers, and the first “此” is not unambiguously reducible to horse-tending. Do not translate mythic language into diagnosis or practical treatment. Expertise in directions does not prove expertise in government. Read removal of harm as an opening question, not a complete policy or abolition of care. Mark the comparison with Bole as our connection rather than a reference made by the boy. Subtraction can have observable benefits; assess who loses protection and whether burdens shift. Stopping injury includes responsibility for consequences. Never turn “remove harm” into authorization to discard dissenting people. The emperor's departure is not demonstrated policy success. Retitle “先看看，什么正在害它们——牧马童子” / “Before You Add One More Thing.”

### Batch 07 editorial work

- **31, Wheelwright Bian:** preserve the ruler's threat, the craftsman's argument by analogy, failed transmission to his son, age, and unresolved ending. The wheelwright does give reasons; his particular difficulty does not prove every craft unteachable or every book useless. Do not infer his emotions, teaching history, or honesty from suffering. Preserve the sharpness of “dregs” rather than claiming it is neutral. Explanation, demonstration, tools, safe practice and feedback can cooperate. No universal theory that words cannot give quantities, no invented history of the formulation, and no demand to risk others while learning. Comparing exposition with narrative is not proof of different authorship; explaining a limit need not contradict that limit.
- **32, highest benevolence:** keep the tiger/wolf provocation and the minister's objection as a serious question about dependents. Affection for insiders does not necessarily entail harm to outsiders. Distinguish limited attention from unjust exclusion, and particular responsibilities from immunity for favoritism. Restore the long continuation about forgetting and moral titles; impartial care is one entrance, not the whole ancient claim. Neither explanation nor brevity determines authenticity. Concern need not mean self-exhaustion or abandonment of existing duties. Omit the unsupported inference that questions about cosmic governance or differing character portrayals settle authorship of the chapter.
- **33, Yao and Shun:** preserve recognition alongside insufficiency; “未大” is a real qualification, not a sentence with no negative. Remove wrong character counts and unreported emotions. The dialogue's order does not document a deliberate pause, a four-step curriculum, or a tested feedback technique. Restore the concluding generalization after Yao's answer. Natural imagery is not a complete institution or an excuse to withhold immediate help. Instructions can enable agency; unsolicited explanation is not automatically coercion. Genuine recognition must acknowledge facts and constraints rather than act as flattering padding before criticism.
- **34, Horses' Hooves:** “善治” is a claim and a reputation under scrutiny, not verified evidence that every action was technically correct or benevolent. Death exceeds half. Do not invent successful hoof treatment or diagnose motives; figures belong to the story, not a modern mortality dataset. Restore explicit blame (“伯乐之罪”), the political argument, the stronger critique of civilization, and acquired evasive behavior. The whole chapter does not stop after two numbers or abstain from explanation. Standards and training can help, but must answer to agency, effects, costs and feedback. Retitle Chinese to “说是善治，马却死了过半——马蹄.”
- **35, Robber Zhi:** restore the brother's warning, coercive terms of speech, victims and threats. The robber cannot be described as taking only replaceable external goods while respecting inner freedom. Bodies and safety are not external possessions. Confucius's proposal includes status and cessation of raiding, not a completed bribe. His fear under threat is not proof of argumentative defeat. A cruel person may make a perceptive criticism; that neither excuses violence nor creates an authorship contradiction. Historical allegations remain the character's claims. Limit the essay to the first encounter, not the chapter's other dialogues. The alternative continuation is explicitly a contemporary writing exercise, not a reconstruction of what the authentic Zhuangzi would write. Separate exposing injustice, stopping injury, repair and consensual help; reject a fixed hierarchy in which a supposedly cleaner robber is the middle rung. Retitle to “骂赢了圣人，也不能替自己免责——盗跖” / “Who Gets to Be Called a Robber?”

### Batch 06 editorial work

- **26, singing beside a body:** restore the friends' preceding conversation and Zigong's continuing questions. Do not invent an absence of all agreements or duties, diagnose hidden grief or envy, or make Zigong permanently incapable of learning. The diviner claims credit without a reported treatment, and does acknowledge difficulty at the third visit. His flight is not proof of completed insight; Huzi's reason for pursuit is unspecified. - **29, mind as mirror:** preserve responding, not only the negatives. Preparation, memory, records, boundaries, and evidence-based caution remain legitimate. - **30, Su Shi and attribution:** distinguish Su Shi's reasons for doubting the two pairs of chapters; restore his proposed connection between the end of Borrowed Words and the beginning of Lie Yukou. Separate tension within a text, a hypothesis of layers, and attribution to the historical Zhuang Zhou. Forms of address, manner versus message, and changing character roles are clues, not automatic proofs. Quality and authorship need separate judgments. Readers may accept an interpretation while rejecting its attribution hypothesis. Retitle Chinese to “苏轼拿开四篇之后——辨析的理由与限度.”

### Batch 04 editorial review — 16–20

All five original manuscripts were read in full. Stories, arguments, contemporary comparisons and ending questions remain, but unsupported mechanisms, absolute claims and categorical judgments of other people's inner lives are removed or qualified.

- **16, morning acorns:** do not describe modern idiom usage as the exact opposite of the original. Restore the concluding “和之以是非…两行,” which prevents reducing the passage to an unequivocal condemnation of the keeper. The text does not diagnose fast/slow cognition, arithmetic inability or malicious intent. Morning and evening distributions can differ materially in timing and risk even when their sum is equal. Keep the framing examples, distinguish equal totals from a changed budget proposal, and extend the question to the persuader's responsibility. Deliberation can correct a frame; slow thinking is not inherently trapped. Retitle Chinese to “七颗橡子，两次心情——朝三暮四.”
- **17, butterfly:** retain both “不知” and “必有分,” without treating either as canceling the other. Vivid conviction is not a demonstration that waking and dreaming have identical evidence. Preserve the adjacent shadow exchange, former certainty, group blind spots and transformation, while removing the universal system-self-proof theorem and the claim that active reflection cannot help. Other perspectives can supply useful evidence without becoming infallible. “物化” is not a literal interspecies travel certificate. Skepticism cannot erase obligations or provide a position immune to correction. Retitle Chinese to “醒来以后，问题才开始——庄周梦蝶.”
- **18, pheasant:** explicitly distinguish Guo Xiang's reading of wild ease from Lin Xiyi's reading of provision without contentment in captivity. Follow the latter as an interpretation, not a proven biological comparison. No literal twenty-character count, measured foraging efficiency, universal loss of ability after captivity, or claim that all cages are mental. Support can increase agency; employment and stable provision are not themselves confinement. Refusal, material constraints, safe experimentation and genuine opportunities to leave all matter. Restore the chapter's concern with bodily preservation. Retitle Chinese to “吃喝有人安排，是否就够了——泽雉.”
- **19, mourning and fire:** Qin Shi's crying does not prove the exact quantity or spontaneity of his inward grief. Kinship comparisons and loud mourning cannot diagnose false emotion.
