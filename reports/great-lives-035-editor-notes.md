# Great Lives 035 — Galileo full-edition review

Reviewed on 2026-09-20. Source title retained: 伽利略，它还是在转 / Galileo, And Yet It Moves.

## Narrative and editorial decisions

Read both original source narratives in full, then recalibrated the Chinese and English as nine-section essays, retaining the telescope, trial, Copernicus comparison, scientific remainder, named series comparisons, and imagined bridge ending. Each revised source contains 49 paragraphs, distributed 5/6/5/5/6/5/5/6/6.

The editorial center is twofold: a decree does not determine planetary motion, and coercion does not forfeit a person's dignity. The legend of the final mutter remains a legend. Survival requires no redemption through later achievement; Socrates need not die for his questions to matter. The final telescope is paired with another person listening to the elderly Galileo.

Corrections are integrated into the story rather than attached as a catalogue of disclaimers:

- Harriot's earlier lunar drawing replaces the claim that nobody before Galileo pointed a telescope at the sky.
- Lunar relief, Jovian satellites, and Venus's phases challenge inherited arrangements but do not themselves prove Earth's motion. Tycho's alternative and Galileo's mistaken tidal argument remain visible.
- The conflict includes scriptural interpretation, earlier restrictions, publication permission, political conditions and obedience; neither a single offended pope nor a uniform science-versus-faith opposition explains it.
- Copernicus's physical claims and Osiander's anonymous preface are distinguished. Bruno's execution is not presented as a simple penalty for proving heliocentrism. Kepler and other workers remain in the story.
- The essay does not identify observed planets with Kant's thing-in-itself, treat method as one man's invulnerable invention, or describe Galileo as the only persecuted scientist.
- The 1992 papal address is a belated acknowledgment and reflection, not the moment Earth became free to move. Newton's Gregorian and English-calendar dates are distinguished without staging a corrective aside for its own sake.
