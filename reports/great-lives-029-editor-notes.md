## Complete narrative coverage

The original Chinese and English essays were read in full. Both have nine narrative sections, not eight. The revision retains all nine movements of the argument and the imagined bridgehead ending:

1. Ambition, contradiction, negation and preservation; “swallowing the remainder” identified as the essay's metaphor.
2. The 1807 *Phenomenology*: night/noon sense-certainty experiment; recognition, lordship and bondage, fear and formative labour. The bowl is explicitly an illustrative example, not a Hegel anecdote.
3. Kant's distinction and Hegel's objection to a fixed separation of knowing and truth; the door/glass images do not promise eventual factual omniscience.
4. Absolute knowing distinguished from absolute spirit and from knowing every fact. Art, religion and philosophy retained. The question of a whole hearing a particular person is the author's critique, not an asserted scholarly consensus.
5. Everyday independence tested by illness and care; the original spiral/cycle comparison retained as a comparison, without proof-by-Darwin, Gödel or Huineng.
6. Marx's inversion of dialectic, labour and institutional promises; communism as real movement distinguished from “history stops.” Neither Hegel nor Marx is collapsed into Qin Shi Huang or made directly responsible by deduction for subsequent regimes.
7. “Cannot go back” recast as inability to unlearn an encountered problem, not a physical time arrow. Zhuangzi and Huineng do not return before history or abolish practice. A loss need not become necessary or fortunate because it enters a later narrative.
8. Marx, Sartre, Lacan, Beauvoir and Fanon retained; mediated and critical inheritances differentiated. The mirror turns back toward Hegel. The essay's preference for tools over finality is not presented as everyone's verdict.
9. Complete imagined bridgehead: unfolding map, branching roads, the person arriving at the map's edge, tools, sickbed/factory gate, castle metaphor, and the option to stop and listen.

## Material corrections

- “Thesis–antithesis–synthesis” no longer substitutes for Hegel's method as a universal formula.
- The 1807 age is phrased “the year he turned thirty-seven,” avoiding a publication-month birthday claim.
- Lordship and bondage does not teach that slavery is good, that the enslaved person has straightforwardly won, or that work automatically emancipates.
- Absolute knowing is not an encyclopaedia of all facts. Absolute spirit is not a future certificate that conflict and history have ceased.
- Gödel's applicability conditions are named; his theorem is not used to refute Hegel, guarantee metaphysical non-closure or prove a philosophy of endless personal ascent.
- The well-known Marx inversion sentence is treated as a paraphrase/image rather than a fabricated exact quotation. *The German Ideology* supplies the real-movement distinction.
- The essay's question about totalizing explanations remains explicit and substantive; factual corrections do not erase the author's philosophical position.
- Existing Markdown asterisks in source prose disappear with the HTML revision; no new raw Markdown formatting is embedded in generated paragraphs.
