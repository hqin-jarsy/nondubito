结构模式Pattern

跟DTAR的区别在于烈——你不能忍，底线被碰了就走。每一段停留中基础层得到更好的保护（烈在工作），但也可能因为底线被触及而更频繁地离开。

人们觉得你难以预测——其实你的离开都有原因，只是他们没看见那条线。

The difference from DTAR is Dignity — you can't absorb violations; when the line is crossed you leave. Foundation gets better protection in each stay.

People find you unpredictable — actually your departures all have reasons; they just never saw the line.

危险地形Blind Spots

驱的推进需求和游的流动需求都很强，而烈让你在底线被触碰时快速离开。每一次离开都有正当理由，但离开的频率本身会成为一种结构性的损耗。

Drive's advancement need and Exit's mobility need are both intense, while Dignity makes you leave quickly when crossed. Every departure has legitimate reason, but the frequency of departures itself becomes a structural drain.

常见误读Misread As

  • 「他很难相处」→ 不是难相处，是他的底线很清晰 'He's difficult to work with' → Not difficult — his limits are clear
  • 「他不稳定」→ 不是不稳定，是不接受底线被触碰后继续待 'He's unstable' → Not unstable — unwilling to continue after limits are crossed

修复逻辑What Helps

❌ 不起作用❌ Doesn't Work

期待他为了"大局"忍受底线被碰。

Expecting him to tolerate limits being crossed 'for the bigger picture.'

✓ 真正有效✓ Actually Works

提前识别并明确说清楚你的底线在哪里。透明化你的边界，让环境有机会在触碰之前调整。

Identify and clearly articulate your limits in advance. Make your boundaries transparent — give the environment a chance to adjust before touching them.
