HC-16 人格图谱

HC-16 Personality Profile

四个维度，十六种自己

Four Dimensions, Sixteen Ways of Being

HC-16 基于四个核心维度的组合：驱动感·边界感·方向感·流动感。没有高低之分，只是更偏向哪边——是对你感知模式和内在结构的深度理解。

HC-16 maps four core dimensions: Drive · Edge · Direction · Flow. No high or low is better — only which way you tend to lean. A deep look at your perceptual patterns and inner structure.
