HC-16 Vulnerability Profile

四个维度，十六种脆弱

Four Dimensions, Sixteen Vulnerabilities

HC-16是一个人格脆弱性图谱系统，基于四个核心维度的组合：驱动·尊严·自由·根基。不是性格优劣的判断，而是对你感知模式和需求结构的深度理解。

HC-16 is a vulnerability profile system based on the interplay of four core dimensions: Drive · Dignity · Autonomy · Base. Not a judgment of character, but a deep understanding of your perceptual patterns and need structure.
