The Bill Does Not Include the Person

三百元以外，不归我处置

三百元以外，不歸我處置

A loss counts. Payment can be claimed and contact ended. Being wronged does not confer ownership of another life.

损失照算，赔偿可以要，关系也可以结束。受了损，不等于从此拥有对方。

03

Not Through My Hands This Time

这一回，没有经我的手

這一回，沒有經我的手

Refusing to pass an injury on, or stepping between it and a person: neither act has to wait for victory to have happened.

勇气不一定有一个壮烈的外形。拒绝传下去，或者站到中间，都不等最后赢了才算。

勇氣不一定有一個壯烈的外形。拒絕傳下去，或者站到中間，都不等最後贏了才算。

04

The Words Are True. Is the Choice Still Yours?

话是真的，选择还在吗

話是真的，選擇還在嗎

Accuracy needs checking. So does what a statement gives someone, or removes from a decision that was theirs.

真假要核，一句话把什么交给了谁、又从谁那里拿走什么，也要另看。善意不能替这一问作答。

真假要核，一句話把什麼交給了誰、又從誰那裡拿走什麼，也要另看。善意不能替這一問作答。

05

The Part Only I Can Own

只有我能认的那一份

只有我能認的那一份

Payment, an account of what happened, and owning a wrong do different work. None can collect forgiveness on the other's behalf.

赔付、交代、认错，不是一件事。认下自己的错，也不能顺便领走对方的原谅。

賠付、交代、認錯，不是一件事。認下自己的錯，也不能順便領走對方的原諒。

06

Forgiveness Is Not a Debt

不原谅，也不欠下一笔

不原諒，也不欠下一筆

An inward claim, an outstanding remedy, and what happened are different things. Telling someone to move on cannot erase them together.

心里的那一笔、外面的赔偿、发生过的事，需要分开。旁人不能拿一句放下吧，把它们一起销掉。

心裡的那一筆、外面的賠償、發生過的事，需要分開。旁人不能拿一句放下吧，把它們一起銷掉。

Source & reading note原论文与阅读说明原論文與閱讀說明

Six essays draw on the Classical Concepts prequel and five papers. Unattributed scenes are imagined; linked texts, recollections and institutional records retain their evidential limits. Treating oneself and others as ends is the source's declared normative starting point, not an empirical law or an assumed consensus. These essays examine particular acts, not entire persons or unrecorded inner lives. Outcomes do not erase earlier acts; waiving a claim, courage and forgiveness do not become another person's debts. Detailed intellectual history and independently unverified historical cases remain in the source papers. This is not legal, clinical or operational safety advice. English is independently written.
