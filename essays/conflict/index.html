When Two Laws Meet

冲突论系列

Two people, each holding something arrived at over years, each of which actually organises how that person lives. One week the two of them require actions that cannot both be taken — and no ruler exists on which one could be shown truer than the other, neither may legislate for the other, and neither can settle the thing by walking out.

Seven essays on what a person does there. Not how to argue better, not how to compromise, not how to get to yes. What handling it takes, so that the handling itself does not take away the position from which a person holds a law at all.

Three reading modes: English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Seven essays · English / 中文 / 繁體
Essay 01
Who Pays the Electricity Bill

Two gates. And size does not decide scope: a small thing handled by suppression can reach the position; a large one, with room on both sides, may never touch it. Four names that look like they set the scope, and do not.

Essay 02
Two Ledgers

An apology does not dissolve the incompatibility of two laws, and the incompatibility does not excuse the apology. Graded handover, protection first, and why an arrangement is not a law.

Essay 03
Not by Person

The paths divide by the current condition of the relation. What licenses a switch is that the reciprocal process is currently unavailable — nothing further about the other's inner life or layer. Three places this is not the War Treatise.

Essay 04
Four Concentric Circles

Face it, deepen, be directed toward a terminus, remain open to questioning. Not four rules but four conditions the handling position itself cannot cancel — and I may stop, which does not prove them wrong.

Essay 05
I Understood Him

Deepening together, and the side where it does not happen. Cultivation is not instruction; the first step is not making him see me; and understanding why he does it settles nothing between us.

Essay 06
A Stopping Point Is Not Stalling

Terminus, stopping point, removal, pause, stalling — five words, each with its own use. The two histories of consensus, mutual acknowledgement of incommensurability, and the hardest distinction in the treatise.

Essay 07
These Two People Have No Terminus

Three gates that pause the unilateral path, what follows a pause, where the four constraints stand afterwards, and an express list of what this treatise did not do.

两条法相遇的时候

SAE Conflict Treatise

两个人，各自持着一件多年来自己走到的事，而每一件都在真实地组织着那个人的生活。某一个星期，两边要求的行动不能同时做到——而没有一把尺可以说哪一条更真，谁也不能替对方立法，谁也不能靠走开把它结清。

七篇散文写的是一个人在那里做什么。不是怎么把话说得更好，不是怎么妥协，不是怎么谈成。是这件事要怎么处理，才不至于处理本身把一个人持有一条法的那个位置拿走。

三种阅读模式：英文、简体、繁體。
秦汉 · 2026 · 七篇 · 英文／中文／繁體
第一篇
谁去交电费

两道门。而碰撞的大小不定射程：一件很小的事若以压制处理也可能触及那个位置，一件很大的事若双方各有余地可能从头到尾没有触及。四个看起来能定射程、其实不能的名目。

第二篇
两本账

道歉不消解两条法的不相容，而不相容也不使那句话可以挂起不认。分级交接，保护先行，以及为什么行动安排不是法。

第三篇
不按人分

分路按当前的关系条件分。转路的根据是当前互向过程不可用这件事本身，不必再加一项关于对方内心或层级的解释。三处与战争论不同。

第四篇
四个同心圆

正视，深入理解，向终，受问。不是四条规范，是这个处理位置本身不能取消的四项条件——而我可以停止，停下不证明这四条错了。

第五篇
我理解了他

共同深入，与走不通的那一侧。涵育不是教化，第一步不是让他看见我，而我理解了他为什么这样，不等于这件事就此说定。

第六篇
止点不是拖着

终，止点，移出，暂停，拖着——五个词各有专用。共识的两种发生史，互相承认不可通约，以及全篇最难的那一处分界。

第七篇
这两个人没有终

三道使单向路暂停的门，暂停之后是什么，四条约束在各处的地位，以及一份明确列出的「这一篇没有做的」。
