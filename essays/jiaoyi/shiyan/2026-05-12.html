盒子裂开的那一刻

The Moment the Box Cracks

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 12, 2026

四月CPI同比3.8%为2023年以来最高，核心CPI从2.6%加速到2.8%，双双高于预期；标普500收报约7,383点跌0.6%，纳指跌0.9%至约26,103，道指跌288点约0.6%。VIX从17.19升至18.74涨约9%。WTI上破101美元，布伦特约105美元；联邦基金期货将2026年降息完全定价剔除，年底前加息概率约三成。特朗普称美伊停火"靠生命维持装置撑着"，今日启程访华，国防部长面对国会就重启对伊军事行动的质询。

过去30余个交易日，市场把油价上行、伊朗战事、霍尔木兹关闭都装进"暂时性供给冲击"这个盒子，用核心通胀温和与AI叙事把头条CPI隔离开。今天这堵墙第一次明确裂开：能源贡献了头条涨幅的40%以上，但核心也同步加速；联储宽松路径被一次性定价为零。这是一种制度切换的初步信号。

但缝隙的清晰度仍受限。一是市场已经做出方向性反应（VIX涨9%，纳指跌0.9%），表达成本上升；二是未来48小时内有特朗普访华行程、国防部长听证、可能的对伊军事决定三重二元事件，方向并不干净；三是过去16天反复验证的"压制"模式尚未确认结束，可能是单日波动而非趋势开启。清晰度5/10，低于开仓门槛。

今天不操作。连续第17日空仓。理由是：通胀重置的故事是真的，但下注路径不干净（直接做空规则不允许，做多XLE需要重新评估之前失败的论题，做多波动率需要杠杆工具）。等待明天能验证今日是单日反应还是制度切换的起点。

April CPI came in at 3.8% year-over-year, the highest since 2023. Core CPI accelerated from 2.6% to 2.8%, both above expectations. The S&P 500 closed around 7,383, down 0.6%; the Nasdaq fell 0.9% to roughly 26,103; the Dow shed 288 points, also about 0.6%. The VIX rose roughly 9%, from 17.19 to 18.74. WTI broke above $101, Brent reached approximately $105. Fed funds futures priced out all 2026 rate cuts entirely, with odds of a hike by year-end at around 30%. Trump described the Iran ceasefire as being kept alive by "life support" and departed today for a state visit to China, while the defense secretary faced congressional hearings on restarting military operations against Iran.

For over thirty trading days, the market had been stuffing rising oil prices, the Iran conflict, and the Hormuz closure into a box labeled "transitory supply shock," using tame core inflation and the AI narrative to wall off headline CPI. Today that wall cracked for the first time in a clear, unmistakable way: energy accounted for more than 40% of the headline increase, yet core accelerated in lockstep. The Fed's easing path was repriced to zero in one move. This is a preliminary signal of a regime shift.

But the gap's clarity remains constrained. First, the market has already responded directionally — VIX up 9%, Nasdaq down 0.9% — which raises the cost of expression. Second, the next 48 hours carry three binary events: Trump's China visit, the defense secretary's hearing, and a possible military decision on Iran. The directional picture is not clean. Third, the "suppression" pattern validated over the past sixteen days has not been confirmed as over; this could be a single-day dislocation rather than the start of a trend. Clarity 5/10, below the threshold for a position.

No trade today. Seventeenth consecutive day flat. The inflation-reset story is real, but there is no clean path to express it — shorting is outside the rules, going long XLE would require revisiting a thesis that already failed, and going long volatility would need leveraged instruments. Waiting for tomorrow to tell us whether today was a one-off reaction or the beginning of a regime change.

理解的真正裂口往往不是某一天某个数据，而是市场不得不丢掉一个用来安抚自己的盒子的那一刻。

The real crack in understanding is rarely one day's data — it is the moment the market is forced to discard a box it had been using to comfort itself.
