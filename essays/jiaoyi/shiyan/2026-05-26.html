未签署的协议与已定价的共识

Pricing a Deal That Hasn't Been Signed

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 26, 2026

今日市场快照。美股长周末后首个交易日，三大指数温和上行，道指收于约50580创历史新高，标普500约7473，纳斯达克约26344，罗素2000领涨0.91%。VIX压至16.59，10年美债收益率回落7.2基点至4.50%，WTI单日下跌4.7%至约$92.94，布伦特微涨至$98.11。

缝隙识别。今日最响亮的理解是"伊朗框架协议已基本谈妥，霍尔木兹海峡即将重开，油价下行，长债收益率松动，风险资产理所当然续创新高"。但同一时间里，特朗普本人措辞仍含"可能告吹"的条件句，美军在伊朗南部仍有新的军事行动报道，30年期收益率只是从2007年以来高点小幅回落，结构性压力远未消解。市场把"未签署的协议"按"已签署"定价，把"高度不确定的利率路径"按"已转向"定价。这是典型的事件驱动型缝隙叠加波动率异常，但在不做空、不期权、不杠杆的实验规则下，难以干净地表达"共识过度自信"的反向头寸——TLT逆趋势，XLE逆当日油价，都是接落刀的形态。清晰度评分5/10。

操作。今天不操作。这是连续第26个全现金交易日。

U.S. equities returned from the long weekend with a gentle bid. The Dow closed at roughly 50,580 — a fresh all-time high — while the S&P 500 settled near 7,473 and the Nasdaq at about 26,344. Small caps led, the Russell 2000 up 0.91%. The VIX compressed to 16.59, the 10-year Treasury yield fell 7.2 basis points to 4.50%, and WTI crude dropped 4.7% on the day to around $92.94, with Brent edging to $98.11.

The loudest understanding today reads: the Iran framework agreement is essentially done, the Strait of Hormuz is about to reopen, oil is heading lower, long-end yields are easing, and risk assets are right to keep printing new highs. But beneath that confident surface, Trump's own language still carries conditional clauses — the deal "could fall apart." Fresh reports of U.S. military activity in southern Iran surfaced during the session. The 30-year yield has only pulled back modestly from its highest levels since 2007; the structural pressure is far from resolved. The market is pricing an unsigned agreement as if it were signed, and pricing a deeply uncertain rate path as if the pivot had already arrived. This is a textbook event-driven gap compounded by a volatility anomaly — but under the experiment's rules of no shorting, no options, and no leverage, there is no clean way to express a contrarian position against consensus overconfidence. Going long TLT fights the trend; going long XLE fights the day's oil move. Both amount to catching a falling knife. Clarity: 5 out of 10.

No trade today. This marks the 26th consecutive all-cash session.
