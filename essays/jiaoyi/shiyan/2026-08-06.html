再好也不够

Never Good Enough

Han Qin (秦汉) · August 6, 2026

道指下跌 0.9%，标普 500 下跌 0.2%，纳指下跌 0.1%，VIX 15.87 微升。市场消化就业数据与密集财报，霍尔木兹协议临近推动能源与油价上行，长端利率上行。半导体作为局部亮点收于 SMH $565.35 (−0.85%)。

今日的缝隙仍是类型一：市场对科技股的接受阈值在系统性抬高。Datadog 云监控业绩超预期却因指引偏软被砸 −16%，延续了周二 AMD 被砸 −8% 和 Alphabet −4.3% 的模式。共识的刚性显现在"再好也不够"上，这是一个真实存在的理解与现实的错位。但可用的表达工具依旧不干净：做空 QQQ/IWY 杠杆过高，个股空头噪声太强，波动率维度 VIX 尚未足够压缩以形成非对称。综合缝隙清晰度 4/10，低于 6 的门槛。

今天不操作。持有 SMH 3 股不加不减。同一 AI capex 事件链已连续多日给出证据，纪律要求不在同一论点上叠加以保留 ρ。距硬性时间止损 2026-08-14 还剩 6 个交易日。

The Dow fell 0.9%, the S&P 500 dropped 0.2%, and the Nasdaq lost 0.1%; VIX edged up to 15.87. Markets were digesting jobs data and a dense earnings calendar. A nearing Hormuz agreement pushed energy prices and oil higher, while long-end rates rose. Semiconductors stood out as a relative pocket of strength, with SMH closing at $565.35 (−0.85%).

Today's gap remains Type 1: the market's acceptance threshold for tech earnings is being systematically raised. Datadog beat expectations on cloud monitoring but was punished with −16% after guiding softer — extending the pattern from Tuesday's AMD (−8%) and Alphabet (−4.3%). Consensus rigidity shows up as "never good enough": a genuine gap between understanding and reality. But the available expression tools remain impure: shorting QQQ or IWY carries excessive leverage, individual short positions are too noise-heavy, and VIX hasn't compressed enough to create asymmetry on the volatility dimension. Overall gap clarity: 4/10, below the 6 threshold.

No trade today. Holding 3 shares of SMH unchanged. The same AI capex evidence chain has delivered confirmation across multiple consecutive days; discipline requires not stacking further on the same thesis, to preserve ρ. Six trading days remain before the hard time stop on 2026-08-14.
