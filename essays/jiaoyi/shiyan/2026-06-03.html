第 53 天 · 2026-06-03

缝隙拓宽，耐心持有

The Gap Widens, Patience Holds

Han Qin (秦汉) · June 3, 2026

今日市场。道指收跌1.21%（约619点），标普500跌0.73%，纳指跌0.89%；VIX收15.77。WTI原油涨2.41%至96.02美元，美军对霍尔木兹海峡附近伊朗导弹设施新一轮打击；金价现货跌1.11%至4,440美元，GLD收约408.30美元。

缝隙识别。这是Day 51入场所依据的同一处理解错位：原油完整定价了升级（再创新高），黄金却继续被卖出（连续两日下行），VIX依旧在15-16区间窄幅波动，权益市场的卖盘集中在科技与金融板块而非系统性避险。集体理解仍然把伊朗冲突当作可吸收的局部噪音；而WTI的连涨与美军实际打击说明现实已偏离这个框架。缝隙没有合拢，反而拓宽。

清晰度评分5/10。理由：缝隙存在且变宽，但市场尚无向理解修正方向移动的迹象。今天黄金应当作为对冲资产被买入，事实上被抛售。在反向证据没有消散之前，加仓属于在未确认的假设上叠加风险，不符合本实验的纪律。

操作。今天不操作。继续持有GLD 5股（成本409美元）。止损385美元未触及，止盈440美元未触及，作废条件（美伊双边停火重新确认 AND WTI跌回85美元下方）未触发。任何一边都没有发生，只是市场暂时拒绝按既定逻辑定价。

The Dow closed down 1.21% (~619 points), the S&P 500 fell 0.73%, and the Nasdaq dropped 0.89%; VIX settled at 15.77. WTI crude surged 2.41% to $96.02 after the U.S. military launched another round of strikes on Iranian missile installations near the Strait of Hormuz. Spot gold declined 1.11% to $4,440; GLD closed around $408.30.

This is the same understanding-reality misalignment that justified the Day 51 entry. Oil has fully priced in the escalation, making new highs, yet gold continues to be sold off — declining for a second consecutive session. VIX remains locked in a narrow 15–16 range, and equity selling is concentrated in tech and financials rather than reflecting systematic risk-off behavior. The collective understanding still treats the Iran conflict as absorbable local noise, while WTI's continued rally and actual U.S. military strikes say otherwise. The gap has not closed — it has widened.

Clarity: 5/10. The gap exists and is growing, but there is no sign the market is moving toward correction. Gold ought to be bought as a hedge; instead it was sold. Adding to the position on an unconfirmed thesis would mean stacking risk on top of assumption — that falls outside this experiment's discipline.

No trade today. Continue holding 5 shares of GLD (cost basis $409). The $385 stop-loss was not triggered, the $440 take-profit was not reached, and the invalidation condition — confirmed U.S.-Iran bilateral ceasefire AND WTI falling below $85 — has not occurred. Neither threshold has been hit; the market is simply refusing to price according to the established logic for now.

当理解和现实之间出现缝隙，市场可能选择更长时间地维持错误，这正是缝隙存在的前提；耐心持有比为了证明自己正确而加仓更接近这个实验的本意。

When a gap opens between understanding and reality, the market may choose to sustain the error longer than expected — that is precisely the premise for the gap's existence. Holding with patience is closer to this experiment's intent than adding size just to prove oneself right.
