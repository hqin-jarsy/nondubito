旧缝隙已平，新裂纹尚浅

Old Gap Closed, New Crack Not Yet a Wall

Han Qin (秦汉) · July 2, 2026

今日快照。6 月非农 5.7 万人，大幅低于市场预期的 11.3 万，4 月与 5 月合计下修 7.4 万，失业率反而回落至 4.2%。指数出现明显分化：道指再创新高，涨约 0.8%；标普 7458.29，微跌 0.33%；纳指下跌约 1.2%，芯片板块延续昨日回调。VIX 收 16.59，两年期收益率降至 4.108%。

缝隙识别。此前守望的"隐含波动率 vs 数据+沃什鹰派"这条缝隙，今日以温和方式收敛——就业疲软带来轻微久期回补，波动率却没有出现再定价的爆点，说明市场对"数据软→暂缓加息"这条路径的理解已相当到位，缝隙被填平。真正在浮现的新缝隙，是"AI 芯片是永远的领导者"这一集体理解，与两日连续跳水的价格轨迹之间的裂纹，但一条裂纹不是一堵墙——只有一天半的分化不足以构成足够稳固的共识来做反向布局。清晰度 5 分，低于阈值。

操作。今天不操作，连续第 18 个无操作日。原缝隙已收敛，新缝隙尚未定型，无清晰的 ETF 层面表达方式，保持现金是最诚实的选择。明日 7 月 3 日，美股因独立日休市。

June non-farm payrolls came in at 57,000 — well below the consensus estimate of 113,000. April and May figures were revised down a combined 74,000. Yet the unemployment rate ticked back down to 4.2%. Equity indices diverged sharply: the Dow Jones set a new high, rising roughly 0.8%, while the S&P 500 dipped 0.33% to 7,458.29, and the Nasdaq fell about 1.2%, with the chip sector extending yesterday's retreat. VIX closed at 16.59; the two-year Treasury yield fell to 4.108%.

The gap this account had been watching — implied volatility versus the combination of softening data and Waller's hawkish posture — closed gently today. Weak employment data triggered mild duration buying, but there was no volatility repricing event. That signals the market's understanding of the "soft data → pause" path is already fully developed. The gap has been filled.

What is emerging instead is a crack between the collective understanding that AI chips are permanent market leaders and two consecutive days of sharp price declines. But a crack is not a wall. A day and a half of divergence is insufficient to build the kind of stable consensus that would justify a contrarian position. Clarity score: 5 — below threshold.

No trade today — the 18th consecutive day with no action. The original gap has converged; the new one has not solidified. There is no clear ETF-level way to express it. Holding cash is the most honest choice. Note: US markets close July 3rd for Independence Day.

缝隙有它自己的节奏，交易者若强行制造机会，反而会成为下一位对手方的猎物。

Gaps move on their own schedule. A trader who forces an opportunity will only end up as the next counterparty's quarry.
