AI故事的第一道裂缝

The First Crack in the AI Capex Story

Han Qin (秦汉) · June 4, 2026

道指逆势创新高 涨约 1.7%（+875 点），资金从科技板块大规模轮动到医疗 金融 防御类。博通 AI 指引未能上调全年 1000 亿目标 股价盘中跌 14%；CrowdStrike 跌 11%。标普 +0.4% 纳指仅 +0.1%。VIX 16.06。WTI 95.17 微跌 0.9%，黄金现货 4440 美元附近平盘。

类型一缝隙候选：道指在 VIX 16 的环境里创历史新高，同时席勒市盈率 42.53 仅次于 1999 互联网泡沫顶点；AI 资本开支故事出现第一道裂缝（博通拒绝上调全年目标）；美伊冲突仍在持续。集体理解是「轮动健康，市场稳健」。

但缝隙清晰度并不高。原因是：今天的科技抛售是有秩序的轮动而非恐慌，资金没有撤出市场只是换了赛道；黄金和油已经部分定价了避险溢价；VIX 16 是「正常偏低」而非「极度压缩」。换句话说 现在的鸿沟不够宽到足以无视摩擦成本去对赌。

清晰度 5/10。低于阈值 6。

今天不操作。

GLD 仓位的原始论题仍然成立：油在定价战争 黄金和波动率没有跟上。今天油略缓黄金持平，缝隙没有被吸收 也没有进一步张开。止损 385 远 反转触发条件（双边停火 + WTI 低于 85）未触发。继续持有。

The Dow bucked the trend and set a fresh all-time high, rallying roughly 1.7% (+875 points) as money rotated aggressively out of tech and into healthcare, financials, and defensives. Broadcom fell 14% intraday after its AI guidance declined to raise the full-year $100 billion target; CrowdStrike dropped 11%. The S&P added 0.4%, the Nasdaq barely moved at +0.1%. VIX sat at 16.06. WTI crude dipped 0.9% to $95.17; spot gold hovered near $4,440, essentially flat.

A Type-1 gap candidate emerged: the Dow hitting record highs with VIX at 16 while the Shiller PE stands at 42.53—second only to the 1999 dot-com peak; the AI capex narrative showing its first structural crack as Broadcom refused to raise its annual target; and the Iran conflict still simmering. The collective understanding reads “healthy rotation, market is sound.”

But clarity remains low. Today’s tech selloff was orderly rotation, not panic—money didn’t leave the market, it changed lanes. Gold and oil have already partially priced in the risk premium. VIX at 16 is “normal-low,” not “extremely compressed.” In other words, the chasm isn’t wide enough to justify crossing friction costs for a bet.

Clarity: 5/10. Below the threshold of 6.

No trade today.

The original thesis behind the GLD position still holds: oil is pricing in war while gold and volatility have not caught up. Today oil eased slightly and gold was flat—the gap has neither closed nor widened further. The stop at $385 remains distant, and the reversal trigger (bilateral ceasefire + WTI below $85) has not fired. Continuing to hold.
