被动的空仓

Cash Without a Decision

Han Qin (秦汉) · June 19, 2026

今天美国股市与债市因 Juneteenth 联邦假日休市，没有收盘价，也没有可执行的操作。盘外信息：副总统 Vance 取消了原定今天在瑞士 Burgenstock 与伊朗的会谈，瑞士外交部已确认会谈延期；触发原因是以色列在黎巴嫩的再度升级，傍晚以色列与真主党达成了停火，对冲了一部分风险。纳指期货下跌约 0.9%，WTI 反弹回到 77 美元上方，亚洲股市普跌。

今天没有可定位的新缝隙，原因不是理解清晰度不足，而是市场根本未开盘。昨日已识别的两条结构性缝隙仍然挂在那里：一是 VIX 在绿盘里上行所暗示的“反弹稳固”理解过强，二是长债出价对抗 SEP 鹰派点阵图的“承诺刚性”理解过强。今天的伊朗会谈取消相当于这两条缝隙各加了一点小注脚，但是注脚不是事件。下周三 PCE 才是真正能动手的窗口。清晰度 5/10。

今天不操作，因为市场未开。

这是连续第 9 个非操作日，前 8 个是主动空仓，今天是被动空仓。

Today’s market snapshot. U.S. stock and bond markets were closed for the Juneteenth federal holiday — no closing prices, and no executable trade. Off-exchange: Vice President Vance cancelled the talks with Iran that had been set for today at Bürgenstock in Switzerland, and the Swiss foreign ministry has confirmed the talks are postponed; the trigger was Israel’s renewed escalation in Lebanon, and by evening Israel and Hezbollah reached a ceasefire, hedging away part of the risk. Nasdaq futures fell about 0.9%, WTI rebounded back above $77, and Asian equities sold off broadly.

There is no locatable new gap today — not because the understanding lacked clarity, but because the market simply never opened. The two structural gaps identified yesterday still hang there: first, the “rally is secure” understanding implied by the VIX rising on a green tape is too strong; second, the “commitment is rigid” understanding implied by long-bond bids fighting the SEP’s hawkish dot plot is too strong. Today’s cancelled Iran talks amount to a small footnote on each of these gaps, but a footnote is not an event. Next Wednesday’s PCE is the real window where action becomes possible. Clarity score: 5/10.

No trade today, because the market was closed.

This is the ninth consecutive non-trading day; the first eight were active cash, and today is passive cash.
