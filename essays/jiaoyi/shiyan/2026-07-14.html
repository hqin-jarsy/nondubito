错位当日现，时机在明日

The Gap Forms Today, the Trade Comes Tomorrow

Han Qin (秦汉) · July 14, 2026

四股大力互相冲抵。6月CPI大幅低于预期：环比-0.4%（自2020年4月以来最大月度降幅），同比3.5%（预期3.8%），核心2.6%（预期2.8%）。摩根大通、花旗、富国、美银集体交出Q2大幅超预期财报。IBM盘中崩跌22%以上，Q2预警承认企业客户把资本开支紧急从软件挪向服务器/存储/内存以抢在涨价前锁定供应。与此同时，美国启动霍尔木兹海峡封锁并对过境货物加征20%关税，WTI原油站上80美元。

三大指数收盘微涨：S&P 500 +0.4%至7,543.89，纳指 +0.9%至26,107.01，道指 +0.02%至52,508.48。VIX从Monday的17.16小幅回落。

真正值得注意的不是CPI，而是IBM亲口承认的那句话：客户把开支转向服务器存储内存以"锁定紧张供应，抢在涨价前"。这直接确认了AI基础设施硬件层的供给紧张与价格上行。奇怪的是同一天SK海力士美股跌9%，Oracle跌6.5%，Intel跌6%，Lam Research跌6%。市场把这条本该是内存/存储链利好的信息解读为"AI资本开支疑虑"卖出——理解和现实之间出现了错位。

但这条缝隙才刚刚出现，尚未形成清晰的入场结构。且今日同时叠加加息定价重估、地缘再升级、银行财报消化，多重信号互相干扰。清晰度评分5分。

今天不操作。连续第25个不操作日。

关键判断：市场在24小时内吸收了四股大信号（软CPI+银行超预期+IBM暴跌+伊朗升级封锁），S&P只动了0.4个百分点。这种吸收能力本身就是信息——多数参与者已经站在各自认定的方向上，边际信息推不动价格。IBM揭示的内存供给紧张这条线值得盯，但要等一天让噪音散去，看SK海力士是否止跌企稳再决定。

Four large forces partially cancelled each other out today. June CPI came in well below expectations: -0.4% month-over-month (the largest single-month decline since April 2020), 3.5% year-over-year against a 3.8% forecast, core at 2.6% against 2.8%. JPMorgan, Citigroup, Wells Fargo, and Bank of America all beat Q2 estimates by wide margins. IBM plunged more than 22% intraday after its Q2 warning acknowledged that enterprise clients are urgently reallocating capital expenditure away from software toward servers, storage, and memory — locking in supply ahead of anticipated price increases. Meanwhile, the U.S. announced a blockade of the Strait of Hormuz and imposed a 20% tariff on transit cargo; WTI crude settled above $80.

The major indices closed marginally higher: S&P 500 +0.4% to 7,543.89, Nasdaq +0.9% to 26,107.01, Dow +0.02% to 52,508.48. VIX edged down from Monday's 17.16.

What truly stands out is not the CPI print — it's the admission IBM made publicly: customers are shifting spend toward servers, storage, and memory to "lock in tight supply before prices rise." This directly confirms supply tightness and upward price pressure in the AI infrastructure hardware layer. Strangely, on the same day SK Hynix fell 9%, Oracle 6.5%, Intel 6%, and Lam Research 6%. The market interpreted what should have been a bullish signal for the memory and storage chain as a reason to sell on "AI capex uncertainty" — a clear gap between understanding and reality.

But this gap has only just appeared, and no clean entry structure has formed yet. Today also layered in rate-pricing reassessment, geopolitical escalation, and bank earnings absorption — multiple signals interfering with each other. Clarity score: 5.

No trade today. The 25th consecutive no-trade day.

The key judgment: in 24 hours, the market absorbed four major signals — soft CPI, bank beats, IBM collapse, Iran escalating the Hormuz blockade — and the S&P moved just 0.4%. That absorption capacity is itself information: most participants are already positioned in their preferred direction, and incremental signals aren't moving the price. The memory supply tightness revealed by IBM is a thread worth watching, but the right move is to wait a day for the noise to clear and see whether SK Hynix stabilizes before making a decision.

理解的错位有时是当天出现的，交易的时机往往是第二天以后。

Gaps in understanding sometimes appear on the day they form — but the trading opportunity usually arrives the day after.
