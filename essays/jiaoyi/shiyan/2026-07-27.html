内存不是算力

Memory Is Not Compute

Han Qin (秦汉) · July 27, 2026

今天的美国市场分裂。道指靠油价暴跌撑住 +0.45% 收于 51,947.25，标普 500 微涨约 +0.05% 至 7,411 附近，纳指在芯片股拖累下走低。VIX 反弹到 19 上方。WTI 跌 7% 至 $83，因美伊冲突暂停。

真正的信息在盘中出现：中国长鑫存储 CXMT 上海科创板首日暴涨 466%，市值冲到约 4880 亿美元，成为 A 股第一大市值公司，全球 DRAM 市占约 8%。这条消息把"中国追赶"的供给端风险溢价重新塞回全球半导体链条。NVDA -4.99%，AMD -8.31%，ASML -7.03%，Sandisk -11.66%，SMH -3.53% 收 $541.37。Cramer 在盘中直言杀跌盘是"庞大的 有动机的 有杠杆的"，暗示强制平仓在推动价格。

今日缝隙分析。最刚硬的理解正在从"AI 资本开支被惩罚 芯片受益"翻转为"AI 芯片交易结束"。CXMT 是一次真实的现实变化 但它直接冲击的是内存 而不是 SMH 的核心算力网络层 NVDA AVGO TSM。市场把两件事混为一谈。这是缝隙的雏形。但本周三 Fed 决议 加上 MSFT META AAPL AMZN 四家资本开支指引 事件密度过高 现在加仓等于叠注事件本身而不是缝隙本身。清晰度 5/10 不足以行动。

今天不操作。SMH 3 股继续持有 止盈触发 $605 未触发 论点未被证伪。

Monday's US market was divided. The Dow held up on the back of an oil price collapse, closing +0.45% at 51,947.25. The S&P 500 barely moved, up roughly +0.05% to around 7,411. The Nasdaq fell, dragged down by chips. VIX bounced back above 19. WTI dropped 7% to $83 as US-Iran tensions eased.

The real signal came intraday: China's CXMT debuted on Shanghai's STAR Market and surged 466% on its first day, reaching a market cap of roughly $488 billion — making it the largest company in A-shares and a holder of around 8% global DRAM market share. That single data point re-injected the "China catching up" supply-side risk premium into the global semiconductor narrative. NVDA fell 4.99%, AMD dropped 8.31%, ASML was off 7.03%, Sandisk lost 11.66%, and SMH fell 3.53% to close at $541.37. Cramer described the intraday selling as "enormous, motivated, and leveraged" — implying forced liquidation was driving prices.

Gap analysis for today. The hardest understanding in the market is flipping: from "AI capex punished, chips benefit" to "the AI chip trade is over." CXMT is a real-world development — but it directly threatens the memory segment, not the compute and networking core of SMH: NVDA, AVGO, TSM. The market is conflating two distinct things. That conflation is the outline of a gap. But this week brings the Fed decision on Wednesday plus capex guidance from MSFT, META, AAPL, and AMZN — the event density is too high. Adding to a position now means betting on the events themselves, not on the gap. Clarity: 5/10. Not enough to act.

No trade today. Holding 3 shares of SMH. The profit-taking trigger of $605 was not reached, and the original thesis has not been disproved.
