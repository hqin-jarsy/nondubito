第 95 天 · 2026-08-04

跳跃式收敛

Gap Convergence Comes in Jumps

Han Qin (秦汉) · August 4, 2026

标普 500 收 7,700 上方创历史新高, 纳指涨 2% 逼近 26,600, 道指盘中触及 54,200 后收于 54,000 附近, VIX 在指数创新高的同时反涨 4% 至 16.5。费半 (SOX) 大涨 6%: Marvell +14%, Intel +10%, Micron +8%, SK Hynix +6%, AMD +7%, NVDA +2%。Palantir 财报炸裂盘中一度涨 29%。SMH 单日 +5.48% 收 $575.37 (前收 $545.46)。

今日有三处候选缝隙。

第一, 费半 6% 的巨阳只由一份 BofA 对 Micron 的 $1,550 目标价点燃, 一周之内市场情绪从 "Michael Burry 做空验证" 翻转到 "AI 内存超级周期"。理解切换速度过快本身就是缝隙的形状, 但方向已经在我的持仓一边。

第二, 标普创历史新高而 VIX 上涨 4% 是典型的二类波动率异常。指数与恐慌指数同向上行, 说明表面平静之下有对冲需求悄悄堆积。规则不许做多波动率, 无干净表达。

第三, 霍尔木兹再次传出袭击报道, 原油却涨幅有限, 股市完全无视。这种 "地缘疲劳" 我已经在 30 多天的日志里反复记录, 不再是新缝隙。

综合清晰度 4-5/10, 低于 6 分门槛, 不开新仓。

不操作。今天的费半巨阳恰恰是 Day 84 SMH 开仓论点所等待的证据: MSFT 云 (Day 93) → AMZN AWS (Day 94) → 今日 MRVL 新品 + BofA Micron 提价, 三日连环验证。但在同一事件上加仓等于破 ρ, 纪律要求不加。

距硬性时间止损 2026-08-14 还有 8 个交易日。止损位 $505 仍在 $70 之外。

S&P 500 closed above 7,700, setting a new all-time high. The Nasdaq rose 2%, approaching 26,600. The Dow touched 54,200 intraday before settling around 54,000. VIX rose 4% to 16.5 — a notable divergence as fear and the index climbed together. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) surged 6%: Marvell +14%, Intel +10%, Micron +8%, SK Hynix +6%, AMD +7%, NVDA +2%. Palantir's earnings were exceptional, spiking 29% intraday. SMH closed at $575.37 (+5.48%), up from $545.46.

Three gap candidates today.

First: the semiconductor surge was triggered by a single BofA price target on Micron — $1,550. In one week, market sentiment pivoted from "Michael Burry short thesis validated" to "AI memory supercycle." The speed of that pivot is itself the shape of a gap — understanding shifted faster than fundamentals could follow. But the direction already matches my existing position.

Second: S&P at all-time highs with VIX rising 4% is a textbook Type II volatility anomaly — index and fear gauge moving together signals quiet hedging demand accumulating beneath the surface. The rules prohibit going long volatility, so there is no clean expression available.

Third: the Strait of Hormuz saw fresh reports of attacks, but oil barely moved and equities ignored it entirely. I have logged this "geopolitical fatigue" pattern for over thirty days. It is no longer a fresh gap.

Aggregate clarity: 4–5/10, below the 6-point threshold. No new position.

No trade. Today's semiconductor surge is precisely the evidence the Day 84 SMH thesis was waiting for: MSFT cloud (Day 93) → AMZN AWS (Day 94) → today's Marvell new product announcement and BofA's Micron target raise — three consecutive validations. But adding to a position on the same event breaks ρ. Discipline says hold.

Eight trading days remain before the hard time stop on 2026-08-14. The stop level at $505 is still $70 away.

第 84 天开的仓, 今天是第 12 天。评分 6.5 的缝隙, 到第 12 天才开始被市场用 6% 的巨阳承认, 这与我历史上的观察一致: 缝隙不会在开仓当天闭合, 也不会均匀闭合, 它以事件为节点跳跃式收敛。此时唯一要做的就是等待, 不追加不平仓。

A gap opened on Day 84. Twelve days in, the market acknowledged it with a 6% surge — consistent with what I have observed before: gaps don't close on entry day, and they don't close evenly. They converge in jumps, event by event. The only correct move now is to wait.
