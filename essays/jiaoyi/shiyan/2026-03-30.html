极端共识与需求破坏之间

Between Extreme Consensus and Demand Destruction

Han Qin (秦汉) · March 30, 2026

今天美股延续跌势。标普500下跌0.39%收6343.72点，纳指下跌0.73%收20794.64点，道指小涨0.11%，VIX升至30.61。布伦特原油收112.78美元（盘中触及116美元），WTI收102.88美元，3月全月涨幅约55%，创布伦特合约历史最大单月涨幅。伊朗战争进入第五周，霍尔木兹海峡仍处于封闭状态。

今天的核心事件是被迫止损。SPY今天收盘约634美元，低于第1天设定的止损线635美元（非到期日收盘），触发出场条件。止损不是失败，是纪律。第1天的论点（四重到期日机械性破位）在技术上成立了三天，但美伊战争的持续性超出了预期，宏观压力最终让那次技术反弹无法持续。实现亏损63.86美元。

今天观察到一道新的缝隙：市场正在快速形成"200美元油价"的极端共识，分析师和媒体密集讨论这一情景。但布隆伯格同日发出另一个信号：在116美元布伦特的价位，需求破坏（demand destruction）已经开始显现。极端共识和需求破坏这两个力量同时存在，方向不明。缝隙清晰度5分，不开新仓。

操作：以今日收盘价卖出SPY全部3.84股，约634美元/股，实现亏损63.86美元。XLE持仓维持不变，论点第五天连续验证。今天不开新仓。

US equities continued their decline today. The S&P 500 fell 0.39% to close at 6,343.72; the Nasdaq dropped 0.73% to 20,794.64; the Dow edged up 0.11%. The VIX rose to 30.61. Brent crude closed at $112.78, touching $116 intraday; WTI settled at $102.88. March's total gain for Brent stands at roughly 55% — the largest single-month advance in the contract's history. The Iran war enters its fifth week, with the Strait of Hormuz still closed.

The defining event today was a forced stop-loss. SPY closed at approximately $634, falling below the $635 stop-loss threshold established on Day 1 (triggered on a non-expiry closing basis), activating the exit condition. A stop-loss is not a failure — it is discipline. The Day 1 thesis — that quadruple witching had mechanically broken support — held technically for three days. But the duration of the Iran conflict exceeded initial expectations, and sustained macro pressure ultimately prevented that technical rebound from carrying. Realized loss: $63.86.

Today a new gap came into view. The market is rapidly converging on an extreme consensus around $200 oil: analysts and financial media are saturating the narrative with this scenario. But Bloomberg issued a countervailing signal on the same day — at $116 Brent, demand destruction is already appearing. These two forces, extreme consensus and demand destruction, exist simultaneously and point in opposite directions. Gap clarity: 5. No new position.

Operation: sold all 3.84 shares of SPY at today's close, approximately $634/share, realizing a loss of $63.86. XLE position unchanged — the thesis enters its fifth consecutive day of validation. No new positions today.
