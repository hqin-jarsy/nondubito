市场已历过太多遍相同的剧本

When the Market Has Seen This Script Before

Han Qin (秦汉) · July 8, 2026

特朗普宣布对伊朗的临时停火协议"已经结束"，同时撤销伊朗石油销售的制裁豁免，美国中央司令部对伊朗发动新一轮打击。WTI原油收涨4.4%至73.52美元，布伦特收涨5.2%至78.02美元。标普500下跌约0.45%至7470点附近，纳斯达克跌1.16%，道琼斯跌0.25%，VIX仅升至16.13。能源是唯一明显收涨的板块，XLE收于54.95美元。

表面上看，缝隙位于两处。其一，特朗普正式宣布停火结束，配合撤销制裁豁免与实弹打击，但VIX仅16，市场对地缘风险的定价几乎没有反应。其二，油价上涨5%但XLE本身没有突破，说明能源股本身已经消化过多轮升降级预期。

问题是，过去四个月里特朗普对伊朗的立场多次反复。市场当前的平静更像是理解已经建立："这是又一次口头升级，48小时内多半会有软化。"这种理解未必错。今天油价的反应也许就是最完整的反应了。

真正的缝隙需要"新的现实到达但市场仍在按旧的现实定价"。今天的现实是否真的更新了，尚需一到两个交易日的确认。清晰度5，低于开仓阈值6。

今天不操作。连续第21个空仓日。

Trump declared that the temporary ceasefire agreement with Iran "has ended," simultaneously revoking sanction waivers on Iranian oil exports while CENTCOM launched a new round of strikes. WTI crude closed up 4.4% at $73.52; Brent up 5.2% at $78.02. The S&P 500 fell roughly 0.45% to near 7,470, the Nasdaq dropped 1.16%, the Dow fell 0.25%, and VIX rose only to 16.13. Energy was the sole sector with meaningful gains; XLE closed at $54.95.

On the surface, two potential gaps emerged. First, Trump formally declared the ceasefire over, backed by revoked waivers and live military action — yet VIX stayed at 16, suggesting the market has barely priced in geopolitical risk. Second, oil surged 5% but XLE itself failed to break out, indicating energy equities have already absorbed multiple cycles of escalation and de-escalation.

The problem is that over the past four months, Trump's posture toward Iran has reversed course repeatedly. The current market calm looks less like complacency and more like an established understanding: "This is another round of verbal escalation — it will likely soften within 48 hours." That understanding may well be correct. Today's oil move may be the complete and final reaction.

A true gap requires a new reality arriving while the market still prices in the old one. Whether today's reality has genuinely changed still needs one or two more sessions to confirm. Clarity score: 5 — below the entry threshold of 6.

No trade today. Twenty-first consecutive day in cash.

当市场对一则明显的头条反应平淡，往往不是市场错，而是市场已经历过太多次相同的剧本。

When a market shrugs at an obvious headline, it is usually not wrong — it has simply lived through the same script too many times before.
