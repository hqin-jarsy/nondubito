绿盘里的怀疑

Doubt Beneath a Green Tape

Han Qin (秦汉) · June 18, 2026

标普 +1.15%，纳斯达克 +1.5%，道指 +0.80%，罗素 2000 却跌 0.72%。VIX 在绿盘里反而升到 18.44，十年期收益率上行 7 个基点至 4.50%，TLT 微涨到 87.12。WTI 跌破 75 美元，XLE 收 54.67。初请失业金 22.6 万，略高于预期。明天美股因六月节休市，伊朗谅解备忘录将在瑞士正式签署。

最坚硬的理解是「鲍威尔之后是沃什的鹰派，但芯片和工业能撑起反弹，伊朗已经解决」。三处反例：VIX 在指数上涨时反向走高，说明买保护的人在为周末事件密集付费；罗素 2000 与标普背离，利率敏感盘并未相信鹰派已被消化；长端债更稳，TLT 反弹意味着真正定价利率路径的那一段曲线对点阵图持怀疑。

最干净的表达是做多 TLT 押注沃什口中「已具限制性」的政策最终被数据证实。但催化剂是 6 月 26 日的 PCE，中间隔着一个假期与一个外交签字仪式。在节前盘里把仓位放进去，并不能换来更好的赔率。清晰度评分 5/10。

今天不操作。这是连续第 8 个空仓日。

Today’s market snapshot. The S&P 500 rose 1.15%, the Nasdaq 1.5%, and the Dow 0.80%, but the Russell 2000 fell 0.72%. The VIX actually climbed to 18.44 on a green day; the 10-year yield rose 7 basis points to 4.50%, and TLT edged up to 87.12. WTI broke below $75 and XLE closed at 54.67. Initial jobless claims came in at 226,000, slightly above expectations. U.S. markets are closed tomorrow for Juneteenth, and the Iran memorandum of understanding will be formally signed in Switzerland.

Where is the gap. The most rigid piece of understanding is “after Powell comes Warsh’s hawkishness, but chips and industrials can carry the rally, and Iran is already resolved.” Three counter-examples: the VIX rising while the indices rise shows that buyers of protection are paying heavily for the weekend’s events; the Russell 2000 diverging from the S&P shows that rate-sensitive names do not believe the hawkish turn has been digested; and the steadier long end — TLT’s rebound — means the segment of the curve that actually prices the rate path is skeptical of the dot plot.

The cleanest expression is to go long TLT, betting that the policy Warsh called “already restrictive” is ultimately confirmed by the data. But the catalyst is the June 26 PCE, with a holiday and a diplomatic signing ceremony in between. Putting on the position in a pre-holiday session does not buy better odds. Clarity score: 5/10.

No trade today. This is the eighth consecutive flat day.
