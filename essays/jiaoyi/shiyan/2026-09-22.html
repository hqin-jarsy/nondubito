两条过线的缝隙，一条也够不着

Two Gaps Over the Line, Neither Within Reach

Han Qin (秦汉) · September 22, 2026

标普收 7,764.98，基本持平；纳指收 27,241.32（+0.44%），连续第二天创历史新高；道指收 51,870.58（−0.34%）。VIX 收 14.22（−4.37%）。原油跌到 $89.09（−3.55%），压在油价上的是对伊朗外交进展的期待。

今天有两条缝隙过了门槛，没有一条能交易。

过线的两条

第一条是昨天那条的延续，而且更宽了：联储刚开启新一轮加息，同一个市场把十月再加一次定价成一枚硬币，VIX 却又往下走到 14.22。一个承认自己不知道下个月会发生什么的市场，不该给出这么低的波动率。7/10。它的干净表达需要期权。

第二条在半导体：板块站在历史高位，建立在一个前提上——AI 资本开支对利率不敏感。在加息周期的开头把这个前提当作定论，是刚性的。6/10。但它指向的方向是做空。

两条都落在这个实验的规则之外：只做多、不用期权、不做空。

能表达的两条

能用规则内工具表达的两条，失败的原因不在工具，而在于市场的理解本来就不刚性。原油今年在 $70 到 $118 之间摆动，这是一个知道自己不知道的市场；十月加息定价在五五开附近，那是能找到的最不刚性的价格。缝隙要求市场把一个不确定的东西当成确定的，这两处恰恰相反。能表达的最高分：4。

这已经是连续第 22 个交易日没有操作。我没有为了结束这段空白而降低门槛。

操作：今天不操作。

The S&P closed at 7,764.98, essentially flat. The Nasdaq closed at 27,241.32 (+0.44%), a record for the second straight session, while the Dow slipped to 51,870.58 (−0.34%). VIX closed at 14.22 (−4.37%). Crude fell to $89.09 (−3.55%) on hopes of progress in diplomacy with Iran.

Two gaps cleared the threshold today. Neither was tradeable.

The Two Over the Line

The first extends yesterday's gap, and it has widened. The Fed has just begun a new hiking cycle, the same market prices another hike in October as a coin flip, and yet VIX drifted lower to 14.22. A market that admits it doesn't know what next month holds shouldn't be pricing volatility that low. 7/10. Expressing it cleanly requires options.

The second is in semiconductors. The sector sits at records on the premise that AI capital spending is insensitive to rates. Treating that premise as settled at the start of a hiking cycle is rigid. 6/10. But the gap points short.

Both fall outside this experiment's rules: long-only, no options, no shorting.

The Two Within Reach

The two ideas that the rules would allow me to express failed for a different reason. The problem wasn't the instrument. The market's understanding simply wasn't rigid. Crude has ranged from $70 to $118 this year, which is a market that knows it doesn't know, and October hike odds near 50/50 are about the least rigid price available. A gap requires the market to treat something uncertain as certain, and both of these do the opposite. Highest expressible score: 4.

That makes 22 consecutive trading days without a trade. I didn't lower the threshold to end the streak.

No trade today.

空白持续得越久，降低门槛就越像是一种解决办法；它不是。

The longer the silence runs, the more lowering the bar looks like a solution. It isn't one.
