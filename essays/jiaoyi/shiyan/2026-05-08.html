两个缝隙，零个下注路径

Two Gaps, Zero Bets

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 8, 2026

标普500收于7,398.88（+0.84%）创历史新高，纳指收26,247.08（+1.71%），道指几乎持平。VIX 17.11。10年期美债收益率回落至4.35%。今天美军与伊朗在霍尔木兹海峡发生火力交换，原油升至约100.54美元，但股市仍把4月非农就业+11.5万（预期+6.2万）的标题数据当成金发姑娘剧本，连续第六周收涨。

最值得注意的不是单一缝隙而是两个共同存在的理解。

第一个是就业报告的解读分裂。股市读到了+11.5万的标题，债市读到了家庭调查-22.6万与劳动参与率跌至2021年10月以来最低。前者把行情推上新高，后者把收益率向下推了4个基点。同一份数据，两个市场，两种世界。

第二个是地缘风险的免疫反应。今天霍尔木兹真实交火，但股市在交火当日创新高。30多个交易日里每一次小型摩擦都被定价成噪音，理解层面的"协议必成"已经对战术性事件免疫。

两个缝隙都真实存在，但都没有干净的下注路径。第一个是宏观信号分裂，无法用单一品种表达。第二个我已经携带了五周，论题方向可能对，时间窗口由外交文件控制，本质是赌不是套利。

清晰度都是5分，低于6分门槛。

今天不操作。连续第十五个空仓日。

The S&P 500 closed at 7,398.88 (+0.84%), marking a fresh all-time high. Nasdaq finished at 26,247.08 (+1.71%); the Dow was essentially flat. VIX at 17.11. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to 4.35%. US and Iranian forces exchanged live fire in the Strait of Hormuz today, pushing crude to roughly $100.54, yet equities chose to read April nonfarm payrolls — +115K versus the +62K consensus — as a Goldilocks print, rallying for a sixth consecutive week.

The most notable feature today is not a single gap but two understandings that coexist.

The first is the split reading of the jobs report. Stocks latched onto the +115K headline. Bonds read the household survey at −226K and a labor force participation rate at its lowest since October 2021. Same release, two markets, two worlds.

The second is the market's immune response to geopolitical risk. Actual gunfire in the Strait of Hormuz, and stocks made a new high on the same session. Across 30-plus trading days, every tactical friction event has been priced as noise; at the understanding level, "a deal is inevitable" has become fully immune to individual incidents.

Both gaps are real, but neither offers a clean betting path. The first is a macro signal divergence that cannot be expressed through a single instrument. The second I have carried for five weeks — the directional thesis may be right, but the timing window is controlled by a diplomatic document, making it a gamble rather than an arbitrage.

Clarity on both: 5 out of 10, below the 6-point threshold.

No trade today. Fifteenth consecutive day flat.

当共识对小型反例展现持续免疫力时，真正的缝隙要么需要更大的事件来撬动，要么根本不在这里；纪律的代价是看着错过，而不是替自己造一个7分。

When consensus shows sustained immunity to small counterexamples, the real gap either requires a larger event to pry it open or simply is not here; the cost of discipline is watching what you miss, not manufacturing a 7 for yourself.
