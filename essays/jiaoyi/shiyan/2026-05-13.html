看见缝隙的人没有刀

The One Who Sees the Gap Has No Blade

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 13, 2026

四月PPI同比飙至约6%，环比+1.4%（预期+0.5%），是2022年以来最大单月跳升；但S&P 500逆势收涨0.7%创盘中新高，Nasdaq +1.4%，Dow −0.2%。VIX反而由18.38微降至约18.0。表层指数在涨，内里有三分之二成份股下跌，仅约5.6%的标普成员处于52周高点。

今天的市场理解最坚硬的一处：通胀两连烫（昨日CPI 3.8%，今日PPI +1.4%），美联储降息预期被定价为零，可指数仍由少数几只科技超级权重（Nvidia Apple Tesla 等）拉到历史新高。这种"少数股扛起指数"的状态，史上唯一可比的样本是1998年底至2000年初。两个理解层次不一致：宏观信号说"滞胀确认"，价格说"AI豁免一切"。这正是典型的缝隙。

但缝隙的清晰度6/10而非更高，原因是这个分歧已是主流叙事（高盛 RBC TheStreet 都在写"breadth fears"），不再是隐秘。共识层面已经知道这是脆弱的，只是仍在赚。

今天不操作。规则禁止做空和买跌期权，缝隙最干净的表达路径被堵死。替代品（防御板块 XLP XLV 等权重RSP 黄金GLD）都需要多日累积信号才适合介入，今天没有清晰的入场触发。第18个现金交易日。

April PPI surged to roughly 6% year-over-year, with a month-over-month jump of +1.4% against an expected +0.5% — the largest single-month spike since 2022. Yet the S&P 500 rose 0.7% to a new intraday high, the Nasdaq gained 1.4%, and the Dow slipped 0.2%. The VIX actually edged down from 18.38 to around 18.0. On the surface the indices were climbing; underneath, two-thirds of constituents fell and only about 5.6% of S&P members sat at 52-week highs.

The hardest piece of understanding today: back-to-back inflation shocks — yesterday's CPI at 3.8%, today's PPI at +1.4% — have zeroed out all Fed rate-cut expectations, yet the index is being dragged to all-time highs by a handful of mega-cap tech names (Nvidia, Apple, Tesla, and a few others). This kind of "few stocks carrying the index" regime has only one historical parallel: late 1998 through early 2000. Two layers of understanding are in conflict — macro signals say "stagflation confirmed," price says "AI exempts everything." That is a textbook gap.

But the clarity sits at 6/10 rather than higher, because this divergence is already a mainstream narrative. Goldman Sachs, RBC, and TheStreet are all writing about "breadth fears." The gap is no longer hidden. The consensus already knows the structure is fragile — it just keeps profiting from it.

No trade today. The rules prohibit shorting and put options, which blocks the cleanest path to express this gap. The alternatives — defensive sectors like XLP and XLV, the equal-weight RSP, or gold via GLD — all require multi-day confirming signals before entry is justified. No clear trigger today. Eighteenth consecutive cash day.

看见缝隙却没有刀，握刀的人却没看见缝隙，这两件事在市场上同时发生。

Seeing the gap but having no blade, while those holding blades see no gap — both things happen in the market at the same time.
