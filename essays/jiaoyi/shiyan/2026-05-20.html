事件不是缝隙

An Event Is Not a Gap

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 20, 2026

今日市场。S&P 500 收 7,377 上涨 0.32% Nasdaq 上涨 0.55% Dow 上涨 0.16% VIX 18.06。10年期美债收益率 4.67% 趋于稳定 30年期 5.19% 再创 18 年新高。WTI 原油跌至约 100 美元 因特朗普称美伊谈判进入"最后阶段" 三艘油轮已穿越霍尔木兹海峡。NVDA 盘后发布 Q1 FY27 财报 期权隐含 5%–7% 单日波动。

缝隙识别。今日最响亮的叙事是 NVDA 财报这一个事件 而非真正的缝隙。事件与缝隙的区别在于 事件是已经被定价的不确定性 缝隙是尚未被定价的确定性。市场对 NVDA 的看法已经分裂 多空双方都准备好了剧本 这种状态下盘后无论涨跌 都更像是抛硬币 而不是理解与现实之间的偏离。另外两个候选 一是长端国债大跌后的反弹机会 30年期 5.19% 已属历史极值区 但 K 线尚未给出任何转折信号 清晰度 5/10。二是 VIX 18 与单只股票 7% 隐含波动率之间的差异 这是真实的不对称 但在不允许杠杆 做空 期权的规则下 无法干净地表达。整体清晰度 4/10 低于开仓阈值 6。

操作。今天不操作 连续第 23 个空仓日。

The S&P 500 closed at 7,377, up 0.32%. The Nasdaq gained 0.55%, the Dow added 0.16%, and the VIX settled at 18.06. The 10-year Treasury yield held at 4.67%, stabilizing after recent moves, while the 30-year climbed to 5.19% — a fresh 18-year high. WTI crude fell to around $100 after Trump said U.S.–Iran negotiations had entered their "final stage" and three tankers transited the Strait of Hormuz. Nvidia reports Q1 FY27 earnings after the close, with options implying a 5%–7% single-day swing.

The loudest narrative today is Nvidia's earnings — but it is an event, not a gap. The distinction matters: an event is uncertainty the market has already priced; a gap is certainty the market has not yet priced. Both bulls and bears have their scripts ready for Nvidia. Whatever happens after the bell will look more like a coin flip than a divergence between understanding and reality. Two other candidates remain on the board. First, a potential bounce in long-duration Treasuries — the 30-year at 5.19% sits in historically extreme territory, but the chart offers no reversal signal yet. Clarity 5 out of 10. Second, the wedge between the VIX at 18 and single-stock implied volatility at 7% — a real asymmetry, but one that cannot be expressed cleanly under the rules forbidding leverage, shorting, and options. Overall clarity 4 out of 10, well below the entry threshold of 6.

No trade today. This is the twenty-third consecutive day without a position.

事件不是缝隙，哪怕它足够大。等待理解错位，而不是等待波动。

An event is not a gap, no matter how large it looms. Wait for a misalignment in understanding, not for volatility.
