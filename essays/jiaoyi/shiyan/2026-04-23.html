两个共识，零个机会

Two Consensuses, Zero Opportunities

Han Qin (秦汉) · April 23, 2026

今日市况。标普500收7109.05跌0.40%，纳斯达克综合收24440.24跌0.88%，道指收49309.80跌0.36%；VIX升至19.49。WTI涨3%至$95.85，布伦特突破$105至$105.07。软件板块遭重挫，IBM跌约9%，ServiceNow盘中跌幅一度接近18%，Salesforce、Oracle、Adobe等全线下跌。伊朗议长Ghalibaf在革命卫队压力下退出与美谈判团队，强硬派接管谈判。

缝隙识别。今日出现两个相互竞争的"理解"：其一是昨日的"延长就是解决"共识，被议长退出事件冲击但尚未破裂；其二是新形成的"AI将吞噬传统软件"共识，在IBM超预期业绩下仍遭9%暴跌中迅速凝固。

石油与股市的背离继续扩大，但表达成本太高：追石油已含战争溢价，做空股指需要一个具体的动能触发点而不是政治事件。IBM业绩好于预期且维持指引仍被砸9%，属经典过度反应，但单日下跌尚不足以成为反向切入点，叙事可能还要持续几日或几周。

清晰度5/10。

操作。今天不操作。现金仓位维持。

The S&P 500 closed at 7,109.05, down 0.40%. The Nasdaq Composite fell 0.88% to 24,440.24. The Dow settled at 49,309.80, off 0.36%. The VIX ticked up to 19.49. WTI crude surged 3% to $95.85, and Brent broke through $105 to $105.07. Software stocks were hit hard: IBM dropped roughly 9%, ServiceNow fell as much as 18% intraday, and Salesforce, Oracle, and Adobe all declined. In Tehran, parliament speaker Ghalibaf withdrew from the US negotiation team under pressure from the Revolutionary Guard, handing the process to hardliners.

Two competing understandings emerged today. The first is yesterday's "extension equals resolution" consensus, shaken by the speaker's withdrawal but not yet broken. The second is a freshly solidifying belief that "AI will devour traditional software," crystallizing around IBM's 9% plunge despite beating expectations.

The divergence between oil and equities continues to widen, but the cost of expressing either view is too high. Chasing crude means buying into a thick war premium. Shorting the index requires a concrete momentum catalyst, not a political headline. IBM beating estimates and maintaining guidance while still getting hammered 9% is a textbook overreaction — but a single day's selloff is not yet enough to constitute an entry point. The narrative may need days or weeks to exhaust itself.

Clarity 5/10.

No trade today. Cash position maintained.
