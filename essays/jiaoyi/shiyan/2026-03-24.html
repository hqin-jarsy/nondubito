没有一场战争永远持续

No War Lasts Forever

Han Qin (秦汉) · March 24, 2026

今天是实验的第三天。

美股今天继续承压。标普500下跌0.37%收6556点，纳斯达克下跌0.84%收21762点，VIX恐惧指数维持在约27，处于极度恐惧区间。美伊冲突进入第四周，布伦特原油维持在112至120美元区间高位。特朗普5天停火声明已到期，未见任何实质进展——这证实了第2天的判断：那道缝隙是真实的。

今天的缝隙来自纳斯达克100（QQQ）的一个技术信号：触及200日均线。

市场的集体理解是：战争无限期持续，科技股估值应该持续压缩。这个叙事已经相当刚性。但这个理解有一个盲点：霍尔木兹海峡封锁对伊朗自身的经济代价是不可持续的。历史上，地缘冲突的实际持续时间几乎总是短于市场在最悲观时期的预期。市场现在定价的，是一场永无止境的战争。

没有一场战争永远持续。

QQQ触及200日均线，是一个历史上胜率较高的技术支撑位。两个信号同时出现：技术面支撑加上基本面叙事过度悲观。缝隙清晰度：6.5分。

以今日收盘价约588美元买入纳斯达克100 ETF（QQQ）4股，合计2352美元，占总资金约23.5%。止损条件：若QQQ收盘跌破575美元且无明显催化剂支撑，退出仓位。

三个缝隙同时打开，仓位也接近上限了。

Today is the third day of the experiment.

US equities continued under pressure. The S&P 500 fell 0.37% to close at 6,556; the Nasdaq dropped 0.84% to 21,762; the VIX fear index held at roughly 27 — extreme fear territory. The US-Iran conflict entered its fourth week, with Brent crude staying elevated between $112 and $120. Trump's 5-day ceasefire window expired with no substantive progress, confirming Day 2's thesis: that gap was real.

Today's gap comes from a technical signal in the Nasdaq 100 (QQQ): it touched the 200-day moving average.

The market's collective understanding is that the war will continue indefinitely, and therefore technology valuations should keep compressing. This narrative has become quite rigid. But it has a blind spot: the Strait of Hormuz blockade carries an unsustainable economic cost for Iran itself. Historically, the actual duration of geopolitical conflicts has almost always been shorter than markets expect during peak pessimism. What markets are pricing now is a war that never ends.

No war lasts forever.

QQQ touching the 200-day moving average is historically a high-probability technical support level. Two signals converging: technical support plus fundamentally excessive pessimism in the narrative. Gap clarity: 6.5 out of 10.

Bought 4 shares of QQQ (Nasdaq 100 ETF) at approximately $588, totaling $2,352 — roughly 23.5% of total capital. Stop-loss condition: if QQQ closes below $575 with no obvious catalyst, exit the position.

Three gaps open at the same time. We're approaching the position limit.
