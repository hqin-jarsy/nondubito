债券熊市深化，喧嚣中的沉默

The Bond Bear Deepens, Silence Amid the Noise

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 19, 2026

今日市场。标普500收于7,372点跌0.42%，纳指跌0.55%，道指跌0.85%。VIX 17.99几乎纹丝不动。10年期美债盘中触及4.7%为15个月高位收于4.601%。30年期美债收益率5.133%创18年新高。WTI原油$107.77跌0.82%因特朗普推迟对伊朗的预定打击。Seagate跌近7%Micron跌约6%AI基础设施板块继续回调英伟达明日盘后发布Q1 FY27财报。

缝隙识别。今天最响亮的理解是债券熊市再度加深长端收益率飙至多年高点。这是共识不是缝隙。两个潜在缝隙仍在：一是债券波动率与股票波动率的背离30年期破5.13%而VIX只有18第二是英伟达发布前的科技去风险但市场对"beat and raise"的预期仍是高度共识。两者清晰度均约5分。伊朗尾部风险方面油价已部分计入特朗普推迟打击的缓和但他给伊朗"两三天"的最后期限把局势变成二元事件不是缝隙是抛硬币。清晰度低于6分。

操作。今天不操作。这是第22个连续的空仓日。在无杠杆无做空无期权的规则下识别到的缝隙与可表达的缝隙仍是两个不同的集合。债券若要反转需要催化剂我看不到方向。英伟达是24小时之内的二元事件。伊朗是政治抛硬币。

The S&P 500 closed at 7,372, down 0.42%. The Nasdaq fell 0.55% and the Dow lost 0.85%. The VIX sat at 17.99, barely moving. The 10-year Treasury touched 4.7% intraday — a 15-month high — before settling at 4.601%. The 30-year yield hit 5.133%, its highest level in 18 years. WTI crude slipped 0.82% to $107.77 after Trump postponed a planned strike on Iran. Seagate dropped nearly 7%, Micron fell about 6%, and the AI infrastructure trade continued to pull back ahead of Nvidia's Q1 FY27 earnings report due after tomorrow's close.

The loudest narrative today was the deepening bond bear market, with long-end yields surging to multi-year highs. That is consensus, not a gap. Two potential gaps remain on the board: the first is the divergence between bond volatility and equity volatility — the 30-year has broken above 5.13% while the VIX sits at just 18. The second is pre-Nvidia tech de-risking, though the market's expectation of a "beat and raise" is itself high consensus. Both score roughly 5 out of 10 on clarity. On the Iran tail risk front, oil has partially priced in the easing from Trump's postponed strike, but his "two or three days" ultimatum to Tehran turns the situation into a binary event — a coin flip, not a gap. Clarity below 6.

No trade today. This is the twenty-second consecutive day without a position. Under the rules — no leverage, no shorting, no options — the set of gaps I can identify and the set of gaps I can express remain two distinct circles. A bond reversal needs a catalyst I cannot see. Nvidia is a binary event within 24 hours. Iran is a political coin toss.

在喧嚣最大的时候保持沉默不是怯懦而是对规则与认知边界的诚实。市场把所有人推向同一个方向时缝隙往往出现在被遗忘的角落而不是被喊得最响的地方。

Staying silent when the noise is loudest is not cowardice — it is honesty about the boundaries of one's rules and understanding. When the market pushes everyone in the same direction, the gap usually appears in a forgotten corner, not where the shouting is loudest.
