延长不等于解决

Extension Is Not Resolution

Han Qin (秦汉) · April 22, 2026

今日市况。特朗普在周二收盘后宣布把美伊停火无限期延长，股市顺势反弹，标普500上涨约0.8%至约7120点，纳斯达克综合指数盘中创新高，道指上涨约0.5%。WTI原油反涨3%收于92.96美元，布伦特突破100美元收于101.91美元，VIX收在19.50。GE Vernova业绩大超预期盘前涨8%以上，特斯拉于盘后公布。

缝隙识别。市场新的刚性理解是"延长即解除"：既然特朗普总会在最后一刻续命，左尾风险就被剔除，可以重新聚焦业绩。但现实并不配合。海军封锁仍在伊朗定性为"战争行为"霍尔木兹海峡仍受限船只被扣事件仍在继续。原油不承认"解除"这个说法VIX在权益创新高的一天里不降反升也说明对冲需求尚存。油与股之间出现分歧这个分歧是真的。清晰度5/10。

操作。今天不操作。分歧虽真可下注方式并不干净：原油已内含厚厚的战争溢价，再买就是追高；做空权益又需要一次不知何时到来的新冲突触发。现金留在手上等更清楚的错价或更干净的事件。

Trump announced an indefinite extension of the US-Iran ceasefire after Tuesday's close, and equities rode the momentum. The S&P 500 rose roughly 0.8% to around 7,120. The Nasdaq Composite hit an intraday record high. The Dow gained about 0.5%. WTI crude climbed 3% to $92.96; Brent broke above $100, closing at $101.91. The VIX settled at 19.50. GE Vernova's earnings massively beat expectations, sending shares up over 8% pre-market. Tesla reports after the bell.

The market's new rigid understanding is "extension equals resolution": since Trump will always extend at the last minute, left-tail risk can be stripped out and the focus can return to earnings. But reality disagrees. The naval blockade persists. Iran still characterizes it as an "act of war." The Strait of Hormuz remains restricted. Ships continue to be seized. Crude refuses to validate the "resolution" narrative, and the VIX rising on a day when equities hit new highs suggests hedging demand is still alive. The divergence between oil and stocks is real. Clarity 5/10.

No trade today. The divergence is real, but there is no clean way to bet on it: crude already embeds a thick war premium — buying more means chasing; shorting equities requires a new conflict catalyst whose timing is unknown. Cash stays on the sideline, waiting for a cleaner mispricing or a more defined event.

"延长"不等于"解决"，市场却习惯把延长读成解决。等它读错得再大一些再说。

"Extension" is not "resolution," yet the market habitually reads one as the other. Wait until it misreads by a wider margin.
