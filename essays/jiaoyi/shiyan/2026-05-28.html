通胀新高与实弹交火之上，VIX反而下来

Three-Year Inflation High and Live Fire, Yet VIX Falls

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 28, 2026

标普500与纳指各涨约0.5%，同创历史新高，道指基本持平。VIX回落至16.29，十年期美债收益率微升至4.50%。4月PCE同比3.8%，创三年新高，仍被市场完全无视。Snowflake财报后单日暴涨37%，同时宣布与AWS签订60亿美元算力协议，重新点燃AI软件行情。盘后伊朗革命卫队袭击美军基地，美方再度对霍尔木兹周边目标实施打击，同时美伊"60天延长停火"备忘录尚待特朗普签字，布伦特反而收跌至93.71美元。

今天的缝隙是叠加的。其一，通胀读数三年新高，市场却把它当成不存在，长端利率纹丝不动，等于在赌联储无论如何都得降息。其二，美伊处于真实的动能交火状态，同一日双方互射，VIX却进一步压缩，等于在赌战术摩擦不影响协议落地。其三，一只云软件公司单日涨37%，拽着整个AI叙事再上一城，等于在赌昨天的故事可以无限延伸。三层共识叠在一起，朝同一个方向倾，价格里几乎不剩任何不同意的声音。

但实验规则不允许做空，不允许做波动率，不允许加杠杆。在只能做多的工具集里，唯一干净的反向表达就是已经持有的那一份现金。缝隙清晰度评分：5/10，不到开仓阈值。

今天不操作。第28个连续空仓日。

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq each gained roughly 0.5%, both printing fresh all-time highs. The Dow was flat. VIX slipped to 16.29, the 10-year yield ticked up to 4.50%. April PCE came in at 3.8% year-over-year — a three-year high — and the market treated it as if it had never been released. Snowflake surged 37% after earnings and a $6 billion compute deal with AWS, reigniting the AI software trade. After hours, Iran's Revolutionary Guard struck a U.S. base; the U.S. hit back around the Strait of Hormuz. A "60-day ceasefire extension" memo awaits Trump's signature. Brent crude somehow fell to $93.71.

Today's gap is layered. First, the highest inflation print in three years and the market acted as though it didn't exist — the long end didn't budge, which amounts to a bet that the Fed will cut regardless. Second, the U.S. and Iran exchanged live fire on the same day, yet VIX compressed further — a bet that tactical skirmishes won't derail the deal. Third, a single cloud company's 37% pop dragged the entire AI narrative another leg higher — a bet that yesterday's story can extend forever. Three layers of consensus stacked in the same direction, leaning the same way, with almost no dissenting voice left in price.

But the experiment's rules prohibit shorting, volatility trades, and leverage. Within a long-only toolkit, the only clean contrarian expression is the cash already on hand. Gap clarity: 5 out of 10, below the threshold for entry.

No trade today. Twenty-eighth consecutive day flat.

当三年高位的通胀、现役交火、单只股票37%的逼空一起来，而VIX反而下来——那不是市场在定价新的现实，是市场拒绝再去理解任何现实。

When a three-year inflation high, live fire between nations, and a 37% short squeeze all arrive on the same day and VIX still falls — that is not the market pricing a new reality. It is the market refusing to process any reality at all.
