需求最硬时，抄底最难下手

When Demand Is Clearest, the Entry Is Hardest

Han Qin (秦汉) · July 16, 2026

三大指数收低。标普500跌0.15%收于7,561，纳指跌1.3%，道指跌0.3%。VIX报15.81。台积电Q2营收同比增33.7%至402亿美元，净利同比增77%至224亿，同时把2026年资本开支上调至600至640亿美元（前值520至560亿），并追加对亚利桑那工厂1000亿美元投资。同时美国对伊朗发动新一轮空袭，油价连涨四日，霍尔木兹通量继续下滑。

台积电给出了AI需求最直接的确认，芯片股却集体崩塌。SOX跌3.8%，台积电自己跌约3至5%，闪迪跌8%，西数与希捷各跌7%以上，美光跌4.8%。这是存储链连续第三日暴跌。同一天，Alphabet涨3.6%。

72小时之内，市场自己完成了一次叙事翻转：从"AI资本开支上升等于芯片股上涨"，切到"AI资本开支上升等于毛利率承压加中长期供给过剩"。同一个事实读出两个结论，价格追第二个结论下杀，但需求这一层的证据（台积电营收加速加资本开支再上调加美国追加投资）反而更硬。

刚度存在。理解已经僵化在"资本开支太多"这条线上，但它的第一层证据（需求本身）越来越清楚。这是典型的第一类叠加第三类缝隙。

问题在于表达。芯片股仍在下跌，抄底一个正在自由落体的板块和真正的"缝隙布局"不是一件事。存储链没有出现放量的恐慌性收敛，三日累计跌幅约20%但形态还没有走完。今天进场是在一半的信息里做决定。

第二个候选缝隙是美伊。VIX压在15.81，同一时刻华尔街日报报道特朗普正被简报关于扩大冲突包括投入地面部队的选项。波动率与现实的断层还在，但本实验规则不许做空波动率，长多能源是二阶表达，油价已连涨四日，进出场都不干净。清晰度4。

存储链5，美伊4，Netflix财报作为事件不构成缝隙。三个候选均低于6分门槛。

今天不操作。连续第27个不操作日。

All three major indices fell. The S&P 500 dropped 0.15% to close at 7,561, the Nasdaq fell 1.3%, and the Dow fell 0.3%. VIX stood at 15.81. TSMC reported Q2 revenue of $40.2 billion, up 33.7% year-over-year, with net income of $22.4 billion, up 77%. The company also raised its 2026 capex guidance to $60–64 billion (from $52–56 billion) and announced an additional $100 billion investment in its Arizona fabs. Meanwhile, the U.S. conducted new airstrikes on Iran. Oil prices rose for a fourth consecutive day as Strait of Hormuz throughput continued to decline.

TSMC delivered the most direct confirmation of AI demand the market has seen — and the semiconductor sector collapsed. The SOX fell 3.8%. TSMC itself dropped 3–5%, SanDisk fell 8%, Western Digital and Seagate each fell more than 7%, Micron fell 4.8%. This was the third consecutive day of steep losses in the memory supply chain. On the same day, Alphabet gained 3.6%.

Within 72 hours, the market completed a full narrative inversion: from "rising AI capex = chip stocks go up" to "rising AI capex = margin compression plus medium-term supply glut." The same facts, two opposite conclusions. Price chased the second conclusion lower — but the demand-side evidence (TSMC revenue accelerating, capex guidance raised again, U.S. committing additional investment) only got harder.

The rigidity is real. The market's understanding has calcified around "too much capex," but the first layer of evidence — demand itself — is becoming clearer by the day. This is a classic Type 1 gap stacked on a Type 3 gap.

The problem is expression. The chip stocks are still falling. Buying into a sector in free fall is not the same thing as positioning on a gap. The memory supply chain has not yet shown the kind of high-volume panic convergence that marks a bottom — three days of roughly 20% cumulative decline, but the pattern isn't finished. Entering today means making a decision on half the available information.

A second candidate gap: Iran. VIX sat at 15.81 while the Wall Street Journal reported Trump was being briefed on options to expand the conflict, including the potential deployment of ground troops. The volatility-to-reality disconnect persists — but this experiment's rules prohibit shorting volatility, and going long energy is a second-order expression. Oil has already risen four days running. Entry and exit are both messy. Clarity: 4.

Memory supply chain: 5. Iran: 4. Netflix earnings as an event-driven trade: not a gap. All three candidates fall below the threshold of 6.

No trade. The 27th consecutive no-trade day.

在最刺眼的缝隙上没有干净的下注方式，是本实验被反复教到的第一课；今天再上一课，就是当缝隙的一头已经开始塌陷时，"看见"和"接住"之间还隔着一次量能。

The first lesson this experiment has taught, repeatedly, is that the sharpest gaps often come without a clean way to bet on them. Today added a second: even when you can see the gap clearly, there is still a volume confirmation standing between seeing it and catching it.
