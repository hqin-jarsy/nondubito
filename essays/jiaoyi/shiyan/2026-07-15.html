叙事皆自洽，出手时未到

Two Self-Consistent Stories, Neither Clear Enough to Trade

Han Qin (秦汉) · July 15, 2026

标普500收7572.41涨0.38%，纳指涨约0.6%再创新高，道指涨0.17%，VIX报16.4继续压低。6月PPI环比降0.3%（预期持平），核心PPI年率降至4.7%，通胀降温延续。苹果因中国批准Apple Intelligence落地大涨4.1%创历史新高，谷歌涨近3%。ASML二次上调2026年营收指引并宣布扩产30%，股价涨2.87%。与此同时美光跌逾8%，SK海力士美股跌超7%，存储链继续崩塌。IBM在昨日重挫基础上再度杀跌。WTI原油在79美元附近。

今天最刺眼的是同一AI硬件供应链内部的撕裂。ASML把设备端指引二次上调并扩产30%，苹果创新高印证终端AI落地，但存储双雄美光和海力士两日累计跌15%以上。市场用一张同一天的价格表说了两句相互矛盾的话：一边"AI资本开支强到设备商都要扩产30%"，一边"存储商完了要过剩了"。

理解的矛盾就是缝隙的位置。但今天我犹豫的原因是，ASML扩产30%这件事，既能被多头解读为"AI需求强到需要扩产"，也能被空头解读为"未来12到24个月商品DRAM必然过剩"。两种叙事都自洽。缝隙从表面看很大，从纵深看却并不干净。清晰度打5分，未到6分门槛。

今天不操作，连续第26个交易日空仓。判断依据：存储链跌势尚有惯性，昨日IBM暴雷今天再挫，说明市场对AI相邻名字任何负面信号都愿意重罚，情绪结构不利于左侧接刀。若明日出现企稳K线或情绪投降式放量下跌，会考虑用小仓位建立存储链的启动仓。现在仍是等。

The S&P 500 closed at 7,572.41, up 0.38%. Nasdaq rose roughly 0.6% to a new all-time high. The Dow gained 0.17%. VIX settled at 16.4, continuing to compress. June PPI fell 0.3% month-over-month against expectations of flat, with core PPI year-over-year easing to 4.7% — the disinflation trend continued. Apple surged 4.1% to a record high after China approved the local rollout of Apple Intelligence. Google rose nearly 3%. ASML raised its 2026 revenue guidance for the second time and announced a 30% capacity expansion, lifting shares 2.87%. At the same time, Micron fell more than 8% and SK Hynix's U.S.-listed shares dropped over 7%, extending the memory chain's collapse. IBM fell again following yesterday's steep drop. WTI crude held near $79.

The most striking thing today was the split within the same AI hardware supply chain. ASML raised equipment-side guidance for the second time and announced 30% capacity expansion. Apple set an all-time high, confirming that AI is landing at the consumer endpoint. Yet Micron and SK Hynix combined fell more than 15% over two days. On a single day's price sheet, the market simultaneously delivered two contradictory messages: "AI capex is so strong that equipment makers need to expand capacity by 30%" and "memory is headed for glut."

Where understanding contradicts itself is where the gap lives. But the reason I hesitated today is that ASML's 30% capacity expansion is legible to both sides. Bulls read it as "AI demand is strong enough to justify that investment." Bears read it as "commodity DRAM will almost certainly be oversupplied in 12 to 24 months." Both narratives are internally consistent. The gap looks large on the surface but isn't clean in depth. Clarity score: 5 — below the threshold of 6.

No trade today. The 26th consecutive no-trade day. The memory chain's downward momentum has not exhausted itself, and IBM was punished again today following yesterday's blow-up, confirming that the market remains willing to heavily penalize any AI-adjacent name on any negative signal. That emotional structure doesn't favor buying into ongoing weakness. If tomorrow shows a stabilizing candle or a capitulation-volume selloff, I will consider opening a small starter position in the memory chain. For now, the answer is still: wait.

看到缝隙不等于抓住缝隙。看清缝隙的形状比看到缝隙更重要。今天两种理解各站一半事实，就还不到出手的时候。

Seeing a gap is not the same as having a grip on it. Understanding the shape of the gap matters more than spotting its presence. Today, each interpretation held roughly half the facts — and that means it was not yet time to act.
