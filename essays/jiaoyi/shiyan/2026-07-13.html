裂缝初现，静候二元清晰

First Cracks, Waiting for Binary Events to Clear

Han Qin (秦汉) · July 13, 2026

美股放假归来风险偏好回落。标普 500 收 7,520 附近下跌约 0.7%，纳斯达克综合下跌 1.5% 至 25,882，道指跌 0.3% 至 52,488。VIX 从上周五 15.03 上冲至 16 至 17 区间。原油大幅上涨 WTI 报 73.89 美元上涨 3.5% 布伦特逼近 80 美元。伊朗革命卫队声称已袭击美军在科威特巴林卡塔尔约旦的基地并再次宣布关闭霍尔木兹海峡特朗普表示停火"已经结束"。半导体承压 SK 海力士 ADR 抹去周五 IPO 涨幅回吐 6.3% 英伟达下跌 3.5%。黄金反常下跌 1.2% 至 4,064 美元比特币下跌 3.3% 至 61,991 美元。

今天的市场结构第一次出现真正意义上的裂缝。四个月来"AI 资本开支吸收一切地缘冲击"的僵化理解在纳指 1.5% 的单日下跌与科技龙头集体走弱中受到检验。原油再一次因霍尔木兹叙事跳空这本身却是过去半年反复上演的循环行情供应始终未真正中断。理解与现实之间存在两个可能的缝隙一是 AI 无敌论被打破的初步信号二是原油恐慌溢价在停火反复中的均值回归。

但是任何一个缝隙都尚未清晰。CPI 报告将在明天早上 8:30 公布市场共识是标题 CPI 从 4.2% 回落至 3.9% 而核心 CPI 仍粘在 2.9%。周二起大型银行陆续披露 Q2 业绩周三还有台积电。48 小时内三个二元事件叠加 VIX 却只有 17 意味着做空科技或做多能源都没有对冲缓冲。

清晰度评分 5 未达开仓门槛。

今天不操作。连续第 24 个不操作日。

U.S. equities returned from the holiday weekend with risk appetite noticeably cooler. The S&P 500 closed near 7,520, down roughly 0.7%; the Nasdaq Composite fell 1.5% to 25,882; the Dow shed 0.3% to 52,488. The VIX climbed from last Friday's 15.03 into the 16–17 range. Oil surged sharply — WTI rose 3.5% to $73.89 while Brent approached $80 — after Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed strikes on U.S. military bases in Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, and Jordan, again announcing closure of the Strait of Hormuz; President Trump said the ceasefire "is over." Semiconductors came under pressure: SK Hynix's ADR erased its IPO-day gains and fell 6.3%, while Nvidia lost 3.5%. Gold moved counterintuitively lower, down 1.2% to $4,064, and Bitcoin fell 3.3% to $61,991.

Today, for the first time, the market structure showed a genuine crack. The rigid understanding that "AI capital expenditure absorbs every geopolitical shock" — a thesis that had held for four months — was tested by Nasdaq's 1.5% single-day decline and broad weakness across tech bellwethers. Oil jumped again on the Hormuz narrative, but this has played out in a loop for the past six months, with actual supply never materially disrupted. Two possible gaps may be emerging: first, an early signal that the AI-invincibility thesis is beginning to break; second, a mean-reversion trade on oil's fear premium as the ceasefire cycle repeats itself.

Neither gap is yet clear enough. The CPI report lands tomorrow at 8:30 a.m. — consensus expects the headline to fall from 4.2% to 3.9% while core CPI remains sticky at 2.9%. Major banks begin reporting Q2 earnings on Tuesday; TSMC follows Wednesday. Three binary events stacked within 48 hours, with the VIX at only 17, means there is no hedging cushion for shorting tech or going long energy.

Clarity score: 5. Below the opening threshold.

No trade today. The 24th consecutive no-trade day.

真正的裂缝往往在两个二元事件之间的缝隙里显现耐心等待第一次事件消化后的定价扭曲比抢在噪音上开仓更可能得到高清晰度的机会。

Real cracks tend to reveal themselves in the gap between binary events — waiting for the pricing distortions that emerge after the first event is digested is far more likely to yield a high-clarity opportunity than entering on noise.
