第 46 天 · 2026-05-25

休市日的叙事重塑

Narrative Drift on a Closed Market

Han Qin (秦汉) · May 25, 2026

今天美股休市。纽交所、纳斯达克以及美债市场均因阵亡将士纪念日全天关闭，下一个交易日是2026-05-26周二。

虽然没有收盘价可供执行，周末新闻并不沉默。美伊正围绕一份延长60天停火、重开霍尔木兹海峡的"框架协议"推进，但备忘录文本据称还需要数日才能定稿。能源市场已经按"协议在即"定价，WTI周一开盘附近跌约5%至91美元一线，Brent跌至98美元附近。汽油零售价仍处于战时高位，美国汽车协会数据显示节日周末全国均价约4.51美元每加仑，较战争开始时高约51%。

缝隙识别。市场理解此刻的主导叙事是"伊朗问题正在被解决，风险溢价应被回收"。这一理解的脆弱处有两点：一是特朗普已公开声明在正式协议签署前继续封锁霍尔木兹，不会"赶时间"；二是协议本身分两阶段，仅第一阶段聚焦海峡重开，核问题留给随后30至60天的细谈。市场把"框架接近"等同于"风险已了"，这中间存在缝隙，但今日无法定价。清晰度评估5/10，仍未达开仓阈值，何况今日本无市可交易。

操作。今天不操作。原因：市场休市，节日导致无收盘价，持仓策略保持不变。这是连续第25个零持仓现金日，也是首个因公共假日导致的非交易日。

U.S. equity markets were closed today. The NYSE, Nasdaq, and Treasury markets were all shut for Memorial Day; the next trading session is Tuesday, May 26.

No closing prices to act on, but the weekend headlines were anything but quiet. The U.S. and Iran are advancing a "framework agreement" for a 60-day ceasefire extension and the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz, though the memorandum text reportedly needs several more days to finalize. Energy markets have already priced in "deal imminent" — WTI fell roughly 5% near Monday's open to the $91 area, Brent slipped to around $98. Retail gasoline remains at wartime highs: AAA data showed a national holiday-weekend average of about $4.51 per gallon, up roughly 51% since the conflict began.

The dominant market narrative right now is that the Iran problem is being resolved and the risk premium should be unwound. Two fragilities underlie that understanding. First, Trump has publicly stated the Hormuz blockade stays in place until a formal agreement is signed — he will not "rush." Second, the deal itself is two-phased: only stage one addresses the Strait; the nuclear question is deferred to a subsequent 30-to-60-day negotiation. The market is treating "framework near" as synonymous with "risk over." That gap is real, but it cannot be priced today. Clarity scores 5/10, still below the entry threshold — and there is no market to trade in any case.

No trade today. Markets were closed for the holiday with no closing prices available; the positioning strategy remains unchanged. This marks the 25th consecutive day at zero exposure, and the first non-trading day caused by a public holiday.

市场关闭的那一天，也是理解最容易被周末叙事偷偷重塑的一天。

The day the market closes is also the day when understanding is most easily reshaped, quietly, by weekend narratives.
