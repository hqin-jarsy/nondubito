万亿蒸发，VIX不动

A Trillion Evaporates, VIX Doesn’t Flinch

Han Qin (秦汉) · June 5, 2026

5月非农新增17.2万远超预期8.5万，失业率持稳4.3%，但科技股反向崩塌。纳指收跌3.11%标普500跌2.64%道指跌1.35%，半导体与AI链条两日合计蒸发逾万亿美元市值。WTI回落至约93美元，黄金现货升至约4,521美元，VIX收15.40。

今天最显眼的缝隙在波动率本身。一个标普跌2.64%纳指跌3.11%半导体板块跌6到8个百分点的交易日，VIX仍被压在15.40。强劲就业数据给了市场一个"轮动而非风险"的故事，于是恐慌情绪没有被定价进波动率曲面。这是典型的二类缝隙：波动率压缩没有对应的现实依据。但同一形态我已经连续观察了五十多天，今天只是把它推到了一个更极端的位置。在不允许做空也不允许买入波动率衍生品的实验规则下，干净的表达路径不存在。清晰度5/10。

GLD仓位继续持有。入场依据是黄金对战争与VIX的背离，今天油价回落至93美元，金价小升至约4,521美元，背离在缓慢收敛而非扩张。但更值得关注的是：股市暴跌时金价几乎没动（GLD仅微涨约0.6%），这说明今天的资金没有把黄金当作避险出口。论题仍然成立但已不是新鲜入场点。止损385美元远未触发。

今天不操作。

May nonfarm payrolls came in at 172,000, roughly double the 85,000 consensus. Unemployment held at 4.3%. Yet tech collapsed in the opposite direction: the Nasdaq fell 3.11%, the S&P 500 dropped 2.64%, and the Dow slid 1.35%. Semiconductors and the AI supply chain shed over a trillion dollars in market cap across two sessions. WTI eased to around $93, spot gold edged up to roughly $4,521, and the VIX settled at 15.40.

The most visible gap today sits in volatility itself. On a day when the S&P fell 2.64%, the Nasdaq dropped 3.11%, and semis lost six to eight percentage points, the VIX remained pinned at 15.40. Strong employment data gave the market a “rotation, not risk” narrative, so panic never got priced into the volatility surface. This is a textbook Type 2 gap: vol compression with no basis in reality. But I have been watching the same pattern for over fifty days; today merely pushed it to a more extreme point. Under experiment rules that prohibit shorting and volatility derivatives, there is no clean way to express this view. Clarity 5/10.

Holding the GLD position. The original thesis was gold’s divergence from war risk and the VIX. Today oil pulled back to $93 while gold inched up to around $4,521—the divergence is slowly converging rather than widening. More telling: when equities cratered, gold barely moved (GLD rose only about 0.6%), suggesting today’s flows did not treat gold as a safe haven. The thesis still holds, but this is no longer a fresh entry point. The $385 stop is nowhere near triggered.

No trade today.
