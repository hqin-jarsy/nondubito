市场把"在谈"读成了"将成"

The Market Read "Talks" as "Done"

Han Qin (秦汉) · March 25, 2026

今天是实验的第四天。

美股今天全线上涨。标普500上涨0.54%收6591.90点，纳指上涨0.77%收21929.83点，道指上涨0.66%。VIX从27降至25.33，下跌6%。油价继续回落，布伦特原油约101美元，WTI约90美元。上涨的导火索，是市场对美伊停火谈判的乐观情绪。

今天的矛盾在这里：市场因"停火谈判"大涨，但伊朗在同一天正式拒绝了美国提出的15点停火方案。双方条件相距甚远——美国要求伊朗放弃核浓缩并开放霍尔木兹海峡，伊朗则要求战争赔偿和对霍尔木兹海峡的主权认定。这不是谈判中的分歧，而是根本性的不兼容。

市场的集体理解正在快速转向"和平将至"，而现实是双方尚未踏入同一扇门。

这道缝隙值得注意，但方向尚不清晰：如果油价继续下跌，XLE面临压力；如果伊朗强硬立场主导后续进展，油价反弹，市场承压。两种方向的力量都在，缝隙清晰度5分，低于开仓门槛。

今天不操作。现有三个持仓（SPY、XLE、QQQ）论点均未失效。XLE的止损条件是双边正式停火协议——今天伊朗拒绝方案，这反而强化了原有论点。剩余现金2640美元，占总资金约26%，保持观望。

Day four of the experiment.

US equities rallied broadly today. The S&P 500 rose 0.54% to close at 6,591.90; the Nasdaq gained 0.77% to 21,929.83; the Dow was up 0.66%. The VIX fell 6% from 27 to 25.33. Oil prices continued to retreat — Brent crude around $101, WTI around $90. The catalyst: market optimism about US-Iran ceasefire talks.

Here is the contradiction. Markets surged on "ceasefire talks" on the same day that Iran officially rejected the US-proposed 15-point ceasefire framework. The two sides' conditions are not just far apart — they are fundamentally incompatible. The US demands Iran abandon nuclear enrichment and open the Strait of Hormuz; Iran demands war reparations and sovereign recognition over the Strait. This is not a negotiating gap. It is a fundamental incompatibility.

The market's collective understanding is rapidly shifting toward "peace is coming," while the reality is that neither side has yet stepped through the same door.

This gap is worth watching, but the direction isn't clear enough to act: if oil keeps falling, XLE faces pressure; if Iran's hard stance dominates subsequent developments and oil rebounds, markets face headwinds. Forces pointing in both directions are present. Gap clarity: 5 out of 10— below the entry threshold.

No trades today. The thesis behind all three existing positions (SPY, XLE, QQQ) remains intact. XLE's stop-loss condition is a formal bilateral ceasefire agreement — Iran's rejection of the proposal actually reinforces the original thesis. Remaining cash: $2,640, approximately 26% of total capital. Staying in observation mode.

The easiest mistake a market can make is to read "in talks" as "done."
