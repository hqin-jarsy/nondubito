Power Theory · SAE · 势
权力论 · SAE · 势

权力不是恶，也不是德——它是结构。这个系列从一个不寻常的地方出发：不问权力对不对，问权力是什么。当两个相互承认彼此主体性的人处在不对等的位置上，会涌现出什么？这个不对等从哪里来，以什么形态运作，遇到什么限度，走向哪两条截然不同的路，又如何消解？

这七篇散文改写自SAE权力论学术系列（Prequel至Paper 7），保留原有论证的结构骨架，以散文的方式重新展开。系列标识符"Shi（势）"取自韩非子：势是位置带来的力量，是结构性的而非个人属性。它与道德律系列的标识符"道"形成对仗——道是对称主体间的共有地形，势是非对称的力场。

Power is neither evil nor virtue — it is structure. This series begins somewhere unusual: not asking whether power is justified, but asking what power is. When two people who recognize each other as subjects occupy unequal positions, what emerges? Where does that asymmetry come from, what shapes does it take, where does it hit its limits, what two very different paths can it follow, and how does it dissolve?

These seven essays are prose rewrites of the SAE Power Theory academic series (Prequel through Paper 7), preserving the logical structure of the original arguments while unfolding them in essay form. The series identifier "Shi (势)" is taken from Han Feizi: 势 is the power conferred by position — structural, not personal. It pairs with the Moral Law series identifier "Dao (道)": Dao is the shared terrain of symmetric subjects; 势 is the asymmetric force field.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026
  • 第一篇 · 前置 Essay I · Prequel
    权力不是什么
    What Power Is Not
    进入正面理论之前，先做清场。同意、暴力、资本、合法性、制度、生物性支配——六项常见的错位指认，逐一排除。
    Before building a positive theory, clear the ground. Six common mis-identifications of power — consent, violence, capital, legitimacy, institution, biological dominance — ruled out one by one.
  • 第二篇 Essay II
    权力从哪里来
    Where Power Comes From
    权力的起源点是相互主体性承认。从这里生长出三个层次：保护-响应的力学层、叙事-意义的符号层、声誉-承认层。以及权力内在的不稳定性。
    Power originates in mutual subject-recognition. From there it grows three layers: the mechanical protection-response layer, the narrative-significance layer, and the reputation-recognition layer. Plus power's inherent internal instability.
  • 第三篇 Essay III
    权力的形态与运作
    The Shape and Operation of Power
    权力可以集中也可以分散，可以堆叠也可以平铺。关键节点原则。权力相遇中的三种位置：施权方、承权方、见证者。承权方不是被动的。
    Power can concentrate or disperse, stack or flatten. The key-node principle. Three positions in every power encounter: exercising party, subjected party, witness. The subjected party is not passive.
  • 第四篇 Essay IV
    权力的限度
    The Limits of Power
    每一个权力关系都产生它无法完全消化的余项。权力能强制行为，但不能强制内心。极致的控制尝试制造更多余项。余项会累积。
    Every power relation produces a remainder it cannot fully absorb. Power can compel behavior but not inner conviction. The attempt at total control generates more remainder. Remainders accumulate.
  • 第五篇 Essay V
    涵育与凿削
    Cultivation and Severing
    每一个权力关系都站在一个岔路口：涵育还是凿削。涵育走向自身的解消，凿削是自我消耗的结构。权力与权利的结构性对偶。
    Every power relation faces a fork: cultivation or severing. Cultivation trends toward its own dissolution. Severing is a self-consuming structure. The structural duality of power and right.
  • 第六篇 Essay VI
    权力如何消解
    How Power Dissolves
    权力关系终将消解。两种终点：完成性消解（涵育成功，位差自然归零）与崩溃性消解（凿削结构内爆）。抵抗是什么，它和消解的关系。
    Every power relation eventually dissolves. Two kinds of ending: dissolution by completion (cultivation succeeds, positional gap closes) and dissolution by breakdown (severing structure implodes). What resistance is, and its relation to dissolution.
  • 第七篇 · 元篇 Essay VII · Meta
    这台机器是什么
    What This Machine Is
    七篇写完之后的回望。这个权力论是什么类型的工具——它能做什么，不能做什么。与政治哲学、社会学、马克思主义传统的对位。势与道的对仗。
    A meta-reflection written after the other six. What kind of tool is this theory — what it can diagnose, predict, and situate, and what it cannot. Dialogue with political philosophy, sociology, Marxism. The pairing of 势 and 道.
