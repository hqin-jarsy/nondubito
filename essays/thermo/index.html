非平衡的结构

The Structure of Non-Equilibrium

涨落-耗散定理在近平衡处优美，但当代实验早已越过了它的射程。非平衡FDT的一般形式是近五十年的开放问题。ZFCρ热力学系列从一个意外方向出发：零参数的纯算术递推，没有粒子、没有温度，却满足精确的涨落-耗散关系。

The fluctuation-dissipation theorem is elegant near equilibrium, but contemporary experiment crossed its limits long ago. The general form of the non-equilibrium FDT has been open for fifty years. The ZFCρ thermodynamics series approaches from an unexpected direction: a zero-parameter arithmetic recursion with no particles and no temperature, yet satisfying a precise fluctuation-dissipation relation.

从η（涨落吸收率）到Tsallis q的插值族身份，再到连续时间振荡器中的通道归一化卷绕比：这是一条从算术到化学振荡的结构性推导链。每一步既是正结果，也是对开放余项的诚实标注。

From η (the fluctuation absorption rate) to the exact interpolation family identity of Tsallis q, to the channel-normalized winding ratio in continuous-time oscillators: a structural derivation chain from arithmetic to chemical oscillation. Each step is both a positive result and an honest annotation of what remains open.
