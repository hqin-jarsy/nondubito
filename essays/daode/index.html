道德律

The Moral Law

The Moral Law · SAE
道德律 · SAE

道德律系列从一个不寻常的地方出发：不问善恶，问结构。当多个真正意义上的自我立法主体共处一个共同体时，他们之间会涌现出什么样的形态？这个问题的答案不是一套从外部施加的规则，而是一组构成性条件——你在这个模式下运作，就必然如此。

这八篇散文改写自SAE道德律学术系列（ML·0至ML·9），保留原有论证的逻辑骨架，以散文的方式重新展开。每篇结尾附有学术原文的链接。全系列现有中、英、日、法、德、西、韩七种语言版本；各语言均按自身读者的思想语感独立重写，中文另提供同页简体与繁体阅读模式。

The Moral Law series begins somewhere unusual: not from good and evil, but from structure. When multiple genuine self-legislating subjects share a community, what form naturally emerges among them? The answer is not a set of rules imposed from outside, but a set of constitutive conditions — operate within this mode, and these conditions necessarily hold.

These eight essays are prose rewrites of the SAE Moral Law academic series (ML·0 through ML·9), preserving the logical structure of the original arguments while unfolding them in essay form. Each essay links to the academic original. The full series is available in Chinese, English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, and Korean; each edition is independently written for its readers, with Simplified and Traditional reading modes for Chinese.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · 八篇 · 8 Essays · Seven Languages · Chinese S/T
  • 第一篇 · 序 Essay I · Preface
    立法主体之道
    The Way of the Legislative Subject
    道德律不从善恶出发，而从结构出发。15DD不是更好的人，而是运算配置不同的主体。大同有余项。
    The moral law begins not from good and evil but from structure. 15DD is not a better person — a person differently configured. Great convergence with remainder.
  • 第二篇 Essay II
    四条道德律
    The Four Theorems
    四条"不能不"。识认是克制，不是给予。构成性条件而非规范性要求。与康德的根本分歧。
    Four cannot-nots. Recognition as restraint, not gift. Constitutive conditions, not normative requirements. The fundamental divergence from Kant.
  • 第三篇 Essay III
    内外之镜
    The Mirror, Inside and Out
    主体反转：公平首先是你对自己是否公平。声誉是共同体持有的关于你的状态，不是你的资产。Goodhart定律的结构性根源。
    Subject inversion: fairness toward oneself first. Reputation is the state the community holds about you, not an asset you own. The structural root of Goodhart's Law.
  • 第四篇 Essay IV
    共凿正和
    Mutual Chiseling, Positive-Sum
    道德庭：原告是加害方。主体性是活动而非资源。物自体不可穷尽。互凿在本体论上是正和的。
    The moral court: the perpetrator is the plaintiff. Subjectivity as activity, not resource. The thing-in-itself is inexhaustible. Mutual chiseling is ontologically positive-sum.
  • 第五篇 Essay V
    混合现实中的人
    The Person in Mixed Reality
    真实共同体始终是混合的。伪15DD群体的辨识。自我保全、选择性合作、必要时退出。科雅扎克与苏菲·朔尔。
    Real communities are always mixed. Identifying pseudo-15DD groups. Self-preservation, selective cooperation, strategic exit. Korczak and Sophie Scholl.
  • 第六篇 Essay VI
    共同体的形态
    The Forms of Community
    集体15DD是涌现属性。利尚邦村：怎么能拒绝他们呢？涵育与殖民的对立。宏观等待，微观立即行动。
    Collective 15DD as emergent property. Le Chambon: "How could we refuse them?" Cultivation vs. colonization. Macroscopic waiting; microscopic immediate action.
  • 第七篇 Essay VII
    看不见的架构
    The Invisible Architecture
    特罗克梅被捕，村庄的庇护没有停。机构架构不是设计出来的。道德庭与14DD法庭的路由，三层识别，聚合见证。没有完美机构。
    Trocmé was arrested; the sheltering did not stop. Institutional architecture cannot be designed. Routing between moral court and 14DD court, three-layer identification, aggregate witnessing. No perfect institution.
  • 第八篇 · 尾声 Essay VIII · Coda
    大同有余项
    Great Convergence, With Remainder
    与道家、佛教、康德、Levinas、Buber、Ubuntu的跨文明对话。不批判，只阐述异同。否定之路。系列在此收尾。
    Cross-civilizational dialogue with Daoism, Buddhism, Kant, Levinas, Buber, Ubuntu. No critique, only structural similarity and difference. Via negativa. The series closes here.
