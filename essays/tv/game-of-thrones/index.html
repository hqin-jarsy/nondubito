Game of Thrones: The Stories Power Tells

《权力的游戏》：权力讲述的故事

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文重写 · 简体 · 繁体

Ten essays on a chair that borrows its force from belief, identities taken and recovered, institutions that outlive their purpose, liberation that demands gratitude—and a finale that broke the habits the series itself had taught its audience.

Game of Thrones begins by teaching its audience not to mistake moral importance for plot armor. Once Ned Stark dies, inherited storytelling rules no longer tell us who will survive. The same suspicion should be brought to the political rules inside the story: a throne, a surname, an oath, and a title work because people continue to act as though they settle questions that remain unsettled.

The ten essays follow characters who are named by others, institutions that protect their procedures after forgetting their purpose, and rulers who confuse acting for people with receiving their recognition. The final essay turns that method back on the series itself. Full-series spoilers throughout.

I
Independent English · 简 / 繁 · TV criticism
The Iron Throne: A chair that promises an end to being questioned

The throne commands no army and grows no food. Its force lies in a shared belief that sitting there can settle what power must continually renegotiate.

Independent English · 简 / 繁 · TV criticism
The Ending: A series broke the habit it had taught its audience

For seven seasons the show trained viewers to test every claim and tolerate uncertainty. Its compressed finale supplied the verdict itself—and changed the relationship at the point of farewell.

十篇从一把借信念生效的椅子，读到被夺走又拿回的名字、活得比目的更久的机构、要求感恩的解放，以及一场违背了剧集亲手培养之观看习惯的结局。

《权力的游戏》先教观众不要把一个人物在道德上的重要性误当成不会死的保证。奈德·史塔克死后，继承来的叙事规则不再能替我们判断谁会活下来。同一份警惕也应当用在故事内部的政治规则上：王座、姓氏、誓言与头衔之所以有效，是因为人们继续把尚未解决的问题当成已经解决。

这十篇追踪被他人命名的人、在目的消失后仍守住程序的机构，以及把“替人做事”和“获得承认”混在一起的统治者。最后一篇再把这套读法转回剧集自身。含全剧剧透。
