Six Feet Under: What Remains in a Life

《六尺之下》：被问到时，手上有什么

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文重写 · 简体 · 繁体

Nine essays on the death before every title sequence, care sold as a service, the parent each child invents, lives that begin late or remain damaged—and a finale that gives everyone an ending.

Every episode begins by turning an ordinary moment into a name and two dates. The Fisher family then receives the body while continuing lives that remain stubbornly unfinished. Death is not a moral conclusion here. It is the question no character can schedule.

These essays ask what can be made before that question arrives: work no invoice captures, an inner parental voice that changes, a life begun after fifty, a self reclaimed from a case study, or sixty years of photographs the series never shows. Full-series spoilers throughout.

I
Independent English · 简 / 繁 · TV criticism
A Death Before Every Opening: Not an ending, but a question

The series gives each stranger ninety seconds of life before a name and two dates. Death interrupts rather than summarizes, turning attention back toward today.

Fisher & Sons provides real care and sells coffins in the same room. The tension is not hypocrisy but the problem of sustaining work whose most valuable hours cannot be billed.

Everyone’s Waiting: What they have when the question arrives

The final montage gives each character a death and dates. Its subject is not how they die but what their ordinary years have placed in their hands.

九篇从每集片头前的一次死亡与殡仪馆的两种价格，读到三个孩子各自造出的父亲，以及那些开始得晚、没有被修好，却仍来得及留下东西的人生。

每一集都先把一个普通时刻变成姓名与两个日期，然后把遗体送进菲舍尔家的地下室，而楼上那些人的生活仍顽固地没有完成。死亡在这里不是道德结论，而是任何人都无法安排何时到来的追问。

九篇关心的是追问抵达以前，人究竟能做出什么：不出现在账单上的照顾，能够改变内容的内在父母，五十岁以后才开始的生活，从案例里拿回来的自我，以及剧集一秒未拍却留下整面照片墙的六十年。全剧剧透。
