Sex and the City: Learning What Is Yours

《欲望都市》：分辨什么真正属于自己

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文重写 · 简体 · 繁体

Ten essays on borrowed dating rules, four distinct forms of independence, love without recognition, the weekly table that outlasts every romance—and leaving a good relationship when the self inside it is disappearing.

The series begins with Carrie trying to apply somebody else’s rule to herself: have sex like a man, leave, and feel nothing. The action can be copied; the person cannot. Across six seasons, four friends keep testing what they have been told to want against the selves those arrangements produce.

These essays do not turn romance into a search for the correct partner. Their longest relationship is the table where different judgments can remain different, memory can challenge self-serving stories, and absence is noticed. Full-series and film spoilers throughout.

I Love Me More: Four stories moving in one direction

十篇从一套借来的约会规则与四种不同的独立，读到爱与承认的距离、比所有恋情更长久的早午餐桌，以及一个人为何可以离开没有做错事的爱人。

剧集从凯莉把别人的规则装到自己身上开始：像男人一样上床、离开、不动感情。动作可以复制，人却不能。六季里，四个朋友不断拿别人告诉她们应该想要的东西，去对照那些安排正在把自己变成谁。

这十篇不把爱情写成寻找“正确对象”的题目。全剧最长的关系其实是一张桌子：不同意见可以不被合并，朋友记得的历史可以纠正自我叙述，而任何人的缺席都会被发现。含六季与两部电影剧透。
