Mad Men: The Self Behind the Pitch

《广告狂人》：提案背后的那个人

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文重写 · 简体 · 繁体

Ten essays on a borrowed name, an industry that sells reassurance, the women who make and outgrow the office, two kinds of unearned position—and a true embrace that may also become an ad.

Don Draper can identify the desire inside a product in seconds. What he cannot do is keep a truth without converting it into a presentation. These essays follow the distance between a brilliant public self and the person who borrowed the name beneath it.

The series is equally attentive to people whose positions are assigned differently: Peggy builds authority through work, Joan converts indispensable labor into ownership only to discover that ownership can be repriced, Betty searches for one room of her own, and Sally learns that witnessing is not the same as consenting to an adult’s version. Full-series spoilers throughout.

十篇从一个借来的名字与制造欲望的行业，读到佩吉、琼、贝蒂与萨莉，再到加州、赫尔希，以及最后那个既可能是真实领悟、也可能已成为广告创意的微笑。

唐·德雷珀能在几十秒里认出一件商品内部最可用的欲望，却没有办法让一样真实的东西只停留在自己手里；它总会被加工成提案。这十篇追踪的，是那个极其成功的公共自我，与借来名字之下的人之间的距离。

剧集也把不同的人放在不同的入口上：佩吉用作品逐步取得位置，琼把不可替代的劳动换成股份后才发现股份仍可被别人重新定价，贝蒂寻找一个真正属于自己的房间，萨莉则学会目击并不等于同意大人的版本。全剧剧透。
