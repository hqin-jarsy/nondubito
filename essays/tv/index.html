电视剧解读

Aug 31, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Seven series · Sixty-five essays · Multiple languages七个系列 · 六十五篇 · 多语言阅读

Television unfolds across episodes and seasons, giving characters, relationships, and institutions time to reveal their structure. These readings move beyond plot summary to ask how people are shaped, recognized, and changed.

电视剧以季与集展开，让人物、关系与制度在更长时间中显露结构。这里不做剧情复述，而是追踪人怎样被世界塑造、被彼此识认，又怎样改变自己的目的。
