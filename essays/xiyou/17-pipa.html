Journey to the West: A New Reading (XVII)

琵琶洞

The Pipa Demon's Cave

Han Qin (秦汉) · March 2026

这一难深了。

前面的难，不管多凶险，有一个默认的前提：系统罩得住。天庭也好，佛家也好，观音也好，总有人能兜底。妖怪再厉害，搬个救兵就解决了。

琵琶洞这一难，这个前提碎了。

故事

蝎子精，占了琵琶洞，要跟唐僧成亲。

唐僧又遇到了女人要嫁他的局面。但这次跟女儿国完全不同。女儿国国王是真心的，是人，是可以理解的。蝎子精不是。她是强取。她是妖。而且她不是一般的妖。

悟空来打。打不过。蝎子精有一根毒刺，蜇一下就完。悟空吃了亏。八戒来帮忙，也吃了亏。

搬救兵？找谁？

观音？观音说我也搞不定。

为什么？

因为蝎子精蜇过如来。

连如来都被蜇过

这句话要停下来想一想。

如来。整部西游记里最高的存在。那只手掌。翻了十万八千里从没离开过的那只手掌。

蝎子精蜇了他。

原著里说，如来在雷音寺讲经，蝎子精蜇了他一下，如来疼了，让金刚去捉她，她跑了。

如来也疼。

如来也有他凿不动的东西。

这意味着什么？意味着蝎子精不是普通的余项。她不是某一个人的余项，不是悟空的，不是唐僧的，不是某个菩萨的。她是天庭和灵山加在一起的余项。是系统本身的余项。

整个系统——天庭加佛家加取经路上所有的力量——在她面前都不完全好使。不是打不过的问题。是她的存在本身就在系统的边界之外。

一个超级余项。

取经团队怎么可能兑付

取经团队是系统里面的人。唐僧是佛家安排的取经人。悟空是被如来压过的猴子。八戒沙僧是天庭贬下来的。他们都在系统内部。

一群系统内部的人，怎么可能对付得了系统本身的余项？

用能力？悟空打不过。搬救兵？观音说搞不定。找如来？如来自己都被蜇过。

系统内部的所有资源都不够用。这不是"再找一个更强的"的问题。是这个问题本身超出了系统能处理的范围。

一条谁都不认账的路

那怎么办？

观音说：我搞不定。但我知道谁可能知道怎么搞。你去找黎山老姆。

黎山老姆是谁？第十三篇讲四圣试禅心的时候出现过。四个菩萨变成母女试探八戒，谁扮的母亲？黎山老姆。观音扮女儿，文殊扮女儿，普贤扮女儿。黎山老姆扮母亲。

她的辈分有多高？观音在她面前是晚辈。搞不好连如来都要叫她一声前辈。

那黎山老姆管吗？

她不管。

她说：你去找毗蓝婆菩萨。但是别说是我说的。

别说是我说的。这六个字太有意思了。一个辈分高到所有人都敬重的存在，指了一条路，但不署名。不留痕迹。我没参与。你自己去。

毗蓝婆菩萨管吗？

她也不管。她笑了。她说：是那个谁指你来的吧。我知道。你去找我儿子吧。

她也不直接出手。她也知道是谁指的路。大家心照不宣，都不说破。但每个人都把球传到了下一个人手里。

最后到了谁手里？

昴日星官。

余项的余项

昴日星官是谁？

大公鸡。

昴日星官的本相是大公鸡。公鸡克蝎子。天生的。不需要法宝，不需要法力，不需要修行。就是克。公鸡往蝎子面前一站，蝎子就完了。

一个超级余项——蝎子精，连如来都蜇过的存在——被一只公鸡解决了。

这个解法的结构极其重要。

不是更强的力量打赢了蝎子精。不是系统调集了更多的资源。是在系统之外，有一个东西，它天然就是这个超级余项的余项。

余项的余项。

蝎子精是天庭和灵山的余项。昴日星官是蝎子精的余项。谁都有余项。谁都有人能管。连超级余项都有自己的超级余项。

这个结构是无穷的。没有任何一个存在是没有余项的。如来有。蝎子精有。昴日星官也有。

所以谁也别自满。

昴日星官平时干什么

这也是一个有意思的细节。

昴日星官平时不管事。他就在天上待着。二十八星宿之一。不参与取经路上的日常。不打妖怪。不布局。不操心。

但他管一件事。他专管那个超级余项。

因为他就是为这个存在的。公鸡克蝎子。不是他努力修炼了某种对抗蝎子的能力。是他的存在本身就是蝎子的余项。他不需要做什么。他在那就够了。

这就是为什么这条路这么曲折——从观音到黎山老姆到毗蓝婆菩萨到昴日星官。因为你不知道谁是那个余项的余项。你在系统内部找，找不到。你得顺着一条谁都不认账的关系链，一个传一个，传到一个你从来没听说过的角落，才能找到那个天然克制超级余项的存在。

这不是能力的问题。这不是资源的问题。这是缘分。是你走到这一步，碰巧了（或者不是碰巧），有人给你指了一条路，那条路上的每个人都假装自己没参与，最后你找到了那只公鸡。

别说是我说的

这一难跟上一篇平顶山是同构的，但更深一层。

平顶山的结构是：我悟空能搞定。大部分难都是这个层面。能力够用。

琵琶洞往下走了两层。

第一层：我悟空搞不定。打不过。三昧真火扛不住不说，蝎子精的毒刺更是近不了身。

第二层：我搞不定，但我知道谁能搞定。搬救兵。这是取经路上常见的解法。去找观音，去找天庭，总有人能兜底。但这次观音说：我也搞不定。

第三层：我都不知道谁能搞定。谁也不知道谁能搞定。

到了第三层，没有答案了。没有人知道怎么解决这个超级余项。

但有人愿意帮一把。

观音不知道谁能搞定，但她知道黎山老姆可能知道点什么。黎山老姆不知道自己指的路对不对，但她觉得毗蓝婆菩萨也许行。毗蓝婆菩萨也不确定，但她让悟空去找她儿子试试。

帮得上帮不上，其实谁也不知道。所以没有人愿意署名。

"别说是我说的"不是谦虚。是真的不确定自己帮得上。帮上了不是我的功劳。没帮上不是我的责任。随手一帮。帮上了，大家继续走。没帮上，我也没损失什么。

平顶山是看破不说破——确定性下的沉默。知道答案但不说。琵琶洞是帮了不说帮——不确定性下的善意。不知道答案但还是伸了手。

前者需要克制。后者需要勇气。

回头看那条关系链。

观音：我搞不定，你去找黎山老姆。黎山老姆：你去找毗蓝婆菩萨。别说是我说的。毗蓝婆菩萨：是那个谁让你来的吧，哈哈。去找我儿子。昴日星官：来了就来了。

每个人都不确定。每个人都帮了一步。每个人都不居功。最后那只公鸡也不觉得自己搞定了什么——他就是往蝎子面前一站，蝎子没了。他没有"搞定"。他只是在那。

这里面没有英雄。没有一个人站出来说"这个超级余项是我解决的"。因为没有人解决了它。它是被一条关系链化解的。链条上每个人做了一点点。每个人都不知道自己那一点够不够。每个人都做了。

后面还有

悟空以为琵琶洞是一次性的运气。碰巧了，找到了那只公鸡。

不是。

后面悟空打不过百眼魔君（黄花观那次），还得去找毗蓝婆菩萨。

同一条路。同一个关系链。你以为是运气，其实是结构。你的余项是固定的。你的余项的余项也是固定的。你碰到了你搞不定的东西，你就得回到那条链上去。不是每次都找不同的人。是同一个方向，同一条路，同一种"我搞不定但我知道谁可能搞得定"。

这也是取经。不是所有的经都在灵山上。有些经在你找毗蓝婆菩萨的路上。

Han Qin (秦汉) · @hqinjarsy · 西游新读系列

This difficulty deepens.

The earlier trials, no matter how dangerous, rested on a default assumption: the system will hold. Heaven, the Buddha-realm, Guanyin—someone can always catch you. However ferocious a demon, send for reinforcements and it's solved.

In the Pipa Demon's Cave, this assumption shatters.

The Story

The Scorpion Demon occupies the Pipa Cave, wanting to marry Tang Seng.

Tang Seng faces a woman who wants to marry him again. But this is nothing like the Kingdom of Women. The queen was sincere, human, understandable. The Scorpion Demon is not. She takes by force. She is a demon. Not an ordinary one.

Wukong comes to fight. Cannot win. The Scorpion Demon has a venomous stinger—one jab and it's over. Wukong gets hurt. Bajie comes to help, also gets hurt.

Call for reinforcements? From whom?

Guanyin? Guanyin says: I cannot manage this.

Why?

Because the Scorpion Demon stung Rulai.

Even the Buddha Was Stung

Stop and think about this sentence.

Rulai. The highest existence in all the Journey to the West. That palm. The one from which Wukong never escaped across 108,000 li.

The Scorpion Demon stung him.

The scripture says: Rulai was lecturing at the Thundersound Temple when the Scorpion Demon stung him. Rulai felt pain. He sent protective spirits to capture her. She escaped.

Rulai felt pain.

Rulai too has things he cannot break.

What does this mean? It means the Scorpion Demon is not an ordinary remainder. Not one person's remainder, not Wukong's, not Tang Seng's, not some bodhisattva's. She is the remainder of Heaven and the Buddha-realm combined. The remainder of the system itself.

The entire system—Heaven plus Buddhism plus all the forces on the scripture-seeking path—does not fully function before her. It's not about losing a fight. Her existence itself sits outside the system's boundary.

A super-remainder.

How Could the Team Deliver?

The scripture-seeking team lives inside the system. Tang Seng is Buddhism's appointed pilgrim. Wukong is the monkey pressed by Rulai. Bajie and Sha Seng are Heaven's exiled officers. All inside the system.

How could people from inside the system handle the system's own remainder?

Use ability? Wukong cannot win. Call reinforcements? Guanyin says she cannot manage. Find Rulai? Rulai himself was stung.

Every resource inside the system isn't enough. This is not a problem solved by "finding someone stronger." This problem exceeds what the system can handle.

A Path No One Takes Credit For

So what do they do?

Guanyin says: I cannot manage this. But I know who might know something. Go find the Old Mother of Mount Lishan.

Who is she? She appeared when we discussed the Four Saints Testing the Heart in Article Thirteen. Four bodhisattvas became a family to test Bajie. Who played the mother? The Old Mother of Mount Lishan. Guanyin was a daughter, Manjushri a daughter, Samantabhadra a daughter. The Old Mother was the mother.

How high her rank? Guanyin is her junior. Perhaps even Rulai would call her senior.

Does she take the case?

She does not.

She says: Go find the Bodhisattva Vīrūḍhaka. But don't say I told you.

Don't say I told you. These six words are delicious. A figure of such seniority that everyone respects her—she points a path but leaves no name. No trace. I'm not involved. Go yourself.

Does Vīrūḍhaka take the case?

She does not. She laughed. She said: Someone sent you here, didn't they? I know. Go find my son.

She doesn't act directly either. She too knows who sent you. Everyone understands, says nothing, yet each passes the ball to the next.

To whom does it finally fall?

The Pleiad Star.

The Remainder of the Remainder

Who is the Pleiad Star?

A rooster.

The Pleiad Star's true form is a rooster. Roosters counter scorpions. Naturally. No magic, no power, no cultivation. Just counter. Put a rooster before the scorpion—the scorpion is done.

A super-remainder—the Scorpion Demon, who even stung the Buddha—solved by a rooster.

The structure of this solution is critical.

Not greater force defeating the demon. Not the system mustering more resources. Outside the system, there exists something that is naturally this super-remainder's remainder.

The remainder of the remainder.

The Scorpion Demon is Heaven and Buddha-realm's remainder. The Pleiad Star is the Scorpion Demon's remainder. Everyone has a remainder. Everyone has someone who can manage it. Even a super-remainder has its own super-remainder.

This structure is infinite. No existence has no remainder. Rulai has one. The Scorpion Demon has one. The Pleiad Star has one too.

So let no one be smug.

What Does the Pleiad Star Do Day-to-Day?

Here's an interesting detail.

The Pleiad Star doesn't usually intervene. He simply stays above. One of the Twenty-Eight Mansions. Not involved in the journey's daily affairs. Doesn't fight demons. Doesn't orchestrate. Doesn't worry.

But he manages one thing. He manages that super-remainder.

Because that's why he exists. Roosters counter scorpions. Not that he trained some anti-scorpion ability. His very existence is the scorpion's counter. He needs do nothing. His presence is enough.

This is why the path is so winding—Guanyin to the Old Mother to Vīrūḍhaka to the Pleiad Star. Because you don't know who is the counter to your counter. You search inside the system, you won't find it. You must follow an unacknowledged chain of connections, passed hand to hand, to some corner you've never heard of, until you find the thing that naturally counters your super-remainder.

It's not about ability. It's not about resources. It's providence. You reach this point, by chance (or not by chance), someone points you toward a path. Everyone on that path pretends they weren't involved. Finally you find the rooster.

Don't Say I Told You

This difficulty mirrors the Pingdingshan article, but goes deeper.

Pingdingshan's structure: I, Wukong, can handle it. Most trials are this level. Power suffices.

The Pipa Cave goes down two more levels.

First level: I cannot handle this. Cannot win. The scorpion's stinger is too much—approach is impossible.

Second level: I cannot handle this, but I know who can. Call reinforcements. The journey usually works this way. Find Guanyin, find Heaven, someone will catch you. But this time Guanyin says: I cannot manage.

Third level: I don't know who can handle this. No one knows.

At the third level, there is no answer. No one knows how to address this super-remainder.

But someone will help.

Guanyin doesn't know who can manage it, but she thinks the Old Mother might know something. The Old Mother doesn't know if her suggestion will work, but she thinks Vīrūḍhaka might. Vīrūḍhaka isn't certain, but she tells Wukong to try her son.

Whether it will help, honestly, no one knows. So no one claims credit.

"Don't say I told you" isn't modesty. It's genuine uncertainty about whether you can help. If it works, that's not my achievement. If it doesn't, that's not my failure. Casual assistance. Works out, we continue. Doesn't work, I've lost nothing.

Pingdingshan is see through don't speak through—certainty under silence. You know the answer but don't say. The Pipa Cave is help but don't claim help—uncertainty under kindness. You don't know the answer but still reach out.

The first requires restraint. The second requires courage.

Look back at that chain.

Guanyin: I cannot manage. Go find the Old Mother. Old Mother: Go find Vīrūḍhaka. Don't say I sent you. Vīrūḍhaka: Someone sent you, right? I know. Go find my son. Pleiad Star: You came, you came.

Each is uncertain. Each helps one step. Each takes no credit. Finally the rooster too doesn't think he solved anything—he just stands before the scorpion and it's over. He didn't "solve" it. He was simply there.

No hero here. No one says "I solved this super-remainder." Because no one did. It was dissolved by a chain of connections. Each person in the chain did a little. Each didn't know if their part was enough. Each did it anyway.

There's More

Wukong thought the Pipa Cave was a one-off luck. By chance he found the rooster.

It wasn't.

Later, Wukong cannot defeat the Hundred-Eyed Demon (the Yellow Flower Temple incident) and must again find Vīrūḍhaka.

Same path. Same chain. What seemed luck was structure. Your remainder is fixed. Your remainder's remainder is fixed too. When you meet something you cannot handle, you must return to that chain. Not different people each time. Same direction, same path, same "I cannot handle this but I know who might."

This too is scripture-seeking. Not all scripture sits at the Buddha-realm. Some scripture lies on the road to finding Vīrūḍhaka.
