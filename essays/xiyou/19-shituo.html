Journey to the West: A New Reading (XIX)

狮驼岭

The Lion-Camel Ridge

Han Qin (秦汉) · March 2026

狮驼岭不是难。

狮驼岭是灵山的镜面对称。

不是一般的妖怪窝

取经路上碰到过很多妖怪。占一座山，抓几个人，跟悟空打一场，赢了输了，过去了。

狮驼岭不是这样的。

狮驼岭是一个制度。

三个妖王：青毛狮子精，黄牙老象精，大鹏金翅雕。占了狮驼国。不是占了一座山。是占了一个国家。整个国家的人被杀光了。尸山血海。腥臭难闻。小妖怪们拿人肉当饭吃，拿人皮当被盖。

这不是一个妖怪在作恶。这是一套系统在运转。有组织，有等级，有分工，有规模。灵山度人。狮驼岭杀人。灵山救多少，狮驼岭杀多少。

两套制度。明面上对立。结构上对称。

这三个妖王是谁

青毛狮子精，文殊菩萨的坐骑。之前在乌鸡国出现过。那次是带着任务来的，随便演一演，走个过场。这次不一样。实力全开。

黄牙老象精，普贤菩萨的坐骑。能力不输狮子精。凶狠更胜。

大鹏金翅雕，号称如来的舅舅。基本就是跟如来一个级别的存在。

文殊的坐骑。普贤的坐骑。如来的亲戚。

这三个不是野妖怪。他们是系统内部的人。平顶山那一篇讲过，金银角是太上老君的童子。狮驼岭比平顶山深得多。金银角是两个小童子偷跑下来玩。狮驼岭是三个顶级存在建了一整套跟灵山对称的杀人制度。

而且狮驼岭不在暗处。它就在明处。灵山知道。灵山认可。它是另一套制度，跟灵山并存。

如来说了什么

悟空打不过。搬救兵。最后如来亲自出面。

如来收了大鹏金翅雕。怎么收的？困在佛顶上。给了一个身份：护法。

杀了一国的人。尸山血海。最后拿了编制。

如来说了一句话，大意是：我管顾你，但凡有祭赛的，先叫你吃。

这是什么灵山？

这是你走了十万八千里要去的那个灵山。这是你不疑了一路的那个方向指向的终点。这个终点里，坐着一个会说"先叫你吃"的如来。

悟空崩溃了

悟空在狮驼岭崩溃了。

不是因为打不过。取经路上打不过的时候多了。搬救兵就是了。

悟空崩溃的是：他连师父死了没有都没去验证，就闹着不干了。

这不是"打不过所以不干了"。这是怀疑人生了。

他怀疑的不是自己的能力。他怀疑的是灵山。他怀疑的是他走这条路的全部意义。

你告诉我灵山是好的。你告诉我取经是为了天下苍生。你告诉我这条路走到头就是正果。

然后我看到了什么？文殊的坐骑杀人。普贤的坐骑杀人。如来的舅舅杀了一整个国家的人。如来来了，不是来伸张正义的。是来给杀人的大鹏一个编制的。"先叫你吃。"

悟空闹着不干。他不是在偷懒。他是在质问如来。你到底是干什么的？你的灵山到底是什么东西？我这一路走来，你让我学空，学不打，学在能和做之间留一个间隙。你让我跟着一个手无缚鸡之力的和尚走十万八千里。你让我相信这条路是值得的。

然后你告诉我杀了一国人的妖怪是你舅舅，你还给他安排了工作？

悟空该笑还是该哭。

灵山的镜面

狮驼岭是灵山的镜面对称。

灵山度人。狮驼岭杀人。灵山讲法。狮驼岭吃肉。灵山有秩序。狮驼岭也有秩序——杀人的秩序。灵山有如来坐镇。狮驼岭有大鹏金翅雕坐镇。

两个系统，互为倒影。

而且它们不是对立的。它们是共存的。灵山没有消灭狮驼岭。灵山收编了狮驼岭的首领。大鹏金翅雕成了护法。杀人的变成护法的。制度没有被推翻。制度被吸收了。

这意味着什么？

意味着灵山包含狮驼岭。

你以为灵山是纯净的。你以为走到灵山就没有黑暗了。你以为真经里没有血腥。

狮驼岭告诉你：灵山的佛顶上困着一只杀了一国人的大鹏。灵山的护法里坐着一个吃人的。真经是用尸山血海的代价写出来的。你要的那个光明，背面就是这个黑暗。

你不能只要灵山不要狮驼岭。它们是一个东西的两面。

所有人的物自体

这就是狮驼岭的物自体。不是某一个人的。是所有人的。

你要灵山，你就要接受狮驼岭。你要真经，你就要接受尸山血海。你要如来，你就要接受如来说"先叫你吃"。你要光明，你就要接受光明的代价。

世界上什么都有代价。只要好的余项，不要坏的余项，不可能。

第八篇说过：那个凿不动的东西永远在。你不能消灭它，只能跟它共在。

狮驼岭是这句话最极端的展开。你要跟之共在的不只是你自己的边界。不只是你自己的过去。不只是你自己凿不动的东西。你要跟之共在的是这个世界本身的结构——有灵山就有狮驼岭，有度人就有杀人，有真经就有血。

你接受吗？

另一半

前面那些是一半。悟空质问如来。如来凭什么给杀人的大鹏编制。灵山到底是干什么的。

这一半够锋利。但这一半把责任放在了如来身上。

另一半更难说出口。

如来的慈悲，不只给了大鹏。也给过悟空。

悟空大闹天宫的时候，他是什么？他是一个搅乱整个系统的存在。他踢翻了炼丹炉，搅了蟠桃宴，打遍天兵天将。如来没有杀他。压了五百年，然后给了他一条路。

大鹏杀了一国的人。悟空大闹了整个天庭。如来对两个人做的事情，结构上是一样的。收，不是灭。给一个位置，让你在新的结构里继续存在。

悟空有什么资格质问如来？

再看看取经团队其他人。

沙僧。流沙河。他在那里干什么？吃人。吃了九世取经人。九个唐僧的前身，被他吃了。头骨挂在脖子上当项链。

八戒。高老庄之前呢？天蓬元帅被贬，变成猪。他吃过人吗？吃过。

唐僧。慈悲为怀。从不杀生。但女儿国喝了子母河的水怀了孕，他喝化胎水了吗？喝了。那个孩子呢？

一路上呢？五庄观偷人参果。女儿国骗国王假成亲要通关文牒。答应老鼋问寿命然后背信弃义。

这些都是应该的？

你们团队一路做的这些事，跟大鹏有什么本质区别？程度不同。结构上是一样的。为了自己的目的，伤害了别人，利用了别人，背弃了别人。

大鹏杀了一国的人为了自己的存在。你们团队伤了一路的人为了自己的取经。

谁有资格站在干净的地方指着别人说你脏？

如来不骂悟空

如来不骂悟空。

不是因为如来软弱。不是因为如来理亏。

是因为骂了就变成惩罚了。变成"你错了我对了"。变成一个可以结算的账目。骂完了罚完了，平了，继续走。

如来不骂。悟空才有机会自己悟。

悟到什么？

悟到他在质问的那个东西，他自己身上也有。他质问如来凭什么给大鹏编制。但他自己就是如来给编制的那个人。他质问灵山为什么容忍杀人的存在。但他自己就是被灵山容忍的那个曾经搅乱一切的存在。

如来对大鹏的慈悲，跟如来对悟空的慈悲，是同一种慈悲。慈悲不挑人。它给悟空，也给大鹏。你接受了给你的慈悲，你就要接受它也给别人。包括你觉得不配得到慈悲的人。

这才是真正的物自体。

不是如来的问题。不是大鹏的问题。不是悟空的问题。是每一个人身上都有这个东西。每个人都伤害过别人。每个人都被慈悲覆盖过。每个人都没有资格只要慈悲给自己而不给别人。

谁也没有责任。因为这不是责任的问题。这是结构。

继续走

悟空崩溃了。闹了。质问了。

如来没有解释。如来只是收了大鹏，给了编制，让取经团队继续上路。

然后悟空上路了。

别人不知道他悟了什么。也许他自己也说不清楚。但有些东西在狮驼岭之后不一样了。

他终于明白了如来为什么没有灭了他。

不是因为如来慈悲。不只是因为慈悲。

是因为如来需要他。灵山需要他。

不是因为他善良。是因为他可以善良。

如来看的不是你是什么。是你可以是什么。大鹏杀了一国的人，如来看的不是他杀了人，是他可以不杀人。悟空大闹了天宫，如来看的不是他闹了天宫，是他可以不闹天宫。收编不是奖赏。收编是如来在赌——赌你可以成为另一种东西。

如来替所有人扛了罪孽。收大鹏是扛。收悟空也是扛。"先叫你吃"是扛。每收一个狮驼岭，如来就多背一份黑暗。灵山的每一块砖都是如来背下来的。五百罗汉的过去，三千揭谛的过去，斗战胜佛的过去——全部是如来背的。

这才是大慈大悲。不是不知道黑暗。是知道黑暗，扛下黑暗，把黑暗变成灵山的一块砖。

悟空服了。

不是被打服的。不是被压服的。是他看见了如来在做什么。如来在做的事情比他以为的大得多。如来不是一个坐在灵山上发号施令的佛祖。如来是一个把所有狮驼岭背在身上的人。

悟空没有跟任何人说这些。

这是最深的空。悟了不说。

哭哭笑笑，继续走。他知道灵山是什么了。灵山是一个由无数个被收编的狮驼岭构成的地方。灵山的光明是用黑暗炼出来的。如来坐在那个光明的中心，背上是所有人的罪孽。

悟空继续走。不是因为他不疑了。是因为他服了。

服跟不疑不一样。不疑是你不怀疑方向。服是你看见了全貌。看见了光明和黑暗是怎么搅在一起的。看见了如来在这中间做的事情。看见了自己在这个结构里的位置。

然后你说：好。我走。

下一个狮驼岭

最后一个问题。也是最深的一个。

取经团队这次逼着灵山收了狮驼岭。大鹏被困在佛顶上，成了护法。

以后就没有狮驼岭了吗？

不但还有。而且必须有。

想一想。五百罗汉哪来的？三千揭谛哪来的？斗战胜佛哪来的？

都是从各自的狮驼岭里收编来的。每一个罗汉都曾经是某个时代的某种存在。每一个护法都有一段被收编之前的历史。灵山不是凭空建起来的。灵山是一次又一次收编狮驼岭建起来的。

狮驼岭不是灵山的敌人。狮驼岭是灵山的供给。

以前的狮驼岭，每一次都被收编了。收编了才有了今天的灵山。今天的狮驼岭，也需要被收编。收编了灵山才能增强实力。灵山增强了实力，才能收编以后更强的狮驼岭。

以后的狮驼岭永远会出现。而且会越来越强。因为世界在变。新的黑暗会以新的形式长出来。灵山不变强，就收编不了下一个。

这是一个永远不会停止的循环。

为什么如来这次带的是过去佛，现在佛，未来佛？为什么是三世佛全部到场？规模空前。

因为如来在告诉所有人：狮驼岭是无限的。过去有。现在有。未来还会有。这不是一次性的事件。这是结构。三世佛到场，就是在说这件事贯穿过去现在未来。

大鹏被收了。下一个大鹏正在某个地方长出来。

悟空今天被收编成了斗战胜佛。但悟空自己就是从狮驼岭走出来的。大闹天宫就是他的狮驼岭。五行山就是他被收编的过程。取经路就是他从大鹏变成护法的路。

他质问如来给大鹏编制。他自己就是那个拿了编制的大鹏。

灵山需要狮驼岭。因为没有狮驼岭，就没有新的罗汉。没有新的罗汉，灵山就是一潭死水。没有黑暗被转化为光明，光明就会停止生长。

这才是真正的物自体。不是"有灵山就有狮驼岭"这么简单。是灵山就是由无数个被收编的狮驼岭构成的。灵山的每一块砖，都曾经是某个狮驼岭的一部分。

你要灵山，你不只是要"接受"狮驼岭。你要给狮驼岭慈悲。因为没有它，就没有你站的这个地方。

Han Qin (秦汉) · @hqinjarsy · 西游新读系列

The Lion-Camel Ridge is not a trial.

The Lion-Camel Ridge is the mirror of the Buddha-realm.

Not an Ordinary Demon's Nest

Many demons were met on the journey. Occupy a mountain, capture some people, fight Wukong, win or lose, move on.

The Lion-Camel Ridge is different.

It is a system.

Three demon kings: the Blue-Maned Lion, the Yellow-Tusked Elephant, the Great Roc. They occupy the Lion-Camel Kingdom. Not a mountain. A country. Every person in it is dead. Corpse mountains, seas of blood. Stinking. Small demons eat human flesh, wear human skin as blankets.

Not one demon doing evil. A system running. Organization, hierarchy, division of labor, scale. The Buddha-realm saves beings. The Lion-Camel Ridge kills them. What the Buddha-realm saves, the Lion-Camel Ridge slaughters.

Two systems. Outwardly opposed. Structurally mirrored.

Who Are These Three Kings?

The Blue-Maned Lion—mount of Manjushri Bodhisattva. He appeared before in the Raven-Cock Kingdom. There, on a mission, performing half-heartedly. This time is different. Full power unleashed.

The Yellow-Tusked Elephant—mount of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva. Power equals the lion's. Ferocity greater.

The Great Roc—claims to be Rulai's uncle. Essentially Rulai's equal.

Manjushri's mount. Samantabhadra's mount. Rulai's relative.

Not wild demons. System insiders. We discussed Pingdingshan: the golden and silver horn demons were the Jade Emperor's attendants. The Lion-Camel Ridge goes far deeper. Those two were small attendants playing truant. This is three supreme beings building an entire system of killing that mirrors the Buddha-realm.

And it's not hidden. It's in plain sight. The Buddha-realm knows. The Buddha-realm permits it. Another system, coexisting.

What Rulai Said

Wukong cannot win. He calls reinforcements. Finally Rulai personally intervenes.

Rulai captures the Great Roc. How? Trapped atop the Buddha-crown. Given an identity: protector.

Killed a nation's people. Mountains of corpses, oceans of blood. Finally given a position.

Rulai said one thing, roughly: I will care for you; whenever there are offerings, you eat first.

What kind of Buddha-realm is this?

The one you walked 108,000 li to reach. The one your unwavering direction pointed toward. In that terminus sits a Rulai who says "you eat first."

Wukong Collapsed

Wukong collapsed at the Lion-Camel Ridge.

Not because he couldn't win. He often couldn't win. Call for reinforcements, it's fine.

Wukong collapsed because: he didn't even verify if his master was dead before declaring he was quitting.

Not "can't win so I quit." He doubted his life.

Not his own power. The Buddha-realm itself. The entire meaning of this journey.

You told me the Buddha-realm is good. You told me scripture-seeking is for all living beings. You told me this path ends in enlightenment. Then what did I see? Manjushri's mount kills. Samantabhadra's mount kills. Rulai's uncle killed an entire nation. Rulai came not to dispense justice. To give the killer a job. "You eat first."

Wukong refused to continue. Not laziness. Questioning Rulai. What are you doing? What is your Buddha-realm? All this way you taught me emptiness, not-fighting, leaving a gap between capability and action. You made me follow a defenseless monk 108,000 li. You made me believe this mattered. Then you tell me the nation-killer is your uncle and you hired him?

Wukong: laugh or weep?

The Buddha-realm's Mirror

The Lion-Camel Ridge mirrors the Buddha-realm.

Buddha-realm saves. Lion-Camel Ridge kills. Buddha-realm teaches dharma. Lion-Camel Ridge eats flesh. Buddha-realm has order. Lion-Camel Ridge has order too—killing's order. Buddha-realm has Rulai. Lion-Camel Ridge has the Great Roc.

Two systems, reflections of each other.

They are not opposing. They coexist. The Buddha-realm didn't eliminate the Lion-Camel Ridge. It absorbed the Lion-Camel Ridge's leader. The Great Roc became protector. Killer became protector. System not overthrown. System absorbed.

What does this mean?

The Buddha-realm contains the Lion-Camel Ridge.

You thought the Buddha-realm pure. You thought darkness vanished upon arrival. You thought the scripture held no blood.

The Lion-Camel Ridge tells you: atop the Buddha-realm's crown is a demon who slew a nation. Among the Buddha-realm's protectors sits a flesh-eater. The scripture was written in corpse mountains and seas of blood. The light you seek has darkness on its reverse side.

You cannot want the Buddha-realm without the Lion-Camel Ridge. They are two faces of one thing.

Everyone's Thing-Itself

This is the Lion-Camel Ridge's thing-itself. Not one person's. Everyone's.

You want the Buddha-realm? Accept the Lion-Camel Ridge. You want scripture? Accept mountains and oceans of blood. You want Rulai? Accept "you eat first." You want light? Accept light's price.

Everything costs. You cannot want the good remainder without the bad remainder.

Article Eight said: the thing you cannot break always remains. You cannot destroy it, only coexist with it.

The Lion-Camel Ridge is this statement's extreme expression. What you coexist with is not just your own boundary. Not just your own past. Not just your own thing you cannot break. The world's structure itself—have the Buddha-realm, have the Lion-Camel Ridge; save beings, kill beings; have scripture, have blood.

Do you accept?

The Other Half

That was half. Wukong questions Rulai: Why give the killer a job? What is the Buddha-realm?

Sharp enough. But it puts responsibility on Rulai.

The other half is harder to speak.

Rulai's compassion did not begin with the Great Roc. It included Wukong.

When Wukong ransacked Heaven, what was he? A being disrupting the entire system. He kicked over the furnace, disrupted the peach festival, defeated every heavenly general. Rulai did not kill him. Pressed him five hundred years, then offered him a path.

The Great Roc killed a nation. Wukong ransacked Heaven. Rulai did structurally the same to both. Absorb, not eliminate. Give a position, let you continue in a new structure.

What right has Wukong to question Rulai?

Look at the other team members.

Sha Seng. Flow-Sand River. What was he doing? Eating people. Nine generations of scripture-seekers. Nine Tang Sengs' past lives. He ate them. Skulls hung as necklaces.

Bajie. Before Gao's Village? The Heavenly Porcine Marshal, demoted, became a pig. Did he eat people? Yes.

Tang Seng. Compassion incarnate. Never kills. But at the Kingdom of Women, after drinking the child-bearing water, he drank the transformation medicine. That child?

Along the way? The Five-Phase Observation, he stole ginseng fruit. The Kingdom of Women, he deceived the king with false marriage to get travel papers. He promised the turtle to ask about its lifespan, then broke that promise.

Should all these be?

What your team did along the way—how is it essentially different from the Great Roc's acts? Degree differs. Structure is identical. For your own purpose, you harmed others, used others, betrayed others.

The Great Roc killed a nation for his existence. Your team harmed people for your scripture-seeking.

Who has the right to stand in a clean place and point, saying you are dirty?

Rulai Did Not Scold Wukong

Rulai did not scold Wukong.

Not weakness. Not fault.

Because scolding becomes punishment. Becomes "you're wrong, I'm right." Becomes a tallied account. Scold, penalize, it's settled, continue walking.

Rulai doesn't scold. Wukong can realize something himself.

Realize what?

That what he questions, he himself embodies. He questions why Rulai gives the Great Roc a job. But he himself is one Rulai gave a job to. He questions why the Buddha-realm tolerates killers. But he himself is one who was tolerated for once disrupting everything.

Rulai's compassion for the Great Roc and for Wukong are the same compassion. Compassion doesn't choose. It gives to Wukong, to the Great Roc. You accept what's given to you, you must accept it's given to others. Even those you think unworthy.

This is the real thing-itself.

Not Rulai's issue. Not the Great Roc's. Not Wukong's. Every being has this. Everyone has harmed someone. Everyone is covered by compassion. No one can deserve compassion only for themselves.

No one bears responsibility. Because responsibility is not the issue. This is structure.

Continue Walking

Wukong collapsed. Protested. Questioned.

Rulai offered no explanation. Rulai simply absorbed the Great Roc, gave him a position, and sent the team onward.

Then Wukong walked.

Others don't know what he realized. Maybe he couldn't articulate it. But something changed after the Lion-Camel Ridge.

He finally understood why Rulai didn't kill him.

Not compassion alone.

Because Rulai needed him. The Buddha-realm needed him.

Not because he's good. Because he can be good.

Rulai sees not what you are. What you can be. The Great Roc killed a nation—Rulai saw not the killing but the possibility of not killing. Wukong ransacked Heaven—Rulai saw not the ransacking but the possibility of not ransacking. Absorption is not reward. Absorption is Rulai's bet—that you can become something else.

Rulai bears everyone's sin. Absorbing the Great Roc is bearing. Absorbing Wukong is bearing. "You eat first" is bearing. Each absorption adds another darkness to Rulai's back. Each brick of the Buddha-realm is on Rulai's back. Five hundred arhats' pasts, three thousand heavenly guards' pasts, the Victorious Fighting Buddha's past—all Rulai's.

This is great compassion. Not ignorance of darkness. Knowing darkness, bearing darkness, transforming darkness into a brick of the Buddha-realm.

Wukong accepted.

Not beaten into submission. Not pressed into submission. He saw what Rulai was doing. Far greater than he imagined. Not a Buddha issuing commands from the Buddha-realm. A being bearing all the Lion-Camel Ridges on his back.

Wukong told no one.

The deepest emptiness. Realized, unsaid.

Laughing and crying, he walked. He knew what the Buddha-realm was. A place built from countless absorbed Lion-Camel Ridges. Its light refined from darkness. Rulai sitting at the center of that light, all beings' sins on his back.

Wukong walked. Not because he doubted nothing. Because he accepted.

Acceptance differs from not-doubting. Not-doubting is not questioning direction. Acceptance is seeing the whole picture. How light and darkness are stirred together. What Rulai does in between. Your place in the structure.

Then you say: good. I walk.

The Next Lion-Camel Ridge

One last question. The deepest.

The team forces the Buddha-realm to absorb the Lion-Camel Ridge. The Great Roc is trapped atop the crown, becomes protector.

Will there be no more Lion-Camel Ridges?

Not only more. They must exist.

Think. Where did the five hundred arhats come from? The three thousand heavenly guards? The Victorious Fighting Buddha?

All from their own Lion-Camel Ridges. Every arhat was once something in some era. Every protector has a pre-absorption history. The Buddha-realm wasn't built from nothing. Built again and again by absorbing Lion-Camel Ridges.

The Lion-Camel Ridge is not the Buddha-realm's enemy. Its supply.

Past Lion-Camel Ridges were all absorbed. Absorption built today's Buddha-realm. Today's must be absorbed. Absorption strengthens the Buddha-realm. Stronger, it absorbs future, stronger ones.

Future Lion-Camel Ridges will always emerge. Stronger each time. The world changes. New darkness grows in new forms. If the Buddha-realm doesn't grow, it cannot absorb the next.

An endless cycle.

Why did Rulai bring past Buddha, present Buddha, future Buddha? Why all three? Unprecedented scale.

Rulai tells everyone: the Lion-Camel Ridge is infinite. Past, present, future. Not a one-time event. Structure. Three Buddhas present means this spans all time.

The Great Roc is absorbed. The next grows somewhere.

Today Wukong is absorbed as the Victorious Fighting Buddha. But Wukong emerged from his own Lion-Camel Ridge. Heaven-ransacking was his. Five-Elements Mountain was his absorption. The journey was his transformation from Great Roc to protector.

He questions why Rulai hired the Great Roc. He is that hired Great Roc.

The Buddha-realm needs the Lion-Camel Ridge. Without it, no new arhats. Without new arhats, it stagnates. Without darkness transforming to light, light stops growing.

The real thing-itself. Not "have Buddha-realm, accept Lion-Camel Ridge" simply. The Buddha-realm is made of counted absorbed Lion-Camel Ridges. Each brick was once part of some Lion-Camel Ridge.

You want the Buddha-realm? Not just "accept" the Lion-Camel Ridge. Give it compassion. Without it, you have no ground to stand.
