Journey to the West: A New Reading

Journey to the West: A New Reading
西游新读

《西游记》不是神魔小说，是一部关于主体如何成形的书。二十三篇细读，从十万八千里读到石头记。

Journey to the West read not as a novel of gods and demons but as a book about how a subject takes shape. Twenty-three close readings, from the hundred and eight thousand li to the record on the stone.

Han Qin (秦汉) · Non Dubito
