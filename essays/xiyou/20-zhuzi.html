Journey to the West: A New Reading (XX)

朱紫国

The Purple Zhu Kingdom

Han Qin (秦汉) · March 2026

朱紫国。悟空学会了骗人。

一只猴子给人看病。荒诞主义都没这么荒诞。

故事

取经团队到了朱紫国。国王病了。病了很久。太医治不好。

悟空揭了皇榜，说我来治。

他会治病吗？不会。他是一只猴子。他会七十二变，会筋斗云，会一棒子打死妖怪。他不会治病。

但他去了。悬丝诊脉——隔着帘子拉一根丝线搭在国王手腕上，装模作样摸了半天脉。然后开了一副药：巴豆，百草霜（就是锅底灰），还有白龙马的马尿做药引。

国王吃了。好了。

悟空骗人了

悬丝诊脉是骗人的。悟空不懂脉。他隔着帘子根本摸不出什么。那根丝线搭上去搭不上去都无所谓。他在表演。

药方是瞎开的。巴豆是泻药。锅底灰是凑数的。马尿是马尿。这不是什么精妙的药理。这是"反正我让他拉一场可能就好了"。

但国王信了。因为悟空的气场在那。因为整个过程足够像一个高明的大夫在做正经的事。

国王信了，就肯吃药。肯吃药，就好了。

为什么要骗

你跟国王说实话会怎么样？

"陛下，我是一只猴子，我不会治病。但我觉得你可能吃点巴豆拉一下就好了。药引是我的马的尿。你喝吗？"

他不喝。

不是因为药不对。是因为人心不接受这种方式。你得让他相信你是一个能治他的人。你得让他觉得这副药是有道理的。你得让他的心先接受了，身体才能接受。

心不开，药进不去。

悟空在这里碰到的物自体是什么？是国王的人心。

人心这个东西，你打不到。你的金箍棒打不到人心。你的火眼金睛看不见人心想要什么。你的七十二变变不了人心的走向。

你所有的能力，在人心面前，不好使。

不是你不够强。是人心不在能力的维度上。你不能劈开一个人的脑子把心病拿出来。你不能用筋斗云飞到人心里面去修理它。

你只能绕。

骗就是绕。不是恶意的骗。是"我不能正面解决这个问题，但我可以从侧面迂回进去"的骗。悬丝诊脉是一场戏。但这场戏是必要的。没有这场戏，国王不喝药。不喝药，病不好。

有些问题不接受正面解决。你得接受这件事。然后你得学会绕。

治标不够

但骗只是治标。

国王的病根不是身体。他的金圣宫娘娘被妖怪掳走了。他思念成疾。太医治不好，因为太医治的是身体，不是心。悟空的药也只是把身体上的症状压下去了。

病根还在那。娘娘没回来，心病就不会好。你吃多少巴豆都没用。

所以悟空还得去打妖怪。去赛太岁那里把金圣宫娘娘救回来。救回来了，国王的病才真正好了。

治标靠骗。治本靠打。

骗能解决表面的问题。你用一场戏让国王吃了药，症状缓了。但症状缓了不等于病好了。你还得去解决病根。病根是娘娘被掳走了。那是一个实实在在的问题。那个问题得用悟空最擅长的方式——打——去解决。

骗也不行。只打也不行。你得先骗，把表面稳住，然后去打，把根解决。两步。缺一步都不行。

悟空的新能力

这一难教了悟空一件新东西。

前面所有的难，悟空用的都是他本来就有的能力。七十二变，筋斗云，金箍棒，火眼金睛。打不过就搬救兵。搬救兵也是一种"打"——找更强的人来打。

朱紫国不是。

朱紫国里悟空做的事情不在他原来的能力清单上。悬丝诊脉不是七十二变。开药方不是打妖怪。骗一个国王吃药不是任何一种他在菩提祖师那里学过的本事。

他是现学的。现编的。现场发挥的。

这意味着什么？意味着悟空的能力在扩张。不是七十二变变成了七十三变那种扩张。是他能做的事情的种类在增加。他开始做一些"不像孙悟空会做的事"。

大闹天宫时的悟空只会打。取经路上的悟空学会了不打。朱紫国的悟空学会了骗。

打是直的。不打是空的。骗是弯的。

直的碰到人心不好使。空的面对病人不够。弯的才能绕进去。

白龙马的尿

最后说一个细节。

药引是白龙马的马尿。

白龙马一开始不愿意。

你让一条龙撒尿当药引？他是西海龙王的三太子。他变成马已经是降格了。现在你还要用他的尿？跟巴豆和锅底灰放在一起？给一个凡人国王喝？

这是他自己的物自体。他过不去。龙的尊严在那。你让我驮人我驮了。你让我当马我当了。你现在让我贡献一泡尿？

但他给了。

第五篇说过，白龙马的整条取经路是他的五行山。他不悟。他苦修。别人的终点是他的起点。他一直在积蓄，在承载，在沉默。

朱紫国这一刻，他也许悟了一点什么。他的修行不只是驮着唐僧走十万八千里。他的修行是放下。放下龙的身份，放到连一泡尿都可以给出去。

他没有出场。没有说话。没有做任何事。他只是给了一泡尿。但这泡尿是药方里不可缺的一味。没有它，药不成。

白龙马从来不是主角。但他从来没有缺席过。即便在他最不起眼的时候——以一泡尿的形式——他也在发挥作用。这也是他的修行。每一次放下，都是他的一步。

这也是一种物自体。你看不见它。你觉得它不重要。但没有它，事情就是不成。

Han Qin (秦汉) · @hqinjarsy · 西游新读系列

The Purple Zhu Kingdom. Wukong learned to deceive.

A monkey treating illness. Not even absurdism is this absurd.

The Story

The team reaches the Purple Zhu Kingdom. The king is ill. Ill for a long time. The physicians cannot heal him.

Wukong posts a notice: I will treat him.

Can he treat illness? No. He is a monkey. Seventy-two transformations, cloud-somersault, power to strike demons dead. He doesn't treat illness.

But he goes. Thread pulse-taking—a silk thread across a screen, placed on the king's wrist. He pretends to read the pulse for a while. Then prescribes: phorbia, lampblack (hearth ash), and the white dragon horse's urine as the guide.

The king takes it. Recovers.

Wukong Deceived

Thread pulse-taking is deception. Wukong doesn't understand pulses. Through a screen, he feels nothing. The thread matters not. Performance.

The prescription is guessed. Phorbia is a purgative. Lampblack is filler. Urine is urine. No medical subtlety. Just "maybe if I make him purge, he'll improve."

But the king believes. Wukong's presence. The whole process looks like a skilled physician working seriously.

King believes, takes medicine. Takes medicine, recovers.

Why Deceive?

Tell the king the truth, what happens?

"Majesty, I am a monkey. I don't treat illness. But I think a purgative might help. The medicine guide is my horse's urine. Will you drink?"

He won't.

Not because the medicine is wrong. Because the human heart won't accept this method. You must make him believe you can heal him. Make him think the prescription makes sense. His heart must accept first; the body follows.

Heart closed, medicine cannot enter.

What does Wukong encounter here as thing-itself? The king's heart.

The human heart—you cannot strike it. Your golden staff cannot hit it. Your fiery eyes cannot see what it wants. Your seventy-two transformations cannot change its course.

Before the heart, all your power fails.

Not insufficient strength. The heart isn't on the power plane. You cannot split open a skull and extract sickness. You cannot use cloud-somersault to reach inside and repair it.

You can only circle around it.

Deception is circling. Not malicious deception. "I cannot solve this directly, but I can loop around from the side." Thread pulse-taking is a performance. But necessary. Without it, the king won't drink. Won't drink, won't recover.

Some problems refuse direct solution. Accept this. Then learn to circle.

Symptom Treatment Isn't Enough

But deception treats symptoms only.

The king's illness isn't physical. His queen was taken by demons. Longing turned to sickness. Physicians can't heal—they treat bodies, not hearts. Wukong's medicine only suppresses symptoms.

The root remains. Queen doesn't return, the heart-sickness won't heal. Take all the purgatives you want, nothing helps.

So Wukong must fight demons. Go to that demon's place, rescue the queen. Rescued, the king truly recovers.

Symptom treatment by deception. Root treatment by fighting.

Deception solves surface problems. A performance makes the king drink, symptoms ease. But eased symptoms don't mean healing. You must solve the root. The root is: the queen was taken. A real problem. Real problems need Wukong's way—fighting.

Deception alone won't work. Fighting alone won't work. You deceive first, stabilize the surface, then fight, solve the root. Two steps. Missing one doesn't work.

Wukong's New Ability

This trial teaches Wukong something new.

All earlier trials: Wukong used his existing abilities. Seventy-two transformations, cloud-somersault, golden staff, fiery eyes. Can't win? Call reinforcements. Reinforcements are also fighting—finding stronger fighters.

Not the Purple Zhu Kingdom.

Wukong's actions there aren't on his original ability list. Thread pulse-taking isn't a transformation. Prescribing isn't demon-fighting. Deceiving a king into drinking medicine isn't any skill from Patriarch Bodhi.

He learned it on the spot. Improvised it. Improvised mid-action.

What does this mean? Wukong's abilities are expanding. Not seventy-two to seventy-three. The types of things he can do are increasing. He's doing "un-Wukong-like things."

Young Wukong knew only fighting. Journey Wukong learned not-fighting. Purple Zhu Wukong learned deception.

Fighting is direct. Not-fighting is empty. Deception is curved.

Direct fails against hearts. Empty isn't enough for patients. Curved can loop in.

The Dragon Horse's Urine

One last detail.

The guide is the white dragon horse's urine.

The horse was reluctant at first.

Make a dragon urinate as medicine guide? He's the Dragon King's third son. Becoming a horse was already a step down. Now you want his urine? Mixed with phorbia and lampblack? For a mortal king to drink?

His own thing-itself. He cannot accept. Dragon dignity is there. I carried people—done. I became a horse—done. Now give a stream of urine?

But he gave it.

Article Five said: the white dragon horse's entire journey is his Five-Elements Mountain. He doesn't realize. He simply endures. Others' terminus is his starting point. He accumulates, carries, stays silent.

At the Purple Zhu Kingdom, perhaps he realized something. His cultivation isn't only carrying Tang Seng 108,000 li. His cultivation is letting go. Releasing dragon status until even urine can be given away.

He doesn't appear on stage. Says nothing. Does nothing. He gives urine. But this urine is essential to the formula. Without it, no medicine.

The white dragon horse is never the protagonist. But he never absents himself. Even in his least visible moment—as a stream of urine—he plays a role. This too is his cultivation. Each release another step.

Also a kind of thing-itself. You don't see it. You think it unimportant. But without it, nothing happens.
