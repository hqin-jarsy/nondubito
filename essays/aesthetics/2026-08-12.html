余项之帧：阿雷克西亚·彭斯的动画逻辑

Remainder-Frames: The Animation Logic of Alèxia Ponce

Han Qin (秦汉)

阿雷克西亚·彭斯（Alèxia Ponce）是一位巴塞罗那的视听设计师与动画师。她的工作看起来像这样：将一张纸浸湿后揉皱，让其干燥成形；将几何图形在拼图的表面上逐帧推移；用阳光在光敏纸上烧出一个瞬间。结果是帧，是动画，是每个序列中数百张图像的积累。她的最新影片《谁踩了那条线》（Qui trepitgi la ratlla，2026）将这个逻辑延伸至儿童绘画：让孩子们自己画下想象中的世界，再将这些画一帧帧动画化，直到游戏的痕迹重新运动起来。

但这里有一件更深层的事。彭斯并不是在构造帧——她是在收割余项。她所使用的每一种支撑物，都是某个已经完成的系统的产物。造纸工业产出纸张，纸是该系统的最终形态，是已构。当彭斯将纸浸水揉皱，她激活的是造纸系统本身的余项：纤维的记忆，纸张对湿度的物理抵抗，那些潜伏在光滑表面之下、从未被预期出现的褶皱形态。这个褶皱不是她设计的，它是已构系统的剩余，是造纸过程无法完全控制的那部分能量的留痕。拼图是另一个完成的对象——它被设计来拼合成一幅可辨识的图像，其已构状态完全。彭斯在它的表面上动作，让拼缝、色块划分、每个碎片的轮廓，成为动画的意外结构基底。她在拼图的余项里找到了帧的几何。

日光印相同理。光化学过程早已被艺术史收编，蓝晒法、光克罗米法，都是已构的技术，有名字，有市场，有美术馆的展墙。彭斯不是用光感材料制作一张完整的成品印相——她把日光的化学行为用作动画序列里的一帧。一帧，不是一件成品。这个区别是决定性的：她将已构技术的余项（日光化学在某一特定时刻的一次性显现）截取为动画时间里的一个单位，而不是让它成为终点。在SAE的框架里，这叫做余项的取用——将已构系统的剩余能量重新引入凿构循环的入口。

《谁踩了那条线》将这个结构推向了最清晰的表达。孩子的玩耍是一个系统，绘画是其余项——玩耍结束后留下的那些痕迹，那些本来属于游戏、不属于艺术的图像。彭斯将这些痕迹再动画化，让儿童游戏的余项重新运动。凿构循环在这里格外明确：游戏（凿）→ 绘画（构，同时是余项）→ 动画（新的凿）→ 影片（新的构）。她的工作是在每一个循环的入口处等待，从上一轮构中取出余项，而不是从零开始建造。

艺术界目前的命名是"模拟动画"或"材料动画"。这只是现象描述，不是结构分析。余项收割的逻辑——将已构系统的剩余作为生成材料的动作——没有名字。今天（2026年8月12日）It's Nice That首次以英语为她做了深度报道，但报道框架停留在"意外的材料和方法"，仍然是现象层面。这正是余项之美的特征：靠近，但未被命名。凿构循环还没有到来，它的逻辑还活着。现在看到它，是因为它还是余项；等它被命名，它就成了构。

Alèxia Ponce is a Barcelona-based audiovisual designer and animator. Her process looks like this: soaking and scrunching paper, letting it dry into form; moving geometric shapes frame by frame across the surface of a puzzle; burning a moment onto photosensitive paper with sunlight. The result is frames — hundreds per sequence, accumulated into animation. Her 2026 film Qui trepitgi la ratlla (Who Steps on the Line) extends this logic to children's drawings: she has children draw their own imaginary worlds, then animates those drawings frame by frame, until the traces of play are set back into motion.

But something deeper is happening here. Ponce is not constructing frames. She is harvesting remainders. Every support she uses is a product of a system that has already completed itself. Industrial papermaking produces a sheet of paper — that sheet is the system's final form, its already-construct. When Ponce soaks and crumples it, she activates the papermaking system's own remainder: the fiber's memory, the paper's physical resistance to moisture, the crease-forms that were always latent beneath the smooth surface, never intended to appear. That crease is not her design. It is the residue of a completed system — the trace of energy the papermaking process could never fully control. The puzzle is another finished object, designed to assemble into a recognizable image, its already-construct state total. She moves across its surface and lets the puzzle's seams, its color-field divisions, its per-piece outlines, become the accidental structural ground of her animation. She finds the frame's geometry inside the puzzle's remainder.

Sun printing works the same way. Photochemical processes have long been absorbed into art history — cyanotype, anthotype, photogram, each with a name, a market, a wall in a museum. Ponce doesn't use light-sensitive materials to make a finished print. She uses solar chemical action as a single frame in an animation sequence. A frame, not a finished work. This distinction is decisive: she cuts the remainder of an already-construct technique (the one-time appearance of solar chemistry at a particular moment) into a unit of animation time, rather than letting it be an endpoint. In SAE terms, this is remainder-taking (余项的取用) — re-routing the residual energy of completed systems back into the entry point of a new chisel-construct cycle.

Qui trepitgi la ratlla pushes this structure to its clearest expression. The act of children's play is a system; the drawings are its remainder — the traces that survive after play ends, images that belonged to the game, not to art. She re-animates those traces, setting the remainder of children's play back into motion. The chisel-construct cycle is especially legible here: play (chisel) → drawing (construct, simultaneously remainder) → animation (new chisel) → film (new construct). Her work is waiting at the entry point of each cycle, taking the remainder out of the last construct, rather than building from nothing.

The art world currently names this "analogue animation" or "materials-based animation." These are phenomenal descriptions, not structural analyses. The logic of remainder-harvesting — taking a completed system's residue as generative material — has no name. Today, August 12, 2026, It's Nice That gave Ponce her first major English-language profile, but framed it as "unexpected materials and methods" — still at the surface level. This is exactly what the beauty of the remainder looks like: approached, but not yet named. The chisel-construct cycle has not arrived. The logic is still alive. The reason to see it now is that it is still a remainder; once named, it becomes construct.
