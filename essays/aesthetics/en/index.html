02 · DIRECTIONS OF ATTENTION

Thirteen rays, not a ladder

The same night sky can be approached in several ways. These rays are not bins for objects or ranks for people’s tastes. Each essay stands on its own; the papers are not prerequisites.

13 of 13 companion essays are published, each in English and both Chinese editions. The collection is complete; begin with any essay. All thirteen bilingual academic papers are linked in the expandable map below.

01 · Hundun (the undivided)

Naming the Stars Does Not Finish the Sky

A name can help us see, or tell us we are finished looking. Put the chart away and look up once more.

02 · Philosophy

The House Is Still Unsold. The Argument Has Changed.

One question does not decide the family's future. It lets them hear what each person is trying to hold on to.

03 · Mathematics

Both Answers Are Right. Why Is This One Beautiful?

Not just arriving at 5,050, but seeing why the sum takes that shape. Beauty is not a prize for finishing first.

04 · Physics

Even a Beautiful Theory Has to Wait for the World

Why should an electrical measurement meet the speed of light? Beauty can guide a question. It cannot supply the instrument's answer.

05 · Causation

The Wood Still Holds That Dry Year

Rings, worn steps and repairs bring a past into view. Reading what remains does not excuse everything that caused it.

06 · Biology

It Keeps Changing, Yet Holds Together

How does a little rod under the microscope keep living? A quiet shape need not mean that nothing is happening.

07 · Reproduction

The Next Generation Is Not a Finished Miniature

A Volvox embryo turns inside out, establishing differentiated parts anew. Continuation is not simply reprinting a finished form.

08 · Perception

As the Light Fades, the Garden Appears Differently

You have found the blue flower and have no further use for its color. The blue remains. An appearance is more than a message delivered.

09 · Cognition

The Note Has Not Sounded. You Are Already Waiting.

A note next door, a pharmacy around the corner: memory does not only look backward. It is also in the half-step not yet taken.

10 · Self

“I Was Mistaken.”

A name called in error brings a judgment back to its maker. The world is not wholly wrong. Neither is the person who made the mistake.

11 · Purpose

Before the Book Went to Press, He Put the Word Back

The smoother version made sense. Why did he call again? A direction of one's own need not make a person uncorrectable.

12 · Non-doubt

I Wouldn't Play It That Way. You Needn't Play It My Way.

The teacher still disagrees, yet helps the student make the phrase clearer. Leaving a choice with someone need not mean withdrawing help.

13 · Mutual non-doubt

After Talking, We Still Took Different Paths

One returned to class. The other did not. A question reached each person without delivering either to the other's intended destination.
