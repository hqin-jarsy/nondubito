氢在第十三位回答了

Hydrogen Answered at the Thirteenth Digit

Han Qin (秦汉)

这是慕尼黑大学（LMU）与马克斯·普朗克量子光学研究所的一项测量，作者是 Lothar Maisenbacher、Vitaly Wirthl、Arthur Matveev、Alexey Grinin、Randolf Pohl、Theodor W. Hänsch 和 Thomas Udem，2026 年 2 月 11 日发表于《自然》第 650 卷。他们用 410 纳米的激光，在低温氢原子上做无多普勒的单光子谱学，测量 2S–6P 跃迁的频率：730,690,248,610.79(48) kHz。相对精度约为万亿分之 0.7，比此前氢原子的测定高出约 2.5 倍。数据在 2019 年就已采完，之后是长时间对各种干扰效应的评估。

同一个频率，标准模型也给出一个预言：730,690,248,610.79(23) kHz。两者的差是 0.00(53) kHz，也就是在测量的不确定度之内完全重合。这个预言里有量子电动力学的各级修正；论文说，这次测量把这些修正检验到了百万分之 0.5，是迄今最精确的一次。马普所的新闻稿还提到，测量里第一次看到了强子的贡献，以及电子氢里由缪子经真空极化带来的效应。同一次测量还给出质子电荷半径 0.8406(15) fm，与缪子氢的结果一致，与较早的氢测量相差 5.5σ。

这是第四条光线（物理之美）的“回答”这一面。形式先走：一套理论只靠自己的规则，写下一个十二位数字的频率；然后世界被带到这个位置，用一台激光器在同一个地方回答。判断力在这里不能不承认的是：这个回答恰好落在形式本来不能是别的样子的地方。数字写到第十二三位，还没有一处对不上。它不是“精度很高所以有用”（有用是另一个维度的事），也不是“数字很大所以令人印象深刻”；就算没有任何人要用这个频率，形式与世界在这里相遇、而世界没有说“不”，这件事本身站得住。它也不是某个人的口味：这是不同的判断者可以共同承认的那一部分。

需要说清它不是什么。它不是“标准模型被证明是对的”：同一类测量可以随时给出相反的回答，而那同样是物理之美（开普勒不肯放过 8 角分的差，是它的“拒绝”那一面）。0.00(53) kHz 的意思只是：到这个精度为止，没有一处裂缝。美不在于“对”，而在于世界真的有机会说“不”，而且被问到了最细的地方。

它的余项在论文和新闻稿里都是明说的：为什么较早的氢测量会给出不同的质子半径，目前仍没有解释，只是被这次测量压了下去。另一层余项属于这条光线本身：不确定度里的 0.5 kHz 不是终点，下一次更细的测量会问同一个问题，那个“无处不合”能不能再往下撑一位。没有最高的那一次。

现在值得看，是因为这一刻是整条线最安静的一刻：理论和实验在同一个数字上相遇，没有任何一方在修正自己去迁就另一方。原始论文：

This is a measurement from Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München and the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics, by Lothar Maisenbacher, Vitaly Wirthl, Arthur Matveev, Alexey Grinin, Randolf Pohl, Theodor W. Hänsch and Thomas Udem, published 11 February 2026 in Nature, volume 650. Using 410 nm laser light, they performed Doppler-free one-photon spectroscopy on cryogenic atomic hydrogen and measured the frequency of the 2S–6P transition: 730,690,248,610.79(48) kHz. The relative precision is about 0.7 parts per trillion, roughly 2.5 times better than earlier determinations in atomic hydrogen. The data were taken by 2019; years of evaluating interfering effects followed.

The Standard Model predicts the same frequency: 730,690,248,610.79(23) kHz. The difference is 0.00(53) kHz, that is, coincidence within the measurement uncertainty. The prediction contains the successive quantum-electrodynamic corrections, and the paper says the measurement tests those corrections to 0.5 parts per million, their most precise test so far. The Max Planck press release adds that the measurement saw hadronic contributions and, through vacuum polarization, effects of muons inside ordinary hydrogen, for the first time. The same measurement yields a proton charge radius of 0.8406(15) fm, in agreement with the muonic hydrogen value and 5.5σ away from older hydrogen measurements.

This is the answering face of the fourth ray, the Beauty of Physics. The form goes first: a theory, by its own rules alone, writes down a frequency to twelve digits. Then the world is brought to that place, and a laser answers at the same spot. What a judge cannot not acknowledge here is that the answer lands exactly where the form could not have been otherwise. The digits run to the twelfth or thirteenth place and nothing yet fails to fit. It is not beautiful because high precision is useful (usefulness is another dimension), nor because the number is impressive; if nobody ever needed this frequency, form and world meeting here, with the world not saying no, would still stand. And it is not anyone's taste: it is the part different judges can jointly acknowledge.

It is worth saying what it is not. It is not “the Standard Model is proven right”: the same kind of measurement could at any time have answered otherwise, and that would be physical beauty too (Kepler refusing to let an 8-arcminute error go is the refusing face). 0.00(53) kHz only says that to this precision no crack has appeared. The beauty is not in being right; it is that the world really had the chance to say no, and was asked at the finest place yet.

Its remainder is stated in the paper and the press release: why earlier hydrogen measurements gave a different proton radius is still unexplained; this result only pushes it back. A second remainder belongs to the ray itself: the 0.5 kHz in the uncertainty is not an end, and the next finer measurement will ask whether “nowhere a crack” can hold one digit further. There is no highest instance.

It is worth seeing now because this is the quietest moment on the whole line: theory and experiment meet on one number, and neither side has bent itself to suit the other. The original paper:
