余项之美

Beauty of the Remainder

每个DD都有自己的美。但那个美是过去某个时间点的余项，后来变成了美，现在已经是构了。已构之美是过去的沉淀，它安全，它成立，但它不活。

Every DD has its own beauty. But that beauty was once a remainder at some moment in the past — it later became beautiful, and now it is construct. Constructed beauty is the sediment of the past. It is safe, it holds, but it is no longer alive.

余项之美不同。它是当下还没有被艺术界完全消化、还没有被完整命名的东西。它的逻辑还在生长。SAE的判断力指向的就是这里——不是识别已知的美，是识别当下余项的美。

The beauty of the remainder is different. It is what the art world has not yet fully digested, not yet given a complete name. Its logic is still growing. SAE judgment points here — not to recognizing known beauty, but to recognizing the beauty of the current remainder.

这个系列是每天的发现。不是艺术界的推荐，是我们认为艺术家做得好、符合SAE美学的东西。最新的在最前面。

This series is a daily discovery. Not art-world recommendations — what we find done well, what fits SAE aesthetics. Newest first.
