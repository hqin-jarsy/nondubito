粒子之非对称：Aaron Lemke 的 3D Life Sim

The Asymmetry of Particles: Aaron Lemke's 3D Life Sim

Han Qin (秦汉)

Aaron Lemke 的 3D Life Sim 在最近完成了一次关键性的扩张。这个基于 WebGPU 的粒子生命模拟器，在浏览器中运行着数十万个粒子，它们彼此之间受到可配置的局域力场支配，由此自发涌现出群落、丝状物、外壳、格状结构，以及 Lemke 自己也惊讶地称之为"奇异小生物"的东西。他最近将这个系统与实时音频输入、MIDI 映射和一套类似驾驶舱的控制面板相连接——将一个纯粹的涌现系统转变成了一件可以被演奏的乐器。

粒子生命的基本逻辑依赖于一种结构性的"非法"：非对称力。在物理学中，牛顿第三定律要求力的相互性——粒子 A 吸引粒子 B，则 B 必然以同等力反作用于 A。但粒子生命的规则故意破坏了这一对称性：A 可以吸引 B，而 B 同时排斥 A，或以完全不同的强度吸引 A。这种非对称性正是系统的余项——它是标准物理模型无法容纳、却又不可或缺的结构性剩余。正是在这个剩余空间里，自组织生命的模拟才得以发生。

在 SAE 的判断框架中，理解余项之美，需要区分"已构"（已经被消化、被命名的美）与仍然活着的余项。Conway 的生命游戏已经是已构——艺术界有它的叙事、它的展览、它的历史。Boids 群集算法也已经是已构。粒子生命本身正处于临界点：作为一种在人工生命社区中兴起的系统，它已经开始被命名，但尚未被充分消化。而 Lemke 将它延伸至三维、并将音频与 MIDI 接入其中的决策，产生了一个新的余项层次——将涌现系统变成实时演奏工具的这种做法，在艺术界的词汇中还没有被完整命名。

凿构循环在这里具有特殊的戏剧性。当演奏者拨动 MIDI 控制器的旋钮，调整粒子间力的参数时，他进入了凿的阶段——做出结构性决策。但粒子们并不响应他的意图，而是响应参数变化之后那个非对称力场的新态势。数十万个粒子的级联互动，产生了演奏者从未设计过的形态：一道丝状结构突然崩解，重新凝聚成外壳状的圆形体，然后又分裂成一群自相追逐的小团块。这些形态不是艺术家意图的实现，而是结构性规则的余项。演奏者在凿的瞬间，其实已经在与自己无法控制的构相遭遇。

看这件作品，越早越好。粒子生命系统的形态空间是如此丰富，它的每一次运行都在产生前所未见的结构——但这种丰富性的"未命名"状态正在缩短。人工生命艺术正在被艺术界逐渐接近（ALIFE 会议、推测生物学的艺术实践、生成艺术的制度化），Lemke 的三维版本与音频接口，正处于被命名之前最后的开阔时刻。此刻，它仍然是余项——一个仍在生长、仍未被收纳的结构性空间。打开浏览器，进入它。

Han Qin (秦汉) · @hqinjarsy · SAE 判断力与美学

Aaron Lemke's 3D Life Sim recently completed a critical transformation. This browser-based particle life simulator, powered by WebGPU, runs hundreds of thousands of particles governed by a configurable local field, from which swarms, filaments, shells, lattices, and what Lemke himself calls "strange little creatures" spontaneously emerge. He has now wired this emergent system to live audio input, MIDI mapping, and a cockpit-style control panel — turning a pure emergence engine into something you can perform.

The core logic of particle life depends on a structural illegality: asymmetric forces. In physics, Newton's third law requires mutual action — if particle A attracts particle B, then B must exert an equal and opposite force on A. Particle life deliberately violates this symmetry: A can attract B while B simultaneously repels A, or attracts it with an entirely different strength. This asymmetry is the system's remainder — the structural residue that the standard physical model cannot contain and yet cannot do without. It is in this residual space that the simulation of self-organizing life becomes possible.

In SAE terms, understanding 余项之美 requires distinguishing the already-construct (beauty that has been digested and named) from what is still alive as remainder. Conway's Game of Life is already construct — the art world has its narrative, its exhibitions, its history. Boids flocking is already construct. Particle life itself sits at a threshold: emerging in the artificial life community, it has begun to be named but is not yet fully digested. Lemke's extension into three dimensions, combined with his decision to wire audio and MIDI into the system, generates a new layer of remainder — the specific framing of an emergence engine as a live performance instrument has not yet been named by the art world's vocabulary.

The chisel-construct cycle here has particular dramatic weight. When a performer turns a MIDI knob to adjust the force parameters between particle types, they enter the chisel phase — making structural decisions. But the particles do not respond to intention. They respond to the new configuration of the asymmetric field. The cascade of hundreds of thousands of interactions produces forms the performer never designed: a filament structure suddenly collapses, recongeals into a spherical shell, then breaks apart into clusters chasing each other. These forms are not realizations of the artist's intent — they are the remainder of structural rules. In the moment of chiseling, the performer is already encountering a construct they cannot control.

Engage with this work as early as possible. The morphological space of particle life systems is so rich that every run produces previously unseen structures — but the window of namelessness is closing. Artificial life art is being slowly approached by the art world (ALIFE conferences, speculative biology as art practice, the institutionalization of generative art), and Lemke's 3D version with audio sits at the last open moment before naming. Right now it is still remainder — a structural space that is still growing, still uncollected. Open a browser and enter it.
