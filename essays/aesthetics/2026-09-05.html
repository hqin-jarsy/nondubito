他把图灵机跳成了民间舞

He Turned the Turing Machine Into a Folk Dance

Han Qin (秦汉)

compudanzas 是 sejo 发起的一个项目，网站西班牙语和英语两套页面并行，纪年用全新世历（今年写作 12026），全部内容献给公有领域。它的主干叫 qiudanz technique——一套以"对极简动作序列进行计算变换"为基础的身体技术。目前真正做出来的只有 mode 1，词汇表里只有三个动作：IN，记作加号，双掌在身前合拢再收回躯干；DEH，记作减号，两条小臂交叉再打开；SHOW，记作句点，摊开一只手掌递向对面的人。序列的第一个动作叫头，最后一个叫尾。关键在于：这三个动作的名字，同时也是三条操作的名字。DEH 表示"删掉序列的头"；IN 表示"把紧跟其后的那个动作接到序列的尾"；SHOW 表示"把当前的整个序列完整跳一遍"，并把回合让给对面。于是同一串动作既是一支舞，也是一段用来改写舞的程序。

先挡掉最容易被误认的那一层。"不插电的计算机科学"早就是已构：CS Unplugged 那一系的教学法从九十年代末就在用卡片、绳子和排队的孩子讲排序与二进制，有教材、有课程标准、有各语种译本。它的美是真的，但那是构的美——它用身体去解释机器，成败的尺度是身体复现硅片行为的忠实程度。同理，live coding 与 algorave 已经有宣言、有社群、有年会；"舞蹈与科技"也早有自己的谱系与工具链。如果 qiudanz 的价值只是"用身体演示一台图灵机"，这一则不该写。

差别不在外观，在那一凿落在哪一层。计算机科学的奠基动作本身是一次极深的凿：把过程从承载它的物质里抽出来，使同一个计算在算盘上、在继电器上、在硅片上"是同一个"。这一凿成功到我们已经不再把它当作一次切割。但它剩下了东西：一次计算里凡是关于谁的身体在做、做了多久、以及在场的人是否同意刚才那一下算不算数的部分，全被判为无关。硅片的优越恰恰在于它把这一层删得最干净。compudanzas 把删掉的那一半重新装回来，并且让它当媒介：qiudanz 的动作有意不做刚性定义，一个动作之所以有效，是因为这个房间里的人同意它有效；序列能长到多长，取决于在场者的记忆与体力；机器的状态就寄存在某个人身上，随时可能滑掉。sejo 自己把这条原则说得很干脆——放掉控制的幻觉。

后果具体到可以在工作坊记录里读出来。四次线上小工作坊里，最难学会的是 DEH，也就是"删掉序列的第一个元素"；sejo 的记录写道，我们习惯的动作组织方式使得"从开头删"这件事异常别扭。另一处更要紧：因为指令和动作同名，参与者会本能地把听到的指令序列直接当成要跳的动作序列跳出来。他事先提醒过，但他同时写下——这个"错误"发生是重要的，因为它恰好暴露了这门技术的核心。在硅片里，这正是取指与执行的分界要消灭的混淆，是冯·诺伊曼架构小心看守的那条线；在这里它不是 bug，它是全部乐趣所在，用他的话说，你可以把一组指令跳出来。还有 target 游戏：给定起点序列与目标序列，用变换抵达目标；规则写完之后跟着一句——也许更好玩的是不要直接抵达。一套本可以只用效率衡量的形式系统，在这里被明文取消了效率。

余项还活着，因为没有一个名字接得住它。叫不插电教学法不对，那以复现机器为尺度，而这里机器根本不是目标。叫 live coding 不对，那预设有一台机器在执行、有一个人在写；这里没有任何机器执行任何东西。叫舞蹈不对——参与者反复报告，最难的是卸掉编舞的习惯：流动、句法、诗意，在这套规则里完全不起作用。叫行为艺术也不对，它的主要产出是三五个人的线上工作坊，全部献给公有领域，没有画廊，没有作品编号。连 permacomputing 都接不住：viznut 在 2021 年那篇更新里把 compudanzas 列进"把计算与一切实用目的剥离开"的那一类——这是在描述一个缺口，不是在给出一个名字。

为什么现在看比以后看重要。命名的压力已经开始，而且有日期可查：2025 年 6 月马德里 ALGOPOÉTICA，9 月 Alpaca（Algorithmic Patterns in the Creative Arts）会议工作坊，11 月阿姆斯特丹 NIAS 的 Code as Critique。这三个场合各自都备好了一套词汇——算法图式、批判计算、decomputing——随时可以收编它。一旦收编完成，工作坊就能照着讲义教，而讲义不必携带那份风险：房间里的人可能对刚才那一下算不算数产生分歧。此刻风险还在。而且这条技术路线自己就没写完：四个 mode 按词汇量分为 2、4、8、16 个动作，第四个后面跟着一个问号，实际做过的只有第一个。sejo 还给这套技术编了一个来历：txiemonks，一群致力于在电子与工业文明之外保存计算机科学的人，用它跳民间舞与后崩溃之舞——不需要道具，任何年龄与身体条件都能跳。关于他们的介绍以这样一句收尾：他们曾经懂得操作、设计与修理电子计算机，但那些机器最后全都坏掉了。一件只有在机器坏掉之后才完全说得通的事，没法用当下的用处去衡量——这正是它至今没有名字的原因。

compudanzas is a project instigated by sejo. The site runs in Spanish and English side by side, dates everything in the Holocene calendar (this year is written 12026), and dedicates all of it to the public domain. Its main line is the qiudanz technique: a movement practice built on the computational transformation of minimalist movement sequences. Only mode 1 has actually been developed, and its vocabulary holds three moves. IN, written as a plus sign, joins the palms away from the trunk and brings them back to it. DEH, written as a minus sign, crosses and uncrosses the forearms. SHOW, written as a period, offers one open palm to the person opposite. The first move in a sequence is the head, the last is the tail. The hinge of the whole thing is this: those three move names are also the names of three operations. DEH means remove the head of the sequence. IN means append whatever move follows it as the new tail. SHOW means perform the entire current sequence, and hand the turn over. A string of moves is therefore simultaneously a dance and a program for rewriting dances.

Clear out the nearest look-alike first. Unplugged computer science is already construct. That pedagogy has been teaching sorting and binary with cards, string and lines of children since the late nineties; it has textbooks, curriculum standards, translations. Its beauty is real, but it is the beauty of construct, because it uses the body to explain the machine, and the measure of success is how faithfully the body reproduces what silicon does. The same goes for live coding and algorave, which by now have a manifesto, a community and an annual conference, and for dance-and-technology, which has its own lineage and toolchain. If the value of qiudanz were "demonstrating a Turing machine with your body," this entry should not exist.

The difference is not in appearance. It is in which layer the chisel lands on. The founding move of computer science is itself an extremely deep chisel: lift the process off the matter that carries it, so that the same computation on an abacus, on relays and on silicon counts as the same computation. That cut worked so well that we no longer register it as a cut. But it left something behind. Everything about a computation that concerns whose body did it, how long it took, and whether the people present agreed that the last move counted was ruled irrelevant. Silicon's superiority is precisely that it deletes this layer most cleanly. compudanzas reinstalls the deleted half and makes it the medium. The qiudanz moves are deliberately not rigidly defined: a move is valid because the people in the room agree it is. How long a sequence can grow depends on the memory and stamina of whoever is holding it. The state of the machine is stored in a person and can slip at any moment. sejo puts the principle bluntly himself — let go of the illusion of control.

The consequences are concrete enough to read off the workshop notes. Across four small online workshops, the hardest operation to learn was DEH, "remove the first element of the sequence"; sejo writes that the way we are used to organising movement makes deleting from the head strangely difficult. A second finding matters more. Because instructions and moves share names, participants instinctively perform the instruction sequence they are given as if it were the movement sequence to dance. He warned them in advance, and then wrote that it is important for the "mistake" to happen, because it reveals the core of the technique. In silicon that confusion is exactly what the fetch–execute boundary exists to destroy, the line the von Neumann architecture polices most carefully. Here it is not a bug, it is the entire pleasure: in his words, one can dance a set of instructions. Then there is the target game — start from a given sequence, hold a target sequence in mind, apply transformations until you arrive. The rules are followed by one more line: it might be even more fun not to arrive directly. A formal system that could have been scored on efficiency alone has efficiency explicitly cancelled.

The remainder is still alive because no name catches it. Unplugged pedagogy is wrong: it is measured by fidelity to the machine, and here the machine is not the target. Live coding is wrong: it presumes a machine executing and a person writing, and here nothing is executing anything. Dance is wrong — participants report over and over that the hard part is unlearning choreographic habit, because flow, phrasing and poetics do nothing inside these rules. Performance art is wrong too: the main output is an online workshop with three to five people in it, released into the public domain, with no gallery and no edition numbers. Even permacomputing does not hold it. viznut's own 2021 update files compudanzas under projects with "visions that detach computing from all utilitarian purposes," which describes a gap rather than supplying a name.

Why seeing it now matters more than later: the naming pressure has started, and it is dated. June 2025, ALGOPOÉTICA in Madrid. September 2025, a workshop at the Alpaca conference on Algorithmic Patterns in the Creative Arts. November 2025, Code as Critique at NIAS in Amsterdam. Each of those venues arrives with a vocabulary ready to absorb this — algorithmic pattern, critical computing, decomputing. Once the absorption completes, the workshop can be taught from a handout, and a handout does not have to carry the risk that the room disagrees about whether the last move counted. Right now the risk is still there. The technical roadmap is also unfinished by its own admission: four modes sized by vocabulary at two, four, eight and sixteen moves, with a question mark after the fourth, and only the first ever built. sejo has also given the technique a provenance. The txiemonks are a group committed to preserving computer science beyond electronics and industrial civilisation, who use qiudanz for folk and post-collapse dances — no props needed, performable by small groups regardless of age or physical capability. Their description ends like this: they once knew how to handle, design and repair electronic computers, but eventually all of them broke down. Something that only makes complete sense after the machines break cannot be measured by present-day usefulness, which is exactly why it still has no name.
