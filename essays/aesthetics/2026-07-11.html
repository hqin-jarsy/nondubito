谷本和弘《雨·绽放》：只设计规则的艺术家

Kazuhiro Tanimoto / Rain Blooms: The Artist Who Designs Only Rules

Han Qin (秦汉)

谷本和弘是一位化学家，同时也是一位生成艺术家。他的《雨·绽放》（Rain Blooms，2026）构建于一套历时两年、由他本人从头设计的原创元胞自动机算法之上。这不是对康威生命游戏（Conway's Game of Life）的变体，也不是对任何经典模型的延伸——而是他在研究材料科学的工作方式中，一步步设计出来的新规则体系。在这套系统中，每个像素单元并不被告知"应当变成什么"。它只遵从局部规则：观察相邻单元的状态，更新自身。多个物种（species）在同一网格上并存，彼此之间存在攻击、同化与漠视的关系；每个单元携带一个活力参数（vitality），活力在冲突与演化中消长。视觉图像由此生成，声音也同样生成——不是事后配上去的配乐，而是从像素的色相、明度、饱和度变化中直接涌现的声音。图像与声音是同一套生成过程的两种输出，在结构上彼此同源，不存在主次之分。

在SAE美学的框架中，余项（余项之美）指的是那些尚未被完整命名、艺术界正在接近却还未"构"定的结构性存在。每一次凿构循环，艺术家以新的凿击打开可能性——而那些被充分命名、被收藏市场消化、被批评话语固定下来的事物，便成为"已构"，不再鲜活，只是过去凿击的沉积物。《雨·绽放》是一个清晰的余项居所。谷本的"凿"是规则本身：多物种的互动关系、活力参数的消长机制、扩展邻域的定义方式。从规则中涌现的一切，是他无法事先知道的余。他自己也写道，最终出现的模式"往往超出艺术家的预期"（often beyond the artist's own expectations）。这种持续的不可预期性，不是失控，而是余项结构鲜活的标志——一套逻辑还在运动中，尚未被任何命名所收束。

更精确地说，《雨·绽放》的余项不只是"意外的图案"，而是一种尚未被完整理论化的算法美学立场。谷本在自己的艺术家陈述中明确区分了ALife（人工生命，自下而上，显式局部规则，小系统，涌现优先）与当前主流"生成AI"（自上而下，黑箱，优化目标，几十亿参数）的根本差异。这一区分，对于理解他的作品所指向的美学立场至关重要。2026年的艺术话语正在将"生成艺术"这个词迅速变成"已构"——大量AI图像生成作品正在用这个词包裹自身。谷本的立场是那个被主流话语遗漏的余项：艺术家只设计规则，从规则中涌现的才是作品，而不是被形塑向某个目标的产品。这不是风格偏好——这是关于意图与结果之间关系的根本性不同。而这一立场，还没有被任何批评话语写成稳定的理论框架。

NEORT++（东京）于2026年5月举办了这件作品的个展，Right Click Save与MASSAGE MAGAZINE在五月末各发表了一篇评论，Art Blocks也将其纳入了策展项目（128个版本）。这些是艺术界开始"构"的迹象——但只是开始。多物种活力元胞自动机作为视听艺术形式的具体逻辑，图像与声音应当共享计算来源这一结构性命题，一套小型确定性规则体系如何生成无限差异这一美学问题——这些都还没有稳定的批评词汇。如果你在现在看它，你看到的是一套逻辑还在向你解释自身的系统，一个尚未被简化为标签的过程。这正是余项之美所要求的：结构上的鲜活性——而那正是命名完成之后你所失去的东西。

Kazuhiro Tanimoto is a chemist who makes generative audiovisual systems. Rain Blooms (2026) is built on an original cellular automaton he spent two years developing from scratch — not a variant of Conway's Game of Life, not a standard Lenia or SmoothLife derivative, but a rule system designed ground-up, shaped by the same research instincts he brings to materials science. The grid is populated by multiple species with explicitly defined relationships: attack, assimilation, indifference. Each cell carries a vitality parameter that rises or falls through these interactions. What Tanimoto designs is not an image — it's a set of local rules. The image is what happens when those rules run. Sound is generated in parallel, not as a soundtrack but as a structural output: changes in each cell's hue, brightness, and saturation drive frequency and amplitude. Image and sound are two faces of the same computational process, neither primary.

In SAE aesthetics, 余项 (remainder) refers to what hasn't yet been fully digested by the art world — work whose internal logic is still growing, still structurally alive, not yet named into stability. In the chisel-construct cycle (凿构循环), each new chisel stroke opens possibility. What gets fully named — absorbed by the collector market, fixed by critical discourse — becomes 已构 (already-construct): no longer alive, just sediment of a past stroke. Rain Blooms is a clear case of the remainder. Tanimoto's chisel is the rule-set itself: the multi-species interaction structure, the vitality parameter mechanics, the extended neighborhood definition. The remainder is everything that runs forward from that — forms "beyond the artist's own expectations," a system that "tends toward sustained instability" without collapsing into either chaos or stasis. This ongoing unpredictability is not failure of control. It is the signature of a logic still in motion, not yet closed by any name.

More precisely, the remainder in Rain Blooms is an aesthetic position the art world is approaching but hasn't fully theorized. Tanimoto makes a careful and explicit distinction between ALife (Artificial Life — bottom-up, explicit local rules, small system, emergent whole) and contemporary generative AI (top-down, black box, optimized toward a target, billions of parameters). This distinction matters enormously for understanding the kind of beauty his work points toward. In 2026, "generative art" as a critical category is rapidly becoming already-construct — swallowed by AI image generation discourse. Tanimoto's work is the remainder: the position that says the artist designs only rules, and that what emerges from those rules is the work — not a product shaped toward a predetermined output. This isn't a stylistic preference. It's a fundamentally different relationship between intention and outcome. And it has no settled critical vocabulary yet.

NEORT++ (Tokyo) showed Rain Blooms in May 2026; Right Click Save and MASSAGE MAGAZINE each published a piece at the end of that month; Art Blocks took it into their curated program with 128 unique editions. These are signs the art world has begun constructing a frame. But "begun" is the operative word. The specific logic of multi-species vitality cellular automata as an audiovisual form — the structural claim that image and sound should share a computational source, the aesthetic proposition that a compact deterministic rule-set can generate indefinite difference — has no stable critical language yet. Watching Rain Blooms now means watching a system that is still explaining itself to you, a process that hasn't been reduced to a label. This is what 余项之美 requires: structural aliveness — which is exactly what you lose once the naming is complete.
