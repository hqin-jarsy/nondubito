Economics

Chisel-Construct Cycle: Economics · 二十三篇 · 七种语言
凿构周期律 · 经济 · 23 Essays · Seven Languages

这个系列用凿构周期律重读经济思想与经济制度的历史，从没有货币的礼物经济，一路追到中本聪与评分社会。

凿构周期律的核心命题：任何秩序都是对某种凿（旧秩序的破裂）的应答构。构有它能做的，也有它做不到的。构做不到的，是它的余项。余项不消灭，只转移，只累积，最终引发下一次凿。

经济这套构，从第一天起就想把人和万物压进同一把可结算的尺子：从库拉圈里名望的分量，到神庙的账簿，到铸币的成色，到复式记账的借贷两栏，到边际效用的最后一单位,到金融衍生品的合成结构。可它每一次想把账做平，都被自己盖不住的余项顶开——不肯被折算的人，洗不干净的礼物，算不清的相关性，救不进系统性风险那把尺子的普通家庭。二十三篇，追着这套构在几千年里，一次次想合上账本，又一次次被余项顶开的历史。

全系列现有中、英、日、法、德、西、韩七种语言版本；每一种语言都按自身读者的表达习惯独立重写。

This series re-reads the history of economic thought and economic institutions through the chisel-construct cycle — from moneyless gift economies to Satoshi Nakamoto and the scored society.

The framework's core claim: every order is a construct built in response to a chisel — the fracture of an earlier order. Constructs have what they can do and what they cannot. What they cannot do generates remainders. Remainders do not disappear; they shift, accumulate, and eventually force the next chisel.

The economy, as a construct, has wanted from its very first day to press people and things into a single settleable scale: from the weight of prestige in a Kula ring, to a temple's ledger, to a coin's purity, to the two columns of double-entry bookkeeping, to the last unit of marginal utility, to the synthetic structure of a derivative. And every time it tries to close its books, its own unabsorbable remainder pries it back open — the person who won't be converted into a number, the gift that won't wash clean, the correlation no model can quite estimate, the ordinary family that never enters the scale of systemic risk. These twenty-three essays follow that construct across several thousand years, closing its ledger again and again, and pried open again and again.

The full series is available in Chinese, English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, and Korean; every edition is independently written for its readers.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · 二十三篇 · 23 Essays · Seven Languages
  • 第 01 篇 Essay 01 of 23
    互惠与馈赠:不结清,是它的运转方式
    Reciprocity and the Gift — Never Settling Was the Point
    礼物经济不是通往货币的前传，而是一套靠不结清运转的关系秩序——库拉圈、夸富宴与债务的谱系里，藏着凿构周期律最早的余项。
    A gift economy is not a prelude to money — it is a relational order that runs on never settling. The Kula ring, the potlatch, and the genealogy of debt hide the chisel-construct cycle's earliest remainder.
  • 第 02 篇 Essay 02 of 23
    神庙账簿与最早的债务豁免:文字,为债而生
    Clay Tablets and Clean Slates — Writing Was Born to Serve a Debt
    最早的文字不是为了写诗，是为了记账。美索不达米亚的神庙账簿把人压进一张债务表，而周期性的清债令，是这套构最早学会的松开余项的办法。
    The earliest writing wasn't for poetry — it was for the books. Mesopotamian temple ledgers pressed people into a table of debt, and the periodic clean-slate decree was the construct's first way of loosening its own remainder.
  • 第 03 篇 Essay 03 of 23
    吕底亚铸币:铸币没有发明价值,它给尺子一副身体
    The Scale Gets a Body — Coinage Didn't Invent Value, It Gave the Remainder a Shape
    价值早就存在，稀缺的从来是让它可以随身携带。吕底亚人打的第一枚琥珀金币，把一把抽象的尺子第一次装进了金属，也让它第一次可以流落到任何人手中。
    Value already existed; what was scarce was making it portable. The first electrum coins struck in Lydia gave an abstract scale a body of metal — and let it fall, for the first time, into anyone's hand.
  • 第 04 篇 Essay 04 of 23
    公平价格与禁高利贷:尺子拒绝称量的那一样东西
    The Just Price and the Usury Ban — The One Thing the Scale Would Not Weigh
    中世纪经院学者一边为公平价格辩护，一边严禁靠时间本身生利。他们守住的不是效率，是一样更古老的东西：不是所有东西都可以被卖。
    Medieval scholastics defended the just price while banning profit made from time itself. What they were guarding was not efficiency but something older: not everything is for sale.
  • 第 05 篇 Essay 05 of 23
    复式记账的发明:不问,账才做得平
    Debit and Credit — Not Asking Was How It Balanced
    复式记账第一次让账本自己证明自己是对的。帕乔利教人做账时留下的几个不必细问的漏口，后来成了这套构闭合自己最省事的办法。
    Double-entry bookkeeping was the first ledger that could prove itself correct. The few things Pacioli taught merchants not to ask about became the construct's cheapest way of closing itself.
  • 第 06 篇 Essay 06 of 23
    宋代交子:金属退场之后,总要有人认账
    Paper and Promise — When the Metal Left, Someone Still Had to Answer for It
    交子是世界上最早的政府纸币，它让货币第一次和金属脱钩。可一张纸要被当真，靠的不是印在纸上的字，是印在纸背后那个不肯写明的信用。
    Jiaozi was the world's first government-issued paper money, the first time currency was cut loose from metal. But a piece of paper is only believed because of a credit standing behind it that the paper itself never dares to name.
  • 第 07 篇 Essay 07 of 23
    大航海与重商主义:先清点,后定价
    The Ledger and the Mountain — First Counted, Then Priced
    重商主义把整个世界读成一本可以清点的账：金银是分数，贸易顺差是加法。可它清点得越彻底，被清点的那些土地和人，就越彻底地变成余项。
    Mercantilism read the whole world as a ledger to be tallied — bullion as the score, a trade surplus as addition. The more completely it counted, the more completely the lands and people being counted turned into remainder.
  • 第 08 篇 Essay 08 of 23
    大西洋奴隶贸易:越是做成了,越是一份供状
    A Price for Every Inch — Success Was the Confession
    这一篇暂停了这个系列一贯的克制。保险单上的估值，货舱图纸上的密度，账本里的一行行数字，把一个人压成一个价，压得越精确，供出的罪状就越清楚。
    This essay suspends the series' usual restraint. An insurance valuation, a hold's stowage diagram, a line in a ledger — the more precisely a person was pressed into a price, the more precisely the price confessed the crime.
  • 第 09 篇 Essay 09 of 23
    斯密与看不见的手:余项,从未被真正请下柜台
    To Be Praised, and to Be Praiseworthy — The Remainder Never Left the Shelf
    看不见的手常被读成一句只讲自利的口号。斯密自己写的另一本书里，早就留着一个不肯被利己心收编的判断者。那个判断者，才是这篇真正要谈的余项。
    The invisible hand is usually read as a slogan for pure self-interest. Smith's other book already kept a judge inside the self that self-interest could never fully recruit — and that judge is this essay's real remainder.
  • 第 10 篇 Essay 10 of 23
    边际革命:价值走进意识,出来时已经是一个数
    Consciousness and the Ledger — Value Went In, a Number Came Out
    杰文斯、门格尔与瓦拉斯几乎同时把价值搬进了个人的意识里，又几乎立刻要求那个意识交出一个所有人都读得懂的数字。搬进去容易，搬出来，从此再没干净过。
    Jevons, Menger, and Walras all but simultaneously moved value inside individual consciousness — then almost immediately demanded that consciousness hand back a number everyone could read. Going in was easy. Coming back out never came clean again.
  • 第 11 篇 Essay 11 of 23
    马克思与劳动价值:余项的记账人,自己的账也平不了
    The Accountant of the Remainder — Even His Ledger Does Not Balance
    马克思是第一个把余项本身当成研究对象的人：商品拜物教，剩余价值，工厂法背后没写明的那道量表。可他留下的转形问题，证明连给余项记账的人，账也做不平。
    Marx was the first to make the remainder itself the object of study — commodity fetishism, surplus value, the unwritten scale behind the Factory Acts. But the transformation problem he left behind proves even the remainder's own accountant cannot balance the books.
  • 第 12 篇 Essay 12 of 23
    无偿劳动:从未被记上账,才是它的运转方式
    Love and Unwaged Work — Never Entering Was the Point
    家务,照护,养育——GDP从不把它们算作生产。这不是统计的失误，是这套构从一开始就靠这样东西撑着：一样必须存在，却永远不许上账的劳动。
    Housework, care, raising children — GDP has never counted them as production. That is not a statistical oversight. The construct has always leaned on exactly this: labor that must exist, and must never enter the books.
  • 第 13 篇 Essay 13 of 23
    公地及其圈占:没有名字的东西,被留在了篱笆外面
    The Commons and Its Enclosure — What Had No Name Was Left Behind
    圈地运动把公地变成了私产，账本也第一次能替土地算出一个明确的主人。可公地上那些从来不需要写进契约的习惯权利，圈完之后，就再也没有地方安放。
    Enclosure turned common land into private property, and for the first time a ledger could name a single owner for a field. But the customary rights that never needed a deed had nowhere left to stand once the fences went up.
  • 第 14 篇 Essay 14 of 23
    金本位:精确到小数点,却在最要紧的地方看不见
    The Scale and the Remainder — Exact to the Decimal, Blind Where It Mattered
    金本位把汇率钉到小数点后好几位，精确得像一门自然科学。可这把尺子最引以为傲的自动调节，恰恰是靠着让某些人失业来完成的。
    The gold standard pinned exchange rates to several decimal places, precise as a natural science. But the automatic adjustment this scale was proudest of ran, in practice, on somebody else's unemployment.
  • 第 15 篇 Essay 15 of 23
    大萧条与凯恩斯:出清,从来不会自己发生
    Effective Demand and the Column Marked Voluntary — Clearing Was Never Automatic
    古典经济学相信市场会自动出清，失业只可能是自愿的。大萧条用千百万人的失业证明了一件事：那道自动清零的机制，从来就没有存在过。
    Classical economics believed markets clear themselves, and unemployment could only be voluntary. The Depression proved, with tens of millions of idle hands, that the self-clearing mechanism was never actually there.
  • 第 16 篇 Essay 16 of 23
    分配性饥荒:粮食还在,名册决定谁能吃到
    The Grain and the Roll — Someone Decided Who Counted
    孟加拉,爱尔兰,大跃进——三场被讲成天灾的饥荒，粮食其实从未真正从世界上消失。真正决定谁饿死的，是一份把谁算进去、谁排除在外的名册。
    Bengal, Ireland, the Great Leap — three famines usually told as natural disaster, in none of which did the grain actually vanish from the world. What decided who starved was a roll that decided who counted and who didn't.
  • 第 17 篇 Essay 17 of 23
    布雷顿森林与一九七一:信用,其实一直都在
    The Chain and the Cover — Credit Was There All Along
    布雷顿森林体系把美元钉在黄金上，又把世界货币钉在美元上。1971年那道关闭黄金窗口的命令，撕掉的与其说是金本位的余温，不如说是一层早已名不副实的封面。
    Bretton Woods pinned the dollar to gold, and the world's currencies to the dollar. The 1971 order that closed the gold window tore away less a lingering gold standard than a cover page that had stopped matching its contents long before.
  • 第 18 篇 Essay 18 of 23
    金融化:账本,开始记录别的账本
    Contracts and Correlations — The Ledger Begins to Record Itself
    标准化的合约,可计算的价格,被拆成好几段的链条——金融化把整个世界的风险都变成了可以打包转让的证券。这一次,账本第一次开始记录起了别的账本。
    Standardized contracts, calculable prices, a chain broken into links — financialization turned the world's risk into securities that could be packaged and passed along. For the first time, the ledger began recording other ledgers.
  • 第 19 篇 Essay 19 of 23
    二〇〇八:接得住的,只是连得紧的那些
    Systemic Risk and the Remainder — Only the Connected Were Caught
    2008年，那把用来决定先救谁的尺子叫系统性风险。它精确地算出了谁连得最紧，却在设计上就没有一栏，用来记一个只连着自己房子和家人的普通人。
    In 2008, the scale used to decide who got rescued first was called systemic risk. It calculated with precision exactly who was tied in tightest — and by design carried no column at all for an ordinary person tied only to a house and a family.
  • 第 20 篇 Essay 20 of 23
    平台与数据经济:可预测,本身就是产品
    Platforms and the Data Economy — Predictability Became the Product
    平台不卖商品,卖的是对你下一步的预测。当一个人的注意力,情绪,乃至下一次心动都被折算成数据,余项这次被逼到了一个前所未有的地方:一个人的内心。
    A platform doesn't sell goods — it sells a prediction of your next move. Once attention, mood, even a coming impulse have all been converted into data, the remainder is finally pushed somewhere it has never been forced before: inside a person's own mind.
  • 第 21 篇 Essay 21 of 23
    评分社会:声誉,也想把账合上
    The Scored Society — Reputation Wants to Close the Books Too
    信用分,好评率,各种排行榜——古老的声誉第一次被压进一个可以实时更新的数字。声誉曾经是余项最后的庇护所之一，如今它自己也想把账合上。
    A credit score, a seller rating, a leaderboard — reputation, that old and informal thing, has for the first time been compressed into a number that updates in real time. Reputation was once one of the remainder's last refuges. Now it wants to close its own books too.
  • 第 22 篇 Essay 22 of 23
    中本聪与加密货币:不必被信任的账本,还是要有人拿去用
    A Ledger No One Has to Honor — Somebody Still Has to Use It
    比特币的野心，是造一本不需要任何人被信任的账本。可一本账再怎么去信任化，最后仍然要有人拿它买东西，仍然要有人决定它值多少——余项从代码里，退回了人群里。
    Bitcoin's ambition was to build a ledger that required no one to be trusted. But however trustless a ledger becomes, someone still has to spend it, and someone still has to decide what it's worth — the remainder retreats from the code back into the crowd.
  • 第 23 篇 · 收束 Essay 23 of 23 · Finale
    声誉与货币的缠绕:一个生来是为了遗忘
    Reputation and Money, Entangled — One Was Built to Forget
    系列的终篇，回到了第一篇的起点。声誉记得一切，货币生来就要遗忘出身——二十三篇之后，这套构依然做不平这本账，因为它从第一天起,就在用一样东西的逻辑,去量另一样东西。
    The series' finale returns to where Essay 1 began. Reputation remembers everything; money was built to forget where it came from. Twenty-three essays later, the construct still cannot balance this ledger, because from day one it has been using one thing's logic to measure the other.
