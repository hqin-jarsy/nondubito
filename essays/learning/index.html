SAE 学习系列

11DD → 15DD 11DD → 15DD Han Qin (秦汉) 双语 · 哲学与心灵 Bilingual · Philosophy of Mind

学习不只一种。Kahneman的快慢系统划分了直觉与理性，但这条线背后还有更多层次——学习会随着主体发展而在不同维度展开。

Learning is not one thing. Kahneman's fast/slow distinction captures something real, but behind that line there are more layers — learning unfolds across different dimensions as the subject develops.

本系列用SAE框架的DD层次重新理解学习：11DD+12DD的记忆库与预测脚本、13DD的审视与否定性、14DD的目的驱动、以及14DD向15DD的跨越——学习理解他者真正独立的目的。四篇连续，每篇可单独阅读。

This series uses the SAE framework's DD layers to reframe learning: the memory library and prediction scripts of 11DD+12DD, the scrutiny and negativity of 13DD, the purpose-driven dimension of 14DD, and the bridge from 14DD to 15DD — learning to recognize the genuinely independent purpose of another. Four papers in sequence, each readable on its own.
