南北朝——接到一起以后，还有哪些事没办完

Northern and Southern Dynasties — The Work Left After Joining Things Together

Han Qin (秦汉)

同一个人的名字，可以同时写在两份名册上。一份说他要种田，一份说他要出征。两份名册都没有错，可谁来种他离开后的田？

制度相接的难处，常常落在这种地方。朝廷把土地、户籍、军队、官职安排进一个体系，并不等于活在里面的人已经有办法同时满足它们。

南北朝的故事，可以从这里重新看。南北并不是各自保管半套文明，等关陇的某个集团把它们拼起来。两边都有知识和武力、官署和家族，也都有人试着把这些东西重新接上。接上以后谁轻松了，谁反而承受了更多，还要继续问。

一、北魏面对的不是一张行政白纸

439年北魏统一北方，是重要的转折，却不是它第一次遇见农业、赋税和官署。《魏书》的相关记载，在这以前就已有迁徙人口、安排农田牛具、征收租调和设置官职的活动。1

因此，不能把故事写成一个只会骑马作战的集团，征服后才第一次发现田地需要管理。游牧本身也需要组织和知识。治理不同的生活方式，困难在具体关系不同，不在一个群体天生简单、另一个天生复杂。

人口登记有时能使被地方强者遮蔽的家庭进入公共视野，也可能使国家的征发更直接。被登记的人得到什么保护、承担什么新义务、遇到错误如何申诉，不能仅凭“中央终于看得见”来判断。

把人从一个大户的名下找出来，未必就是把人交还给他自己。他也可能只是从一种要求下面，被移到另一种要求下面。

二、改革不是一个人一次做完的决定

484年定官俸，485年推行均田，486年设三长，这些措施各有参与者和讨论。冯太后、李安世、李沖以及执行的地方人员，都应当出现在孝文帝改革的故事中，而不只作为一个天才计划的背景。2

均田的“均”，尤其容易使今天的读者误会。露田的还受、桑田的世业、某些条件下的买卖、土地不足时减少授田，规则本来就有所区别。它不是国家把每块土地全部收回，再让每个人实际得到一样多。3

三长把若干家户编成邻、里、党，赋予主持者职责和一定免役。名册变细了，征收和照顾都可能更接近具体的人；主持者也可能拥有新的支配力量。李沖的建议曾遭反对，争论还涉及推行的时机。487年的灾荒记载中，救济有命令，地方落实仍可能失误。3

493年决定迁都洛阳，又是另一组工作。马前的说服场面很醒目，房屋、粮食、种子和道路却不能靠说服同时准备好。元澄、李沖等人的活动和反论，让我们看到迁都既是权力选择，也是会落在许多人日子上的搬迁。4

494至496年间的服饰、朝廷语言、迁居者归葬和改姓规定，有各自时间与范围。不能说一道命令便消除了所有人在日常生活里的差异。精英联姻连接了某些家族，却不自动使边镇军户、佃农或奴婢获得同样的地位。

甚至临时颁行的官制，也曾要求补充阙失。能提出修改不等于修改总能安全通过，但仍不同于“设计完成后再没有修订的可能”。

三、边镇的困难，有人说过

六镇的名字——沃野、怀朔、武川、抚冥、柔玄、怀荒——容易让人想到一道整齐的防线。那里的人却有家庭、职位和前途，不只是地图上的六个据点。

元深的上疏对比边镇旧日与当时的处境，是为了推动改变。它提示地位、用人和待遇的困难，却不是每个军人经历完全相同的一份普查。李崇也建议改镇为州；这位李崇，不是早年推动三长的李沖。5

没有人提过办法、办法没有被接受、后来才办而局势已经恶化，是不同的失败。把它们全叫“余项终于反扑”，会把本来可以讨论的岔路抹掉。

523年的柔然侵扰与救助，524年沃野起事，此后的战争和重组，又各有过程。534年东魏、535年西魏的建立，不是起事当天自动落成的两个结果。孝文帝早在499年去世，更不能说成被这场起义亲自推翻的改革者。5

改革确实可能使一些人的位置提高、另一些人的需要越来越难被看见。但这不意味着有一团数量不变的矛盾，只从民族口袋搬进地理口袋。粮食、身份、任用和地方防御，可能互相影响，也需要分别处理。

最令人惋惜的，不是抽象规律又兑现了一次，而是有人提出过具体问题，却没能在还有余地的时候改变它。

四、会写文章的人，也可能在办理县务

南朝不能简单排成四次寒门武人取代文弱贵族。萧衍有家族官职背景，也有文学、军政经历。把他只放进“外来的低级军官”一格，连人物本身都容不下。6

刘勰更有意思。我们记得《文心雕龙》，容易忘记他曾在僧祐处从事经籍工作，后来又处理县务、文书和礼制问题。文字的细密与日常工作的能力，并不是两条越走越远的路。7

文化可以被用来建立排斥，也可以保存实际知识。要批评前者，不需要宣布写诗、读书越精致的人就越不会管理粮食和军队。

侯景之乱尤其不能用“一个带着几千人的外来者，轻轻推倒了自然老死的门阀”来带过。侯景此前已进入梁的政治关系。548年围攻台城，549年城陷，期间有救援、有不成功的协调、有缺粮与谈判，兵力也发生变化。最初的人数不是整个战争的投入。8

552年侯景败亡，554年江陵又遭攻陷，是不同的灾难。杀戮、劫掠、强制迁移是真正的行动，不能改名为一种制度的自然死亡。谢哲等人跨越梁陈任职，说明仍有有限延续；这也不会让其他人失去的家园重新回来。

承认幸存者，不必否认毁灭。承认毁灭，也不必说所有人、所有知识都在同一天消失。

五、关陇的联系，不只是一张联姻图

西魏、北周的组织变化，为隋唐提供了许多可用资源。若只说“胡汉融合，兵农合一”，容易让正在变化的工作看起来已经全部完成。

苏绰留下的文书、会计与治理办法，涉及怎样记录、怎样核算、怎样劝农、任官和处理赋役，也保留严刑与等级教化。它不是一份只要执行就处处温和有效的说明书，却把许多判断变成了别人可以继续使用的程序。9

543年的招募、554年的姓氏编属、556年的六官，与隋590年的军人州县登记，并非同一时刻的成套制度。尤其不能把后来“平时种田，战时当兵”的概括，全部倒回宇文泰时期。10

一名军人可以被编入地方户籍，同时仍受军事指挥。可是他什么时候离家，田地由谁照料，家里还需承担哪些征收，必须在具体安排中解决。两张名册终于接上，不等于两份要求不再冲突。

后世所谓关陇集团，可以帮助我们寻找婚姻、同僚和军政联系，却不等于一个有完整成员名册、只凭能力入会的统一组织。独孤信诸女的婚姻影响深远，他自己又被逼自尽，正说明关系重要，却不是一张安全保证书。11

南方知识也不只是由北方保存的汉文化间接转来。王褒、庾信因战争和出使等不同经过留在北方，直接参与了这里的文字和官署生活。知识确实流动，流动者却未必能够自由选择去处。

六、统一的日期之后，生活还要继续安排

南北朝留下许多可以继续使用的办法。梁505年的教育安排，包括供食、策试和授官，但不是历史上第一次考试，也不是成熟科举制度已经一次建成。谁能学习、谁被举荐、考过以后怎样任职，仍是不同的问题。12

儒释道的交往也不能直接写成已经获得稳定保障的多元共存。北周574年禁断佛道，就是一道明显的限制。获准参与辩论，不等于实践信仰的权利从此不可撤回。12

南方开发增加了可用资源，但没有同一口径的南北生产总量，便不宜据此宣布南方已经全面超过北方。精英的混合出身，也不会天然使他们更平等地对待所有外人。一场婚姻能解决两家的关系，不能替没有参加婚姻的人解除压迫。

589年隋灭陈，朝廷还要组织人员商议礼乐；590年军民编籍继续调整，南方又有起事。版图合成一块，与一户人家知道明天该交什么、向谁求助，是两种不同的完成。13

这才是“汇合”值得讨论的地方。过去留下的知识和制度，减少了某些从头摸索的工作，却不替后来的人完成全部选择。

同一个名字写进两份名册以后，总得有人停下来问：我们加在他身上的事情，放在一起，他还有办法生活吗？

The same name can appear on two lists. One says a man must cultivate his land. The other says he must leave to fight. Neither entry need contain a clerical error. Who will cultivate the land while he is away?

This is where institutional coordination becomes a question about a life. Connecting land, registration, military service, and officeholding does not ensure that people can meet all the demands being connected.

China's Northern and Southern Dynasties were not two halves of a civilization waiting for someone to assemble them. Both contained knowledge and force, offices and families. People tried to reconnect these resources. We still need to ask who gained room to live and who received another obligation.

I. Northern Wei did not begin with a blank administrative page

Northern Wei's unification of the north in 439 was a major change, not its first encounter with agriculture, taxation, or civil office. The Weishu describes earlier population movements, agricultural arrangements, draft animals, levies, and appointments.1

The story therefore cannot begin with people who knew only riding and fighting suddenly discovering that fields required management. Pastoral life also required organization and expertise. Different forms of livelihood posed different practical problems; they did not divide populations into inherently simple and sophisticated minds.

Household registration could bring people concealed within a powerful family's dependencies into official view. It could also make their labor and resources more directly available for requisition. What protection they received, what new duties they acquired, and how they could challenge an error remained separate questions.

Removing a household from one patron's control did not necessarily return its members to themselves. It might transfer them from one set of demands into another.

II. Reform was not one person's finished design

Official salaries in 484, the equal-field regulations in 485, and the three-headmen system in 486 were separate measures with participants and disputes. Empress Dowager Feng, Li Anshi, Li Chong, and local officials belong in the account alongside Emperor Xiaowen, rather than appearing as scenery around a solitary designer.2

“Equal-field” can itself mislead. The regulations distinguished land subject to return and reallocation from hereditary mulberry land. They allowed some transactions under particular conditions and smaller allocations where land was scarce. The name did not mean that every piece of property became state-owned or every person actually received an identical area.3

The three-headmen arrangement grouped households into successive local units, giving their heads responsibilities and exemptions. More detailed registration could make collection and assistance more specific. It could also create new opportunities for domination. Li Chong's proposal met objections, including questions about timing. The record of disaster in 487 shows relief orders alongside failures of local implementation.3

The decision to move the capital to Luoyang in 493 required another set of tasks. A dramatic exchange on the road could help secure agreement, but houses, supplies, seed, and transport did not all materialize with it. Yuan Cheng's and Li Chong's activities and objections show a relocation affecting ordinary arrangements as well as the balance at court.4

Rules concerning dress, court language, burial for relocated people, and surnames followed at different points between 494 and 496. Their scope varied. They did not erase everyone's daily differences in one act. Elite marriages connected particular houses without automatically extending equal standing to border households, tenants, or enslaved people.

A provisional organization of offices also invited additions to correct omissions. That invitation did not guarantee that every objection would be safe or effective. It nevertheless differs from a design closed permanently to revision.

III. People had reported the border's difficulties

The six northern garrisons—Woye, Huaishuo, Wuchuan, Fuming, Rouxuan, and Huaihuang—look orderly on a map. Their inhabitants had households, careers, and expectations. They were not simply six defensive markers.

Yuan Shen's petition contrasted earlier and current conditions to argue for change. It illuminates problems of status and appointment without constituting a survey proving that every soldier had the same experience. Li Chong also proposed converting garrisons into regular administrative provinces. This was Li Chong 李崇, not the earlier advocate of the headmen system, Li Chong 李沖.5

No proposal, a rejected proposal, and implementation delayed until conditions deteriorate are different failures. Calling all three a displaced pressure finally exploding removes paths that people actually tried to open.

Rouran attacks and assistance in 523, the Woye rising in 524, and the subsequent wars had distinct sequences. The eastern Wei court established in 534 and the western court in 535 were not ready-made consequences appearing on the first day of revolt. Xiaowen had died in 499, long before the rising.5

Reform could improve some people's standing while making others' requirements harder to see. That does not demonstrate an unchanged quantity of conflict moving from an ethnic compartment into a geographical one. Food, status, appointments, and defense interacted, but each required practical attention.

What matters is not simply that a pattern repeated. Someone had identified a difficulty, and an opportunity to alter it passed without adequate action.

IV. Literary accomplishment did not exclude practical work

The southern dynasties cannot be reduced to four successive victories of low-born soldiers over helpless cultured aristocrats. Xiao Yan, founder of Liang, had family connections to office and a career combining literary, military, and administrative activity. The category “outsider of humble military rank” does not contain him.6

Liu Xie offers another corrective. He is remembered for the literary treatise The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons. He also worked with Sengyou's collection of texts and later dealt with county administration, documents, and ritual proposals. Attention to language and competence in ordinary affairs were not necessarily diverging pursuits.7

Culture could enforce exclusion. It could also preserve practical knowledge. Criticizing the former does not require claiming that refined readers and writers inevitably stopped understanding armies, grain, or administration.

Hou Jing's rebellion is especially ill served by the image of a few thousand outsiders giving a naturally dying aristocracy one final nudge. Hou Jing was already connected to Liang before rebelling. The siege began in 548; the palace enclosure fell in 549. Relief forces existed, coordination failed, food ran short, negotiations took place, and the size of the forces changed. Initial troop figures cannot stand for the whole war.8

Hou Jing's defeat in 552 and the fall of Jiangling in 554 were further, distinct events. Killings, looting, and forced movements were actions, not the natural death of an institutional species. Xie Zhe's career across Liang and Chen indicates limited continuity; it does not return other people's homes.7

Acknowledging survivors need not minimize destruction. Acknowledging destruction need not declare that every person and every skill disappeared together.

V. The Guanlong connections were more than a marriage chart

Changes under Western Wei and Northern Zhou supplied later rulers with usable resources. The phrases “ethnic integration” and “farmer-soldiers” can make work still in progress sound complete.

Su Chuo's procedures concerned documents, accounting, agriculture, appointments, justice, and levies. They also included severe punishment and hierarchical instruction. They were not a uniformly gentle manual whose mere adoption guaranteed success. They did make certain judgments and routines available for other officials to continue using.9

Recruitment in 543, military surname affiliations in 554, the six great offices in 556, and military households' local registration under Sui in 590 belong to different stages. A later description of men farming in peacetime and fighting when required cannot simply be projected backward onto Yuwen Tai's entire system.10

A soldier could enter civilian registers while remaining under military command. When he left home, who tended his land? What other dues did the household owe? Joining the records did not settle the demands those records made. Coordination on paper could coexist with an impossible workload in a household.

The later term “Guanlong group,” referring to western elite connections, helps identify marriages, offices, and military associations. It should not become a single organization with a complete membership list and admission based solely on merit. Dugu Xin's daughters made influential marriages; Dugu Xin himself was compelled to die. Connections mattered without guaranteeing safety.11

Southern knowledge also reached the north directly. Wang Bao and Yu Xin remained there through different circumstances involving conquest and diplomatic service. They participated in northern literary and official life. Knowledge moved, but its bearers did not necessarily choose their destination freely.

VI. After the date of unification

The period left later generations many usable arrangements. Liang's educational provision of 505 included support, examinations, and routes into office. It was not the invention of every form of examination, nor the complete later civil-service system born in one year. Access to learning, recommendation, testing, and appointment remained different thresholds.12

Interaction among Confucian, Buddhist, and Daoist traditions likewise did not amount to secure pluralism. Northern Zhou's suppression of Buddhist and Daoist institutions in 574 supplies an obvious limit. Permission to participate in a debate did not guarantee continued freedom of religious practice.12

Development in the south increased available resources, but the sources used here do not supply comparable totals proving that southern output had overtaken the north. Mixed ancestry among elites did not inherently make them more inclusive toward everybody else. A marriage could alter two families' relationship without freeing people excluded from that bargain.

After Sui conquered Chen in 589, specialists still had to deliberate over ritual and music. Registration changed again in 590, and the south experienced uprisings. A unified map and a household knowing what it owed tomorrow, or where it could seek help, were different accomplishments.13

This is what makes convergence worth examining. Inherited procedures could spare later people some work of invention. They did not settle every decision about purposes, burdens, and the possibility of objection.

After placing the same name on two lists, someone still had to ask whether the person between them could go on living under their combined demands.
