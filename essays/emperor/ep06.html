战国——胜出以后，还剩什么

The Warring States — What Victory Leaves Unsettled

Han Qin (秦汉)

地图上少了一个国名，那里的人并不会跟着消失。田还要种，水渠还要修，出征者的家里还有人在等。胜者接收了土地，也接收了土地上的难题。

我们知道战国的结局：秦在前221年完成对六国的征服。这很容易让人倒着读历史，给每个败者分配一种缺陷，再把秦的做法说成唯一答案。但统一六国，和回答六国居民此后的所有问题，显然不是同一件事。

前453年智氏败亡、前403年周王承认韩赵魏为诸侯、前386年承认田和为齐侯，是不同阶段的变化。兵力、实际控制与被承认的名位，没有在同一天全部换手。那些已经拥有实力的人仍来求周王承认，也说明旧秩序还在被使用，只是它能办成的事变了。1

七个大国，连同没有挤进“七雄”名单的小国，各自寻找新的组织办法。这不是七份写好的方案同时开考。人会流动，政策会调整，结盟的人明年可能兵戎相见。所谓淘汰赛，是独立权力中心越来越少；它不是给活在其中的人打分。

一、战争先走进了家里

一个人离家当兵，别人就要接过他的农活。军粮要运，衣服要织，家里病了的人仍需照料。出征的人数越大，这些看不见的接替就越重要。战争在两军相遇以前，已经改变了许多家庭的生活。

春秋并非没有残酷，战国也不是某一天突然取消了所有规矩。泓之战中，宋襄公不肯攻击尚未列阵的敌人，子鱼当场就反对他的理由；同一时代的记述里，还有鄫子被用为牺牲的事。一个人的辩词，不能代表整个时代共同遵守的底线。2

到了战国，兼并土地、控制人口、持续征发变得更加紧密。失败不只意味着承认对方是霸主，还可能失去独立统治。但灭掉一个王国，与杀死已经放下武器的人，仍是不同的决定，不能让“竞争激烈”替所有暴力免责。

《史记》写长平降者四十万，又写前后斩首俘虏四十五万。这是两种统计范围的传世记载，不是今天清点过的同一批遇难者。数字巨大，很容易遮住人：每个被计入的人都来自某处，也在某处留下了尚未完成的生活。3

国家能力于是伸向田地、户籍、税收、道路和仓库。它要知道哪里可以征人，哪里可以征粮。记录让组织成为可能，也可能让没有进入记录的损失越来越难说出口。账上运走一车粮，是一笔完成的任务；家里少了种子，却是明年还要面对的事。

二、七国不是七种固定性格

魏最早的一批改革故事，常被缩成李悝变法四个字。《汉书》追述他的农业办法时，却认真计算一家人的收入和支出：吃穿之外，还有疾病、丧葬和祭祀。丰年粮价太低，农民也会受损；歉年太高，买粮的人活不下去。平籴讨论的正是这两头。农业强国不是只把产量往上加。4

后来有关《法经》的记载，说明法律整理如何被后人理解；我们没有一部可直接核对的李悝完整原本。吴起、商鞅、孙膑从魏走向别国的经历，也值得读，却不能据此替魏国算出一个“改革只完成一半”的比例。

楚悼王支持吴起削减贵族利益，王一死，反对者杀了吴起。但故事没有在箭射出时结束：继王追惩攻击王尸的人，《史记》记受到牵连的有七十余家。改革者死亡是真实的政治断裂，却不等于每一条改革都同时撤销、反对者毫发无损地回到原处。5

齐的稷下让学者得到住处、待遇和论说的机会。赞助能给思想时间，也把思想放在与权力相处的关系里，不能直接当作现代大学的自治。齐还扩张、征战，后来遭联军重创，莒与即墨坚守，田单又收复失地。它不是一间只会挣钱、不会组织战争的书斋。6

赵武灵王推行胡服骑射，不只换衣服和兵器。他需要说服王族、改变习惯，让训练与供养接得上。赵奢任田部吏、处理税务的记载，也提醒我们，赵国并非只有勇将、没有行政。一次补给危机不能证明此前完全没有补给组织。7

韩国的申不害重视任官与责实。《韩非子·定法》批评韩的地方，恰恰包括晋国旧法与韩国新法并行、先后命令不一致，臣下得以选择有利的规定。问题不是“没有制度”，而是不同制度的接口可以被利用。8

燕昭王与乐毅的合作，同样不只是明君发现贤臣。乐毅从别处来到燕国，还要组织联盟、指挥远征；换了君主，指挥关系随之改变。把这些过程读出来，才知道人才为什么能办事，又为什么办不下去。最终兼并六国时，韩在前230年先亡；次序本身并不是一张制度能力排行榜。69

三、商鞅把哪些事情接在了一起

商鞅来自卫，曾在魏任事，后经景监引见进入秦。三个国家的名字已经说明，秦的改革并非与外界无关的独创。秦孝公提供支持，商鞅推进变化，但变化也不是一纸命令就全部装好。10

先看土地。调整田界、征取和土地权利，改变了人们怎样耕种、怎样同国家发生关系。它不能被直接等同于现代完整的私有产权；对农户来说，获得某些经营机会与承担更直接的征收，可以同时发生。

再看军功。战场表现成为获得身份和利益的重要道路，出身不再独占所有机会。但皇位仍世袭，太子犯法时，处罚落在其师傅身上；告奸、特殊动员也可能与奖爵相连。不是所有人、所有职位都被放进同一把尺里。《定法》甚至问：斩首有功，为什么就证明会做另一项工作？医者、工匠、官吏，各有需要学会的事。810

户籍与连坐把家庭重新编进国家责任网。邻居之间的关系不再只是借工具、帮农忙，也包含告发与被牵连的危险。可这不等于官吏从此可以消失。登记、核实、追查、处罚都要人做；有表格，并不保证表格是真的。

农战导向把耕织与军事放到优先位置。留在家中纺织、照料和接替农活的人，也是动员得以持续的条件。社会不曾真的只剩两种人。较后秦帝国里乌氏倮、巴寡妇清的商业活动，便限制了“一切工商都被消灭”的说法；它们也不能倒推成商鞅时代的一般优商政策。11

最后是尺度。商鞅方升上有前344年的铭文，又有统一后前221年加刻的诏文。同一件器物让人看见，建立标准与重新确认标准，是持续的工作。统一容量使跨地征收和运输更容易核对，却不能告诉我们每户交出同量粮食后，还剩下怎样的生活。12

这几组变化彼此支持，才形成了秦强大的动员能力。商鞅死后，许多安排继续运转，说明它们已进入利益、职务与日常行为。不过，继续运转不等于此后永远不可更改。改变的代价越大，越需要问谁承担，而不是提前宣布任何改变都不可能。

四、等待不是后来人才想到的

长平之战中，廉颇曾坚壁不战。更早，李牧在赵国北边长期采取谨慎守御，对手是匈奴，不是秦；秦自己的王翦伐楚，也曾坚壁休士、等待机会。古人并不缺少“先不接战”的想象力。37

困难在另一处：知道可以等，和能够继续等，是两回事。

军中粮食从哪里来？边地居民能承受多久？宫廷是否信任统帅？盟友会不会先退出？守将每多等一天，可能都要向远处的人解释一天。等待不是把时间放在那里，它要由粮食、空间、承诺与许多人的劳动支撑。

秦也不只有冲锋。邯郸受挫、战争间歇、郑国渠的续建，都显示它有调整与长远投入。秦赢了，不能证明它没有弱点；六国输了，也不能证明他们从未拥有正确选项。选择能否持续实施，需要条件，而条件会被交战双方不断改变。313

所以，对秦胜出的解释不能只剩“最彻底的战争机器”。地理、农业、行政积累、军事选择与联盟分裂，都进入结果。胜利验证了一些能力，没有给这个国家以后的一切行动发合格证。

五、思想也在寻找能说话的地方

同一时期，有人在问怎样获胜，有人在问为什么普通人必须承受这些要求。

孟子把没有稳定生计的人与刑罚连起来：先让人无以为生，再把越界的人抓起来，并不是治理的成功。荀子从欲望、物资与争夺谈礼，试图说明分配与界限如何使共同生活持续。两者不只提供好听的道德名词，也都在讨论具体的人怎样生活。14

法与术的讨论关心规则、职务和实际表现怎样接合；《老子》则把过密干预、征取与民生放在一起追问。墨子的兼爱不是要求每个人拥有数量相同的感情，而是让别人的家、国与损失进入自己的考虑。《公输》中的反战，也有说理、攻守演示和组织防御；光把原则说出来还不够。墨家对共同标准与向上规谏的安排，又留下谁能纠正上位者的问题。814

兵学关注费用、时机与地势，纵横之士要处理盟约和反复变化的承诺。不同宫廷给人另寻去处的可能，却不是所有人的言论都因此安全。秦也吸纳外来者，吕不韦组织宾客著书；前237年的逐客令和李斯上书使它撤回，说明开放与排斥要由人争取，不是某个国名天然保证的品格。915

前221年的统一、前213年的书籍禁令、前212年的处刑，并非同一天按下的一个开关。独立赞助中心减少，与全部思想瞬间消失，不是一回事。越是在权力收拢的时候，越要问：谁还能引用另一种理由，向正在作决定的人说不？

六、最后一个对手消失之后

从战国带到下一篇的，有四个问题。

第一，成效都有它所回答的任务。能击败敌军，不等于能处理居民的每一种需要。第二，知识与人会跨境流动；胜者的一部分力量，可能来自它后来消灭的那些中心。第三，改革互相连接以后，修改一项便会牵动别项；这使调整需要更周到，不使调整从此多余。第四，对手被征服了，原先分散在不同地方的生活要求并没有消失，只是换了一个受理者。

这些尚未被安排接住、仍在起作用的要求，就是本系列追问的余项。它们不是一笔已经测出、在国与国之间搬运的固定总额。

秦统一之后，南北军事行动仍在继续，不能把它的处境写成“无仗可打”。更切近的问题是：征服、边防、工程与日常生活一起向同一套行政提出要求时，哪里可以报告做不到，哪里允许改一改？地图涂成一种颜色之后，这些地方反而更重要了。

A border disappears from a map. The work on either side does not. Fields still need tending, canals need repairs, and someone has to care for the people who cannot march with an army. A conqueror acquires territory without acquiring ready answers to everything happening there.

We know how China's Warring States period ends: Qin completes its conquest of six major rival kingdoms in 221 BCE. Knowing the ending makes it tempting to assign each loser a fatal defect and call the winner the answer. But an army's success and a society's capacity to live under its conqueror are different questions.

Even the beginning resists a single date. The defeat of the Zhi lineage in 453, the Zhou king's recognition of Han, Zhao, and Wei in 403, and his recognition of Tian He as ruler of Qi in 386 were separate developments. Military control and recognized standing did not change hands in one transaction. Powerful men still sought recognition from a weakened king. The old forms continued to do some work, though not the work they once had done.1

Seven large states, alongside smaller ones, tried changing arrangements of land, service, authority, and reward. They were not seven finished constitutions entering a controlled experiment. People moved between them. Alliances shifted. A policy could outlive its sponsor or change while retaining its name.

I. Before the army reaches the battlefield

When a farmer leaves to serve, somebody else must take up the work. Cloth must be woven, grain transported, children fed. Mobilization reaches into a household long before two armies meet. Its scale depends not only on how many people can be called out, but on how long those remaining can keep life going.

Earlier warfare was not uniformly restrained. At the battle of Hong, Duke Xiang of Song defended his refusal to strike before his enemies were ready; Ziyu disputed that judgment. The same period's accounts include the use of the ruler of Zeng as a sacrificial victim. One nobleman's defense of a convention cannot establish the moral practice of an age.2

In the Warring States, territorial annexation and the sustained mobilization of populations became increasingly connected. Defeat could end a ruling house's independent power. Yet taking a kingdom and killing surrendered soldiers remained different decisions. Competitive pressure does not relieve people of responsibility for choosing the latter.

The Shiji gives four hundred thousand for the surrendered men at Changping and four hundred and fifty thousand for a broader total of captives and casualties. These are transmitted figures with different scopes, not a modern count of one identical group. Their enormity can conceal what they are meant to convey: people taken from places where other lives still depended on them.3

Registers and measures helped authorities organize resources across distance. They could also make an unrecorded loss hard to explain. A cartload of grain delivered is a completed item on a ledger. Seed missing from a household is a problem that may not become visible until the next harvest.

II. Seven states, more than seven ways of changing

The reformer Li Kui is often introduced through agricultural improvement and law. The Hanshu's later account makes his agricultural problem more intimate: a household has food and clothing expenses, but also illness, funerals, and other obligations. Low grain prices can ruin producers; high prices can ruin buyers. Stabilizing purchases addressed both ends. Increasing output was not the whole task.4

Later legal histories also associate Li Kui with a law code, but we do not possess his complete original for direct examination. The departures of Wu Qi, Shang Yang, and Sun Bin from Wei are important stories. They do not supply a numerical measure of how thoroughly Wei had reformed.

In Chu, King Dao supported Wu Qi's attack on aristocratic privileges. After the king died, opponents killed the reformer. Yet the account continues: the succeeding ruler punished those involved in the attack on the royal corpse, with more than seventy families implicated. A sponsor's death mattered enormously. It does not follow that every reform vanished while every opponent returned unscathed to the old arrangement.5

Qi's Jixia establishment offered scholars support and opportunities to argue. Patronage gave thought time; it also placed thinkers in relationships with rulers. It was not a modern guarantee of academic autonomy. Nor was Qi merely wealthy and scholarly. It expanded, suffered a devastating coalition attack, held out at Ju and Jimo, and recovered territory under Tian Dan. Its story includes military organization, not its complete absence.6

King Wuling's adoption of different clothing and mounted archery in Zhao required more than buying equipment. Habits, aristocratic acceptance, training, and supply had to change together. Zhao She's earlier work in taxation likewise prevents us from describing Zhao as a collection of brave soldiers without administration. Failure in one extended campaign does not demonstrate that no supply organization had existed.7

In Han, Shen Buhai's concern with offices and performance was not simply a ruler's private psychological trick. The Han Feizi criticizes conflicting old and new laws, and earlier and later commands, which officials could selectively exploit. The problem was partly the interaction of rules, not their total absence.8

Yan's recruitment of Yue Yi also involved migration, coalition building, distant command, and a change of ruler. “A good king found a good general” leaves out the relationships that allowed the general to act. In the final conquests, Han fell first, in 230 BCE. The sequence is not a league table of entire societies.69

III. What Shang Yang connected

Shang Yang came from the small state of Wey, served in the larger state of Wei, and entered Qin through an introduction by Jing Jian. The similar names can obscure the point: Qin's reformer had already crossed political boundaries. His work drew on a world that Qin would eventually conquer.10

Consider five connections. First, changes in fields, boundaries, and extraction altered how cultivators dealt with authority. These cannot simply be equated with a complete modern system of private property. Greater opportunities for some households could coexist with more direct demands from the state.

Second, military achievement opened routes to rank and reward. Birth no longer monopolized every opportunity. It did not cease to matter: kingship remained hereditary, and the crown prince's violation of the law was answered by punishing his tutors. Rewards were not all produced by one battlefield calculation. The Han Feizi itself asks why success at taking heads should qualify someone for quite another occupation. A physician, artisan, and official have different work to learn.810

Third, household registration and collective liability reorganized local relationships. A neighbor could be a source of help and also a potential informer. None of this removed the need for administrators. Records had to be made, checked, investigated, and acted upon. A form does not verify itself.

Fourth, agricultural and military priorities connected household production to campaigning. Weaving, care, and the replacement of absent workers mattered to that connection. Society never literally consisted only of farmers and soldiers. The later imperial accounts of the traders Wushi Luo and the widow Qing of Ba limit claims that all commerce had disappeared. They do not establish a uniform pro-commercial policy in Shang Yang's earlier period.11

Finally, common measures made claims comparable across distance. The vessel known as Shang Yang's square sheng bears an inscription dated to 344 BCE and an additional imperial inscription from 221. A standard was established, then reaffirmed. Its continuing use required work. Measuring the same amount of grain in two places still did not tell an official what each household would have left after surrendering it.12

These connections contributed to Qin's formidable mobilizing capacity. Many arrangements survived Shang Yang, showing that they had entered interests, offices, and ordinary practice. Their survival did not make every rule irreversible. It made alteration consequential: changing one demand could disturb the rewards and responsibilities connected to it.

IV. Waiting was already an available idea

At Changping, Lian Po defended fortified positions rather than accepting the battle Qin wanted. On Zhao's northern frontier, Li Mu had used prolonged caution against the Xiongnu; those encounters must not be confused with his later resistance to Qin. Qin's own general Wang Jian also waited behind defenses during his campaign against Chu.37

The ancient world did not need a future strategist to invent delay. The question is how someone who knows the value of waiting obtains permission and resources to continue.

Who supplies the army? How much land can be yielded? How long will allies remain? Will the court still trust a commander whose achievement consists in a battle not yet fought? Every additional day may have to be explained to someone far away. Time is sustained by grain, space, promises, and labor.

Qin, too, could suffer reverses, pause, and invest in future capacity. The setback at Handan and continued work on the Zhengguo Canal complicate the image of a machine capable only of charging forward. Recognizing those capacities does not make Qin invulnerable. It makes the comparison more useful.313

Geography, agriculture, administrative accumulation, military decisions, and the division of opponents all entered the outcome. Qin's victory demonstrated real abilities. It did not certify those abilities as adequate to every task that would follow.

V. Where could another argument be heard?

While states asked how to win, thinkers asked what their demands did to the people sustaining them.

Mencius connected punishment to livelihood: depriving people of a stable means of living and then catching them when they transgress is not a successful way to govern. Xunzi approached ritual through desire, limited resources, and the need for distinctions that permit shared life. Neither argument is exhausted by attaching the label “morality” to it.14

Discussions of law and administrative technique asked how rules, offices, and actual performance could be brought together. The Laozi questioned intrusive government and excessive extraction. Mohist concern for other households and states did not require identical emotional quantities for everyone. It required taking their losses into account. In the Gongshu story, resistance to an attack involves persuasion, a demonstration of attack and defense, and organized preparation. A good principle still needs people able to act on it. Mohist proposals for shared standards and remonstrance also leave questions about correcting those above.814

Military thinkers considered cost, timing, and terrain. Diplomats worked with promises that could fail between their making and their fulfillment. Several courts created opportunities to seek another patron, but did not make speech safe for everyone. Qin itself attracted outsiders; Lü Buwei supported a major compilation. The expulsion order of 237 and Li Si's successful argument against it show that inclusion was contested rather than guaranteed by a state's permanent character.915

Unification in 221, restrictions on books in 213, and a separate episode of executions in 212 were distinct events. Fewer independent centers of patronage did not mean every alternative thought vanished overnight. It did mean that where one could bring another argument became an increasingly urgent question.

VI. After the last rival

Four questions travel from this period into the Qin empire. What task has a success actually answered? How much of a state's knowledge has arrived from elsewhere? Which obligations must be reconsidered when linked institutions change? And who receives the demands that previously went to several independent centers?

The series calls those unresolved but still active demands a remainder. This is not a measured, fixed quantity being transferred between states. It is a way to keep attention on what a successful arrangement has not settled.

Qin continued military operations north and south after unification. Its new predicament cannot be explained by claiming there were no wars left to fight. Conquest, frontier defense, construction, and ordinary life now made overlapping demands on the same administration. Where could someone report that a demand could not be met? Where could a decision be revised?

Those places mattered more, not less, once the map had become a single color.
