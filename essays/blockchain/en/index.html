The Blockchain Cycle

The ledger keeps settling. What counts as settled remains disputed.

Twenty-one essays follow blockchain from pizza, mining, and exchanges to stablecoins, bridges, Layer 2, and real-world assets. Each asks what a system records with new precision—and which costs, responsibilities, and judgments remain with people.

HOW TO READ

Not a technical chronicle, but a boundary that keeps moving.

Every essay begins with a verifiable event: a trade, failure, law, liquidation, or migration. The question is not merely whether the technology followed its rule, but what that rule managed to state and whose experience it left off the ledger.

Read in order to see the same cycle recur across sixteen years, or enter at any familiar episode. The English edition is independently rewritten for readers who do not need every documentary detail on the first pass.
