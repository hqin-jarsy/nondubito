Essay 08 of 12

防范之梯

The Ladder of Precautions

Han Qin (秦汉)

读一个否认者的证词

这一节要读一个人的证词：一个否认别人能说不的人，他自己的动作怎样替他作证。奴隶主不以奴隶为目的，却要防奴隶逃跑、起义；他不必防牲口起义。纳粹要把犹太人骗进毒气室，告诉他们那是浴室。

防范的样式，读得出否认者自己预设了对方是什么。没有人对一件物撒谎，没有人禁止一件物识字，也没有人为了防人收留一件物去立法。每一样这样的措施，都冲着一份否认者嘴上说不存在的能力。

这些例子——逃奴法与路条，禁识字，浴室之谎，屠宰场的弯道——是这部书举的；奴隶主一例权力论也用过，读法不同，这部书把分歧照录，不抹平。

五级

关栏。防的是不可控的余项；对牲畜也要这样防。禁识字与灌输。对付的是对方的自我，已经有的，或者正要成形的。这一级要分开看：禁识字是一道公开的禁令，照价值论的分法属于锁定，不属于殖民；灌输，权利论判为侵。

逃奴法与路条。他的“不”可以向第三个人喊出来，可以被接住；制度禁止的，正是第三个人的回应。动物之间也有示警，同伴也会应和；这一级读出的不是有没有信号，是那一声“不”能被第三个人听作一个不、接过去、站到他那一边。防起义：被压迫的人之间能相认，能合力。骗入死地：直到杀戮，仍须绕过他的理解。

读数是：梯子的每一级，读出他的动作取用了哪一样回应——能懂，能说，能找人，能与别人合力；最上一级读出的是，直到杀戮，动作仍要绕过对方的理解。这是逐级的读法，不是一把量完整程度的尺；对方实际做得到什么，另据事实核。他的账因此是两本。权力论把这第二本账写作“校验债”：“只要 B 仍是主体……A 就不能一次性确认 B 已经是工具……A 必须反复校验。”

先替反方说话

屠宰场用弯道安抚牲口，不也是防范吗？那管理的是感官的线索。浴室之谎作用于理解，只对能相信一句话的存在才有效，读数不变。杀戮这一项，大半也收得住：欺骗、突袭、挑熟睡的时候下手，都是在对付对方能说不——权利论说过，睡眠“是行使带宽与运作的暂时中断，不是退场”。仍在这道梯子之外的，只有不取用任何人意志回应的动作。

最强的反方，在本框架自己的文字里。权力论 P1 写过：完全剥夺主体位置的对方，“对其的操控不是权力”——仿佛真有一种状态，否认者只记一本账，记的是物。这部书这样读：真到了那一步，防范就只剩下关栏一级，动作不再冲着他的意志，已经在域内零点的范围之外。权力论没有说那一步走不到；它说，走到那一步就只剩“对物的处理”，而 B 在本体上仍是主体——它写明：“任何把 §6 读为 SAE 承认某些人真的可以被还原为物 的解读, 都是误读”。权力论 P6 又说：凿削性权力“恰恰不能走到那一步”，“它要的是 B 的可用主体性”。

这部书与 P1 的分歧在鞭子：P1 把奴隶主的鞭子读作“11DD 12DD 的驯化操作”；鞭子若是用来让人听懂命令、照命令做，它冲着的就是对方的理解与选择，是胁迫，在域内。分歧照录。P1 同节还有一句，方向相同：鞭打一个奴隶至死，对其他奴隶构成“最强烈的 13DD 登记压力”——连杀戮，也是说给别人的意志听的。

三种唯我，一道戳

《第一批判》发生论记下过一支：“把价值收归唯我的一支，历史上实居”。第二批判把它分成三样。理论上的唯我，不动手，不开口，不指向任何人：在域内零点之外，施行一致，照常是一条分支。否认能说不的支配，嘴上说对方说不了不，动作上却一直对付着那份能力：可以理解，历史上有人住，施行不一致——住在里面的人记着两本账。

坦然的支配，认对方能说不，只是不以他为目的：施行一致，是一条有人住的分支，这一戳打不到它。这部书对它说得出的，是它站在哪里；它若锁住一个能说不的人，权力论读得出它付的那一笔——施权者自己先被这份锁定绑住，叫反缚；还有它在外面留下什么痕迹。它错不错，这部书说不出（第十二篇）。

施行不一致这一戳，不是无效判决。《第一批判》早已拒绝了康德的无效判决——“我们不判他的主张无效，而将它登记为另一条分支，标上它的条件”——又对公理的反对者说过：“说出反对的人历史上真实有过，立不住的是立场，不是人”。这里照此：立不住的只是他那一句否认；他的法、他这个人，都不因此作废，也不给任何人处置他的许可。这一戳依据的，是对方自己动作的描述。

打回旧版

同一道检查，也要打回本框架自己的旧版。《理性不得不》在条一说不可退出、退出即降格；到了条四，它又说两个人“都把自己暴露给对方的追问”，“两个都可追问、都在被追问中调整”。一边不许停，一边靠追问与调整活着。它还说：“退步也是发展的一种形态”——于是“不能停”就怎样都证伪不了。

这类旧句，框架自己已经打回过几次：《第一批判》写过“我们曾把这台机器写成一个无法退出的强制循环”；方法论0 第二版撤回了“所以‘非’不可不运作”；论文4 第二版写明，“主体成为工具”一类推论还没有都照新读法收束。

《第一批判》同时留下一句对批评者也有效的提醒：“对旧版的诊断不经重审而加于新版”，本身就是一种误诊。所以打回旧版之前，先查它的新版是否已改：行动论已经把这些句子降了，第二批判只审还没有改的那些。

Reading a Denier’s Testimony

This chapter of the book reads one person’s testimony: how the acts of someone who denies that others can say no testify in his place. The slaveholder does not take the slave as an end, yet he has to guard against the slave running away and rising up; he has no need to guard against his cattle rising up. To get the Jews into the gas chambers, the Nazis told them these were showers.

The pattern of precautions shows what the denier himself presupposes the other to be. One does not lie to a thing; one does not forbid a thing to read; one does not pass laws against those who might shelter a thing. Each such measure is aimed at a capacity the denier says is not there.

The examples — fugitive-slave laws and passes, the ban on literacy, the lie about the showers, the curved chutes of a slaughterhouse — are the book’s own. Power Theory also used the slaveholder, and reads him differently; the book records the difference rather than smoothing it over.

Five Rungs

Fencing in. What is guarded against is an uncontrollable remainder; livestock too are guarded against this way. The ban on literacy, and indoctrination. What is dealt with is the other’s self, already there or about to take shape. The rung splits: a ban on literacy is an open prohibition — by Value Theory’s division, locking, not colonization; indoctrination, Rights Theory rules a violation.

Fugitive-slave laws and passes. His no can be shouted to a third person and caught; what the institution forbids is precisely the third person’s response. Animals too give alarm calls, and their fellows answer; what this rung reads is not whether there is a signal, but that the no can be heard by a third person as a no, taken up, and that the hearer can stand on his side. Guarding against uprising: the oppressed can know one another and join forces. Luring to death: right up to the killing, his understanding must still be bypassed.

The reading: each rung shows which response his acts draw on — understanding, speaking, finding someone, joining with others; the top rung shows that even the killing has to go around the other’s understanding. It is a rung-by-rung reading, not a gauge of completeness, and what the other can actually do is checked against the facts. So he keeps two sets of books. Power Theory calls the second one “the debt of verification”: “A can never confirm once and for all that B has become a tool. … A must verify again and again.”

A Word for the Other Side

Slaughterhouses use curved chutes to calm the animals; is that not a precaution too? The chute manages sensory cues. The lie about the showers works on understanding, and works only on a being that can believe a sentence. The reading stands. Killing, too, is largely covered: deception, ambush, striking while the victim sleeps all deal with the other’s capacity to say no — and sleep, as Rights Theory puts it, is “a temporary interruption of the bandwidth of exercise and of operation, not an exit.” Outside the ladder lies only an act that draws on no one’s will at all.

The strongest opponent is in the framework’s own writings. Power Theory P1 says that over one whose subject-position has been fully stripped, “the operations performed on it are not power” — as though a denier could keep a single set of books listing a thing. The book reads it so: if that point is ever really reached, the precautions shrink to fencing in, the act no longer aims at his will, and it lies outside the in-domain zero point. Power Theory does not say the point cannot be reached; it says what remains is “only the processing of an object,” while B stays a subject: “Any reading of §VI as SAE permits some humans to be actually reducible to objects is a mis-reading.” Power Theory P6 adds that chiseling power “precisely cannot go that far”: “What it wants is B’s usable subjecthood.”

The book parts from P1 over the whip. P1 reads the slaveholder’s whip as “an 11DD–12DD domestication-operation.” But a whip used to make a person understand an order and carry it out is aimed at his understanding and choice; that is coercion, inside the domain. The disagreement is recorded as it stands. P1 itself adds a line that cuts the same way: whipping a slave to death exerts “the strongest registration pressure” on the other slaves — even the killing is addressed to other people’s wills.

Three Kinds of Self-Enclosure, and a Stamp

The First Critique had recorded, as historically inhabited, “the branch that gathers all value into the self.” The second critique splits it three ways. Theoretical solipsism lays a hand on no one, says nothing, points at no one: outside the zero point, consistent, a branch as usual. Denial-of-no domination says the other cannot refuse while its acts deal with that capacity all along: intelligible, historically lived in, and performatively inconsistent — two sets of books.

Candid domination concedes that the other can say no and simply does not take him as an end. It is consistent, an inhabited branch, and the stamp does not reach it. What the book can say of it is where it stands; that if it locks in someone who can say no, Power Theory reads off what it pays — the one who locks is bound first by the locking, back-binding; and what traces it leaves outside. Of its wrongness the book can say nothing (Essay 12).

The stamp of performative inconsistency is not a verdict of invalidity. The First Critique had refused Kant’s verdict of invalidity — “we do not rule his claim invalid, but register it as another branch, labeled with its condition” — and said of the axiom’s opponents: “people who spoke the denial have really existed; what cannot stand is the position, not the person.” So here: what cannot stand is his one denial. His law, and he himself, are not voided, and no one is given license to dispose of him. The stamp rests on a description of his own acts.

Turning the Check on Old Versions

The same check is turned on the framework’s own earlier versions. Reason Cannot Not says in its first clause that one cannot exit, that to exit is to be degraded; in its fourth it asks “both subjects to expose themselves to the other’s interrogation,” “two interrogable, mutually-calibrating subjects.” On one side stopping is forbidden; on the other it lives by interrogation and adjustment. It also says that “regression is also a form of development” — and then “cannot stop” can be falsified by nothing at all.

The framework has turned such sentences back before. The First Critique: “We once wrote this machine as an inescapable forced cycle.” Methodology 0’s second edition withdrew “therefore negativa cannot not operate.” Paper 4’s second edition says that inferences of the “subject becomes a tool” kind have not yet all been brought under the new reading.

And the First Critique left a warning that applies to critics too: “a diagnosis of an old version applied, without re-examination, to the new” is itself a misdiagnosis. So before turning an old sentence back, the book first checks whether a later version has already changed it. Action Theory had already lowered these sentences; the second critique examines only those that have not yet been changed.
