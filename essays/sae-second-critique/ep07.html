出示多于持有：自家的案卷

Displaying More Than It Holds: The Case File at Home

Han Qin (秦汉)

一个形式，七栏账

《第一批判》幻相论把认识一侧的种种毛病归成一个形式：“一个构，出示了它不曾挣得的凭证。”判定的办法是对账：一项主张在使用中出示的地位凭证，只要有一项不在其经检验、复核与索引所获准的条目之中，即构成冒领——“出示的集合越出持有的集合”。这一判定“是回溯的、不问动机的”：被诊断的是呈现的形式，不是呈现者的存心。

实践一侧，形式不变，只换主语：一条法，或一个不得不，出示了它不曾挣得的效力凭证。账上的栏也换成实践的栏：模态——以什么方式约束，没有条件、挂在定义上还是挂在位置上；来源——公理推出、一条公设还是一个定义；辖域——约束立它的人自己，还是别人；时站——说的是今天的我，还是昨天的、明天的；对象——管一次运作，还是一个人；担保——推出的、承担的，还是没有记下来源的；代价——价落在哪里，说出来没有。

《第一批判》在门口立的纪律，这里照守：“判的是冒领，不定罪于人。”病例只取自本框架自己已发表的文字；康德的普遍立法在这部书里是一条分支，不是病例。

无条件，演绎，横向

冒领无条件（模态）。“这不是选择……是存在方式”；“无条件的”。出示的是“没有条件”；持有的是一个位置上的不得不——那句话自己就带着前件：“你的DD层级到了那里”。还要补一句公道话：《自己的法》把辖域说对了——“它的约束力只落在持有它的那个主体身上”。病在模态一栏，不在辖域一栏。

冒领演绎（来源）。道德律第一篇说四条定理“是SAE的唯一公理‘非’在多Self情境下的具体展开”；权利论 Paper 0 在一页之内说了三遍，说法各不相同。还有两处更直接，各自另立了一条公理：战争论说它的推导依据“是SAE的公理本身——主体性不可被消灭……”；经济学 P6 说“SAE的核心公理是‘余项不得不发展’”。这几处都写在方法论0 撤回“其余皆定理”之前；这部书照录，不替它们改。

冒领横向（辖域）：一条法出了立法者自己的边界，去要求别人。论文5 有一句现成的诊断：“把自己的单一cannot-not普遍化了——将一个个体化的行动必然性强加为所有人都应该服从的法则”，到最后只剩一句“你也应该跟我一样”。它最容易犯的地方，是拿交叠当推论的前提：你我都把人当目的，所以你也该如何如何。它把一个定义保证的重合，用成了向别人发令的根据。“每个人都应该做自己的哲人王”就是一处。

以层级数人，终审，今站

冒领人型（对象）：把 15DD 当作一类人，按人头数层级——“12DD占大多数+14DD在中间+15DD作为少数”。这里要说准病在哪里。同一张列表的下一条就是：同一个体在不同语境中可能运作在不同层，“不是固定类型”。病不在原篇没有限定，在比例句可以脱落同篇的限定；改法是在句内补上运作的索引，不是删句。这一宗该有的样子本框架里也有：经济学 P3 拿行为输出作观测的代理，写明代理不是定义，并且可错。

冒领终审（时站与担保）：一次判读被当作对一个人的定论。道德律 P3 给了声誉一种朝定论走的倾向：“动机是可被观察的，但观察需要时间。日久见人心”；又说可持续地策略性伪造 15DD 声誉，“只能向真正变成15DD汇合”。冒领落在两处：读动机，担保汇合。它的反面，冲突论早已写下：“暂停的是这条路，不是对这个人的终审。”

冒领今站：昔日的声誉不经重审作今日的判断——《第一批判》的原话是“以对他人昨日之构裁其今日”。它的镜像，第五篇已经见过：今天的我替明天的我签下代价，当成无需再问的永久授权。

驱动，标准，免费，未记来源，趋同

冒领驱动：余项“不得不有外部出口”；共同的做法是“逼出来的”。出示的是一股推着人走的力；持有的是条件与判断。冒领标准：行动论说“这个要求就是这么高”，“要求”这个词容易被读成一把衡人的尺；这部书把它读作定义的强度。道德律自己说过：“四条不是Self努力达到的标准。”所以这部书的做法是：不用标准，用定义。15DD 是一个定义，不是别人拿来量我的尺。

冒领免费：一个行动或一条公设自称什么都没留下，就是在实践一侧否认余项。《自己的法》说共振：“振不到一起，各自继续振，谁也没损失。”若“损失”指价值地位，确实不损；若指那一次没有振到一起，那就是一笔代价，只是不出声——权力论在别处说过：“沉默不是无代价的。”冒领未记来源的效力：冲突论说它的约束“不发出规范效力”，同一节又说停止决定的可回看性“是一条更上游的效力”。在本框架交出“应当”的整条链上，这是这部书查到的唯一一处自称有效力、却没有写明来源的。这部书只把它记下，不替它补来源。

冒领趋同：历史的走向被说成必然——“14DD向15DD成长是本体必然”。《第一批判》早已说过：“守恒不是动力。”行动论后来把这一说降了：走多快，“是人选出来的”，“具体的时期可以倒退”。一个关于历史走向的主张，要分开交代三样：经验前提，从前提到结论的推理，能用到哪一段实际的历史；把必然的口吻放在这三样之上、一样也不交代，就是冒领。最后是一宗候选：“恶”字。系在操作的方向上，如“恶不是一个位置身份，是退化与压制的方向”，并不冒领；被用作一个人终身的身份，才另受诊断。

殖民与侵

还有两宗，与冒领同形，却不是冒领之下的种。殖民，价值论 P3 的定义是：“殖民是这样一个结构状态：某项内容在承担者处被当作构成已经完成，而这道构成不是在承担者自己的法里做成的，占着那个位置的是一个外来目的。”它说的是构成在哪里做成，不是内容从哪里来。要害是一句：“它不成功地构成，只成功地被当作构成。”

这正是出示多于持有的形状。可价值论写明两者不互相还原：殖民比冒领多出两件——读数已经被占，对账的通道已经封上。“冒领可以在对账的通道敞开时发生，也可以在那里被纠正；殖民是这道通道已封的那个状态。”赤裸的强制从不宣称那是你自己的价值，不进这个属。

侵，权利论 P5 立“核不可夺”：核是“被主体以自己的法封装的余项 ρ”；侵不是夺走核，是“以外部位置代写第一人称封装”。它举的最清楚的一例是灌输：动机是传授，被灌输者也感受着受益，这次作用仍是侵。在一个外部位置冒作别人第一人称的情形上，两宗可以相交；是不是殖民、是不是侵，各按原定义判，不由一宗推出另一宗——价值论说的自我殖民，就用不着一个外部的位置。

One Form, Seven Columns

The First Critique gathered the ailments of cognition into a single form: “a construct shows a credential it never earned.” The test is a reconciliation of accounts: if any credential a claim shows in use is not among the entries granted it by test, review and index, “the set shown exceeds the set held.” The verdict “is retrospective and asks no motive”: what is diagnosed is the form of the presentation, not the intent of the presenter.

On the practical side the form stays and the subject changes: a law, or a cannot-not, displays a credential of validity it never earned. The ledger gets practical columns: modality (how it binds — unconditionally, by definition, or by position); source (axiom, postulate, or definition); jurisdiction (its maker, or others); station (today’s self, or yesterday’s, or tomorrow’s); object (an operation, or a person); warrant (derived, undertaken, or unrecorded); cost (where it falls, and whether it is stated).

The First Critique’s rule at the door is kept: “what is judged is the false claim; no person is convicted.” And the cases are drawn only from the framework’s own published writings. Kant’s universal legislation is, in this book, a branch, not a case.

The Unconditional, Deduction, the Lateral

The false claim of the unconditional (modality). “This is not a choice … it is a mode of being”; “unconditional.” What is displayed is “no condition”; what is held is a cannot-not hung on a position — and the sentence carries its own antecedent: “when your DD level arrives there.” In fairness, One’s Own Law kept jurisdiction right — “its binding force falls only on the subject who holds it.” The lesion is in the modality column.

The false claim of deduction (source). Moral Law calls its four theorems “the specific unfolding of SAE’s sole axiom”; Rights Theory says it three ways on one page. Two places go further and set up axioms of their own: the War Treatise argues from “SAE’s axiom itself — subjectivity cannot be eliminated …”; Economics P6 says “SAE’s core axiom is ‘the remainder must develop.’” All were written before Methodology 0 withdrew “everything else is theorem”; the book records them as written and does not change them on their behalf.

The false claim of the lateral (jurisdiction): a law crosses its maker’s boundary and makes demands on others. Paper 5 diagnosed the shape — “the universalization of a single cannot-not, imposing an individualized action-necessity as a law everyone should obey,” ending in “you should be like me.” Its easiest form is to take overlap as a premise: you and I both take people as ends, so you too should … A coincidence guaranteed by a definition is being used as grounds for giving orders. “Everyone should be their own philosopher-king” is one such place.

Counting Heads, the Final Verdict, the Present Station

The false claim of a human type (object): treating 15DD as a kind of person and counting levels by heads — “12DD constitutes the majority + 14DD in the middle + 15DD as a minority.” The lesion must be located exactly. The same list says, one line later, that the same individual may run at different levels in different contexts — “not a fixed type.” The fault is not a missing qualification but a sentence that can shed the qualification its own paper supplies; the fix is to write the index of operation into the sentence, not to delete it. Its proper form exists too: Economics P3 used behavioral output as a proxy, marked it as not the definition, and marked it fallible.

The false claim of a final verdict (station and warrant): a reading taken as the settled verdict on a person. Moral Law P3 gave reputation a drift toward it: “Motivation is observable, but observation requires time. 日久见人心 — over time, the human heart becomes visible”; and faking a good reputation “can only converge to actually becoming 15DD.” The false claim falls in two places: reading the motive, and guaranteeing convergence. Its opposite was already in the Conflict Treatise: “What is suspended is this path, not a final judgment on this person.”

The false claim of the present station: yesterday’s reputation used, without re-examination, as today’s judgment — the First Critique’s “judging another’s today by yesterday’s construct of him.” Its mirror is the one met in Essay 05: today’s self signing for tomorrow’s, as a permanent authorization that never needs asking again.

Drive, Standard, Costlessness, Unsourced Validity, Convergence

The drive: the remainder “cannot not have an external outlet”; joint action “forced into being.” Displayed: a force; held: conditions and judgment. The standard: Action Theory’s “the demand is that high” is easily heard as a yardstick for people; the book reads it as the strength of a definition. Moral Law itself said: “The four are not standards that the Self strives to reach.” Hence the book’s rule: not a standard, but a definition. 15DD is a definition, not a ruler others hold up to measure me.

Costlessness: an act or a postulate claiming to have left nothing behind denies the remainder. One’s Own Law on resonance: when two do not resonate, “each continues to vibrate; neither loses anything.” If “loses” means standing, true; if it means the meeting that did not happen, that is a cost, only a silent one — as Power Theory said elsewhere, “Silence is not without cost.” Unsourced validity: the Conflict Treatise says its constraints “issue no normative force,” yet the reviewability of a decision to stop is “an effect further upstream.” Along the whole chain on which the framework hands over its ought, this is the only claim of binding force the book found with no stated source. It records it, and supplies none.

Convergence: the direction of history called necessary — “14DD growing toward 15DD is ontologically necessary.” The First Critique had already said: “conservation is no motor.” Action Theory later lowered the claim: how fast it goes “is chosen by people,” and “particular periods can go backward.” A claim about history must account separately for its empirical premises, its reasoning, and which stretch of actual history it covers; the tone of necessity laid over all three, accounting for none, is the false claim. Last, a candidate only: “evil.” Tied to the direction of an operation, as in “evil is not a positional identity; it is the direction of regression and suppression,” it claims nothing false; used as a person’s lifelong identity, it would.

Colonization and Violation

Two more cases share the form of false claiming without being species of it. Colonization, in Value Theory P3: “a structural state: a content stands, in the bearer, registered as constitution already completed, while that constitution was not made in the bearer’s own law, and what occupies that position is an external end.” It is about where the constitution was made, not where the content came from. Its crux: “It does not succeed in constituting. It succeeds only in being taken as constitution.”

That is exactly the shape of displaying more than is held, yet Value Theory insists the two do not reduce to each other: colonization has two things more — the reading is already occupied, and the channel of reckoning is already sealed. “A misclaim can occur while the channel of reckoning is open, and can be corrected there; colonization is the state in which that channel has been sealed.” Naked coercion, which never claims the content is yours, falls outside it.

Violation, in Rights Theory P5: the core is “the remainder ρ encapsulated by the subject under its own law,” and violation does not seize it — it is “ghostwriting the first person from an external position.” Its clearest instance is indoctrination: the motive is transmission, the instructed even feels the benefit, and it is still violation. The two can intersect where an outside position passes itself off as someone’s first person, but each is judged by its own definition, and neither is inferred from the other — self-colonization needs no outside position at all.
