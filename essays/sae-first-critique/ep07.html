Essay 07 of 12

谁追问追问者

Who Questions the Questioner?

Han Qin (秦汉)

自问为什么不够

一个人可以认真检查自己的论证。他重读三遍，逐条核对前提，甚至主动写出最强反对意见。这些作业都有价值，却不能担保他已经看见自己的全部盲区。因为设计检查的人、选择检查范围的人和接受检查结果的人仍是同一个。

这不是说人一定自欺，而是独立性无法由同一来源自证。一个程序可以内部一致地运行，却把同一项遗漏复制到每个环节。自问能提高账的质量，不能为“我没有漏掉自己看不见的东西”提供最后担保。

他者的追问因此进入《第一批判》。他不只是补充更多信息，而是从未与我的路径完全重合的位置发问。那一份不重合，使校验取得了自问无法自己制造的独立性。

他者也会错

独立不等于正确。别人可能误读文本，带着自己的盲区，或者只把另一套习惯当作普遍标准。若一见外部反对就降档，理论只是把判断权从作者交给声音最大的人。

所以他问是一项校验来源，不是终审权。反对者也要说明对象、层级、分支与公设；也要区分他指出的是内容错误、地址错误，还是另一套可选承担。他的主张同样进入登记，同样可以被追问。

这里的对称性不是礼貌。若“提出批判的人”天然免审，批判很快会成为一种身份。SAE 反对的是凭证冒领，不是某一类人；追问的是主张，不是把作者整个人送上法庭。

无穷倒退怎样停下

若 A 审 B，C 又审 A，谁来审 C？寻找最终担保人会把问题推成无穷链。常见的解决办法，是在某处指定权威：共同体、专家、形式系统、理想观察者。但那个指定本身又需要凭证。

《第一批判》不让链条在一位不受审的裁判那里停止。它换了问题：不是“谁能保证永远无误”，而是“当前判断怎样公开自己的地址，并在新追问到来时可被重审”。终止一次具体审议，不等于宣布终止一切未来复核。

一篇文章可以暂时结论明确，一个制度可以在期限内作决定，一项研究可以按现有证据发布；它们只需保存依赖、异议与修订入口。可行动性来自暂时结算，不来自绝对终审。

追问不是审判人格

哲学争论最容易把主张和人绑在一起。指出一项论证冒领，被听成指控作者虚伪；承认一项局部成立，又被理解为支持作者的全部立场。《第一批判》要求把两者拆开。

主张有地址，人不是一组主张的总和。我们可以说“这句话在经典分支内可推，跨支不成立”，不必判作者没有资格发言；也可以说“这位作者在别处贡献巨大”，却不因此给当前句子增加凭证。

这种拆分也保护被批评者的修订能力。若错误等同人格，撤回一句话就像撤回自己；若审的是登记，降档和重址只是作业的一部分。能改，不必先认罪。

一座永不休庭的法庭

《第一批判》最后给出的制度形状，不是终审法院，而是一座永不休庭的法庭。它有三项装置：登记，让每项承担写清内容、地位、担保和替代分支；诊断，用凭证与使用之间的差额识别冒领；被追问，让他者参与效力的结算。

“永不休庭”不意味着每件事永远悬而未决。案件可以按当前材料落定，条目可以投入使用；只是判决不获得超越其地址的永久执照。新的证据、新的分支、新的他者都可能要求重开。

谁追问追问者？任何能把自己的问题也放进同一程序的人。答案不是一个名字，而是一种可反身的作业。没有人凭出身取得最后一句；每一句的分量，来自它愿意公开多少账，又经得住多少轮来自非同一路径的追问。

Why Self-Questioning Is Not Enough

A person can examine an argument conscientiously. She rereads it, checks each premise, and writes the strongest objections she can imagine. These practices matter, but they cannot certify that every blind spot has been found. The designer of the audit, the selector of its scope, and the receiver of its result remain the same person.

The point is not that self-deception is inevitable. It is that independence cannot be certified by its own source. A procedure may operate with perfect internal consistency while reproducing one omission at every stage. Self-questioning improves an account; it cannot finally warrant the sentence “I have found everything that I am unable to see.”

The other's interrogation therefore enters the First Critique. Another person contributes more than additional information. She asks from a path that does not coincide completely with mine. That noncoincidence gives the check an independence I cannot manufacture through another round of self-inspection.

Others Can Be Wrong

Independence is not correctness. Another reader may misunderstand the text, carry a different blind spot, or mistake local habits for universal standards. If every external objection automatically lowers a claim's status, judgment merely moves from the author to the loudest critic.

Interrogation is therefore a source of validation, not a court of final appeal. An objector must also state objects, levels, branches, and postulates. She must distinguish a content error from an address error and both from an alternative undertaking. Her objection enters the register and remains open to questioning.

This symmetry is not courtesy. If “the critic” is naturally exempt, critique becomes an identity endowed with privilege. SAE diagnoses the false claiming of propositions, not a class of people. It puts claims on trial without placing the whole person of the author in the dock.

Stopping Without a Final Guarantor

If A audits B and C audits A, who audits C? A search for complete assurance produces an infinite chain. The conventional solution appoints an authority somewhere: a community, experts, a formal system, or an ideal observer. But that appointment needs a credential of its own.

The First Critique refuses to stop the chain at an unexamined judge. It changes the question from “Who guarantees permanent infallibility?” to “How does the current judgment expose its address and remain available for review?” Ending a particular deliberation need not mean ending every future examination.

An essay can reach a definite conclusion. An institution can decide before a deadline. A study can be published on present evidence. They need only preserve dependencies, objections, and routes of revision. Action comes from provisional settlement, not absolute appellate authority.

Interrogating Claims, Not Personalities

Philosophical disagreement easily binds propositions to persons. To identify credential overreach sounds like an accusation of dishonesty. To grant one local point sounds like support for an author's entire position. The First Critique asks us to separate them.

Claims have addresses; persons are not the sum of their claims. We can say that a sentence is derivable in a classical branch and has no cross-branch standing without declaring its author unqualified to speak. We can honor an author's achievements elsewhere without transferring that prestige into a new credential for the present sentence.

The separation protects revision. If an error is identical with a personality, withdrawing a sentence feels like withdrawing oneself. If the audit concerns registration, downgrade and readdressing are ordinary parts of work. A person may correct an account without first confessing to being a defective person.

A Court That Never Permanently Adjourns

The institutional shape of the First Critique is not a supreme court but a court that never permanently adjourns. It has three standing devices. Registration states the content, status, warrant, and alternatives of each undertaking. Diagnosis compares credentials in use with credentials earned. Interrogation lets noncoincident others participate in settlement.

Permanent availability for review does not leave every matter undecided forever. A case can be settled under present materials and an entry used in further work. The judgment simply receives no license that outruns its address. New evidence, another branch, or another reader may provide grounds to reopen it.

Who questions the questioner? Anyone willing to place the question inside the same reflexive procedure. The answer is not a name but a kind of work. No origin entitles anyone to the final word. The weight of a sentence comes from the account it exposes and the rounds of noncoincident interrogation it can withstand.
