Essay 06 of 12

构怎样冒领地位

How a Construct Claims More Than It Earned

Han Qin (秦汉)

幻相是一种凭证问题

日常说“幻相”，容易想到感官错觉：水里的筷子看起来弯了，远处的路面像有水。《第一批判》说的幻相不是“看起来不像真的”，而是一项构在使用时出示了它没有取得的地位。

一个模型可以很准，一套分类可以很有用，一段推演可以完全有效；幻相仍可能发生。问题不必在内容为假，而在凭证升级：局部结果被当成无条件必然，工作假设被当成公理，作者的解释被当成对象自己的声音。

判准因此很明确：主张实际使用的凭证，只要有一项不在它经过检验与复核所获准的条目里，就发生冒领。审的是主张与登记册是否相符，不是使用者心里有没有恶意。

前四类：必然、过审、现站、施行者

第一类是冒领必然。一个结果在给定公设内可推，便被说成任何可能框架里都不能不如此。第二类是冒领过审。条目只是被登记、尚待复核，却在引用中被写成已经证明。

第三类是冒领现站。发生序列描述一种结构位置，文本却把自身或某个对象直接宣布为“已经到达”该站，仿佛层级名称自带测量。第四类是冒领施行者。一个产物、制度或算法完成了某类输出，便被当作已经承担了判断，或者反过来，因其出身不符合预设名单便被拒绝承认任何作业。

四类都可能藏在很普通的语法里。“必然”“已经说明”“真正的主体”“只是工具”这些词并不自动错误；危险在于它们暗中增加了登记册没有的地位。

后四类：跨支、零点、整体、跨域

第五类是冒领跨支效力。经典逻辑下的推演没有标出分支索引，换一套逻辑仍以原结论通行。第六类是冒领零点或公理地位。可替换的承担被写成任何反对者都会在反对中承认的东西。

第七类是冒领整体。某处对应成功，便宣布两套哲学已经互证；几个样例吻合，便把一个图案写成整条序列的生成律。第八类是冒领跨域。描述结构的判断直接变成规范命令，或者心理、历史、物理中的结果未经重新取得资格便进入另一个领域。

这八类不是互斥盒子。一句话可以同时冒领过审、整体与跨域效力。它们也不是封闭分类；新增类型只要能明确凭证与地址，便可进入清单。

幻相为什么比错误更顽固

普通错误可以由反例纠正。幻相往往会吸收反例，因为它掌握的是解释反例的资格。一个自称整体的理论会把反对者重新解释成尚未达到相应层级；一个自称必然的模型会把失败归因于输入不纯；一个冒领施行者地位的制度，会把受其判断的人排除在申诉之外。

幻相还会沉淀。一次方便的省略在多次引用后变成背景前提，后来的人只看见结论，看不见最初那条尚未过审的承担。权威、术语密度和形式复杂度都会提高追溯成本，却不能提高地位。

所以诊断不能只问“这句话错在哪里”，还要问“它以什么资格拒绝被改写”。越是能让自己的失误消失在解释里的构，越需要回到账本查凭证。

处置不是删除

发现冒领以后，不必把整项构扔掉。最常见的处置是降档与重址：把“必然”改回“在 P1—P4 下可推”，把“证明了主体”改回“呈现了某类输出”，把“普遍规律”改回“在这组样本里的稳定关系”。

内容可能一个字不改，地位却被纠正。这正说明批判不是破坏欲。它保存实得，同时撤回多拿的凭证。一个局部有效的模型在失去整体权威以后，反而更容易被可靠使用。

八类幻相给 SAE 西哲对读一条很实际的纪律：相似不是同源，映射不是推导，照亮不是证明。任何“柏拉图已经说过 SAE”“亚里士多德补完了 SAE”或“SAE 解决了康德”的句子，都必须重新报出它的地址。好的对接不需要冒领胜利。

Illusion as a Credential Failure

In ordinary language, illusion suggests perceptual error: a stick looks bent in water or a distant road looks wet. The First Critique uses illusion in a different sense. A construct becomes illusory when it presents a status that it has not earned.

A model can be accurate, a classification useful, and a derivation valid while illusion still occurs. The content need not be simply false. The failure is credential promotion: a local result appears as unconditional necessity, a working assumption appears as an axiom, or an author's account appears as the object's own voice.

The criterion is exact. False claiming occurs whenever a claim uses a credential absent from the list it has obtained through examination and review. The audit compares use with registry. It does not speculate about whether the person making the claim intended deception.

The First Four: Necessity, Validation, Station, Performer

The first type claims necessity. A result derivable under stated postulates is presented as unavoidable in every possible framework. The second claims validation. An item has merely been entered for review, yet later citations treat it as demonstrated.

The third claims a present station. A developmental sequence names a structural position, and a text directly announces that itself or an object has “reached” it, as if a level-name were already a measurement. The fourth claims performer status. A product, institution, or algorithm generates an output and is therefore credited with having assumed the judgment; or, in the opposite direction, an origin not found on a predetermined list is used to deny that any relevant work occurred.

All four can hide in ordinary grammar. Words such as “necessarily,” “already established,” “genuine subject,” and “mere tool” are not automatically wrong. The danger lies in the additional status they introduce without an entry in the register.

The Next Four: Branch, Zero Point, Totality, Domain

The fifth type claims cross-branch validity. A derivation made in classical logic omits its branch index and travels unchanged after the logic has been replaced. The sixth claims the position of a zero point or axiom. A replaceable undertaking is presented as something every opponent must enact even while opposing it.

The seventh claims totality. A successful correspondence at one interface becomes proof that two philosophies validate each other as wholes; a few matching examples turn a pattern into the generative law of an entire sequence. The eighth claims cross-domain validity. A structural description becomes a normative command, or a result from psychology, history, or physics enters another domain without obtaining standing there.

The eight types are not mutually exclusive. One sentence may claim validation, totality, and cross-domain force at once. Nor is the list closed. A new form can be added whenever its credential and address can be identified.

Why Illusion Persists Better Than Error

An ordinary error can be corrected by a counterexample. Illusion often absorbs counterexamples because it has claimed the authority to interpret them. A theory claiming totality redescribes dissenters as people who have not reached the relevant level. A model claiming necessity blames failure on impure inputs. An institution claiming performer status excludes the people it judges from the category of legitimate appellants.

Illusion also sediments. A convenient omission is repeated until it becomes background. Later readers inherit the conclusion but no longer see the unreviewed undertaking beneath it. Authority, technical vocabulary, and formal complexity raise the cost of reconstruction; they do not raise the claim's status.

Diagnosis must therefore ask more than “Where is this sentence mistaken?” It must also ask “By what credential does the sentence resist revision?” The more effectively a construct makes failure disappear inside its own explanation, the more urgently its credentials need comparison with the ledger.

Readdressing Instead of Deleting

Detecting false claiming does not require discarding the construct. The common remedy is downgrade and readdressing. “Necessary” returns to “derivable under P1–P4.” “This proves a subject” returns to “this exhibits a class of outputs.” “Universal law” returns to “stable relation in this sample.”

The content may remain unchanged while its status is corrected. This is why critique is not a taste for destruction. It preserves the gain and withdraws the extra credential. A locally effective model often becomes more dependable after it loses authority over the whole.

The eight illusions impose a practical discipline on SAE's dialogues with Western philosophy. Resemblance is not common origin. Mapping is not derivation. Illumination is not proof. Any sentence claiming that Plato already said SAE, Aristotle completed it, or SAE solved Kant must submit a new address. Good comparison has no need to claim victory it did not earn.
