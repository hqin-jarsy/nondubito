SAE第一批判：十二讲

The SAE First Critique: Twelve Readings

如果这是你第一次接触 SAE，建议先读《SAE基础：人如何成为目的》；那里提供完整的普通读者入口。

《SAE第一批判》是 SAE 西哲系列反复调用的基础文本。它不替框架增添一座更深的地基，而是审查框架自己的辖域、凭证、公设、形式化与发生序列：哪一项可推，哪一项只是承担，哪一项越出了已经挣得的地址。

这十二篇按读者真正需要调用的论证单位重写，不机械摘要原论文的五章。原论文仍是完整论证与权威底本：阅读全文 ↗ · DOI ↗

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · 十二篇 · 八种语言
第 01 篇
先审自己

一门以追问为方法的哲学，若先把自己排除在追问之外，第一步就已经失效。《SAE第一批判》为何必须先把刀转向自身。

第 02 篇
只有一条公理

“非不能非”为什么是施行性公理，而“认识必须继续”“余项必然逼出下一步”都不是。先拆掉三种被误写成必然的东西。

第 03 篇
为什么叫第一批判

它继承康德的划界工作，却把批判对象从理性能力移到 SAE 自己的地位账；同名不是复刻，更不是把康德收编进 SAE。

第 04 篇
一条主张管到哪里

错不只发生在结论上，也发生在地址上。对象、层级、分支、公设四栏，怎样把越界的主张送回它真正成立的位置。

第 05 篇
为什么主体凿不尽自己

当握刀者把自己变成对象，作业完成时的主体已不再等于被写下的主体。主体的物自体不是神秘内核，而是行动与自述之间持续移动的边界。

第 06 篇
构怎样冒领地位

幻相不是简单的错觉，而是一项构出示了超过过审清单的凭证。八类冒领把最常见的哲学越界逐一登记。

第 07 篇
谁追问追问者

他者提供自问无法担保的独立校验，但他者也没有豁免。没有终审者，如何仍让判断有成色，而不坠入无穷倒退。

第 08 篇
六条公设，不是六块地基

六条公设是可替换的承担，不是由唯一公理推出的定理。认识、余项、构、离散与反身如何各自取得明确地位。

第 09 篇
形式化不能替哲学落刀

形式语言能登记、分离、验证与传导，却不能替我们选择场域、公设或解释。作业者定理划出规则执行与哲学判断之间的界。

第 10 篇
一条公设换掉以后

前提撤回不会让整套理论瞬间变假。依赖清单与传导条款把受影响的结论送回待审，并保存它们曾经有效的历史地址。

第 11 篇
路怎样走来，不等于它凭什么有效

发生论给 0D—16DD 逐站分级，却不把历史次序变成合法性证明。序列、方向、后验输入与实然—应然之缝各归各账。

第 12 篇
照亮，而不证明

复数道路可以共享前提、局部互证并暴露彼此盲区，却不能整体替对方奠基。开放审计如何取代一次性的永久执照。

Foundational SAE Text · A Common Reference for SAE Dialogues

The SAE First Critique: Twelve Readings

《SAE第一批判：十二讲》

New to SAE? Begin with SAE Foundations: How a Person Becomes an End, the complete reader-facing entrance.

The SAE First Critique is the foundational text repeatedly invoked by SAE's dialogues with Western philosophy. It does not add a deeper foundation. It audits the framework's own jurisdictions, credentials, postulates, formalization, and developmental sequence.

These twelve essays are independent reader-facing rewrites organized around arguments that later series can cite directly, rather than chapter summaries. The paper remains the complete and authoritative source: read the paper ↗ · DOI ↗

Essay 01
Critique Begins at Home

A philosophy organized around interrogation fails at its first step if it exempts itself. The First Critique therefore turns the audit inward before judging anything else.

Essay 02
Only One Axiom

Why Negativa cannot be denied is performative, while cognition's continuation and the remainder's alleged motor are not. Three mistaken necessities must be dismantled first.

Essay 03
Why Call It a First Critique?

It inherits Kant's work of delimitation while moving the audit from the faculties of reason to SAE's own status ledger. The shared name marks a task, not an annexation.

Essay 04
How Far Does a Claim Reach?

Error can lie in an address as well as a conclusion. Four coordinates—object, level, branch, and postulate—return overextended claims to the places where they actually stand.

Essay 05
Why the Subject Cannot Finish Its Own Portrait

When the performer becomes an object, the subject who completes the account is no longer identical with the subject it describes. The noumenal limit is a moving gap between action and self-portrait.

Essay 06
How a Construct Claims More Than It Earned

Illusion is not mere appearance but a construct presenting credentials beyond its reviewed record. Eight forms of false claiming map philosophy's recurrent overreach.

Essay 07
Who Questions the Questioner?

Others provide a check self-questioning cannot certify, but they receive no immunity. How can judgment retain force without either a final judge or an infinite regress?

Essay 08
Six Postulates, Not Six Foundations

The six postulates are replaceable undertakings, not theorems derived from the sole axiom. Cognition, remainder, construction, discreteness, and reflexivity each receive an explicit status.

Essay 09
Formalization Cannot Make the Cut

Formal language can register, separate, validate, and propagate, but it cannot choose the field, postulates, or interpretation for us. The worker theorem marks the boundary between rule execution and judgment.

Essay 10
When a Postulate Is Replaced

Withdrawing a premise does not make an entire theory instantly false. Dependency lists return affected claims for review while preserving the historical address at which they once stood.

Essay 11
Genesis Is Not Warrant

The genesis audit grades 0D–16DD without turning historical order into justification. Sequence, direction, posterior inputs, and the is–ought gap receive separate accounts.

Essay 12
Illumination, Not Proof

Plural paths may share premises, prove local claims, and expose blind spots without grounding one another as wholes. Open audit replaces the fantasy of a permanent license.
