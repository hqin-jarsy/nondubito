为什么主体凿不尽自己

Why the Subject Cannot Finish Its Own Portrait

Han Qin (秦汉)

把自己摆到桌上

人可以把自己当作对象：回忆一次决定，分析一种习惯，写下“我是怎样的人”。反身性让这件事成为可能。可一旦真的动手，主体与普通对象之间便出现了差别。

一块石头不会因为被测量而同时学会一种测量自己的方法。主体会。那个接受分析的“我”是作业开始时被截取的对象；而读完分析、承认或拒绝结论、决定下一步的“我”，已经经历了这次作业。产物保存了主体的一幅图，完成产物的主体却又走到图外。

所以“主体不能把自己收尽”不是说内心藏着一个永远神秘的实体。它说的是一个结构事实：握刀者可以转成对象，但作业本身会改变握刀者的地址。自画像完成之时，画画的人已经多走了一步。

行动者和产物之间的缝

自我知识常被想成不断提高分辨率。今天知道一点，明天再补一点，资料够多以后也许能得到完整模型。但若每次认识都是行动，新增资料不只是补图，也进入了主体的历史。

写日记最能看见这条缝。昨天的你写下一个解释，今天的你可以发现它在回避，可以接受它，也可以因它改变。昨天的文字没有因此变假，它仍准确记录当时能取得的构；但它不再拥有代表“整个我”的资格。

SAE 把物自体在这里理解成极限名：每一个关于“我”的构都收纳不尽的那一侧。它按对象而生，不是万物背后共用的一团黑暗。对于主体，这个极限格外明显，因为对象本身也在继续划界。

会移动的对象

普通测量也会扰动对象，但主体问题不等于物理扰动。关键不是观察让心理状态发生一点变化，而是判断的作者与判断的对象可以是同一个单位。主体能审查自己使用的规则，撤回过去的承担，并把旧产物当作新一轮作业的材料。

这使“最终自我说明”带有内在困难。若一份说明真能预先穷尽主体以后每次反身作业，它就必须连主体如何回应这份说明也一并写入；但主体读到它以后仍可把这项预写本身作为对象。每次封口都给下一次反身留下新位置。

这不证明自由意志的任何完整理论，也不证明主体不受因果条件影响。它只限制一种冒领：任何固定模型都不能仅凭对过去表现的高拟合，取得等同主体本身的地位。

目的从哪里来

主体收不尽自己，还关系到目的。外部系统可以预测偏好、设计选项、推荐最可能被接受的目标；主体也可以沿用习惯、传统或别人给出的目的。但这些来源都不能仅凭“我事实上这样选择过”，取得“这就是我为自己立下的法”的资格。

自我立法不是无中生有。语言、身体、家庭、制度与偶然遭遇都进入主体，构成他可以使用的材料。差别在于，材料的来源不能替代承担。一个目的由哪里进入，与主体是否把它认作自己的，是两栏。

因此《第一批判》不把“目的来源”安在一个纯粹内在的神秘点上，也不交给外部因果清单。它把问题留在作业上：谁能撤回、修改并承担这条目的；谁为它出示担保；谁在后果到来时仍以自己的名字站在那里。

模型可以有用，但不能冒名

心理测验、行为模型、医疗诊断和 AI 画像都可以捕捉真实模式。说它们不穷尽主体，不等于说它们一概无效。恰恰相反，辖域写清以后，它们会更可靠：这个模型预测某类行为，这个诊断解释某组症状，这份记录保存某段历史。

问题出在凭证升级。预测成功被写成人格本质，诊断被写成一生的名字，过去的选择被写成未来的许可，这时产物开始冒领主体。它从“关于你的一项有效构”变成“你就是这个构”。

主体的物自体为这种升级设限。它不是替主体宣布任何选择都正确，而是要求对主体的每一项构保留地址：这是一次作业的产物，不是握刀者的终结。人能被认识，也能认识自己；正因为他还能接过这份认识继续行动，没有哪幅自画像有权宣布画笔已经放下。

Putting Oneself on the Table

Human beings can make themselves objects. We revisit a decision, analyze a habit, and write sentences beginning “I am the kind of person who…” Reflexivity makes self-knowledge possible. Once the work begins, however, the subject differs from an ordinary object.

A stone does not learn a method of self-measurement by being measured. A subject can. The “I” placed under examination is the object selected at the beginning of the operation. The “I” who reads the result, accepts or contests it, and decides what to do next has passed through that operation. The product preserves a portrait of the subject, while the subject completing the product has already moved beyond the portrait.

The claim that a subject cannot take itself in completely does not posit a secret inner entity. It names a structural feature: the performer can become an object, but the performance changes the performer's address. When a self-portrait is finished, the painter has one more act in her history.

The Gap Between Agent and Product

Self-knowledge is often imagined as increasing resolution. We add information today, correct the image tomorrow, and eventually hope to produce a complete model. But if knowing is itself an action, new information does not merely fill the picture. It joins the history of the person depicted.

A diary makes the gap visible. Yesterday's writer records an explanation. Today's reader may discover an evasion in it, accept it, or be changed by seeing it. The earlier text need not become false. It can remain an accurate construct of what was then available. What it loses is the credential to represent the whole person.

SAE uses the thing-in-itself here as a limit-name: the name for what every construct of “me” fails to take in. The limit is indexed to an object rather than a shared darkness hidden behind all things. In the case of a subject, the limit is unusually vivid because the object is also continuing the work of demarcation.

An Object That Moves

Ordinary measurement may also disturb what it measures, but the problem of the subject is not merely physical disturbance. The author of a judgment and the object judged can be one unit. A subject can examine the rule used in an earlier examination, withdraw a previous undertaking, and turn an old product into material for new work.

This gives the idea of a final self-description an internal difficulty. If an account were to exhaust every later reflexive act, it would have to include how the subject responds to that very account. Yet once the subject reads the forecast, she may take the forecast itself as an object. Every closure creates another place from which closure can be examined.

This argument proves neither a complete theory of free will nor independence from causal conditions. It supports a narrower limit: no fixed model acquires the status of the subject merely because it fits the subject's past behavior with extraordinary accuracy.

Where Ends Come From

The unexhausted subject also matters for ends. External systems can predict preferences, arrange choices, and recommend the goal most likely to be accepted. A person can inherit ends from habit, tradition, or authority. But the fact that an end entered through these routes does not by itself establish that the person has legislated it as her own.

Self-legislation is not creation from nothing. Language, embodiment, family, institutions, and accident supply the material with which a person works. The distinction is that origin does not substitute for assumption. Where an end came from and whether the subject owns it as an undertaking belong in separate columns.

The First Critique therefore locates the source of ends neither in a mysterious pure interior nor in an external causal inventory. It returns the question to performance. Who can withdraw, revise, and assume this end? Who supplies its warrant? Who remains named when its consequences arrive?

Useful Models Without Impersonation

Psychological tests, behavioral models, medical diagnoses, and AI profiles can identify genuine patterns. To say that they do not exhaust a subject is not to dismiss them. A clear jurisdiction makes them more reliable: this model predicts a class of behavior; this diagnosis explains a group of symptoms; this record preserves a period of history.

Trouble begins with credential promotion. Predictive success becomes an essence of personality. A diagnosis becomes the name of a life. Past choices become permission to govern the future. At that point a product falsely claims the subject's place. “A valid construct about you” becomes “what you are.”

The noumenal limit of the subject blocks that promotion. It does not declare every decision correct. It requires every construct of a person to retain its address: a product of one operation, not the conclusion of the performer. A person can be known and can know herself. Precisely because she can take up that knowledge and act again, no self-portrait may announce that the brush has finally been laid down.
