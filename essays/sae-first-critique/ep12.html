照亮，而不证明

Illumination, Not Proof

Han Qin (秦汉)

路一旦复数，关系就要重写

发生论把主体、对象与宇宙列作后验输入，把公设与分支保留为可替换承担，于是从同一零点出发的道路不会只有一条。不同主体在不同历史与对象中作业，选择的粒度、分支和解释也可能不同。

复数不等于相对主义。道路都有资格入册，却不因此具有同样效力。某条路可以依赖更少未经审查的前提，能解释更多反例，或更诚实地保存余项；这些差别仍然可判。

真正需要改写的是道路之间的关系。不能再让一门哲学以自己的整套公设证明另一门，也不能靠对方的威望给自己补地基。

局部证明仍然可能

“不相互奠基”不等于哲学之间只能彼此欣赏。若两条道路明确共享对象、局部前提和推理规则，一项跨支证明完全可能。它证明的是这条具体主张在共同账面上的效力。

例如两套理论都接受一组历史材料，也共同采用某种因果规则，就可以检验一个局部解释是否成立。证明结束后，共享部分得到加强，但两套理论没有合并；各自未共享的公设仍由各自承担。

这条界防止“互相照亮”被降成没有判准的友好会谈。该证明的地方仍可证明，只是不批发整体互证。

照亮比证明更广

很多重要相遇并没有共享足够前提。柏拉图的洞穴可以让 SAE 的余项获得图像，SAE 的辖域可以让康德边界语言的一处风险变得可见；这类相遇没有推导关系，却改变了读者能看见什么。

照亮首先来自差异。他者从我没有走过的路径提出问题，使我看见自家登记册遗漏的对象、未经标注的分支，或者一直被当作背景的公设。照亮可以击中盲区，却不能自动把他者的答案搬来填空。

它比局部证明更广，因为不要求完整共享公设；又比类比更严格，因为被照亮的具体条目必须进入审计，说明发生了什么变化。

交叠不是趋同

不同道路会有交叠：共享施行性的零点，共享互认前的某些主体条件，也共享宇宙、对象与历史输入。这些交叠是真实取得，不只是遥远一致的预告。

但交叠不许诺最终统合。两条路可以在一个问题上并行，在下一处再次分开；可以同意诊断，却不同意规范处置。把交叠写成“最终都在说同一件事”，会抹去各自承担并制造整体幻相。

对 SAE 西哲系列而言，这是一条核心方法：先读准对方，再确认共享到哪一栏，最后让差异反向审问 SAE。不是把经典变成 SAE 的例证，也不是用经典权威给 SAE 盖章。

用公开审计替代永久执照

《第一批判》没有以一次性演绎给所有范畴颁发永久执照。它仍然称为批判，凭的是三项常设装置：登记、诊断与被追问。每项承担写清地址；冒领通过凭证对账被发现；他者参与后续结算。

这不是降低标准，而是承认地基不能证明自己、自己不能担保全部盲区。与其假装一次推导结束了历史，不如让依赖、版本与异议长期公开。

最后的句子于是有了真正分量：这部批判写完即入册，此后照亮它的光来自读它的人。读者不是替作者完成赞同，而是把自己的路径带进来，发现可以局部证明之处，也指出不能被对方接管的余项。哲学由此保持可用，又不假装封闭；能够相遇，又不要求互相吞并。

Plural Roads Require a New Relation

The genesis audit lists subject, object, and universe as posterior inputs and keeps postulates and branches replaceable. Roads beginning from the same performative zero will therefore not be singular. Different subjects work through different histories and objects, selecting different grains, branches, and interpretations.

Plurality is not relativism. Roads receive equal standing to enter the registry, not equal validity. One may rely on fewer unexamined premises, explain more counterevidence, or preserve remainder more honestly. Such differences remain open to judgment.

What must change is the relation among roads. One philosophy cannot use its entire postulate set to prove another, and it cannot borrow another tradition's prestige as a missing foundation.

Local Proof Remains Possible

The refusal of mutual grounding does not reduce philosophical exchange to appreciation. When two paths explicitly share an object, local premises, and inferential rules, cross-branch proof is possible. It establishes the validity of a particular claim on the common ledger.

Two theories may accept the same historical evidence and a shared causal rule, then test a local explanation. At the end of the proof, the shared claim has stronger standing. The theories have not merged. Their unshared postulates remain separately assumed.

This distinction prevents “mutual illumination” from becoming a friendly conversation with no criteria. Where proof is available, it should be used. What is refused is wholesale mutual validation.

Illumination Reaches Farther Than Proof

Many important encounters do not share enough premises. Plato's cave can give imagery to SAE's remainder; SAE's jurisdiction can expose a risk inside one use of Kantian boundary language. No derivation connects these cases, yet the encounter changes what a reader can see.

Illumination begins in difference. Another path brings a question from outside mine and reveals an omitted object, an unmarked branch, or a postulate long treated as background. It can strike a blind spot without transferring the other path's answer into the empty place.

Illumination reaches farther than local proof because it does not require a sufficiently shared postulate set. It is also stricter than loose analogy, because the item illuminated must enter the audit and state what changed.

Overlap Is Not Convergence

Different roads overlap. They may share the performative zero, some conditions of subjectivity before mutual recognition, and posterior inputs from a common universe, object, and history. These overlaps are real gains rather than predictions of distant agreement.

But overlap promises no final synthesis. Two paths may run together on one problem and divide at the next. They may agree on a diagnosis while disagreeing on the normative response. To say that they are “ultimately saying the same thing” erases their undertakings and creates an illusion of totality.

For SAE's dialogues with Western philosophy, this becomes a central method. Read the other account accurately. Identify the exact shared column. Then allow the difference to question SAE in return. A classic is neither an illustration annexed by SAE nor an authority whose seal validates it.

Public Audit Instead of a Permanent License

The First Critique does not issue permanent licenses to all its categories through a once-for-all deduction. It remains a critique through three standing devices: registration, diagnosis, and interrogation. Each undertaking declares an address. Credential overreach is detected by comparison with the ledger. Others participate in later settlement.

This does not lower the standard. It acknowledges that a foundation cannot prove its own unconditional standing and that self-review cannot certify all its blind spots. Rather than pretend one derivation has ended history, the theory keeps dependencies, versions, and objections public.

The closing sentence then carries real weight: once written, the critique enters the register, and the light that illuminates it comes from its readers. Readers do not complete the book by agreeing. They bring their paths, locate points of local proof, and expose remainders no theory may appropriate. Philosophy can remain usable without pretending to be closed, and capable of encounter without requiring mutual absorption.
