方法论系列

凿构循环的操作手册——把整个 Self-as-an-End 框架展开成一套可执行的逻辑操作系统。从非与余项到知识传导、主体条件、人机共生、意识分析与四分形 · 全十二篇 · 八种语言。

The operational manual of the chisel-construct cycle — the Self-as-an-End framework developed as an executable logical operating system, from negativa and remainder to knowledge transmission, subject-conditions, human–AI symbiosis, consciousness analysis, and the four-fold pattern · Twelve papers · Eight languages.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 全十二篇 · 八种语言Han Qin (秦汉) · Twelve papers · Eight languages
  • 第 0 篇Paper 0
    非 · Negativa：论否定先于存在
    非 · Negativa: On Negation Prior to Being
    “非”不是五个切面之一，而是切面得以分化的条件；0DD 是它的第一个自指肖像。
    Negativa is not one cross-section among five but the condition of their differentiation; 0DD is its first self-referential portrait.
  • 第 I 篇Paper I
    凿构循环：推导、方法、应用与AI接口
    The Chisel-Construct Cycle
    以凿、构、余项、桥与物自体五个切面，把 SAE 压缩成可执行的方法系统。
    Five cross-sections—chisel, construct, remainder, bridge, and thing-in-itself—turn SAE into an executable method.
  • 第 II 篇Paper II
    凿构的认识论地图
    The Epistemological Map of Chisel-Construct
    演绎、归纳、还原与溯因构成 2×2 地图；每个象限都带着迫使主体离开的结构性余项。
    Deduction, induction, reduction, and abduction form a 2×2 map; each quadrant carries a remainder that compels departure.
  • 第 III 篇Paper III
    怎么用AI找余项
    How to Find Remainders with AI
    人提供方向，AI扩展构；问题的句式、退出时机与主体的自向不疑共同决定余项质量。
    The person supplies direction while AI expands constructs; question form, exit timing, and self-directed non-doubt shape remainder quality.
  • 第 IV 篇Paper IV
    知识演化的十二态传导模型
    The Twelve-State Transmission Model of Knowledge Evolution
    先验、后验与定理三个节点，六条双向路径，在涵育与殖民两种相位中组成十二态。
    Three nodes—a priori, a posteriori, and theorem—form six directed routes in cultivation and colonization phases: twelve states.
  • 第 V 篇Paper V
    主体作为方法论的结构性条件
    The Subject as Structural Condition of Methodology
    十二态不会自行运转；节点归属、墙的诊断与冲突裁决都需要承担后果的主体。
    The twelve states do not execute themselves; node attribution, wall diagnosis, and conflict adjudication require a subject who bears consequences.
  • 第 VI 篇Paper VI
    相变窗口与实验设计
    Phase-Transition Windows and Experimental Design
    若响应有阈值且暴露高度异质，二元分组与二元分析会系统性稀释真正跨过阈值者的信号。
    When response is thresholded and exposure heterogeneous, binary assignment plus binary analysis systematically dilutes the signal of threshold crossers.
  • 第 VII 篇Paper VII
    否定方法论：Via Negativa与排除律的形式结构
    Negative Methodology
    在缺少决定性正向见证的开放归因问题中，以可审计的排除律缩小候选集，同时拒绝伪造终局。
    For open attribution problems without decisive positive witness, auditable exclusion laws narrow the field without fabricating closure.
  • 第 00 篇Paper 00
    余之道
    Via Rho: The Way of the Remainder
    从每次操作留下的余项反向追踪“非”；Via Rho 是 Via Negativa 的方法对偶。
    Trace backward from the remainder left by every operation toward Negativa; Via Rho is the methodological dual of Via Negativa.
  • 第 VIII 篇Paper VIII
    人与AI共生的方法论
    The Methodology of Human–AI Symbiosis
    人以主体性完成全局而有目的的压缩，AI以算力展开；分离的语境与一加四星形架构保护方向。
    The human supplies global, purposive compression and AI unfolds it with compute; separated contexts and a one-plus-four star architecture protect direction.
  • 第 IX 篇Paper IX
    意识分析框架
    A Framework for Analyzing Consciousness
    先问有无余项，再问能否跨过13DD相变；两个顺序判据给出三类五相，并将当下AI归入类意识。
    Ask first whether remainder exists, then whether 13DD can be crossed: two ordered criteria yield three classes and five phases, placing current AI in quasi-consciousness.
  • 第 X 篇Paper X
    四分形
    The Four-Fold Pattern
    标、向、隔、合：标而不构，加法给方向，乘法给记忆，闭合同时给构与余项。
    Mark, direction, interval, union: mark without constructing, add direction, bind memory, then close into construct and remainder together.
