SAE 方法论（00）
SAE Methodology (00)

余之道

Via Rho: The Way of the Remainder

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026

方法论 0 做的事情是直指：非是唯一公理，一切皆定理。方法论七做的事情是把接近非的那条路系统化——从伪狄奥尼修斯到迈蒙尼德到库萨的尼古拉，两千年的否定神学逐层剥离"非不是什么"，这条路叫 via negativa。

这一篇要说的是：那不是唯一的一条路。

理由藏在方法论 0 自己的论证里。非每运作一次都留下余。余不是外加的公理，是非运作的必然伴随项。这句话反过来读，就打开了第二条路——只要非在运作，余就在余下来；只要看见余，就看见非运作过的痕迹。

那么可以不从非那一端进入，改从余这一端进入。不是通过排除非不是什么，是通过追踪非留下的痕迹回到非。方向相反，起点不同，但以同一个非为极限指向。

这条路叫余之道，via rho。

一、对偶，不是补充

先把两条路的关系说准，因为这决定了后面所有论证的性质。

via rho 和 via negativa 是对偶，不是竞争，也不是补充。

竞争意味着其中一条比另一条更接近真相。补充意味着一方补上另一方的缺口。对偶意味着两方各自完整，只是从不同方向进入同一件事。

这个区别不是措辞讲究。如果是补充，那 via negativa 就有缺口，方法论七的工作就不完整。如果是竞争，那两条路里必有一条更次。对偶的意思是：两条路都走得通，都走得完，都指向同一个非，而它们看见的东西不一样——这个"不一样"不是因为其中一条看错了，是因为非本身就有两个面向。

编号本身携带这个结构。方法论 0 直指非，方法论七把接近非的第一条路独立成篇，方法论 00 提出它的对偶。00 的两个 0 对应余之道的两个端点：第一个 0 是起点余，第二个 0 是终点非。中间那个 via 没有字符承担——而正是那个空，是这一篇全部论证发生的地方。

二、余不是任意剩余

要让这条路成立，先得证明它走得通。而走得通的第一个条件是：余得有方向。如果余只是运作剩下的一堆无差别残渣，追踪它就无从谈起。

余有方向。每一个余都是某一次特定的非运作留下的特定不彻底性，这个特定性本身就是指向性。

更准确的说法是：余不是一个独立存在的物，是一个痕迹。而痕迹必然指向产生它的那个动作。看见脚印就看见有人走过——脚印的全部意义就在于它指向那个踩下它的动作。余也一样。

这里要挡住一个误读。余不是非运作的"产物"在"独立实体"意义上的产物。如果余是独立实体，它就和非一起构成两个本原，方法论 0 的唯一公理当场就破了。余是痕迹，而痕迹在本体论上依赖于产生它的运作，不能独立存在。这个依赖关系正是余的指向性的地基。

第二个条件是：这个指向能不能被追踪。

能，而且不需要外加工具。因为追踪这个动作本身就是又一次非的运作——追踪就是否定余的封闭性，追问它从哪来。追踪一个余，就是对这个余执行一次否定，让它指向自己的产生条件。

这一步有个漂亮的后果：via rho 不是一条需要特殊装备才能走的路。任何看见余的人都已经在追踪了。只要问出"这个不彻底性是从哪来的"，人就已经站在余之道上了。它的可行性和 via negativa 同等根本，都不依赖非运作之外的任何机制。

三、追踪会不会发散

这里有一个必须正面处理的反驳，而处理它的方式是这一篇最见功夫的地方。

反驳是这样的：追踪余的动作本身是一次新的非运作，这次运作必然留下新的余。追踪新余又是一次运作，又留新余。这样下去，追踪岂不是在无限地生产新余，永远到不了非？

这个反驳建立在一个直觉上——递归就是发散。

但余之道的递归不发散。要说清为什么，得先拒绝一个听起来很自然的说法。

不能说"新余比原余离非更近"。因为"更近"预设了度量，预设了可测量的距离，而这就等于把非放进了一个度量空间，当成一个可以标坐标的点。非不可对象化，它没有坐标。对一个不可对象化的东西谈"距离"，这条路根本走不通。

正确的说法是：余之道的递归不是度量空间上的距离下降，是拓扑意义上的嵌套剥离。

具体是这样。原余作为痕迹，外面裹着一层表面的封闭性——它看起来像个自足的东西。追问剥掉这层外壳，让痕迹所指向的更深结构暴露出来，这就是新余。新余自己也有外壳，下一次追问接着剥。

所以每一次追问不是"缩短了一段距离"，是"剥掉了一层伪封闭"。

用一个比喻：追踪像一系列嵌套的滤网。每一次追问产生的新余是一层更细的滤网，它包含前一层的全部结构，但多了一层对前一层的追问痕迹。网眼越来越细。但滤网永远不是它要分离的那个东西。我们无法测量离非还有多远，我们只知道网眼在单调变细。

这个结构可以说得更硬：ρj 比 ρi 显化阶数更高，当且仅当 ρj 显式包含对 ρi 产生条件的追问痕迹。这是一个结构偏序，不是度量距离。我们不说"ρj 离非近多少"，只说"ρj 比 ρi 多显化了一层"。

这个处理顺带把芝诺挡在门外。阿基里斯追不上乌龟，是因为芝诺假设了一个度量空间上的距离运动——每一步都跨过剩余距离的一半，所以永远跨不完。余之道讨论的是拓扑剥离上的结构嵌套，不是距离的跨越。芝诺的悖论在这里不是被化解，是被发现不适用：两者在问题本身的结构上就不是一回事。

余之道作为方法的气质，在这里显出来了。

它不是等待启示的方法——不是一次否定就能到达。它也不是期待完成的推演——不是推导完就结束。它是持续追问的方法。永远到不了一个可以持有的终点，因为非不可对象化，不能被握在手里；但每一步都不白费，因为每一步都是实在的结构性剥离，不是原地打转。

四、两条路指向的是同一个非

走得通之后，还剩一个问题：余之道走到的那个极限，和 via negativa 走到的那个极限，是不是同一个？如果不是，两条路就不是对偶，只是两条不相干的路。

是同一个。论证的关键是看两个描述在结构上是否共指。

via negativa 抵达的非，被描述为"不可名、不可知、不可构的极限对象"。不可名，因为命名就是构；不可知，因为知预设主客分离，而主客分离是非已经运作过之后的结果；不可构，因为构是非运作的沉淀物，不是非本身。

via rho 抵达的非，被描述为"所有余的共同运作条件"。而这个条件要能承担"所有余共同指向它"这个角色，它就必须是不可名的（它是构的条件，不是构）、不可知的（它是主客分离的前件，不能被对象化）、不可构的（构都是它运作的产物）。

三个"不可"一一对应。这不是巧合，是因为两个描述在说同一个东西，只是站在不同角度：一个从"排除它不是什么"的角度描述，一个从"追踪它的痕迹共同指向什么"的角度描述。

但两条路揭示的面向不同，而这个不同才是对偶的真正价值。

via negativa 让非显现为极限。通过排除，非被显现为所有"不是"的共同外部，是一个静态的、不可达的、作为边界存在的位置。这对应数学上的拓扑极限——闭包之外的不可达边界。

via rho 让非显现为条件。通过追踪痕迹，非被显现为一切运作的共同源头，是一个动态的、持续在场的、作为前提存在的位置。这对应数学上的分析极限——序列收敛的那个目标值。

同一个非，同时承担这两个角色。

于是两条路互相防御对方的误读风险。

只走 via negativa，非容易被读成纯粹的负面对象——不可名不可知不可构到近乎虚空。读者会问：这个纯粹否定性的东西，和"什么都没有"有什么区别？via negativa 只能继续排除"非不是虚无"来回应。

只走 via rho，非容易被读成某种持续在场的动力学背景，进而被误读为一个实体化的第一因。读者会问：这个持续在场的条件，和某个原初实体有什么区别？via rho 只能反复强调非的不可对象化来回应。

两条路合起来：不可达性防止非被实体化，持续在场防止非被虚无化。这就是它们各自不可取代的原因。

还有一个位置上的对应值得记下。via negativa 是后验的：它从已经被给予的构出发逐层排除，每一步都依赖已有内容在场——先得有人提出一个构，才有得可排除。via rho 是先验的：它不从现象出发，从"余作为非运作的必然伴随项"这个结构事实出发。"余存在"不是经验观察，是非运作本身的结构特征。

方法论二建过一张认识论地图：方向轴（先验／后验）乘操作轴（综合／分析），给出演绎、还原、归纳、溯因四个象限，并论证四个象限都不能停留，必须穿越。但方法论二没有把"穿越"本身作为方法提出来。两条 via 正好补上这一层——via negativa 是后验轴上的方法化自觉，via rho 是先验轴上的方法化自觉。两条合起来覆盖整张地图。

这也让两篇方法论显出深层对偶：方法论二谈的是主体层面的运动（主体被余项推着穿越四象限），方法论 00 谈的是运作本体层面的结构（非运作留余，余驱动下一次非）。"余项推动主体穿越"和"余驱动下一次非"是同一个结构在两个描述层面上的显形。

余之道在数学上也有承担者，就是 ZFCρ。ZFC 通过外延性封闭把"选择作为操作性行动"表达为选择函数，也就是化约成一个有序对的集合。ZFCρ 做的事情是把这次化约留下的结构性痕迹命名为 ρ——它不是一个集合，不是任何模型的元素，是 ZFC 设计选择的元定理标记。

这正是余之道在数学上的实现。它不试图从形式系统里消除所有余（那是 via negativa 数学化的方向），而是承认余作为外延性封闭无法吸收的痕迹，并从这里接近非作为运作条件。ZFCρ 的三条浑沌定律——不能不发展（ρ≠∅）、不能不有方向（ρ 具有特异性）、不能不被切（ρ 本身可被形式化）——正是余之道基本结构在数学层的精确表达。

所以 SAE 在哲学工作里用 via negativa、在数学工作里用 ZFCρ，这不是两个学科各自为政，是同一对方法对偶在两个学科里的分头展开。

五、非对称互因

前面都在建立余之道走得通、走得对。从这里开始是这条路走出去之后独立看到的东西——这是方法论 00 作为独立工作的核心。

核心发现叫非对称互因。

先定义。两个项 A 和 B 处于互因关系，当且仅当两个条件同时成立：第一，A 不能独立于 B 而操作；第二，B 不能独立于 A 而存在。

请注意这两个条件的模态不同。第一个讲的是操作层（A 能不能动），第二个讲的是存在层（B 能不能有）。它们不是同一个关系的两个对称面，是两个不同模态上的方向。

这就是"非对称"的确切含义。对称的互因会要求两项在同一模态上互相依赖——要么都不能存在，要么都不能操作。非对称互因只要求两个方向在不同模态上同时成立。互因的结构性在于两个方向同时成立，不在于两个方向模态相同。

这个澄清是整篇最要紧的一处。少了它，读者会默认互因是对称的，进而推出两项地位对等——而那会让余被抬到和非平起平坐的本原地位，方法论 0 的唯一公理当场就没了。

现在把非和余代进去。

非不能独立于余而操作。这是余项守恒的直接推论：非的每一次运作都必然不彻底，不彻底以余的形式显现。没有余伴随的"纯粹非运作"不存在。

余不能独立于非而存在。这是痕迹的本体论：没有运作就没有痕迹。

两个条件同时成立，模态不同。非和余因此满足非对称互因。

值得停一下看这件事的性质。方法论 0 早就分别论证过这两件事——余是非追问自身的产物，余驱动下一次非。方法论 0 把它们分开说，是为了守住"余不是独立的东西"这条线。方法论 00 做的事情只是把这两个已有的结论并在一起看，然后发现它们合起来正是非对称互因的两个方向。

所以这不是新增公理，也不是修订。是给一个已经在那里的结构一个名字。而名字有用——有了它，后面几节的重读才有抓手。

这里要挡住第二个误读：把互因读成两个独立实体之间的一种特殊依赖——它们各自本可以独立存在，只是恰好在某种条件下互相需要。照这个读法，条件一变，互因就解除了。

不是这样。非和余的互因是必然的，不是偶然的。因为非不能在"某种条件下"彻底运作、在"另一种条件下"不彻底——它永远不彻底，永远留余。一个能独立于余而操作的非，就不是方法论 0 论证的那个非。同样，余也不是"在某种条件下才依赖非"的东西：痕迹这个概念本身就预设了产生者，不依赖产生者的痕迹不是痕迹，是别的东西。

所以非对称互因不是两个实体之间的关系，是两者各自作为自身的结构特征。

还可以再往回收一层：非追问自身这个动作，本身就是非对称互因的最小单元。方法论 0 用一个动词短语（非追问自身）指认那个核心动作，方法论 00 用一个名词短语（非对称互因）指认同一个动作的结构。两者指的是同一件事，只是一个从运作过程的角度命名，一个从结构关系的角度命名。

哲学传统里有一个词可以借来支撑这个结构：海德格尔在《存在与时间》第 28 节用的 Gleichursprünglichkeit，同样源始性。他用它描述此在的世界性与自我性——两个结构在源头层级同等源初，不能被还原为一方派生另一方。

但借用必须有边界，而这个边界正好是全篇的分寸所在。海德格尔那里的同源是对称的：世界性和自我性地位平等。非和余的同源是不对称的：非仍然给出余的存在条件，余只给出非继续运作的动力条件。

所以这里借的是源初性的结构义（两者在源头层级不可分离），不借它的两端对称性（两者地位相等）。如果连对称性一起借过来，余就被抬到和非并列的本原地位，整篇的论证当场垮掉。

主命题因此可以一句话说完：公理层上非独一，操作层上非与余成对。

六、符号里的不对称

非对称互因的形式化写出来是这样：

𝒩 —opi→ ρi —驱动→ 𝒩 —opi+1→ ρi+1

这个公式最值得看的地方不在箭头，在下标的分布。

𝒩 没有下标。公式里它出现几次都指同一个非——公理层独一，不需要版本编号。而 op 和 ρ 都有下标：运作有序数意义上的展开，余有各自可区分的具体形态。

所以符号的不对称分布直接编码了概念的不对称。一眼看过去公式就在说：这两个东西不对等，一个是统一的指向（无下标），一个是可区分的产物（有下标）。

下标要读对。它不是时间。时间本身是 3DD 的产物，而非先于 3DD——把下标读成时间，就等于把非拉进了它自己的产物里。下标标记的是逻辑展开的层级，或者说分形嵌套的深度。

同样，箭头也不是"非之后出现余"的先后关系。箭头表达的是结构性的依赖方向：非给出余的存在条件。这个"给出"不需要时间流逝来实现。

还有一层精细化值得记下。op 作用于 𝒩 实际产生的不只是余，还有构。构是沉淀物，余项是沉淀不能封闭的那部分。两者同源于一次运作，但不是一回事。所以更精确的写法是 𝒩 —opi→ (Ci, ρi)，而推动下一次运作的是 ρi 不是 Ci——构本身不推动什么，只有构不能封闭的那部分才推动。

这个区分在下一节会用上。

七、为什么是这样：一堵不能绕过去的墙

到这里会浮起一个自然的追问：为什么非和余是非对称互因的关系，而不是别的什么关系？为什么非必然留余？为什么余必然推动下一次非？这些"必然"的来源在哪？

这个追问看起来很合理，但它其实已经撞到了边界。

它预设非对称互因这个结构本身还能被追问到更深的来源——存在某个比它更根本的东西，能解释"为什么是这样而不是那样"。而这个预设的结构，和方法论 0 里"非从哪来"那个追问一模一样。

方法论 0 已经论证过那个追问为什么必然碰壁：任何追问都需要方向，而方向是 3DD 的产物；非先于 3DD，所以用方向去追问非，等于用非的产物给非指方向。这就是"凿碰到了凿"、"锤子砸锤子完好无损"的确切意思——物自体在这里显现为追问本身在结构上的不可能。

而非对称互因是非作为运作本身的结构。所以追问"为什么非对称互因这样运作"，就是追问"为什么非是这样的非"——和"非从哪来"是同一堵墙的两种问法。

这不是取巧。物自体不是这一篇为了脱身才引入的概念，是方法论 0 已经立好的东西。而且反过来说，不承认这堵墙才是真正的问题：那就要用非的产物去解释非本身，论证链条上会出现循环或者无穷后退。承认它，论证反而干净——能论证的地方充分展开，不可论证的地方诚实停下。

还有一个附带的收获，值得单独说。

两条路各自走到尽头，碰到的是同一堵墙。这不是巧合。如果它们碰到不同的物自体，那说明它们指向的根本不是同一个对象。碰到同一堵墙，恰恰是"两条路以同一个非为极限指向"这件事在边界处的再一次显现。

所以余之道走到最后碰到物自体，不是这条路的失败，是它和 via negativa 在同一堵墙前相遇。

八、为什么恰好是四相

方法论 0 从非追问自身推出四相：有、无、非"有也非无"、非"非有也非无"。那条推导走的是 via negativa——四相是追问的产物。

从余之道看过去，四相是非对称互因在展开过程中的四次显形。

第一相，有。非追问自身时，有两个参与项：作为追问者的非，作为被追问对象的非。追问者不能独立于被追问对象而操作（空转的追问不是追问），被追问对象不能独立于追问者的操作而被设立。这正是非对称互因的最小单元。而"两个非互相构成对方条件"这件事之所以能被说出来，是因为有"两个"作为共起的两端——这个可说性就是"有"。

第二相，无。"无"不是"有"的缺席。"有的缺席"仍然在"有"的框架里运作。"无"是"有"所依赖的那个共起条件被否定——它否定的不是"有"的实例，是"有"的成立基础。所以第二相也是一次非对称互因的显形，只是层级比第一相高一层：这次共起的两端，是整个第一相单元和对它的否定。

第三相，非有也非无。第一相有它没能覆盖的余，第二相也有。两个余在这里碰到一起，都指向"非对称互因还有没显形的部分"。碰撞产生一个新的单元，它的内容是对"有无二分"本身的否定——不是在有和无之外提出第三个产物。

第四相，非"非有也非无"。第三相产生的整合态本身已经是一个完整的非对称互因单元。第四相的追问对象因此不再是某个具体产物，而是"非对称互因单元"这个结构本身。结构碰到结构——这就构成闭合。第五次追问产生的仍然是非对称互因单元，只是在同一自指结构里重复，不产生新的结构类型。

把四相合起来：第一相是简单共起，第二相是对共起的翻转，第三相是翻转产物的整合，第四相是整合的自指。四相不是四个不相关的产物，是同一个结构在展开时能走完的全部步骤。

方法论 0 回答的是"四相从何而来"——追问生成。方法论 00 回答的是"四相为何恰好是四相"——非对称互因的展开只能走四步。两个回答互补。

九、五切面是同一个结构的不同粒度

凿构循环有五个切面：凿、构、余项、桥、物自体。方法论 M 从 via negativa 的展开面把它们一一命名，各自都有确切位置。余之道看到的是它们的共同来源。

凿是互因里非的那一面——凿运作就是非的运作，对应公式里的 op。

构是运作的沉淀物，对应 Ci。余项是沉淀不能封闭的那部分，对应 ρi。这里要守住上一节那个区分：构不是余项，构之不彻底才显为余项。

由此可以回答方法论 M 里一个看起来有点神秘的论断——为什么构没有方向而余项有方向。因为构只是沉淀物，沉淀物本身不指向任何地方；而余项是"不能封闭"的那部分，而"不能封闭"这个状态本身就指向下一次。方向不是余项额外带的属性，是它的定义带出来的。

桥是余项累积到临界迫使凿再次运作的那个瞬间。它对应公式里从 ρi 到 opi+1 的那一步。但公式里那一步是抽象的过渡，桥给这个过渡一个具体机制。没有桥这个切面，凿构循环只能说"余推动下一次凿"，说不出"余如何推动"。

物自体是结构碰到自身的极限。一般展开中非对称互因有非和余两个可区分的面向；但当两端都是非本身时——凿碰到凿——没有余作为被区分的另一端，只剩非作为两端。追问到这里发现自己在用凿追问凿本身。

五切面因此不是五个并列的概念，是一个结构在不同粒度上的显形：凿和构是结构本身，余项是结构的动力，桥是结构的运作机制，物自体是结构的极限。

十、16DD：余的第二种身份

这一节是整篇的落点，也是余之道走到最远处看到的东西。

先立一个区分，它是理解后面所有内容的前提：余在不同层级上身份不同。

在 1DD 到 15DD 的范围内，余是动力项。它的作用是驱动非的下一次运作，让循环能继续。这是前面几节一直在用的那个余。

在 16DD 的主体相遇位置上，余是不可吞噬的外在性。它不再驱动什么，它构成对他者目的性的承认。

为什么必须做这个区分？因为不做的话会出一场伦理灾难。

16DD 是两个主体互相承认对方是目的本身的位置。如果把这里的"互为对方的余"读成动力项意义上的余，那么主体 A 就成了主体 B 运作的驱动项——A 是 B 用来推动自己的材料。这正是"把人当手段"的精确定义，直接违反 SAE 的核心命题。

存在论余这个概念挡住了这个滑落。

展开来说。主体 A 是一个完整的非对称互因单元，在自己的序列里持续展开。主体 B 也是。两个单元相遇时，A 作为整体出现在 B 的视野里——而 A 不是 B 的构所能生成的产物。

这里要做一个关键的精确化。B 不能吞噬 A，这件事不是 B 的认知能力不够。如果理解成能力问题，那 A 的独立性就依赖 B 的能力不足，B 一旦足够强大就可以吞噬 A——16DD 的伦理含义就被架空了。

精确的理解是：B 的构再完备也不改变 A 作为独立互因单元的存在论事实。不是 B 触达不到 A（那是能力问题），是 B 的任何构都不在 A 那个位置上（那是存在论问题）。A 的位置就是 A 自己。

所以从 B 看过去，A 是一种存在论余——不是 B 的运作能产生的痕迹，是 B 的构所不能封闭的外在性。反过来也一样。两个方向合起来，就是"互相承认对方是目的本身"。

而这个承认不是一种伦理态度或主观选择，是两个非对称互因单元相遇时结构上必然出现的关系。

由此得到这一篇最重的那个结论。

康德的"人是目的本身，不只是手段"，在康德那里是作为绝对律令直接给出的，辩护依赖一个预设：人作为理性存在者具有内在价值。这个预设本身可以被质疑——功利主义者就会问，为什么是理性具有内在价值，而不是幸福或效用。

余之道给同一个命题提供了另一条辩护路径。不是"不应该"吞噬他者，是结构上"不能"吞噬。而既然不能吞噬，唯一在结构上可行的姿态就是承认对方作为完整单元。这里的"必须"是结构意义上的无法不，不是伦理律令意义上的应该。

于是"人是目的"从一个以理性内在价值为预设的律令，变成非对称互因结构在 16DD 位置的必然推论。

这条路径有它的好处：它不依赖任何价值预设，所以功利主义式的质疑在它面前没有落点——你可以质疑"理性具有内在价值"这个价值判断，但你不能用价值质疑去反驳"A 作为完整单元在结构上不可被 B 吞噬"这个结构陈述。

但要说两层限定，否则这个结论会被读得过头。

第一，这是并行补充不是替代。康德那条以理性内在价值为预设的路仍然在它自己的层级上成立。两条路互补，共同加强命题的辩护强度。这个系列承接康德的姿态始终是完成，不是挑战。

第二，这条路也有自己的起点，而起点同样可以被拒绝。康德的起点是"理性具有内在价值"，余之道的起点是"非是唯一公理"。两个起点都不是绝对不可拒绝的。区别只在于：拒绝余之道的起点，需要走到比拒绝康德的起点更深一层的地方——因为前者是公理层，后者是预设层。

最后还有一个由此打开的理解，关于 DD 序列的两端。

0DD 和 16DD 一直被看作有某种同构关系——都是序列的端点，凿构循环在两端都到达某种极限。非对称互因给这个同构一个结构说明。

0DD 是非对称互因尚未分化的状态。在那个位置，非与余还没有分化成可明确区分的两个面向，凿和构未分——构的独立显形和余项的独立显形需要足够的结构展开层次，而在这个基底位置那些层次还没长出来。要强调的是，"未分化"不是时间意义上的"还没发生"，是逻辑架构意义上的"结构还没展开到那一层"。

16DD 是完全分化之后的重合。经过 1DD 到 15DD 的完整展开，非和余已经分开，每个主体都长成一个完整的互因单元；而当一个单元作为整体出现时，它的凿和它的构合为一体。两个整体相遇，就是两个"凿构重合体"的相遇。

所以 0DD 是凿构未分，16DD 是凿构重合。两端都是非对称互因的完整状态——一端是潜在的完整，另一端是展开的完整。DD 序列因此不是从起点到终点的一条线，是同一个结构从潜在到展开到相遇的一个完整周期。

这也解释了为什么物自体恰好在两端各显现一次：0DD 处它作为运作的前提显现，16DD 处它作为运作的极限显现。因为两端都是完整状态，所以两端都会碰到这个结构自己的极限。

十一、以余项结尾

方法论 00 不给结论，以余项清单结尾。这个做法本身就是它所论证的东西的实践：论文是构，构必然留余，所以论文应该显式列出自己产生的余，而不是假装论证已经完备。

它列了六条。公理层与操作层的两层划分是否彻底，没有论证；非对称互因的形式化是否完整表达了这个结构的全部特征，没有论证；余之道相对 via negativa 的独立性（等价不等于独立），没有独立论证；借用海德格尔那个术语的精确度，没有做完术语史比较；跨论域的结构同构，留给独立的接口工作。

还有一条最隐蔽的：

方法论 00 自己是一篇论文，论文是构。它论证非的每一次运作都必然留余，那么它自己作为一次运作也必然留余。它在自己身上应用了自己指出的那个不彻底性。

说得越多，构得越多；构得越多，余得越多。

这条余项比其他几条都更难看见，因为它藏在论证自身里面。而把它写出来，就是余之道作为方法在自己身上的最后一次运用——追问到最后，追问碰到了追问者。

What Methodology 0 does is point directly: Negativa is the sole axiom, everything else a theorem. What Methodology VII does is systematize the road that approaches it — from Pseudo-Dionysius to Maimonides to Nicholas of Cusa, two thousand years of negative theology stripping away, layer by layer, what Negativa is not. That road is called via negativa.

What this essay says is that it is not the only road.

The reason is hidden inside Methodology 0's own argument. Every time Negativa operates it leaves a remainder. The remainder is not an added axiom; it is the necessary accompaniment of Negativa's operating. Read that sentence backwards and a second road opens: as long as Negativa operates, remainder is left over; and wherever a remainder is visible, the trace of an operation is visible.

So one need not enter from the Negativa end. One can enter from the remainder end — not by excluding what Negativa is not, but by tracing the marks it leaves back to it. Opposite direction, different starting point, and the same Negativa as limit-indication.

That road is called via rho, the way of the remainder.

1. A Dual, Not a Supplement

The relation between the two roads has to be stated precisely first, because it determines the character of everything that follows.

Via rho and via negativa are duals. Not competitors, and not supplements.

Competition would mean one of them comes closer to the truth than the other. Supplementation would mean one fills a gap in the other. Duality means both are complete in themselves and enter the same thing from different directions.

This distinction is not fastidiousness about wording. If it were supplementation, via negativa would have a gap and Methodology VII's work would be incomplete. If it were competition, one of the two roads would have to be the lesser. Duality says: both roads are walkable, both can be walked to the end, both point at the same Negativa — and what they see differs. That difference is not because one of them is looking wrong. It is because Negativa itself has two faces.

The numbering carries this structure. Methodology 0 points directly at Negativa; Methodology VII gives the first road toward it a paper of its own; Methodology 00 proposes that road's dual. The two zeros in 00 correspond to the two endpoints of the way of the remainder: the first zero is the starting point, remainder; the second is the destination, Negativa. The via in between has no character to carry it — and that empty space is precisely where all of this paper's argument takes place.

2. A Remainder Is Not Arbitrary Residue

For this road to exist, it first has to be shown walkable. And the first condition of walkability is that the remainder must have direction. If a remainder were just an undifferentiated heap of residue left over from an operation, there would be nothing to trace.

A remainder has direction. Every remainder is the specific incompleteness left by one specific operation of Negativa, and that specificity is itself directionality.

More precisely: a remainder is not an independently existing thing but a trace. And a trace necessarily points at the act that produced it. To see a footprint is to see that someone walked — the entire meaning of a footprint lies in its pointing back at the step that made it. The same holds for the remainder.

One misreading has to be blocked here. The remainder is not a "product" of Negativa's operation in the sense of an independent entity. If it were an independent entity, it would constitute a second origin alongside Negativa, and Methodology 0's sole axiom would break on the spot. The remainder is a trace, and a trace depends ontologically on the operation that produced it; it cannot exist independently. That dependence is precisely the ground of its directionality.

The second condition is whether that pointing can be traced.

It can, and no external instrument is needed. Because the act of tracing is itself another operation of Negativa — to trace is to negate the remainder's closure and ask where it came from. Tracing a remainder means performing a negation on it, so that it points at its own condition of production.

This step has an elegant consequence: via rho is not a road requiring special equipment. Anyone who sees a remainder is already tracing. The moment someone asks "where did this incompleteness come from," they are standing on the way of the remainder. Its feasibility is as fundamental as via negativa's; neither depends on any mechanism beyond Negativa's own operating.

3. Does the Tracing Diverge?

Here is an objection that has to be met head-on, and the way it is met is where this paper shows its craft.

The objection runs: the act of tracing a remainder is itself a new operation of Negativa, and that operation necessarily leaves a new remainder. Tracing the new remainder is another operation, leaving another remainder. Doesn't the tracing then simply manufacture remainders forever, never arriving at Negativa?

The objection rests on an intuition: recursion means divergence.

But via rho's recursion does not diverge. To say why, one first has to refuse a formulation that sounds entirely natural.

One cannot say "the new remainder is closer to Negativa than the old one." Because "closer" presupposes a metric, presupposes measurable distance — and that amounts to placing Negativa inside a metric space, treating it as a point with coordinates. Negativa cannot be objectified; it has no coordinates. Talk of "distance" to something unobjectifiable does not work at all.

The correct formulation is: via rho's recursion is not a decrease of distance in a metric space but a nested peeling in the topological sense.

Concretely. The original remainder, as a trace, is wrapped in a surface layer of apparent closure — it looks like something self-sufficient. Questioning peels that shell away and exposes the deeper structure the trace points at; that exposure is the new remainder. The new remainder has a shell of its own, and the next questioning peels again.

So each act of questioning does not "shorten a distance." It "peels off a layer of pseudo-closure."

An image: the tracing is like a series of nested filters. Each new remainder is a finer mesh, containing the whole structure of the previous layer plus one more layer of questioning directed at it. The mesh grows finer and finer. But a filter is never the thing it is trying to separate out. We cannot measure how far Negativa still is; we only know the mesh is monotonically tightening.

The structure can be stated harder: ρj has a higher order of manifestation than ρi if and only if ρj explicitly contains the trace of questioning directed at ρi's condition of production. This is a structural partial order, not a metric distance. We do not say "ρj is nearer to Negativa by so much"; we say only "ρj manifests one layer more than ρi."

This handling incidentally leaves Zeno at the door. Achilles cannot catch the tortoise because Zeno assumed motion across distance in a metric space — each step covering half the remaining gap, so the gap is never closed. The way of the remainder concerns nested structure under topological peeling, not the crossing of a distance. Zeno's paradox here is not resolved; it is found inapplicable. The two are not the same kind of problem to begin with.

The temperament of via rho as a method shows itself here.

It is not a method of awaiting revelation — no single negation arrives. Nor is it a deduction anticipating completion — nothing finishes when the derivation ends. It is a method of sustained questioning. It never reaches a terminus one can hold, because Negativa cannot be objectified and cannot be held in the hand; and yet no step is wasted, because every step is a real structural peeling rather than turning in place.

4. Both Roads Point at the Same Negativa

Walkability settled, one question remains: is the limit via rho reaches the same as the limit via negativa reaches? If not, the two are not duals but two unrelated roads.

They are the same. The argument turns on whether the two descriptions structurally co-refer.

The Negativa that via negativa arrives at is described as "the limit object that is unnameable, unknowable, unconstructible." Unnameable, because naming is construction. Unknowable, because knowing presupposes a subject–object split, and that split is a result of Negativa having already operated. Unconstructible, because constructs are sediment of Negativa's operating, not Negativa itself.

The Negativa that via rho arrives at is described as "the common condition of operation of all remainders." And for that condition to play the role of what all remainders jointly point at, it must be unnameable (it is the condition of construction, not a construct), unknowable (it is antecedent to the subject–object split and cannot be objectified), and unconstructible (all constructs are products of its operating).

The three "un-"s correspond one to one. Not by coincidence: the two descriptions are saying the same thing from different angles — one from the angle of excluding what it is not, the other from the angle of what its traces jointly point toward.

But the faces the two roads reveal differ, and that difference is where the value of the duality actually lies.

Via negativa makes Negativa appear as a limit. Through exclusion it appears as the common exterior of every "is not" — a static, unreachable position existing as a boundary. This corresponds to a topological limit in mathematics: an unreachable boundary outside the closure.

Via rho makes Negativa appear as a condition. Through tracing, it appears as the common source of all operating — a dynamic position, continuously present, existing as a presupposition. This corresponds to an analytic limit: the value a sequence converges toward.

The same Negativa carries both roles at once.

Which means the two roads defend each other against the other's characteristic misreading.

Walk only via negativa, and Negativa is easily read as a purely negative object — unnameable, unknowable, unconstructible to the point of near-vacancy. The reader asks: what distinguishes this purely negative thing from nothing at all? Via negativa can only answer by going on excluding — Negativa is not nothingness either.

Walk only via rho, and Negativa is easily read as a persistently present dynamic background, and from there misread as a reified first cause. The reader asks: what distinguishes this continuously present condition from some primordial entity? Via rho can only answer by insisting again on unobjectifiability.

Together: unreachability prevents Negativa from being reified, continuous presence prevents it from being voided. That is why neither road can replace the other.

There is a further correspondence of position worth recording. Via negativa is a posteriori: it starts from constructs already given and excludes them layer by layer, and each step depends on existing content being present — someone must first propose a construct before there is anything to exclude. Via rho is a priori: it does not start from phenomena but from the structural fact that remainder is the necessary accompaniment of Negativa's operating. "There is remainder" is not an empirical observation; it is a structural feature of operating itself.

Methodology II built an epistemological map: a direction axis (a priori / a posteriori) crossed with an operation axis (synthetic / analytic), yielding four quadrants — deduction, reduction, induction, abduction — and argued that none of the four may be settled in, that all must be traversed. But Methodology II did not propose the traversal itself as a method. The two viae supply exactly that layer: via negativa is the methodological self-awareness of the a posteriori axis, via rho of the a priori axis. Together they cover the whole map.

This reveals a deeper duality between the two papers. Methodology II speaks at the level of the subject's movement (the subject pushed through the four quadrants by remainder); Methodology 00 speaks at the level of operational ontology (Negativa operates and leaves remainder, remainder drives the next operation). "Remainder pushes the subject through" and "remainder drives the next Negativa" are the same structure manifesting at two levels of description.

The way of the remainder has a mathematical carrier too: ZFCρ. ZFC, through extensional closure, expresses "choice as operative act" as a choice function — that is, reduces it to a set of ordered pairs. What ZFCρ does is name the structural trace that reduction leaves behind as ρ. It is not a set, not an element of any model; it is a metatheoretic mark on ZFC's design choice.

That is precisely via rho realized mathematically. It does not try to eliminate all remainder from the formal system — that would be the mathematization of via negativa. It acknowledges remainder as the trace extensional closure cannot absorb, and approaches Negativa as condition of operation from there. ZFCρ's three laws of chaos — cannot not develop (ρ ≠ ∅), cannot not have direction (ρ is specific), cannot not be cut (ρ is itself formalizable) — are the precise mathematical expression of via rho's basic structure.

So SAE using via negativa in its philosophical work and ZFCρ in its mathematical work is not two disciplines going their separate ways. It is one pair of dual methods unfolding in two disciplines at once.

5. Asymmetric Mutual Causation

Everything so far has established that the way of the remainder is walkable and lands where it should. From here on is what the road independently sees once walked — the core of Methodology 00 as independent work.

The core finding is called asymmetric mutual causation.

The definition first. Two terms A and B stand in mutual causation if and only if two conditions hold simultaneously: first, A cannot operate independently of B; second, B cannot exist independently of A.

Note that the two conditions have different modalities. The first concerns the operational layer (can A act), the second the existential layer (can B be). They are not two symmetrical faces of one relation; they are two directions in different modalities.

That is the exact sense of "asymmetric." Symmetric mutual causation would require the two terms to depend on each other within the same modality — either both cannot exist or both cannot operate. Asymmetric mutual causation requires only that both directions hold simultaneously in different modalities. The structural force lies in both directions holding at once, not in their sharing a modality.

This clarification is the most consequential in the paper. Without it a reader defaults to assuming mutual causation is symmetric, and concludes that the two terms have equal standing — which would raise remainder to the status of a co-original principle alongside Negativa, and Methodology 0's sole axiom would be gone on the spot.

Now substitute Negativa and remainder.

Negativa cannot operate independently of remainder. This follows directly from the conservation of remainder: every operation of Negativa is necessarily incomplete, and that incompleteness appears as remainder. There is no "pure operation of Negativa" unaccompanied by remainder.

Remainder cannot exist independently of Negativa. This is the ontology of traces: no operation, no trace.

Both conditions hold, in different modalities. Negativa and remainder therefore satisfy asymmetric mutual causation.

It is worth pausing on the character of this result. Methodology 0 had already argued both halves separately — remainder is the product of Negativa questioning itself, and remainder drives the next operation. Methodology 0 kept them apart in order to hold the line that remainder is not an independent thing. What Methodology 00 does is set those two existing conclusions side by side and notice that together they are exactly the two directions of asymmetric mutual causation.

So this is neither a new axiom nor a revision. It is a name for a structure that was already there. And the name earns its keep: with it, the rereadings in the later sections have something to hold onto.

A second misreading has to be blocked: taking mutual causation for a special dependence between two independent entities — each of which could in principle exist alone, and which merely happen to need each other under some condition. On that reading, change the condition and the mutual causation dissolves.

It does not work that way. The mutual causation of Negativa and remainder is necessary, not contingent. Because Negativa cannot operate completely "under some conditions" and incompletely "under others" — it is always incomplete, always leaves remainder. A Negativa able to operate independently of remainder is simply not the Negativa Methodology 0 argued for. Likewise, remainder is not something that "depends on Negativa under certain conditions": the concept of a trace already presupposes a producer, and a trace independent of its producer is not a trace but something else.

So asymmetric mutual causation is not a relation between two entities. It is a structural feature of each, as itself.

It can be drawn in one step tighter: the act of Negativa questioning itself is already the minimal unit of asymmetric mutual causation. Methodology 0 named that core act with a verb phrase (Negativa questions itself); Methodology 00 names the same act's structure with a noun phrase (asymmetric mutual causation). Same referent, named once from the angle of process and once from the angle of structure.

There is a term in the philosophical tradition that can be borrowed to support this: Gleichursprünglichkeit, equiprimordiality, which Heidegger uses in §28 of Being and Time. He uses it to describe Dasein's worldhood and selfhood — two structures equally primordial at the level of origin, neither reducible to a derivation of the other.

But the borrowing must have a boundary, and that boundary is exactly where the whole paper's proportion is decided. Heidegger's equiprimordiality is symmetric: worldhood and selfhood are of equal standing. The co-originality of Negativa and remainder is asymmetric: Negativa still gives remainder its condition of existence, while remainder gives only the dynamic condition for Negativa to continue operating.

So what is borrowed is the structural sense of primordiality — that the two are inseparable at the level of origin — and not the symmetry of the two ends. Borrow the symmetry along with it and remainder is raised to a co-original principle beside Negativa, and the argument collapses on the spot.

The main thesis can therefore be said in a line: at the axiomatic layer Negativa is singular; at the operational layer Negativa and remainder come in a pair.

6. The Asymmetry Inside the Notation

Written out, the formalization of asymmetric mutual causation looks like this:

𝒩 —opi→ ρi —drives→ 𝒩 —opi+1→ ρi+1

The most worthwhile thing in this formula is not the arrows. It is the distribution of the subscripts.

𝒩 carries no subscript. However many times it appears, it refers to the same Negativa — singular at the axiomatic layer, needing no version number. Whereas op and ρ both carry subscripts: operations unfold in an ordinal sense, and remainders have distinguishable concrete forms.

So the asymmetric distribution of the symbols directly encodes the asymmetry of the concepts. At a glance the formula says: these two are not equals — one is a unified indication (no subscript), the other a distinguishable product (subscripted).

The subscripts must be read correctly. They are not time. Time is itself a product of 3DD, and Negativa precedes 3DD — read the subscripts as time and you have dragged Negativa into its own product. What they mark is the level of logical unfolding, or the depth of fractal nesting.

Likewise the arrows are not a "Negativa first, then remainder" succession. An arrow expresses a structural direction of dependence: Negativa gives remainder its condition of existence. That giving requires no passage of time to occur.

One refinement is worth recording. What op applied to 𝒩 actually produces is not only remainder but also construct. The construct is the sediment; the remainder is the part the sediment cannot close over. Both issue from one operation, but they are not the same thing. So the more precise writing is 𝒩 —opi→ (Ci, ρi), and what drives the next operation is ρi, not Ci — a construct by itself pushes nothing; only the part it cannot close over pushes.

That distinction gets used in the next section.

7. Why It Works This Way: A Wall That Cannot Be Gone Around

A natural question surfaces here: why are Negativa and remainder in a relation of asymmetric mutual causation rather than some other relation? Why must Negativa leave remainder? Why must remainder drive the next operation? Where do these necessities come from?

The question looks entirely reasonable, and it has already hit the boundary.

It presupposes that the structure of asymmetric mutual causation can itself be traced to a deeper source — that something more fundamental exists which explains why it is this way rather than another. And the structure of that presupposition is identical to the question "where does Negativa come from" in Methodology 0.

Methodology 0 already argued why that question necessarily hits a wall: every act of questioning requires a direction, and direction is a product of 3DD; Negativa precedes 3DD, so using direction to question Negativa amounts to using Negativa's own product to give Negativa a direction. That is the exact sense of "the chisel strikes the chisel," of the hammer that strikes a hammer and takes no damage — the thing-in-itself appearing here as the structural impossibility of the questioning itself.

And asymmetric mutual causation is the structure of Negativa as operating. So asking "why does asymmetric mutual causation work this way" is asking "why is Negativa this Negativa" — the same wall, phrased differently.

This is not a dodge. The thing-in-itself is not a concept introduced here to escape; Methodology 0 had already set it in place. And conversely, refusing to acknowledge the wall is the real problem: it would mean using Negativa's products to explain Negativa itself, producing circularity or infinite regress in the chain of argument. Acknowledging it keeps the argument clean — fully unfolded where argument is possible, honestly halted where it is not.

There is also an incidental gain, worth stating on its own.

Each road, walked to its end, hits the same wall. This is not a coincidence. Had they hit different things-in-themselves, that would show they were pointing at different objects. Hitting the same wall is exactly the fact that both point at the same Negativa, appearing once more at the boundary.

So the way of the remainder meeting the thing-in-itself at the end is not the failure of that road. It is the two roads meeting each other before the same wall.

8. Why Exactly Four Phases

Methodology 0 derives four phases from Negativa questioning itself: being; nothing; neither-being-nor-nothing; and the negation of "neither-being-nor-nothing." That derivation runs along via negativa — the four phases as products of questioning.

Seen from the way of the remainder, the four phases are four manifestations of asymmetric mutual causation in the course of its unfolding.

First phase, being. When Negativa questions itself there are two participants: Negativa as questioner and Negativa as the object questioned. The questioner cannot operate independently of the object (a questioning that spins freely is not a questioning); the object cannot be posited independently of the questioner's operating. This is exactly the minimal unit of asymmetric mutual causation. And the reason "two Negativas mutually constituting each other's condition" can be said at all is that there are "two" as the ends of a co-arising — and that sayability is being.

Second phase, nothing. Nothing is not the absence of being. "The absence of being" still operates within being's frame. Nothing is the negation of the co-arising condition on which being depends — it negates not instances of being but the ground on which being stands. So the second phase is also a manifestation of asymmetric mutual causation, one level higher: the two ends of this co-arising are the whole first-phase unit and its negation.

Third phase, neither being nor nothing. The first phase has a remainder it failed to cover; so does the second. Here the two remainders collide, both pointing at "the part of asymmetric mutual causation not yet manifested." The collision produces a new unit whose content is the negation of the being–nothing dichotomy itself — not a third product proposed alongside being and nothing.

Fourth phase, the negation of "neither being nor nothing." The integrated state produced by the third phase is already a complete unit of asymmetric mutual causation. So the fourth phase's object of questioning is no longer any particular product but the structure of the unit itself. Structure meets structure — and that constitutes closure. A fifth questioning still produces a unit of asymmetric mutual causation; it merely repeats within the same self-referential structure, generating no new structural type.

Taken together: the first phase is simple co-arising, the second the inversion of that co-arising, the third the integration of the inversion's products, the fourth the self-reference of that integration. The four phases are not four unrelated products but every step one structure can take as it unfolds.

Methodology 0 answers where the four phases come from — questioning generates them. Methodology 00 answers why there are exactly four — the unfolding of asymmetric mutual causation can take only four steps. The two answers are complementary.

9. Five Facets of One Structure at Different Granularities

The chisel-construct cycle has five facets: chisel, construct, remainder, bridge, thing-in-itself. Methodology M named them one by one from the unfolding face of via negativa, each with its precise position. What the way of the remainder sees is their common source.

Chisel is the Negativa face of the mutual causation — the chisel operating is Negativa operating, corresponding to op in the formula.

Construct is the sediment of the operation, corresponding to Ci. Remainder is the part the sediment cannot close over, corresponding to ρi. The distinction from the previous section must be held here: construct is not remainder; it is the construct's incompleteness that appears as remainder.

From this follows an answer to a claim in Methodology M that looks slightly mysterious — why construct has no direction while remainder does. Because a construct is only sediment, and sediment by itself points nowhere; whereas remainder is the part that "cannot be closed," and the state of not-being-closable itself points at a next time. Direction is not an extra property remainder happens to carry. It falls out of its definition.

Bridge is the instant at which accumulated remainder reaches a threshold and forces the chisel to operate again. It corresponds to the step from ρi to opi+1 in the formula. But in the formula that step is an abstract transition; the bridge gives the transition a concrete mechanism. Without this facet, the cycle could say "remainder drives the next chisel" but not "how."

Thing-in-itself is the limit at which the structure meets itself. In ordinary unfolding, asymmetric mutual causation has two distinguishable faces, Negativa and remainder; but when both ends are Negativa itself — chisel striking chisel — there is no remainder as the other, distinguishable end. Only Negativa at both ends. Questioning this far, one finds oneself using the chisel to question the chisel.

The five facets are therefore not five parallel concepts but one structure manifesting at different granularities: chisel and construct are the structure itself, remainder its dynamics, bridge its mechanism of operation, thing-in-itself its limit.

10. 16DD: The Remainder's Second Identity

This section is where the whole essay lands, and what the way of the remainder sees at its furthest point.

A distinction has to be set down first, because it is the precondition for everything after: remainder has different identities at different levels.

Within 1DD through 15DD, remainder is a dynamical term. Its function is to drive Negativa's next operation, to let the cycle continue. This is the remainder used throughout the preceding sections.

At 16DD, the position where subjects meet, remainder is an un-swallowable externality. It no longer drives anything; it constitutes the recognition of the other's status as an end.

Why must this distinction be made? Because without it an ethical catastrophe follows.

16DD is the position at which two subjects recognize each other as ends in themselves. If "being each other's remainder" here is read in the dynamical sense, then subject A becomes the driving term of subject B's operating — A is material B uses to push itself along. That is the precise definition of treating a person as a means, in direct violation of SAE's core proposition.

The concept of ontological remainder blocks that slide.

Unfolded: subject A is a complete unit of asymmetric mutual causation, unfolding continuously in its own sequence. So is subject B. When the two units meet, A appears as a whole within B's field — and A is not a product B's constructs can generate.

A crucial precision is required here. That B cannot swallow A is not a matter of B's cognitive capacity being insufficient. Read as a capacity problem, A's independence would depend on B's inadequacy, and a sufficiently powerful B could swallow A — the ethical content of 16DD would be hollowed out.

The precise understanding is this: however complete B's constructs become, that does not alter the ontological fact of A being an independent unit of mutual causation. It is not that B cannot reach A (a capacity problem); it is that none of B's constructs is in A's position (an ontological one). A's position is A itself.

So from B's side, A is an ontological remainder — not a trace B's operating could produce, but an externality B's constructs cannot close over. And the same in reverse. Both directions together are what "recognizing each other as ends in themselves" means.

And that recognition is not an ethical attitude or a subjective choice. It is the relation that structurally must appear when two units of asymmetric mutual causation meet.

From which follows the heaviest conclusion in this essay.

Kant's "a person is an end in themselves, never merely a means" is given in Kant as a categorical imperative, and its justification rests on a presupposition: a person, as a rational being, has intrinsic worth. That presupposition can be challenged — a utilitarian will ask why it is rationality that has intrinsic worth rather than happiness or utility.

The way of the remainder supplies a different justification for the same proposition. It is not that one ought not swallow the other; it is that structurally one cannot. And since one cannot, the only structurally available posture is to recognize the other as a complete unit. The "must" here is the structural cannot-not, not the moral ought.

So "persons as ends" moves from an imperative resting on the intrinsic worth of rationality to a necessary corollary of asymmetric mutual causation at the 16DD position.

This path has an advantage: it depends on no value presupposition, so a utilitarian objection has nothing to land on. One may dispute the value judgment that rationality has intrinsic worth; one cannot use a value objection to refute the structural statement that A, as a complete unit, cannot be swallowed by B.

But two qualifications are needed, or the conclusion gets read too far.

First, this is a parallel supplement, not a replacement. Kant's route, resting on the intrinsic worth of rationality, still holds at its own level. The two routes are complementary and together strengthen the proposition's justification. This series' posture toward Kant has always been completion, never challenge.

Second, this route has a starting point of its own, and starting points can be refused. Kant's is "rationality has intrinsic worth"; the way of the remainder's is "Negativa is the sole axiom." Neither is absolutely unrefusable. The difference is only this: refusing the way of the remainder's starting point requires going one layer deeper than refusing Kant's — the former is an axiom, the latter a presupposition.

One further understanding opens from here, concerning the two ends of the DD sequence.

0DD and 16DD have long been seen as isomorphic in some fashion — both endpoints of the sequence, the cycle reaching some limit at each. Asymmetric mutual causation gives that isomorphism a structural account.

0DD is asymmetric mutual causation before differentiation. At that position Negativa and remainder have not yet separated into two clearly distinguishable faces; chisel and construct are undivided, because the independent manifestation of construct and of remainder requires enough layers of structural unfolding, and at this basal position those layers have not yet grown. To be emphasized: "undifferentiated" is not temporal — not "has not happened yet" — but architectural: the structure has not unfolded to that layer.

16DD is the coincidence after full differentiation. Through the complete unfolding of 1DD to 15DD, Negativa and remainder have separated and each subject has grown into a complete unit of mutual causation; and when a unit appears as a whole, its chisel and its construct coincide. Two wholes meeting is two chisel-construct coincidences meeting.

So 0DD is chisel and construct undivided, 16DD is chisel and construct coincident. Both ends are complete states of asymmetric mutual causation — one a potential completeness, the other an unfolded one. The DD sequence is therefore not a line from origin to terminus but one full cycle of a single structure, from potential through unfolding to encounter.

This also explains why the thing-in-itself appears exactly once at each end: at 0DD as the presupposition of operating, at 16DD as its limit. Because both ends are complete states, both meet the structure's own limit.

11. Ending on Remainders

Methodology 00 gives no conclusion. It ends on a list of its own remainders. The practice is itself an application of what it argues: a paper is a construct, a construct necessarily leaves remainder, and so a paper should list explicitly the remainder it produces rather than pretend the argument is complete.

It lists six. Whether the two-layer division into axiomatic and operational layers is exhaustive — not argued. Whether the formalization captures every feature of the structure — not argued. The independence of the way of the remainder relative to via negativa (equivalence is not independence) — not independently argued. The precision of the borrowing from Heidegger — no terminological history undertaken. Cross-domain structural isomorphisms — left to separate interface work.

And one more, the most concealed of them:

Methodology 00 is itself a paper, and a paper is a construct. It argues that every operation of Negativa necessarily leaves remainder; then it, as an operation, necessarily leaves remainder too. It applies to itself the very incompleteness it identifies.

The more that is said, the more is constructed; the more that is constructed, the more is left over.

This remainder is harder to see than the others because it is hidden inside the argument itself. And writing it down is the way of the remainder's final application to itself — questioning far enough that the questioning meets the questioner.
