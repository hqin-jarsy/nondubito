The Structure of Work

Work is never only a list of tasks. It distributes authority, responsibility, time, recognition, risk, and the right to leave. These thirty essays begin with familiar workplace tensions and ask how people can cooperate deeply without being reduced to roles.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Six parts · Thirty essays · English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese
