Stories and Structures · Collection 01

Structures in Fables

Fables are short because they close quickly: a reversal arrives, a moral lands, and the animals fall silent. These essays reopen twenty-three familiar endings and ask what their neat lessons leave for us to decide.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Four parts · Twenty-three essays · English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese
