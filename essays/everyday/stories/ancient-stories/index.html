Stories and Structures · Collection 02

Structures in Ancient Stories

Nineteen stories from China, Greece, India, Mesopotamia, Japan, Egypt, and the K’iche’ Maya world. Rather than extracting a single moral, each essay asks what an old scene still leaves unsettled about making, changing, knowing, and living with others.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Four parts · Nineteen essays · Eight languages

A six-essay path

Start with one scene from each movement of the collection. Every essay stands alone, and no theoretical background is required.
