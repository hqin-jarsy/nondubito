Everyday Life · Series 06

Structures Within Time

How does a life remain one life when awareness fades, memory fails, and the person changes? These essays move from waking, dreams, and forgetting to waiting, promises, routines, and death—asking how we reconnect our moments without letting the past or future possess the present.

This series is not a guide to time management and offers no medical, sleep, dream, meditation, or mental-health advice. It describes ordinary experience without presenting a theory of consciousness as settled science.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Six parts · Thirty essays · Eight languages

Where this leads

A person does not remain one person by never breaking, forgetting, or changing. This series follows the ways a life is reconnected through memory, responsibility, and renewed choice. For the wider framework, begin with Five Minutes to Understand Non Dubito.
