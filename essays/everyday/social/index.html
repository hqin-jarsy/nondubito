Everyday Life · Series 07

Structures Within Social Life

How can we enter shared life without surrendering our judgment—or turning other people into versions of ourselves? Thirty essays on adaptation, expression, rules, dissent, belonging, distance, and mature social life.

This is not a guide to networking, persuasion, popularity, or social manipulation. The essays examine ordinary experience and the terms of living with others.
