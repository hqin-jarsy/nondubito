The Structure of Conflict

Not every disagreement is a misunderstanding, and not every conflict ends in consensus. These thirty essays ask how two people can identify what is truly incompatible, separate disagreement from harm and power, and find an arrangement—or an ending—without turning either person into the problem.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Six parts · Thirty essays · English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese

A six-essay path

Read one essay from each part to move from recognizing conflict to protecting people, redesigning action, and ending without a final verdict. No theoretical background is required.

冲突里的结构

不是每一次分歧都是误会，也不是每一场冲突都必须以共识结束。这三十篇从日常争执进入，讨论怎样辨认真正不相容的要求，怎样把分歧与伤害、权力分开，并在不把任何一个人变成问题本身的前提下，寻找安排、边界或结束。

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · 六章 · 三十篇 · 英文、简体中文、繁体中文

衝突裡的結構

不是每一次分歧都是誤會，也不是每一場衝突都必須以共識結束。這三十篇從日常爭執進入，討論怎樣辨認真正不相容的要求，怎樣把分歧與傷害、權力分開，並在不把任何一個人變成問題本身的前提下，尋找安排、邊界或結束。
