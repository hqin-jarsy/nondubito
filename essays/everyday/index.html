Everyday Life

Every day we care, help, disappoint, apologize, depend, and leave. This section offers no quick relationship fixes. It slows down the moments that repeatedly hurt and asks what place each person is allowed to occupy.

How we read

No theory is required, and no one is diagnosed. Each essay stays with one familiar difficulty: who decides, who bears the cost, who may still refuse, and whose reasons actually enter a shared life.

日常生活

我们每天都在关心、帮助、失望、道歉、依赖和离开。这里不提供速成的关系技巧，而是从那些反复使人受苦的时刻开始，辨认一段生活究竟把谁放在了什么位置。

这里怎样写

不先要求读者学习理论，也不替任何人诊断。每篇文章只把一个熟悉的困难放慢：谁在决定，谁在承担，谁还能拒绝，谁的理由真正进入了共同生活。

日常生活
