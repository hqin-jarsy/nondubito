Everyday Life

Everyday life is where institutions and relationships become concrete: in what others may ask of us, which reasons count, who carries the cost, and whether we can still change direction.

How we read

No theory is required, and no one is diagnosed. Each essay slows down one familiar difficulty and asks who decides, who bears responsibility, and whether every person remains more than a function.

日常生活

日常生活是关系与制度真正落到人身上的地方：别人可以要求什么，谁的理由算数，谁承担代价，以及一个人是否还能改变方向。

这里怎样写

不先要求读者学习理论，也不替任何人诊断。每篇文章只把一个熟悉的困难放慢：谁在决定，谁在承担，以及每个人是否仍然不只是一项功能。
