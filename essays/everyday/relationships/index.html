Everyday Life · Series 01

Structures Within Relationships

The hardest moments in a relationship are not always failures of kindness. Often they concern who gets to decide, who carries consequences they did not choose, and whether a refusal is allowed to remain an answer. These thirty essays begin in ordinary language and ask how closeness can avoid becoming control without collapsing into indifference.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Six parts · Thirty essays · Eight languages
