Everyday Life · Series 01

The Structure of Relationships

The difficulties in relationships often lie not in someone’s lack of kindness, but in who decides for whom, who bears consequences they did not choose, and whose refusal is never allowed to count as an answer. These thirty essays begin with everyday language and gradually explore how closeness can avoid becoming a takeover without retreating into lives that have nothing to do with one another.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Six parts · Thirty essays · Eight languages

If you do not want to read everything at once: six starting points

Reading one essay from each chapter will give you an outline of this way of understanding relationships. No theoretical background is required, and you do not need to follow the numbering.
