The Structure of Money

Money protects shelter, time, refusal, and the ability to change course. It can also become the only measure of a life. These essays move from scarcity and enough to investment, status, shared finance, giving, and inheritance.

This series is not investment, tax, legal, insurance, or personalized financial advice. It recommends no asset, product, or allocation.
