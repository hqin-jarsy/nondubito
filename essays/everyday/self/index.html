Everyday Life · Series 04

Structures Within the Self

How do we observe, understand, and change ourselves without turning self-knowledge into another fixed identity? These essays move from thoughts and bodily states to shame, discipline, and the moment understanding can return us to the world.

This series is not medical advice and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medication, or crisis guidance. Bodily states are discussed as conditions of experience and judgment, never as substitutes for professional care.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Six parts · Thirty essays · Three languages
