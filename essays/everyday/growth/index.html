The Structure of Growth

Growth is often sold as endless self-improvement. These thirty essays begin elsewhere: with the difficult work of discovering what is worth carrying, developing the abilities it requires, and remaining open to correction without handing another person the authorship of your life.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Six parts · Thirty essays · English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese

A six-essay path

Read one essay from each part to follow the movement from borrowed measures to a direction that can learn, act, and remain open. No theoretical background is required.
