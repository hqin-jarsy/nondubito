Chapter 37 of 81

退后一步，不是撤去照顾

Step Back Without Withdrawing Care

秦汉（字大知）· Han Qin

一、忍不住想管

每一个当过 leader、建过组织、做过 platform 的人，都有过那个冲动——

看到事情在自然发生，忍不住想去管。

团队自己运转得好好的，忍不住想加个流程。社区自己长起来了，忍不住想立个规则。产品自己有了方向，忍不住想做个规划。孩子自己在探索，忍不住想纠正。

看到任何"自己在发生"的东西，手就痒，想去插一脚、定个调、立个框。

老子在《道德经》第三十七章里，讲的就是这个冲动的反面——

这一章放在道经结尾。按帛书德经在前、道经在后的次序，它也位于全书末尾。它让我们想象少一点强加的生活；不是许诺所有事情无人照看也会变好。

二、道恒无名

“道恒无名。”本系列选择沿“无名”的线索，连接后面的“无名之朴”。这里借用凿与构的语言来解释，是我们的哲学读法，不是说经文已经直接写出了这套术语。

给一件事命名，常常也在给它划边界。叫它“成功”，某些成果便站到前面，代价可能退到后面；叫一个孩子“听话”，很容易只注意他有没有服从，而忘了听他到底在想什么。名字帮助我们认识，也可能让我们误以为已经认识完了。

沿着这条线，“无名”可以提醒我们：不要让一个已有的名称，把还在发生的生命说死。“不构”也应当理解为不把自己的框架强加成他人唯一的可能，而不是从此放弃语言、制度、计划。

比如团队给自己定了“高效”的目标，仍可以问：这个效率是谁的效率，省下的时间有没有变成另一个人的加班？一个名字若还能接受这些问题，就在帮助理解；若把问题全挡在外面，就开始替现实作主。

通行本另有“道常无为而无不为”的读法，第四十八章也有“无为而无不为”。所以不能说老子从不用这组表达。选择“无名”这条路，不需要把另一条路贬成浅薄或错误。

三、让万物自化，不是放任

“侯王若能守之，万物将自化。”经文让治者守住一种不抢先替万物决定形状的姿态。与第三十二章的“守朴”相连，本文把它读为：不要把自己的规划当成世界唯一可以走的路。

一个有经验的团队，可能比管理者更清楚怎样分工。一个社区，也可能在相处中长出互助的办法。若每一件小事都要等待上面批准，原本能作判断的人便逐渐只学会等指令。

但自化并不等于自动向好。团队仍需要资源、公平分工、清楚的职责；社区仍需要保护成员、处理欺凌。目标与规则也可以共同商定，并不是说出一个目标就犯了错。

要减少的是越俎代庖，不是撤去条件。一个有用的分辨是：我的安排是在增加别人判断和行动的能力，还是在让所有事情越来越离不开我的批准？

四、支持，而不占满空间

“化而欲作，吾将阗之以无名之朴。”本系列采用“阗”的写法，借它“充实”的字义，把这一句读向涵育：不要急着压住正在发生的东西，先看看它需要什么条件。

这是本文的哲学选择，不是仅凭“阗”和“镇”两个字就能完成的校勘证明。古书存在通假与不同训读；“镇”也不能一概等同于现代意义上的暴力镇压。我们要讨论的，是两种对待人的姿态，而不是宣判另一版本必然提倡殖民。

团队有了不同意见，先听清楚具体困难，提供信息、时间和公平的协商办法，比立刻要求统一口径更有用。孩子开始探索，不是把他的生活排满我们认为正确的活动，而是给他安全的条件、可以求助的大人，以及自己尝试的空地。

但不要把涵育理解成“用好东西填满空间，让坏东西无处出现”。谁定义好坏？谁决定别人心里可以长什么？如果这些都由我包办，所谓涵育就会重新变成控制。支持可以充分，人的空间不必被填满；冲突与差异也不都应被当成杂草。

五、名位之外的自己

“阗之以无名之朴，夫将不辱。”采用本中的重复，让我们把目光留在怎样支持生命，而不是怎样占住名位上。它可以加强阅读时的重音，却不能据此断定作者有意删改了别的版本。

本文沿“不辱”来读；它与“不欲”的关系涉及不同训读，不宜只说一个具体、一个肤浅。我想从中取出的，是不把自己的全部价值押在一块招牌上。头衔受损，不等于作为人的价值被取消。

这绝不是“觉醒后便不会遭受羞辱”。霸凌、歧视与伤害仍然真实，受害不是修养不够的证明。人有理由反对伤害、求助、退出。内在不全被他人的评价夺走，与外在需要保护，两件事可以同时成立。

六、天地将自正

“不辱以静，天地将自正。”我们可以把这看成一个愿景：世界不必处处成为某个人意志的延长。人有自己的节奏，事物也有不由我们设计的联系。

“天地”可以打开比人间治理更宽的视野，但不能据此说“天下”的写法只有浅薄的意义。这里借天地谈开放的生活，不是给天、地分别安上唯一的现代哲学定义。

静也不必成为另一项考核。有些人需要练习、休息或专业帮助，才能安定下来。规则同样可以保护空间：协商的程序、公共安全的底线、可以申诉与退出的安排，并不天然就是污染。需要拒绝的是不许修正、不许回应的框架，而不是所有框架。

七、给读者的

下一次忍不住想管，先停一下，不是永远不做，而是先问：这是对方需要的帮助，还是我忍受不了事情不照我的样子发生？

团队需要的是更多审批，还是一个能拍板解决困难的人？孩子需要的是纠正，还是在危险之外多试一次？自己需要的是再立一个目标，还是承认已经累了？问题不同，回应也不同。

真正少一点干预，有时要先多做一点不显眼的工作：把资源交代清楚，承担该承担的风险，让求助有回应，让拒绝和退出成为真实的选择。发生伤害时，仍然要保护人。

世界不会因我退后一步就保证变好。但如果我把每一个位置都占住，别人的可能性就更难展开。退后，不是撤走照顾，是把本来属于别人的位置还给他。

I. The Urge to Intervene

Everyone who has led a team, built an organization, or run a platform has felt it — that impulse. Seeing something unfold naturally and being unable to resist stepping in.

The team is running smoothly on its own — can't help adding a process. The community is growing on its own — can't help laying down rules. The product has found its direction — can't help drawing up a roadmap. The child is exploring — can't help correcting them.

Whenever anything "is just happening," your hands get itchy. You want to insert yourself, set the tone, build a framework.

Chapter 37 of the Daodejing is about the opposite of that impulse —

This closes the Dao section. In the silk ordering, with De before Dao, it also stands at the end of the book. It invites a life with less imposition, not a promise that everything unattended will improve.

II. The Dao Endures Without Name

“The Dao endures without name.” This series follows namelessness into the “nameless unhewn wood” later in the passage. Using chiselling and constructing to explore it is our philosophical reading, not a claim that those modern terms are already stated in the ancient text.

Naming often draws a boundary. Call an undertaking a success, and its achievements come forward while its costs may recede. Call a child obedient, and it becomes easy to watch whether the child complies rather than listen to what they think. Names help us recognize things; they can also make us believe recognition is complete.

Along this path, namelessness reminds us not to let an existing label finish the description of a living process. “Not constructing” means not imposing our framework as someone else’s only possibility. It does not mean abandoning language, institutions or plans.

A team that names efficiency as its goal can still ask: whose efficiency? Has the time saved for one person become overtime for another? A name that admits such questions supports understanding. One that excludes them starts governing reality in its place.

The received version offers “constant non-action, yet nothing left undone,” and chapter 48 also contains that pairing. We cannot say Laozi never uses it. Choosing the thread of namelessness does not require declaring the other path shallow or mistaken.

III. Self-Transformation Is Not Neglect

“If lords and kings could keep to it, the myriad things would transform of themselves.” Read beside chapter 32’s unhewn wood, the line suggests not deciding in advance the only shape everything may take. Your plan need not become the world’s sole permitted route.

An experienced team may know more than its manager about how to divide the work. Neighbours may develop ways of helping one another through living together. Requiring approval for every small decision can teach capable people to do little except wait for instructions.

But self-transformation is not automatic improvement. Teams need resources, fair workloads and clear responsibilities. Communities need protection against bullying. Goals and rules can be agreed together; naming a goal is not itself an offence.

What we reduce is taking over other people’s judgment, not providing its conditions. Ask whether your arrangement increases their ability to decide and act, or makes more and more of life dependent on your permission.

IV. Support Without Occupying All the Space

“As transformation gives rise to something, I shall fill it with the nameless unhewn.” This series adopts the character tian and draws on its sense of fullness to explore cultivation: before suppressing what is emerging, ask what conditions it needs.

That is an interpretive choice, not a textual proof obtained by looking up two characters. Early texts use loan characters and admit different readings. The received zhen cannot simply be equated with violent repression. We are exploring two ways of treating people, not proving that another edition necessarily advocates domination.

When a team disagrees, listen to the actual difficulty. Offer information, time and a fair way to negotiate. When a child explores, do not fill every hour with activities you approve of. Provide safety, someone to ask for help, and room to try.

For that reason, the image of filling every space with good things until nothing bad can grow is misleading. Who decides what counts as good? Who gets to choose what may grow in someone else's life? Support can be generous without occupying all the space. Disagreement is not necessarily a weed.

V. A Self Beyond Status

The adopted text repeats “fill with the nameless unhewn” before “there will be no disgrace.” Repetition gives the action weight in our reading; it does not prove an author's intention behind the differences between editions.

This essay follows the reading “no disgrace,” while acknowledging that its relation to “no desire” involves interpretive choices. The useful thought is not to stake all one's value on a title. Losing status does not cancel a person's worth.

That is not immunity from humiliation. Bullying, discrimination and injury remain real. Being harmed does not reveal a failure to become enlightened. You may resist, seek help and leave. Refusing to let another person's judgment define your entire worth is compatible with needing protection.

VI. Heaven and Earth Will Right Themselves

“In stillness, heaven and earth will right themselves.” Read this as a horizon: the world need not become an extension of one person's will. People have rhythms of their own; relationships arise that no manager designed.

“Heaven and earth” offers a wider image than political administration. That does not make the received “all under heaven” shallow, or turn heaven and earth into fixed labels for a modern philosophical system.

Stillness must not become another test people fail. Rest, practice or professional help may be needed. Rules can protect room for life too: fair procedures, safety boundaries, appeals and the freedom to leave. The target is a framework that refuses correction and response, not every framework whatsoever.

VII. For the Reader

The next time you feel the urge to intervene, pause. Not forever: long enough to ask whether this is help the other person needs, or your discomfort with something happening differently from your plan.

Does the team need another approval stage, or someone to remove an obstacle? Does the child need correction, or another safe attempt? Do you need a fresh target, or permission to admit exhaustion? Different problems call for different responses.

Less interference sometimes requires more quiet work first: making resources available, accepting your share of risk, answering requests for help, making refusal and exit possible. When harm occurs, protection remains necessary.

The world is not guaranteed to improve because you step back. But if you occupy every position, other people's possibilities have less room to unfold. Stepping back is not withdrawing care. It is returning a place that was never yours to occupy.
