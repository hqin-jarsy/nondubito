Dazhi Daodejing Commentary
大知道德经解

以帛书甲本为底本，逐章散文讲解《道德经》。不走玄学路线，不做辞章训诂——每一章都从帛书字面出发，把老子真正在说的那件结构性的事，用今天能懂的话说清楚。

全系列导言一篇，正文八十一章。按九章一组，共分九组。每章配有帛书与通行本的字差考据，以及与《余项经》框架的对照。

A prose commentary on all eighty-one chapters of the Daodejing, based on the Mawangdui silk manuscript. Not metaphysics, not classical exegesis — each chapter is read from the silk text's exact wording, and what Laozi was actually saying, structurally, is put in terms legible today.

The series opens with an introduction, followed by eighty-one chapters organized in nine groups of nine. Each chapter includes textual variants between the silk manuscript and the received text, with cross-references to the Yuixiang Jing framework.
