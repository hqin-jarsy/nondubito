第十二章
Chapter 12 of 81

五色不是太多颜色

The Five Colors Are Not Too Many

秦汉（字大知）· Han Qin

一、刷过抖音的人都有过的经历

刷过抖音的人都有过这个体验：

看一个视频很爽。看二十个视频还爽。看一个小时之后想去做点别的——

发现做不下去了。

打开书，看不进去。
打开自己的项目，开不了头。
跟朋友聊天，注意力散。

这件事在 2026 年每天都在发生。

老子第十二章描述的，就是这件事——

两千四百年前。

第十二章一上来五句——

"五色使人之目盲，
五音使人之耳聋，
五味使人之口爽，
驰骋畋猎使人心发狂，
难得之货使人之行妨。"

五句话，每句都是 2026 年我们熟悉的某个现象的远古版本。

但要读对这一章，得先把"五色"这两个字读对。

二、五色不是太多颜色

历代读这一章最常见的读法是——

五色 = 太多颜色。

按这个读法，整章成了一条养生戒律——别看太多颜色会眼花，别听太多音乐会耳鸣，别吃太多味道会失味。老子被读成一个劝人节制感官的养生家。

但这一章可能不是讲过量。

老子那个时代，五色不是泛指"多种颜色"。

五色是周代已经成熟的一套分类系统——青、赤、黄、白、黑五类。

这五类跟五行（木、火、土、金、水）耦合。五色配五行、五音配五行、五味配五行——是老子那个时代每个识字人都知道的文化常识。

"五"是精确指向已经被分类好的五类，不是"多"的意思。

所以读这一章，先把"五"字从"多"调成"已经被分类过的五类"。

这一字读对，整章意思就反过来。

五色不是太多颜色——是已经被分类过的五类颜色。
五音不是太多声音——是已经被分类过的五类音。
五味不是太多味道——是已经被分类过的五类味。

放到今天来读——

这就像所有 TikTok 视频都已经被算法按你的偏好分过类了。
所有 Spotify 歌单都已经按你的过去听歌历史分过类了。
所有 Netflix 内容都已经按你看过的剧分过类了。
所有 Yelp 餐厅都已经按你过去评过分的标签分过类了。

你不是看见各种各样的视频——是看见已经被算法分类过的视频。
你不是听见各种各样的音乐——是听见已经被算法分类过的音乐。
你不是吃到各种各样的味道——是吃到已经被分类过的味道。

这跟老子说的"五色"在结构上是完全同一件事——外面给你的不是原物，是被分类过的物。

三、目盲不是眼瞎

铺好这一层，再读"目盲"两个字。

最自然的读法是——五色看多了，眼睛就瞎了。

但老子用的不是"眼花"，是"盲"。

盲是真看不见。

什么真看不见？

不是看不见五色——五色看得见，你正天天在看。

看不见的是——色本身。

老子那个时代的人把无限光谱分成了青、赤、黄、白、黑五类。分类本身有用——可以沟通"这是青色的衣服"。

但当人的视觉位只按这五类接收——看见一片颜色立刻归类成"这是青"或"这是赤"——人就失去了对色本身的连续光谱的感受能力。

青和赤之间那无数种既不是青也不是赤的颜色、色的饱和度梯度、色的光泽质地、色的温度感——这些都被"五色"这个构吞了。

人以为自己在看色，实际上只在识别五类标签。

目盲不是眼睛坏了。眼睛还在，光进来了。

是"看"这件事被构占满了位。

进来的光马上被五色标签收编。真正的"看"反而不发生了。

放到今天——

打开抖音之后看了一个小时短视频，你的视觉位被算法分类过的内容塞满。

你不是看不见东西——你正在看。

但你看的不是世界——是被算法分类过的世界。

把手机放下，看窗外的树——你会发现树看起来"没意思"。

不是树没意思。是你的视觉位被算法的分类构塞了一个小时，对没有被分类过的物失去了感知能力。

目盲。

耳聋同理。

不是听不见声音——你正天天听 Spotify。

但你听不见音本身。

无限音程被分成宫、商、角、徵、羽五类。今天的版本——Spotify 算法把所有音乐按"放松""锻炼""专注""睡眠"这些标签分类过。

两音之间的微分音、音色的细节、节奏的呼吸、余韵——全被分类构吞了。

耳聋不是听不见声音，是被分类训练过的耳朵失去了听音本身的能力。

口爽同理。

爽在古汉语里有"失"的意思——失味。

吃多了五味分类过的食物，舌头只认这五类。两味之间的过渡、食物的温度口感、味的层次变化——全没了。

今天的版本——Yelp 五星评级、网红推荐、餐厅标签把食物已经分类好了给你。

去过几家"五星网红餐厅"之后，去家门口的小馆子吃饭，你会觉得"就那样"。

不是小馆子不好。是你的味觉位被分类构塞了，对原味失去了感知能力。

口爽。

四、心位和行动位

前三句是感官位。后两句把这件事扩展到心位和行动位。

"驰骋畋猎使人心发狂"——心位。

驰骋畋猎是快节奏的刺激活动——骑马疾驰，狩猎追击。

不是说狩猎是坏事。在老子那个时代，狩猎是正常的生产活动。

老子拎出"驰骋畋猎"是因为它是心位被强构长时间占满的典型场景——刺激不停、肾上腺素不停、下一个猎物不停——心位上再没有空让其他内容进来。

放到今天——

这就是短视频刷不停。一个接一个的高刺激内容，下一条比上一条更刺激，你的心位被这个流持续占满。

刷一个小时之后想停下来安静地待着，做不到。心位上被强刺激构占住了，停不下来。

"心发狂"——不是疯，是心位被一种强构占满之后失去平稳运作的能力。

"难得之货使人之行妨"——行动位。

难得之货是稀有珍贵的东西。

放到今天——

限量款球鞋。限量发售的手表。某个网红同款的包。NFT。某种"只有少数人买得起"的体验。

人看到难得之货，行动位被"要得到这个难得之货"的构占满——整个人的行动方向被这个构劫持。

走路、说话、做事都围着这个构转。

这就是"行妨"——行动被一个外来的构绑架，真正的行动方向失去了空间。

五句合起来——

视觉位（五色）、听觉位（五音）、味觉位（五味）、心位（驰骋畋猎）、行动位（难得之货）。

每一位都可能被一种构塞满。每一位被塞满之后那一位的原本功能就失效。

这五句不是五个零散的养生告诫。是五个位上同一个结构问题的演示——构塞满位，功能失效。

正好是第十一章的反面。

第十一章讲：构里留位让运作能在里面发生。
第十二章讲：构把位塞满了，里面的运作就停了。

车轮的毂心被填实，轴就转不了。
视觉位被五色塞满，"看"就不发生。

同一件事，正反两面。

五、三个字

这一章后半段有一个三字字差，比较重要。

帛书写："是以圣人之治也，为腹而不为目。"

通行本写："是以圣人，为腹不为目。"

通行本删了"之治也"三个字。

三个字删掉之后，整章的重心就换了——

帛书读起来：圣人的治理是为腹不为目。主语是治理，对象是民。

通行本读起来：圣人自己是为腹不为目。主语是个人养生，对象是自己。

如果按帛书读，前半段五句话讲的是民被各种构殖民感官位心位行动位的现象——后半段讲圣人作为治理者怎么应对。整章是一份治理诊断书。

如果按通行本读，前半段成了"感官过量的坏处"——后半段成了"圣人都是吃饱就行别看景色"的个人养生处方。

差别大到——

帛书里老子的"圣人之治"是在指向立构者——治理者不要塞民的感官位。

通行本里"圣人"变成了指向被立构者——读者你自己要节欲。

锋芒方向反过来了。

本来批评统治者的话被收成了对民众的道德训诫。

做这次删除的人是谁、什么年代、为什么删——已不可考。现存最早的完整通行本是王弼三世纪的本子，那时"之治也"已经不在原文里了。

每一位历代注家都在他自己的时代框架下做真实的思想工作——这种 patch 都不是个人的恶意。

但每一次小 patch 合起来，结构后果就出来——批评立构者的锋芒被反转成训诫被立构者。

这是 Paper 2 的总观察——通行本的关键字差，几乎都在做同一件事：把老子具体的教导磨成抽象的描述，把指向立构者的批评收成指向被立构者的训诫。

不是恶意。是系统性方向。

六、为腹不为目

按帛书读这一章，最后一句的意思就清晰了——

"是以圣人之治也，为腹而不为目，故去彼而取此。"

腹和目这两个字第三章已经出现过。"实其腹"和"不见可欲"是同一套治理操作。

腹是基础层——身体的根本，生命的底子。生理营养、生活安稳、最基本的生存条件。

目是涌现层的感官位——眼睛接收视觉刺激的那一位。目位容易被五色塞满，前面讲过。

为腹——治理者在基础层上做涵育。让民有饭吃、有住处、有稳定生活。这是实打实的厚化动作。

不为目——治理者不在涌现层立构塞民的感官位。民的目位应该空着让民自己看，让民自己接收色音味的真实感受，让民自己判断。

治理者不该负责给民的目位塞内容。

合起来——

治理者只在腹这一层做功（厚基础层），不在目这一层立构（让出涌现层）。

这是涵育和殖民的分界——

涵育者：在被涵育者的基础层上做功，在涌现层上让位。
殖民者：在被涵育者的涌现层上立构（告诉别人该看什么、想什么、追求什么），同时在基础层上剥夺（让别人没底子，只能被构带着走）。

殖民是反向的两层处理。

读到这里再回头看第十二章这五句——

五色、五音、五味、驰骋畋猎、难得之货——

每一项都是别人立构塞民涌现层位的动作。

放到今天看——

立"五色"的是某个内容平台的算法，立"五音"的是某个音乐平台的推荐系统，立"难得之货"的是某个营销团队的稀缺性设计。

每一个动作的发起者都不是民自己——是外面的某个构殖民进民的位。

老子在第十二章告诉治理者——不要做这个发起者。

放到今天，这一章告诉所有"想做有影响力的人"的——不要做塞别人涌现层的那一方。

不管你是平台、是品牌、是教育者、是导师、是父母——

不在他人的涌现层立构，是涵育与殖民的分界线。

第十二章本来是一份治理诊断书。

它诊断了五个位被殖民的现象。
它指出了治理者的两层处理原则——基础层为根，涌现层为花。
它把涵育和殖民两条 path 的分界划得很清楚。

两千四百年后读这一章，每一句都还在描述当下。

一、An Experience Anyone Who Has Scrolled TikTok Will Recognize

Anyone who has scrolled TikTok knows this feeling:

One video — great. Twenty videos — still great. After an hour, you want to do something else —

And find you can't.

Open a book, can't read.
Open your own project, can't start.
Talk to a friend, attention scattered.

This happens every day in 2026.

What Laozi describes in Chapter 12 is exactly this —

Two thousand four hundred years ago.

Chapter 12 opens with five lines:

"五色使人之目盲，
五音使人之耳聋，
五味使人之口爽，
驰骋畋猎使人心发狂，
难得之货使人之行妨。"

Five lines, each describing a phenomenon we recognize right now.

But to read this chapter correctly, you need to read the two characters "五色" (wǔ sè, "the five colors") correctly first.

二、The Five Colors Are Not Too Many Colors

The most common reading of this chapter across the centuries is —

The five colors = too many colors.

On this reading, the whole chapter becomes a health-and-moderation rule: too many colors will dazzle the eye, too much music will deafen the ear, too many flavors will ruin the palate. Laozi becomes a temperance counselor urging restraint of the senses.

But this chapter is probably not about excess.

In Laozi's time, "five colors" did not mean "many colors in general."

The five colors — blue-green, red, yellow, white, black — were a mature classification system of the Zhou dynasty. These five were coupled to the Five Phases (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water). Five colors corresponded to Five Phases; five tones corresponded to Five Phases; five flavors corresponded to Five Phases — cultural common knowledge that every literate person of Laozi's time would have known.

"Five" points precisely to an already-classified set of five categories, not to "many."

So first, adjust how you read "five": not "a lot" but "an already-classified five categories."

That one adjustment reverses the whole chapter's meaning.

Five colors are not too many colors — they are the five classified categories of color.
Five tones are not too much sound — they are the five classified categories of tone.
Five flavors are not too many tastes — they are the five classified categories of flavor.

In today's terms —

This is like all TikTok videos already sorted by algorithm to match your preferences.
All Spotify playlists already sorted by your listening history.
All Netflix content already sorted by your past viewing.
All Yelp restaurants already sorted by the tags you previously rated.

You are not seeing a variety of videos — you are seeing a pre-classified variety.
You are not hearing a variety of music — you are hearing a pre-classified variety.

This is structurally identical to what Laozi means by "the five colors" — what the outside gives you is not the raw thing but the pre-classified thing.

三、Eye-Blindness Is Not Going Blind

With that foundation, reread "eye-blindness" (目盲).

The natural reading: see too many colors and your eyes go bad.

But Laozi does not say "dazzled" — he says blind.

Blind means genuinely cannot see.

Cannot see what?

Not the five colors — you see those. You see them every day.

Cannot see — color itself.

Laozi's contemporaries divided the infinite spectrum into five categories: blue-green, red, yellow, white, black. The classification is useful — you can say "this is a blue-green garment." But when a person's visual position receives only according to these five categories — seeing a color and immediately filing it as "this is blue-green" or "this is red" — the person loses the capacity to experience the continuous spectrum of color itself.

All the infinite shades between blue-green and red, the gradations of saturation, the qualities of luster and texture, the sense of temperature — all of it swallowed by the construct "five colors."

The person thinks they are seeing color. In fact they are only recognizing five category labels.

Eye-blindness is not broken eyes. Light still enters.

It is "seeing" itself being occupied by construct — incoming light immediately conscripted by the five-color labels. Genuine "seeing" no longer happens.

In today's terms —

After an hour of scrolling TikTok, your visual position has been filled by algorithmically classified content.

You are not blind — you are looking.

But what you are looking at is not the world — it is the algorithmically classified world.

Put the phone down and look at the tree outside the window — you will find the tree seems "boring."

The tree is not boring. Your visual position has been packed with the algorithm's classification construct for an hour, and you have lost the capacity to perceive the unclassified.

Eye-blindness.

Ear-deafness works the same way.

You are not deaf — you listen to Spotify every day.

But you cannot hear sound itself.

The infinite range of pitch is divided into five tones: gong, shang, jue, zhi, yu. Today's version — Spotify classifies all music by labels like "relaxation," "workout," "focus," "sleep."

Microtonal intervals, timbral detail, rhythmic breathing, resonance and decay — all swallowed by the classification construct.

Ear-deafness is not unable to hear sound; it is an ear trained by classification that has lost the capacity to hear sound itself.

Mouth-loss works the same way.

Shuang (爽) in classical Chinese carries the meaning "loss" — losing the sense of taste.

Eating too many pre-classified five-flavor foods, the tongue recognizes only these five categories. The transitions between flavors, the temperature and texture of food, the layering of taste — all gone.

Today's version — Yelp five-star ratings, food influencers, restaurant tags pre-classify food for you.

After visiting several "five-star influencer restaurants," you eat at the neighborhood place and feel "it's just okay."

The neighborhood place is not bad. Your palate position has been packed with classification construct and lost the capacity for the unclassified taste.

Mouth-loss.

四、The Heart-Position and the Action-Position

The first three lines are about sensory positions. The final two extend the same thing to heart-position and action-position.

"驰骋畋猎使人心发狂" — heart-position.

Riding at full gallop, hunting, pursuing — fast-paced, high-stimulation activity.

This is not saying hunting is bad. In Laozi's time, hunting was normal productive work.

What Laozi singles out is that it is a typical scenario where the heart-position is occupied by strong construct for a sustained period — stimulation continuous, adrenaline continuous, next quarry continuous — the heart-position has no room left for anything else.

Today's version —

Short video feed. One high-stimulation piece after another, each more stimulating than the last, the flow occupying the heart-position continuously.

After scrolling for an hour, try stopping and sitting in stillness — can't. The heart-position has been occupied by high-stimulation construct and cannot return to stable operation.

"Heart runs wild" — not madness; the heart-position occupied by one strong construct has lost the capacity for steady operation.

"难得之货使人之行妨" — action-position.

Hard-to-get goods — rare and precious things.

Today's version —

Limited-edition sneakers. Limited-release watches. An influencer's same-as bag. NFTs. An experience "only a few can afford."

When a person sees hard-to-get goods, the action-position is filled by the construct "must obtain this hard-to-get thing" — the whole person's direction of action is hijacked by this construct.

Walking, talking, doing — all orbiting this construct.

"Action obstructed" — action hijacked by an external construct; the genuine direction of action has lost its space.

Five lines together —

Visual position (five colors), auditory position (five tones), taste position (five flavors), heart-position (riding and hunting), action-position (hard-to-get goods).

Each position can be packed by a construct. Each position packed loses its original function.

These five lines are not five scattered pieces of health advice. They are demonstrations of the same structural problem across five positions — construct packs position, function fails.

This is the exact inverse of Chapter 11.

Chapter 11: leave room within the construct so operation can happen inside.
Chapter 12: construct packs the position full; operation inside ceases.

Fill the hub hole — the axle cannot turn.
Pack the visual position with five colors — seeing does not happen.

Same thing, two faces.

五、Three Characters

There is a significant three-character textual difference in the second half of this chapter.

The silk manuscript reads: "是以圣人之治也" — "Therefore, in the sage's governance..."

The standard text reads: "是以圣人" — "Therefore, the sage..."

Remove "之治也" — three characters — and the whole chapter's center of gravity shifts.

The silk-manuscript reading: The sage's governance acts for the belly and not for the eye. The subject is governance; the object is the people.

The standard-text reading: The sage himself acts for the belly and not for the eye. The subject is personal cultivation; the object is the self.

The difference is enormous:

In the silk manuscript, the first five lines describe the phenomenon of people having their sensory, heart, and action positions colonized by various constructs — and then the second half discusses how the sage as ruler responds. The whole chapter is a governance diagnosis.

In the standard text, the first five lines become "the harms of sensory excess" — and the second half becomes "the sage himself eats to be full, doesn't look at scenery" — a personal health prescription.

The difference is so large that —

In the silk manuscript, Laozi's "sage's governance" points at the one who establishes constructs — the ruler must not pack the people's sensory positions.

In the standard text, "the sage" points at the one who receives constructs — you, the reader, must restrain your desires.

The direction of the blade is reversed.

What was a critique of the ruling class becomes moral instruction to the people.

六、For the Belly, Not for the Eye

Reading this chapter against the silk manuscript, the final line's meaning is clear —

"是以圣人之治也，为腹而不为目，故去彼而取此。"

"Therefore, in the sage's governance, act for the belly and not for the eye. Hence: set aside that, take up this."

Belly and eye both appeared in Chapter 3. "Fill their bellies" and "do not display what can be desired" are the same set of governance operations.

Belly is the foundation layer — the body's root, the basis of life. Physiological nutrition, stability of life, the most basic conditions of survival.

Eye is the sensory position of the emergent layer — the position at which the eyes receive visual stimulus. The eye-position is easily packed by the five colors, as discussed above.

Act for the belly — the ruler does nurturing work on the foundation layer. Make sure the people have food, shelter, stable lives. This is solid, substantive deepening work.

Not for the eye — the ruler does not establish constructs on the emergent layer to pack the people's sensory positions. The people's eye-position should be left open for them to see for themselves, to receive their own genuine impressions of color, sound, and flavor, to make their own judgments.

The ruler should not be responsible for filling the people's eye-position with content.

Together —

The ruler does work only at the belly layer (deepening the foundation layer) and does not establish constructs at the eye layer (making room in the emergent layer).

This is the boundary between nurturing and colonizing —

A nurturing subject does work at the nurtured one's foundation layer, makes room at the emergent layer.
A colonizing subject establishes constructs in the nurtured one's emergent layer (telling others what to see, think, pursue), while stripping the foundation layer (leaving others without a base, drifting wherever the construct carries them).

Colonizing is the inverted two-layer treatment.

Read to this point and go back to the chapter's five lines —

Five colors, five tones, five flavors, riding and hunting, hard-to-get goods —

Every one is someone else establishing a construct to pack the people's emergent-layer positions.

In today's terms —

The one establishing the "five colors" is a content platform's algorithm; the one establishing the "five tones" is a music platform's recommendation engine; the one establishing "hard-to-get goods" is a marketing team's scarcity design.

Every initiator of these actions is not the people themselves — it is some external construct colonizing the people's positions.

Laozi tells the ruler in Chapter 12 — do not be that initiator.

In today's terms, this chapter speaks to everyone who "wants to have influence" —

Do not be the party packing other people's emergent layers.

Whether you are a platform, a brand, an educator, a mentor, a parent —

Not establishing constructs in another's emergent layer is the boundary line between nurturing and colonizing.

Chapter 12 is a governance diagnosis.

It diagnoses the phenomenon of five positions being colonized.
It identifies the ruler's two-layer treatment principle — foundation layer as root, emergent layer as flower.
It draws the boundary between nurturing and colonizing with great clarity.

Two thousand four hundred years later, every line still describes the present.
