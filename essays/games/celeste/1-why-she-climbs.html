She Cannot Say Why

她说不出为什么

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting of the Chinese argument for English-language readers. Plot claims have been checked and interpretive claims are marked as such.

Madeline parks at the foot of Celeste Mountain, looks up, and begins climbing.

She has no guide and little evidence that she is prepared. The people she meets keep asking why she has come. Her answers grow less convincing the more she repeats them: she wants to prove she can do it; she needs to do it; she simply has to.

She cannot yet explain the action that has already begun.

When action comes before explanation

In ordinary life, an unusual decision is expected to arrive with an account of itself. Why leave the job? Why move? Why pursue something that appears unnecessary? A clear explanation reassures everyone, including the speaker, that the decision has been rationally contained.

But some changes begin before the language for them exists. A person may know only that remaining in place has become unbearable. That feeling does not prove that the chosen direction is wise. Inarticulacy is not a certificate of depth. It does mean that a motive can be real while its best description remains unfinished.

Madeline’s “I have to” does not settle the argument. It tells us that the mountain is not tourism.

Stubbornness and determination

The old woman who lives on the mountain remarks that it can be difficult to distinguish stubbornness from determination.

From the outside they perform the same movements. Advice is given; a person persists. She fails; she returns. Success cannot provide the distinction retrospectively, because a stubborn person can succeed and a determined person can fail.

Nor must Madeline possess a single motive. She can genuinely want the summit while also using the climb to flee something below. The proportions may change as she moves. What begins as self-punishment can become discovery; what sounds like courage can still conceal refusal.

Celeste wisely leaves the old woman’s question open. Reaching the top does not issue Madeline a clean motive for everything that happened before it. The difference between determination and stubbornness has to be answered repeatedly in how she proceeds, not stamped onto her at departure.

The mountain reveals; it does not cure

Celeste Mountain gives form to what its visitors bring. Madeline’s anxiety steps out of a mirror. Mr. Oshiro’s investment in his ruined hotel acquires a monstrous force. The mountain does not distribute one standardized “inner demon.” It makes concealment difficult.

That is why the summit cannot be understood as the exit from a clinic. Climbing does not automatically cure Madeline, and failure to climb would not condemn her.

On level ground, a person can conceal a question beneath appointments, work, and practiced conversation. A platform game removes most of those distractions. Here is a room with wind, spikes, gravity, and a ledge on the other side. The large question remains unanswered, but for a moment it becomes the smaller one: how will you cross?

Celeste’s clarity is built from these local problems. It never requires Madeline to solve her whole life before attempting the next jump.

Strawberries and self-appointed goals

Strawberries wait in inconvenient and dangerous places. They are unnecessary for reaching the summit. They do not increase Madeline’s abilities or determine whether the central journey is completed.

It is not quite true, however, that they change nothing. The number collected changes the strawberry pie in the epilogue and the friends’ reactions to it. The game remembers the detours without converting them into a universal measure of legitimacy.

This makes the strawberries more interesting than either a vital resource or a meaningless collectible. A player can adopt an additional goal. The game responds, but it does not imply that someone who collected fewer failed to climb the real mountain.

Value can leave a visible result without becoming a standard everyone must satisfy.

No person has to be eliminated

Celeste contains pursuers and dangerous creatures. Oshiro chases Madeline. The Mirror Temple contains Seekers. Chapter Six even stages something resembling a boss encounter with her other self.

But the game does not make killing anyone its solution. Madeline has no conventional weapon; her fundamental vocabulary is movement, evasion, and return. Even the most combat-like encounter ends not with an enemy’s destruction but with a changed relationship.

The game also offers Assist Mode: players may slow the game, add dashes, gain invincibility, or otherwise reshape the ascent. The journey remains difficult enough to make persistence tangible, while difficulty ceases to function as a single gate determining who deserves the story.

Celeste Mountain will not tell Madeline why she came. It will make it increasingly difficult for her to pretend that the question belongs to somebody else.

The next essay turns to the person who steps out of the mirror to keep asking it.

玛德琳把车停在山脚，抬头看了一眼蔚蓝山。

她没有向导，也不像一个准备充分的登山者。当地人提醒她，这座山会对人做些奇怪的事。

她还是开始往上走。

一、为什么偏偏是这座山

一路上，希欧问她为什么来，老奶奶问过，后来从镜子里走出来的“另一个自己”也逼她回答。

玛德琳说想证明自己做得到，说自己需要做这件事，最后常常只剩一句：我必须。

这些回答不完整。但说不出来不等于没有缘由，更不等于那件事天然正确。它只是说明，行动有时走在解释前面。一个人已经在往某处去，语言却还没追上。

我们习惯先要求一份合理说明：为什么要辞职，为什么要离开，为什么非做这件别人看来没有必要的事。说明越清楚，旁人越安心。可有些改变的起点恰恰没有成熟的说明，只有一种继续留在原处会更糟的感觉。

玛德琳的“必须”不能替她证明登山明智。它只让我们知道，这座山对她不是一次普通旅行。

二、固执还是决然

老奶奶说，有时很难分清固执与决然。

从外面看，两者确实很像：别人劝过，一个人仍不改方向；失败很多次，他还是回来。

区别不只在“坚持了多久”，也不只在最后有没有成功。一个人可以真心想完成一件事，同时用它逃避另一件事；也可能起初只想证明自己，走着走着才明白自己究竟在做什么。

因此，登顶不能替玛德琳倒推一份完美动机。成功不会把所有固执洗成决然，失败也不会证明最初的愿望虚假。老奶奶的问题一直悬着，是因为它需要在行动中反复回答，而不是在出发前盖章。

三、山不是病因，也不是解药

蔚蓝山会把人带来的东西显现出来。玛德琳的焦虑从镜中走出；旅馆老板奥希罗困在自己的执念里。山没有把同一种“心魔”发给每个人，它让每个人更难藏住原本就在回避的部分。

这也意味着，山顶不是诊所的出口。爬上去不会自动治好玛德琳，爬不上去也不会给她定罪。

平地上的人可以用忙碌盖住很多东西：上班、回消息、照常说笑，把最难回答的问题推到下周。平台游戏却把她放进一连串很小、很明确的房间：这里有尖刺、距离、风和重力；眼下只问你怎样到达另一边。

大问题没有被解决，却被拆成了下一次跳跃。

四、草莓没有那么“没用”

山上散着许多草莓。它们通常在更危险、更绕远的位置，不是通关所必需，也不会增强玛德琳的能力或决定她能否登顶。

但说它们“完全不影响任何结局”并不准确。收集数量会改变尾声里草莓派的样子以及同伴的反应。游戏认真记得玩家绕过的那些远路，只是不给它们更高的道德地位。

这正是草莓可爱的地方。主线目标之外，玩家可以替自己增加一个目标；游戏会回应，却不会说收集得少的人没有真正完成旅程。

有些事的价值既不等于“毫无结果”，也不必上升成统一标准。它可以只是一块你愿意多走几步去够到的东西。

五、这里没有一个该被消灭的人

《蔚蓝》并非真的没有追逐者和危险生物。奥希罗会追来，镜之寺院有怪物，第六章甚至用近似战斗的方式让玛德琳冲向另一个自己。

但游戏没有把解决办法写成杀死谁。玛德琳没有常规武器；她主要做的是移动、躲避、重试。即使最像Boss战的那场相遇，终点也不是把对方从自己身上清除。

与此同时，游戏提供辅助模式：减慢速度、增加冲刺、给予无敌等。它明确把这些选项放在个人旅程里，而不是把使用者赶出“真正玩家”的资格。

难度仍然重要，因为一次次失败让攀登有了触感；但难度不再是替所有人规定好的门槛。游戏可以要求坚持，也可以承认每个人需要的山路不同。

蔚蓝山不替玛德琳回答为什么。它只让她无法继续假装，那个问题与她无关。
