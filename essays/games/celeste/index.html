Reading Celeste

《蔚蓝》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on an unexplained climb, an inner voice that argues from real evidence, two forms of helping, and an integration that leaves more than one voice intact.

Celeste turns a difficult platformer into an unusually patient account of anxiety. Failure is recorded but stripped of finality; assistance can change the route without defining the climber; the frightening part of the self is neither obeyed nor destroyed. These essays read mechanics and story together, including the places where the game's details resist an overly tidy moral.

四篇从一次说不清原因的登山、一个拿真实材料作出过度推论的声音，读到两种帮助，以及并不消灭分歧的自我接纳。

《蔚蓝》用一部高难度平台游戏，耐心地写焦虑：失败被如实记录，却没有终审权；帮助可以改变走法，却不替人规定标准；那个令人害怕的自己既不必服从，也无法靠消灭解决。四篇把机制与故事放在一起读，也保留作品不肯被一句漂亮道理收平的地方。
