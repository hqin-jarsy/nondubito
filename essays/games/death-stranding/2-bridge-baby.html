Bridge Baby

布桥婴

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting of the Chinese argument for English-language readers. Plot claims have been checked and interpretive claims are marked as such.

Sam carries a pod against his chest. Inside its amber fluid floats an infant, tethered by a cord. When Sam falls, the baby cries. When he cradles the pod and rocks it gently, the crying stops.

It has a designation: BB-28.

A baby classified as equipment

A Bridge Baby is removed from a brain-dead “stillmother” and maintained in an artificial womb that simulates her uterus. Existing on the threshold between life and death, the BB allows a connected porter to perceive the Beached Things that living eyes cannot ordinarily see.

The game does not present the being in the pod as abstract energy. It gives us a face, a cry, an umbilical connection, and a recognizable stage of human development. At the same time, Bridges classifies it as equipment.

Those facts can coexist. An institution does not have to deny that the organism is a baby in order to prescribe an entirely instrumental relationship to it. Deadman tells Sam not to become attached. A BB is a tool, he insists, and a defective tool can be replaced.

The coldest part is not that nobody can see what is inside the pod. It is that people can see perfectly well and become practiced at placing what they see under a different heading.

Procedure governs the smallest gestures

Do not give it a name. Do not become attached.

These instructions do not police belief so much as conduct. What will Sam call the baby? Will he comfort it after a fall? When he looks down, will he see a serial number or somebody looking back?

That makes the procedure harder to defeat than the claim that a BB is not human. Definitions can be disputed. Procedures are repeated. The operator does not have to believe a lie; he only has to complete the handoff, perform the calibration, and accept decommissioning as routine.

A tool does not cry when its user falls. Once the “tool” does cry, maintaining the classification requires a human being to train himself not to answer.

Moving toward the living

BB-28 does not remain unchanged. Continued connection with Sam brings weight gain, increased brain activity, accumulating memories, and a stronger orientation toward the world of the living. Deadman eventually describes the development in terms of self-awareness.

This does not establish a universal rule by which every BB automatically stops functioning at the same threshold. It gives us something more particular: this baby is exceeding the use designed for it.

Bridge Babies already have a short operational life, roughly a year, after which standard procedure calls for decommissioning and incineration. Replacement and disposal are not accidental failures at the edge of the system. They belong to its normal operation. Growth, memory, and attachment are not maintenance goals; they make it increasingly difficult to preserve the category of equipment.

Sometimes what a system calls malfunction is simply a life refusing to remain where its use has placed it.

Necessity is not imaginary

The argument would be easy if Bridge Babies were merely the invention of villains who enjoyed cruelty.

Without a BB, a porter is far less able to detect BTs. Crossing dangerous territory becomes harder; rebuilding a national network exacts a greater cost. BB-28 protects Sam, and through him may protect many of the people his deliveries sustain.

The institution is persuasive precisely because the instrument works. Every report can record benefits. Every sacrifice can be entered in the column labeled “greater losses avoided.” No participant has to take pleasure in harm. They need only agree that no alternative is available.

But saving more people does not make the person being used disappear from the account. Necessity deserves acknowledgment; it does not replace attention to its victim.

What is buried in the bridge

Late in the story, Deadman reveals a deeper history. BBs were conceived as catalysts for the chiral network and integrated into the infrastructure of the knot cities. He can only speculate that Amelie herself installed them, but he is clear about the pattern: the network was built through sacrifice after sacrifice.

The game’s “bridges” now take two forms.

There are ladders, roads, and shelters that players leave for one another, enabling strangers to cooperate without demanding repayment. Then there is the BB embedded in infrastructure, allowing some people to connect because another life—unable to consent or withdraw—has been fixed into the interface.

Coverage alone cannot tell us whether a network is good. We also need to ask how it was built, who gets to cross, and who has been sealed into the foundation.

Sam cannot stand outside the system

Sam has no position of moral cleanliness from which to denounce the arrangement. He uses BB-28 to survive and to complete the network. He understands the rule and continues the mission.

Death Stranding does not first give him a speech. It gives him repetition. He looks down after a fall. He checks the pod. He rocks the crying baby. Over time he stops speaking to a number and begins speaking to Lou.

None of those gestures abolishes an institution. They alter a smaller fact first: the relation between Sam and BB-28 is no longer simply the relation between operator and equipment.

Large refusals often begin there—not with a perfect declaration from outside the system, but with a person who can no longer use the life before him as if it were a thing.

Based on Kojima Productions' 2019 Death Stranding and the 2021 Director's Cut, whose main story is unchanged. It does not cover the sequel. Full spoilers.

山姆胸前挂着一个舱。

琥珀色的液体里浮着一个婴儿。山姆摔倒时它会哭；他把舱抱在怀里轻轻摇晃，它又会安静下来。

它有一个编号：BB-28。

一、设备里面是一个婴儿

布桥婴取自脑死亡、由机器维持生命的“静母”，在人工舱中模拟子宫环境。它停留在生与死的边界，所以能让使用者感知活人看不见的搁浅者。

游戏没有把舱里的存在设计成一团抽象能源。它给我们一张脸、哭声、脐带般的连接和具体的发育阶段。与此同时，机构又把它归入设备清单。

这两件事并不矛盾。制度不必否认它在生物学上是婴儿，照样可以规定人该如何对待它。戴德曼对山姆的警告近乎直白：不要把BB当孩子，不要依恋；它是工具，失效了就换。

最冷的地方不是有人看错了，而是所有人都看见了，却仍然学会把看见的东西放进另一个分类里。

二、规程管的是动作

“不要起名字，不要产生感情。”

这不是一套关于思想的禁令。它管的是两个很小的动作：怎么称呼它，跌倒以后要不要哄它，低头时看见的是编号还是一个“谁”。

这样的规程比一句“它不是人”更难反驳。定义可以争论，操作规程却每天都在发生。使用者无需相信一个谎，只要照常交接、检测和报废，一整套关系就会被固定下来。

工具不会因为你摔倒而哭。可当一件“工具”哭了，人必须花力气训练自己不去回答。

三、它正在朝生活这一边走

BB-28并非静止不变。随着山姆不断与它连接，它体重增加，脑活动和记忆累积，开始更明显地偏向生者的世界。戴德曼甚至用“自我意识”描述这种变化。

这不表示每一个BB都会在某个统一时刻自动失去功能。它说明的是更具体、也更难处理的事：至少眼前这个编号，正在超出为它准备好的用途。

布桥婴本来就有大约一年的运作寿命，之后会被退役并焚化。系统因此不是等设备偶然损坏，而是从一开始就把更换与销毁写进了流程。成长、记忆和依恋不是维护目标，反而会使“把它当设备”越来越困难。

所谓故障，有时只是一个存在不肯永远停在别人替它规定的位置上。

四、必要性不是虚构的

若只把布桥婴写成恶人的残忍发明，这个故事就容易了。

没有BB，送货员很难侦测搁浅者；没有相关技术，穿越危险区域和重建网络都要付出更大代价。它确实保护了山姆，也可能保护沿途更多人。

这套制度最有说服力的地方，正是它真的有用。每一份报告都可以写出收益，每一次牺牲都能被放进“避免更大损失”的一栏。没有人需要以作恶为乐，只要所有人都同意眼下没有更好的办法。

可“救了更多人”并不会让被使用者从账上消失。困难也正在这里：承认必要性，不能替代对代价的注视。

五、桥墩里埋着什么

故事后段，戴德曼透露了更深的一层：BB最初就是开罗尔网络的催化剂，被纳入各结点城的基础设施。至于是不是亚美莉亲自安置，他也只说“很可能”。可以确定的是，一次次牺牲被藏进了连接全国的技术底座。

这让游戏里的“桥”突然有了两种样子。

一种是玩家留给玩家的梯子和公路：陌生人可以互相帮忙，不要求回报。另一种是基础设施里的BB：有些人之所以能彼此连接，是因为另一个无法同意、也无法退出的生命被固定成了接口。

连接因此不能只按覆盖率衡量。网越大，不等于它越正当；还要问桥是怎样架起来的，谁能够走过，谁被砌在下面。

六、山姆每天都看得见

山姆无法站在制度外面发表干净的谴责。他用着BB-28，靠它避开搁浅者，也靠它完成重建网络的任务。他知道规程，还得继续往前走。

游戏没有让他先写一份宣言。它只让他一遍遍低头：跌倒以后检查舱体，听见哭声时轻轻摇晃，慢慢把一个编号叫成“Lou”。

这些动作没有立刻推翻任何制度。它们先改变了一个更小的事实：山姆与BB-28之间，不再只是使用者与设备。

很多变化不是从一句宏大的反对开始，而是从一个人再也无法若无其事地使用眼前这个“东西”开始。
