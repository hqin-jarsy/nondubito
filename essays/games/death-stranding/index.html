Death Stranding: Ropes, Bridges, and the Lives Beneath Them

死亡搁浅：绳、桥与被留下的人

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on delivery, asynchronous cooperation, the human cost hidden inside a network, grief, and Sam's final refusal to treat Lou as equipment.

Death Stranding makes connection feel difficult before it makes connection sound noble. A road must be built, cargo must be carried, and help often arrives from someone we will never meet. Yet its network also depends on lives classified as infrastructure. These essays read the game through that unresolved tension: a rope can hold people together, but a bridge may conceal somebody in its foundation.
