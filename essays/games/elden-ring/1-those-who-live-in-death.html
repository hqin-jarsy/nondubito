Essay I of IV · Elden Ring《艾尔登法环》解读 · I / IV

Those Who Live in Death

死诞者

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting of the Chinese argument for English-language readers. Plot claims have been checked and interpretive claims are marked as such.

Strike a skeleton in an Elden Ring graveyard and its bones collapse. Turn away too quickly and blue-white light gathers around the remains. The bones reassemble. The skeleton stands again.

Players soon learn the extra movement: knock it down, then strike the reforming remains before they rise. What looks like a minor enemy gimmick teaches the deepest disorder of the Lands Between. Death no longer completes itself.

Death has been administered

It is tempting to summarize the Golden Order by saying that nobody can die. That is too simple. People are killed throughout the Lands Between; corpses and tombs are everywhere. What Queen Marika removed and sealed was Destined Death, with the fated deaths of the demigods especially taken from the Elden Ring. Proper death was oriented toward return to the Erdtree, and Erdtree burial was granted as an honor.

The result is not the absence of death so much as an order that claims authority over how death occurs and where the dead belong.

The Night of Black Knives breaks that administration. Godwyn the Golden dies only in soul. His living corpse is buried at the Erdtree’s roots, where the fragment of Death spreads through the subterranean network as Deathroot and gives rise to Those Who Live in Death.

The skeleton rises because an unauthorized form of death has entered the roots—one the Golden Order neither sanctions nor successfully receives.

The thing that cannot be removed must be carried

The Golden Order begins when Destined Death is confined. Marika charges her shadowbound beast, Maliketh, with guarding the Rune of Death. After a fragment is stolen, he seals the remaining power inside his own body.

Death has not been destroyed. It has been made into one being’s permanent burden.

Maliketh is guard, lock, and container. His extraordinary strength belongs to him, but its lifelong use has been assigned in advance. The Order concentrates what it cannot absorb in a single body, removes that body from ordinary life, and behaves as though the contradiction has disappeared.

A lock proves that what lies behind it still exists. When the Tarnished defeats Maliketh, Destined Death is unleashed rather than invented. The excluded power no longer has to be suppressed by him alone.

Every order creates an outside

Erdtree burial sounds gentle: the dead return to common roots and receive a place within a shared sacred order.

Those Who Live in Death do not make that return. They refuse or fail to answer the Erdtree’s call, suspended between categories. Golden Order fundamentalists hunt them not because each skeleton has committed a moral offense, but because its continued existence demonstrates that the classification is incomplete.

An order is often more disturbed by what it cannot classify than by someone who openly argues against it.

Fia’s later project is not to normalize the undead by forcing them back into the old path. Her Mending Rune would place “life within Death” inside Order, granting a lawful condition to lives previously treated only as errors awaiting elimination.

A landscape without conclusions

Almost every region of the Lands Between looks caught after an ending that never arrived. The Shattering has no victor. Demigods clutch Great Runes inside territories that decay around them. Radahn still wanders the battlefield; Malenia sleeps in the Haligtree. Fortresses are half-ruined, festivals continue, and institutions repeat gestures whose purposes have been forgotten.

Destined Death cannot explain all of this. War, ambition, Scarlet Rot, and the broken Elden Ring have distinct effects. Together, however, these conditions produce a single lived impression: the old order cannot conclude its affairs, and no new order has arrived to receive what remains.

The player repeatedly performs the skeleton’s “second strike” on behalf of the world—kill, collect, mend, complete—hoping one more action will make something final. Every conclusion opens another absence.

Guidance on the ground

Grace appears as gold lines pointing toward significant destinations. It does not force a route. Players can ignore it, wander underground, and find entire regions the rays never make legible as the “next step.”

The light nevertheless remains. It externalizes an answer to the question of where one ought to go.

The Tarnished are those from whom Grace was once withdrawn. When Godfrey’s wars ended, he and his warriors lost the gold in their eyes and were exiled. After the Elden Ring broke, Grace called them and their descendants back.

Their worthiness is not restored because they have repented or changed. The Order needs candidates again.

This gives the Tarnished an unusual position. They have been excluded by the system and recruited to repair it. They can follow Grace, but they also possess a history that should prevent them from mistaking its light for the only possible direction.

The radiant tree is a locked door

The Erdtree illuminates the Lands Between. Burial, belief, and sovereignty all converge upon it. Yet when the Tarnished reaches its entrance, thorns refuse access.

The Two Fingers’ prescribed journey fails at the threshold. Even Enia, their interpreter, is surprised. To enter, the player must use the taboo Flame of Ruin and later release Destined Death. The Order commands a champion to repair it, bars the champion from doing so, and thereby makes transgression a requirement of obedience.

The Lands Between do not suffer merely because a ring is broken. They suffer because an order cannot contain its own exceptions, endings, or repair.

The next essay turns to Marika—the god of the Golden Order, the vessel of the Ring, and the person who shattered it.

墓地里的骷髅被打散以后，骨头会重新拼起来。

玩家很快学会补上第二击：先把它打倒，再趁灵魂般的光尚未聚回去时彻底结束。这个小动作不是随手设计的花样。它让玩家亲手适应交界地最深的异常——死亡在这里并不会自然完成。

一、不是所有的死都一样

《艾尔登法环》里的死亡很容易被一句“大家都死不了”概括，但这并不准确。普通人仍会被杀，墓地和遗体到处都是。改变的是“命定之死”被玛莉卡从黄金律中封存，半神的命定死亡尤其被剥离；死者应当回归黄金树，英雄还会获得归树的厚葬。

这不是没有死亡，而是一套秩序接管了死亡应该怎样发生、怎样被收回。

黑刀之夜破坏了这套安排。葛德文只死去灵魂，肉体却无法真正死亡。他被葬在黄金树根部，死亡卢恩的痕迹沿树根化为死根，催生了死诞者。

于是骷髅站起来，不是因为死亡被完全取消，而是因为一种未经黄金律许可、又无法被它顺利接住的死亡在地下扩散。

二、清不掉的东西由谁背

黄金律之所以成立，正因为命定之死被封存。玛莉卡让自己的影子野兽玛利喀斯看守死亡卢恩；碎片被盗以后，他更把它封进自己身体。

这套安排没有让死亡消失。它把死亡变成由一个存在永久背负的东西。

玛利喀斯因此同时是守卫、锁和容器。他的力量看起来属于他，使命却先于他的选择。秩序把无法纳入自身的危险集中到一个人身上，然后把那个人放到远处，仿佛问题已经解决。

可只要还需要一把锁，门后之物就仍然存在。褪色者最终击败玛利喀斯，命定之死被释放；被排除的部分不是从无处创造出来，只是终于不再由他独自压住。

三、归树也有一个外面

黄金树的死亡秩序听上去温和：死者返回树根，得到一个被共同秩序接纳的位置。

问题是死诞者回不去。它们拒绝或无法回应黄金树的召唤，卡在活与死之间。黄金律基本主义者因此猎杀它们，不是因为每一个骷髅都作恶，而是因为它们的存在本身证明秩序并未覆盖世界的全部。

一个制度最难面对的，有时不是公开反对它的人，而是它的分类里放不下、却仍然存在的东西。

菲雅后来为死诞者争取的也不是“恢复正常”。她要把“死中求生”的原则写进新的律法，使这批被排除者不再只是等待清除的错误。

四、没有结束的地景

整个交界地都带着没有结束的样子。破碎战争没有胜者，半神各自抱着一块大卢恩困在领地里。拉塔恩仍在战场游荡，玛莲妮亚沉睡在圣树深处；城堡塌了一半，仪式还在继续，参与者已忘记最初为何开始。

这不能全归因于命定之死被封存。战争、野心、腐败与法环破碎各有自己的作用。但这些景象共同强化了一种生活经验：旧秩序已经无法把事情收尾，新秩序又迟迟没有到来。

玩家因此不断替世界做“补一下”的动作。杀死、收集、修复、完成支线，好像只要多做最后一步，一切就能真正结束。可每个结束又会留下新的缺口。

五、地上的光

赐福的引导以金色光线指向重要方向。它不是强制路线：玩家可以完全不理会，走进洞穴、海岸和地下世界，许多精彩之处也根本不在光所指的直线上。

但光一直存在。它把“你接下来可能该去哪里”变成外部可见的指示。

褪色者则是曾失去赐福的人。葛孚雷与战士们在征战结束后被夺去金色眼眸，流放出交界地；法环破碎以后，赐福又把他们及其后裔召回。

资格先被撤回，又在秩序需要继任者时重新发放。个人是否改变并不重要，重要的是光现在是否需要他。

这正是褪色者身份里最微妙的地方：他既被秩序排除过，又被秩序选来修复自己，因此有机会服从光，也有经验不把光当作唯一方向。

六、黄金树是一扇关着的门

黄金树照亮整个交界地，死者被送往它的根部，信仰与权力都以它为中心。

可褪色者走到树前，入口被拒绝的荆棘封死。双指原先给出的道路在这里失效，连圆桌厅堂的解释者也措手不及。

要进入树内，必须动用被黄金律视为禁忌的巨人火焰，之后还要释放命定之死。秩序要求英雄修复它，却把修复者挡在门外；想完成它交付的任务，反而先得触犯它。

交界地的问题因此不只是法环破了。它还在问：一部律法若连自己的结束、例外与修复都无法容纳，最后会让谁去背那些被排除的部分？

下一篇写玛莉卡——黄金律的神、法环的容器，也是亲手把它砸碎的人。
