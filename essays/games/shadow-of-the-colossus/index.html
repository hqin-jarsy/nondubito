Reading Shadow of the Colossus

《旺达与巨像》解读

Aug 30, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on a resurrection pursued without consent, sixteen beings turned into steps on somebody else’s road, and the unpriced act with which Mono begins her restored life.

Shadow of the Colossus gives the player one clear objective and almost no ordinary opposition. The emptiness makes every act legible: Wander must travel to beings that were not pursuing him, climb their bodies, and end lives the interface has already converted into a numbered list.

The game never denies Wander’s love for Mono. It asks what love becomes when one person decides the price, another cannot answer, and sixteen unrelated lives are entered into the account.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Game criticism
He Never Asked Her

Wander rejects a law that sacrificed Mono, then makes her resurrection the reason sixteen other beings may be sacrificed without consultation.

She Woke Owing a Debt

2026Three reading modes

三篇从一场无法征得同意的复活、被做成道路的十六个巨像，读到莫诺醒来后抱起长角婴儿的那个无价动作。

《旺达与巨像》给玩家一个极清楚的目标，却几乎没有普通意义上的敌人。正因为世界如此空，每个动作才变得清楚：旺达必须主动找到那些没有追赶他的存在，爬上身体，并结束已经被界面排成清单的生命。

作品从未否认旺达对莫诺的爱。它追问的是：当一个人决定价码、另一个人无法回答、十六条无关的生命被写进账里时，爱会变成什么。
