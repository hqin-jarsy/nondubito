Reading The Last of Us Part II

《最后生还者 第二部》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on Joel’s rescue and lie, two true accounts that each demand a death, Abby’s turn toward Lev, and a final refusal that requires neither forgiveness nor mutual understanding.

The Last of Us Part II does not ask the player to discover that both sides are secretly the same. It does something harsher: it gives each side a history that remains true and refuses to let one history cancel the other.

The essays focus less on a moral slogan about revenge than on withheld choice, belated knowledge, and the difference between understanding an enemy and declining to destroy one.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Game criticism
He Never Asked Her

The Fireflies prepare to kill an unconscious Ellie; Joel rescues her and then lies for four years. The rescue denies one choice, while the lie prevents her from knowing what was denied.

Two True Accounts

The game makes the player kill Abby’s friends before living beside them. Knowledge arrives after agency, when it can no longer become the flattering choice to spare someone.

Lev

The Beach

Ellie releases Abby without learning her story or forgiving her. Refusal can occur before understanding—and after the possibility of recovering what was lost has already gone.

2026Three reading modes

四篇从乔尔的救人与谎言、两套都要求一条命的真账，读到艾比转向勒弗，以及那次既不以原谅、也不以互相理解为前提的停手。

《最后生还者 第二部》并不是要玩家发现双方原来完全一样。它做的事情更难受：给双方各自一段成立的历史，同时拒绝让任何一段把另一段抵消。

这四篇不把作品压成一句“复仇无用”，而是追踪被拿走的选择、来得太迟的认识，以及理解一个敌人与不再毁掉她之间的差别。

英文 · 简 / 繁 · 游戏解读
他没有问过她

火萤准备在艾莉失去意识时牺牲她，乔尔救出她，又把真相隐瞒四年；救人替她决定了一次，谎言则让她无从知道自己被决定过。

两套账

游戏先让玩家杀死艾比的朋友，再让玩家与他们共同生活；认识发生在行动之后，已经来不及变成一次令自己满意的宽恕。

勒弗

海滩
