Reading The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

《巫师3：狂猎》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on the Bloody Baron’s accurate confession, the costs hidden inside Velen’s famous choice, the plans built around Ciri, and five small moments in which Geralt refuses to live for her.

The Witcher 3 is celebrated for difficult choices, but difficulty alone is not its achievement. Its strongest quests ask who gets to choose, who pays, and whether a compassionate purpose can still turn a person into material.

The series moves from Velen to Ciri. Geralt cannot purify every consequence or defeat the White Frost for her. What he can do is help create the conditions under which her action remains hers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Game criticism
He Told It Accurately

The Bloody Baron eventually describes his violence without grammar that moves the blame. Accuracy does not undo the act, and confession does not automatically become change.

Everyone Wants What She Carries

The Wild Hunt, Avallac’h, the Lodge, and Emhyr approach Ciri with different purposes. Even protection and love can become plans in which her power matters more than her answer.

He Did Not Do It for Her

A snowball fight, refused payment, a closed door, a wrecked laboratory, and Skjall’s grave teach Ciri that Geralt trusts her to act without abandoning her to act alone.

四篇从血腥男爵准确却无力的认账、威伦名题里被分配给别人的代价，读到围绕希里展开的计划，以及杰洛特五次没有替她生活。

《巫师3》以艰难选择闻名，但“难选”本身并不是它真正的成就。最好的任务会继续追问：谁在选择，谁在付账，一个听起来仁慈的目的是否仍会把另一个人当成材料。

这四篇从威伦走到希里面前。杰洛特无法把所有后果洗干净，也不能替她面对白霜；他能做的，是让她最后的行动仍然属于她。

他自己把话说准了

血腥男爵最终没有用语法挪走责任，而是准确说出自己的暴力；准确不能撤销伤害，承认也不会自动变成改变。

没有干净的选项

呢喃山丘的选择把死亡分给孩子、村民、安娜与男爵；少见的任务顺序会改变结果，却没有把决定变成没有代价的第三条路。
