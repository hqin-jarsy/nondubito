SAE Meaning Theory

The Meaning of a Life Lies Outside It

意义论系列

A man of forty finds the notebook he kept at twenty. What happens in that chair is not a score and not a reading. Those things are still in his name, and he is handling them again. Meaning, here, is not a quantity but a relation — the first-person carrying a person maintains between his own different positions in time.

Attribution closes on one person; the outlet of direction is not sealed within that person. Which is to say: the meaning of a life lies outside that life. And that outside may be empty, and being empty is no loss.

Eight essays, from the five papers of the SAE Meaning Theory. They set no score, rank no life, and issue no instruction.

Three reading modes: English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Eight essays · English / 中文 / 繁體
Essay 01
There Is No Number

A man of forty finds the notebook he kept at twenty. What happens in that chair is not a score. Three exclusions, each on its own grounds; why a description is not a coarse reading; and why no procedure can close the account from outside.

Essay 02
Still in His Name

Both ends of this relation are one person at different times. A new determination lands only on the current end and cannot shut the ends still to come. Thirty years confer nothing. Only the endpoints close, and the outlet of direction does not.

Essay 03
He Thought It Was What He Wanted

The subject he read was his father's choice, and he took it for his own. Two ledgers: what was actually brought off, and what was run as though it had been. Consequences are not entries, and seeing through it does not move the entry.

Essay 04
Neither Behind Nor Ahead

Three kinds of incompleteness that do not add up to one. Nobody is waiting behind the layers, and no finished self is reachable ahead — the reason is reflexivity, not time. Growth is the acquiring of reach, not approach to a finished self.

Essay 05
Closed from Ahead

Irrecoverability is not the past hardening; it is a door ahead being closed. Giving way, failing, losing, departing: four shapes, each needing its own proof. An order maintained from outside, and one negative line — do not fake completion.

Essay 06
Someone in Next Year's Plans

He has done nothing for that friend in years, and the friend still shapes how he arranges the year. Looking-toward, the three outward forms, what support may not counterfeit, and the hardest test: he can stop revolving around me.

Essay 07
What Stops and What Continues

Incapacity is not departure, and departure does not of itself prove the position has exited — on that, neither yes nor no. What continues are conditions and effects; what later people own is their own. Handing over, without possession.

Essay 08
How Others Have Put It

Kant's postulate recast as a posture; Kierkegaard, Heidegger, Levinas, Wittgenstein, Aristotle, Wolf and Metz, the narrative tradition, and the three imperishables. Where the lines fall, and what this narrowing bought and cost.

SAE 意义论

人生的意义在人生之外

SAE Meaning Theory

一个人四十岁上翻出二十岁时写的本子。坐在那里发生的事不是打分，也不是读数。那些事仍在他名下，而他正在重新处理它们。意义在这里不是一个量，是一段关系——一个人在自身不同时间位置之间维持的第一人称承接。

归属闭于一人，方向的出口不封于一人。也就是说：人生的意义在人生之外。而那个外面可以空着，空着也不亏。

八篇散文，改写自《SAE 意义论》五篇论文。它们不打分，不排高下，也不发一条应当。

三种阅读模式：英文、简体、繁體。
秦汉 · 2026 · 八篇 · 英文／中文／繁體
第一篇
没有那个数

一个人四十岁上翻出二十岁写的本子。坐在那里发生的事不是打分。三路排除各有各的根据；描述为什么不是量得粗的读数；以及为什么没有一套从外面结清这份账的程序。

第二篇
仍在他名下

这段关系的两端是同一个人的不同时间。新的裁断只落在当前这一端，而它裁不死后面那一端。三十年不产生资格。闭合的只是端点，而方向的出口不封。

第三篇
他以为那是自己要的

他读的那一门是父亲替他定的，而他当年以为那是自己要的。两本账：实际做成的，与当作做成的。影响不是条目；识破也不移动条目。

第四篇
不在前，也不在后

三种未完成不能收成一句。层层剥开的后面没有一尊等着的自己，前面也没有一个做完的自己——理由是反身，不是时间。成长登记的是够得着，不是靠近。

第五篇
前面被关掉了

不可补回不是过去变硬，是前面有一扇门关上了。让掉，失败，失去，退场，四样各须另证。外面替你维持着的那套次序，以及一条负面的边界：不伪造。

第六篇
明年的安排里有一个人

他好些年没有为那位朋友做过什么，而朋友仍在他排这一年的时候在那里。看向，朝外的三种形态，支持不得冒充什么，以及最硬的那道检验：他能够不再围绕我。

第七篇
什么停下，什么继续

失能不是退场；而退场也不凭自身证明那个位置已经退出——这一处两头都不说。继续的是条件与作用，后来者认领的是后来者自己的。交出，而不占有。

第八篇
别人是怎么谈的

康德的设准改成了一种姿态；克尔凯郭尔，海德格尔，列维纳斯，维特根斯坦，亚里士多德，沃尔夫与梅茨，叙事一支，以及三不朽。分界在哪里，这次收窄换来了什么，放弃了什么。
