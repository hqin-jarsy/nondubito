生命元素周期表 III：从“我”到物自体

Periodic Table of Life III: From “I” to the Thing-in-Itself

关于作者：秦汉 About the author: Han Qin

从"我"出发

前两篇建立了先验域和生命、认知两轮。第二篇停在12DD的余项：预测无法预测预测者本身，"我"必然涌现。

这篇接过来。"我"涌现之后发生什么？

前三轮——先验、生命、认知——都是自然的：逻辑的自然、生物学的自然、心灵的自然。第四轮是"我"第一次出现，"我"面对的问题不再是自然的，而是关于意义的。精神分析恰好是处理这个领域的学科：自我如何形成，意义如何建立，他者如何被承认。

第四轮：自由（13DD–16DD）

13DD自我无目的（选/生）：认知转向自身的第一刀。"我"从预测律的余项中被选出——认知系统第一次发现"是我在做这些事情"。

弗洛伊德结构模型中，自我从本我中分化出来。本我是纯粹的驱力——欲望、冲动、快乐原则。自我在本我与现实之间调停。框架的读法：本我对应9DD–12DD（有选择、感知、记忆、预测，但无"我"）；自我对应13DD（第一次有了"我"）。

13DD还带来了自传性记忆：不只是"我在这里"的瞬时意识，而是"我一直在这里"的连续意识。这也是为什么大多数人最早的记忆始于2–3岁——13DD建立之前，记忆（11DD）存在，但没有"我"来组织它。

13DD的余项：对死亡的意识。自我意识一旦涌现，就立刻面对一个不可逃避的推论：我会死。这不是学来的知识，而是13DD（自我意识）与12DD（预测）的必然组合。

14DD自我有目的（定/自）：13DD面对死亡，必须赋予行动以意义，否则行动没有理由。超我是14DD结构必要性的产物：你知道你会死；你必须决定什么值得做。弗兰克尔的意义疗法是14DD的临床对应——不是"治愈创伤"，而是"找到行动的方向"。

15DD单向NonDubito（展/他）：自我（自我意识，13DD）面对死亡（无目的/目的）之后扩展向他者——单向地将他者视为目的本身。这是孤独主体的自我扩展：我单方面将他者的地位提升到目的。这仍然是"我的"认可。

16DD相互NonDubito（固/死）：双向的、不可撤回的相互承认。每一方都将对方视为目的，而非手段。这是非双重虚无的完成形式，是整个生命元素周期表的终点。

物自体

16DD之后，凿构循环遭遇了它无法构造的东西——物自体。

物自体不能被放大：无法被构造的东西无论分辨率多高都无法被构造。它在粗粒度和细粒度视角下占据同一个位置——因为它标记的是框架本身的边界，而非某个有待被放大的内容。

这是整个序列唯一一个在两个尺度下完全相同的位置，也是框架对自身局限性最诚实的声明。

Starting from "I"

The first two papers established the a priori domain and the life and cognition rounds. The second paper stopped at the remainder of 12DD: prediction cannot predict the predictor itself, and "I" necessarily emerges.

This paper picks up from "I." What happens after it emerges?

The first three rounds — a priori, life, cognition — are all natural: the natural of logic, of biology, of mind. Round 4 is the first time "I" appears, and the problems "I" faces are no longer natural but concern meaning. Psychoanalysis is precisely the discipline that addresses this domain: how the self forms, how meaning is established, how the other is recognized.

Round 4: Freedom (13DD–16DD)

13DD Self-without-Purpose (Select/Birth): The first time cognition turns toward itself. "Self" is selected from the remainder of the law of prediction — the first time the cognitive system discovers "it is I who am doing these things."

In Freud's structural model, the Ego differentiates from the Id. The Id is pure drive — desire, impulse, the pleasure principle. The Ego mediates between the Id and reality. The framework's reading: the Id corresponds to 9DD–12DD (selection, perception, memory, prediction present, but no "I"); the Ego corresponds to 13DD (the first time there is "I").

13DD also brings autobiographical memory: not just the momentary awareness of "I am here" but the continuous awareness of "I have been here." This is why most people's earliest memories date to around age 2–3 — before 13DD is established, memory (11DD) exists, but there is no "I" to organize it.

The remainder of 13DD: awareness of death. Once self-awareness emerges, it immediately faces an unavoidable inference: I will die. This is not learned knowledge — it is the necessary combination of 13DD (self-awareness) and 12DD (prediction).

14DD Self-with-Purpose (Determine/Self): 13DD faces death and must give action meaning, or action has no reason. The Superego is the product of 14DD's structural necessity: you know you will die; you must decide what is worth doing. Frankl's logotherapy is the clinical counterpart — not "healing trauma" but "finding the direction of action."

15DD Unilateral Non Dubito (Expand/Other): Self (self-awareness, 13DD) facing death (without-/with-purpose) expands toward the other — unilaterally extending the status of End to the other. This is the solitary subject's self-expansion: I unilaterally elevate the other's status to that of a purpose. Still "my" recognition.

16DD Mutual Non Dubito (Solidify/Death): Bidirectional, irrevocable mutual recognition. Each party treats the other as an end, not a means. This is the completed form of Non Dubito, the terminus of the entire Periodic Table of Life.

The Thing-in-Itself

After 16DD, the chisel-construct cycle encounters what it cannot construct — the thing-in-itself.

The thing-in-itself cannot be magnified: what cannot be constructed cannot be constructed regardless of resolution. It occupies the same position at both coarse and fine scales — because it marks the boundary of the framework itself, not some content awaiting magnification.

This is the only position in the entire sequence that is identical at both scales, and the framework's most honest statement of its own limits.
