知道了为什么还不够

Why Knowing Is Not Enough

Han Qin (秦汉)

书快完了。德性讲完了，友爱讲完了，快乐讲完了，幸福讲完了。

第十卷第九章一开口，他问了一句：

关于这些，关于那些德性，还有友爱和快乐，要是已经说得够了，说了个大概，那么这件事该不该算是做到头了。

这里有一个问号。而后面紧跟着一个"还是"，那一支后头还有第二个问号：

还是像人们说的那样，在做的事情上，终点不是把每一样都看明白弄清楚，而是把它们做出来；关于德性也一样，知道还不够，得试着有它，用它，要么就得有别的什么法子让我们变好。

这两支都还是问句。第二支已经把方向摆了出来，可它还没有论证。论证从下一句才起。

要是那些道理本身足以把人做成好人，它们理当挣到许多大笔的酬劳，照忒奥格尼斯说的；那样的话，弄到那笔酬劳才是要紧的事。

"照忒奥格尼斯说的"这半句，贴的是他刚说完的那几个字，不是泛泛地引他的看法。

可事实上，那些道理看来只有这么点力气：它们能把年轻人里性子疏朗的那一些鼓动起来，能让一副本来就高贵又真心爱好东西的性子，被好东西整个占住；至于多数人，那些道理没有本事把他们劝到好上头去。

为什么。他答得很直：

因为他们生来就不是服羞的，是服怕的；他们不碰坏事，不是因为那事丑，是因为有罚。

他们照着情绪过日子，追自己那一份快活，追能带来这份快活的东西，躲开与之相反的难受；至于什么是好的，什么是真正甜的，他们连个想头都没有，因为他们压根没尝过。

然后是这一章的第三个问号，也是整段的转轴：

这样的人，什么样的道理能把他们重新塑一遍。

他自己接了一句：那些从老早起就长进性子里的东西，靠一句道理挪不动，就算挪得动也不容易。能指望的顶多是，该有的都有了的时候，我们分得着一点德性。

讲完这一段，他把人怎么变好这一问重新摆了一遍，摆成三条路：有人说靠天生，有人说靠习惯，有人说靠教。

书从这儿转弯。三条路怎么处置，往下那一段归另外两篇。

而这一篇停在这儿，不是收得早。是他这一段里本来就还没有法，没有强制，没有立法的人。罚这个字在这儿只以"他们怕的是罚"的样子出现，是在说那些人怎么被推动的；至于该拿什么去推，一个字还没有。

他在这一段里做的只有一件事：一个诊断。

两本书在最后一页做了同一个动作：把自己交出去。

《第一批判》那本书的收尾是这么写的：这部批判至此写毕，随即入册，立于被追问之下，此后照亮它的光来自读它的人。它开篇还写过一句更硬的：写完那一刻起它就立在被追问之下，你翻开它的这一刻起，追问的是你。

它连怎么被查都替人备好了。凡例那一节明写：这几条写作之法录在这儿不是自陈，是武装读者，法在明处，追问才有抓手；读者要是在书里抓到违反此法的地方，那正是这几条法等着的用处。

它还把这件事立成了整本书的建筑：它没有把一次论证当成一张永久的执照，而是把每一条主张的来路和可以换掉的地方都留在明面上，让后来的人随时重审。它自己给这个安排下过一句判词：批判这个名分不由一纸判决书担保，由永不休庭的法庭担保。

它还写过这个法庭为什么散不了：当下的这一问，尚未被问。不闭合的那一处，由他者和后来者接手。

两边都不肯让"讲完了"等于"办完了"。

分开的地方，就在他做完那个诊断之后的下一步。

他诊断完就往外走。对着服怕的那些人讲道理不起作用，所以他去找一样能对着"怕"起作用的东西。找的是什么，怎么找，从下一句起，归另一篇。

这边交出去的时候，手上只有说理。开篇那一句，凡例那一节，收尾那一句，还有那座永不休庭的法庭。每一样都是把话说清楚，把规矩摆到明处，把可以驳的地方先标出来。全是说理。

一个诊断出说理不够，就掉头去找别的；一个把全部指望留在说理上。

羞和怕这一对，带过孩子的人分得出来。

小孩不碰某样东西，可以有两种。一种是他知道那样不好。一种是他知道碰了要挨说。

表面上一模一样，都是没碰。可你一转身，两种就分开了。

而要紧的不是他嫌服怕的那些人不好。他把这件事当成一个关于办法的事实来处理：对着服怕的人讲道理，讲得再对也不起作用，所以得换一样东西。

这边欠他的那一笔，就在这个位置上。

先要撤掉一句容易顺手写下的话：这边没有失败条件。这句话不成立。这边的失败条件写得很清楚：一样东西被搬去用的地方越多，没标地址的越界处就越多；长期缺少外头的复核，那些没结清的东西会一笔垫着一笔往下走。

空着的那一格在别处。

把这边为着被人追问所做的安排点一遍。把规矩写在明处，武装读者。不把一次论证当成永久执照，改成随时可以重审。发问者的身份本身不算凭证，谁问都算，问题的成色由那一问自己的对账结算。而且它明写过：自问担保不了发现自己的全部盲区，所以外头那一道校验是自问替代不了的。承担的人还可以不是一个人，是一伙人依着公开的程序一起作判。

这几样各管各的。有的说清为什么非得有人来，有的把来了以后的手续备齐，有的把门槛压到最低。

没有一样是让人来。

把成本压低和把动机给出，是两件事。这本书把前一件做得很足，后一件一个字没写。

而这边不是漏了，是拆过。这边在讲动力的地方专门拆掉过一样东西：没有哪一片没做完的地方，逼得着你去做下一步。做不做，在做的人。

拆得对。可拆完以后，这本书自己的最后一步，要的正是那个没人保得住的下一步。

这一层在办公室里也见得着，而且不好看。

一份写得很好的东西发出去，没有人回。不是被驳了，是没人回。写的人会当作是自己写得不够好，于是改，改完再发，还是没人回。

问题不一定在那份东西上。它落在了一个没有什么东西保着有人会来读的位置上。

这一本十三篇，每一篇接他亲手摆上桌的一道。摆完了。

照他那一问：这算办完了吗。

按他的答法，讲完了不能算办完。而按他那个分法，这十三篇整个都在"看明白"那一头，一篇也没到"做出来"那一头。它们做成的是把两边并排摆开，让碰得正的地方和岔开的地方都露在外头。

而他那个词还在那儿等着。

自足这个词，他在这本书里最后一次用，是问道理能不能独自把人做成好人。而那是一个假设，被他下一句当场否掉了。

否掉的是"独自"，不是道理。

这一本十三篇，是道理。

两句话摆在一处，谁也不替谁作证。一句说：这本书的效力不由自家宣布，得有人来参与结算。一句说：道理说得再对，也不能独自把人做成好人。

而这一本自足不自足，照它自己那条规矩，不由它自己说。

Has the Inquiry Reached Its End?

At the opening of the final chapter of the Nicomachean Ethics, Aristotle asks whether the project is complete. Virtue, friendship, pleasure, and happiness have been treated in outline. Is that the end?

The answer is built into the contrast that follows. In practical matters, the aim is not to contemplate and understand each item but to do it. With virtue, knowledge is insufficient; we must try to possess and exercise virtue or find some other way of becoming good.

Aristotle then tests the power of argument. If arguments by themselves could make people good, they would deserve enormous rewards. In fact they can encourage generous young people and help a well-disposed character become possessed by what is noble. They cannot turn the many toward goodness.

The diagnosis is unsentimental. Many people respond not to shame at what is base but to fear of punishment. They pursue familiar pleasures and avoid pains; having never tasted what is noble, they do not even possess the relevant attraction. What argument could remodel a character formed in that way?

At this point, the book changes direction.

A Fact About Means

Aristotle is not merely insulting the unreceptive. He treats their condition as a fact about method. If the audience is moved by fear rather than reason, more elegant reasoning will not solve the problem. Habits planted over years are not easily removed by a sentence.

He therefore reopens the question of formation. People are said to become good by nature, habituation, or teaching. Nature is not in our control and belongs only to the genuinely fortunate. Teaching does not work on everyone; the learner's soul must already have been prepared by habits, like soil capable of nourishing seed.

Law enters because habit needs structure and because most people obey necessity more readily than argument. Upbringing must be ordered from youth and sustained through adult life. The command of a parent lacks the force of law, while law combines compulsion with a form of practical reason.

This transition is philosophically decisive. Aristotle does not take the insufficiency of argument as a reason to abandon reason. He searches for institutions capable of forming the conditions under which reason can later be heard.

A Book That Hands Itself Over

SAE's critical project ends with a different gesture. Its claims are placed under continuing review. The rules of writing are made explicit so that readers can locate violations. No argument receives a permanent license; grounds and revisable points remain visible. The court stays open because the present question has not yet been asked by every later person who may encounter it.

Both works refuse to identify “said” with “done.” Aristotle's conclusion says that understanding virtue is not virtue. SAE says that a framework's validity cannot be secured by its own declaration; readers and later reviewers participate in settlement.

The difference appears immediately after the diagnosis. Aristotle turns outward from argument to habituation, law, and legislation. SAE's handoff remains largely within reason-giving: expose the premises, arm the reader, invite objection, preserve revisability.

One project discovers that argument is not self-sufficient and looks for another kind of force. The other builds its final hope around argument becoming answerable to more argument.

Lowering the Cost Is Not Supplying the Motive

SAE does not lack failure conditions. A claim transported across too many contexts without an address accumulates liabilities. An account isolated from external review can compound its blind spots. The framework also explains why self-questioning cannot substitute completely for another perspective.

It has therefore done substantial work to lower the cost of participation. The rules are visible. The status of the questioner does not decide the quality of the question. A group can share judgment through public procedure. The reader is given handles by which to challenge the text.

None of these arrangements makes a reader arrive.

Reducing the threshold and generating motivation are different achievements. An office can publish an exemplary draft, document every assumption, and request review. No one responds. The silence does not prove the document was poor; it may occupy a system in which no incentive, time, norm, or relation protects the act of reading.

SAE deliberately denies that an unfinished remainder compels the next step. There is always somewhere else to cut, but no gap forces a person to continue. That restraint protects agency. It also means that the framework's own final review depends on an action it cannot guarantee.

From Ethics to Politics

Aristotle does more than append “law matters.” He follows the problem through private and public education to the competence of the legislator. Politicians possess experience but do not reliably articulate or transmit it. Sophists claim to teach politics while misunderstanding its object. Collections of laws are useful only to readers already capable of judging them.

The final sentences therefore announce a new inquiry into constitutions: what preserves and destroys them, what arrangements are best, and what laws and customs they require. The Ethics ends by converting an unresolved dependency into a program of political study.

This does not settle the moral dangers of coercive formation. Who writes the law? Whose virtue is imposed? Which lives are excluded from citizenship? Aristotle's own political world makes these questions unavoidable. But he has identified a debt that cannot be paid by another chapter of ethical explanation alone.

SAE should preserve the same pressure. If external review is indispensable, an account of open procedure is not yet an account of the practices, institutions, and motives that sustain reviewers. The missing reader is not a minor implementation issue. The validity structure depends on someone taking up the invitation.

Thirteen Essays, All on One Side

This series has placed thirteen questions from Aristotle beside SAE. It has clarified agreements, marked divergences, and let each framework expose debts in the other. By Aristotle's distinction, all of that remains on the side of seeing and understanding. None of it guarantees the formation of a good person or the arrival of a serious respondent.

That does not make argument useless. Aristotle denies its independence, not its value. Argument can move a receptive person, articulate what practice aims at, and help law become more than naked force. SAE likewise needs reasons in public if review is to be more than power answering power.

The final question is therefore not whether reason works. It is whether reason works alone. Aristotle says no and turns toward politics. SAE says its own effect must be settled by others, then waits for others who remain free not to come.

These essays are arguments. Whether they are self-sufficient cannot, by their own rule, be decided here.
