过到什么份上才算过

How Far Is Too Far?

Han Qin (秦汉)

第二卷第九章一开口，他就把前面收了尾：性格上的德性是个中道，怎么是的，它是两种毛病中间的那一个，一头过一头不及，说到这儿够了。

收完他没有往下推。他做了一件在这本书里不多见的事：他给了三条上手的办法。

给之前先说了一句：所以做个好人是件力气活。

他说得很实在。在每一样事情上取那个中间，都是力气活；就像一个圆的圆心，不是谁都找得着，得是懂的人才找得着。

接下来那一句写得很利落：

发火这件事人人会，也容易；给钱花钱也是。可对谁发，发多少，什么时候发，为着什么事发，怎么个发法，这就不是人人会的了，也不容易。所以做得好这件事既少见，又值得夸，又漂亮。

这句话里那五样，一样也不是刻度。它们只是把"该发多少火"这个问题拆成了五个更细的问题，而每一个还是同样难。

然后是三条办法。

第一条：先躲开更歪的那一头。他引了卡吕普索一句：把船开出这团烟和这片浪。两个极端里总有一个错得更厉害。既然正中间极难打中，那就照人说的走第二条水路，在祸害里头挑小的。

第二条：看清自己往哪边倒，然后往反方向拽自己。怎么知道自己往哪边倒，看什么让你快活，什么让你难受，就知道了。往反方向使劲，拽得远一点，反倒能落到中间。他说这就像扳弯木头的人干的活。

第三条：处处防着快活。因为我们评判快活的时候，不是不受贿的。特洛伊城头那些老人看见海伦的时候是什么心思，我们对着快活就该是什么心思，把他们那句话对它念一遍；这么把她送走，就少错一点。

这三条有个共同的样子：一条也不给你一把量完照数执行的尺。第一条还明明白白在比较两个极端哪个错得更厉害，所以它们不是没有理由的手艺动作；它们是三条上得了手的办法，不是一套能自动算出中间点的公式。

而收尾那一段，是他给这套办法自己标的界。

他说，这也许难，尤其在一件一件具体的事上。因为怎么发怒，对谁发，为着什么事发，发多久，这些不容易划出来。

他还举了个我们自己嘴上的证据：有时候我们夸那些不足的人，说他和气；有时候又管发狠的人叫有男子气。

接着他把责备的线往下描：偏一点的不受责备，不管往哪边偏；偏得多的才受责备，因为偏得多瞒不住。

然后是这一章真正的那一句：

可到什么份上，过了多少，才算该受责备，不容易用道理划出线来。因为凡是要靠觉察的东西，都没有一样是容易划的。这一类事情落在一件一件的具体情形里，而判断落在觉察上。

他不是只在说发怒。发怒是他的例子，例子说完就丢开了。他接下去写的是凡是要靠觉察的东西，一句都没有留在发怒上。

所以这不是一个题材上的小注。这是他给整套中道说法标的界：这套办法能把你带到那一处，带不进那一处里面去。

他标完界，最后一句还是往回收的：这么多至少说清了，中间那个状态在一切事上都值得夸，而人得有时候朝过头那边偏一点，有时候朝不及那边偏一点，因为这样最容易打中中间和那个好。

他没有给那条线一把通用的刻度，也没有因为通用的刻度给不出来就把中道扔掉。中间和那个好照旧在它们的位置上；只是它在具体一件事里怎么落，得由个案的觉察来判。

他没有说个案本身没有边。他说的是普遍的道理量不出每一个个案。

他站住的那一处，这边也站得住。

两边都不认那一句：一条普遍的公式已经替每一个个案把线画完了。

这边不认它，用的是一个先摆出来才看得见的分法。

一次划界落在一个场子里。场子里的每一样东西受一次判，而判下来只有四种落法：落在这一边，落在那一边，两边都落上，一边也不落。四种是判下来的形式，不是关于那件事的四种真假。

这一篇要的是第三格：两边都落上。

要紧的是这一格的地位。它本身只是一种落法，不是一个待清理的错。只有在收下了"同一个地址上不许两边都命中"这一条的那一支上，它才带着一个负担：接着往下分条件。而这边把那一条登记成可以换掉的。换掉了，那一格就是一个正当的落法，不承诺散开。

日常里的对照也在用，只是不点名。

一件事既算迟到又算没迟到，因为"哪一刻算到"还没有定下来。这不是那件事本身没有边，也不是谁看走了眼。这时候要么把"到"定下来，要么承认这一格就这么记着。

而表格上另有一栏画横杠。那是第四格：一边也不落。填"没有"是一次判断，画横杠是说这儿轮不到判断。这一格跟第三格不是一回事，摆在一起是为了不混。

可这条线怎么才落得下来，两边接下来那一步不一样。

他把个案的收尾交给觉察。这一类事情落在一件一件的具体情形里，而具体的东西要靠觉察。这一章只说到这儿；至于那份觉察为什么跟经历和明智的人连着，是第六卷才补的。

这边接下来那一步不是去找一个看得准的人，是把这一次的地址再摊开一格：面对的是什么，在哪一层上说的，认了哪几条前提，哪一样还没定。

而摊开本身不替你判。摊到第几格算够，还是一次判断。

他把最后那一下交给一个人的眼力，这边把它交给下一次判断。而下一次判断，还是判断。

带过孩子就知道这两步的分别。

孩子闹到什么份上该拦，什么份上该由他去，这件事上没有一条线。有经验的人确实看得出来，可你问他凭什么看出来的，他未必说得出来，只能说这回不一样。

说不出来这件事，他那边有个正面的说法：判断落在觉察上，而觉察是那个人的觉察。（这一章只说到觉察；觉察和经历的关系，第六卷才写。）

这边会往另一头走：这回哪里不一样。是孩子今天没睡够，还是场合不同，还是这一次拦了以后你自己接不接得住。这些一条条摆出来，原先记在同一个地址上的两次命中，可能就分到两个地址上去了。

可摆出来够不够分得开，仍然是下一次判断的事，不由这张单子先替你保住。

他在那条界上留了东西给人用。他给了三条办法：先躲开更歪的那一头，看清自己往哪边倒然后往反方向拽，处处防着快活。这三条一条也不给你刻度，可它们是能上手的：你今天就能照着做一件。

这边在那条界上留的是四种落法。

四种落法定的是这一判怎么入账。它没有从入账的形式里长出一条上手的办法来。

这一句不是说上游漏写了一样本该写的东西。那张表本来就是定入账形式的，它没有许诺产出行动上的办法。是这一篇拿他那三条量过来，量出的一处类型差，而这一量是本篇自己下的判。

也不是说这边欠一套应当。这边不出应当，这一条写在门口。差的不是应当，是上手。

而中道那件事，恰恰是要下手的。孩子这会儿正闹着。

他那三条办法本身也不保证打中。他自己写着：第二条水路，在祸害里头挑小的。第二条水路的意思是风停了，划桨走，比不上顺风。

所以他给的不是一把尺，是三个方向感。

而方向感把你带到那一处，最后那一下还得由觉察来判。三条办法帮你走近个案，走不完个案。

他在那一处站住了，还回头递了三样东西出来。

这边站在同一处，手里拿着一张定入账的表。

The Mean Has No Ruler

“Virtue is a mean” is among the most repeated and least helpful summaries of Aristotle. It invites a picture of arithmetic moderation: locate two extremes, measure the distance, and stand in the middle. Aristotle repeatedly blocks that picture. The mean is relative to us, determined as practical reason would determine it, and action concerns particulars in which exactness is unavailable.

By the end of Book II, the elegant definition has become a practical problem. How does anyone find the mean when desire and habit distort perception? Aristotle offers rough aids rather than an algorithm. Avoid the more opposed extreme. Notice the direction in which you personally tend to be carried and pull yourself toward the other side, as one straightens bent wood. Above all, guard against pleasure, because we are poor judges when pleasure is involved.

The final decision remains a matter of perception. At what point anger becomes excessive, generosity wasteful, or caution cowardly cannot be stated with universal precision. Small deviations may escape blame; larger ones are evident. The boundary is real without being mechanically specifiable.

Direction Before Measurement

Aristotle's advice works by bias correction. It does not tell everyone to move toward the same extreme. A person disposed to rashness needs restraint; a person disposed to fear may need deliberate exposure to risk. The practical recommendation is indexed to the agent's deformation.

That makes the mean unlike compromise. If one party proposes cruelty and another mercy, the virtuous response is not halfway between them. Nor is mediocrity a mean between excellence and failure. The relevant excess and deficiency are defined by the field of action, the circumstances, the people involved, and the reasons for acting.

Calling the mean “relative to us” does not make it whatever feels comfortable. Feeling is part of the evidence and part of the problem. The well-formed agent takes pleasure and pain in the right objects, but someone in formation cannot simply use present pleasure as the measure of what is right.

What a Fourfold Audit Can Do

SAE approaches indeterminate cases through a different instrument. Its fourfold form asks whether a distinction leaves something uncollected on the included side, misplaces an item across the boundary, permits dual belonging under different conditions, or fails to govern the case at all. The value of the form is bookkeeping. It prevents “either/or” from silently deciding what the criterion has not earned.

Consider a parent deciding whether help has become control. The action may genuinely support the child and still occupy space the child needs. It may be help in one respect and intrusion in another. Or “help versus control” may be the wrong division because the immediate issue is safety. The fourfold form can display these possibilities without forcing them into one scale.

But displaying the branches does not choose among them. SAE can make the address explicit and reveal that “both” is sometimes a legitimate result rather than indecision. The next act still requires judgment. No table tells the parent how much intervention is due tonight.

Aristotle offers more practical orientation. Look for your characteristic error. Distrust the pleasure that makes a judgment easy. Correct directionally. SAE offers more explicit accounting. State the criterion, identify overlap, and record what remains outside it. One helps the agent move; the other helps the agent see what the movement claims.

The Irreducible Last Inch

Ethical systems often promise to eliminate the final inch of judgment. A rule, score, checklist, or optimization function will settle the case if enough inputs are provided. Aristotle's refusal is not anti-rational. It is a claim about the object. Conduct varies with particulars, and a theory should seek the precision its material permits.

The same refusal should discipline SAE. An audit can become another fantasy of exactness if the completion of the form is treated as completion of the case. Each entry is itself a judgment: which address matters, what counts as evidence, whether an overlap is genuine, who has standing to object. The instrument does not stand outside the activity it organizes.

A physician can describe risk ranges and still face a patient whose case falls between established categories. A manager can articulate every tradeoff and still decide whether to make an exception. A friend can understand both sides and still have to speak—or remain silent—at a particular moment.

There is no disgrace in this remainder. Practical wisdom exists because action cannot be reduced to demonstration. The mistake is to hide judgment behind a number or to praise vagueness as wisdom. Aristotle's heuristics are deliberately rough but directional. SAE's form is explicit but non-commanding.

The mean is not a point already marked on a ruler. It is the fitting response perceived by a formed agent in a particular situation. We can correct our bias, expose the branches, and make the account answerable. Then the last step remains what it was before the tools arrived: a judgment for which someone must take responsibility.
