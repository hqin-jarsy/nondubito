Great Lives

一百零八种人生

108 Lives and an Unfinished Kingdom of Ends

一座尚未完成的目的王国

A philosophical narrative told through particular lives: how people become themselves, build worlds, meet the limits of what they have built, and leave room—or fail to leave room—for another.

这是一部由具体人生写成的思想叙事：人怎样成为自己，怎样构成一个世界，怎样遭遇自己所构之物的边界，又怎样为他者留下位置——或者没有留下。

What this series is

Not a hall of fame. Not 108 final verdicts.

不是名人录，也不是一百零八份盖棺论定。

These essays ask how a person becomes their own within an inherited world, what kind of world they build, and whether that world leaves room for someone who does not fit its definitions.

As the series unfolds, a bridge comes into view. Beyond it lies not Kant’s solitary ideal but a gathering: an unfinished Kingdom of Ends. The final place is left not for another famous name, but for the reader who is still choosing.

这些文章关心的是：一个人怎样从继承来的世界里成为自己，怎样用自己的工作构成一个世界，以及那个世界能否容纳不符合其定义的人。

随着系列推进，一座桥逐渐出现。桥的远处不是康德独自站立的理想，而是一群人的相遇：一座尚未完成的目的王国。最后留下的位置，不属于下一位名人，而属于仍在选择的读者。

What does “great” mean here?这里的“伟大”是什么意思？

Scale, not sainthood. Qin Shi Huang, Napoleon, and Thatcher belong beside Hypatia, Du Fu, and Levinas because achievement and danger can both make a human structure visible. Inclusion is an invitation to judge, not a declaration of innocence.

它指向尺度，而不是封圣。秦始皇、拿破仑、撒切尔与希帕蒂娅、杜甫、列维纳斯同在这里，因为成就与危险都可能把人的结构放大给我们看。写入不是赦免，而是邀请读者判断。

A short way in

Eight lives reveal the whole journey.

先读八篇，看见整段旅程。

You do not need to read 108 essays before the series has something to say. This route moves from self-direction through construction and closure to the other and the reader’s open place.

不必读完一百零八篇，系列才开始说话。这条路线从自己的方向出发，经过构与闭合，走向他者，最后把位置交还给读者。

The unfinished ending

“Around Kant, there is one empty seat.”
“康德周围，有一个空位。”

It asks for no greatness, remembrance, book, or monument—only the recurring work of treating oneself as an end and another person as an end. The seat remains empty because no living person receives it once and for all.

它不要求伟业、名声、著作或纪念碑，只要求一个人在具体时刻把自己当作目的，也把别人当作目的。这个位置始终空着，因为活着的人不会一次性获得它。
