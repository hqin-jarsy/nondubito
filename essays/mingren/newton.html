牛顿，一个人构了一个宇宙

Newton, One Man Constructed an Entire Universe

Han Qin (秦汉) · March 2026

一、瘟疫

1665年。牛顿二十二岁。瘟疫打断了剑桥的生活。

伦敦大瘟疫的阴影伸向各地。牛顿回到林肯郡伍尔斯索普的老家——他出生的那栋乡间石屋。直到1667年春，他大部分时间在这里，中间也曾短暂返回剑桥。

后来，人们把1665—1666年叫作他的“奇迹年”。不是一夜之间，而是一段格外密集的工作。这个名字，也让我们想起爱因斯坦的1905年。

在石屋里，他做了什么？

他推进级数、流数与求积的研究，把变化率和累积量之间的联系组织成新的方法。微积分并非从一片空白中出现：费马、笛卡尔、沃利斯、巴罗等人的工作已经铺路。牛顿走得极远，但走的是一条有人留下脚印的路。

他用棱镜研究光。白光展开成彩色的光带，颜色的不同与折射程度相连。后来著名的七色划分，不是自然只允许七块彼此分离的颜色。要紧的是，白光并非单一的东西被玻璃染坏了；它本来就包含不同的成分。

他也开始把地上的落体与天上的月亮放进同一个问题：拉住苹果的引力，能不能一直伸到月亮那里？成熟的万有引力理论，还要经过约二十年的推演、争论与检验。

二十二岁。瘟疫。乡下。这个画面确实惊人，却不是一个人脱离所有前人和来往者，凭空完成了全部物理学。

爱因斯坦的1905年留下四篇重要论文；牛顿的这段岁月让数学、光学与引力三条研究线迅速生长。一个人在专利局任职，一个人离开了大学的日常。两处都不是我们想象中的宏大学术舞台。

但苦难不会自动生产发现，隔离也不是天才的处方。我们看见的是：日常被打断以后，一个人仍怎样继续工作。

在本文的画面里，牛顿偏向构，爱因斯坦偏向凿。不是说一个只建设、一个只拆除。爱因斯坦也建造；牛顿的建造，也推开了旧的天地分界。

二、构

1687年，《自然哲学的数学原理》出版。Principia。一部足以改变后来人怎样提问的书。

三条运动定律。

第一条：在惯性参考系中，合外力为零时，物体保持静止或匀速直线运动。运动本身不需要一只手不断推着它。

第二条：运动的改变与所受的力相联系。用今天对恒定质量物体的写法，就是合力F = ma。

第三条：两个物体相互作用，彼此所受的力大小相等、方向相反。它们落在不同物体上，不会因此在同一个物体上自行抵消。

再加上万有引力：对两个质点，吸引力与质量的乘积成正比，与距离的平方成反比。F = Gm₁m₂/r²。把广大的天体怎样纳入计算，还需要几何、质量分布与证明。

几条原则，打开一片惊人的计算空间。

苹果落地。月亮绕地球。地球绕太阳。潮汐。开普勒所描述的行星轨道。彗星的来去。近地面抛石的弧线。它们开始进入共同的力学语言。但要精确计算潮汐、月球运动和多体相互作用，远不是四句话就能完成的事。

在忽略空气阻力、把近地重力看作恒定时，抛石的弧线是抛物线；在理想的两体引力问题中，可以推出开普勒式的轨道。假定不是羞耻。说清假定，计算才知道自己站在哪里。

从脚下到天上，一套原则。这是这个“构”最有力的地方：苹果与月亮不必再分属两套互不相干的物理学。

亚里士多德传统曾区分天上以太的运动与地上四元素的变化。此后的天文学、伽利略等人的研究已不断松动这条边界。牛顿把这些变化推进到一个新的、能够系统推演的尺度。

如果借秦始皇作对照：一个把地域纳入共同的行政尺度，一个把天上地下纳入共同的数学语言。政令靠权力施行，物理理论必须面对测量。这里相照的是统一的雄心，不是把人和物当成同一种材料。

一个王朝的年数，也不能替一套理论计寿。秦统一后的皇朝约十五年；牛顿力学的影响跨过两个世纪，直到今天仍在工作。

三、他知道缺口在哪

这个建造如此有力，牛顿却没有说自己已经解释了一切。

引力究竟怎样起作用？

地球与月亮隔着数十万公里。能够写出它们之间的关系，并不等于知道那关系的最终原因。

在1713年加入《原理》的《总释》中，他说：Hypotheses non fingo。通常译作“我不虚构假说”。他尚不能从现象中推出引力这些性质的原因。

这里有一个值得停留的区别：我可以把运动算到很准，不等于我已知道为何自然有这样的力量。

这不是禁止科学提出假设。牛顿在别处也有推测与问题，包括以太和物质的作用方式。在这段话里，他不愿把尚未得到支持的解释，当成已经获得的知识。

两百多年后，广义相对论用时空几何重新描述引力。自由落体沿弯曲时空中的测地线运动；引力场的变化仍有传播过程，引力波以光速传播。时空几何的语言，并没有让引力变化瞬间抵达远方。

用本文的语言说，牛顿在这里给未知留了位置。他没有把一个算得很好的理论，直接宣布为终极解释。

这与秦代书禁中压制异议的姿态，可以相照。但一个物理问题与一个人的发言权，不是同一种余项；两个时代的长短，也不能证明一条奖善罚恶的历史公式。

牛顿对世界的兴趣，并没有在力学门口停下。

早在《原理》之前，他就长期研究炼金术与神学：抄录书稿，做实验，核对教会史，阅读《但以理书》和《启示录》，推敲预言的年代。书桌上的这些工作与数学并行，不是物理学“做完了”以后才开始的退场。

在这些纸页里，他同样想寻找隐藏的秩序。我们可以追问这种渴望，却不能凭兴趣的种类判定一个人的病，也不能替他宣布：因为太成功，所以相信自己无所不知。

1720年，另一种秩序给了他沉重的损失：南海泡沫。交易记录的研究支持他先卖出获利、随后又高价投入的轮廓。他损失很大；常见的两万英镑数额带着估算与传闻的成分，并不意味着他从此一贫如洗。

一句广为归于他的感叹说，天体的运行可以计算，人们的疯狂却算不了。它的传承不如《原理》里的文字可靠。我们可以把它留作这个故事的回声，不把它当作账簿旁的逐字录音。

月球的轨道与市场中的判断，要求的知识并不相同。钱包的损失不反驳万有引力，也提醒我们：在一个领域取得的精确，不会自动变成对所有生活的掌握。F = ma不是关于人的全部学问。

四、另一面

能建造如此有力的理论，并不保证一个人会公平地对待别人。

牛顿与莱布尼茨各自发展微积分。牛顿的早期工作在先，莱布尼茨先发表，并带来了影响深远的符号与方法。后来，优先权之争越过了数学，变成声望、抄袭指控与学术权力的争夺。

1712年，皇家学会成立委员会审查争议。会长牛顿深度介入材料选择和报告写作，报告维护他的优先权，又不公正地暗示莱布尼茨抄袭。他后来还匿名评述这份报告。一个当事人借机构的声音为自己裁决，不能因他确实伟大就变得无关紧要。

他与胡克在光学和引力问题上的冲突，也留下了伤痕。后人甚至把胡克画像的失踪写成牛顿毁像的故事。可是，画像是否曾在皇家学会悬挂，证据本身就不确定。两人的争执是真的，毁像不能因此成为事实。

另一张桌子在皇家造币厂。他1696年任督办，1699年转任厂长；亲自访谈嫌犯和告密者，整理证词，追诉伪币。调查并非纸上的数学游戏，它进入当时严酷的刑罚制度，有被告最终被处死。

石屋、论文、委员会、铸币和审讯。不是一种才能自动照亮一切，也不是一种过错把一切才能烧掉。

哥德尔的不完备定理讨论特定形式体系，不能证明一个人格“一致但不完备”。评价牛顿如何使用权力，需要书信、行动与受影响的人，不需要借数学为伦理判断盖章。

华盛顿的交权不能抹去他奴役人的事实；爱因斯坦对量子理论完备性的异议，也不能与奴役、学术权力的不公并列成同一种道德过错。各自的问题必须各自看清。伟大的工作不是无罪证明，批评一个人也不取消他作为人的位置。

五、站在巨人的肩膀上

1676年，牛顿写信给胡克：“如果我看得更远，那是因为我站在巨人的肩膀上。”

信中也肯定了笛卡尔与胡克的工作。这句话后来被读成谦逊，也被猜测为讥讽。我们不必把无法坐实的内心戏，押在胡克的身材上。先看它承认的关系。

他的构，不是从零开始。

开普勒描述行星运动的规律。伽利略推进落体与运动研究。笛卡尔使代数与几何发生新的联系。巴罗、沃利斯，以及提供观测与问题的其他人，也在路上。哈雷的鼓励和对出版的支持，使《原理》走出书桌。

牛顿继承这些工作，也改变它们。他不是把碎片原样装进盒子，而是让一些原先分开的结果能够互相推出、互相检验。

把孔子与荷马放在旁边，可以看见一个有限的共同处：传承并不排斥创造。孔子所说的“述”，荷马传统的口传与成篇，都不能简单等同于一个人的编书工程；它们也不是牛顿力学的早期版本。

然而，新东西往往是在已经有人生活、提问、歌唱的地方长出来。说出前人的名字，不会使自己的工作变小。

“述”不是只能重复。接过来的东西，经过另一双手，可以获得新的关系与力量。

六、两百年

牛顿的构维持了多久？

从1687年《原理》到1905年狭义相对论，是两百一十八年。再到1915年的广义相对论，又过十年。两个日期，改动的是不同层面的时空与引力问题。

在漫长岁月中，牛顿力学深刻改变了工程和天文学。人们用它及其后续发展造桥、造船、造火车，计算行星与彗星。热、电、磁、光的研究也在生长，物理学从未只有一本《原理》。

困难也没有等爱因斯坦才出现。十九世纪，水星近日点的异常进动已经成为问题。一个理论取得巨大成功，并不意味着两百年里没有任何不合。

然后，爱因斯坦改变了提问的尺度。

狭义相对论重写高速运动中的时空关系；广义相对论重写引力。在速度远小于光速、引力场较弱等适当条件下，牛顿式描述可以作为很好的近似重新得到。

近似不是随便。它有精度，也有边界。造一座桥时足够好的公式，在精密天体测量中未必足够；即使是月球轨道，高精度计算也不能一概抹掉相对论修正。

因此，“被包含”是一种有条件的说法：新理论解释旧理论何以在一大片范围内如此有效，也解释它何时不够。旧理论的绝对时空并没有原封不动地搬进新理论。

量子理论还改变了微观世界的描述。经典力学没有因此退出课堂、工厂与发射场；它的继续有用，也不等于它就是所有尺度的最后答案。

这种关系不能随意套到本系列每个人身上。苏格拉底的空地不是别人的低速极限，孔子的仁不是某个公式的近似，康德的物自体也不是一种测量误差。不同的思想，需要不同的比较尺度。

牛顿的理论有适用范围，是物理学经由推导与实验得到的判断，不是哥德尔定理再一次在历史中应验。这里的“余项”，只是提醒我们把眼睛留给尚不能解释的现象。

七、他和这个系列的每个人

再把这些人放在一起。这是本系列的阅读图像，不是给他们各下一句判词。

秦始皇扩大制度的统一，也以书禁与刑杀压缩异议；皇朝短促，制度遗产却继续变化。华盛顿两次交权，也没有让庄园里所有人获得自由。亚历山大的统一王权散开，文化联系没有跟着归零。

康德写三大批判，也追问理性的边界。佛陀教人用筏渡河、不执著于筏，不是要毁掉一切教导。爱因斯坦让美与秩序引路，也面对理论与世界的距离。荷马传统让歌声跨过代际，进入文字。

牛顿则把天上地下的许多运动放进共同的数学关系。后来的理论改变这张图的边界，没有把图上所有道路擦掉。

今天的火箭与桥梁仍离不开经典力学及其后续发展。它不是本系列中唯一可能被继承的工作，却让“继承而改变”变得格外具体：旧公式仍在纸上，仍能让计算落到事物上。

这不是没有缺口的构，是经得起反复使用、也必须接受再检验的构。可靠，不等于包办一切。

我愿意把它想成一块很结实的砖。不是宣布牛顿赢过所有人，而是想到那么多后来者，仍能把重量交给它。

在本系列想象的桥头，有凿的人，有构的人，有被生活凿过的人，也有离去的人。牛顿不是整座桥。但少了他的那块砖，桥确实会缺去很大一截。

八、苹果

最后，还是回到苹果。

斯图克利在1752年写成的回忆录里，追记了1726年与牛顿的一次谈话。两人在花园喝茶，牛顿讲起自己年轻时看到苹果落下，由此思考引力的往事。

在这段回忆中，苹果为什么朝地心落去，而不向旁边或上方飞？若物质相吸，苹果也在吸引地球。问题从树下伸出去，逐渐伸向月亮与天体。

它不是苹果砸中头部的记录，也不是可以复原全部发现过程的即时笔记。但一个人晚年怎样讲述自己的起点，同样值得听。

一个苹果落地。一个人没有让“本来如此”成为最后的回答。

本文把这一问叫作凿：让熟悉的事重新变得陌生。随后不是一句灵感包办天下，而是二十年前后漫长的计算、交谈、修改与建造。

问：苹果为什么落地？答：试着用万有引力把它与月亮连起来。F = Gm₁m₂/r²。答案让问题走得更远，也留下新的问题。

于是，我们把最后一个画面留在伍尔斯索普：一个二十出头的年轻人，一栋石屋，一座花园，一个落下的苹果。这是沿着晚年回忆展开的文学镜头，不是替缺失的日记补写现场。

后来，他构起一个可以计算的天地。不是全部宇宙。却足以让树下的人，重新看见天上的月亮。

注释

牛顿生于英国旧历1642年12月25日，即公历1643年1月4日，1665年夏约二十二岁。1665—1667年离校期间曾短返剑桥。“奇迹年”是对早期密集成果的后称，不表示《原理》的成熟理论已在乡间完成。微积分有长期前史；光谱连续，七色是分区名称，不是七种离散的自然成分。

三定律据《原理》1687年“公理或运动定律”。F = ma是恒定质量、惯性系中的现代常用形式；引力平方反比式按质点书写，推广到球状或不规则物体需处理质量分布。轨道、潮汐和抛体的具体求解包含理想化及后续发展，本文不把它们简化成四条口号的全部成果。

“Hypotheses non fingo”见1713年第二版新增《总释》；所链接的是1729年英译。它针对尚未由现象推出的引力原因，不是牛顿从来不提出假说的宣言。广义相对论用时空几何描述引力，变化并非瞬时远距作用；引力波以光速传播。

炼金、圣经研究与数学自然哲学长期并行。其宗教解释与当时主流教义也有差异，不能概括为晚年失去科学能力。本文不从兴趣、争执或信仰推断心理疾病。南海交易依据档案研究；常见的两万英镑及“人们的疯狂”名言不能与原始账目或牛顿亲笔文字等量齐观，也不作单一现代币值换算。

牛顿早有流数研究，莱布尼茨独立发展并先发表微积分。1712年皇家学会调查与其后报告、牛顿匿名评论须分清；牛顿作为会长和当事人的介入是实质性问题。胡克画像的存在、归属与下落证据不充分，不能据此指控毁像。造币厂材料支持亲自访谈、收集证词和追诉，不据戏剧化传记添写换装潜入情节。

巨人肩膀一语见1676年致胡克书信，信中同时承认笛卡尔和胡克的贡献；将它断为嘲讽胡克身材，证据不足。孔子、荷马的对照是本文对传承与创造的阅读，不主张三者工作具有同一种历史机制，也不把学术前人压成只递来一块材料的人。

水星近日点异常在十九世纪已经被辨认。狭义相对论1905年、广义相对论1915年的地位不同；经典近似涉及低速、弱场等条件及所需精度，微观现象还涉及量子理论。哥德尔定理不能证明一个人的品格、物理理论的经验局限或文明的寿命。non dubito指不动摇于主体是目的而不只是手段，不等于不批评其行为。

I. The Plague

1665. Newton was twenty-two. Plague interrupted life at Cambridge.

The Great Plague of London cast its shadow across the country. Newton returned to Woolsthorpe in Lincolnshire, to the stone farmhouse where he was born. He spent much of the period before spring 1667 there, with a brief return to Cambridge in between.

The years 1665–1666 would later be called his miraculous years. Not one night of revelation, but a period of extraordinarily concentrated work. The name also recalls Einstein’s 1905.

What happened in that stone house?

Newton developed his work on series, fluxions and quadrature, bringing rates of change and accumulated quantities into a new relationship. Calculus did not appear out of empty space. Fermat, Descartes, Wallis, Barrow and others had prepared paths. Newton traveled very far along ground that already held footprints.

He investigated light with prisms. White light opened into a colored band, different colors associated with different degrees of refraction. The famous division into seven colors did not mean nature allowed only seven separate pieces. The important point was that glass had not spoiled a simple, pure whiteness: white light already contained different components.

He also began bringing falling bodies and the Moon into one question. Could the gravity drawing an apple down reach all the way to the Moon? A mature theory of universal gravitation still lay about twenty years of reasoning, dispute and testing ahead.

Twenty-two. Plague. The countryside. An astonishing picture, but not a person cut off from every predecessor and correspondent, producing the whole of physics from nothing.

Einstein’s 1905 yielded four important papers; Newton’s period saw mathematics, optics and gravity grow rapidly together. One worked in a patent office, the other away from university routines. Neither setting looks like the grand academic stage we might expect.

Suffering does not automatically produce discovery, and isolation is no prescription for genius. What we see is someone continuing to work after ordinary life has been interrupted.

In this essay’s picture, Newton leans toward building, Einstein toward carving. Not that one only constructed and the other only demolished. Einstein built too. Newton’s construction also pushed aside an old division between heaven and earth.

II. Construction

In 1687 the Mathematical Principles of Natural Philosophy appeared. The Principia. A book that changed the questions later people could ask.

Three laws of motion.

First: in an inertial frame, when the net external force vanishes, a body remains at rest or moves uniformly in a straight line. Motion does not need a hand continually pushing it forward.

Second: change of motion is related to applied force. In today’s familiar formulation for constant mass, the net force is F = ma.

Third: two interacting bodies exert forces of equal magnitude and opposite direction on each other. Those forces act on different bodies; they do not cancel themselves on a single one.

Add universal gravitation: for two point masses, attraction is proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of their separation. F = Gm₁m₂/r². Bringing extended celestial bodies into the calculation also requires geometry, mass distributions and proof.

A few principles opened an astonishing field of calculation.

Falling apples. The Moon around Earth. Earth around the Sun. Tides. Kepler’s planetary orbits. Comets coming and going. The arc of a stone thrown near the ground. They entered a common mechanical language, though calculating tides, lunar motion and many-body interactions precisely requires far more than four sentences.

Neglect air resistance and treat near-Earth gravity as constant: the stone follows a parabola. In an ideal two-body gravitational problem, Keplerian orbits can be derived. Assumptions are not disgraceful. State them, and a calculation knows where it stands.

From underfoot to overhead, one set of principles. Here lies this construction’s force: the apple and the Moon need not belong to unrelated kinds of physics.

The Aristotelian tradition distinguished the motions of celestial aether from the changes of four terrestrial elements. Subsequent astronomy, Galileo and others had already loosened that boundary. Newton carried the transformation into a new, systematically demonstrable framework.

Set Qin Shi Huang beside him for a moment. One brought territories under shared administrative standards; the other brought heaven and earth into a common mathematical language. Decrees are enforced through power. Physical theories must face measurement. The comparison concerns an ambition to unify, not the treatment of people and objects as equivalent material.

Nor can a dynasty’s lifespan measure a theory’s life. Qin’s unified dynasty lasted about fifteen years. Newtonian mechanics crossed two centuries and is still at work.

III. He Knew Where the Gap Was

The construction was powerful. Newton did not therefore claim to have explained everything.

How, ultimately, does gravity act?

Earth and Moon are separated by hundreds of thousands of kilometers. Writing their relationship is not the same as knowing its ultimate cause.

In the General Scholium added to the Principia in 1713, Newton wrote: Hypotheses non fingo. “I frame no hypotheses.” He had not derived the cause of gravity’s properties from phenomena.

Pause over the distinction. I can calculate motion with great accuracy. That does not mean I already know why nature possesses such a power.

This was no prohibition on scientific conjecture. Elsewhere Newton offered hypotheses and questions about aether and the action of matter. Here he refused to pass an unsupported explanation off as knowledge already secured.

More than two centuries later, general relativity described gravity through spacetime geometry. Freely falling bodies follow geodesics in curved spacetime. Changes in the gravitational field still propagate; gravitational waves travel at the speed of light. Describing gravity geometrically does not make its changes instantaneous.

In this essay’s language, Newton left a place for what was unknown. A theory that calculates well need not be proclaimed the final explanation.

We can place that posture beside the suppression of dissent through Qin’s book prohibitions. But an unanswered physical question and a person’s right to speak are not the same kind of remainder. Two different historical durations prove no law rewarding virtue and punishing vice.

Newton’s interests did not stop at the door of mechanics.

Long before the Principia he was studying alchemy and theology: copying manuscripts, conducting experiments, examining church history, reading Daniel and Revelation, interpreting prophetic periods. These labors shared his desk with mathematics. They were not a retirement project begun after physics was finished.

He sought hidden order in these pages too. We can question that desire without diagnosing a person from the subjects he studied, or declaring that success had persuaded him he knew everything.

In 1720 another kind of order cost him dearly: the South Sea Bubble. Archival research supports the outline of profitable early sales followed by renewed investment at high prices. His losses were substantial. The familiar £20,000 figure includes elements of estimate and transmitted story; it does not mean he ended up penniless.

A saying widely attributed to him contrasts calculating heavenly motions with being unable to calculate people’s madness. Its transmission is less secure than the text of the Principia. It can remain an echo of this story without becoming a verbatim recording beside the accounts.

The Moon’s orbit and a judgment in a market call for different knowledge. A loss of money does not refute universal gravitation. It does remind us that precision gained in one field is not automatic mastery of every part of life. F = ma is not the whole study of human beings.

IV. The Other Side

The ability to build a powerful theory does not guarantee fairness toward other people.

Newton and Leibniz developed calculus independently. Newton’s early work came first; Leibniz published first and introduced enormously influential notation and methods. Their later priority dispute moved beyond mathematics into reputation, accusations of plagiarism and institutional power.

In 1712 the Royal Society appointed a committee to examine the dispute. Newton, its president, was deeply involved in selecting material and composing the report. It supported his priority while unfairly implying that Leibniz had plagiarized. Newton later anonymously reviewed the report as well. A party to a dispute speaking his own verdict through an institution does not become a trivial matter because that party is great.

His conflicts with Hooke over optics and gravity also left scars. Later stories even made the absence of Hooke’s portrait into an act of destruction by Newton. Yet the evidence that such a portrait ever hung at the Royal Society is itself uncertain. Their quarrels are real; destruction of a portrait does not follow from them.

Another desk stood at the Royal Mint. Newton became Warden in 1696 and Master in 1699. He interviewed suspects and informers, assembled testimony and prosecuted counterfeiting. This was no mathematical game on paper. His investigations entered a harsh penal system, and some defendants were executed.

The farmhouse, papers, committee, coinage and interrogations. One talent does not illuminate everything; one wrongdoing does not burn every achievement away.

Gödel’s incompleteness theorems concern particular formal systems. They cannot establish that a personality is consistent but incomplete. To judge Newton’s use of power we need letters, actions and the people affected, not a mathematical stamp on an ethical judgment.

Washington’s relinquishment of office does not erase his enslavement of people. Einstein’s objection to quantum theory’s completeness cannot be placed beside slavery and institutional injustice as the same sort of moral failing. Each question needs its own scrutiny. Great work is no certificate of innocence; criticizing a person does not cancel that person’s standing as a human being.

V. Standing on the Shoulders of Giants

In 1676 Newton wrote to Hooke: “If I have seen further, it is by standing on the shoulders of giants.”

The letter also praised work by Descartes and Hooke. Posterity has read the sentence as modesty, or suspected mockery. We need not stake an unverifiable inner drama on Hooke’s stature. First look at the relationship the words acknowledge.

His construction did not begin from zero.

Kepler described regularities in planetary motion. Galileo advanced the study of falling bodies and motion. Descartes brought algebra and geometry into new relations. Barrow, Wallis and others supplying observations and questions were also on the road. Halley’s encouragement and support for publication helped the Principia leave Newton’s desk.

Newton inherited these works and changed them. He did not place the fragments unchanged into a box. He made previously separate results available for mutual derivation and testing.

Placing Confucius and Homer nearby reveals a limited common ground: transmission does not exclude creation. Confucius’ transmitting, and the Homeric movement from oral performance to composed epic, cannot simply be reduced to one person editing a book. Neither is an early version of Newtonian mechanics.

Yet new things often grow where others have already lived, questioned and sung. Naming predecessors does not diminish one’s work.

To transmit is not only to repeat. What passes into another pair of hands can acquire new relations and new strength.

VI. Two Hundred Years

How long did Newton’s construction last?

From the Principia in 1687 to special relativity in 1905: 218 years. General relativity followed another decade later, in 1915. Those dates mark changes to different questions about space, time and gravity.

Across that long period, Newtonian mechanics profoundly reshaped engineering and astronomy. People used it and its later developments to build bridges, ships and trains, to calculate planets and comets. Heat, electricity, magnetism and light were also being investigated. Physics never consisted of the Principia alone.

Difficulties did not wait for Einstein to appear. The anomalous advance of Mercury’s perihelion had become a problem in the nineteenth century. A theory’s enormous success does not mean two centuries without discrepancies.

Then Einstein changed the scale of the questions.

Special relativity recast spacetime relations at high speeds; general relativity recast gravity. Under suitable conditions, including speeds much lower than light and weak gravitational fields, Newtonian descriptions reappear as excellent approximations.

Approximation is not carelessness. It has an accuracy and a boundary. A formula good enough for a bridge may not suffice for precision astronomy. Even the Moon’s orbit requires relativistic corrections at sufficiently high accuracy.

So being contained is a conditional description. The newer theory explains why the older works over so much territory and where it falls short. Newton’s absolute space and time did not move into the new theory unchanged.

Quantum theory also transformed descriptions of the microscopic world. Classical mechanics did not therefore leave classrooms, factories and launch sites. Its continuing usefulness does not make it the final answer at every scale.

We cannot apply this relationship indiscriminately throughout the series. Socrates’ clearing is not another thinker’s low-speed limit; Confucius’ humaneness is not an approximation to a formula; Kant’s thing-in-itself is not a measurement error. Different thoughts need different measures of comparison.

That Newton’s theory has a domain of validity is a conclusion drawn through physical reasoning and experiment, not Gödel’s theorem fulfilled again in history. Remainder here simply reminds us to keep looking at phenomena not yet explained.

VII. Newton and the Others

Bring these figures together once more. This is the series’ reading image, not a single-sentence verdict on each life.

Qin Shi Huang enlarged institutional uniformity and narrowed dissent through book prohibitions and executions. His dynasty was short; its institutional legacies continued to change. Washington returned power twice without giving everyone on his estate freedom. Alexander’s unified kingship broke apart; cultural connections did not reset to zero.

Kant wrote three Critiques and asked after reason’s limits. The Buddha taught people to use a raft without clinging to it, not to destroy every teaching. Einstein followed beauty and order while facing the distance between theory and world. The Homeric tradition carried voices across generations into writing.

Newton brought many motions in heaven and on earth into common mathematical relationships. Later theories changed the map’s boundaries without erasing all its roads.

Today’s rockets and bridges still depend on classical mechanics and its subsequent development. His is not the only work here that could be inherited, but it makes inheritance through change unusually concrete: the old formula remains on paper and still connects calculation to things.

This is not a construction without gaps. It withstands repeated use and must remain open to testing. Reliable does not mean all-explaining.

I like to imagine it as a particularly solid brick. Not to declare Newton the winner over everyone else, but to think of how many later people can still entrust weight to it.

At this series’ imagined bridgehead stand carvers, builders, people carved by life, and those who have departed. Newton is not the entire bridge. Without his brick, however, a substantial stretch would be missing.

VIII. The Apple

At the end, return to the apple.

In the memoir he completed in 1752, William Stukeley recalled a conversation with Newton in 1726. They drank tea in a garden. Newton told him of seeing an apple fall when he was young, and thinking about gravity.

In this recollection, why did the apple move toward Earth’s center rather than sideways or upward? If matter attracts matter, the apple also draws Earth toward itself. The question reached beyond the tree, gradually toward the Moon and the heavens.

It is no account of an apple striking his head, nor an immediate notebook entry from which the entire discovery can be reconstructed. But the way someone tells the beginning of his life’s work in old age is worth hearing too.

An apple falls. Someone refuses to let that is simply how things are become the final answer.

This essay calls the question carving: making the familiar strange again. What followed was not one flash explaining everything, but roughly twenty years of calculation, conversation, revision and construction.

Question: why does the apple fall? Answer: try connecting it to the Moon through universal gravitation. F = Gm₁m₂/r². The answer carries the question farther and leaves new questions behind.

We leave the final picture at Woolsthorpe: a man in his early twenties, a stone house, a garden, a falling apple. It is a literary scene drawn along a late recollection, not a replacement for a missing contemporary diary.

Later he built a calculable world of heaven and earth. Not the whole universe. Enough, however, for someone beneath a tree to see the Moon anew.

Notes

Newton was born on December 25, 1642 in the English Old Style calendar, January 4, 1643 Gregorian, and was twenty-two in summer 1665. His plague-period absence included a brief return to Cambridge. The miraculous years name concentrated early work, not a completed Principia. Calculus had a long prehistory; the spectrum is continuous, not seven separate natural ingredients.

The laws follow the 1687 Principia’s Axioms, or Laws of Motion. F = ma is the familiar modern constant-mass formulation in an inertial frame; the inverse-square expression here is for point masses. Extended bodies require treatment of mass distributions. Orbits, tides and projectiles involve idealizations and later developments, not four slogans that finish the calculation.

Hypotheses non fingo appeared in the General Scholium added in 1713; the linked English translation is from 1729. Its context is the cause of gravity not yet derived from phenomena, not a lifelong ban on conjecture. General relativity describes gravity geometrically without making changes instantaneous: gravitational waves propagate at light speed.

Alchemy, biblical studies and mathematical natural philosophy overlapped for decades. Newton’s religious interpretations also differed from prevailing doctrines; neither interest nor belief establishes a retrospective medical diagnosis. South Sea trading is discussed through archival research. The familiar £20,000 loss and saying about people’s madness do not have the same evidential status as original accounts or Newton’s own writing. No single modern currency equivalent is asserted.

Newton developed fluxions earlier; Leibniz independently developed and first published calculus. Distinguish the 1712 Royal Society inquiry, the subsequent publication and Newton’s anonymous review. His involvement as president and interested party matters. Evidence for Hooke’s portrait, its ownership and disappearance does not establish destruction by Newton. Mint records support interviewing, gathering evidence and prosecution, not the dramatic disguise scenes repeated in popular biography.

The shoulders-of-giants sentence comes from Newton’s 1676 letter to Hooke, which acknowledges both Descartes and Hooke. Mockery of Hooke’s stature is not established. The Confucius and Homer comparison is the essay’s reading of transmission and creation, not an assertion of identical historical mechanisms or a reduction of predecessors to passive suppliers.

Mercury’s anomalous perihelion advance was identified in the nineteenth century. Special relativity in 1905 and general relativity in 1915 have distinct roles. Classical approximations depend on such conditions as low speed, weak field and required accuracy; microscopic phenomena also involve quantum theory. Gödel’s theorems prove neither character defects, empirical limits of physics nor civilizational lifespans. Non dubito means not wavering on the subject’s standing as an end, not merely a means; conduct remains open to criticism.

The apple story follows Stukeley’s 1752 memoir recalling the conversation of April 15, 1726. It contains no apple striking a head. The universe, brick, bridge, group portrait and final farmhouse garden are the author’s interpretive images. They neither exclude predecessors and collaborators nor supply new historical evidence.
