波普尔：把方向翻过来的人

Popper: The Man Who Reversed the Direction

Han Qin (秦汉)

一、那把火钳

1946年10月25日。剑桥，国王学院。

道德科学俱乐部在布雷斯韦特的房间里开会，波普尔来作报告，维特根斯坦在场。他们争论：哲学有没有真正的问题，还是那些问题应当通过澄清语言来消解。后来，这次会面被一根火钳带进了哲学史。

可是，火钳在谁说哪句话时被拿起、放下，对方走出房间之前还是之后响起那句反击，不同回忆并不一致。同期会议记录也没有替后来的所有细节签字。不能因为一个故事好讲，就让最生动的版本自动成为最可靠的版本。

我们先把火钳放回壁炉边。

声音大，不是论证；声音小，也不是。让别人难堪不等于指出了错误。一次哲学争论更不是一场已经完成的科学实验。波普尔的思想不必靠他在那个房间里“赢了”，才有机会被读懂。

他反复提出的一个问题是：你给出了一个关于经验世界的理论。那么，这个理论不允许什么事情发生？什么样的可能结果，会使它遇到真正的困难？

这与“凡是不可证伪的都没有意义”不是同一句话。

一首诗未必是一项可证伪的假说。一项数学证明也不是靠等候明天的实验来成立。它们没有因此变成废话。波普尔要讨论的，是经验科学怎样承担出错的风险，而不是替所有人类言说发放存在许可证。

如果连这两件事都不分开，我们拿走了维特根斯坦手里的火钳，却把它放进了读者手里。

二、维也纳，那个他不属于的圈子

波普尔1902年出生在维也纳。后来，他与维也纳学圈相接近，和费格尔、卡尔纳普等人有实际讨论，却不是学圈成员。他不是站在一个从未进去过的世界外面骂它；他和那个世界共用不少问题，又拒绝接受若干答案。

学圈本身也不是一句口号。把它写成所有成员始终相信“不能被有限观察完全验证的便无意义”，会先把逻辑和数学放错地方，也会抹去内部修订。卡尔纳普1936年的《可检验性与意义》，已经明确区分最终验证与不同程度的确证。

所有天鹅都是白的。

见过一千只白天鹅，并没有看过所有地方、所有时间的天鹅。再看一千只，也不能仅凭这个数目让“所有”自动封口。一个可靠的反例，在逻辑上却足以与这项全称断言相冲突。

于是黑天鹅游了进来。

但它进入实验记录，比进入逻辑例句麻烦。那真的是天鹅吗？颜色判断可靠吗？照片有没有问题？别人能否重新检查？波普尔自己的方法论并没有要求科学家见到一张异常照片，就立即烧掉理论。他区分可证伪性与实际证伪，并要求对基本陈述、可重复的效应与检验条件作出可批评的判断。

逻辑上的不对称，不能替实践提供一双绝不看错的眼睛。

还有第三件事：一个理论没有被推翻，不等于它已经受过严厉检验。也许没有人认真试过。没有撞过墙，不等于已经走通了路。

可证伪、已被证伪、尚未被证伪。三个说法，不能挤成一个。

《研究的逻辑》德文版于1934年秋问世，扉页标着1935年；1959年，英文版《科学发现的逻辑》出版。这些问题由此走向了更多读者。

三、猜想在前

如果把观察想成一个桶，科学便像向桶里不停倒入事实。桶满了，规律自然浮出来。

波普尔不接受这个画面。看什么、测什么、把什么当作意外，都已经涉及问题与期待。即使拥有阿莱夫，仿佛一眼看见一切，也没有因此决定应当追问什么。

上一篇的孔德也承认，理论与观察相互需要。波普尔把注意力转向了这段关系里的另一个动作：不只再找一些支持，还要安排一种情形，让自己的答案有可能失败。

猜想可以先于这一次检验。它不必因此是康德意义上的先验知识。猜想可能带着旧实验、学来的数学、一个长期解不开的问题。时间上先提出，不等于在根据上独立于经验。

方向盘回到提出问题的人手里，不等于路面从此听他指挥。

他提出一个答案。世界可能不照这个答案运行。于是需要一项检验，让这两者的分歧有机会显露出来。

“大胆”也不等于随便说错话。一个理论越明确地排除某些可能结果，便越可能承担具体的检验风险；这不是鼓励挑选明知错误的答案，更不是把“尚未失败”当作“没有内容”。真正经过困难检验而暂时站住的理论，恰恰不能与从未碰过现实的说法混为一谈。

波普尔后来回忆1919年日食观测时，看重爱因斯坦理论所承担的风险。但问题并不只是光弯不弯，还涉及偏折量能否与理论预期相符。一次观测有仪器、误差和数据处理，不能被压成“没有看见弯曲，整套理论当场死亡”的按钮。这里首先是波普尔回望这件事的哲学读法，不是他亲赴观测现场的记录。

他也拿弗洛伊德、阿德勒的若干解释，以及某些马克思主义者对预测的重新解释作对照：在他的批评中，危险是任何结果都能被事后吸收，以至理论不再冒险。这是他的论证对象与判断，不是本篇对全部心理学、临床实践或所有马克思主义文本的总判决。

补充假设也不天然有罪。换掉一个坏仪器、修正一个可独立检验的条件，与专为躲开反例而添加保护层，不是同一回事。波普尔已经讨论过怎样区分这些做法。

世界能回话，重要。我们怎样判断自己听到了什么，同样重要。

四、他画的那条线

线这边是经验科学。线那边，有别的工作。

首先要保住这句话里的“别的”。

波普尔的划界标准不是意义标准。一个形而上学设想可以提出问题、启发研究，却尚未成为可检验的科学理论。伦理判断可以要求理由，而不必伪装成测量结果。批评它们，不等于把它们扔进实验室；不把它们扔进实验室，也不等于放弃批评。

他晚年的“世界三”更不是一张只收录实验命题的清单。其中有故事、音乐、艺术作品，也有数学问题和科学理论。这些是人创造出来、又能产生未被创造者预先想到的后果与问题的内容；不是在人类出现之前就完成登记的一间永恒仓库。

因此，不能说屈原只要带着诗走来，就会在波普尔门口被拒绝。诗本来就在他承认的范围里。要争论的，是他怎样理解这些不同的工作，而不是假称他不知道它们存在。

他也修改过自己的判断。1978年发表的《自然选择与心灵的涌现》，重新讨论了他此前对自然选择理论可检验性及逻辑地位的看法。修正不只是他要求别人做的事。

真正值得警惕的，是另一种用法。

假如有人把“这不是经验科学”接成“所以没有人需要认真听”，线就变成了墙。一种工作有它的边界，和边界之外不再有值得做的工作，相隔很远。

上一篇用了水泥。这一篇仍可用环氧树脂：一层很薄、很精密的材料，也能把出口封住。

可是，树脂不是可证伪性本身。

它是有人用一项工作的准入条件，去注销其他工作的那一刻。

五、他自己的缝隙

一个猜想已经写在纸上。我们可以问怎样检验。

在它还没有被写出来的时候呢？

《科学发现的逻辑》把思想的发生过程与提出之后的逻辑检验区分开。这个范围选择并不自动构成破绽：一本研究桥梁承重的书，没有包办人为什么想去对岸，并不因此算错了重量。

但另一本书仍然可以打开。科学家怎样注意到别人略过的地方？怎样学会提出尚不存在的问题？什么样的训练、合作和生活，使某条路变得可见？

爱因斯坦回忆少年时追逐光束的思想实验，直接相关的是狭义相对论的问题史，不宜挪来充当广义相对论整套方程的单一出生证明。即使是思想实验，也在同已知物理的张力中工作，不是脑中凭空亮了一下便取消了此前的学习。

麦克林托克的诺贝尔演讲里，有种植、授粉、染色体研究和意外的实验结果。她谈到的1944年工作，背后已有多年经验。凯勒1983年的传记以“对有机体的感觉”为题；读到“感觉”二字时，也应当看见她在田间和实验室里花过的时间。

熟练的感觉，可能正长在长期观察里。

猜想的来路，不能代替接受它的理由。可它不因此成了一件不值得研究的事。

库恩将注意力放到科学家在共同框架里解题的工作上。波普尔的回应并不是否认这种工作存在；他承认“常规科学”的现实，争论它应当在怎样的程度上成为规范。不能把两人的分歧压成一个人说“见反例立刻推翻”，另一个说“反常积到固定刻度自动换范式”。

费耶阿本德则沿着科学史，质问那些声称没有例外的方法规则。“怎么都行”是论争中挑衅性的表达，不是说任何断言都同样真实。真正的问题是，规则面对自己没有预料到的发现时，是否还有调整的余地。

这些问题并未使检验多余。它们使检验之前、之中、之后的人重新可见。

余项，是现有说法没有说尽的部分。它留下继续发问的位置，不替任何人按下继续键。动力仍在人的判断与选择里。

六、他和孔德

如果孔德只是一袋水泥，波普尔只是一桶树脂，这个系列已经犯了它批评的错误。

孔德关于理论、观察与相对知识的文字，可以纠正我们。波普尔关于艺术、形而上学和自我修正的文字，同样可以。把他们写进一个系列，不是把他们的反对意见留在门外。

标题里的“翻转”因此需要范围。它不是从绝对被动走向绝对主动，也不是让猜想从此不欠经验任何解释。它是转换检验的朝向：不只搜集站在自己一边的材料，还设计可能使自己的答案站不住的局面。

波普尔的工作也不只在科学划界里。1945年的《开放社会及其敌人》讨论制度如何容纳批评、限制掌权者可能造成的损害，以及政府能否不用流血便被更换。他对柏拉图等人的解释长期有争议；制度问题的提出，与每一项思想史解释是否成立，须分别检查。

在这里，提问的尺度已经改变：不只是纸上的命题会不会错，还有一个安排怎样面对受到它影响的人。

Self-as-an-End的追问从这里另开：谁在发问？谁可以回答？一个人的问题，是否因为暂时不能被压成所许可的命题，就连说完的机会也没有？这不是宣布波普尔从未考虑主体，而是本系列自己要承担的方向。

诗不必先变成一条物理定律，才值得阅读。声称诗里有一条物理定律的人，却仍须为这项声称负责。

人与他的命题，也必须分开。

命题可以失败。一个人不因一句话被反驳，就失去作为人的位置。容许纠错，和把他人当作可以淘汰的错误，不是一回事。

七、他在这一组中的位置

这一组要安排的是不同的相遇，不是一张最后只剩作者获胜的淘汰表。

下一篇，狄拉克将带着方程来。我们可以问数学的形式怎样引出尚待检验的可能，但不能让方程漂亮成为免于现实检验的理由。

屈原会带着诗来。需要问的是诗怎样使一个人的处境可见，而不是怎样把诗加工成一份实验报告。

费希特会把提问的人带进问题里。麦克林托克会带着熟练的眼睛、手和玉米走来。他们要做的，不只是替已有的问题提交另一份答案，也可能是让原来的问题变得陌生。

波兰尼所说的默会认识，会进一步追问：我们怎样认出一张脸，怎样掌握一种手艺，却不能把全部要领逐项写出。他讨论的是实际认识的结构，不是在颁发“说不清便一定更深刻”的证书。

这些来客不需要共同反对一个人，才有各自的工作。

波普尔留下的那个问题仍可跟着他们：你提出的主张，愿意接受怎样的质问？这一组也须把问题交还自己：我们以什么理由要求对方用这种方式回答？

不是三个人排成通往终点的楼梯。

是走到一处，发现还有不同的门。

八、桥头

波普尔走过来的时候，步子很快。桥头没有壁炉，也没有火钳。

孔德还在那里，摊着他的日历。

波普尔看了一会儿，问：“假如后来再来一个弗洛伊德，你的日历会不会重排？”

孔德的手留在纸边，没有回答。

休谟在旁边笑了一下。康德站得稍远，听着。

波普尔找了一个位置，站在孔德旁边，面朝不同的方向。他问桥上的来客：“你们的说法，可能在哪里遇到困难？”

这是一个可以带上桥的问题。它不因此成了上桥的全部条件。

远处传来声音。像一首诗。又像有人在低声推敲一个方程。还有一个人正在说明，为什么这一株玉米与其他的不同。

波普尔听着。几种声音一起传来，他一时没有接话。每一种，都还没有说完。

风翻动孔德的日历。有人按住一页，又把下一页留在风里。

夜深了。波普尔还站着。

他的追问还在。只是此刻，答案没有赶在那个人前面。

而站在面前的那个人，不是一项等待被证伪的假说。

他知道怎样追问一个命题。

这一次，他先等那个人把话说完。

I. The Poker

October 25, 1946. King’s College, Cambridge.

The Moral Sciences Club is meeting in Richard Braithwaite's rooms. Karl Popper has come to speak; Ludwig Wittgenstein is there. Their disagreement concerns whether philosophy has genuine problems or whether its apparent problems should dissolve through the clarification of language. A fireplace poker will eventually carry this evening into histories of philosophy.

Eventually: the distance matters. Later accounts disagree about the sequence of words and gestures, when the poker was put down, and whether a celebrated reply preceded or followed Wittgenstein's departure. The contemporary minutes do not certify every detail of the versions that followed. A story does not become more reliable by becoming easier to tell.

Let us put the poker back beside the fireplace.

Loudness is not an argument. Neither is quietness. Embarrassing someone is not the same as identifying an error, and a philosophical encounter is not a completed scientific experiment. Popper's work does not need him to have “won” that evening before we may read it seriously.

One question he repeatedly asks is this: you have offered a theory about the empirical world. What does it rule out? What possible result would bring it into genuine difficulty?

That is not the same as saying that everything unfalsifiable is meaningless.

A poem need not be a falsifiable hypothesis. A mathematical proof does not acquire its validity by waiting for tomorrow's experiment. Neither thereby becomes nonsense. The question concerns how empirical science exposes itself to error, not how to issue every human utterance a licence to exist.

Without that distinction, we merely take the poker out of Wittgenstein's hand and place it in the reader's.

II. Vienna: Close to the Circle, Outside It

Popper was born in Vienna in 1902. He later engaged with members of the Vienna Circle, including Herbert Feigl and Rudolf Carnap, without becoming a member himself. This was not a man denouncing a world he had never encountered. He shared questions with it and disputed some of its answers.

The Circle was not a single sentence, either. To make every member permanently maintain that only conclusively verifiable empirical statements possess meaning would already misplace logic and mathematics. It would also erase changes within the movement. In Testability and Meaning in 1936, Carnap explicitly distinguished final verification from degrees of confirmation.

All swans are white.

A thousand white swans are not all swans in every place and time. Another thousand cannot, merely by adding to the total, close the word “all.” A sound counterexample, however, conflicts logically with that universal assertion.

Into the example swims a black swan.

Its entry into an experimental record is more troublesome. Is this really a swan? Is the colour assessment reliable? Is something wrong with the photograph? Can somebody else inspect the evidence? Popper's methodology does not require us to burn a theory upon receiving one anomalous photograph. He distinguishes falsifiability from actual falsification and discusses the acceptance of basic statements, reproducible effects, and conditions of testing.

A logical asymmetry does not supply observers with infallible eyes.

There is a third issue. A theory that has not been refuted may never have undergone a serious test. Perhaps nobody tried. Not having hit a wall is not evidence that a route has been successfully travelled.

Falsifiable. Falsified. Not yet falsified. Three different descriptions.

Logik der Forschung appeared in autumn 1934, with 1935 on its title page. The English edition, The Logic of Scientific Discovery, followed in 1959, carrying these questions to a wider readership.

III. The Conjecture Comes First

Imagine observation as a bucket. Keep pouring facts into it, and eventually a law will rise to the surface.

Popper rejects that picture. What to examine, what to measure, what to regard as unexpected: these already involve questions and expectations. Even an Aleph, a literary point from which everything seems visible at once, would not decide what deserves investigation.

Comte, whom we met in the preceding essay, also recognized that theory and observation need one another. Popper shifts attention to another move within that relationship: not merely finding more support, but arranging a situation in which one’s answer could fail.

A conjecture can precede this particular test without being a priori knowledge in Kant's sense. It may carry earlier experiments, learned mathematics, or an old unresolved problem within it. Coming first in time does not establish independence from experience in its grounds.

The person proposing a question takes the wheel. The road does not thereby take orders.

An answer is offered. The world may not behave as that answer says. A test must give the disagreement an opportunity to become visible.

Boldness is not a recommendation to make mistakes casually. By ruling out definite possibilities, a theory can expose itself to demanding tests. This does not make a knowingly false theory preferable, nor does it make a surviving theory empty. An account that has stood up to difficult attempts at refutation is not interchangeable with one that has never risked a collision with experience.

In his later account of the 1919 eclipse observations, Popper emphasized the risk borne by Einstein's theory. The issue concerned the predicted deflection, not simply whether light bent at all. Instruments, uncertainties, and the assessment of observations intervene between a theoretical expectation and an experimental judgment. There is no button labelled “No bending seen: entire theory dead.” Moreover, we are reading Popper's retrospective philosophical example, not his report from an observing expedition.

He contrasted it with certain explanations associated with Freud and Adler and with some Marxist reinterpretations of predictions. In his criticism, the danger was that any outcome could be accommodated after the event, leaving nothing at stake for the theory. Those are his selected targets and judgments, not this essay's verdict on every psychological theory, clinical practice, or Marxist text.

An auxiliary hypothesis is not guilty simply because it is auxiliary. Replacing a defective instrument or revising an independently testable assumption differs from adding a shield whose only task is to prevent an unwelcome result from counting. Popper himself discusses such distinctions.

The world's ability to answer matters. So does our judgment about what we have heard.

IV. The Line He Drew

On one side, empirical science. On the other, other kinds of work.

We must preserve the word “other.”

Popper's criterion of demarcation is not a criterion of meaning. A metaphysical idea can raise questions and stimulate research without yet constituting a testable scientific theory. Ethical judgments can demand reasons without masquerading as measurements. Criticizing them need not mean putting them in a laboratory; not putting them there need not mean abandoning criticism.

His later “World 3” is not an inventory restricted to experimental propositions. It includes stories, music, works of art, mathematical problems, and scientific theories. These are human creations capable of generating consequences and problems their makers did not foresee. World 3 is not a timeless warehouse whose contents were registered before human beings existed.

Qu Yuan would therefore not automatically be turned away from Popper's door for arriving with a poem. Poetry already has a place within his account. We may question how that place is understood; we may not invent its absence.

He revised his own judgments too. In Natural Selection and the Emergence of Mind, published in 1978, he changed his earlier assessment of natural selection’s testability and logical status. Revision was not only something he asked of others.

A different use of the line deserves scrutiny.

Suppose someone turns “This is not empirical science” into “Therefore nobody needs to listen.” The line has become a wall. There is a long distance between giving one kind of work its limits and declaring that nothing worthwhile happens beyond them.

The previous essay used cement. This one may still use epoxy resin: a thin, finely applied layer can seal an exit too.

But the resin is not falsifiability itself.

It is the use of one activity's admission conditions to cancel other activities.

V. His Own Gaps

A conjecture is already on the page. We can ask how to test it.

What about the time before it reached the page?

The Logic of Scientific Discovery distinguishes the occurrence of a thought from the logical examination of a proposed claim. That choice of scope is not automatically a defect. A book about a bridge's load-bearing capacity does not miscalculate a weight merely because it has not explained why anybody wants to reach the opposite bank.

Another book can nevertheless open. How does a scientist notice what others pass over? How does an unfamiliar question become possible? What training, collaboration, and experience make a previously invisible route available?

Einstein's recollection of pursuing a light beam belongs directly to the problem history of special relativity, not to a one-scene birth certificate for the whole of general relativity. Even a thought experiment works amid tensions in known physics. A flash of imagination does not erase the learning that made the flash intelligible.

McClintock’s Nobel lecture is full of planting, pollination, chromosome studies and unexpected results. Years of experience lay behind the work she recalled from 1944. Keller’s 1983 biography was called A Feeling for the Organism; that feeling carries time spent in the field and the laboratory.

A practised feeling may grow within sustained observation.

The origin of a conjecture cannot replace the reasons for accepting it. Neither does it become unworthy of inquiry for that reason.

Kuhn drew attention to scientists solving problems within a shared framework. Popper's response was not to deny that such activity exists. He acknowledged “normal science” and disputed its normative role. Their disagreement is ill served by a cartoon in which one says “A counterexample destroys the theory immediately” and the other “Anomalies reach a fixed threshold and automatically replace the paradigm.”

Feyerabend used the history of science to challenge supposedly exceptionless rules. “Anything goes” was a provocation, not a claim that every assertion is equally true. The question is whether a rule can make room for a discovery it did not anticipate.

None of this makes testing unnecessary. It makes the people before, within, and after a test visible again.

A remainder is what an existing account has not exhausted. It leaves room for another question; it does not press the continuation button for us. Judgment and choice remain with the person.

VI. Popper and Comte

If Comte becomes nothing but a sack of cement and Popper a can of resin, this series has performed the reduction it criticizes.

Comte's words about theory, observation, and relative knowledge can correct us. So can Popper's words about art, metaphysics, and revision. Including these writers in a series does not entitle us to leave their objections outside it.

The reversal in the title therefore has a scope. It is not a move from absolute passivity to absolute initiative, nor a release of conjectures from accountability to experience. It changes the direction of testing: do not merely collect material on your side; devise situations in which your answer could lose its footing.

Popper also worked beyond scientific demarcation. The Open Society and Its Enemies, published in 1945, discusses institutional criticism, constraints on harm by rulers, and the possibility of replacing governments without bloodshed. His readings of Plato and other thinkers have remained disputed. Asking an institutional question and establishing each interpretation of an earlier thinker are separate undertakings.

Here the scale of questioning changes: not only whether a proposition can be wrong, but how an arrangement faces the people affected by it.

Self-as-an-End opens its own inquiry at this point. Who is asking? Who may answer? When a person's question cannot yet be compressed into an approved proposition, do they lose even the opportunity to finish speaking? This is a direction the series takes responsibility for, not a claim that Popper never considered subjectivity.

A poem does not have to become a physical law before it deserves a reader. Someone claiming that the poem contains a physical law must still answer for that claim.

A person and a proposition must also be distinguished.

The proposition can fail. Refuting a sentence does not remove its speaker's standing as a person. Allowing correction is not the same as treating another human being as an error to be discarded.

VII. His Place in This Group

This group arranges encounters, not an elimination contest in which the author alone remains undefeated.

Next comes Dirac with an equation. We can ask how mathematical form discloses possibilities awaiting a test, without allowing beauty to release an equation from contact with experience.

Qu Yuan will bring a poem. The question is how poetry makes someone's situation visible, not how to process it into an experimental report.

Fichte will bring the questioner into the question. McClintock will arrive with the trained eye, the hand and the maize plant. They may offer more than another answer to an existing problem. They may make the problem itself unfamiliar.

Polanyi's tacit knowing asks how we recognize a face or acquire a skill without being able to list every component of what we know. It investigates the structure of actual knowing; it does not confer a certificate of profundity on whatever cannot be clearly explained.

These visitors need not oppose the same man to have work of their own.

Popper's question can travel with them: what kinds of challenge will your claims accept? The series must return the question to itself: on what grounds do we demand that someone answer in this particular form?

Not three men arranged as a staircase to the finish.

A place reached, and more than one door discovered.

VIII. At the Bridge

Popper approaches quickly. There is no fireplace here, and no poker.

Comte still has his calendar spread before him.

Popper looks at it for a while. “Suppose a Freud arrives later. Could your calendar be rearranged?”

Comte's hand stays beside the paper. He does not answer.

Hume gives a small laugh. Kant stands a little farther away, listening.

Popper finds a place beside Comte, facing a different direction. He asks the visitors, “Where might your accounts encounter difficulty?”

That is a question one can bring to the bridge. It does not become the complete set of conditions for stepping onto it.

A sound comes from a distance. Something like a poem. Someone else seems to be working quietly through an equation. Another person is explaining what distinguishes this maize plant from the others.

Popper listens. Several voices reach him together. For a moment he says nothing. Each still has something to finish.

Wind turns a page of Comte's calendar. A hand holds one page down and leaves the next in the wind.

Night deepens. Popper stays.

His question remains. For now, the answer has not arrived ahead of the person.

And the person before him is not a hypothesis awaiting falsification.

He knows how to question a proposition.

This time, he first waits for the person to finish speaking.
