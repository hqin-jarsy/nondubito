A School Must Leave Room to Grow

学校不只把孩子教会

學校不只把孩子教會

Timetables, care, report cards, teachers' time and school rules. Nine essays about reaching every child without deciding who that child must become.

課表、照看、成績單、老師的時間與學校的規則。九篇散文，問每個孩子能否被真正接住，也能留下自己的問號。

01

The Timetable Is Ready. Where Is the Child?

课表排好了，那个孩子呢

課表排好了，那個孩子呢

A school can organize learning for hundreds. Its harder task is to make sure no child's own questions disappear inside the arrangement.

学校很会安排许多人一起学。更难的一件，是安排之后，每个孩子仍能留下自己的问题和去处。

學校很會安排許多人一起學。更難的一件，是安排之後，每個孩子仍能留下自己的問題和去處。

02

Teach It Clearly. Leave It Open to Checking.

教得清楚，也留得下一个为什么

教得清楚，也留得下一個為什麼

Children sometimes learn before they can verify. That need does not make a textbook unanswerable or a timetable more important than learning.

知识不妨先学下来。但教材、进度和作业，不该把日后回头查的路一起收走。

知識不妨先學下來。但教材、進度和作業，不該把日後回頭查的路一起收走。

03

Someone This Child Can Turn To

每个孩子，知道可以去找谁

每個孩子，知道可以去找誰

Assigning a mentor is not the same as making guidance available. Time, continuity and a usable route matter more than a completed pairing list.

导师名单可以配齐，真正的回应却需要时间。学校能安排条件，不能验收一个人怎样长成。

導師名單可以配齊，真正的回應卻需要時間。學校能安排條件，不能驗收一個人怎樣長成。

04

Holding the Phone Is Not Holding the Person

替孩子收起手机，不是替他收起一生

替孩子收起手機，不是替他收起一生

Care sometimes requires constraint. That is a reason to explain its grounds and limits, not a reason to make the child's own voice disappear.

学校需要照看，也可能需要约束。越是不能全由孩子自己决定的地方，越要把来处、限度与复核说清。

學校需要照看，也可能需要約束。越是不能全由孩子自己決定的地方，越要把來處、限度與複核說清。

05

A Child Who Cannot Leave Still Needs a Way Through

走不开的孩子，也要有别的路

走不開的孩子，也要有別的路

Changing a class, finding another teacher, reaching someone who can act: compulsory attendance must not become compulsory silence.

换一位老师、换一个班，把一件事说到能处理的地方。孩子不能随时走开，不能变成学校不用回答的理由。

換一位老師、換一個班，把一件事說到能處理的地方。孩子不能隨時走開，不能變成學校不用回答的理由。

06

Do Not Schedule the Good Teacher Out of Existence

别把好老师排成没有时间的人

別把好老師排成沒有時間的人

If schools want teachers to attend to children, guidance, care and explanation must be work with time, not an unlimited addition to the working day.

希望老师认真对待每个孩子，就不能把指导、照看与答问都留给下班后的热心。

希望老師認真對待每個孩子，就不能把指導、照看與答問都留給下班後的熱心。

07

A Report Card Has Not Finished the Person

成绩单没有写完一个人

成績單沒有寫完一個人

A test can tell us something. Placement, recommendations and comments that turn it into a future require a further judgment.

考试可以告诉我们一些事。分班、推荐和评语拿它去决定更多事情时，需要再问一步。

考試可以告訴我們一些事。分班、推薦和評語拿它去決定更多事情時，需要再問一步。

08

The Money Has Reached the School. Has It Reached the Child?

钱进了学校，还要走到孩子身边

錢進了學校，還要走到孩子身邊

Public support protects education a child can actually receive, not the permanent existence of a name above a gate.

保障的不是一块永远不动的校牌，而是孩子今天能够得到、明天能够接上的教育。

保障的不是一塊永遠不動的校牌，而是孩子今天能夠得到、明天能夠接上的教育。

09

The Rules Can Be Finished. The Door to Questions Cannot Be Closed.

校规可以写完，问题不能就此关门

校規可以寫完，問題不能就此關門

Schools must make decisions. Those unable simply to leave need a way to reach the people and rules making them.

学校要作决定。它也要让走不开的人，能够问到真正作决定的地方。

學校要作決定。它也要讓走不開的人，能夠問到真正作決定的地方。

Source & reading note原论文与阅读说明原論文與閱讀說明

Nine essays draw on the School prequel and eight papers, concerning kindergartens, primary and secondary schools. Institutional responsibilities remain distinct from particular teaching encounters and university arrangements. Unattributed scenes are imagined; linked policies retain their dates and scope, and written rules do not certify implementation. Protection, teaching and conditions for growth are not interchangeable. Leaving room does not mean abandoning instruction, care or a response to harm. A child's basic standing does not wait for achievement or maturity. School, teacher, family and public responsibilities remain separately answerable. Developmental models are not established age schedules. These essays are not case-specific legal, medical or safeguarding instructions. English is independently written.
