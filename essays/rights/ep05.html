第五篇：承认性权利与权力的接口

Essay 5: Where Rights Meet Power

Han Qin (秦汉)

前面两条脊柱已经各自立住了。这一篇要问一个之前一直被推迟的问题：承认性权利和社会权力之间，到底是什么关系？一个会不会变成另一个？

一、同一道缝，两种读法

任何一项正在运作的承认性权利，都可能牵涉持有者、负有义务者以及其他受影响者。从持有者一端看，它是一组可以主张的自由、请求、权力或豁免；从受影响者一端看，如果行动空间因此收窄，同一制度安排也构成权力。受影响者能否回应会改变权力如何受到问责，却不决定权利分析是否适用：无法拒绝的人仍然是权利主体。

二、权利显出权力的一面：有条件的

不是每项权利的行使都会支配别人，权力这一面取决于它是否改变他人的可行动范围。财产权即使没有被当场行使，也会通过可预期的排除与执法背景塑造他人的选择；当业主具体排除某个人时，这种潜在结构才变成一次明确的权力行使。相反，一个人表达意见未必因此控制了任何人。权利与权力相接，但不能被简单等同。

三、权力沉淀成权利：条件苛刻得多

反过来走要难得多。稳定的强制不会因为持续得久，就自动变成正当权利。制度形式、稳定预期与执行力量可以让一段权力关系沉淀成有效的法律位置；但它要成为正当权利，还必须不侵犯基础站位，并能向所有受影响者给出可检验的理由。受影响者有机会反对、获得代表与寻求救济，是正当性的重要证据，却不能成为权利存在的先决条件，否则最无力回应的人反而最容易被排除。

四、重新定向权：向新方向展开

有一类"铰链型"权利横跨基础与承认性两条脊柱。第一种可称为重新定向权：它的基础内核，是主体在当前发展路径被堵死时，仍有形成新计划、改变身份安排和尝试另一种生活的空间；它的制度一层，则涉及教育、迁徙、转业或身份变更等通道是否真实可用。外部若系统性封死所有可行方向，侵犯的就不只是某一次选择，而是主体重新组织自身生活的能力。

五、退出权：守住不被吞没的那条底线

第二种是退出权。它在自愿结社、雇佣、亲密关系和信仰共同体中尤其重要：名义上的退出若伴随不可承受的报复、失去全部生存条件或根本没有可用程序，就不是真实退出。但退出权不是取消一切持续义务的万能钥匙；亲职责任、有效契约和依法判定的刑罚都可能限制即时单方离开。关键是限制必须有正当理由、比例边界、复核程序，并且不能把一个人永久交给另一方任意支配。

六、财产权重访：横跨整条光谱的例子

财产权可以把整条光谱放在同一幅图里。生存、居所和基本控制提供道德根据；具体所有权则由法律界定，并通常同时包含排除他人的请求、处分财产的权力和不被他人随意改变产权的豁免。公司股权、专利等形式高度依赖制度。它们是否有效，要看法律构成；它们是否正当，则还要看排除的范围会不会侵犯他人的基础权利。

结语

两个方向并不对称：权利在改变他人行动空间时会显出权力的一面；权力却只能借助制度变成有效位置，还必须通过基础权利和公共理由的检验，才可能成为正当权利。重新定向权与退出权站在两条脊柱相接的地方，保护主体不被一条既定路径或关系永久吞没。下一篇将进一步讨论限制、侵犯与救济的边界。

The two spines are each standing on their own now. This essay takes up a question deliberately postponed until now: what is the actual relationship between a recognition-based right and social power? Can one turn into the other?

1. One Seam, Two Readings

Any operative recognition-based right may involve a holder, a duty-bearer, and other affected parties. From the holder's side it is a set of claims, liberties, powers, or immunities. From the affected party's side, the same institutional arrangement also constitutes power when it narrows the range of possible action. The ability to respond affects how that power can be held accountable; it does not determine whether rights analysis applies. A person unable to refuse remains a rights-holder.

2. When a Right Operates as Power

Not every exercise of a right governs another person. The power dimension appears when a right alters someone else's practical options. Even an unasserted property right shapes conduct through the background expectation of exclusion and enforcement; when an owner excludes a particular person, that latent structure becomes a concrete exercise of power. By contrast, expressing an opinion need not control anyone. Rights and power meet conditionally, not by definition.

3. When Power Becomes a Right: A Much Higher Bar

The reverse direction is harder. Stable coercion does not become a justified right merely by lasting. Institutional form, settled expectations, and enforcement can turn a power relation into a valid legal position. To become a legitimate right, however, that position must also respect basic standing and withstand reasons addressed to everyone affected. Opportunities to object, obtain representation, and seek review are important evidence of legitimacy, but they cannot be preconditions of having rights; otherwise those least able to respond would be the easiest to exclude.

4. Rights of Reorientation: Opening a New Direction

A class of hinge rights spans the basic and recognition-based spines. The first is a right of reorientation. Its basic core protects a subject's room to form new plans, revise an assigned identity, and attempt another way of life when an existing path closes. Its institutional layer concerns whether education, movement, occupational change, and changes of status are genuinely available. When external structures systematically close every feasible direction, they attack more than one choice: they attack the subject's capacity to reorganize a life.

5. Exit Rights: Guarding the Floor Against Being Swallowed

The second hinge right is the right of exit. It matters especially in voluntary associations, employment, intimate relationships, and religious communities. A nominal exit burdened by retaliation, loss of every means of survival, or the absence of any usable procedure is not a real exit. Yet the right is not a universal key that cancels every continuing obligation: parental responsibility, valid contracts, and lawfully imposed sentences may constrain immediate unilateral departure. Such constraints require justification, proportional limits, review, and protection against permanent subjection to another's arbitrary will.

6. Property Revisited: An Example Spanning the Whole Spectrum

Property places the spectrum in a single frame. Survival, shelter, and minimal control provide a moral ground. A particular title is legally defined and commonly combines claims excluding others, powers of transfer, and immunities against unauthorized alteration. Corporate equity and patents are especially institution-dependent. Whether they are valid depends on legal construction; whether they are legitimate also depends on whether the scope of exclusion violates the basic rights of others.

Conclusion

The two directions are asymmetric. A right displays a power dimension when it changes another person's practical options. Power can become a valid institutional position only through law and settled practice, and a legitimate right only after it survives the tests of basic standing and public justification. Rights of reorientation and exit sit where the two spines meet, protecting a subject against being permanently absorbed by an assigned path or relationship. The next essay turns to limits, violations, and remedies.
