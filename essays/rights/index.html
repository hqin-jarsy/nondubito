Rights Theory · SAE
权利论 · SAE

"权利"常常被裹挟在恩赐、许可、律法条文，或者"权力愿意留给你的余地"这些说法里，很少有人问：权利本身究竟是什么。这个系列从清理这些误认开始，逐步搭建一套结构性的权利理论——权利来自一个以自身为目的的主体如何为自己立法，从最基础的生命与身体层面的权利，一路展开成一整条通向承认性权利的谱系。

这七篇散文改写自SAE权利论学术系列（Prequel至Special Study），保留原有论证的结构骨架并作出必要修订：从五种误认的清理，到起源与谱系，到权利为何不以义务为本源，到两条脊柱各自的厚度与边界，再到权利与权力、制度有效性与道德正当性的接口，最后讨论限制、侵犯、给益，以及正在形成中的主体如何逐步行使已经拥有的权利。

Rights are usually wrapped in language borrowed from gift-giving, permission, statute, or "whatever space power is willing to leave you" — rarely does anyone ask what a right actually is. This series begins by clearing away those misidentifications, then builds a structural theory from the ground up: rights arise from how a being that is an end in itself legislates for itself, unfolding from the most basic rights of life and body into a full spectrum reaching toward recognition-based rights.

These seven essays rewrite the SAE Rights Theory academic series (Prequel through the Special Study), preserving its architecture while refining several claims: from five common misidentifications, to the source and spectrum of rights, to why rights are not grounded in duty, to the thickness of the two spines, the interfaces among rights, power, institutional validity, and moral legitimacy, and finally to limits, violations, beneficence, and the progressive exercise of rights already held by a developing subject.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026
  • 第一篇 · 前置 Essay 1 · Prequel
    权利不是什么
    What Rights Are Not
    在建立任何正面的权利理论之前，先做清场。恩赐、许可、权力、律法、利益——五种常见的错位指认，逐一排除；再问自由不是免于什么，而是不得不。
    Before building a positive theory, clear the ground. Gift, permission, power, law, and interest are disentangled; freedom is then recast as action governed by reasons that remain one's own.
  • 第二篇 Essay 2
    权利的源与谱
    The Source and Spectrum of Rights
    权利从不能被化约成物的主体站位展开，形成基础权利与承认性权利两条脊柱；顶端的保证不是强迫主体回答，而是不允许制度把主体永久消音。
    Rights unfold from a subject who cannot be reduced to an object, forming basic and recognition-based spines and culminating in a guarantee against being permanently silenced by others or institutions.
  • 第三篇 Essay 3
    权利为何不以义务为本源
    Why Rights Are Not Grounded in Duty
    权利的基础根据不来自某个先在的义务人；但一旦具体化为请求权，就会产生或对应朝向他人的派生义务。
    A right's foundational ground does not come from a prior duty-bearer, yet a right specified as a claim generates or correlates with duties to others.
  • 第四篇 Essay 4
    两条脊柱细说
    Two Spines in Detail
    基础权利保护完整站位及其不可替代条件；承认性权利则必须区分制度有效、现实可执行与道德正当。
    Basic rights protect complete standing and its irreplaceable conditions; recognition-based rights require separate tests of validity, enforceability, and legitimacy.
  • 第五篇 Essay 5
    承认性权利与权力的接口
    Where Rights Meet Power
    权利在改变他人行动空间时显出权力的一面；权力则必须经过制度构成与正当性检验，才可能成为正当权利——外加重新定向权、退出权与财产权。
    Rights display a power dimension when they alter others' options; power becomes a legitimate right only through institutional construction and justification — plus rights of reorientation, exit, and property.
  • 第六篇 Essay 6
    权利的限、侵与生长
    The Limits, Violations, and Growth of Rights
    限制与侵犯首先由结构效果区分，动机、明知与可避免性影响责任；权利谱系保持开放，却没有自动进步的保证。
    Limits and violations are distinguished first by structural effect, while motive, knowledge, and avoidability shape responsibility; the spectrum remains open without guaranteeing progress.
  • 第七篇 · 压轴 Essay 7 · Capstone
    权利与给益
    Rights and Beneficence
    给益不会自动生成持续请求权，也不会抹去接受者已有的权利；正在形成中的主体已经拥有完整站位，变化的是行使方式。
    Beneficence does not automatically create a continuing claim or erase existing rights; a developing subject already has complete standing while modes of exercise evolve.
