SAE 基础入口 · 从这里开始

SAE基础：人如何成为目的

SAE Foundations: How a Person Becomes an End

这十五篇不是六篇论文的压缩摘要，而是按普通读者真正会遇到的问题重新组织：系统什么时候开始替人决定目的，殖民怎样进入自我与亲密关系，个人、关系、制度为何必须一起看，自由如何从“能选择”走到自己的“不得不”，以及多个目的相遇后制度为何出现。

前十三篇构成完整、自足的基础路径。最后两篇标为“理论深潜”，追问主体性与否定性的本体论来源；不读它们，也不影响理解和使用 SAE。

建议读法：第一次接触 SAE，顺读第 01—13 篇即可。读完以后可进入《SAE第一批判》，再继续 SAE 与西方哲学、佛学及各应用系列；若想追问理论地基，再读第 14—15 篇。
Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · 十五篇 · 八种语言
第一部 · 系统与人
第 01 篇
当系统越来越成功，人还剩下什么

一个没有恶意、运转良好的系统，也可能把人逐渐改写成指标、资源和实现系统目标的手段。SAE 从这里开始。

第 02 篇
系统该在何处出现，何处退场

基础层需要制度坚定出现，涌现层需要制度克制退场。两层不是福利与自由的取舍，而是两种不同的制度责任。

第 03 篇
自由不是能选，而是改得起

菜单上的选择不等于真实自由。分叉权追问替代道路是否存在、可达、可负担，也是否仍能被想象。

第二部 · 殖民进入内部
第 04 篇
最难识别的殖民说：“这正是我想要的”

当指标、竞争与优化逻辑变成自我认同，外部命令已经不再必要。内在殖民因此比显性的控制更难退出。

第 05 篇
爱为什么也会殖民

关系越深，越容易把边界视为不信任。亲密殖民不是虚假的爱，而是真实感情被用来免除对他者的承认。

第 06 篇
一个人怎样被另一个人重新看见

主体性并不总能靠独自反省恢复。来自深关系、指向存在而非表现的承认，能够为一个被殖民的人重新打开自我。

第三部 · 三层生态
第 07 篇
人、关系与制度为什么不能分开看

制度提供边界条件，关系承担传导，个人完成最终实现。三层形式相似，却不能互相替代。

第 08 篇
涵育、透支与耗竭

感觉良好不一定结构健康，边界稳固也不等于生命展开。四种状态帮助我们区分涵育、休眠、透支与耗竭。

第 09 篇
为什么只改变一个层面通常不够

三层之间有六个传导方向。单点改变常被旧循环吸收；解锁至少需要两个层面同时出现缺口。

第四部 · 从自由到制度
第 10 篇
自由是自己的“不得不”

自由不只是做想做的或拒绝不想做的，而是让真正属于自己的内在必然逐渐长成，同时防止伪“不得不”占领自我。

第 11 篇
两个目的相遇以后怎么办

承认他者不是替他感受，也不是接受他的一切。互凿、离开与外部仲裁构成两个不可化约主体之间的三种处理方式。

第 12 篇
为什么还需要制度

当互凿与退出都无法处理纠缠的余项，共同接受、可重复、可问责的仲裁框架便成为必要。制度从这里产生。

第 13 篇
制度应该多厚，又如何允许自己被凿

制度厚度取决于退出成本与关系密度，不取决于规模。正当制度还必须保留独立的自修凿通道，并遵守最小化原则。

理论深潜 · 选读
第 14 篇
主体性不是意识

意识、否定性与主体性是三个不同问题。科学可以寻找余项，伦理却仍须决定是否承认一个存在为主体。

第 15 篇
“非”从哪里来

若世界存在本体随机性，它怎样可能在宏观尺度留下余项？这是一条有明确条件与防火墙的本体论解释，而非 SAE 伦理框架的前提。

The Reader's Entrance to SAE · Start Here

SAE Foundations: How a Person Becomes an End

《SAE基础：人如何成为目的》

These fifteen essays are not compressed summaries of six academic papers. They reorganize SAE around the questions a reader actually encounters: when systems begin to supply human purposes, how colonization enters identity and intimacy, why persons, relationships, and institutions must be read together, how freedom grows from options into a cannot-not, and why institutions arise when multiple ends share a finite world.

Essays 1–13 form a complete, self-sufficient foundation. Essays 14–15 are marked as optional theory deep dives into the ontological sources of subjecthood and Negativity. Nothing in the practical framework depends on reading them.

Suggested path: New readers can read Essays 1–13 in order, continue to The SAE First Critique, and then enter the dialogues and applied series. Return to Essays 14–15 only if you want the deeper ontology.
Han Qin (秦汉) · 2026 · Fifteen essays · Eight languages
Part I · Systems and Persons
Essay 01
When Systems Succeed, What Happens to the Person?

A well-run system needs no villain to reduce people to metrics, resources, and instruments of its own success. SAE begins with that problem.

Essay 02
Where Systems Must Act—and Where They Must Step Back

Institutions must secure the Base Layer and withdraw from the Emergence Layer. These are not competing values but two different institutional duties.

Essay 03
Freedom Is Not Having Options; It Is Being Able to Change Course

A menu of choices is not freedom. Fork Rights ask whether another path exists, can be reached and afforded, and remains imaginable.

Part II · Colonization Within
Essay 04
The Most Dangerous Colonization Says, “This Is What I Want”

Once metrics, competition, and optimization become identity, external commands are no longer necessary. Internal colonization governs in the first person.

Essay 05
How Love Becomes a License

The deeper the relationship, the easier it is to treat boundaries as distrust. Intimate colonization begins when genuine love becomes an exemption from recognition.

Essay 06
Being Seen Back into Existence

Subjecthood is not always restored through solitary reflection. Recognition from a deep relationship can reopen a self that performance has closed.

Part III · A Three-Layer Ecology
Essay 07
Why Persons, Relationships, and Institutions Must Be Read Together

Institutions supply boundary conditions, relationships transmit, and persons realize the result. The three layers share a form without becoming interchangeable.

Essay 08
Flourishing, Dormancy, Overdraw, Depletion

Feeling fulfilled does not guarantee structural health, and safety alone is not flourishing. Four states distinguish cultivation from dormancy, overdraw, and depletion.

Essay 09
Why One-Layer Reform Usually Fails

Six pathways connect the three layers. A single reform is often absorbed by the old loop; durable unlocking requires openings in at least two layers.

Part IV · From Freedom to Institution
Essay 10
Freedom as a Cannot-Not

Freedom is neither appetite nor refusal alone. It is the capacity to grow an inner necessity of one's own without letting a counterfeit must occupy the self.

Essay 11
When Two Ends Meet

Recognition is neither feeling for another nor accepting everything. Mutual chiseling, walking away, and arbitration govern encounters between irreducible ends.

Essay 12
Why Institutions Become Necessary

When mutual chiseling and exit cannot resolve an entangled remainder, a public, repeatable, accountable framework of arbitration becomes necessary.

Essay 13
How Thick Should an Institution Be?

Institutional thickness follows exit cost and relational density, not size. A legitimate institution also preserves independent channels for its own revision.

Theory Deep Dive · Optional
Essay 14
Subjecthood Is Not Consciousness

Consciousness, Negativity, and subjecthood are different questions. Science may detect a remainder; recognition remains an ethical act.

Essay 15
Where Negativity Comes From

If ontic randomness exists, how might it leave a macroscopic remainder? This is a conditional ontology with a firewall, not a premise of SAE ethics.
