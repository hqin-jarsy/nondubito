Essay 04 of 15

最难识别的殖民说：“这正是我想要的”

The Most Dangerous Colonization Says, “This Is What I Want”

Han Qin (秦汉)

监工离开以后

最有效的控制，不是让一个人害怕监工，而是让他在监工离开以后继续监督自己。他醒来先看睡眠分数，工作时计算产出，运动时追踪消耗，休息时担心“浪费潜力”。没有人命令他优化；他甚至以高度自律为荣。

问题当然不在记录、竞争或进步本身。数字可以帮助判断，训练可以扩展能力。变化发生在工具取得身份地位时：我不再使用指标了解生活，而是通过指标才知道自己是谁；我不再为了某个目的提高效率，而是把可被持续优化当成存在的理由。

SAE 称之为“内在殖民”：系统逻辑被接纳为自我认同的基础。外部制度不再只是影响行动，它进入主体内部，替主体生成评价自己的语言。

五种常见的自我

指标自我把不可量化之物视为不真实。竞争自我只能通过相对位置判断价值，目标来自排行榜而非内部。优化自我把自己当成一项永无完工的工程，连快乐也要证明效率。叙事锁定的自我被“我一直是这种人”绑住，过去的身份取消未来的分叉。恐惧驱动的自我则不断前进，不是因为想去哪里，而是无法承受落后、淘汰或让人失望。

这些形态可能带来杰出表现。内在殖民并不等于懒惰、失败或精神崩溃。相反，一个被殖民的人可以非常可靠、勤奋和成功。持续性与绩效只能说明动力强，不能说明动力属于谁。

这也解释了为什么“做自己喜欢的事”不是充分答案。被安装的欲望仍会以第一人称出现。一个社会若从小只承认一种成功，后来的人真诚地说“这就是我想要的”，并不能自动证明目的来自内部。

不是揭露一个假的自我

谈内在殖民，很容易滑向另一种傲慢：仿佛理论家知道你的真实欲望，而你不知道。SAE 不替任何人宣布某个目标是假的。它检查的是生成条件，而不是目的内容。

一个目标是否仍允许其他重要目的共存？移走奖惩、掌声与身份标签以后，它是否还有吸引力？短暂停止时，出现的是遗憾与返回愿望，还是羞耻、恐慌和“没有它我就什么都不是”的坍塌？这些问题不能从外部替人作答，却能帮助区分热爱与占领。

真正的自己也不是埋在制度影响之前的一块纯净内核。人总是在语言、关系和历史中形成。问题不在“是否受影响”，而在影响之后是否仍能反思、拒绝、改写，并让多个目的在内部相互校验。

恢复第一人称

内在殖民不能只靠更强的自我管理解决。把戒掉优化也变成新的优化项目，只会让同一逻辑换一套衣服。恢复主体性，需要的往往是一段停止计分的时间、一种不按表现确认人的关系，以及来自另一种生活的参照。

最重要的动作不是立刻找到“真正使命”，而是重新听见差异：哪些不得不是害怕不如此便不配存在，哪些愿望在没有观众时仍会回来，哪些价值无法被同一张表格比较。

显性的命令至少告诉你权力从哪里来。内在殖民最危险的地方，是权力用你的声音说话。解除它也不是消灭所有纪律，而是重新取得一句话的作者身份：“这是我想要的”中的“我”，仍有能力追问这句话。

After the Supervisor Leaves

The most efficient form of control does not make a person fear the supervisor. It makes him continue supervising himself after the supervisor has gone. He wakes and checks a sleep score, measures output at work, tracks calories during exercise, and worries that unstructured rest is wasted potential. No one orders him to optimize. He may take pride in the discipline.

The problem is not measurement, competition, or improvement as such. Numbers can inform judgment and training can enlarge capacity. The change occurs when a tool acquires the status of identity. I no longer use metrics to understand part of my life; I know who I am only through metrics. I no longer improve for the sake of a purpose; continuous improvability becomes the purpose.

SAE calls this internal colonization: system logic is accepted as the ground of self-understanding. Institutions no longer merely shape conduct from the outside. They enter the subject and supply the language in which the subject evaluates himself.

Five Familiar Selves

The metric self treats what cannot be measured as unreal. The competitive self knows worth only through relative position, so the ranking produces the goal. The optimization self becomes a permanently unfinished project and requires even pleasure to justify its productivity. The narrative-locked self is governed by “I have always been this kind of person,” allowing an old identity to cancel future forks. The fear-driven self continues not because a destination attracts her, but because falling behind, being excluded, or disappointing others feels unbearable.

These forms can produce excellent performance. Internal colonization is not synonymous with laziness, failure, or breakdown. A colonized person may be disciplined, inventive, admired, and highly reliable. Persistence demonstrates the strength of a motive; it does not establish authorship of the motive.

This is also why “do what you want” is an insufficient answer. An installed desire still speaks in the first person. If a culture recognizes only one form of success from childhood onward, a sincere adult declaration—“this is exactly what I want”—does not by itself prove that the end emerged from within.

Not a Hunt for a Hidden True Self

The language of colonization can become arrogant. A theorist may assume that your stated purposes are false while claiming access to the desires you ought to have. SAE refuses that role. It evaluates conditions of formation, not the content of a purpose.

Can this end coexist with other important ends, or does it consume them? If rewards, punishment, applause, and identity labels are removed, does the attachment persist? When pursuit is interrupted, does one experience regret and a wish to return, or shame, panic, and the sense that without this achievement there is no self? No outsider can settle these questions in advance. They are nevertheless useful distinctions between devotion and occupation.

Nor is there a pure self buried beneath every social influence. Persons develop through language, history, and relationship. The question is not whether influence occurred, but whether the person can still examine, refuse, and revise it—and whether multiple ends remain available as internal checks on one another.

Recovering the First Person

Internal colonization is rarely cured by more aggressive self-management. Turning de-optimization into another optimization project leaves the same logic in charge. Recovery may require time in which nothing is scored, a relationship that recognizes existence rather than performance, and exposure to a life organized by a different reference frame.

The first task is not to discover a grand authentic mission. It is to hear distinctions again: Which necessities arise from the fear that without achievement I do not deserve to exist? Which desires return when no audience remains? Which goods refuse comparison on a single scale?

An external command at least reveals the location of power. Internal colonization is dangerous because power speaks in one's own voice. Liberation does not mean abolishing discipline. It means recovering authorship of the sentence “this is what I want”—including the capacity of the “I” to question the sentence.
