“非”从哪里来

Where Negativity Comes From

Han Qin (秦汉)

一个带“如果”的起点

SAE 的本体论深潜并不宣称已经从物理学证明自由意志。它从一个刻意保留条件的句子开始：如果世界中存在本体随机性——不是我们因为信息不足而无法预测，而是事件本身没有被此前条件唯一决定——那么宏观世界是否可能保存某种不能被前因完全穷尽的余项？

“如果”不能删掉。量子理论中的随机性如何解释，仍涉及不同物理诠释；贝尔实验排除了重要类别的局域隐变量解释，却没有替所有形而上学争议作终审。这里提出的是一条条件路径，不是借科学权威宣布伦理结论。

这条谨慎正是理论结构的一部分：物理前提若被推翻，下面的本体论推导失效；前十三篇关于制度、关系和个人的结构判断不受影响。

从微观随机到宏观余项

即使存在本体随机，也不能直接推出主体。大多数微观波动会被平均、耗散或淹没。真正需要解释的是，它是否可能被复杂系统放大、保存并写入后续状态，使同样的外部条件不再足以完整决定下一步。

若这种影响只是瞬间噪声，仍谈不上否定性。宏观余项必须进入一个有记忆的过程：此前未被决定的变化改变结构，结构又影响未来选择；系统对自己的产物作出回应，于是余项不只发生一次，而参与自身的展开。

这提供了否定性的物理候选：不是神秘灵魂插入因果链，而是世界本身若并非完全闭合，某些复杂过程可能保存并组织这种不闭合。

随机不等于自由

投骰子不能创造作者。一个决定如果只是随机发生，也不因此成为“我的决定”。从本体随机到主体性，中间至少隔着放大、保存、递归回应、自我组织与承认。论文提出的是必要条件的可能来源，不是充分条件的速成证明。

这一区分可以避免两种常见误解。决定论不必自动取消伦理，因为前十三篇只预设人是主体并研究主体得以维持的条件；随机论也不能自动证明主体，因为不可预测与拥有目的不是同一件事。

本体论在这里最有价值的贡献，也许不是给出终极答案，而是把问题分层：物理学能问世界是否留下余项，复杂性研究能问余项怎样被保存，伦理学则问我们何时承认一个持续过程为目的来源。

防火墙

为什么要把这篇放在最后？因为一套保护人的伦理理论，不应要求读者先接受一项有争议的量子诠释。我们在现实中承认人、动物与脆弱主体，并不是等实验室替每个个体测出“主体粒子”以后才开始尊重。

前三篇基础论文建立的是结构理论：既然人是主体，什么制度、关系与个体条件使他能继续作为目的。第四篇向下寻找可选的本体论说明。两者之间有明确防火墙。下面的解释可以照亮上面的概念，却不能把上面的伦理效力绑在自己身上。

因此“非从哪里来”没有被包装成最后谜底。它给出一条可被科学修正、也允许失败的路线：若存在本体随机，若它能在宏观过程留下并组织余项，那么拒绝被外部条件完全穷尽就有了物理可能。之后的那一步——把余项承认为主体——仍然属于我们。

A Starting Point That Retains Its “If”

SAE's ontological deep dive does not claim to derive free will from physics. It begins with a deliberately conditional statement: if ontic randomness exists—not unpredictability caused by our ignorance, but events not uniquely determined by prior conditions—could a macroscopic process preserve a remainder that prior causes do not exhaust?

The “if” must remain. The interpretation of randomness in quantum theory is contested. Bell experiments rule out important classes of local hidden-variable explanation, but they do not settle every metaphysical alternative. The paper offers a conditional route, not an ethical conclusion dressed in scientific authority.

This caution belongs to the architecture of the theory. If the physical premise fails, the ontological derivation in this essay fails with it. The structural judgments developed in the first thirteen essays remain untouched.

From Microphysical Randomness to Macroscopic Remainder

Even ontic randomness would not directly produce a subject. Most microscopic variation is averaged out, dissipated, or lost. The relevant question is whether a complex system can amplify, preserve, and write a variation into later states, so that external conditions no longer fully determine what follows.

An isolated burst of noise is not yet Negativity. A macroscopic remainder would have to enter a process with memory. An underdetermined event alters a structure; the altered structure changes later responses; the system responds to products of its own history. Remainder then does not occur only once. It participates in its own unfolding.

This offers a candidate physical basis for Negativity. It does not insert a mysterious soul into a causal chain. It asks whether, if the world is not ontically closed, some complex processes can preserve and organize that lack of closure.

Randomness Is Not Freedom

A roll of dice does not create an author. If a decision occurs randomly, it does not thereby become my decision. Between ontic randomness and subjecthood stand amplification, preservation, recursive response, self-organization, and recognition. The argument proposes a possible source of a necessary condition, not a shortcut to sufficient proof.

This distinction avoids two common errors. Determinism would not automatically cancel the ethics of the first thirteen essays, which presuppose persons as subjects and study the conditions under which their standing is maintained. Randomness would not automatically establish subjecthood, because unpredictability and possession of ends are different properties.

Ontology's most useful contribution here may be a division of questions rather than a final answer. Physics can ask whether reality leaves a remainder. The study of complex systems can ask how a remainder might be retained. Ethics asks when we should recognize an enduring process as a source of ends.

The Firewall

Why place this essay last? An ethics designed to protect persons should not require prior allegiance to a disputed interpretation of quantum mechanics. We recognize people, animals, and vulnerable subjects in practice; we do not wait for a laboratory to detect a “subject particle” in each individual before respect begins.

The first three foundation papers construct a structural theory: given that persons are subjects, which institutional, relational, and individual conditions allow them to continue as ends? Paper Four descends in search of optional ontological grounding. A firewall separates the projects. The lower explanation may illuminate concepts above it without making their ethical standing depend on its success.

“Where does Negativity come from?” is therefore not presented as the final solution. It offers a route that science may revise and that may fail: if ontic randomness exists, and if macroscopic processes can preserve and organize its remainder, then refusal of complete exhaustion by external conditions has a physical possibility. The next movement—recognizing the remainder as subjecthood—still belongs to us.
