Reading My Name Is Red

《我的名字叫红》解读

Aug 30, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on the dead man who speaks from a well, a tradition that calls style an imperfection, the horse’s nostrils that betray a murderer, and the painting Shekure wants but cannot have.

Pamuk places a murder at the meeting point of two ways of seeing. Ottoman miniaturists seek a sacred view that belongs to no individual eye; Venetian portraiture admits the situated gaze of painter, subject, and viewer.

These essays do not ask which art is more advanced. They ask how a person’s trace is disciplined, erased, recognized, and finally retold.

A Dead Man Speaks from a Well

A murdered illuminator speaks first, and fifty-nine chapters keep moving the place from which the world is seen. The novel answers an argument about perspective through its form.

The Painting That Does Not Exist

Shekure wants to preserve two moments of happiness, but neither artistic tradition can contain them. She chooses narrative and admits that narrative will distort them too.

四篇文章，从井底开口的死人、把风格视为瑕疵的传统、暴露凶手的马鼻孔，到谢库瑞想要却不存在的那幅画。

帕慕克把一场谋杀放在两套观看方式的交界处：奥斯曼细密画追求不属于任何个人的神圣视野，威尼斯肖像则承认画家、模特与观者各自所在的位置。

这组解读追问的不是哪一种画法更先进，而是一个人的痕迹如何被训练、抹去、辨认并重新讲述。
