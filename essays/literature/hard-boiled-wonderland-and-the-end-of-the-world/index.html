Reading Hard-Boiled Wonderland and the End of the World

《世界末日与冷酷异境》解读

Sep 5, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on the shadow required at admission, a career implanted without knowledge, the Town’s complete ecology of erasure, and the final choice to release one half while living among what remains.

Murakami’s alternating worlds ask whether peace is still peace when the capacity to miss what was lost has been removed. These readings follow the procedures, professions, and gentle functionaries through which that question becomes concrete.

Haruki Murakami’s Hard-Boiled Wonderland and the End of the World, first published in 1985. Full-book spoilers.

The Town’s admission procedure removes both the heart and the future ability to judge its loss; only the discarded shadow remembers what wholeness was.

四篇文章，从入场时必须交出的影子、在不知情中植入的职业，到小镇完整的清除生态，以及让一半离开、自己走向残余之地的最终选择。

村上春树交替书写的两个世界追问：若连思念失去之物的能力也被拿走，安稳是否仍是安稳。这组文章顺着手续、职业与温和执行者，让这个问题逐渐变得具体。

村上春树《世界末日与冷酷异境》，中文篇依据赖明珠译本。林少华译本作《世界尽头与冷酷仙境》。全书剧透。
