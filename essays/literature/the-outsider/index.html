Reading The Outsider

《局外人》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on a man who does not cry, a trial that confiscates his first person, and an outsider whose location depends on who draws the boundary.

Meursault kills a man and is tried through the story of his mother’s funeral. Camus constructs that displacement carefully. The court needs a character it can condemn, while Meursault refuses the ordinary language by which a character is made socially legible.

The essays do not turn him into a secret saint. They ask what can be inferred from absent performance, who owns a first-person life in court, and whether the final encounter with an indifferent world is liberation, exclusion, or both.

Albert Camus's The Outsider. Full-book spoilers throughout.

三篇文章，从一个没有哭的人、一场接管其“我”的审判，到“局外”边界究竟由谁划定。

默尔索杀了人，却在母亲葬礼的故事中受审。加缪精确地建造了这种错位：法庭需要一个可供谴责的人格，而默尔索拒绝使用让人格在社会中变得可读的常规语言。

三篇文章不把他改造成隐藏的圣人。它们追问：不表演意味着什么、法庭上谁拥有一个人的第一人称，以及他最后面对冷漠世界的时刻究竟是解放、放逐，还是两者兼有。

阿尔贝·加缪《局外人》。全书剧透。
