Reading Attack on Titan

《进击的巨人》解读

Aug 28, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Five essays on walls, inherited guilt, manufactured choices, future memory, and a freedom that becomes real only when a person can still say no.

These essays treat Attack on Titan not as a puzzle whose final task is to acquit or condemn Eren, but as a sequence of political structures. One regime removes knowledge; another leaves knowledge intact while removing every usable exit. Eren then appears to break both systems, only to discover that foreknowledge can become a wall more absolute than stone.

The English edition is an independent rewriting for readers familiar with the complete manga or TV anime. It preserves the Chinese arguments while clarifying what Eren actually knew: future-memory fragments, not an exhaustive map of every possible future. Full-series spoilers follow.

五篇从墙内失忆与墙外规训，读到未来记忆、地鸣与尤弥尔，追问自由是否必须包含说“不”的能力。

这五篇不把《进击的巨人》当成一道只需裁决艾伦对错的题，而把它读成一连串政治结构：墙内拿走知识，墙外保留知识却封死出口；艾伦试图打破两者，未来记忆却成为比石墙更牢的围墙。
