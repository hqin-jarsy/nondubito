Reading Old Tales South of the City

《城南旧事》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on the woman called mad, a thief a child refuses to classify, two women whose labor was sold, and the death that marks growing up.

Lin Haiyin’s child narrator, Yingzi, does not possess adult authority. That limitation becomes an ethical resource. She listens to people already classified by the city—madwoman, thief, servant—before learning the conclusions that are supposed to make listening unnecessary.

The essays examine the value and danger of that openness. A child can see what adult categories discard, but she can also intervene without understanding all the consequences. Growing up is not simply acquiring the adult map; it is deciding what not to lose when the map becomes unavoidable.

四篇文章，从被叫作疯子的女人、孩子不肯分类的贼、两个劳动被出售的女人，到标志长大的死亡。

林海音的儿童叙述者英子没有成人权威。这种限制反而成为一种伦理资源：她先听那些已被城市分类的人——疯子、贼、佣人——说话，然后才学会那些本该让倾听变得多余的结论。

四篇文章同时讨论这种开放的价值与危险。孩子能看见成人分类丢掉的东西，也会在不理解全部后果时介入。成长不只是取得成人地图，也是在地图不可避免时决定什么不该丢。

林海音《城南旧事》。全书剧透。
