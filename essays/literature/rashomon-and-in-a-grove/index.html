Reading Rashōmon and In a Grove

《罗生门》与《竹林中》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on the excuse that precedes wrongdoing, testimonies that restore agency, and the stubborn facts that survive incompatible stories.

Akutagawa’s “Rashōmon” and “In a Grove” are distinct stories later fused in cultural memory by Kurosawa’s film. Read together, they examine two moments of self-justification: the sentence needed before an act and the narrative constructed after it.

The last essay also separates Akutagawa’s text from the film. The missing dagger makes the woodcutter suspicious, but the story never confirms him as the thief; Kurosawa’s adaptation supplies that confession. Uncertainty about the sequence does not make every fact relative.

三篇文章，从作恶之前需要的说法、用供词重建的主体性，到互相冲突的故事之下仍未移动的事实。

芥川龙之介的《罗生门》与《竹林中》是两篇不同的小说，后来因黑泽明的电影而在文化记忆中合流。并读它们，可以看见自我辩护的两个时刻：行动之前需要的一句话，以及行动之后编成的一段身份叙事。

最后一篇也区分原作与电影：失踪的短刀使樵夫可疑，但小说没有确认他就是偷刀者；明确的自白来自黑泽明的改编。情节无法完全还原，并不等于所有事实都相对。
