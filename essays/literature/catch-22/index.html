Reading Catch-22

《第二十二条军规》解读

Sep 5, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on the regulation no one has seen, Milo’s perfectly inclusive ledger, the fatal wound beneath correct treatment, and Yossarian’s need for both a route out and people who let him take it.

Heller’s absurdity is rarely random. Again and again a procedure functions flawlessly around the wrong object, or a rule becomes stronger because no one can locate its author. These readings follow the people bureaucracy cannot accommodate and can classify only as error, cowardice, or absence.

Joseph Heller’s Catch-22, first published in 1961. Full-book spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Literary criticism
The Regulation No One Has Seen

Catch-22 needs no stable text because its name exists to stop “by what authority?”; reasonable local rules combine into a structure no author can cancel.

四篇文章，从无人见过的军规、米洛把人人纳入的完美账本，到正确处理下被遮住的致命伤，以及约塞连既需要出路、也需要让他走的人。

海勒的荒诞很少是随机的：程序总在错误对象周围完美运转，规则也因找不到作者而变得更强。这组文章追踪那些无法被官僚系统容纳、只能被归入错误、怯懦或不存在的人。

本文依据上海译文出版社南文、赵守垠、王德明译本（主万校），人名地名从该本。全书剧透。

I
英文 · 简 / 繁 · 作品解读
那条谁也没见过的军规

第二十二条军规不需要固定条文，它的名字就是为了终止“凭什么”；合理的局部规则合成了没有作者、也无人能撤销的结构。

防弹衣底下那处伤
