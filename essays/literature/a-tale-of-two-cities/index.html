Reading A Tale of Two Cities

《双城记》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on aristocratic indifference, inherited liability, revenge with sufficient cause, and a guillotine unable to distinguish two men.

Dickens gives the French Revolution causes before he gives it victims. Hunger, arbitrary imprisonment, and aristocratic impunity make revolt intelligible; the Terror then turns causal justice into a machinery unable to judge persons one by one.

These essays hold both movements together through the Marquis, Darnay, Madame Defarge, and Carton. The novel’s question is not whether history has reasons, but what happens when reasons become a substitute for judgment.

四篇文章，从贵族的冷漠、被继承的责任、有充分理由的复仇，到无法区分两个人的断头台。

狄更斯先写法国革命的原因，后写它的受害者。饥饿、任意监禁与贵族免责使反抗可以理解；恐怖统治又把因果正义变成一套无法逐人判断的机器。

四篇文章通过侯爵、达尔内、德伐日太太与卡顿，把这两个方向同时保留下来。小说的问题不是历史有没有理由，而是理由何时会取代判断。

查尔斯·狄更斯《双城记》。全书剧透。
