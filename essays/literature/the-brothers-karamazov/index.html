Reading The Brothers Karamazov

《卡拉马佐夫兄弟》解读

Sep 5, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Five essays on suffering that cannot be balanced, authority disguised as mercy, responsibility spoken in the first person, ideas that become actions, and a small promise beside a stone.

Dostoevsky lets every major claim meet its strongest opponent. No voice is safely outside the drama it interprets, and no idea remains only an idea. These readings follow the moments when argument becomes relation, action, or refusal.

Fyodor Dostoevsky’s The Brothers Karamazov, first published in 1880. Full-book spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Literary criticism
The Ticket Returned

Ivan refuses any final harmony that makes one tortured child’s suffering worthwhile, while insisting that his refusal comes from love of life rather than contempt for it.

五篇文章，从不能被结清的苦难、伪装成慈悲的权威，到只能用第一人称承担的责任、化为行动的观念，以及石头旁边一个很小的承诺。

陀思妥耶夫斯基让每一个重要主张都碰见最强的反方。没有哪种声音能安全站在它所解释的戏剧之外，也没有哪个观念永远只是观念。这组文章追踪论证变成关系、行动或拒绝的瞬间。

本文依据臧仲伦译本（译林出版社），另有耿济之译本（人民文学）与荣如德译本（上海译文）通行。全书剧透。

伊万拒绝任何能把一个受苦孩子纳入和谐的结算，同时坚持他的拒绝来自对生命的贪恋，而非厌弃。
