Reading We Three

《我们仨》解读

Aug 30, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on a dream that records two years of separation, the breakfasts and private jokes that made a family, the quiet scale Yang Jiang gives history, and the survivor who writes in the plural.

We Three is small enough to look slight. Its moral intelligence lies in what it refuses to enlarge: public achievement, historical verdict and even grief itself never displace the meals, notes, walks and nicknames through which three people made a home.

The essays follow that allocation of attention. They ask why apparently useless acts can preserve a person from a world obsessed with use, and why remembering a shared life is not the same as making loss the centre of the story.

Yang Jiang’s We Three (《我们仨》), first published in 2003. Full-book spoilers throughout.

四篇文章，从那场记录两年失散的万里长梦，到一家人用早饭、散步、外号与字条做成的家，再到杨绛如何分配记忆的比例，以及一个幸存者为什么仍用复数写作。

《我们仨》很薄，薄得容易被当成一本温情小书。它真正锋利的地方，在于它拒绝让公共成就、历史判词甚至丧失本身，挤掉那些没有用途的日常。

四篇文章沿着这份轻重追问：没有用的事情为什么能保住一个人；而把共同生活记下来，为什么不等于把悲痛放在全书中央。
