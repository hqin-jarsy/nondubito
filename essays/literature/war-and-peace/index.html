Reading War and Peace

《战争与和平》解读

Sep 5, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Six essays on philanthropy that receives its own gratitude, a daughter kept by an unlocked door, Andrei’s accidental turns, command after action, Karataev’s transformation into meaning, and Tolstoy’s two incompatible endings.

War and Peace repeatedly separates what people believe they direct from what their actions actually set in motion. It also refuses to leave life at the level of a historical theory. These essays move between estates, households, battlefields, prison columns, and the unresolved argument at the novel’s end.

Leo Tolstoy’s War and Peace, published in its familiar form between 1865 and 1869. Full-book spoilers.

六篇文章，从只收到自身感动的慈善、没有上锁却留住女儿的门，到安德烈偶然发生的转向、晚于行动的命令、被化成意义的卡拉塔耶夫，以及托尔斯泰彼此拉扯的两个尾声。

《战争与和平》反复拆开两件事：人以为自己在指挥什么，以及行动实际上推动了什么。它也不肯把生命只留在历史理论里。这组文章往返于田庄、家庭、战场、俘虏队伍与小说末尾没有解决的争论。

本文依据刘辽逸译本（人民文学出版社），人名地名从该本。另有草婴、娄自良、张捷译本通行。全文剧透。
