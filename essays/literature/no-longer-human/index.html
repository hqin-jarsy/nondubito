Reading No Longer Human

《人间失格》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on performance, passivity, violated trust, and the framed testimony behind the verdict “disqualified from being human.”

Ōba Yōzō tells his life as evidence for a verdict already reached: he has failed to be human. Yet the novel places that confession inside other voices. The resulting question is not whether his suffering is sincere, but whether sincerity makes his interpretation final.

The essays track the strategies by which Yōzō survives other people, the decisions he avoids, and the disaster after Yoshiko’s trust is violated. The last essay returns to the frame, where a woman who knew him supplies a radically different description.

Osamu Dazai's No Longer Human. Full-book spoilers throughout.

四篇文章，从表演、被动、被侵犯的信任与框架叙事，重读“人间失格”究竟是谁的判决。

大庭叶藏把自己的一生写成一份判决已经作出的证词：他没有资格做人。但小说把这份自白放在其他声音之中。问题不在于他的痛苦是否真诚，而在于真诚是否足以让他的解释成为终审。

四篇文章依次讨论叶藏如何用表演在人群中活下去、如何逃避决定、良子受害之后他怎样倒置了罪责，以及框架叙事为何在最后给出另一份证词。
