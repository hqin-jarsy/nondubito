Reading The Handmaid's Tale

《使女的故事》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on possession, the slow arrival of dictatorship, women’s traded powers, and the archive that preserves a voice while refusing to finish its life.

Atwood’s regime does not merely confine fertile women. It replaces their names, redistributes their relations, and makes survival depend on performing a function. Offred’s first-person voice resists by keeping what the state treats as waste: sensory detail, wordplay, memory, and unresolved feeling.

These essays follow that conflict from a possessive name to the “Historical Notes.” They ask how authoritarianism becomes ordinary, why women can become both victims and administrators of a patriarchal order, and what an archive gains—and loses—when it turns testimony into evidence.

Margaret Atwood's The Handmaid's Tale. Full-book spoilers throughout.

四篇文章，从名字、政变、女性之间被分配的权力与二百年后的档案，重读基列如何占有一个人。

基列不只囚禁有生育能力的女性。它替换她们的名字，重新分配她们的关系，并让生存取决于是否履行一种功能。奥芙弗雷德的第一人称保存的，恰是制度当作无用之物的感官、双关、记忆与未决感受。

四篇文章从一个所有格名字走到“历史资料”。它们追问极权如何变成日常、女性为何既会成为受害者也会成为父权制度的管理者，以及档案把证词变成材料时保存了什么、又失去了什么。

玛格丽特·阿特伍德《使女的故事》。全书剧透。
