Reading Dostoevsky's Crime and Punishment

《罪与罚》解读

Aug 28, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on the theory written before the murder, the life no calculation includes, Sonya beyond the role of redeemer, and the long distance between admitting defeat and admitting wrong.

Crime and Punishment is often summarized as a guilty murderer's journey toward confession. That summary begins too late. Before Raskolnikov raises an axe, he has already written a theory that divides humanity into those who must obey and those who may step across obstacles.

These essays follow what the theory cannot contain: an unplanned victim, a woman repeatedly made useful to other people's stories, and a confession that changes conduct long before it changes the way a person sees other lives.

四篇从拉斯柯尔尼科夫的论文与谋杀实验，读到算法之外的第二个人、被当作救赎工具的索尼娅，以及认输与认错之间漫长的距离。

《罪与罚》常被概括成一个杀人犯因罪疚而自首、最终获得救赎的故事。但真正的起点更早：动手以前，拉斯柯尔尼科夫已经写好一套把人分为“普通”与“非凡”的理论。

这四篇追踪那套理论装不下的东西：计划之外的被害者、不断被别人派上用场的索尼娅，以及从行为上认输到真正放弃那张人类分类表之间的距离。
