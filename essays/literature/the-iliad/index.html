Reading the Iliad

《伊利亚特》解读

Sep 5, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Four essays on the price list that cannot contain a life, Hector’s dependence on other people’s eyes, the court engraved on Achilles’ shield, and Priam’s bounded truce with grief.

The Iliad does not offer a modern moral in ancient costume. It sets incompatible human claims beside one another: honor and survival, vengeance and settlement, glory and the ordinary life visible only on a shield. These readings stay with those collisions.

Homer’s Iliad, an ancient Greek epic whose twenty-four books cover roughly fifty days in the tenth year of the Trojan War. Full-poem spoilers.

四篇文章，从无法为生命定价的礼单、赫克托尔所依赖的目光，到阿基琉斯盾牌上的法庭，以及普里阿摩斯为悲痛争取到的十二天。

《伊利亚特》不是披着古代外衣的现代道德课。它把彼此不相容的要求并排放着：荣誉与生存、复仇与裁断、英雄之名与只能在盾牌上看见的日常生活。这组文章停留在这些碰撞里。

本文依据罗念生、王焕生译本（人民文学出版社），人名地名从该本。另有陈中梅译本通行。全诗剧透。
