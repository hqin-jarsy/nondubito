Reading The Count of Monte Cristo

《基督山伯爵》解读

Aug 30, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Five essays on distributed guilt, Faria’s education, engineered revenge, the innocent child, and the late discipline of waiting and hope.

Dumas gives revenge the scale of an institution: money, information, disguise, law, and social appetite all become instruments. These essays ask not only whether Dantès’s enemies deserve judgment, but what happens when an injured man claims the authority to design everyone else’s consequences.

Alexandre Dumas’s The Count of Monte Cristo, serialized from 1844 to 1846. Full-book spoilers throughout.

五篇文章，从一封告密信的责任链、法利亚神甫的教育、被设计的复仇，到那个无辜的孩子，以及最后的等待与希望。

大仲马把复仇写成了一套制度：金钱、信息、身份、法律与社会欲望全都成为工具。这组文章不只问唐泰斯的仇人是否该受惩罚，也问一个受害者取得安排所有人后果的权力之后，会站到什么位置。

大仲马《基督山伯爵》，一八四四至一八四六年连载。全书剧透。
