Reading The Metamorphosis

《变形记》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on labor before the insect, the grammar that turns a son into a thing, and the family’s less visible transformation.

Gregor Samsa’s changed body is the novel’s impossible fact. Everything around it is painfully ordinary: debt, timetables, rent, family duty, workplace suspicion. Kafka joins the two so that the fantastic transformation reveals a social one already underway.

These essays ask what Gregor had become before the morning in bed, how care changes with classification, and why the family’s return to ordinary life is not a simple recovery.

Franz Kafka's The Metamorphosis. Full-book spoilers throughout.

三篇文章，从甲虫之前的劳动、由“他”到“它”的语法，再到家庭更不显眼的变形。

格里高尔·萨姆沙变异的身体，是小说里不可能的事实。围绕它的一切却异常普通：债务、时刻表、房租、家庭责任与公司的怀疑。卡夫卡把两者接在一起，让奇异的变形显出早已发生的社会变形。

三篇文章追问：那个早晨之前格里高尔已经成了什么，照料如何随着称谓而改变，以及一家人恢复“正常生活”为何并非简单的康复。

弗兰茨·卡夫卡《变形记》。全书剧透。
