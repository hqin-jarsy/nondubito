Reading Rickshaw Boy

《骆驼祥子》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on one rickshaw lost three times, Huniu and Xiaofuzi’s constrained choices, and a living man whose deterioration is more than poverty.

Xiangzi wants something small and intelligible: to own the rickshaw he pulls and keep the product of his labor. He is unusually strong, disciplined, and willing to save. The repeated destruction of that plan tests the promise that effort can secure an independent self.

The essays refuse both a simple victim story and a moral fall story. Structures repeatedly strip Xiangzi’s work of result; he also learns their logic and begins to impose costs on others. The ending is bleak because survival remains after the person’s organizing judgment has been damaged.

三篇文章，从一辆三次失去的车、虎妞与小福子的受限选择，到一个仍活着却发生了不止贫困之变化的人。

祥子要的东西很小也很清楚：拥有自己拉的车，让劳动所得属于自己。他强壮、自律、肯积攒，几乎是个人奋斗叙事最有利的候选人。那个计划一次次被毁，检验的是“努力能够保住独立自我”的承诺。

三篇文章同时拒绝简单的受害者故事与道德堕落故事。结构反复剥夺祥子劳动的结果；他也学会了这套逻辑，并开始把成本转给别人。结尾之所以黑暗，是因为肉身仍然生存，而组织这个人的判断已经受损。

老舍《骆驼祥子》。全书剧透。
