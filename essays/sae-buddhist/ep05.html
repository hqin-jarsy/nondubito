Essay 05 of 6 · Huayan

一切互摄之后，还有没有他者

If Everything Interpenetrates, Is There Still an Other?

Han Qin (秦汉)

华严最迷人的图像，是因陀罗网。无数宝珠彼此映照，每一颗珠子都映出所有其他珠子，而每个倒影里又包含整张网的倒影，重重无尽。

这个图像常被快速翻成“万物互联”。可互联还远远不够。互联网的节点可以彼此传递信息，却仍有大量遮蔽、延迟与断路；因陀罗网要说的是更强的事：每一事都在其他一切事中显现，一与多同时成立，差别没有消失，障碍却被解除。

把这套关系与 SAE 放在一起，真正的问题不是“双方都重视关系”，而是：一段完整的关系，究竟要不要保留一个不能被我收进来的他者？

五教走到哪里分岔

华严五教——小乘教、大乘始教、大乘终教、顿教、圆教——是按教义之深浅所作的判释，不是时间顺序，也不是一张人格等级表。因此把它与 DD 序列相接，只能是非严格映射。

在接口读法里，小乘教的自度结构横跨 13DD（自我主体）与 14DD（自目的主体）；大乘始教松动自性执，却还没有稳定地转向具体他者；终教以佛性与如来藏打开“一切有情皆可成佛”的方向；顿教以离言离相切断可停留结构；圆教则进入 15DD（自我立法主体）以上的关系域。

分岔恰恰发生在最高处。圆教不能直接标成 16DD。双方都已经越过“只为自己的目的”，都在处理主体之间的高阶关系；可是华严的完成是事事无碍的互摄整全，SAE 的完成是双向不疑而仍守住他者的不可穷尽。

这不是高低差，是终态拓扑不同。

无外，与有外而无外力

华严的法界缘起没有一个站在法界外面启动万物的第一因。万法依一真法界、法性而起，在同一无外整体中彼此成立。这里的“无起点”不是没有根据，而是没有外部起动者；它以在场之“体”为地基，并且有一个可以正面安立的圆教终态。

SAE 的方向几乎相反。它有一个形式起点——Negativa，先于存在的否定——然后逐层凿构；但它没有封闭终点。每一个构都把余项留在外面，16DD 也只是不可由单方占据的关系极限。它以否定为地基，而不是以一张本来在场的全体为地基。

因此两边形成两组反号。华严没有外部第一因，却有可安立的“最”；SAE 有形式起点，却没有可闭合的终态。华严以满为基，SAE 以否定为基。两者都完整，谁也不是谁尚未发展完的版本。

互摄是否等于相互理解

SAE 承认他者是目的，并不因此宣称知道他者的目的内容。15DD 能确认“那里有一个目的来源”，却只能借自己的 14DD 作模板去模拟；模拟越精细，越应知道它停在结构标记上，不能抵达他者自身。

16DD 的双向不疑更不是两个人终于彼此透明。恰恰因为双方互认对方是真主体，对方那一面才不能被单方穷尽。若我说“我已经完全理解你”，这句话很可能把你重新降成了我模型里的对象。

华严从另一方向回答。事事无碍不是一方理解另一方，而是每一事在全体互摄中得以充分显现。差别仍在：一不是多，总不是别；但一与多、总与别不再互相阻碍。完整不靠保留一个外部，而靠没有任何一法被排除在关系之外。

于是中心缝可以一句说清：华严以全互摄完成他者关系，SAE 以不可全摄完成他者关系。华严担心外在性仍是障碍；SAE 担心没有外在性就失去真正的他者。

因陀罗网只能对上一半

SAE 当然也可以画网络：主体彼此作用，关系会反身影响节点，每一次互动又改变后来互动的条件。因陀罗网的“网之形”在这里有局部接口。

可一到“无尽互映”，对应就断了。SAE 的反身是有界的。任何节点对网络的模型都是一次构，都有所蔽；每一次标出遮蔽，又形成新的构，再留下新的余项。反身可以不断加深，却没有一个节点能够把全网完整映入自己。

所以不能把华严简单称作“余项网络拓扑”。余项在 SAE 里正是不能被既成整体内摄的东西；华严则把所有差异保留在无外整体内部。两边都反对把局部规定当成最终穷尽，却把“溢出”放在相反位置：一个溢出仍在全体之内，一个溢出成为全体之外的余项。

两个相反的危险

华严可以问 SAE：为什么真主体必须以不可内摄为条件？有没有另一种完整关系，差异仍然保持，却能够无所遮障地共同在场？把外在性设为真他者的必要条件，会不会只是 SAE 偏爱的某一种关系拓扑？

SAE 可以问华严：当一切都在全体中互摄，具体解释者如何避免借“全体”的名义替另一个节点说完？“我从整体看见你的意义”，怎样与“我把自己的整体观覆盖到你身上”区分？

两边都有防线。华严的六相圆融坚持总别、同异、成坏同时成立，不允许一节点吞并其他节点；SAE 的余项守恒坚持外在不是关系缺陷，而是真主体不被占有的条件。但这两道防线正好依赖各自的公理，所以无法裁决对方。

问题最终回到标题：一切互摄之后，还有没有他者？

华严会说，互摄不是取消他者，而是让每个他者以自身差别进入无碍整体。SAE 会说，若没有任何不能被我方收摄的外在，我就无法确认面对的是主体而不是一个极其丰富的对象模型。

两种答案都不是半成品。它们是两套关于关系完整性的完整哲学：一套把外部消解为无碍互摄，一套把外部保存为互不占据。接口不负责缝合这道裂口。它只负责让我们看见，世界上不必只有一种“完整”。

Huayan's most compelling image is Indra's net. Countless jewels reflect one another. Each jewel contains the reflections of all the others, and within every reflected jewel the entire net appears again, without end.

The image is often reduced to “everything is connected.” That is far too weak. Networked nodes can exchange information while remaining delayed, partial, and opaque. Indra's net makes a stronger claim: every phenomenon is present through all the others. One and many remain distinct, yet their distinction no longer functions as obstruction.

Placed beside SAE, the interesting point is not that both philosophies value relation. It is a sharper question: must a complete relation preserve an other that cannot be included from my side?

Where the Five Teachings Diverge

Huayan's five teachings—the lesser vehicle, the initial, final, sudden, and perfect Mahāyāna teachings—classify depth of doctrine. They are not a chronology and not a universal scale for ranking persons. Any interface with the DD sequence must therefore remain non-strict.

On such a reading, the self-liberative orientation of the first teaching spans the emergence of 13DD, the self, and 14DD, the self as an end. The initial Mahāyāna teaching loosens attachment to intrinsic nature but has not yet stabilized a turn toward a concrete other. The final teaching opens Buddha-nature and the possibility of awakening for all beings. The sudden teaching interrupts attachment to language and stages. The perfect teaching enters the relational domain at and beyond 15DD, the self-legislating subject.

The divergence occurs precisely at the top. The perfect teaching cannot simply be labeled 16DD. Both frameworks have moved beyond the subject organized only around its own end. Both describe an advanced relation among subjects. But Huayan completes that relation through unobstructed interpenetration, while SAE completes it through mutual non-doubt that still preserves the other's inexhaustibility.

This is not a difference in rank. It is a difference in terminal topology.

No Outside, and an Outside without External Force

Huayan dependent arising contains no first cause standing outside the dharma-realm to initiate the ten thousand things. Phenomena arise through the one true dharma-realm and through nature, within a whole that has no external remainder. “No beginning” here does not mean lack of ground. It means no external initiator. Huayan begins from a plenitude of presence and can positively articulate a perfect culmination.

SAE moves almost in reverse. It has a formal starting point—Negativa, negation prior to being—and constructs upward through directed layers. It has no closed terminus. Every construct leaves remainder outside itself, and 16DD is a relational limit that no side can occupy alone. Its ground has the character of negation rather than an already present whole.

The frameworks therefore form two counter-signs. Huayan has no external first cause and has an articulable highest completion. SAE has a formal beginning and no globally closed end. Huayan takes fullness as ground; SAE takes negation as ground. Neither is an unfinished version of the other.

Does Interpenetration Mean Mutual Understanding?

SAE's recognition of another as an end does not imply knowledge of that other's purposes. At 15DD the subject can register that another source of ends is present. But simulation borrows one's own 14DD as a template. However refined the model becomes, it should know that it reaches a structural marker, not the other's content from within.

Mutual non-doubt at 16DD does not make two subjects transparent. The opposite follows. Because each recognizes the other as a true subject, an aspect of the other remains unavailable to unilateral possession. “I understand you completely” is often the sentence by which a subject is reduced again to an object inside someone else's model.

Huayan answers from another direction. Unobstructed interpenetration is not one party's mental representation of another. It is the full appearing of each phenomenon through the whole. Difference remains: one is not many, whole is not part. Yet one and many, whole and part, no longer block one another. Completion is achieved not by preserving an outside but by excluding nothing from relation.

The central seam can therefore be stated simply. Huayan completes otherness through total interpenetration. SAE completes otherness by refusing total inclusion. Huayan suspects externality of retaining obstruction. SAE suspects the abolition of externality of abolishing the true other.

Indra's Net Matches Only Halfway

SAE can certainly draw networks. Subjects act on one another; relations feed back into nodes; each encounter changes the conditions of future encounters. The form of a net is a legitimate partial interface.

The correspondence breaks at infinite mutual reflection. Reflexivity in SAE is bounded. Every model a node forms of the network is a construct and therefore occludes. Marking that occlusion creates another construct with another remainder. Reflexivity can deepen indefinitely, but no node internalizes the whole network without loss.

Huayan should therefore not be redescribed as a “topology of remainder.” In SAE, remainder is exactly what no achieved whole has included. Huayan retains difference within a whole without outside. Both reject the finality of a local description, but they locate overflow differently. In Huayan, recontextualization remains internal to the whole. In SAE, overflow appears as a remainder external to every claimed whole.

Two Opposed Risks

Huayan can ask SAE why true subjectivity must require non-inclusion. Could there be another complete form of relation in which differences remain while all obstruction disappears? Is externality genuinely necessary, or merely SAE's preference for one relational topology?

SAE can ask Huayan how a concrete interpreter avoids speaking for another node in the name of the whole. How does “I see your meaning from the standpoint of totality” differ from imposing my image of totality upon you?

Each tradition has an internal defense. Huayan's harmony of the six characteristics preserves whole and part, identity and difference, formation and dissolution together; one node does not swallow the rest. SAE's conservation of remainder treats externality not as failed relation but as the condition under which a subject is not possessed. Yet each defense depends on its own axiom and cannot compel the other side.

The title's question remains. If everything interpenetrates, is there still an other?

Huayan answers that interpenetration does not erase the other. It allows every other to enter the unobstructed whole in its own difference. SAE answers that without something unavailable to unilateral inclusion, it cannot distinguish a true subject from an extraordinarily rich object-model.

Neither answer is a fragment. They are two complete philosophies of relational completion: one dissolves outside into unobstructed interpenetration; the other preserves outside as non-possession. An interface should not close that seam. It should show why the world does not need only one meaning of “complete.”
