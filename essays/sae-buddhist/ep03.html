Essay 03 of 6 · Madhyamaka / Sanlun

空不是终点

Emptiness Is Not a Destination

Han Qin (秦汉)

“空”大概是佛学里最容易被用错的一个字。它有时被理解成什么都没有，有时被理解成一个比万物更深的本体，还有时变成一句情绪安慰：既然都是空，就别太在意。

中观的空不是这三种东西。它不是虚无，不是一件隐藏的实体，也不是让经验失去分量的快捷键。它是一把针对“自性”的刀：凡是被说成自生、自足、不依他、在自身内就完整成立的东西，都经不起缘起的检验。

SAE 也说任何构都不能闭合。但两者从不同地方出发。中观从依存关系推出无自性：一件事既然待缘而起，就不可能凭自己成立。SAE 从遮蔽推出余项：一个构之所以成形，正因为它划了界；既然划界，就必定把某些东西留在外面。一个从“依他”入手，一个从“有所蔽”入手，结论相近，引擎不同。

八不不是八把刀

《中论》开头的八不——不生不灭、不常不断、不一不异、不来不出——经常被读成四组平行的辩证法。其实它们不是八把各砍一个靶子的刀，而是从八个方向总破同一个自性执；其中“不生不灭”是根，其余常断、一异、来去都依赖一个能够自性生起、自性存续的实体。

一旦这个根被拔掉，另外六边同时失去底托。没有一个自性地生灭的“我”，也就没有那个我去永恒、断灭、保持绝对同一或作为实体来去。

用 SAE 的坐标读，这把刀在 14DD（自目的主体）处尤其锋利。主体一旦把自己立为目的，很容易进一步把这个“我”坐实成一切理由的最终来源。八不并不直接把人推向 15DD（自我立法主体），也不以“承认他者”为中观自己的目标；它只是松开那个被自性化的中心。中心松动以后，转向他者才成为可能。这里必须守住方向：中观做的是破，转向他者是 SAE 从破之后读出的下游，不是中观原本藏着的伦理目的。

破而不立，还是立而承认余项

三论宗说“破邪显正”，但“显正”不是在废墟上另立一座正确理论。吉藏的正，是不把任何理论变成可住之处；破到不再认领一个有自性的正见，破本身就是显正。

SAE 在这里既相近又分开。它同样拒绝一个无余终点，却认为每一次破都会沉淀成新的构。既然新的语言、判断和行动已经形成，假装自己什么也没立并不诚实。SAE 的纪律不是“永不立”，而是“立的时候承认余项”：认领这个构真实发生、真实约束后续，同时承认它没有穷尽对象，也会被下一次凿改变。

于是两边面对同一事实，给出两种守法。中观让所有立都保持假名与施设的地位，不允许它获得自性重量；SAE 认领构的真实牵制力，但拒绝它取得终局资格。一个破而不住，一个立而不固。

四重二谛为什么不会回到原点

吉藏把二谛层层推进：先说有为空、空为真；再把有与空这一对一起降为俗，以非有非空为真；然后连二与不二都放下，说“非二非不二”；最后宣布前三重都只是教门，归于无所依得。

第三步尤其要紧。简单的双重否定很容易返回肯定：非不有，看起来又成了有。吉藏没有这样写。他把否定的对象抬高一层，从框架内的“有／空”转向框架本身的“二／不二”，所以否定没有塌回起点。

第四步也不是再加一个更高真理。它把前面的阶梯整体收作教门，然后让语言停在“无所依得”。在中观看来，这不是抵达一个不可言说的实体，而是连道路与目的地都不再取得自性。SAE 的凿构循环也拒绝把收束写成综合，却仍把余项指向一个实而不可穷尽的外部。两边都禁止抵达，理由却正相反：一边因为无所得，一边因为余项永不归零。

真正的缝在“假名”

“因缘所生法，我说即是空，亦为是假名，亦是中道义。”同一缘起之法，在中观那里是空，所以只能假名安立；假名可以在世俗层面发生因果、承担教化，却不因此获得自性。

SAE 会多说一步：构虽无自性，却有真实牵制。一次法律判断、一段童年记忆、一种语言习惯，当然都依缘而生，也当然没有独立本体；但它们会改变后来什么容易发生、什么几乎不可能发生。这个沉淀的约束不是自性，却也不能被写成毫无分量的名字。

中观会追问：所谓“真实牵制”，是否正是 SAE 偷藏的一缕自性？SAE 会回答：提出这次追问、维持这场论证，本身已经是一场真实运作；若把运作的约束也完全读薄，持续建构便失去了落脚处。两边都无法把对方完整翻进自己的语言。这不是误会，是中心缝。

空也要空掉

中观最后把刀转向 SAE 自己。SAE 以 Negativa——先于存在的否定——作起点，那么这个起点是否被豁免于否定？若每一次对它的否定都被解释成“你正在证明否定仍在运作”，它岂不是成了最不可破的自性？

这一问不能靠重复公理来回答。SAE 能接住它，只因为 SAE 同时承认：关于 Negativa 的这套说明也是构，也有余项；Negativa 不是一个可被供奉的对象，更不是一条获得终极豁免的命题。中观以“空亦复空”守住不立，SAE 以“本文也是构”守住立而不固。

两边相遇的，不是一个共同终点，而是拒绝把终点坐实的姿态。中观说无所得，SAE 说无构闭合。同一个不肯停下的手势，指向两个不能互译的方向。

所以空不是终点。把空当成最后答案，正是在空里重新造出一个自性；把余项当成永恒真理，也同样可能让 SAE 住进自己的洞见。真正困难的不是把世界说空，而是连这句“世界是空的”也不允许成为住所。

“Emptiness” may be the most frequently mishandled word in Buddhist philosophy. It is taken to mean that nothing exists, that a deeper Nothing lies behind appearances, or that ordinary concerns can be dismissed because everything is ultimately unreal.

Madhyamaka emptiness means none of these. It is not nihilism, a concealed substance, or an emotional shortcut. It is a disciplined attack on svabhāva: the claim that something arises from itself, stands through itself, or possesses an intrinsic identity independent of conditions.

SAE also denies that any construct closes on itself. But the engines differ. Madhyamaka reasons from dependence: what arises dependently cannot possess intrinsic nature. SAE reasons from occlusion: a construct becomes determinate by drawing a boundary, and a boundary necessarily leaves something outside. One begins with dependence; the other with exclusion. Their conclusions approach each other without becoming identical.

Eight Negations, One Attachment

The opening of Nāgārjuna's <em>Mūlamadhyamakakārikā</em> gives the eight negations: no arising and no ceasing, no permanence and no annihilation, no identity and no difference, no coming and no going. They are easily presented as four parallel dialectical pairs. But they do not function as eight blades aimed at unrelated targets. They jointly dismantle the attribution of intrinsic nature. Arising and ceasing form the root; permanence, annihilation, identity, difference, coming, and going presuppose something that first arises and persists through its own power.

Remove that root and the other extremes lose their support. Without an intrinsically arising self, there is no self-substance available to remain eternal, disappear absolutely, stay simply identical, or travel through time as an independent thing.

On an SAE reading, this operation is especially forceful at 14DD, the self as an end. Once a subject organizes action around its own purposiveness, it is tempted to convert that “I” into the final source of reasons. The eight negations do not themselves propel anyone toward 15DD, the self-legislating subject, and Madhyamaka does not secretly aim at SAE's recognition of another end. It loosens the reified center. Only after that loosening does a turn toward another become structurally available. The distinction matters: Madhyamaka performs the critique; SAE identifies one possible downstream consequence.

Refusing to Establish, or Establishing with Remainder

The Sanlun formula “refuting error and revealing the correct” does not mean replacing a false doctrine with a final true one. For Jizang, correctness lies in refusing to make any account a place of attachment. Refutation reveals the middle precisely by declining to establish a position with intrinsic status.

SAE is close to this discipline and nevertheless departs from it. It also refuses a remainderless terminus, but it holds that every negation sediments into another construct. New language, distinctions, and practices have actually appeared. Pretending that nothing has been established obscures their effects. SAE therefore does not prohibit establishment. It requires an establishment to acknowledge its remainder: to own the reality of the construct's operation without granting it final jurisdiction.

The two traditions answer the same pressure with different disciplines. Madhyamaka keeps every establishment at the level of dependent designation, denying it intrinsic weight. SAE grants constructs real constraining force while denying them closure. One refuses to dwell in what it establishes. The other inhabits provisionally and keeps the wall open to another cut.

Why the Fourfold Two Truths Do Not Return to the Start

Jizang develops the two truths through successive levels. First, existence is conventional and emptiness ultimate. Next, the opposition between existence and emptiness becomes conventional, while “neither existence nor emptiness” functions as ultimate. Then both duality and nonduality are displaced by “neither dual nor nondual.” Finally, the preceding levels are all declared pedagogical gates, leaving no object of dependence or attainment.

The third move is crucial. A simple double negation could return to affirmation: not-not-existence looks like existence again. Jizang changes the level of the target. The negation no longer applies to a term within the existence/emptiness frame; it applies to the dual/nondual frame itself. The movement therefore does not collapse backward.

The fourth move is not a fifth and higher truth. It gathers the previous ladder as teaching and lets discourse end in non-attainment. For Madhyamaka this does not point toward an ineffable entity beyond language. It deprives both path and destination of intrinsic status. SAE likewise refuses to turn closure into a higher synthesis, yet its remainder still points beyond the achieved construct toward a real but inexhaustible outside.

Both forbid arrival, for opposite reasons. Madhyamaka forbids it because there is nothing intrinsically attained. SAE forbids it because remainder never reaches zero.

The Central Seam Is Conventional Designation

Nāgārjuna's famous verse links dependent arising, emptiness, conventional designation, and the middle way. For Madhyamaka, a dependently arisen phenomenon is empty and therefore conventionally designated. Conventional things can still sustain causal practice, language, and teaching. Their emptiness does not make them inert. But their efficacy does not confer intrinsic nature.

SAE adds a claim that Madhyamaka can reasonably suspect. A construct, though dependent and non-self-sufficient, exerts real constraint. A childhood memory, a legal judgment, or a grammatical habit changes what can happen next. None is an independent substance. Yet each sediments into a topology that makes some later paths easy and others nearly inaccessible.

Madhyamaka can ask whether “real constraint” preserves a trace of the intrinsic nature SAE claims to reject. SAE can answer that the act of making and sustaining the critique is itself a real operation. If all operational constraint is thinned into designation, a constructive framework loses the ground from which continuing construction can be described.

Neither response translates cleanly into the other framework. This is not a temporary misunderstanding. It is the central seam. Madhyamaka is complete in its project of eliminating intrinsic establishment. SAE is complete in its project of constructing while preserving remainder. Each regards the other's necessary discipline as one step too much or too little.

Emptying Emptiness—and Questioning Negation

Madhyamaka finally turns the blade toward SAE's own beginning. SAE starts from Negativa, negation prior to being. Is that beginning exempt from negation? If every attempt to deny it is answered with “your denial demonstrates that negation is operating,” has SAE not created the most invulnerable svabhāva of all?

Repeating the axiom would only intensify the problem. SAE can receive the challenge because it also says that every articulation of Negativa is a construct with remainder. Negativa is not an object to be venerated, nor a proposition granted final immunity. The description can be revised, displaced, or cut even if the operation it attempts to mark cannot be turned into a stable thing.

Madhyamaka guards non-establishment by emptying emptiness itself. SAE guards provisional establishment by saying, “this text too is a construct.” They do not meet at a common destination. They share a refusal to reify destination. Madhyamaka names the gesture non-attainment; SAE names it non-closure.

Emptiness is therefore not a destination. To possess emptiness as the final answer would manufacture the very intrinsic nature emptiness undoes. To possess remainder as an unquestionable truth would expose SAE to the same danger. The hard practice is not merely to say that the world is empty. It is to refuse to make even that sentence a place to live.
