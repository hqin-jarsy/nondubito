Reading Xiao Shiyilang

《萧十一郎》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on a useful bad name, two people named for flawless jade, a manor that collects living dolls, and the different acts by which Shen Bijun and Feng Siniang step outside assigned roles.

Gu Long first wrote Xiao Shiyilang as a screenplay in 1969, then turned it into a novel serialized and published in 1970. Its cinematic economy suits a hero who rarely argues for himself: reputations speak loudly, actions answer quietly, and the ending refuses to certify who returns.

These readings treat the first novel’s open ending as open. Events from the 1973 sequel Huopin Xiao Shiyilang are mentioned only where they correct the tempting impression that Feng Siniang receives no answer at all. Full-novel spoilers follow.

I
Independent English · 简 / 繁
A Useful Bad Name

Xiao’s name is credible enough for any crime and defended by no school, making it public property for anyone who needs a culprit. He neither clears it nor lives down to it.

II
Independent English · 简 / 繁
Two Pieces of Jade

Shen Bijun and Lian Chengbi are both named for flawless jade. One leaves a perfectly assigned place; the other feeds more lives into the cracks of a perfect public self.

III
Independent English · 简 / 繁
Doll Manor

Xiaoyao Hou collects living people as dolls, while the Deer-Cutting Saber concentrates borrowed value. The open ending leaves two lives on a dead-end road and Feng Siniang finally answering Yang Kaitai.

三篇从一个最好用的恶名、两块完整无瑕的“璧”与一座收藏活人的玩偶山庄出发，读萧十一郎怎样不辩解也不活成罪名，以及沈璧君与风四娘各自走出的那一步。

古龙一九六九年先为《萧十一郎》写电影剧本，随后改写为小说，并于一九七〇年连载、出版。剧本留下的紧凑很适合这个不爱替自己申辩的主角：名声说得很响，行动答得很轻，结尾则没有替任何人证明谁一定回来。

这三篇保留第一部小说的开放结局；一九七三年的续作《火并萧十一郎》只在需要避免把风四娘写成“什么也没得到”时提及。全文包含结局剧透。

I
英文重写 · 简 / 繁
恶名

萧十一郎的名字既真有盗贼经历，又没有门派替他澄清，因而成了人人可以借来栽赃的公共工具；他既洗不掉，也不肯照着活。

璧

沈璧君与连城璧都以无瑕之玉为名；一个第一次离开别人配好的位置，另一个则把越来越多的人压进“完美君子”的裂缝里。

玩偶
