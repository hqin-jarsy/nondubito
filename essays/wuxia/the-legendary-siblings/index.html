Reading The Legendary Siblings

《绝代双骄》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Five essays on a revenge devised to save two infants, two rival educations, a heroic reputation manufactured from betrayal, and a duel both brothers enter intending to lose.

Nearly everyone in The Legendary Siblings tries to finish someone else’s life in advance. Yaoyue writes a twenty-year ending for two newborns. The villains hope to manufacture a perfect weapon. Yihua Palace turns obedience into a flawless personality. Jiang Biehe constructs a public self so persuasive that the jianghu defends it for him.

The novel keeps answering these designs with people who learn what they were taught and then use it otherwise. These essays follow that unfinished distance between an upbringing and a life. Full-novel spoilers follow.

五篇从一个为救人而编出的复仇计划出发，细读恶人谷与移花宫的两种教育、被经营出来的大侠名声，以及一场双方都准备替对方死去的决斗。

《绝代双骄》里，几乎每个人都想替另一个人把一生预先写完：邀月替两个婴儿写下二十年后的结局，十大恶人想造出一件最完美的凶器，移花宫把服从做成无缺的人格，江别鹤则造出一个让整个江湖替他维护的自己。

小说一次次让人学会别人教给他的东西，又把它用向别处。这五篇细读的，正是教育与一生之间那一点始终无法被写完的距离。全文包含结局剧透。
