The Old Master’s Ledger

老伯的账

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting of the Chinese argument, shaped for English-language readers.

Everyone calls Sun Yubo “Old Master” or simply “Uncle.” The name makes authority sound familial. It is not inaccurate, but it is incomplete.

Sun is the leader of the Dragon Gate organization and the center of a vast estate: land, businesses, retainers, informants, and armed force. Opposite him stands King Wanpeng’s Twelve Flying Rocs. Their conflict is not a duel between two offended swordsmen. It is a struggle between institutions.

Sun’s deepest power is his ability to make service feel like belonging. People do not merely fear him. They believe that protecting him protects a world in which they have a place.

He does care

The estate cannot be explained by coercion alone. Sun remembers old friends, accepts costs on their behalf, and supports the livelihoods attached to his household. Han Tang’s near-absolute loyalty is not purchased each morning at sword point. Nor are Lü Xiangchuan’s years of elegant, effective administration.

Sun also loves his children. His son Sun Jian and daughter Sun Die are not emotionally disposable pieces.

That is why the plan involving Sun Jian is disturbing in a more interesting way than ordinary cruelty. Sun believes his son can help lure and kill King Wanpeng. He places someone he loves inside a strategic calculation because he also believes a leader must use everything available to preserve the larger house.

The plan fails. Sun Jian is killed; Han Tang dies under a coordinated attack by the Rocs. Sun receives the news first as a father and almost immediately must process it as the center of an organization: why did the plan fail, and who inside made failure possible?

He suffers the loss without reconsidering the logic that made the loss usable.

The loyal deputy who inventories the throne

Lü Xiangchuan is one of Sun’s most trusted assistants. He is poised, observant, socially gifted, and exceptionally capable. He also intends to replace his master.

He waits because killing a leader is simpler than inheriting the system behind him. Before acting, Lü must know the estate’s wealth, dependencies, personnel, and hidden sources of resilience. His service is genuine work, but it is also a prolonged audit.

Eventually Sun reveals the full books. The gesture is the highest proof of trust and the final missing piece of intelligence.

Betrayal here is not simply the opposite of competent service. Lü reaches the ideal position from which to betray precisely by becoming the ideal subordinate. The more completely he satisfies the organization’s need, the more thoroughly he can attack it.

Lu Mantian is another internal traitor. Sun has already seen through him but delays exposure; Lü later kills Lu himself. Two men treating the estate as an object of acquisition begin by eliminating each other from within.

A ledger large enough to include everyone

Sun is often right. He can test loyalty, exploit an enemy’s false move, survive apparent defeat, and preserve a final reserve. Yi Qianlong eventually proves to be the friend whose loyalty did not need theatrical display.

Yet the more sophisticated the organization becomes, the more easily “Who is this person?” turns into “What function can this person perform?” A son can carry risk, a killer can purchase advantage, a deputy can receive secrets, and a daughter’s suffering can first appear as damage to the family name.

Sun’s problem is not that he feels nothing. He can love and calculate at the same time, with equal seriousness.

King Wanpeng later offers him the office of Silver Roc master on terms that cannot be refused. The demand reveals the same logic in its barest form. Sun need not be destroyed. He can remain recognizably himself, continue governing, and simply be entered beneath a larger name.

What the account cannot price

Nearly everyone in the novel is calculating: Gao wants Happy Forest, Lü wants the Sun estate, Lu wants elevation, and King Wanpeng wants absorption. Sun sees farther than most of them, which is one reason he survives.

But Gu Long does not equate seeing farther with seeing persons completely.

As Ye Xiang dies from a clash with Sun, he still signals that Lu Mantian is the traitor. The gesture has no clean entry under profit, obedience, or exchange. It may serve Little Butterfly, Meng, Sun, or Ye’s own final judgment at once.

Meng begins as an assassin sent to kill Sun and later helps him—not because Sun has successfully bought another subordinate, but because Meng reaches his own conclusion. Little Butterfly, expelled from the household’s order, forms a life beyond its ledger.

Sun’s world is powerful because everyone has a place. In the end, it is saved by people doing what their places never required.

Primary text: Gu Long, Liuxing, Hudie, Jian (流星·蝴蝶·剑). Full-novel spoilers.

版本说明：中文原作；简体与繁体同页切换。

江湖上都叫孙玉伯“老伯”。

这个称呼很亲近，像是家族里一个人人敬重的长辈。但他不只是一家之长。他是龙门帮的首领，也是孙府庞大网络的中心：田产、买卖、门客、眼线和武力，都从这里伸出去。与他对峙的，是万鹏王的十二飞鹏帮。

老伯最厉害的地方不是武功，而是他让许多人相信，替他做事也就是替自己守住一处可以站立的地方。

他真的在乎手下的人

孙府不是靠恐惧单独撑起来的。老伯记得旧交的难处，肯替朋友承担代价，也把许多人的生计护在自己的势力之下。韩棠对他近乎绝对忠诚，律香川多年把孙府上下打理得井井有条，都不是因为每天有人拿刀逼着。

他也爱自己的儿女。孙剑与孙蝶不是两颗随时可弃的棋子。

于是，当他把孙剑放进诱杀万鹏王的计划里时，问题并不是“他有没有父爱”。他有。他只是同时相信，一个当家人必须在更大的局面里使用自己拥有的一切。

计划失败，孙剑被杀，韩棠也死在飞鹏帮高手围攻之下。老伯震惊、悲痛，紧接着开始查内奸。一个父亲还没有时间把儿子的死活完，就必须变回孙府的中枢。

他承担了这笔损失，却没有因此怀疑把人放进计划的方式。

越了解一个人，越知道怎样取代他

律香川是老伯最信任的助手之一。他聪明、得体、能办事，也早已想取代老伯。

他不急着动手，因为杀死一个人容易，接住这个人身后的全部却很难。孙府有多少产业、哪些人真正忠诚、哪一处是命脉，这些都必须先弄明白。多年的效力既是取得信任，也是完成盘点。

后来老伯真的把全部家底摊给他看。那是最高的信任，也是最后一块缺失的情报。

背叛在这里并非忠诚的简单反面。律香川正是因为把一个得力助手做到了极致，才来到最适合背叛的位置。他越符合孙府对他的需要，就越有能力攻击孙府。

陆漫天也在暗中反叛。老伯已经看出，却暂时不揭穿；律香川后来杀了陆漫天。两个把孙府当成猎物的人，先在内部互相清除。

一本把所有人都算进去的账

老伯的判断常常是对的。他会试探，会将计就计，也能在局势崩坏时留下最后一手。易潜龙最终证明是他真正可靠的朋友。

可越精密的组织，越容易把“这个人是谁”改写成“这个人能做什么”。儿子可以承担风险，杀手可以换取胜势，助手可以接触所有秘密，女儿的遭遇也首先会被看成孙府的耻辱与损失。

老伯并非没有感情。他的问题恰恰是：他可以一面爱，一面算，而且两件事都做得很认真。

十二飞鹏帮提出让他担任银鹏舵主，也使用了同一种逻辑：不必消灭老伯，只要把他和整座孙府并入更大的名册。人仍然做原来的事，只是从此归另一个中心解释。

算不进去的人

这部小说里人人都在算：高老大要快活林，律香川要孙府，陆漫天要更高的位置，万鹏王要吞下对手。老伯比大多数人算得更远，也因此活到了最后。

可小说并没有说最会算的人就最懂人。

叶翔临死前仍向杀伤自己的老伯示意陆漫天是叛徒。这个动作可以叫忠告、补偿、爱屋及乌，也可以说是为了孟星魂和小蝶。它没有一项可以准确入账的回报。

孙蝶被逐出孙府以后，也在账本之外活出一段新的关系。孟星魂原本是来杀老伯的人，最后却帮助了他；不是因为老伯成功收买，而是因为孟星魂自己判断该这样做。

老伯的世界靠人各就各位而强大。它最后获救，却恰恰靠几个人做了位置没有要求他们做的事。

注：本文所据为古龙《流星·蝴蝶·剑》。
