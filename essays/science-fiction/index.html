Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Science fiction is often described as literature about the future. These readings begin elsewhere: with the unfamiliar person already standing inside the future. A city trains citizens not to see their neighbors; an alien language changes time; a manufactured mind outgrows its assigned use.

One hundred forty-four works and 572 essays are arranged below by the question each makes most vivid. This is a reading map rather than a rigid genre taxonomy: every work appears on one shelf, and connected novels stay together. Open a shelf, jump directly to another, or search across title, author, and theme.
