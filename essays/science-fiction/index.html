科幻小說解讀

Sep 1, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Science fiction is often described as literature about the future. These readings begin elsewhere: with the unfamiliar person already standing inside the future. A city trains its citizens not to see their neighbors; a health system cares for the body until no private life remains; an alien language changes time; a manufactured mind outgrows the purpose assigned to it.

The category now brings together thirty-four works and 145 essays. Each series can be entered independently; together they ask how a civilization meets lives it cannot safely reduce to resource, threat, experiment, function, category, or mirror.

科幻小说常被说成关于未来的文学。这里从另一个位置进入：未来里那个已经在场、却不能被我们的计划完全概括的主体。一座城市训练居民看不见近在身边的人；医疗系统照料身体，直到私人生活无处可退；陌生语言改变时间；被制造的心灵走出了原定用途。

目前三十四套作品、共一百四十五篇文章可以各自独立进入，也共同追问：文明面对不能被安全还原成资源、威胁、实验、功能、类别或镜子的生命时，究竟怎样相遇。
