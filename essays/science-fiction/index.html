Science fiction is often described as literature about the future. These readings begin elsewhere: with the unfamiliar person already standing inside the future. A city trains citizens not to see their neighbors; an alien language changes time; a manufactured mind outgrows its assigned use.

Sixty-four works and 287 essays are arranged below by the question each makes most vivid. This is a reading map rather than a rigid genre taxonomy: every work appears on one shelf, and connected novels stay together.
