Four essays on the ocean that both saves and separates humanity, sensory M-skin, the body inherited from Andy, and a happy life composed by Momo’s mother.

Chi Ta-wei's 1995 work The Membranes. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

The Ocean Saves Humanity. When Does Protection Become a World No One May Cross?

A membrane can preserve life and mediate contact, but safety becomes domination when those inside cannot inspect the labor or decisions maintaining it.
