The Person Who Could End the War

能够结束战争的人

能夠結束戰爭的人

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English essay on Perhaps the Stars. Full-book spoilers.

The war has made ordinary authority too weak and extraordinary authority dangerously attractive. J.E.D.D. Mason may be able to end what no institution can stop.

Some people see in him not merely a leader but a being whose distance from ordinary humanity makes surrendering judgment feel rational.

Ending immediate violence can justify power without consecrating it

A ceasefire sometimes needs rapid coordination, control of weapons, and guarantees no single faction can supply. Refusing all exceptional action may leave civilians inside a war everyone agrees should end.

But the mandate should be tied to the specific work: stop attacks, open corridors, verify disarmament, and create a route back to shared decision. It cannot expand simply because the same person performed the first task well.

Records, multiple witnesses, expiring commands, and the ability to investigate the peacemaker afterward distinguish necessary intervention from the founding of a new sovereign.

No faction may decide what humanity is for

Utopians look toward the stars; Gordians and others offer different accounts of human purpose, embodiment, and the future of Earth. War turns these visions into camps that treat compromise as betrayal of the species.

Experts may identify climate limits, technical possibilities, and time horizons. They cannot consent on behalf of future people to one final destination.

A plural future can support space exploration without writing off Earth, and repair Earth without forbidding those who choose long projects beyond it. Shared resources require negotiation; shared humanity does not require one schedule.

Peace cannot return to the world before disclosure

The old stability rested on murder and hidden family rule. Restoring it would mean restoring the conditions that made the war possible.

Former enemies have all crossed lines. Accountability must distinguish actions and repair victims without demanding that one side become pure enough to rule the rest.

J.E.D.D. Mason's most convincing use of extraordinary power is therefore to make his own centrality temporary. Peace begins when the person capable of ending the war helps create a world that can continue after refusing him—and when the stars remain one possible horizon rather than the command hidden inside victory.

版本说明：中文原作；简体与繁体同页切换。涉及完整剧情。

战争使普通权威显得过于无力，也使非常权威变得危险地诱人。J.E.D.D.梅森也许能够结束任何机构都无法停止的冲突。

一些人看见的不只是一名领袖，而是一种与普通人距离遥远到足以使交出判断显得合理的存在。

结束眼前暴力可以授权力量，却不能把力量神圣化

停火有时需要快速协调、控制武器，以及任何单一阵营都无法提供的保证。拒绝一切非常行动，可能让平民继续困在所有人都同意应当停止的战争里。

可是，授权应与具体工作绑定：停止攻击、打开走廊、核验裁军，并建立回到共同决定的道路。不能因为同一个人出色完成第一项任务，权限便自行扩大。

留下记录、安排多方见证、让命令到期，也允许人们事后调查和平缔造者，才能区分必要介入与新主权奠基。

没有派系可以决定人类为了什么存在

乌托邦派望向群星；戈尔迪安派等群体则提供不同的人类目的、身体与地球未来。战争把愿景变成阵营，使妥协显得像背叛整个物种。

专家能够说明气候限制、技术可能与时间尺度，却不能替未来的人同意一个最终目的地。

多元未来可以支持太空探索而不放弃地球，也能修复地球而不禁止愿意投身星外长期工程的人。共同资源需要协商，共同人类却不需要唯一时间表。

和平不能回到真相公开以前的世界

旧稳定建立在谋杀与家庭秘密统治上。恢复它，便是在恢复使战争成为可能的条件。

昔日敌人都曾越界。追责必须区分行动、修复受害者，却不能要求某一方先变得足够纯洁，再统治其余所有人。

J.E.D.D.梅森对非常力量最有说服力的使用，因此是使自己的中心位置成为暂时。和平从能够结束战争的人帮助建立一个即使拒绝他仍可继续的世界开始——群星也继续作为可能的地平线，而非藏在胜利里面的命令。

戰爭使普通權威顯得過於無力，也使非常權威變得危險地誘人。J.E.D.D.梅森也許能夠結束任何機構都無法停止的衝突。

沒有派系可以決定人類為了什麼存在

舊穩定建立在謀殺與家庭秘密統治上。恢復它，便是在恢復使戰爭成為可能的條件。

昔日敵人都曾越界。追責必須區分行動、修復受害者，卻不能要求某一方先變得足夠純潔，再統治其餘所有人。
