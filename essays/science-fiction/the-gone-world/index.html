Four essays on temporary futures, investigation across timelines, injuries that versions cannot cancel, and the choice to erase one’s own world.

Tom Sweterlitsch's 2018 work The Gone World. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Are the People of a Temporary Future Merely Evidence?

Investigators enter futures that collapse when they return, but impermanence does not make the residents’ experiences fictional or release visitors from relationship.

Why Can’t Injuries in Different Timelines Cancel One Another?

Moss’s missing limb and the suffering of alternate victims show that a restored version does not reimburse the person who endured harm in a branch that later vanishes.

English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Is Erasing Your Own World a Sacrifice—or a Decision Made for Everyone?

Moss discovers her timeline is temporary and acts against the Terminus, placing herself among those erased while still choosing an irreversible fate for countless others.
