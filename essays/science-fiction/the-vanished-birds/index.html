Five essays on time divided by trade routes, Ahro’s protection, Fumiko’s imperial infrastructure, blood turned into an engine, and rescue after the station explodes.

Simon Jimenez's 2020 work The Vanished Birds. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

The destruction ends his captivity, but revenge, civilian risk, and the years already taken prevent escape from becoming a clean restoration.
