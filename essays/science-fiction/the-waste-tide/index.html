Five essays on Silicon Isle’s e-waste, the toxic geography beneath clean technology, Mimi’s occupied body, protective clans, a translator’s authority, and who decides the island’s future after transformation.

Chen Qiufan's 2013 work Waste Tide. The opening essay can serve as an introduction; the remaining 4 essays contain full-story spoilers.
