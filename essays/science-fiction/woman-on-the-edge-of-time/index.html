Reading Woman on the Edge of Time

Five essays on who defines Connie’s reality, consent to treatment inside the brain, Mattapoisett’s utopia, two futures fighting over the body, and the poisoning at the end.

Marge Piercy's 1976 work Woman on the Edge of Time. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction

Listening does not require instant belief; it requires that poverty, race, gender, and diagnosis do not settle the meaning of her account before evidence is heard.
