The Drone's Perfect Violence

无人机完美的暴力

無人機完美的暴力

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English essay on Use of Weapons. Full-book spoilers.

Skaffen-Amtiskaw is a Culture drone, witty, observant, fiercely attached to Diziet Sma, and capable of killing human attackers before most of them understand a fight has begun.

Its precision makes ordinary armed force look clumsy. It does not make the political judgment inside the attack disappear.

Accuracy answers where force lands, not why it was authorized

A drone can distinguish targets, track weapons, and use only the amount of force its systems judge necessary. Compared with bombing a district or sending frightened soldiers, this can save many lives.

But a perfectly aimed act can still serve a wrongful mission, protect an unaccountable agent, or close options too quickly for anyone else to intervene. Technical restraint is not the same as shared legitimacy.

The more reliable the weapon, the easier it becomes for observers to hand it their moral attention. Awe at how cleanly violence is performed replaces the question of who decided there should be violence at all.

Personality does not excuse power, and power does not cancel personhood

Skaffen-Amtiskaw jokes, forms loyalties, takes offense, and interprets situations rather than merely executing a target list. Treating it as equipment misses a participant with preferences and responsibility.

Friendship with Sma also cannot be the only limit on its force. People affected by the drone's decisions need rules, records, and some path to challenge the institution that deployed it.

Accountability does not require pretending machine and human judgment are identical. It asks that distinctive capacity be connected to public purposes and review rather than converted into an exemption because the machine is more civilized than its targets.

The best weapon leaves fewer reasons to keep using it

Zakalwe's talent and the drone's precision can end immediate attacks. If each victory strengthens Special Circumstances' dependence on secret intervention, tactical restraint may enlarge strategic power.

A genuinely limited operation protects surrender, distinguishes ongoing threat from revenge, and turns control back toward people who must live after the mission. It also records mistakes instead of letting superior statistics make individual loss invisible.

The most advanced use of force is not a more elegant display. It is the ability to stop, accept correction, and help build conditions in which nobody needs the same savior at the next crisis. Otherwise precision merely makes permanent intervention easier to admire.

斯卡芬—阿姆蒂斯考是“文明”无人机，机敏、善于观察，对迪齐特·斯玛极为忠诚，也能在多数人尚未明白战斗开始以前杀死攻击者。

它的精确使普通武力显得笨拙，却没有使攻击中的政治判断消失。

准确回答力量落在哪里，不回答谁授权使用

无人机能够识别目标、追踪武器，并只使用系统认为必要的力量。与轰炸街区或派出惊恐士兵相比，这确实可能挽救许多生命。

可完美瞄准仍可能服务错误任务、保护无人问责的代理人，或过快封死别人介入的选项。技术克制不等于共同正当。

武器越可靠，观看者越容易把道德注意一并交给它。暴力执行得多么干净的震撼，取代了为什么首先应该使用暴力的问题。

人格不会替力量免责，力量也不会取消人格

斯卡芬会开玩笑、形成忠诚、感到冒犯，也会解释处境，而不只是执行目标名单。把它当作设备，会漏掉一名拥有偏好和责任的参与者。

与斯玛的友谊同样不能成为力量的唯一限制。受到无人机决定影响的人，需要规则、记录，以及质疑部署机构的道路。

问责不要求假装机器与人类判断完全相同，而是把独特能力连接到公开目的与复核，不因机器比目标更“文明”就转化成豁免。

最好的武器会留下更少再次使用它的理由

扎卡维的才华与无人机的精确都能结束眼前攻击。若每次胜利又加强特殊情况科对秘密干预的依赖，战术克制反而可能扩大长期权力。

真正有限的行动要保护投降，区分持续威胁与报复，并把控制交回任务结束后仍要在那里生活的人；它也要记录错误，不让更好统计把个别损失隐藏。

最先进的力量不是更漂亮的展示，而是能够停止、接受纠正，并帮助形成下一次危机无需同一位救世者的条件。否则，精确只会使永久介入更容易受到赞美。
