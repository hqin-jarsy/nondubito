Three essays on plural need in an abundant society, a historic visitor’s private boundaries, and gifts that do not restore the factory—asking why Dex and Mosscap keep traveling together.

Becky Chambers's 2022 work A Prayer for the Crown-Shy. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

Why Can an Abundant Society Not Give Mosscap One Answer About Need?
