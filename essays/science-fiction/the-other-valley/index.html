Reading The Other Valley

《The Other Valley》解读

《The Other Valley》解讀

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Four essays on parental visits, a death that cannot be disclosed, the Conseil’s monopoly, and the temptation to keep approaching the past instead of mourning it.

Scott Alexander Howard's 2024 work The Other Valley. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
May Parents Cross the Border to Watch a Child Who Has Not Yet Died?

A mourning visit joins three family moments at once, while secrecy and committee approval leave the living child unable to consent to becoming a resource for future grief.

II
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
If Odile Knows Edme Will Die, Is Silence Protection or Betrayal?

The secret could alter the timeline and burden Edme with foreknowledge, but withholding it also shapes intimacy and transfers judgment to an institution he cannot question.

III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
May the Conseil Decide What a Person Is Allowed to Know?

Temporal stability needs common rules, but secrecy gives one institution exclusive power to define reality while selecting for the emotional distance that best preserves itself.

2026Three reading modes
IV
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Can’t Mourning Be Postponed Forever When the Past Remains Nearby?

Geographical access makes an earlier life visible without returning it to the present; repeated visits can convert grief into occupation of another person’s time.
