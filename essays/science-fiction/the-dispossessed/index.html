《一无所有》解读

《一無所有》解讀

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Five essays begin with a stone at the spaceport and end beside a sealed hatch. Between them: an assignment no one signs, a room made by two lives, Odo's words hardening into an answer, and a theory given to every world at once.

Ursula K. Le Guin's 1974 novel The Dispossessed: An Ambiguous Utopia. Essay I introduces the two worlds; Essays II–V contain full-book spoilers.
