Cara Is Dead in 372 Worlds—How Does Poverty Become a Corporate Asset?

卡拉在372个世界都死了，贫穷为什么会变成公司的核心资产

卡拉在372個世界都死了，貧窮為什麼會變成公司的核心資產

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting for readers of Micaiah Johnson's The Space Between Worlds. It preserves the Chinese argument while reorganizing its sequence for an English-language essay. Full-book spoilers.

Multiverse travel has a severe rule: a traveler can enter another Earth only if her counterpart there is dead. Most wealthy residents of Wiley City survive across many worlds and cannot travel far. Cara’s counterparts have died in 372 of 382 known worlds, making her exceptionally employable.

The institute describes this as compatibility, almost a rare gift. Yet the deaths arise from poverty, violence, pollution, and scarce healthcare in Ashtown. The technology did not create those conditions. It discovers that their repeated result can be mined for value.

The survivor’s salary does not compensate the dead

Cara’s job genuinely improves her life. She earns money, gains physical security, and approaches citizenship. Respecting that choice means not dismissing her as a helpless corporate dupe. It also means refusing the company’s use of one success story as proof of fairness.

The other Caras cannot collect compensation. Their families and neighborhoods receive no share even though high mortality created the industry’s labor supply. A business dependent on vulnerability should disclose that dependence, invest in reducing it, and give affected communities authority over the resulting wealth. Otherwise improvement becomes contrary to the employer’s interest: if more Caras live, fewer can travel.

The most dangerous missions fall on people already easiest to remove. Cara lacks citizenship and can be expelled if she stops being useful. Parallel counterparts make replaceability literal, but resemblance does not make one life a substitute for another. Each version has relations and history no new hire can restore.

Consent needs somewhere safe to return

Hazard pay is insufficient when declining work also threatens housing, healthcare, and legal belonging. Travelers need independent medicine, long-term protection, accurate risk, and a right to refuse without deportation. Only then is each crossing closer to an occupational choice than a toll for entering society.

Adam Bosch wants to overcome the commercial obstacle posed by counterparts who remain alive. The novel points to a different problem worth solving: why the same kind of person dies first in nearly every possible world. Innovation should not celebrate that pattern as human infrastructure. Its success would be a future in which all the Caras live, even if that makes the machine less profitable.

平行世界旅行有一条残酷限制：目的地中的对应人物必须已经死亡。富裕威利城居民在多数世界都能活到成年，因此难以穿梭；阿什镇出身的卡拉则因对应人物大量早逝，可以抵达几乎所有已知地球。

公司把这称为她的“适配性”，像一项幸运天赋。可372次死亡并非随机背景，而来自贫困、暴力、污染和缺乏医疗。技术没有创造这些条件，只是发现它们能够盈利。

卡拉的高工资没有补偿那些已经不能领取工资的她

0号地球上的卡拉得到工作、住房和接近公民身份的机会。与留在荒镇相比，这份职业确实扩大了她的生活。不能因为公司获利，便否定她主动使用唯一通道的判断。

然而，企业叙事会把一个幸存者的上升当作系统公平证明。其他世界的卡拉已经死亡，她们的生活条件构成岗位价值，却没有任何家庭或社区从技术利润中得到补偿。

若跨世界产业依赖某些地区的高死亡率，它至少应投资改变这些地区、公开风险来源，并让受影响社区参与收益。否则，改善死亡条件反而会损害公司的人才供应，形成最阴暗的利益冲突。

危险任务主要落在最容易被替换的人身上

穿梭会造成身体损伤、迷失和政治危险。研究所选择卡拉，不只因为她能进入更多世界，也因为她缺少威利城身份，难以要求完整保护。她若死亡，公司还可能在别的地球找到相似人选。

“可替换”在平行世界中有了字面意义，却仍是一项政治分类。对应人物外貌和记忆相近，不表示任何一个都可补上另一个留下的位置；公司只是从管理角度忽略了差异。

高风险职业需要的不只是风险津贴，还要有拒绝任务、独立医疗、长期保障和不因退出便失去居留的权利。否则，工资只是把驱逐威胁写成奖金。

技术真正该消除的不是旅行限制，而是某些人总在每个世界先死

亚当·博施关注怎样让更多客户穿梭，把“对应者仍活着”视为商业障碍。可在卡拉看来，真正重复的规律是：无论世界细节怎样变化，贫困者总更可能在年轻时被杀死。

平行宇宙原本像无限可能，阶级暴力却把无限可能压成相似结局。改变世界不只是打开更多入口，也要让同一类人不再在每条时间线上被提前清除。

The Space Between Worlds 用一项耀眼技术提出朴素问题：当创新从不平等中获利，它会试图结束不平等，还是保护这座矿藏？卡拉的价值不应来自372具尸体；真正值得建设的世界，是即使所有卡拉都活着，仍愿意给她们位置。
