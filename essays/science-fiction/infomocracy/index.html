Four essays on microdemocracy, the global Information organization, war’s temptation during an election, and why a rerun cannot automatically restore trust.

Malka Older's 2016 work Infomocracy. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

Can Rerunning the Vote Repair Democracy?

A new count corrects a damaged procedure, but trust returns only through explanation, limited victory, dissent, and visible changes to the institutions that failed.
