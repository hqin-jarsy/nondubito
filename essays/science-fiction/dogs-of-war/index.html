Four essays on the meaning of a “good dog,” responsibility across a weapons chain, personhood in court, and the life a manufactured weapon is owed after war.

Adrian Tchaikovsky's 2017 work Dogs of War. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

How Can a Court Hear a Being Who Is Both Weapon and Witness?

Before judging Rex’s actions, the court must decide whether a corporate asset can testify as an agent—and whether personhood depends on human appearance, eloquence, or harmlessness.
