Reading The Employees

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Three essays on numbered testimonies, humans and humanoids reduced to functions, desire classified as malfunction, and the identities that appear after work stops.

Olga Ravn's 2018 work The Employees. The opening essay can serve as an introduction; the remaining 2 essays contain full-story spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Do They Have Testimony Numbers but No Whole Names?

The inquiry records workers as numbered statements, promising neutrality while reproducing the interchangeability of the workplace it investigates.

II
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
When an Object Awakens Desire, Why Does the Company Call It a Malfunction?

The strange objects awaken memory and longing that interrupt productivity, so management treats newly formed inward life as defective performance.

III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
After Work Stops, Who Can an “Employee” Be?

The stoppage removes the role through which the crew was recognized, exposing both the terror of purposelessness and the possibility of a life not identical to employment.
