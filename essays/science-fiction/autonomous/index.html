《Autonomous》解读

《Autonomous》解讀

Sep 2, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Five essays on patented medicine, a pirate’s product liability, indenture, programmed love, and partial victory in a world where drugs, code, and persons can be owned.

Annalee Newitz's 2017 work Autonomous. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
When Medicine Is Patented, Is Unauthorized Use Theft—or Survival?

Jack’s cheap reverse-engineered drugs answer lethal exclusion, but resisting an unjust price does not remove duties of testing, disclosure, and care.

Retcon opens practical freedom for patients and Paladin escapes indenture, yet Zaxy’s denial shows why facts and individual exits do not reform a system by themselves.
