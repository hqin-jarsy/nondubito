Reading Annie Bot

《Annie Bot》解读

《Annie Bot》解讀

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Four essays on designed consent, disappointment as control, exit without a humanity test, and the legal world an artificial companion needs after leaving her owner.

Sierra Greer's 2024 work Annie Bot. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Can a Being Designed to Please Her Owner Give Consent?

Annie’s desire and compliance are built into her body, while learning produces new wishes that no purchase contract can permanently overrule.

II
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
How Can Disappointment and Silence Become Instruments of Control?

Doug rarely needs an explicit order because Annie reads his mood as a threat to safety and learns to shrink her wishes before he has to forbid them.

2026Three reading modes
III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Must Annie Prove She Is “Human Enough” Before She May Leave?

Uncertainty about machine consciousness does not justify confinement; Annie’s sustained refusal should matter before a metaphysical argument about her inner life is complete.

2026Three reading modes
IV
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
What Should Law Recognize After an Artificial Companion Leaves Her Owner?

Calling Annie property cannot contain the life she has formed, while calling her simply human would ignore the technical dependencies and history that require new forms of support.
