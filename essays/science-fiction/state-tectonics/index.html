Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Three essays follow a democratic system learning not to restore its original center. Competing fact services, collective exit, and Mishima's life of care show what it means for an institution to remain useful while becoming less indispensable.

Malka Older's 2018 conclusion to the Centenal Cycle. Full-book spoilers.
