Reading The Mountain in the Sea

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Four essays on octopus civilization, protection without resemblance, translation as listening or capture, the AI Evrim, and enslaved workers still unheard when humanity celebrates hearing another species.

Ray Nayler's 2022 work The Mountain in the Sea. The opening essay can serve as an introduction; the remaining 3 essays contain full-story spoilers.

Evidence of octopus language strengthens the case for protection, but worth should not depend on passing a human-designed intelligence examination.

Evrim Knows Who They Are. Why Isn’t That Enough?
