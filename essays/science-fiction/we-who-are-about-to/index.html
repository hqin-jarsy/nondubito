Reading We Who Are About To…

《我们这些即将……的人》解读

《我們這些即將……的人》解讀

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Three essays on doomed survivors, the claim of “continuing humanity” upon a woman’s body, resistance that becomes killing, and the right to die when rescue is no longer credible.

Joanna Russ's 1977 work We Who Are About To…. The opening essay can serve as an introduction; the remaining 2 essays contain full-story spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Can’t “the Continuation of Humanity” Automatically Claim a Person’s Body?

The survivors turn an impossible colony into a reproductive command, treating one woman as infrastructure for an abstract species future.

II
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
When Self-Defense Becomes Killing the Other Survivors, Is She Still Resisting?

The narrator faces coercion, but her preemptive violence expands beyond stopping an immediate assault and leaves no shared world in which judgment can continue.

2026Three reading modes
III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
If Survival Is Impossible, Does a Person Have the Right to Choose Death?

The narrator’s final choice refuses compulsory optimism, while raising the question of how autonomy can be recognized amid trauma, isolation, and vanishing alternatives.
