《Gnomon》解讀

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Three essays begin with a corpse total knowledge cannot explain, enter Hunter's layered stories, and end with the danger of a government that can absorb every criticism. Privacy appears not as secrecy's luxury but as a condition for moral and political life.

Nick Harkaway's 2017 novel. Full-book spoilers.
