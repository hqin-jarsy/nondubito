Three essays on gentle invasion, a partner’s choice to become a child again, and the right to preserve grief—asking why a post-scarcity utopia must still make room for unhappiness.

Chana Porter's 2020 work The Seep. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

Why Is The Seep Still an Invasion If It Makes People Happy?

The alien presence genuinely ends scarcity and war, but power also works by reshaping desire until refusal looks like pathology and no outside remains from which to judge it.
