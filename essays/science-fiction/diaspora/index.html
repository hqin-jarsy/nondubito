Reading Diaspora

《Diaspora》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Five essays on a parentless digital birth, three forms of embodiment, rescue under catastrophe, a thousand divergent copies, and a journey whose value does not depend on finding a final answer.

Greg Egan's 1997 work Diaspora. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

Exploration need not be validated by a superior civilization waiting at the end; its meaning can lie in the lives and revisions made along the way.
