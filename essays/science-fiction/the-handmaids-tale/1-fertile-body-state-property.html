The Bed at the Center of the Room

房间中央那张床

房間中央那張床

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English essay on The Handmaid's Tale. Full-book spoilers.

The bed is placed at the center of the room. Serena Joy sits behind Offred and holds her hands. The Commander enters, reads scripture, and performs what Gilead calls the Ceremony.

No one in the room is permitted to describe it in the language of desire. That is part of the design. If each body performs an office, coercion can pass as administration.

Three people are arranged into one household

Gilead has taken an old biblical story and turned it into a monthly procedure. The Wife supplies status, the Commander seed, the Handmaid fertility. Offred's body is physically placed between the married couple so that any resulting child can be claimed as theirs.

The arrangement does not remove jealousy or humiliation. Serena's grip is not tenderness; the Commander's impersonality is not innocence. The ritual gives each person a script precisely so no one needs to say what is being done to the woman whose refusal has no legal force.

Calling the act duty also protects the men who built the system. No individual desire appears to command it. Scripture, infertility, and the future of the nation seem to speak by themselves.

The state decides which body caused the crisis

Birth rates have collapsed, yet Gilead recognizes only female infertility. A Commander may be sterile, but the official vocabulary cannot say so. Fertile women therefore carry both the diagnosis and the remedy.

This fiction shapes Offred's options. Serena later sends her to Nick because the household needs a child and the Commander may be unable to provide one. The secret breaks a rule without returning the body to its owner. Offred is transferred from one solution to another.

A crisis can be real while the burden assigned in its name remains unjust. Gilead's leaders do not invent ecological damage or declining fertility; they use fear to decide whose freedom is expendable.

The door closes after the Commander leaves

Outside the Ceremony, the Commander invites Offred to his study. They play Scrabble, he lets her read, and later takes her to Jezebel's. These acts feel intimate because the regime has made ordinary conversation contraband.

Small pleasures are still pleasures. They do not equalize the relation. The Commander can end the meetings, expose her, or retreat into his public rank; Offred carries the danger. His kindness is most comfortable when it lets him imagine himself as an exception to the system that gives him access to her.

The bed remains at the center even when the scene moves elsewhere. It is the hidden architecture of every favor: one person may offer a little freedom because he already belongs to the class that took the rest away.

床摆在房间中央。瑟琳娜·乔伊坐在奥芙弗雷德身后，握住她的双手；主教走进来，诵读经文，随后完成基列所谓的“受孕仪式”。

房间里没有人可以用欲望的语言描述正在发生的事，这正是设计的一部分。只要每具身体都在履行职务，强迫便能伪装成行政程序。

三个人被排列成一个家庭

基列把古老的圣经故事改造成每月一次的流程。妻子提供身份，主教提供精子，使女提供生育能力。奥芙弗雷德的身体被放在夫妻之间，以便可能出生的孩子直接归入他们的家庭。

这种安排没有消除嫉妒与羞辱。瑟琳娜的握持不是温柔，主教的冷淡也不是无辜。仪式给每个人一份台词，正是为了让他们不必说出：那个无法合法拒绝的女人正在遭遇什么。

把行为叫作义务，也保护了建立制度的男人。表面上没有个人欲望在发号施令，仿佛经文、不孕和国家未来自己开口要求。

国家决定由哪一种身体承担危机

生育率确实崩塌，基列却只承认女性不孕。主教也可能没有生育能力，但官方语言不允许这样说。于是，有生育能力的女人同时承担诊断与解决方案。

这项谎言规定了奥芙弗雷德的选择。后来瑟琳娜把她送到尼克那里，因为家庭需要孩子，主教又可能做不到。秘密安排违反一条制度规则，却没有把身体还给她本人；她只是从一种解决方案转交给另一种。

危机可以真实存在，以危机之名分配的代价仍然可能不正当。基列并未发明环境污染和生育下降，而是借恐惧决定谁的自由可以被牺牲。

主教离开以后，门再次关上

仪式之外，主教私下邀请奥芙弗雷德进入书房。他们玩拼字游戏，他允许她阅读，后来又带她去“耶洗别”。这些行为之所以像亲密，是因为政权已经把普通交谈变成违禁品。

微小快乐仍然是真快乐，却不会使关系平等。主教可以结束会面、揭发她，或者退回公开身份；主要风险都由奥芙弗雷德承担。他的善意最舒适的时候，是让自己相信他属于制度的例外，却不必放弃制度赋予他的接近权。

即使场景已经离开卧室，那张床仍在中央。它是每项恩惠背后的隐藏结构：一个人之所以能归还一点自由，是因为他属于先拿走其余部分的群体。
