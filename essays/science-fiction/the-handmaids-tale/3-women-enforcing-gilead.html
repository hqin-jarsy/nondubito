The Women Who Guard the Red Center

守卫红色中心的女人

守衛紅色中心的女人

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English essay on The Handmaid's Tale. Full-book spoilers.

Aunt Lydia teaches the women to police one another before Gilead has to do it for them.

At the Red Center, blame is rehearsed as a chorus. When Janine describes being raped, the other women point and chant that it was her fault. The ritual converts fear into participation: join the accusation, or risk becoming its object.

The Aunts are allowed to read

Gilead grants the Aunts privileges forbidden to most women. They may read and write enough to administer the Handmaids, keep records, teach doctrine, and carry cattle prods. Limited authority makes the hierarchy appear partly female.

The privilege is real, but delegated. The Aunts can decide how to discipline women inside rules they did not publicly legislate and cannot apply upward to Commanders. Their power depends on making the lower ranks governable.

Understanding that dependence need not absolve them. It tells us where responsibility sits: not equally with the founders, not nowhere, but in each moment they turn room for action into another woman's confinement.

Serena Joy lives inside the sermon she once preached

Before Gilead, Serena advocated a domestic role for women. After the regime arrives, she is trapped in the house her politics idealized, excluded from the public authority men built with those ideals.

Her bitterness has an object above her, yet much of it moves downward toward Offred. She holds the Handmaid during the Ceremony, withholds information about the daughter, and arranges sex with Nick to obtain a child. A woman harmed by patriarchy can still use the smaller power patriarchy gives her.

Serena's gardening suggests a life that exceeds her assigned role; it does not turn cruelty into mere frustration. Suffering explains pressure without replacing judgment.

Moira and Ofglen refuse in different ways

Moira escapes the Red Center dressed as an Aunt, becoming a legend in Offred's imagination. Later she is found at Jezebel's, constrained again. Ofglen joins Mayday, kills a man to spare him torture, and finally kills herself before interrogation.

Neither story supplies an easy standard by which to condemn women who do less. Courage does not permanently protect Moira, and resistance drives Ofglen into an impossible last exit. A political order should be judged by whether ordinary people can refuse without needing to become legends.

The women of Gilead are not one moral category. Some enforce, some evade, some resist, and most do several things across time. The colors of their clothes make the hierarchy look complete. Their uneven, changing lives keep showing the seams.

版本说明：中文原作；简体与繁体同页切换。涉及完整剧情。

基列尚未亲自动手以前，嬷嬷莉迪亚已经教会女人彼此监督。

红色中心里，责备被排练成合唱。珍妮娜讲述遭受强奸时，其他女人一起指向她，高喊都是她的错。仪式把恐惧变成参与：加入指控，否则便可能成为下一个对象。

嬷嬷们获准阅读

基列给予嬷嬷普通女性没有的特权。她们可以读写到足以管理使女、保存档案、教授教义，也能携带电击棒。有限权力使等级制度看上去仿佛也由女性掌握。

权力是真的，却来自委托。嬷嬷可以在一套自己没有公开制定、也无法向上用于主教的规则里，决定怎样惩罚其他女人。她们的位置依靠使下层女性变得容易管理。

看见这种依赖，不等于替她们免责，只是帮助精确放置责任：不能与制度创立者等量，也不能说完全不存在，而在每次把有限行动空间改造成另一个女人的牢笼时出现。

瑟琳娜住进了自己曾经宣讲的道理

基列以前，瑟琳娜曾鼓吹女性回归家庭。政权建立以后，她被关进自己的理想房屋，又被男人用那些理念建成的公共权力排除在外。

她的怨恨有一个位于上方的对象，却大量向下落到奥芙弗雷德身上。她在仪式中握住使女，扣留女儿消息，又为了得到孩子安排她与尼克性交。被父权制度伤害的女人，仍可能使用父权制度留给她的较小权力。

瑟琳娜照料花园的样子，显出她还有超出角色的生活，却不能把残忍改名为单纯受挫。痛苦解释压力，不会取代判断。

莫伊拉与奥芙格伦以不同方式拒绝

莫伊拉穿成嬷嬷逃离红色中心，一度成为奥芙弗雷德想象中的传奇；后来，她又被困在“耶洗别”。奥芙格伦加入“五月日”，在公开处决中先杀死一名男人以免他受刑，最终又在审讯到来前自杀。

两段人生都不能变成责备其他女人反抗不够的尺。勇气没有永久保护莫伊拉，抵抗也把奥芙格伦逼进一条不可能的最后出口。评价一种政治秩序，应看普通人能否拒绝，而不必先成为传奇。

基列女性不是同一种道德角色。有人执行，有人躲避，有人反抗，大多数人还会在不同时间做出不一样的事。衣服颜色把等级画得完整，变化而不整齐的生命却不断显出接缝。
