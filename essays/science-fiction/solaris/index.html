A space station where no one comes to meet Kelvin, the Harey who returns, and a hand reaching toward the ocean. Three essays on Stanisław Lem’s novel.

On Stanisław Lem’s 1961 novel Solaris. Essays II and III discuss the ending.

I

No One Came to Meet Him

When Kelvin reaches the station, the first colleague he meets is afraid of him. Recognizing someone may not tell you where they came from.

Between Hand and Sea
