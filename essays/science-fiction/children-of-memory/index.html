Five essays on a colony that never existed, Liff without an external body, ravens that deny consciousness, Miranda’s many lives, and a simulated story that may be waking up.

Adrian Tchaikovsky's 2022 work Children of Memory. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.
