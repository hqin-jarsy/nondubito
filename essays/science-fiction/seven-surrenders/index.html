《Seven Surrenders》解读

《Seven Surrenders》解讀

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Three essays follow the collapse of a peace maintained through hidden murder and private kinship. The victims, the family, and Bridger each reveal a different cost of letting a small circle decide which future everyone else must inhabit.

Ada Palmer's 2017 second novel in the Terra Ignota quartet. Full-book spoilers.
