Five essays on life without a next generation, Xan’s gentle rule, the Quietus, the ownership of the first baby, and the ring Theo puts on after victory.

P. D. James's 1992 work The Children of Men. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
If There Will Be No Next Generation, Why Does Anyone Living Now Still Matter?

The end of the species destroys familiar projects, but it does not turn present people into leftovers; meaning also lies in how contemporaries answer one another.
