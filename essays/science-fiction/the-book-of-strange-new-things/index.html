Five essays on corporate mission, alien translation, a marriage stretched across worlds, the moral insulation of a safe base, and a return that cannot guarantee forgiveness.

Michel Faber's 2014 work The Book of Strange New Things. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.
