Four essays on gifts that cannot erase occupation, the translator caught between two sides, guilt expanded to an entire species, and the limits of redemption claimed on another people’s behalf.

Cadwell Turnbull's 2019 work The Lesson. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.
