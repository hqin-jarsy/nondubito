The Body Heaven Gives Back

天界发还的身体

天界發還的身體

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English essay on Lord of Light. Full-book spoilers.

A person dies and may return in another body. The technology aboard the old colony ship can preserve memory, change sex, escape disease, and let knowledge outlive one set of organs.

On this planet, rebirth passes through Heaven. The people who run the machinery call their decision karma.

A medical possibility becomes a gate of political obedience

The first colonists control mind transfer, cloned bodies, and the education needed to operate them. Ordinary residents are assessed for loyalty and religious conduct before receiving another life—or a body chosen to express punishment.

The system does more than ration a scarce resource. It explains its own result as cosmic desert. A weak body proves the recipient deserved weakness; protest proves further spiritual failure.

When the allocator also writes the moral meaning of allocation, no experience can correct the rule. Rebirth stops enlarging life and becomes a wage paid for submission.

The gods accumulate centuries while ordinary people begin again

Brahma, Kali, Yama, and their peers move through bodies while preserving technical knowledge, alliances, and old rivalries. Others may lose memory and social position between incarnations.

Longevity therefore compounds like wealth. The gods appear wise partly because they have prevented anyone else from building an equally continuous life. Their past decisions become tradition before a new generation can gather evidence against them.

Long experience can be valuable without entitling one mind to hold office for centuries. A public technology would need open education, records, rotating authority, and rules that separate personal lifespan from the lifespan of political control.

Freedom includes the right not to be remade

Mind transfer loosens the tie between identity and one body. For some, that opens gender, health, and forms of life previously unavailable. For others, a familiar body and the option to end are themselves meaningful.

Universal access cannot mean universal compulsion. People need information about continuity and memory, a say in the body they receive, and the ability to refuse another incarnation without being declared irrational.

Sam's demand to open the machinery does not solve every problem of population, bodies, and responsibility. It restores those questions to the people who must live them. The technology becomes liberating when Heaven can no longer use a promised next life to own the present one.

一个人死去，还能在另一具身体里回来。旧殖民船留下的技术可以保存记忆、改变性别、摆脱疾病，也让知识活过一组器官。

可在这颗星球上，转生必须经过天界。掌握机器的人把自己的决定叫作业报。

医疗可能变成政治服从的关卡

初代殖民者控制心智转移、克隆身体与操作教育。普通居民必须先接受忠诚和宗教审查，才可能获得下一生；身体甚至会被特意挑选，用来表现惩罚。

制度不只分配稀缺资源，还替分配结果写好宇宙意义。得到衰弱身体，证明当事人配得衰弱；提出抗议，又证明精神仍不合格。

分配者同时规定分配的道德含义时，任何经验都无法纠正规则。转生不再扩大生命，而成为服从后发放的工资。

诸神积累几个世纪，普通人却不断重新开始

梵天、迦梨、阎摩等人穿过不同身体，保留技术知识、政治联盟与古老恩怨；普通人在转生之间却可能失去记忆和社会位置。

长寿像财富一样复利。诸神显得更加智慧，部分原因正是阻止别人形成同样连续的人生。一代普通人还来不及收集反证，诸神过去的决定就已经变成传统。

长期经验可以有价值，却不使一颗心天然有权占据几百年的职位。公共技术需要开放教育和档案、轮换权力，也要把个人寿命与公共权力寿命分开。

自由也包括不被重新制造的资格

心智转移松开了身份与单一身体的关系。对一些人，它打开性别、健康与原本无法进入的生活；对另一些人，熟悉身体与选择结束同样重要。

普遍可用不能变成普遍强制。人们需要知道记忆与连续性可能怎样变化，参与选择下一具身体，也能拒绝再次转生而不被判为不理性。

山姆要求开放机器，没有一次解决人口、身体来源和旧责任，只是把问题还给真正生活其中的人。当天界再也不能用许诺的下一生占有眼前这一生，技术才开始带来自由。

自由也包括不被重新制造的資格
