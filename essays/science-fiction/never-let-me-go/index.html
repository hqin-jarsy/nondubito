Reading Never Let Me Go

《别让我走》解读

Aug 26, 2026 Han Qin (秦汉) Eight languages · Kazuo Ishiguro八种语言 · 石黑一雄

Kazuo Ishiguro's novel imagines lives protected with extraordinary care and denied the one thing care cannot replace: a place in the decisions that determine what those lives are for.

Never Let Me Go follows Kathy H. as she remembers Hailsham, the friends with whom she grew up, and a social order that can acknowledge an inner life while continuing to use the body that contains it.

These three essays move from the good school, to the limits placed on hope, to the empty answer waiting behind the only door Kathy and Tommy know how to open.

石黑一雄写了一群被悉心照顾、也被彻底排除在自身命运之外的人。三篇解读追问：善意如何与使用共存，希望如何被预先限缩，以及，一个人有没有被当成人，究竟应当由什么来判断。

《别让我走》（另译《莫失莫忘》）从凯茜·H的回忆展开：黑尔舍姆、露丝与汤米，以及一个能够承认他们有内心、却仍然继续使用他们身体的世界。

三篇文章从“一所很好的学校”开始，经过被划定上限的希望，最后走到凯茜与汤米唯一知道的那扇门后。
