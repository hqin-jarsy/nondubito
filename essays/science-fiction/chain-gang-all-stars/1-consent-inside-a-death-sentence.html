The Contract Signed Inside a Death Sentence

死刑里签下的合同

死刑裡簽下的合同

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English essay on Chain-Gang All-Stars. Full-book spoilers.

A prisoner signs the CAPE contract and walks into a televised circuit where killing may eventually earn High Freedom.

The signature is visible. The years of confinement, torture, despair, and closed alternatives that shaped the hand are kept outside the camera frame.

A real choice can still be manufactured by terrible conditions

CAPE offers something prison largely withholds: movement, status, comrades, and a possible end to the sentence. Those benefits are not imaginary.

But refusing means returning to an institution that can isolate, degrade, and break a person. Loretta Thurwar's earlier protest and the violence used against prisoners reveal how the state prepares the chooser before presenting the form.

Consent requires more than a spoken yes. It needs credible alternatives, independent advice, time, and the ability to withdraw without being returned to retaliatory conditions.

Technology continues the sentence inside the apparent freedom

Influencers and surveillance systems can shape movement, punishment, and bodily response during the program. The contestants become stars without recovering ordinary control over their own bodies.

Rules are written by the same company that profits from danger and narrates every violation for viewers. Appeal occurs inside the entertainment product.

Independent medical care, private communication, external legal review, and an immediate route out would all reduce control. CAPE cannot provide them without undermining the spectacle it sells.

High Freedom advertises the exception and hides the population

A tiny number may survive long enough to win release. Their existence lets the program say every contestant had a road.

A road designed to kill almost everyone is not a general alternative to prison. The survivor becomes evidence for a system built on the dead, while those who tried and failed are blamed for losing the opportunity.

The honest test is whether a prisoner who refuses CAPE still receives safety, due process, medical care, and a finite possibility of return. If personhood exists only after winning the arena, the contract has not created freedom; it has sold the absence of freedom as a prize.

版本说明：中文原作；简体与繁体同页切换。涉及完整剧情。

一名囚犯签下CAPE合同，进入一条电视直播的死亡巡回赛道；不断杀人，也许最终可以赢得“至高自由”。

签名清楚可见，塑造这只手的多年囚禁、折磨、绝望与封闭选择，却被留在镜头以外。

选择可以真实，也可以由糟糕条件制造

CAPE提供监狱大多拒绝的东西：移动、身份、同伴，以及结束刑期的可能。这些好处并非虚假。

可是拒绝意味着回到一个能够隔离、羞辱并摧毁人的机构。洛蕾塔·瑟沃曾经的抗议与监狱暴力，揭开国家怎样在递出表格以前先准备选择者。

同意需要的不只是一句“是”，还需要可信替代、独立建议、时间，以及退出后不会被送回报复条件的能力。

技术让刑罚在表面自由内部继续

“影响器”和监控系统能够塑造节目中的移动、惩罚与身体反应。选手成为明星，却没有重新取得对自身身体的普通控制。

规则由从危险获利、又替观众讲述每次违规的同一家公司制定，申诉也只能发生在娱乐产品内部。

独立医疗、私人通讯、外部法律复核与立即离开道路，都能减少控制；CAPE无法真正提供，因为这些会损害它出售的景观。

“至高自由”宣传例外，也隐藏整群人

极少数人也许能活到释放。他们的存在让节目宣称每名选手都曾拥有道路。

一条被设计成杀死大部分人的路，并不是普通监禁的普遍替代。幸存者成为建立在死者之上的制度证据，尝试却失败的人则被责怪没有把握机会。

真实检验是：拒绝CAPE的囚犯是否仍有安全、正当程序、医疗，以及有限而真实的回归可能。若必须赢下赛场才重新成为人，合同没有创造自由，只是把自由缺席包装成奖品。
