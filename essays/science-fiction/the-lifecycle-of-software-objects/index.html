Reading The Lifecycle of Software Objects

《软件体的生命周期》解读

《軟體物件的生命週期》解讀

Sep 3, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Three essays on digients as products and children, growth that cannot be installed in one upgrade, and decisions made for young minds whose own judgment is still developing.

Ted Chiang's 2010 work The Lifecycle of Software Objects. The opening essay can serve as an introduction; the remaining 2 essays contain full-story spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Is a Manufactured Life a Product or a Child?

Digients are sold as software, yet sustained interaction gives them histories and attachments that outgrow the buyer–product relation.

2026Three reading modes
II
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Can’t Growth Be Completed by a Single Upgrade?

Intelligence can be copied or accelerated, but maturity emerges through time, consequence, and relationships rather than the installation of finished answers.

2026Three reading modes
III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Who Decides the Future of a Life Not Yet Mature?

Caregivers must sometimes choose for digients, but temporary authority becomes domination if it never builds the conditions for the dependent mind to refuse.

2026Three reading modes
