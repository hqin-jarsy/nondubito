Reading Blindsight

《盲视》解读

《盲視》解讀

Sep 1, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Five essays on Siri, Theseus, and Rorschach—asking whether intelligence needs consciousness, whether fluent answers imply a listener, and whether an inefficient inner life is still worth preserving.

Peter Watts's 2006 novel Blindsight. Essay I introduces the mission and its altered crew; Essays II–V contain full-book spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
The Man Who Explains the World but Cannot Enter His Own Life

Siri translates experts and other people into public narratives while excluding himself from the model. The novel keeps returning the parts of life that do not improve efficiency.

II
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Does Theseus Need Crew Members or a Set of Functions?

The ship decomposes persons into replaceable functions and finally preserves only data and a narrator. If every member becomes a use, who remains in “we survived”?

2026Three reading modes
III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Rorschach Thinks—but Is There a Who Inside?

Rorschach and its scramblers suggest intelligence without consciousness. Respecting strangeness means neither inventing a subject nor ranking the right to persist by cognitive performance.

2026Three reading modes
IV
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Is It Communication If No One Is Listening?

Fluent answers can be generated without a listener. Communication is not agreement but the presence of another who can understand, judge, and refuse.

2026Three reading modes
V
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
If Consciousness Is Doomed, Should We Preserve It?

The novel treats consciousness as a possibly losing adaptation without turning survival efficiency into a verdict on value. A life that suffers does not become weightless because it is inefficient.

彼得·沃茨作品。第一篇可作导读，第二至第五篇涉及完整剧情。

I
英文 · 简 / 繁 · 科幻小说解读
一个替所有人解释世界的人，为什么无法进入自己的生活

席瑞善于把专家和他人翻译成公共叙事，却也把自己留在模型之外；小说让那些“不提高效率”的生活不断返回。

II
英文 · 简 / 繁 · 科幻小说解读
忒修斯号需要的是成员，还是一组功能

飞船把人格、身体与专业拆成可替换功能，最终只保存资料和叙述者；若所有成员都成为用途，“我们幸存”还剩下谁？

III
英文 · 简 / 繁 · 科幻小说解读
罗夏会思考，却有“谁”在里面吗

罗夏与攀爬者展现无意识智能的可能；承认陌生不等于把陌生美化成主体，也不等于用智能成绩决定谁值得延续。

IV
英文 · 简 / 繁 · 科幻小说解读
交流如果没有听者，还算交流吗

流畅回答可以在没有听者时生成；真正交流不以最后同意为标准，而以对方能够理解、判断并拒绝为前提。

V
英文 · 简 / 繁 · 科幻小说解读
如果意识注定失败，我们还要保留它吗

小说把意识写成可能失败的进化策略，却没有把生存效率变成价值判决；会痛苦的生命不能因“无用”而失去自己的分量。
