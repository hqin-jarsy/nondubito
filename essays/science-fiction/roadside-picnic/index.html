Three essays on a visitation without encounter, a Zone that regulates danger and desire, and whether Redrick’s final wish before the Golden Sphere can still be his own.

Arkady and Boris Strugatsky's 1972 work Roadside Picnic. The opening essay can serve as an introduction; the remaining 2 essays contain full-story spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Do the Aliens Leave No Answers?

The Visit changes Earth profoundly without addressing humanity, replacing the fantasy of first contact with the humiliation of being incidental.

English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Does the Zone Contain Danger—or Human Desire?

Authorities restrict lethal anomalies, but prohibition and unequal access also create the stalker economy through which hope and profit enter the Zone.

III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
When Redrick Prays for Happiness for Everyone, Is It Still His Own Wish?

At the Golden Sphere, Redrick reaches for a universal wish but can find its words only through another man’s remembered ideal, leaving authorship unresolved.
