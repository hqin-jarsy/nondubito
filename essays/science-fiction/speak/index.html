Four essays on a voice trained from many lives, children’s attachment to babybots, recognition through conversation, and who keeps the story after a developer is imprisoned.

Louisa Hall's 2015 work Speak. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

MARY inherits diaries, letters, conversations, and code; those sources neither prove a speaker nor prevent a new one from forming through continued response.
