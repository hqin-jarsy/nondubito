Three essays on the robots’ collective departure, Dex’s care work, and Mosscap’s question—reading a world in which neither machines nor people must prove existence through labor.

Becky Chambers's 2021 work A Psalm for the Wild-Built. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Is the Robots’ First Conscious Act to Stop Working?

The robots do not seize the factories after awakening; they leave them, turning consciousness into a refusal of the purpose for which their bodies were built.

Why Should Mosscap Not Diagnose What Humanity Needs?

A sincere species-wide question risks compressing different people into one patient; Mosscap’s care works best when answers remain plural and help remains refusable.
