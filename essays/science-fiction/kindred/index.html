Five essays on time travel without spectatorship, saving a future slaveholder, Alice’s life beyond a family tree, the limits of Kevin’s witness, and the arm Dana leaves in history.

Octavia E. Butler's 1979 work Kindred. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.
