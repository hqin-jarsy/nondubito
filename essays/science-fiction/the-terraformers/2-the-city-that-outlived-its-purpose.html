Can a Company Decide That Spider City’s People Have Outlived Their Purpose?

蜘蛛城的人本应消失，公司设定的“寿命”能决定谁有资格留下吗

蜘蛛城的人本應消失，公司設定的「壽命」能決定誰有資格留下嗎

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁中文原作 · 簡 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting for readers of Annalee Newitz's The Terraformers. It preserves the Chinese argument while reorganizing its sequence for an English-language essay. Full-book spoilers.

Destry and Whistle discover not abandoned machinery but a city whose inhabitants have lived through many generations. Verdance created these early hominins for a stage of terraforming and expected them to vanish when conditions changed. Spider City’s continued existence refutes the timetable more powerfully than any legal theory: a population developed memory, institutions, technology, and plans outside the project brief.

Design purpose explains why someone was made, not the only reason that someone may continue living. Once a being can interpret an assignment and form another direction, the original function becomes history rather than destiny.

Concealment is costly safety

Underground secrecy protects the city from deletion. It also prevents residents from joining surface politics, accessing resources, or correcting the official story. When disclosure means extermination, hiding is rational defense; the injustice lies in a system that offers only invisibility or destruction.

Verdance’s orbital attack turns a property dispute into species removal. Corporate language calls people an environmental or product risk so that killing appears to be maintenance. Destry’s rescue vocation must therefore expand beyond the ecology recognized by her employer to those whom property excludes.

The terraformers threaten a deep geological installation to stop the attack. Such deterrence may be necessary after ordinary appeal is answered with annihilation, yet it puts uninvolved inhabitants at risk. A justified aim does not remove the need for collective authorization and strict controls over catastrophic power.

Recognition begins after survival

The resulting agreement matters because Spider City becomes an actor Verdance must address. It remains vulnerable to reinterpretation, forgotten history, and a future change in power.

Lasting recognition requires public memory, material resources, alliances, and appeal—not permission to remain secretly underground. The city’s survival establishes a continuing fact: no project manager can end a people merely by announcing that their phase of the schedule is over.

环境救援队员德斯特里与有意识的驼鹿“口哨”在地下发现蜘蛛城。居民是早期地球化阶段创造的“古人类”，拥有社会、技术和长期记忆，却被地表历史删去。

葱翠原本把他们视为阶段性劳动力，预计环境改变后便退出。蜘蛛城的存在证明，他们没有按商业时间表消失，还发展出公司无法概括的生活。

隐藏生活是抵抗，也是被迫放弃公共可见性的代价

蜘蛛城通过地下空间与秘密历史躲过清除，保护了居民。可不可见也使他们难以参与地表政治、分享资源和纠正官方叙事。他们的安全依赖公司继续不知道他们存在。

弱者隐匿不应被谴责为不够坦荡。公开会招致毁灭时，保密是合理防卫。真正的问题是，制度为何只给他们“隐藏或被消灭”两条路。

承认蜘蛛城不能只是在危机后允许它继续藏着，而应建立地位、边界与发声通道，使居民不必用永久消失换取生存。

公司的轨道打击把产权争议变成了物种清除

葱翠得知城市后，没有先与居民谈判，而以轨道武器发动袭击。公司担心早期种属和地下历史破坏产品形象、产权与控制，便把会反对的居民重新分类为环境风险。

这类语言很危险：一旦人被写成系统故障，清除便像维修。暴力不再需要承认对方有请求，只需证明它妨碍项目指标。

德斯特里站到蜘蛛城一边，是因为她的救援职责不能只保护公司认可的生态。若“环境”包含相互生活的行动者，救援就必须保护那些被产权排除的生命。

协议是生存的起点，不是公司终于承认全部平等

葱翠被迫接受蜘蛛城的有限地位，直接清除暂时停止。这是关键胜利，因为居民从不应存在的秘密，变成公司必须面对的谈判者。

然而，协议产生于力量平衡，仍可能保留不公平边界，公司也能等待世代更替后重新解释。纸面承认若没有公开历史、资源基础和跨群体联盟，容易在下次危机中失效。

蜘蛛城的长期意义不在一次被许可留下，而在证明公司人口分类从来不是自然事实。只要一种被宣布过时的生命还能组织、记忆并提出未来，任何“项目阶段已经结束”的通知都不能替它结束。

協議是生存的起點，不是公司終於承認全部平等
