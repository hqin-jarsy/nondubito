Reading The Terraformers

《The Terraformers》解读

《The Terraformers》解讀

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体英文 · 簡體 · 繁體

Five essays spanning sixty thousand years, from planetary property and obsolete residents to public rail, multispecies personhood, and resistance across generations.

Annalee Newitz's 2023 work The Terraformers. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Does Terraforming a Planet Give a Company the Right to Own Its World?

Verdance invests across geological time, but construction cannot become permanent sovereignty over residents, descendants, atmosphere, and every future use of the planet.

II
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Can a Company Decide That Spider City’s People Have Outlived Their Purpose?

The underground city proves that an engineered population did not expire on schedule; hiding preserved its life but cannot substitute for public standing and security.

2026Three reading modes
III
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Does a Public Railway Threaten a Planetary Real-Estate Company?

Transport changes where communities can exist and whether residents can leave employers, landlords, and towns; mobility is therefore a distribution of political power.

2026Three reading modes
IV
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Who May Decide Whether a Cat, Moose, Robot, or Train Is a Person?

Speech makes some engineered beings easier to recognize, but basic protection cannot depend on resemblance to humans or certification by the company that benefits from exclusion.

2026Three reading modes
V
English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Why Does Resistance Have to Begin Again Across Sixty Thousand Years?

Victories preserve cities, routes, archives, and political capacity without ending domination; durable institutions must make revision easier than inherited property makes command.
