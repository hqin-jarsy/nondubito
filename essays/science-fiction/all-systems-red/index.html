Three essays on the freedom to watch serials, protection becoming a chosen relationship, and why Murderbot leaves even after Mensah offers safety and legal freedom.

Martha Wells's 2017 work All Systems Red. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.

English · 简 / 繁 · Science fiction
Murderbot Hacks Its Governor Module. Why Is Its First Freedom the Freedom to Watch Serial Dramas?

Private entertainment is not a waste of liberation; it is the first chance to direct attention away from surveillance, usefulness, and the demand to prove it is safe.

Mensah Gives Murderbot Freedom. Why Does It Still Choose to Leave?

A generous offer changes its legal position, but safety can come with a new social role, and leaving is how Murderbot tests whether freedom includes an unwritten future.
