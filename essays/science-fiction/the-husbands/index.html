Four essays on infinitely refreshed spouses, deleted shared lives, the fantasy of a perfect match, and the commitment Lauren makes by burning the attic.

Holly Gramazio's 2024 work The Husbands. Every essay in this series discusses the complete story and contains full spoilers.
