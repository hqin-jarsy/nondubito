Reading March Comes in Like a Lion

《三月的狮子》解读

Aug 31, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Five essays on an unspoken condition of adoption, a deliberately lost match, a household that asks for nothing, standing beside the bullied, and whether the skill that secured a home ever became Rei's own.

March Comes in Like a Lion makes decisive ethical structures out of meals, matches, school corridors, rent, and the quiet calculations by which a child tries to deserve a place. No villain designs Rei's childhood. Harm emerges from good intentions organized around one narrow axis of value.

These essays follow the first two anime seasons while drawing on the manga where useful. They resist calling the Kawamoto household a cure: care is real, the family's burdens remain, and no relationship is asked to solve every wound. Spoilers through season two follow.

五篇从收养中没说出口的条件与一盘故意输掉的棋出发，读一个什么也不要的家、站到被霸凌者身边的代价，以及将棋究竟有没有成为零自己的东西。

《三月的狮子》从饭桌、棋局、学校走廊、房租，以及一个孩子计算自己凭什么留下的安静动作里，写出决定性的伦理结构。没有恶人设计零的童年；伤害从善意沿着一根过窄的价值轴运转出来。

这五篇以动画前两季为主，必要处参照漫画；不把川本家写成治愈一切的解药——照护是真的，各人的负担也仍然存在，没有一段关系被要求解决全部伤口。剧透至第二季。
