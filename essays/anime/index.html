日本动漫与漫画

Aug 31, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Fourteen series · 67 essays十四个系列 · 六十七篇

Anime and manga can sustain a philosophical problem across battles, meals, school terms, political systems, and years of character time. These readings treat the medium on its own terms: serial form, visual repetition, world-building, and the long consequences of a choice.

日本动漫与漫画能够让一个哲学问题穿过战斗、饭桌、学期、政治制度与人物的多年时间持续展开。这里不把它们当作文学的附属，而按连载形式、视觉重复、世界建构与选择的长期后果来细读。
