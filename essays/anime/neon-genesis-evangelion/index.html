Reading Neon Genesis Evangelion

《新世纪福音战士》解读

Aug 31, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Five essays on why Shinji pilots, the replaceable person, Asuka's falling number, Gendō's attempt to recover one person, and the wall that both hurts and makes relation possible.

Evangelion is often described as a story of damaged people unable to connect. These essays ask a more structural question: what forms of connection are offered to them, what do those forms demand, and when does recognition become another way of using a person?

The TV series and The End of Evangelion supply the primary ending discussed here. Rebuild continuity is outside the argument. Full spoilers follow.

五篇从真嗣开机的理由出发，读可替换的人、明日香不断下落的数字、碇源堂想找回的那一个人，以及既造成痛苦又让关系成为可能的墙。

《新世纪福音战士》常被概括成一群受伤的人无法互相连接。这五篇换一个结构问题：作品给他们什么样的连接，这些连接要求什么，以及识认在什么时候会变成另一种使用。

以TV版与《The End of Evangelion》为主要文本，不纳入新剧场版连续性。全文剧透。
