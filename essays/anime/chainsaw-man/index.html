Reading Chainsaw Man

《电锯人》解读

Aug 31, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Five essays on hunger, manipulation, single-purpose lives, the moment control fails, and two pieces of white bread at opposite ends of a story.

Chainsaw Man gives Denji desires so small that readers can mistake them for freedom. Food, shelter, touch, and affection are real needs; precisely because they are real, anyone who supplies them can learn where to place a hand on his life.

These essays read Part 1 through that vulnerability. They do not turn Denji into a fool or Makima into an abstract symbol. The question is how care, appetite, purpose, and control can share the same visible form—and how an ordinary life grows beyond the list used to manage it. Full spoilers follow.

五篇从涂果酱的面包与一张愿望清单出发，读到操纵的把手、单一目的、支配失效的一刻，以及故事两端的两片白面包。

《电锯人》给电次的愿望小得让人误以为那就是自由：食物、住处、触碰与被爱都是真实需要；也正因为真实，谁能供应它们，谁就知道把手放在他人生的什么位置。

这五篇沿着这份脆弱性读第一部，不把电次写成傻子，也不把玛奇玛化约成一个符号；问题是照护、欲望、目的与控制如何拥有同一种外观，以及日常怎样长出愿望清单里没有的东西。全文剧透。
