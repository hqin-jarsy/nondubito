Reading The Intouchables

《触不可及》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on care without pity, trust across fixed social descriptions, and the difficult boundary between opening a door and pushing someone through it.

The friendship in The Intouchables is often described as one man teaching another how to live. The film is more interesting when neither becomes the other's cure. These essays examine what ordinary argument restores, what trust actually risks, and where affectionate interference begins to threaten the freedom it hopes to revive.

Based on Olivier Nakache and Éric Toledano's 2011 film The Intouchables, inspired by Philippe Pozzo di Borgo and Abdel Sellou. Plot details have been checked against the film transcript and closing titles.

三篇解读：没有怜悯姿态的照护、越过社会定论的信任，以及替人打开门与把人推进去之间那条困难的边界。

《触不可及》常被概括成一个人教另一个人重新生活。可当两个人都不被写成对方的药，这段关系才更有意思。三篇文章分别追问：普通的争吵恢复了什么，信任真正冒了什么风险，以及善意的介入从哪里开始威胁它想唤回的自由。

依据奥利维埃·纳卡什与埃里克·托莱达诺执导的二〇一一年电影《触不可及》，故事取材于菲利普·波佐·迪博尔戈与阿卜杜勒·塞卢；关键情节已参照影片对白与片尾字幕校订。
