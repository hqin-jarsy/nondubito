Reading Scent of a Woman

《闻香识女人》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on truth priced by institutional power, a final trip that requires a guide but rejects a witness, and two different forms of refusal.

Scent of a Woman places Charlie between adults who know what he cannot afford to lose. The film becomes sharper when its famous speeches do not settle every question: informing can be truthful, silence can protect privilege, preventing suicide can cross boundaries, and polished help can still be coercion.

Based on Martin Brest's 1992 film Scent of a Woman. The account corrects a prior interpretive error: Frank needs Charlie as a sighted guide and sends him away before attempting suicide; the film does not establish that he hired the boy as a witness to his death.

三篇解读：被制度权力标价的真相、需要向导却拒绝见证人的最后旅程，以及两种并不相同的拒绝。

《闻香识女人》把查理放在几个知道他最怕失去什么的成年人之间。若不让著名演说替所有问题封口，电影会更锋利：告发可以说真话，沉默也可能保护特权，阻止自杀会碰到边界，而体面的帮助仍可能带着强迫。

依据马丁·布莱斯执导的一九九二年电影《闻香识女人》；校订中特别纠正一处旧解读：史莱德需要查理充当有视力的向导，并在自杀前将他支开，影片没有说他雇孩子是为死亡作见证。
