Control What Enters the Record

他们只需要控制他写什么

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting for readers of Memento. It preserves the Chinese argument while reorganizing its sequence for an English-language essay. Full-film spoilers.

Control What Enters the Record

Natalie hides every pen in the room before she begins. Then she tells Leonard, to his face, that she intends to use him. She insults his dead wife until he strikes her. She walks outside and sits in her car, waiting for his memory to clear.

Leonard understands what is happening only for a few desperate minutes. He searches for something to write with. He knows the event is about to disappear and cannot preserve it.

When Natalie returns with a bruised mouth, she tells him that a man named Dodd has just beaten her. Leonard sees an injured woman and no record that contradicts her. Soon his own system will convert her account into instructions.

Manipulation at the point of entry

Natalie does not forge a Polaroid or alter a tattoo. She blocks one event from entering the archive, waits for the witness to vanish, and supplies a replacement narrative. Leonard performs the rest of the labor himself: listening, writing, identifying Dodd, and acting.

Her confession is especially cruel because it demonstrates that truth alone has no power to protect him. She can accurately announce the manipulation while the words still disappear. Disclosure without memory leaves no accountable listener.

Teddy uses a related method. He knows Leonard's routines and repeatedly offers names, explanations, and directions at moments when they may shape the next surviving note. He does not need to persuade a continuous person over time. He can address the archive through brief windows of access.

The two manipulators differ. Natalie wants Dodd removed; Teddy, a corrupt police officer, appears to redirect Leonard's vengeance toward criminals such as drug dealer Jimmy Grantz. Their positions are the same in one crucial respect: each understands that controlling the input can make Leonard's later actions look self-authored.

A system designed not to reconsider

Leonard created external memory so he would not have to solve his life from nothing every few minutes. Once a proposition has been adequately recorded, reconsidering it would defeat the purpose. Discipline is what makes the system useful.

Discipline also makes it programmable.

Someone with continuous memory can compare today's account with yesterday's, notice a changed tone, or recall that a trusted person behaved strangely. Leonard sometimes questions and improvises, but the written record carries extraordinary weight because it is the only bridge he can deliberately send forward.

That is why Natalie's hidden pens matter more than a sophisticated counterfeit. She attacks the recording threshold itself.

Trusting yesterday's self

Leonard is not foolish. He understands verification, distinguishes “facts” from “conclusions,” and tries to protect himself against instruction. His deepest assumption is nevertheless unavailable for inspection: the earlier Leonard who wrote the note is treated as an ally of the later one.

Usually that assumption is reasonable. Without it, he could not coordinate a life at all. But “same body” does not guarantee “same purpose.” Anger, fear, pride, or the need for meaning may lead the author to prepare a future the reader would not choose if he possessed the missing context.

Natalie and Teddy exploit the gap between author and reader. The final essay goes further. It shows Leonard recognizing the gap and using it himself.

At that point, the story is no longer primarily about a disabled man deceived by others. It becomes a story about self-government when one version of a person can write binding law for another—and the second has no court of appeal.

一间酒吧的后屋。一个女人和一个男人在说话。

女人忽然开始骂人，骂得很难听，骂到他妻子头上，骂了很久。男人忍不住了，一巴掌打了过去。女人捂着脸走出去，坐进车里，等着。

一、她在等他忘记

娜塔莉在车里坐了很久，她知道要等多长时间——她知道这个人的记忆能撑几分钟。时间到了，她走回去，嘴角肿着，眼睛红着，哭着告诉莱纳德：刚才有个叫多德的男人来过，打了她。

莱纳德什么都不记得。他看见的是一个刚刚被打过的女人，而这件事在他手里的任何一份记录上都没有说明。他信了。

二、她先把笔藏了起来

而在骂他之前，她做了一件更要紧的事：她把屋里所有的笔都收走了。

莱纳德挨了那一巴掌之后，第一反应是掏纸条——他要把刚才发生的事记下来。他摸遍全身，翻遍桌子，找不到一支笔。他知道自己马上会忘，他知道唯一的办法是写下来，而他写不了。 镜头拍了他慌乱地找笔的样子，那是全片最难受的一段：一个人眼睁睁看着一件事从自己身上流走，而他阻止不了。

请把娜塔莉做的事情拆开看。她没有催眠他，没有下药，没有伪造任何一份文件；她做的只有两步——让他记不下来那件对她不利的事，然后在他失去记忆之后给他一个新的说法。而这两步都不需要她动他的系统，那套系统会自己完成剩下的工作：莱纳德会把她的说法写下来，会拍照，会在背面注明，然后按照那行字去找多德。她不需要控制他，她只需要控制进入那套系统的东西。

有一场戏，娜塔莉把话挑明了。她当着莱纳德的面说：她要利用他，她已经在利用他了，而说完之后他就会忘记。她甚至告诉了他自己的动机。莱纳德坐在那里听着，几分钟以后这段对话消失了。她说的每一句都是真话，而这不妨碍她继续骗他。

三、泰迪做的是同一件事

泰迪是另一个人。他是警察，跟莱纳德认识很久了，他做的事情看上去是帮忙：提供线索，指出方向，告诉他下一个该查谁。

而他也在利用这套系统——他每一次出现，都是在往那套档案里放东西。有一处细节值得注意：泰迪对莱纳德的状态非常熟悉，他知道什么时候该说什么，知道哪些话会被记下来，哪些话说了等于没说。他跟这个人打交道的方式，是跟那套档案打交道。

所以娜塔莉和泰迪用的是同一个办法。他们都发现了同一件事：莱纳德当然仍会临场反应、怀疑和推断，但记录在他的判断中拥有压倒性的分量。 想让他做什么，不需要说服他，只需要在合适的时刻让某样东西进入他的记录。娜塔莉的做法是藏笔加编故事，泰迪的做法是提供线索加口头引导——形式不同，位置相同，都是在那个"记什么"的环节上动手。

四、那套系统本来是保护他的

第一篇写过：莱纳德造那套系统，是为了对付自己的记忆缺陷；他不信自己的脑子，所以把判断固化成文字，让未来的自己直接执行。这个设计的核心是"不要再重新判断"。

而正是这一点，让他可以被编程。一个拥有连续记忆的人还能把今天的说法与昨天对照，察觉口气与情节的变化；而莱纳德把判断跨时间交给记录，一旦入口被污染，后来的自己就很难找回已经消失的反证。

片中有一处，莱纳德差一点看穿了。他在娜塔莉家里过夜，第二天早上看着她，隐约觉得哪里不对。而他手里那张照片背面写着的，是他自己前一天写下的话，那行字说她是可以信任的。他照做了。

五、他没有设的那道防线

必须说清楚：莱纳德不笨。他是受过训练的调查员，他懂得怎么核实信息，他甚至给自己设过防线——他在纸条上写过不要接受电话里的指示。

而他能想到的防线全部指向外面：防别人骗他，防自己忘记，防信息在传递中失真。他没有设一道防线，去防那个写字的人。 因为那个人是他自己。

下一篇会写到，在某一个时刻，那个写字的人做了一件他完全清楚自己在做什么的事。
