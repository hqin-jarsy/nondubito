Reading Yi Yi

《一一》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on reciprocal vision, the distance between judgment and action, and a family that reserves space for speech only when an answer is no longer possible.

Yi Yi begins with a wedding and ends with a funeral, but its structure is less a circle than a collection of partial views. Yang-Yang’s photographs do not complete other people; they offer them a perspective unavailable from where they stand.

The adults are not inert. Their lives are crowded with work, care, guilt, and choices. The question is whether these activities still form a life each person can recognize and answer for—and whether another person is allowed to interrupt the story they tell themselves.

Edward Yang’s 2000 film Yi Yi. Full-film spoilers throughout.

三篇从洋洋的后脑勺照片出发，读到“替别人看见”和“替别人下定义”的区别，读到判断怎样在妥协中退成旁白，也读到一个家庭为何在回答已经不可能时，才终于给讲述留出位置。

《一一》从婚礼开始，以葬礼结束；它的结构与其说是圆，不如说是一组彼此不完整的视角。洋洋没有用照片补完别人，只把他们自己无法取得的角度递还给他们。

片中的成年人并非无所事事。工作、照料、内疚和选择塞满了生活。电影追问的，是这些活动是否还组成一段当事人能够认领、能够负责的日子，以及另一个人是否有机会打断他对自己的判决。

杨德昌二〇〇〇年《一一》。全片剧透。
