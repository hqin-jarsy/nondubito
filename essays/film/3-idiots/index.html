Reading 3 Idiots

《三傻大闹宝莱坞》解读

Aug 28, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on an institution that converts learning into rank, the unequal risks Farhan and Raju must carry for themselves, and the danger of using Rancho’s later success to justify every uncertain act.

3 Idiots is often reduced to a consoling instruction: follow your passion and success will follow. The film is more interesting than that slogan. Joy Lobo does excellent work and dies before recognition arrives. Chatur succeeds by the system’s standards. Farhan and Raju face costs Rancho cannot absorb for them. Rancho himself helps, intrudes, makes mistakes, and acts without knowing the end.

These readings move from institutional design to personal authorship and then to hindsight. They ask what a college actually produces when it rewards approved language over understanding; what friends may reveal without becoming new directors of a life; and whether an action can be worth doing when later success is neither known nor guaranteed.

三篇从一所把学习改造成排名的学校，读到法尔汉与拉朱必须亲自承担的不同风险，以及为什么不能用兰彻后来的成功，倒过来替他当年所有没有把握的行动作证。

《三傻大闹宝莱坞》常被压缩成一句安慰人的指令：追随热爱，成功自然会来。电影其实比这句话复杂。乔伊做出了优秀的东西，却没有等到认可；查图尔按照制度的标准取得了成功；法尔汉与拉朱面对的代价，兰彻无法替他们承担；兰彻本人也会帮助、越界、犯错，并在不知道结果时行动。

三篇从制度设计写到个人署名，再写到后见之明。它们追问：一所学校奖励规定说法而不是理解时，究竟在生产什么；朋友可以指出什么，又该在哪里停止成为新的生活导演；以及当后来的成功既未知也无保证时，一项行动是否仍能在当下成立。
