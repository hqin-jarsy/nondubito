Reading Modern Times

《摩登时代》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on assembly-line logic carried in a worker’s body, the prisons, factories, streets, and stores through which the Tramp circulates, and the unnamed young woman who can still say what she wants.

Modern Times makes systems visible through bodies. Management appears on screens while labor is divided into gestures so small that a person can be replaced without changing the line. The Tramp carries those gestures beyond the factory because the factory has already entered his nerves.

Yet Chaplin does not remain inside one workplace. His character moves through prison, shipyard, department store, restaurant, strike, and road, gathering people whose common problem is not self-realization but how to reach the next day. The Gamin introduces another question: what could they want beyond survival?

Charlie Chaplin’s 1936 film Modern Times. Full-film spoilers throughout.

三篇从一条长进工人身体里的流水线逻辑，读到流浪汉穿过的监狱、工厂、街道与商店，以及那个没有名字、却仍能说出自己想要什么的女孩。

《摩登时代》通过身体让系统显形。管理者出现在屏幕上，劳动则被拆成小到可以替换人的动作；流浪汉离开工厂后仍继续拧螺丝，因为流水线的命令已经进入他的神经。

卓别林却没有停在一个工作场所。他让人物穿过监狱、船厂、百货公司、餐厅、罢工与公路，带出一群共同面对“怎样撑到明天”的人。那个“街头女孩”则提出另一道题：生存以外，他们还能想要什么？

查理·卓别林一九三六年《摩登时代》。全片剧透。
