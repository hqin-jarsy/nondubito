Reading V for Vendetta

《V字仇杀队》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on a regime that turns people into categories, a liberation imposed without consent, and the unresolved ownership of the final lever.

Three Acts, Three Forms of Power

Norsefire compiles a list, V builds a prison, and Evey moves a lever. Across those three acts, the series asks one question: when someone is certain that he represents the right, can he still leave another person room to refuse, depart, and decide?

The answer does not require treating dictator and dissident as equivalents. Their purposes and consequences differ radically. Only after preserving that difference can we see the subtler boundary the film exposes: a person who fights domination may still decide, in the name of liberation, what somebody else must become.

This series reads James McTeigue's film adaptation as its own text rather than treating it as interchangeable with Alan Moore and David Lloyd's graphic novel.

三篇解读：一个政权怎样先把人改写成类别，一场以自由为名却未经同意的改造，以及那根操作杆究竟属于谁的决定。

三个动作，三种权力

诺斯费尔列名单，V 建牢房，艾薇推下操作杆。三篇沿着这三个动作追问同一件事：当一个人确信自己代表正确，他是否仍愿意给别人留下拒绝、离开和自行决定的空间？

这不是要把独裁者与反抗者简单画上等号。恰恰相反，只有承认他们目的与后果的差异，我们才看得清另一条更隐蔽的边界：反抗支配的人，也可能在解放之名下替别人作主。

本系列以詹姆斯·麦克提格执导的电影版为文本，不把电影与阿兰·摩尔、大卫·劳埃德的漫画原作视为同一作品。
