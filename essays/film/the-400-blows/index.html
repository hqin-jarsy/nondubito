Reading The 400 Blows

《四百击》解读

Sep 4, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on partial reasons accumulating into confinement, tenderness braided with secrecy, and an institutional interview that hears more than the system can hold.

François Truffaut never needs Antoine Doinel to be innocent in order to refuse his reduction to a delinquent. These essays follow the administrative chain around him, the bargain inside maternal tenderness, and the final look that prevents the film from closing his case.

Based on François Truffaut's 1959 French theatrical film The 400 Blows (Les Quatre Cents Coups), black-and-white Dyaliscope, approximately 99 minutes. The French title refers idiomatically to raising hell or getting into every kind of mischief.

I
English · 简 / 繁 · Film criticism
Every Step Has a Reason

A chain of understandable decisions does not become blameless: humiliation, violence, relinquishment, and processing each add something to Antoine's confinement.

The Questioner Stays Offscreen

2026Three reading modes

三篇解读：局部理由怎样累积成羁押、温柔如何与秘密缠在一起，以及一次听见很多却未必容得下这些话的制度访谈。

弗朗索瓦·特吕弗不需要先证明安托万·杜瓦内无辜，才拒绝把他压缩成一个问题少年。三篇文章追踪包围他的处理链条、母亲温柔里的隐性交换，以及让影片拒绝结案的最后一眼。

依据弗朗索瓦·特吕弗执导的一九五九年法国公映版《四百击》，黑白 Dyaliscope 宽银幕，片长约九十九分钟。法语片名来自 faire les quatre cents coups，意近“闯遍各种祸、胡闹个够”，不是字面上的四百次殴打。

每一步都有理由

一串可以理解的决定并不会因此变得无人负责：羞辱、暴力、放弃监护与制度处理，各自把一些东西加进安托万的羁押。
