Reading Witness for the Prosecution

《控方证人》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on a barrister who mistakes professional instinct for access to innocence, a courtroom that must reconstruct an absent event through evidence, and a performance that manufactures the credibility on which a lawful verdict depends.

Witness for the Prosecution is a courtroom mystery, but its deepest trick is not simply that somebody lies. Billy Wilder makes almost everyone speak through an institution that cannot inspect the past directly. A housekeeper’s hearing, a widow’s will, a bloodstain, a marriage, a packet of letters, and a defendant’s manner become meaningful only after rules and advocates arrange them.

These readings begin with Sir Wilfrid Robarts returning from a near-fatal heart attack and accepting the case he first refuses on medical orders. They then distinguish a court’s proper demand for admissible evidence from the impossible demand that it reproduce the murder itself. The final essay follows the letters purchased for forty pounds at Euston Station and stops before the disclosure that changes what every earlier performance meant.

三篇写一个把职业直觉误当成无罪判断的律师、一间只能通过证据重建缺席事件的法庭，以及一场怎样生产可信度、最终让合法判决建立其上的表演。

《控方证人》是一部法庭悬疑片，但它最深的机关不只是“有人撒谎”。比利·怀尔德让几乎所有人都通过一个无法直接检查过去的制度说话。管家的听力、寡妇的遗嘱、血迹、婚姻、一叠信和被告的举止，只有经过规则与律师的安排，才在法庭上取得意义。

三篇从韦菲爵士心脏病后回到办公室写起：他起初遵医嘱拒绝案件，见过克里斯汀以后才正式接手。接着区分法庭正当地审查证据，与法庭不可能亲自重演谋杀之间的距离。最后一篇追到尤斯顿车站那叠以四十英镑买来的信，并在改变所有既有表演意义的最终揭示之前停下。
