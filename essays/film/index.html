Cinema

电影

Aug 28, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Four series · Twelve essays · Three reading modes四个系列 · 十二篇 · 英 / 简 / 繁

A film does not only tell a story. It arranges sight, sound, duration, power, and the distance between one person and another. These close readings begin with plot but ask what the camera and the structure make visible that a summary cannot.

电影不只讲述故事，也安排目光、声音、时间、权力，以及人与人之间的距离。这里从情节出发，却更关心镜头与结构让什么显形，而这些东西无法被剧情梗概替代。

《肖申克的救赎》解读
Reading The Shawshank Redemption

三篇从布鲁克斯之死、安迪的有用与瑞德的假释，重读体制如何把人变成功能，以及一个人如何在用途之外重新出现。

Three essays on institutionalization, usefulness, and the forms of recognition that remain when a person is no longer treated as a function.

《霸王别姬》解读
Reading Farewell My Concubine

三篇从被削到合格的小豆子、被打进身体的“女娇娥”，读到菊仙如何赎回自己，并试着把关系带到戏外。

《阿甘正传》解读
Reading Forrest Gump

三篇从不断被规格判定的阿甘，读到丹中尉如何重领自己的生命，以及电影怎样既看见珍妮的痛，又把她压进别人的故事。

Three essays on a man repeatedly judged by categories, a soldier who outlives an inherited destiny, and the woman whose inner life the film both recognizes and withholds.

Reading Titanic
