电影

Aug 28, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Sixteen series · Forty-eight essays · Three reading modes十六个系列 · 四十八篇 · 英 / 简 / 繁

A film does not only tell a story. It arranges sight, sound, duration, power, and the distance between one person and another. These close readings begin with plot but ask what the camera and the structure make visible that a summary cannot.

电影不只讲述故事，也安排目光、声音、时间、权力，以及人与人之间的距离。这里从情节出发，却更关心镜头与结构让什么显形，而这些东西无法被剧情梗概替代。
