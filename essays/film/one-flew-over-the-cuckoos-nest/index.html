Reading One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest

《飞越疯人院》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on procedure that hides its author, pleasures the ward cannot administer, and an escape that restores agency without becoming a clean moral victory.

These readings do not reduce the film to a contest between a good rebel and an evil nurse. McMurphy is manipulative, violent, and serving time after sex with a minor; Nurse Ratched often speaks through recognizable therapeutic and administrative responsibilities. The sharper question concerns what their positions allow.

The ward makes order legible and recovery ambiguous. Speech becomes record, voluntary patients face choices they do not yet feel able to carry, and professional language can hide a coercive judgment as neutral concern.

Miloš Forman’s 1975 film One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest. Full-film spoilers throughout.

三篇从程序怎样隐藏判断的作者，读到病房为何容不下未经管理的快乐，也读到一次恢复行动能力却并不纯净的逃离。

这里不把电影简化成好反叛者与坏护士的对决。麦克墨菲会操控人、会使用暴力，也因与未成年人发生关系而服刑；拉契特护士的许多话则来自可辨认的治疗与管理职责。更尖锐的问题是，他们各自的位置允许什么。

病房让秩序易于衡量，却让康复难以衡量；发言变成记录，自愿入院不等于已有离开的能力，而专业语言也可能把一次精确的强迫伪装成中性的关心。

米洛斯·福尔曼一九七五年《飞越疯人院》。全片剧透。
