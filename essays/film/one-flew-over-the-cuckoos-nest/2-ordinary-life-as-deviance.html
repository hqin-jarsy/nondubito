Essay II of III · One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest《飞越疯人院》解读 · II / III

Ordinary Life as Deviance

没有信号，比赛仍然发生了

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)Independent English edition中文原作 · 简 / 繁

Edition note: An independent English rewriting for viewers of Miloš Forman’s 1975 film One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest. It preserves the Chinese argument while reorganizing its sequence for an English-language essay. Full-film spoilers.

McMurphy is not a clean liberator. He fakes mental illness to escape prison labor and carries a record of violence and sexual misconduct. The film does not require moral innocence for a person to expose an institution’s structure.

I. A reasonable request

He asks to alter the schedule for the World Series. Ratched answers reasonably: routine stabilizes patients, and sudden change may disturb them. She offers a vote. The democratic form matters because the conflict will not be between obvious tyranny and freedom.

McMurphy gathers nine votes among eighteen patients, then coaxes Chief Bromden’s raised hand. Ratched declares the meeting already closed. Her interpretation is procedurally available. Its timing makes clear that she controls not only the rule but when the rule can still count.

II. Baseball without a broadcast

McMurphy sits before the dark television and begins calling an imaginary game. Patients gather, shout, and celebrate a home run no screen displays. They damage nothing and leave the schedule formally unchanged.

The scene is intolerable to the ward because the group has produced a shared event that the ward did not authorize. The institution can control the television signal but not the meaning the patients create around its absence.

III. The pattern of ordinary pleasures

Cards, basketball, fishing, alcohol, sex, and a party are reckless in context, yet they are not organized as a political program. McMurphy brings habits from ordinary life into a place where almost every unscheduled desire is classified as disruption.

The ward reveals itself by converting familiar pleasures into symptoms and security incidents. McMurphy does not always oppose authority deliberately. He simply keeps acting as though his day belongs to him.

IV. The open door

He later learns that many men are voluntary patients. They can request discharge. They remain because the ward relieves them of decisions they find frightening: schedules are given, mistakes interpreted, responsibilities deferred.

Freedom is therefore not the missing legal permission. It is the difficult practice of deciding what to do with a day and accepting what follows. McMurphy cannot simply hand that practice to somebody else by declaring him free.

V. The asymmetry

The men most afraid to leave are legally able to go. McMurphy, who most wants the outside, is committed through the authority he attempted to exploit. His performance of illness places him within an institution empowered to decide when the performance has ended.

The ward’s door is open for people who cannot yet author their exit and closed for the man who assumed authorship would always remain his. That reversal drives the tragedy.

版本说明：中文原作；简体与繁体同页切换。片名、人名与关键情节已按米洛斯·福尔曼一九七五年《飞越疯人院》上映版校订。全片剧透。

麦克墨菲不是一位干净的解放者。他为逃避监狱农场的苦役而装病，案底里还有暴力与法定强奸；他带来的快乐也常常建立在冒险、欺骗和替别人作主之上。

可一个有严重问题的人，仍然可能准确碰到制度的问题。电影的力量就在于，它没有要求我们先把麦克墨菲封成英雄，才允许病房里的窒息成立。

一、一场看似公平的表决

他想改作息看世界大赛。拉契特解释固定日程对病人的稳定很重要，然后同意表决。理由合理，形式也民主。

麦克墨菲终于凑到足够的手，却被告知会议已经结束。争议不只是哪一票算不算，而是谁能决定投票何时仍然有效。参与者获得了举手的权利，主持者保留了定义结果的权力。

二、关掉电视，比赛仍然发生了

麦克墨菲坐在黑屏前，开始解说一场不存在的转播。其他人慢慢围过来，为看不见的本垒打欢呼。他们没有打开电视，也没有改变日程，却共同制造出一个病房没有安排的事件。

这一幕之所以痛快，不是想象战胜了现实，而是意义短暂地不再由机构发放。护士站可以控制信号，却不能规定人们必须怎样理解一块黑屏。

三、日常快乐并不因此自动正确

打牌、篮球、钓鱼、酒和派对，把院外的生活带了进来，也确实让一些人第一次主动参与。但这些行为并非全都无害：麦克墨菲会操纵、会冒险，也低估了别人要承受的后果。

重要的不是“凡是违规都值得赞美”，而是机构把未经安排的愿望过早地只读成症状和事故。一个人需要练习选择，也需要有机会在失败以后承担、修正，而不是永远由别人替他避免生活。

四、自愿入院不等于已经能够离开

麦克墨菲震惊地得知，病房里一些人是自愿住院的。他把法律上的许可直接理解成“想走就走”，于是无法理解他们为什么留下。

可门没有上锁，不代表人已经拥有走出去所需的信心、关系和生活条件。长期被羞耻与恐惧支配的人，不会因为另一个人高喊“你自由了”就突然能够承担自由。帮助不能只替对方推开门，还要承认跨过门槛必须是他的动作。

五、最讽刺的交换

一些不敢离开的人保留着提出出院的可能；最确信自己随时能走的麦克墨菲，却发现院方可以继续收治他。他原本把装病当成一场由自己掌控的表演，没想到解释表演是否结束的权力已经不在自己手里。

他带给病友的并不是一份可以转交的自由，而是一次经验：一天可以临时有另一种过法，一群人也可以共同产生未经批准的意义。经验不会替任何人完成离开，却让“我只能这样”第一次出现裂缝。

病房最不能容忍的，未必是公开反抗，而是有人在不请求定义的情况下开始生活。但电影也提醒我们：唤醒另一个人的愿望，不等于取得替他决定风险与代价的资格。
