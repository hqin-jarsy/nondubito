Reading The Attorney

《辩护人》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays on a commercially minded lawyer with no reason to become a dissident, the familiar face that makes abstraction impossible, and a political trial in which every evidentiary point can be won without changing the verdict.

The Attorney protects its protagonist from retrospective sainthood. Song Woo-seok begins as a self-made practitioner selling routine registration work, proud of wealth earned without elite credentials and dismissive of student activism. That beginning is not an embarrassment to be edited out. It gives his later decisions their weight.

The series follows a change in the object of knowledge. “Political detainees” can remain a category; Park Jin-woo’s injured face cannot. It then asks what advocacy means when proof, procedure, and constitutional language are heard by a court without being allowed to govern it.

Yang Woo-suk’s 2013 film The Attorney. Full-film spoilers throughout.

三篇从一个完全没有理由成为异议者的商业律师，读到一张熟悉的脸如何让抽象知识失效，以及一场每项证据都能赢、判决却依旧输掉的政治审判。

《辩护人》没有让主人公在回忆中提前成圣。宋佑硕起初靠房产登记业务挣钱，为没有精英学历却能白手起家而骄傲，也反感学生运动。这个开头不是该删去的尴尬，正是它赋予后来选择以分量。

三篇追踪的是知识对象怎样改变：“政治犯”可以只是一个类别，朴镇宇受伤的脸却不行；继而追问，当证据、程序与宪法语言都被法庭听见、却不被允许支配裁判时，辩护究竟还意味着什么。

杨宇硕二〇一三年《辩护人》。全片剧透。
