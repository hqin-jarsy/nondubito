Reading Shoplifters

《小偷家族》解读

Aug 29, 2026Han Qin (秦汉)English · Simplified · Traditional英文 · 简体 · 繁体

Three essays that first balance the household’s compromised accounts, then examine a family whose admission rule is simply presence, and finally ask what becomes of care that has no lawful name.

Shoplifters refuses the clean opposition between warm chosen family and cold legal family. The Shibatas steal, conceal a death, misuse a pension, teach a child theft, and keep children who are not theirs. They also recognize burns, feed whoever is at the table, and create relations that cannot be dismissed as unreal.

The order of the readings matters. Sentiment must not erase material exploitation; legal classification must not erase experienced care. Kore-eda holds both records open at once.

Hirokazu Kore-eda’s 2018 film Shoplifters. Full-film spoilers throughout.

三篇先把这个家庭妥协而脆弱的账算清，再考察一个以“已经在这里”为唯一入场条件的家，最后追问没有法律名称的照料会怎样。

《小偷家族》拒绝把温暖的选择家庭与冰冷的法律家庭做成干净对立。柴田一家偷窃、隐瞒死亡、冒领年金、教孩子偷东西，也留下不属于自己的孩子；他们同时认出烫伤，让已经坐在桌边的人有饭吃，并建立无法简单说成虚假的关系。

三篇的顺序很重要：感情不能抹掉物质剥削，法律分类也不能抹掉已经发生的照料。是枝裕和让两份记录同时保持打开。

是枝裕和二〇一八年《小偷家族》。全片剧透。
