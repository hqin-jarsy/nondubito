War Theory

War Theory · SAE
战争论 · SAE

战争是目的王国必须处理、却最不希望发生的那件事。对它保持沉默，等于把这个场域整个让给别的话语去主宰。而一旦要说，就得先说清楚一件事：这个系列真正的主角不是战争，是涵育——主体性在时间中持续深化、互相生长的那个过程。战争在这张地图上位置很低，它是涵育被打断时不得不启用的兜底。

七篇散文改写自《SAE战争论：涵育如何不被战争本身吞噬》。从判准的地基开始（不是正义，是结构），经过"上兵伐谋"之上还有四级的那条序列、"不打赢、不打输"的目标函数、谋与诡的四级分流，到四条以"不得不"起头的定理，再到胜利、失败、均势三种结束形态与反玉碎的硬核立场，最后让位给涵育本身——并顺带回答一个看上去毫不相干的问题：宇宙为什么是安静的。

学术原文有一条纪律：正文不触碰任何具体的人类战争事件。散文是教育层，会用时间距离足够远的历史实例，但只用来让抽象结构变得可以看见，不用来判断谁对谁错。

正篇七篇之后，是兵法接口九篇：让 14DD 那一侧的战争哲学自己说话，逐一对接康德、克劳塞维茨与《论持久战》、老子与《司马法》与《墨子》、正义战争论传统与卢梭、孙子、甘地、新渡户，最后结账。

谈战争，为的是和平。

War is the thing the Kingdom of Ends must handle and least wants to happen. Staying silent about it hands the whole field over to other discourses. And once you do speak, one thing has to be settled first: the protagonist of this series is not war but cultivation — the process by which subjectivity deepens and grows into other subjectivity over time. War sits very low on that map. It is the backstop invoked only when cultivation has been broken.

These seven essays rewrite SAE War Treatise: How Cultivation Is Not Swallowed by War Itself. They begin with the ground the standards rest on — structure, not justice — then move through the sequence with four levels above "the highest use of arms attacks strategy," the objective function of not losing rather than winning, and the four-way split between strategy and deceit; then the four theorems that all begin with "cannot not"; then victory, defeat, and stalemate, and the refusal of collective self-annihilation. The last essay yields the ground back to cultivation — and answers, in passing, a question that looks entirely unrelated: why the universe is quiet.

The academic original holds a hard line: no specific human war event appears in its main text. These essays are the educational layer. They use historical examples far enough away in time, but only to make an abstract structure visible, never to rule on who was right.

After the seven come the nine interface essays: the war philosophy of the 14DD side speaking for itself — Kant; Clausewitz and On Protracted War; Laozi, the Sima Fa and the Mozi; the just war tradition and Rousseau; Sunzi; Gandhi; Nitobe — and then the reckoning.

About war, for the sake of peace.

第一组 · 正篇
Group One · Main Sequence

战争论 · 七篇

The War Treatise · Seven Essays

改写自《SAE 战争论：涵育如何不被战争本身吞噬》。从判准的地基一路写到四条定理、三种结束形态，最后让位给涵育本身。

Rewritten from SAE War Treatise: How Cultivation Is Not Swallowed by War Itself. From the ground the standards rest on, through the four theorems and the three forms of ending, and finally yielding the ground back to cultivation.

  • 第一篇 · 引 Essay 1 · Prologue
    不情愿的一篇
    The Reluctant Treatise
    写给不得不进入战争、却仍希望自己不滑离"人是目的"的那个人。真正的主角是涵育；判准的地基不是正义，是结构；判准向内用是反思，向外用就滑向了它要反对的东西。
    Written for the person who cannot avoid a war and still hopes not to drift from persons-as-ends. The protagonist is cultivation; the ground is structure, not justice; a standard pointed inward is reflection, pointed outward it becomes what it opposed.
  • 第二篇 Essay 2
    上兵伐谋之上
    Above the Highest Use of Arms
    伐谋之上还有伐辩、伐思、不伐、不兵四级，每上一级涵育占的空间就更大。而战争根本不在这条序列里——它是五级全部失效后的兜底。
    Above attacking strategy lie disputation, self-thought, non-attack, and no-arms — each giving cultivation more room. And war is not on the sequence at all: it is the backstop when all five fail.
  • 第三篇 Essay 3
    不打赢，不打输
    Not to Win, But Not to Lose
    战争的完成永远在战争之外。真正的输是在过程中打断自己文明的涵育——所以一场战争可以军事上全胜而结构上早已输掉。不战也是备战：平时深化涵育才是最好的准备。
    A war completes outside itself. Real losing is breaking your own civilization's cultivation along the way — so a war can be won militarily and lost structurally. Not fighting is also preparing: deepening cultivation in peacetime is the best preparation there is.
  • 第四篇 Essay 4
    谋、阴谋、阳谋、诡
    Strategy, Scheme, Open Play, and Deceit
    阳谋的力量来自结构位势差而不是信息差，所以时间站在它这一边。诡的要害不在骗对方——在于它必须对己方隐瞒，而这锁死了整条上行序列。
    Open play draws its force from structural position rather than an information gap, so time is on its side. Deceit's crux is not fooling the enemy but concealing from one's own — and that locks down the entire upward sequence.
  • 第五篇 · 核心 Essay 5 · Core
    四个"不得不"
    Four Cannot-Nots
    不得不开战、不得不扩大、不得不以结束为方向、不得不被追问。四条同心圆式的约束，从动作的启动一路穿透到战争方作为主体存在的形态本身。
    Cannot not start, cannot not expand, cannot not be directed toward ending, cannot not be open to questioning. Four concentric constraints, penetrating from the initiation of an act down to the very form in which a warring party exists as a subject.
  • 第六篇 Essay 6
    胜利、失败、均势
    Victory, Defeat, Stalemate
    胜利方的两条义务与代替解放的防火墙；失败不等于输，以及为什么玉碎在结构上永远不合格；均势里最隐蔽的毒性叫镜像同化——你没在战场上输，却在对峙中变成了对面的镜像。
    A victor's two obligations and the firewall against liberating on someone's behalf; why defeat is not losing and self-annihilation is never structurally adequate; and the hidden toxicity of stalemate — you never lost on the battlefield, but across the standoff you became their mirror.
  • 第七篇 · 压轴 Essay 7 · Capstone
    它们在那里等着
    They Are Waiting
    写完就让开，把注意力交还给涵育。真正走完序列的文明不会更显眼，会更隐——所以宇宙安静这件事，问的不是"他们在哪里"，是"我们在哪里"。
    Finish, then step aside, and hand attention back to cultivation. A civilization that walks the sequence to its end grows quieter, not louder — so the silence of the universe asks not where they are, but where we are.
第二组 · 兵法接口
Group Two · Interfaces

兵法接口 · 九篇

Interfaces with the Philosophy of War · Nine Essays

正篇只处理 15DD 那一侧。这一组让另一侧说话：逐一读历史上的兵法与战争哲学，问它们与 SAE 在哪里同行，在哪里分开，在哪里必须批评，在哪里说不上话。四条工作规则贯穿全组：同意的地方要说清为什么同意；不同意的地方陈述 SAE 自己的立场，而不是纠正对方；文本触及地基的地方，必须批评；问题不在 SAE 范围内的地方，不发言。次序按与 SAE 的接口厚度排，不按高下。

The main sequence treats only the 15DD side. This group lets the other side speak: it reads historical works of war philosophy one by one and asks where they run alongside SAE, where they diverge, where they must be criticised, and where SAE has nothing to say. Four working rules govern every volume, and the volumes are ordered by thickness of interface, not by rank.

  • 卷一 Volume I
    界外的一半
    The Half Outside the Boundary
    康德与 SAE 共享一块地基，却在「战败国是否可以被要求改变宪法」上给出相反的条款。同一块地基不自动推出同一条线——从地基到某条具体的线，中间还有一段路要走。
    Kant and SAE share a ground and give contrary provisions on whether a defeated state may be made to change its constitution. The same ground does not automatically yield the same line.
  • 卷二 Volume II
    谁给的授权
    Who Gave the Authorisation
    克劳塞维茨与《论持久战》的约束共享一个形式：战争由外部政治目的授权，因而受它约束。而这个形式拦不住授权者本身——目的可以在过程中自行扩张。
    Clausewitz and On Protracted War share one form of constraint: war is authorised by an external political purpose and constrained by it. And that form cannot restrain the authoriser itself.
  • 卷三 Volume III
    手在改造握它的人
    The Hand Is Being Reworked by What It Holds
    康德，阿伦特，马基雅维利与 SAE 各给出一条「手段改造使用者」的机制，四条彼此不包含。并排之后显出的是一个具体的盲区。
    Kant, Arendt, Machiavelli and SAE each supply one mechanism by which a means reworks the party that wields it — and the four do not contain one another. Setting them side by side reveals a specific blind spot.
  • 卷四 Volume IV
    四种许可
    Four Permissions
    老子，《司马法》，《墨子》与 SAE 都拒绝把战争当作普通的政策选项，而四家立的关口分属四类：态度，目的，规范，结构。四家并不共有「其他路都走不通」这一条。
    Laozi, the Sima Fa, the Mozi and SAE all refuse to treat war as an ordinary policy option — and their four gates are of four different kinds: attitude, purpose, norm, structure.
  • 卷五 Volume V
    那个位置空着
    That Position Is Left Empty
    四条定理最容易被读成一套新的正义战争论。本篇存在的目的就是防止这个误读：判定「谁是正义方」的那个位置，SAE 不设立——而格劳秀斯与它站在同一边。
    The four theorems are most easily read as a new just war theory. This volume exists to prevent that misreading: SAE does not establish the adjudicating position — and Grotius stands on the same side.
  • 卷六 Volume VI
    审慎不是不得不
    Prudence Is Not Cannot-Not
    孙子的慎战与定理一在结论上重叠，而两条判准问的是不同的东西：一条问这场仗本身会怎样，一条问开战之前那个关系处在什么状态。于是技术进步能松动前者，不能松动后者。
    Sunzi's caution and Theorem One overlap in conclusion, and the two criteria ask after different objects: one after the properties of the prospective war, the other after the state of the relation before it begins.
  • 卷七 Volume VII
    边界设在哪一端
    Which End Is the Boundary Set At
    甘地与 SAE 在一点上结构相连：都拒绝把对方化约为可处置之物。分歧不在「要不要行动」，而在两条判准的主触发变量不同——一条指向自己的人，一条指向双方之间的渠道。
    Gandhi and SAE are structurally joined at one point: both refuse to reduce the other party to a disposable thing. The disagreement is not whether to act but where each criterion's trigger variables sit.
  • 卷八 Volume VIII
    那个孩子
    That Child
    新渡户《武士道》在生死问题上与 SAE 的一致远超预期。而分歧集中在一处：一个孩子被交出去替死，而他为它辩护，称那个男孩是「一个自觉而自愿的牺牲者」。
    Nitobe's Bushido agrees with SAE on living and dying far beyond expectation. The divergence is concentrated at one place: a child delivered to die in another's stead, defended as “a conscious and willing victim.”
  • 收束 · 不编号 Conclusion · unnumbered
    收束
    Conclusion
    这不是第九卷，是八卷之后的结账：跑完八卷之后，SAE 手里多了什么它原来没有的东西。答案不是「我们比他们高明」，是一个形状。
    Not a ninth volume but the reckoning after the eight: what SAE has in hand that it did not have before. The answer is not that we are better than they are — it is a shape.
