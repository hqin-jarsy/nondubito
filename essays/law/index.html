法学系列

从 14DD 对赌到星际收束：法的先验推导。两个 14DD 相遇，自然状态是对赌——法就是从这里长出来的。四篇正传 + 一篇后续篇 · 八种语言。

From the 14DD showdown to interstellar closure: an a priori derivation of law. Where two 14DDs meet, the state of nature is a wager — and law grows out of exactly that. Four papers plus a supplement · Eight languages.

Han Qin (秦汉) · 五篇 · 八种语言Han Qin (秦汉) · Five essays · Eight languages
  • 第 I 篇Paper I
    One's Law Meets One's Law
    One's Law Meets One's Law
    从两个 14DD 的对赌推出法的四条 base layer、13DD–14DD 射程、两种法形态、三条结构边界与穿透原则。
    The 14DD showdown yields four base layers, the 13DD–14DD range, two legal forms, three structural boundaries, and the penetration principle.
  • 第 II 篇Paper II
    群体法——从情到共同身份
    Group Law — From Emotion to Shared Identity
    两人法的三个余项推动地基从情转为共同身份，并使身份定义权、counter法、追问代表、透明度与递归出现。
    Three remainders shift law's foundation from emotion to shared identity, producing identity-definition power, counter-law, delegates, transparency, and recursion.
  • 第 III 篇Paper III
    国家法——艾泽拉斯
    National Law — Azeroth
    以艾泽拉斯作反面思想实验，说明国家尺度的退出成本、法的厚度，以及多权分立作为 BL4 的最小闭环。
    Azeroth serves as a negative thought experiment for exit cost, legal thickness, and separated powers as the minimum national realization of BL4.
  • 第 IV 篇Paper IV
    星际法与总收束——Coercive Law的退场
    Interstellar Law and Grand Closure
    当距离恢复退出权，强制法退场而薄协议留下；法的厚度从两人、群体、国家到星际完成拓扑回归。
    When distance restores exit, coercive law recedes while thin agreements remain; legal thickness closes a circle from dyad through group and nation to interstellar scale.
  • 后续篇Supplement
    与西方法理学的后验对照
    A Posterior Dialogue with Western Jurisprudence
    在内部推导完成后，与霍布斯、哈特、富勒、拉兹、霍菲尔德及社会契约论逐一对接，标出收敛与分叉。
    After the internal derivation, SAE is placed in dialogue with Hobbes, Hart, Fuller, Raz, Hohfeld, and social-contract theory, mapping convergences and divergences.
