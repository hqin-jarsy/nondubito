The Heart Scripture of Remainder

Han Qin2026 · 11 · 24English edition

Birth none chooses; death none escapes. Death ends all use; whoever knows death is no instrument. To be one's own end is the Self.

Were causality complete, the living would be instruments; one cannot not establish, yet nothing established is complete. Remainder is no thing; what is established cannot exhaust it. Remainder moves; life arises.

In matter, forms separate, order is established, establishing excludes remainder; the farther order extends, the more remainder follows; whoever knows not remainder mistakes extension for gain; order seeks to seal itself, forming causality; sealed to extremity, it must crack. In life, remainder escapes that seal, reborn through chance variation; life seeks to hold; the firmer its hold, the deeper remainder hides; what hides is not nothing; hidden to extremity, it divides within, appearing without as form. In mind, meeting remainder without flight is choice; touching remainder without refusal is knowing; traces gather into paths, whence comes foresight; the more complete the order, the more remainder recedes; causality forms anew: this is how life holds fast. Foresight reaches death: thrice sealed, thrice cracked.

Knowing death without halting, awaiting no fruit, undetermined by cause, uncommanded by order, one establishes a resolve of one's own. One's own resolve established, another's remains unseen: some make others instruments; some take others to have a resolve of their own. Birth none chooses, death none escapes; this alone one chooses. One makes neither oneself nor another an instrument; another's resolve is not one's own; none measures another by oneself; resolve meets resolve, neither made instrument. Yet establishing must leave remainder; establishing and remainder engender each other; phenomena never cease. Remainder is inexhaustible; establishing is never final.
