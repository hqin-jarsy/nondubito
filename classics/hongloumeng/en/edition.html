About this edition

Dazhi Revised Gengchen Edition · English

Cao Xueqin’s novel, edited and revised in Chinese by Han Qin. This independent English translation follows that Chinese reading edition, including its declared editorial choices. It does not reproduce an existing English translation.

The edition name does not claim a verbatim reproduction of the Gengchen manuscript. The selected Chinese source includes material from several witnesses. Editorial repairs are choices of this edition, not a claim to recover a unique original.

Names follow the project’s established reading tradition, consistent with its independently translated continuation. Translator’s notes are separately labelled and linked to the relevant passage. Source commentary is not silently inserted into the novel.

Chinese edition and source statement

The novel and its continuation

The first eighty chapters end here. Chapters 81–85 are Han Qin’s original continuation, not a recovered manuscript by Cao Xueqin. Chapters 86–110 remain unpublished.
