# SAE terminology lock

Current canonical wording for Non Dubito. Use this file when writing, editing, or translating entrance pages and new essays.

## Non dubito

**中文：** 对“主体是目的，而不只是手段”这一结构事实的不疑与不动摇。

**English:** Not wavering on the structural fact that a subject is an end, not merely a means.

This is not certainty about the world, personal infallibility, immunity from criticism, or an unshakable self-image. A subject's judgments and actions remain open to challenge; challenge does not cancel the subject's standing as an end.

## 13DD–16DD

| Address | Canonical name | Relational meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13DD | 自我主体 / self-subject | The self appears; remainder can be registered as “my remainder.” |
| 14DD | 自目的主体 / subject as its own end | The subject forms and carries a direction and a cannot-not of its own. |
| 15DD | 自我立法主体 / self-legislating subject | The subject can recognize the other as an end without waiting for return recognition: unilateral non dubito. |
| 16DD | 双向不疑 / mutual non dubito | Two subjects recognize one another as ends. |

## Editorial rules

- Keep the forms `13DD`, `14DD`, `15DD`, and `16DD` in every language. Do not replace them with translated number labels.
- On first use when context requires it, annotate the formal name: 13DD 自我主体, 14DD 自目的主体, 15DD 自我立法主体, 16DD 双向不疑.
- Do not collapse a level's formal name into its relational expression. In particular, 15DD is the self-legislating subject; unilateral non dubito is its relational operation.
- Entrance pages must use the current definition above. If an essay discusses an earlier formulation, mark it explicitly as historical development rather than current terminology.
- The reader-facing explanation of the remainder-to-subject step is `essays/sae-foundations/remainder-and-subject.html`.
