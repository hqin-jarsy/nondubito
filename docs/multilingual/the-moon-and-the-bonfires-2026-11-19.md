# The Moon and the Bonfires: eight-language edition

Four complete essays, 27 / 28 / 28 / 35 source blocks, with five new full editions. The independent English sequence retains 51 blocks. All five target languages received complete independent review; final exact hashes and the resolved language issues are in their review receipts. The integration editor read the full original and revised Chinese/English candidate. Editorial review is not native-editor certification.

## Substantive repairs

The Italian source requires more than spelling corrections. The narrator withholds his name from the reader; he does not necessarily lack a name or record. The hospital pays a fostering benefit collected at the municipality. His twenty-year absence is not twenty years entirely in America. Both foster sisters died, and La Mora's former household has vanished without the property simply being empty.

Irene resisted Arturo and her father initially opposed him. The false premise of obediently accepting a father-arranged marriage and its direct conclusions were revised, keeping the essay's question about the sisters' constrained lives. Valino's wife had already died; the relevant household and sharecropping dispute, violence, fire, escape and hanging were corrected. Nuto participated in Santa's burning, not only heard about it. Lack of a grave is a consequence and a possible reader's interpretation, not an asserted motive to erase memory. Nuto had already taken Cinto into his home; Anguilla planned to return to Genoa. The novel's last image is the fire's mark, not two men merely discussing an apprenticeship.

The chosen Chinese title and names remain. A false claim of no available Chinese translation was removed. Source links and review limits are documented in `independent-source-review.md`. A separate metadata ledger synchronizes the primary index/cards/descriptions and traditional variants; the original source snapshot's metadata is archival, while corrected metadata lives in this ledger and the English payload. Five-language decks already reflected the repaired factual boundaries and were not rewritten during integration.
