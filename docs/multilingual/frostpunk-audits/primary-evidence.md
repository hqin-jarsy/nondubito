# Frostpunk (2018): primary-evidence corroboration

## Scope and limits
Read coordinator preaudit in full. This is a targeted evidence pass, not a new full four-essay review, not a gameplay session, and not a complete video viewing. No repository files changed. Only the original 2018 PC game is in scope; sequel, board game and 1886 remake excluded. Actually inspected the original-game high-resolution New Faith screenshot, developer slide 35, and publicly supplied 10-second video storyboard frames covering 0:00–8:10 of the identified Winterhome playthrough. Developer diary and DevGAMM video were located but NOT watched. Audio, exact music and dialogue timings not assessed.

## 1. Winterhome can enact New Order: direct visual corroboration
Primary recording: Pravus, published 2018-10-24, “Frostpunk | The Fall of Winterhome - The New Order (Part 8)”.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sJQtSbIgm7w

The public player metadata supplies 160×90 storyboard frames at 10-second intervals. I downloaded and visually inspected sheets M0 and M1 (25 frames each, 0–240 and 250–490 seconds). At about 5:40 the Purpose-law screen is visible; 5:50 and 6:00 show the New Order confirmation event with heading “There will be blood”; about 6:30–6:50 show “The night of changes”. City management continues afterward in the same recording. These are actual game frames, not merely an inference from the video title. Files: /tmp/frost-sheet0.jpg and /tmp/frost-sheet1.jpg; public player response cached at /tmp/frost-winterhome-video.html. Other sheets downloaded but NOT inspected.

This independently corroborates enactment of the ultimate Order law in the Winterhome scenario and contradicts the scenario-wide assertion that its law machinery cannot be used. The small frames do NOT support reliable verbatim transcription of event body text, nor did I establish that this moment is after the last irreversible-generator-failure announcement. Therefore do not claim I directly verified every law remains signable throughout every final-evacuation minute. Do not turn this into a universal survival prescription. The justified correction is to remove the scenario-wide unavailability claim, not to invent an opposite claim that authoritarian laws solve the generator or guarantee evacuation.

Suggested prose boundary: the city cannot be saved, but this does not mean the law system has vanished; its powers cannot repair the doomed generator or eliminate the evacuation choices. The original categorical claim that hopelessness makes crossing the line impossible loses its stated factual foundation. Preserve allocation ethics, without replacing it with a new political thesis.

## 2. Ultimate laws are not merely silent icon changes
Actually inspected a 1920×1080 original-game screenshot embedded in GameWatcher’s Frostpunk review:
https://www.gamewatcher.com/reviews/frostpunk-review/13025
Direct image: https://images.gamewatcherstatic.com/image/file/4/2c/92324/20180429172646_1.jpg
Local inspected copy: /tmp/frost-newfaith.jpg

The New Faith event screen explicitly announces a night of changes, reports 16 citizens killed and survivors recanting, and states: “Hope will never be a problem again.” It depicts armed coercion and a casualty. This is enough to reject the literal description that final signing merely changes an icon without an event or overt violence. It also supports hope-mechanic replacement in the base game, but is NOT itself Winterhome evidence. The Winterhome New Order frames above independently show the analogous overt event there. No claim about sound/music has been verified.

The screenshot does not prove discontent becomes irrelevant, every law has identical mechanics, or that dissent can literally never occur. Keep hope and discontent distinct.

## 3. Doomed city and constrained evacuation: developer primary slides
Przemysław Solski (11 bit studios senior game designer), IGC2018 presentation:
https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/igc2018-11bit-przemyslaw-solski/120365970

Read the slide-text sequence, with special attention to prototype versus final design. Actually viewed slide 35’s original image:
https://image.slidesharecdn.com/11-181023013638/85/IGC2018-11bit-Przemyslaw-Solski-35-320.jpg
Local inspected copy: /tmp/frost-slide35.jpg

Slides 34–35 describe the revised scenario flow: rebuild, examine the generator, discover it cannot be fixed, then evacuate. Slide 35 states that the dreadnought is known from the beginning, players no longer search for an evacuation destination, an evacuation centre sends people and resources, and not everyone can be saved. Slide 59 distinguishes minimum crew/resources from optional decks, passengers and food. This supports retaining irrecoverable city loss and morally difficult allocation. It does not support an assured railway journey to New London, nor equating the city’s destruction with absence of any successful scenario outcome.

## Editorial decision list
### Corroborated and suitable for minimum correction
- Remove Winterhome-wide inability to use final Purpose laws / cross the law boundary; actual Winterhome recording shows New Order enactment.
- Remove the absolute inference derived solely from that false premise, without reversing the author’s viewpoint.
- Retain the doomed generator/city and limited evacuation; replace assured train-to-New-London destination with land-dreadnought evacuation unless another source establishes a narrower scene.
- Correct “only silent icon swap/no event” for ultimate laws: actual game event screens explicitly represent coercion and deaths.

### Still unresolved by this pass; do not promote to verified
- Every law’s availability in the last evacuation phase, exact lockouts and all event conditions.
- The complete set of crossing-the-line flags and distinctions across endings. Neither community lists nor these screenshots establish code-level flags.
- Prison graffiti event wording, choices, costs and music; prison ability cooldown/capacity.
- Exact child-shelter/apprenticeship values and proximity claims, heating details, storm peak temperature and remaining player actions.
- A universal no-death/no-law strategy claim, or the opposite claim of inevitable failure without coercion.
- Exact ultimatum thresholds/durations and whether any particular event forcibly changes them.

These remaining points should use direct law UI/event captures, original-game data or suitably sourced developer documentation before precise mechanics are retained. Interpretive arguments may remain as arguments, not proven universal mechanics.
