# Literary expansion, 16 November 2026

## Kokoro: four essays

Complete English revisions restore the Chinese narrative sequence, including the
uncle and inheritance, K’s origins and two families, the ordinary wedding and move,
the wife’s exclusion, and the father/train ending. Japanese, French, German,
Spanish and Korean each add four full essays and a contents page. Published HTML
remains the editable source; no additional manuscript database is required.

The six versions were reviewed against the Chinese scenes and argument. English
and German were additionally cross-read independently in full. German phrasing,
Spanish typhoid terminology, and Korean aunt terminology were corrected. The
closing discussion preserves the novel’s refusal to confirm Sensei’s death; the
student’s secrecy obligation is not described as costless. Chinese prose is unchanged.

Checks: 30 HTML pages parse; local links, eight hreflang routes, page language,
body presence and placeholder/encoding checks pass. Headless Chrome checks passed
80 page/language/viewport cases at 390 and 1440 pixels, including the three primary
reading modes. The French mobile page was visually inspected. Search and sitemap
updates are scoped to this series. No push was performed.

## Doctor Zhivago: five essays

Five full English revisions and twenty-five new essays in Japanese, French,
German, Spanish and Korean, each with a contents page. The English revisions
restore the forest battle, Pamphil, family and political roles, the sledge, the
laundress, the manuscripts and publication history, rather than summarize them.

All six language versions were reread against the Chinese scenes and argument.
English/German and Japanese/Korean also received independent cross-reading;
the French candle essay was separately checked in full. Review corrected the
age-sixteen subject to Lara, the person whose decision Komarovsky takes away,
being kept free versus being released, stabbing versus necessarily killing, and
spoken words versus an unspoken thought. Illness vocabulary was aligned across
languages (English typhus / German Fleckfieber); Japanese naming was unified.

Three Chinese clarifications, mirrored in Traditional Chinese: the two amulets'
shared prayer does not invent a documented mother for the telephonist; Zhivago
is explicitly named as the person escaping the forest; the funeral room is the
room of the candle, not the room of the shooting. No argument is changed.

Checks: 36 HTML pages parse with eight language routes and valid local links;
96 desktop/mobile language cases passed. Traditional dictionaries parse, git
whitespace checks pass. Search and sitemap updated for this series only.
Published HTML remains editable. No push performed.

## Hulan River: four essays

Twenty full essays and five contents pages added in Japanese, French, German,
Spanish and Korean. Existing English was read against Chinese and retained as
complete. All five new language versions received a full self-review and a
second full cross-reading (the latter read the translation payloads, not rendered
pages). Corrections included a Japanese particle, the German exit metaphor,
French noun gender, and Korean scalding agency and sticky-cake terminology.
The distinction between sympathy and exoneration and the limits of the garden
are retained, rather than reduced to reassuring lessons.

One Chinese factual clarification, also in Traditional: the young wife underwent
the scalding baths and eventually died, not necessarily in the tub itself. Chapter
5 of the public Chinese novel describes her revival and subsequent death.

Checks: 30 HTML pages, eight-language links, local references, placeholders and
encoding passed; 80 desktop/mobile cases passed before the final wording-only
corrections. Final structure and whitespace checks passed. Search and sitemap
updates are limited to this series. No push performed.

## To Live: four essays

Twenty full essays and five contents pages added in Japanese, French, German,
Spanish and Korean. All five received complete source-based self-review and
independent full cross-reading; English was read against Chinese, with selected
passages cross-checked again. Review retained the rice shop, coins, blood-taking,
shoes, sheep, borrowed life, marriage and old ox, rather than compress the plot.

English corrections: remove the incorrect home location for Jiazhen's plea;
restore li rather than miles; identify the county magistrate's wife rather than
an important patient's wife; avoid claiming the ox has no remaining use. Spanish
wife/blood-taking references and Korean magistrate, aftermath and maternity
phrasing were clarified. A Chinese death-sequence contradiction in essay 3 was
removed: the point is the destitute family's lack of property, not that only
Fugui and Kugen survived Jiazhen. Traditional and all six foreign versions agree.

Checks: 30 HTML pages, language routes, local links, encoding and placeholders
pass. 80 desktop/mobile cases passed before these wording-only corrections;
final structure and whitespace checks passed after them. Scoped search and
sitemap updated. No push performed.

## All Quiet on the Western Front: three essays

Three expanded English essays and fifteen new Japanese, French, German, Spanish
and Korean essays, plus five contents pages. A separate post-draft pass reread
all three Chinese and all eighteen foreign essays in their final HTML, paragraph
by paragraph for story, argument, negation and natural prose. This is distinct
from automated validation; there was no independent full second-language review
of every version in this group.

Review restored Kat's sentence about those at the top, the clear distinction
between forgetting and hypocrisy, and Paul's inability to recall the dead man
once outside the shell hole. An added didactic aside was removed from all six
versions. English/German family references and German canteen/French margarine-tin
wording were clarified. Chinese and Traditional Chinese prose is unchanged.

Final checks: 24 HTML pages parse; eight-language routes and local links pass;
64 desktop/mobile cases passed after the final corrections, as did git whitespace
checks. Search and sitemap updates scoped to this series. No push performed.

## Final independent spot-check

An independent reader sampled the five groups, not a second full audit of every
page. Four definite findings were corrected: Spanish Zhivago unbuttons a shirt
collar rather than ambiguously opens a neck; Korean All Quiet explicitly says
the former joy will never return; Zhivago's Traditional Chinese refers to Italian
and Russian editions rather than written characters; To Live's Chinese reader
analogy says afterwards rather than incorrectly after Fugui's death, with the
Traditional mapping updated. Kokoro's Chinese naming and outsider-impact claims
are left unchanged for the owner's editorial decision.

After corrections, all 150 HTML pages passed structural checks and 240 additional
browser cases covering the three affected series passed. The initial five-group
browser pass covered 400 cases. These are mechanical rendering checks, not
substitutes for the language reviews documented above. Search indexes were
refreshed for affected groups; there was no resulting excerpt change. No push.
