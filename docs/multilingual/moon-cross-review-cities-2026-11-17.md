## Findings and disposition

1. **DE, essay 1, corrected and verified:** `Kinderwunsch` normally means the desire to have a child, not a wish held in childhood. Replaced by `Wunsch aus der Kindheit`.
2. **KO, essay 3, corrected and verified:** `법원의 결심` was not a clear rendering of a court's concluding verdict. Replaced by `법원의 최종 판결`.
3. **KO, essay 1, optional clarity improvement applied and verified:** `먼 곳의 시정` changed to `먼 곳의 시적 동경` to avoid the common municipal-affairs reading of the ambiguous noun.
4. **KO, essay 1, recommended wording:** `집의 벽을 그린다` can suggest drawing the walls themselves; `집의 벽에 그림을 그린다` clearly says painting on the walls. Sent to the author; nonblocking phrasing clarification.
5. **ES, essay 2, recommended disambiguation:** replace `en su estudio` with `en el estudio de Stroeve` to make ownership of the studio explicit. Sent to the author; nonblocking pronoun clarification.
6. **ES, essays 1/4, optional diction:** `deseo(s) de infancia` is less open to a “childish” reading than `deseo(s) infantil(es)`. This is not a factual error.
7. Japanese correction-report phrases such as “not an axe” and admonitions not to invent scenes were observed in the initial draft; the author removed them. A later targeted recheck found those passages gone. The substantive factual qualifications remain without requiring readers to know an earlier erroneous draft.

The reviewer did not edit the author's prose files. All findings were sent to the Moon worker, who owns the text and rebuild.
