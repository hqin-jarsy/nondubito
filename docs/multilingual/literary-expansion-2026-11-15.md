French, German, Spanish and Korean each add four complete essays and a contents page.
Chinese article bodies are unchanged; the traditional dictionary only gains the new eight-language interface labels.

blocks. The English edition restores the birthmark, Sikang and Little Guizi,

## Pride and Prejudice

Four compressed English adaptations upgraded to complete essays; five further
languages add four essays and a contents page each. All six editions follow the
74 / 103 / 111 / 112 Chinese prose/heading blocks, with natural narrative pace.
Self-review and independent full-text cross-reading completed; economic amounts,
Charlotte's room, Lydia's chain of disadvantage, and the two protagonists' separate
self-correction and remaining dependence on money/luck were specifically checked.

Minimal Chinese corrections, mirrored in traditional Chinese and new editions:
- Mrs Forster, not Charlotte's friend, receives Lydia's letter; 丽述亚 → 丽迪亚.
- Sally repairs the muslin gown before packing/sending it, not after Lydia returns.
- Charlotte's back sitting room keeps Collins in his existing front study; no
  invented conversion of the larger sitting room into his study. The later echo
  and the reversed conditional are corrected too.
- Remove unsupported 'overnight' travel and unnecessary 'in her room' reading
  location, preserving the argument rather than adding an academic digression.

https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1342/1342-h/1342-h.htm .

cover 400 blocks; traditional dictionary keys cover corrected Chinese text nodes.

## The Dispossessed / 一无所有

Five complete new editions (Japanese, French, German, Spanish, Korean), each with five essays and a local index. All 108 prose/heading blocks are retained (19 / 23 / 22 / 22 / 22). Original English and both Chinese bodies were reviewed and left unchanged. Edition-specific spoiler notices, the distinction between social exclusion and massacre, the costs of care, Vea's refusal, and the risks attached to gifting knowledge remain explicit. Existing embedded navigation was replaced only in new editions by their localized navigation, not treated as prose.

Each translator reviewed the full five-essay batch; a second reader reviewed each language against Chinese. Two Japanese usages of 開き直る were corrected to clearly mean reopening rather than defiant self-justification. Thirty-six HTML pages passed strict parsing, body alignment, relative-link, eight-language alternate-link and analytics checks. Search and sitemap additions were scoped to this series. No browser-rendered or native-reader certification is claimed. No Chinese source changes and no push.

## Journey / 风之旅人

Five complete editions, three essays each; all 206 prose/heading blocks retained (65 / 69 / 72). Full three-essay self-review plus independent cross-reading, including all French and German prose by the coordinator, completed. Concrete scenes, counterarguments, development attributions and spoiler notes remain. English was already a readable independent edition and was not unnecessarily rewritten.

Minimal source corrections, synchronized with Traditional Chinese: proximity recharges scarf energy rather than permanently increasing length; damage does not automatically grow back; mountain incapacity is described as freezing/energy loss rather than assuming every player has lost a scarf to machines. The final list of IDs can enable contact, as the following PSN anecdote itself states: the point is now that knowing the name afterwards cannot alter the preceding anonymous encounter, not that contact is impossible. The English mountain sentence and its existing manuscript were aligned. These preserve the argument and do not turn it into a technical explanation.
