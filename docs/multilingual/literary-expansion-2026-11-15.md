# Literary series: multilingual expansion

## Old Tales South of the City

Four complete English essays replace the former compressed adaptations; Japanese,
French, German, Spanish and Korean each add four complete essays and a contents page.
Chinese article bodies are unchanged; the traditional dictionary only gains the new eight-language interface labels.

All six editions cover the Chinese essays' 111 / 110 / 133 / 103 prose and heading
blocks. The English edition restores the birthmark, Sikang and Little Guizi,
the graduation scene, Aunt Lan's mixed motives and choice, Song Ma's children,
and the father's beating/copper coins and adult narrator's deliberate choices.

Each new language was self-reviewed and independently cross-read against Chinese.
Final corrections addressed Japanese causal phrasing and the carter, Korean spatial
phrasing, French final-sentence cadence, and German moral ambiguity. A reasonable
inference is not silently presented as a fact in the first essay's two conclusions.
No claim is made that paragraph counts alone establish translation quality.

Checks: 30 HTML pages parse; all local links resolve; eight language routes and
analytics are present; complete paragraph/heading coverage; git whitespace checks.
Search and sitemap updates are scoped to this series and preserve unrelated entries.

Published HTML is the editable source. No new parallel manuscript JSON, source
hash layers or mandatory heavy editorial pipeline is introduced. The temporary
import drafts are not needed for later edits. No remote push was performed.
