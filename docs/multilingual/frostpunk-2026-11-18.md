## Representative changes and evidence

- Child shelters support hope; subsequent apprenticeship laws aid medicine or research. They are not a blanket nearby-workstation production bonus. The initial child-labour law concerns safe jobs, not children assigned directly as workshop engineers or automatically into every mine. Four actual French law panels were inspected.
- The central generator remains essential, but Steam Hubs and workplace heaters mean distance from its original radius is not the complete heating model. Actual original-game technology panels were inspected. The prose remains an account of dependency, not a technical guide.
- Aggregate management does not mean no individual can be helped. The Runaway Child event visibly lets the captain equip a particular father for his journey. A management perspective that favors totals remains the argument; total impossibility of individual action does not.
- The protest written on a wall belongs to the Propaganda Centre, not a fabricated prison-graffiti event. Its original screenshot presents punishment or removal of the writing; the selected removal option slightly increases hope. The screenshot did not expose the punishment tooltip, so precise punishment effects were not invented. The institution-making-opposition-manageable argument remains.
- Ultimate-law signing is not followed merely by a silent icon change. The actual New Faith event depicts suppression, deaths and coerced renunciation. Continuity remains in the familiar signing gesture, not in a denial of the consequences shown afterward. Hope replacement is distinguished from disappearance of Discontent or every possibility of losing control.
- Actual good-ending frames show that every successful run does not receive the same worth-it question. The essay now opens with a crossing-the-line result. An exhaustive ending-flag algorithm was not obtained from code or developer documentation; two terminal laws supply examples for the civic-voice reading, not a certified six-law completeness claim.
- Winterhome can enact New Order: selected original-game recording frames show the confirmation and subsequent event. This does not establish availability at every final-evacuation minute. Developer slides confirm a doomed generator/city and constrained evacuation by land dreadnought, not assured rail transport to New London. The impossibility of saving the city no longer proves the impossibility of extending power. Evacuation quotas and treatment of those left behind remain the final problem.

Source and independent audits are preserved in `frostpunk-audits/` and the multilingual data folder.
