## Findings for coordinator

1. **72, block 30: repayment promise / reported speech.** Chinese: 夏爷爷因今儿偶见一所房子，如今竟短二百两银子，打发我来问舅奶奶家里，有现成的银子暂借一二百，过一两日就送过来。 Korean ends: 외숙모마님 댁에 현금이 있으시면 일이백 냥 잠깐 빌리고 하루 이틀 뒤 가져다드리라 하셨습니다. The imperative report blurs Xia’s promise to return the borrowed money into an instruction to the messenger. Replace the request/promise portion with: 외숙모마님 댁에 현금이 있으시면 일이백 냥 잠깐 빌려 달라고 여쭈라 하셨어요. 하루 이틀 뒤에는 돌려드리겠다고 하셨습니다.

2. **73, block 29: reimbursement, not initial request.** Chinese: 常时短了这个，少了那个，那不是我们供给？谁又要去？ Korean: 늘 이것 모자라고 저것 없을 때 우리 손으로 안 댄 게 있나요? 누가 달라고 했어요? In context the daughter-in-law claims they provided missing expenses without demanding reimbursement; the next sentence counts thirty taels allegedly advanced. Replace 누가 달라고 했어요? with 그렇다고 누가 갚아 달라고 했나요?

3. **73, block 24: preserve the pawning transaction.** Chinese: 必是老奶奶拿去典了银子放头儿的. Korean: 틀림없이 유모가 가져다 은으로 바꿔 판돈을 댔을 거라. 은으로 바꿔 leaves sale/pawn indeterminate, although subsequent redemption is central to the quarrel. Replace with 틀림없이 유모가 가져다 전당 잡혀 은을 마련하고 판돈을 댔을 거라. This is a precision correction, not a missing plot episode.

## Chapter 74 continuation review

### Findings first

4. **74, block 18: entering and going out are confused.** Chinese: 凤姐听了，即唤平儿进来吩咐出去。 Korean: 희봉은 평아를 들여보내라고 불러 분부했다. Replace with: 희봉은 곧 평아를 불러들여 밖에 나가 그들을 부르라고 분부했다. Xi Feng summons Ping Er into the room and instructs her to go out to summon the servants just mentioned; she does not summon someone to send Ping Er inside.

5. **74, block 45: avoid a Korean idiom with the wrong meaning.** Chinese: 说嘴打嘴，现世现报在人眼里。 Korean: 입 자랑하다 입 맞고 남들 눈앞에서 현세의 벌을 받는구나. Replace 입 자랑하다 입 맞고 with 입 자랑하다 제 입을 얻어맞고. The existing 입 맞고 suggests kissing; the Chinese concerns boasting and getting slapped in the mouth, explicitly linked to her hitting herself.

6. **74, block 33: clarify the turn from the Zhen family to this household.** Chinese: 你们今日早起不曾议论甄家，自己家里好好的抄家，果然今日真抄了。 Korean: 오늘 아침 견씨 집안을 두고 이야기하지 않았나요? 멀쩡한 집에서 가산 몰수를 당하더니 정말 오늘 그렇게 되었지요. Recommended contextual rendering: 오늘 아침 진씨 집안의 가산 몰수 이야기를 하지 않았나요? 제집이 멀쩡한데 그런 이야기를 하더니, 과연 오늘 우리 집도 정말 뒤지게 되었군요. The current wording suggests the Zhen confiscation itself took place today; Tan Chun is bitterly applying that morning’s discussion to the search now taking place in her own family. Retain the following 우리에게도 차츰 오는군요 and the self-destruction speech.

7. **74, block 38: optional material precision.** Chinese: 一大包金银锞子，约共三四十个. Korean: 금은 조각 한 꾸러미, 모두 삼사십 개쯤. Prefer 금은 소괴 한 꾸러미 or 작은 금은 덩이 한 꾸러미: these are cast ingots/lumps of money, not arbitrary fragments. Quantity is correctly retained. This is lower priority than items 4–6.

## Chapter 75 final continuation review

### Findings first

8. **75, block 15: Yuan Yang’s joking request for more food.** Chinese: 你够了，我不会吃的。 Korean: 마님은 충분하셔도 저는 이것을 못 먹어요. This incorrectly suggests she cannot eat the servants’ rice. She is joking that even if You Shi has enough, she herself still needs to eat. Replace with: 마님은 충분하셔도 저는 안 먹나요? This keeps her playful reason to send for Tan Chun’s share, rather than introducing a complaint about the food’s quality.

9. **75, block 22: subject of liking and avoiding people.** Chinese: 好酒者喜之，不饮者则不去亲近. Korean: 술 좋아하는 사람들은 그를 좋아했고 안 마시는 사람들은 가까이하지 않았다. The description concerns Xing Dequan’s own indiscriminate friendship with drinkers, followed by his not approaching nondrinkers; the current Korean turns it into others liking or avoiding him. Replace with: 술 좋아하는 사람은 좋아하고 술 안 마시는 사람에게는 가까이 가지 않았다. The next statement that he treats ranks and servants alike then follows naturally.

10. **75, block 29: preserve loss, not incapacity, in the discreet rendering.** The source’s crude joke contrasts losing money with losing a male body part. Current Korean: 돈을 잃은 것이지 사내까지 못쓰게 된 것도 아닌데. This shifts the joke toward incapacity/impotence. A restrained alternative is: 돈을 잃었지 사내로서 가진 것까지 잃은 것도 아닌데. Do not add sexual acts or graphic detail. The current Korean correctly attributes the joke to a young libertine after Xing Dequan’s explanation, rather than to Xing Dequan himself. Ages 16–17 remain in block 23. This is an intentionally non-explicit euphemistic rendering of coarse anatomical vocabulary, not a claim of word-for-word unsoftened translation.

11. **75, blocks 2 and 6: flour-based drink, not powdered tea.** Chinese 茶面子 in block 2 and 面茶 in block 6 refer to a grain-flour preparation mixed with water; current Korean uses 가루차 both times, which suggests tea powder. Suggested wording for block 2: 어제 그 애 이모 댁에서 좋은 볶은 곡물가루를 보내셨으니 한 사발 타 드세요. Suggested block 6 end: 모두 곡물가루를 탄 음료를 마셨다. Avoid specifying a particular grain beyond the source.
