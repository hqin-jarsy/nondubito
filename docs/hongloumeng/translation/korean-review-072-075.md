## Precisely scoped non-graphic adaptation

Chapter 75, block 29 contains a coarse anatomical sexual taunt addressed to the two sixteen-/seventeen-year-old wine-serving youths identified in block 23. The Korean retains the speaker, money-loss comparison, accusation that the youths ignore the loser, and the ensuing collective laughter and You Shi’s disgust, but renders the explicit anatomical expression non-graphically as “돈을 잃었지 사내로서 가진 것까지 잃은 것도 아닌데”. This is an intentional local euphemistic adaptation, not a claim of word-for-word unabridged sexual wording. The surrounding social coercion and commercial power imbalance, the youths’ explanation that their master requires deference to wealth and power, and the adults’ blame remain. No ages were changed. Ordinary adult/euphemistic narrative elsewhere was not softened merely for being sexual.
