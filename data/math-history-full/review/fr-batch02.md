## Author-level issues reported, not silently changed
These are residual claims in the frozen Chinese, faithfully retained in French unless explicitly noted. They require author approval, not translation-only rewriting.

### ep07
- §2 follows the new qualification “这些术文没有明确展开第四件那个问题，这不等于当时从无人思考它” with “最后这一句的意思不是没能回答，是没有提出”, and earlier says “把它从那一格拿出来单独问它是什么，是后人才会做的动作”. Suggested scope: the surviving procedural texts do not explicitly ask the question; this does not establish that nobody at the time asked it.
- §3 “当时的人不需要有这个想法，因为那个东西本来就不在数里面” generalizes a particular treatment of negative answers into all contemporaries number concepts. Restrict to the cited text/problem.
- §4 “而资格这件事，在别处基本没有人提出过要求” conflicts with its following explicit caveat “这种材料分布不证明别处从来不问”. State what the surviving materials establish, not universal absence of thought.
- §5 “而它的定义没有人怀疑” and “用词一旦定下来，后面两千年能说什么就跟着定了” are unsupported absolutes relative to the essay own differentiated account. The negative cross-product value in Chinese is an elementary error; French is already correct and unchanged.
- §8 “而且不用它就拿不到答案” needs the radical-route qualifier supplied in ep08 §4, rather than excluding all real methods.
Evidence basis: internal contrasts and direct arithmetic, not newly completed archival research. Relevant source locators include https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Brahmagupta/ and https://math.univ-lyon1.fr/~fle/Accueil_files/Article_past_object_postprint.pdf .

### ep08
- §8 “第三篇里那个缺口，两千年没有人看见” contradicts the qualifications added in ep03 about ancient discussion. Suggested: the gaps were not systematically and explicitly addressed in the later formal sense. Existing Euclid reference: https://mathcs.clarku.edu/~djoyce/elements/bookI/bookI.html .
- §8 “一个所有人都不承认，所有人又都绕不开的中转站” loses both the particular radical-route restriction and the varied reception documented in §§4–6. Suggested: an intermediate stage unavoidable along that general radical route, not yet universally recognized. Relevant supplied source: https://www.math.wm.edu/~vinroot/Wantzel.pdf .
- §8 “两百多年里，数学家对它的全部把握就是这一句” and the sweeping identity language erase the partial geometrical interpretations explicitly described in §6, including Wallis in 1685. Suggested: operational success preceded a widely accepted systematic interpretation. Relevant supplied source: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Wessel/ .

### ep10
- §8 “而在他全部的日子里，只有最后一夜是有日期的” directly conflicts with the essay dated birth, submissions, letters and hospital records. Suggested: the last night happens to have a date, a place and surviving papers and is particularly suited to the narrative placement of an insight. This preserves the literary argument without falsely denying other dates. Relevant supplied sources: https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1896_3_13__197_0/ and https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1846_1_11__381_0.pdf .
