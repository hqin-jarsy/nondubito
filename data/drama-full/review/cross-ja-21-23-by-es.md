## Local clarification required

**21 paragraph 2:** 「一時は本当に、以前の主人イフィクラートに、したくないことをさせられたのだ」 is intended as a causative potential (Arlequin could make his former master do unwanted things), but 「させられた」 is also readily read as causative passive (Arlequin was made to do them by the master). That reading reverses the role-swap power relation. Suggested unambiguous wording: 「一時は本当に、以前の主人イフィクラートに、したくないことをさせることができたのだ」. This is a preventive agency disambiguation, not a claim that the wider essay misunderstands the role reversal. At the reviewer's last direct read before report writing, the old wording was still present.
