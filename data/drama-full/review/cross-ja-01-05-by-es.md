# Independent cross-review of Japanese 01–05 by Spanish translator

## Scope and evidence

Read ALL 105 Japanese body paragraphs (19 + 20 + 22 + 22 + 22), not samples, against ALL corresponding canonical Chinese paragraphs already read in full for the Spanish translation. Also read each Japanese work/title/deck, source note, and source-label list. The Chinese body_zh, not the independent English text, was the controlling source.

No Japanese file was edited. Recommendations were sent to the coordinator.

## Two recommended corrections

1. **05, body paragraph 21.** Current: 「ただ辛抱強く他人の話を最後まで聞かせることではない」. Chinese: 「不只要求掌权者耐心让别人把话说完」. The causative 聞かせる gives the wrong action to the ruler: making/letting someone hear rather than letting the other finish speaking. Use 「ただ辛抱強く他人の話を最後まで聞くことではない」 or 「ただ辛抱強く他人に最後まで話させることではない」.

2. **05, body paragraph 22.** Current: 「あなたを大切に思うからこそ、望みどおりのことだけは言えないのだと」. Chinese: 「正因为在意你，所以不能只照你希望的那样说」. だけは言えない can mean that precisely the desired words cannot be spoken, rather than refusing to say only the desired words. Use 「あなたを大切に思うからこそ、望みどおりのことだけを言うわけにはいかないのだと」.

Neither affects paragraph completeness; both clarify meaning in the essay's final argument.

## Optional small fluency/clarity improvements

- 01 paragraph 15: 「彼女の固さ」 is understandable but stiff; 「彼女の決意の固さ」 better expresses 她的坚定.
- 02 paragraph 5: 「私密な場所」 sounds like a Chinese calque; 「二人きりの私的な場所」 or a natural formulation of privacy would read more smoothly. Do not suggest there really are only two people in the room: the point is that closing the door does not increase actual privacy.
- 05 paragraph 9: the sequence 「自分には」「自分の見解」 makes the latter 自分's referent momentarily ambiguous. 「父自身の見解だけが正しいと思わせてはならない」 makes explicit that the belief being challenged is the father's.
- 01 title 「許してやる」 adds an overtly condescending shade compared with neutral Chinese 「我原谅你」. This matches the scene's paternalism, so it is not a material error; 「君を許す」 is available if closer quotation-level neutrality is preferred.
