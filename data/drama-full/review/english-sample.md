# Existing English sample review

2026-11-08. The existing English is an independent public-facing edition, not a paragraph-for-paragraph translation. It is preserved, not replaced with a new translation.

Coverage: read the complete Chinese and English of 01; read the complete English of 06, 11, 16 and 23, comparing the opening, a central hinge and final two Chinese paragraphs directly. This is a targeted sample, not an audit of all 23 English essays or of every Chinese-English sentence.

- 01: maintains the distinction between forgiveness and apology; does not flatten Nora into a finished authentic self; retains unequal responsibility, childcare costs, uncertain next steps, and the offstage future. English condenses some stage detail (including the dove imagery) but does not become a summary.
- 06: preserves the Stepdaughter's challenge to representation without declaring all acting fraudulent; distinguishes censorship, composition, the Mother's renewed pain, and the unintended curtain. Ending does not claim all positions have been reconciled.
- 11: retains the plurality of citizen voices and both military merit and governing obligations. It does not simply label the first vote authentic and the second manipulated. The tribunes' strategic role and the possibility of refusal remain explicit.
- 16: keeps the biased arguments about parenthood, Athena's thunderbolt, the conditional bargain, and the limited/non-universal scope of the peace. The acquittal and the Furies' relocation are distinct departures.
- 23: distinguishes the robot Helena from the human character. The shift from substitute sacrifice to refusing either death remains intact; love is not a test for a right to life. Reproduction is identified as Alquist's hope rather than demonstrated fact, specifically in the English version used by the essay.

No sampled issue warranted rebuilding the English edition. This does not certify every detail in the unsampled English essays or every claim against primary play texts. No source content changed.
