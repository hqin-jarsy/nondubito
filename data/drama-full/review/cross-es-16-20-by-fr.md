## Optional precision suggestion

The Chinese leaves the photograph question open, and paragraph 8 of the Spanish correctly says that absence of comparison does not establish substitution. Suggested local tightening: “el problema de la fotografía: cada persona la vio por separado, sin cotejarla con los demás, y él mismo no llegó a verla.” This is an optional prevention of momentary ambiguity, not a finding that the essay asserts substitution. ## Full semantic review

### 16 — Las euménides

The Furies’ blood-relative criterion, non-blood marriage explanation, Apollo’s maternal devaluation and Athena’s paternal/male allegiance are stated as characters’ partial arguments, not unquestionable divine truths. The grief over lost responsibility remains legitimate as a concern while blighting uninvolved land, crops and children remains unjust. Athena’s first honor/dwelling reassurance does not erase experienced loss. Zeus, key, thunderbolt and the “not needed” qualification preserve the threat inside persuasion. Questions about place, honor, power and durability, everyday household/birth/land/harvest relations and bilateral continuing obligations are all retained. The agreement neither revises nor certifies the original sentence; blessings protect the same lives previously threatened. The force is not made docile. Outward war and honor remain, preventing universal-love closure or erasure of outsiders and the dead mother. Underground residence, torches and escort are an enacted beginning, not a guarantee of future fidelity. Orestes’ departure and the Furies’ continuing presence remain distinct. Metadata retains approximately 734–1047, Smyth, Kosmos revision and reasons/promises/completed actions.

### 17 — La tormenta

Window closure, recall before departure, stranger’s face and apparently absent woman retain their order. Two children, the demoiselle/servant’s daughter distinction, expulsion and continued survival preserve responsibility rather than merely changing the story’s dignity. Thirty years of recollection differ from earning a living and raising children. Shirts, embroidered repairs, photo, recognition and immediate questions about purpose/instigator survive. Genuine feeling and guilt are not declared nonexistent, but do not oblige Shiping to console him or reconcile. Birthday, furniture and closed window do not answer how she has lived or why she must justify her appearance. Visit, later exclusion, cheque and tearing remain; compensation does not necessarily mean forgiveness, nor refusal prove virtue. Her limited request and reluctance to demand immediate recognition are not treated as wholly unpressured. Zhou Ping strikes Dahai in the latter’s conflict with Zhou Puyuan; Shiping protects the son raised with her, cannot finish recognition and leaves with him. Her memories do not assign the elder son’s life or confer exclusionary power; her choices among living sons exceed a test of Puyuan. The final memory/living-person distinction is complete. The note explicitly retains the textbook excerpt, 2004 Selected Works of Cao Yu, cuts and modifications, Act II and exclusion of the final act. No false claim to an unabridged original edition.

### 19 — El pato salvaje

Morning reconsideration concerns the method, not diminished love. Gregers’ intended revelation, Hjalmar’s departure and paternity doubt, rejection of approach and escape from the child, Gina’s practical response and Gregers’ preferred inner struggle all survive. Found-child speculation, gifted duck, care independent of origin and prayers for both father and bird preserve the child’s own reasoning. Rising in surprise, usefulness question, courage test, voluntary language, grandfather help and secrecy from Gina remain. Pressure is not erased by absence of an explicit command. Gina’s imperfection is retained without nullifying her insistence on the child’s immediate needs. Gregers converts reconsideration into character/courage judgment rather than re-examining the advice. Father’s return, repeated rejection, packing, weather/fatigue and requested day or two contrast with the child’s denied pause. Shot, Gregers’ premature account, moved father, renewed-life hopes and the as-yet-unfound child retain sequence. Discovery, fatal injury, Relling’s confirmation and both parents carrying her to her small room remain, without procedural detail. Predictions of the father’s future are not presented as shown futures. The text expressly rejects treating her life as justified by adult growth, a multi-person tragedy as the sole consequence of one sentence, or all concealment as therefore warranted. The ending restores simultaneous love for father and duck. Source note preserves IV end/V, Marx Aveling, Gutenberg 73631, 112–141 page markers, death disclosure and no detailed method.

### 20 — La tempestad

Time, appointed ending, intact magic, grove confinement and differentiated mental distress remain. Tears, beard and winter eaves do not excuse fault or invoke an invented punishment rule. Gonzalo’s special history, provisions and books from Prospero’s own library distinguish him from the enemies. Ariel remains a spirit using a counterfactual human identity, not a human learning pity. Prospero’s assessment of repentance is later distinguished from Alonso’s actual restoration/apology and Antonio’s missing admission. Prior rescue, promised shortened service, obligation rhetoric and renewed tree threat all survive. Ariel’s testimony is not a self-reward bargain and listening does not erase coercion. Staff-breaking/book-sinking remain intentions; music, still-bound arrival, gradual recovery and tears remain enacted stages. Pardon does not ratify usurpation. Clothes, flowers/flight song, praise, farewell sorrow and immediate captain/boatswain errand distinguish joy and favor from freedom. Final sea task precedes release, without inventing deception or a fully staged free future. Caliban’s failed murder plot, cleaning order and separate future prohibit universal-liberation closure. Mercy, restitution, confession and end of imposed service retain separate timelines. Source note retains Folger V.1 and I.2, final arrangements, and exclusion of the epilogue audience address from the island scene.
