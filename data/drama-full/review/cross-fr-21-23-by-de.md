## Targeted refinements reported to coordinator and French translator

1. **22 paragraph 2:** “des livres et des tableaux de bon goût” treats the Chinese room furnishings “端正的书画” as books plus paintings. The Chinese refers to written/pictorial works among the decor, rather than explicitly adding books. Prefer a natural phrase for displayed written works and pictures, preserving their respectable character. This is a concrete-object refinement, not an argument change.
2. **22 paragraph 9:** “La même foi exige du fils que le rite manquant soit accompli, et de la mère...” can assign the ritual duty to the son. The Chinese contrasts what the same faith demands in the son's interpretation with what it demands in the mother's interpretation: he insists she complete the ceremony; she insists she not perform it falsely. Prefer “Pour le fils, la même foi exige que le rite manquant soit accompli ; pour la mère, elle exige...” or an equally explicit perspective distinction.

These are small precision refinements. Implementation and final choice of natural French wording remain with the translator/coordinator. No material omission or changed central argument was found.
