## Full semantic self-review

Compared every paragraph in sequence against the Chinese during translation and reviewed the completed arguments, staging and notes for omissions, negation, qualifiers, agency, and chronology. Specific checks:

- 11: The citizens disagree before the candidate arrives; gratitude is not a statutory prohibition. Voices are personal responses, not paper ballots. Humble clothing, the withdrawn companion, the awkward first assent, the wish for future friendship, wounds as both private experience and claimed debt, the desire to change clothes, and pending Senate confirmation remain. The tribunes identify a real danger but also stage an anger trap and prescribe an explanation for the earlier vote. Neither first assent nor later objection is treated as wholly inauthentic. The closing “partly” and the distinctions between influence, manipulation, and genuine reconsideration remain.
- 12: Two years, glass garden door, right-hand lock, left-hand chair, adjacent supper and laughter, doctor's later entry, and the stuffed bird remain distinct. Trigorin's story is a notebook idea, not a completed novel or premeditated scheme. Nina's repeated self-corrections are not collapsed into a final triumphant declaration. Third-class travel to Elets, the whole winter's engagement, unwelcome attentions, stage technique and internalized contempt, faith and endurance, desire for fame, hunger, rejected supper, concealed visit, continuing love, old play's final recitation and departure all remain. Nina does not witness the stuffed bird or Trigorin's forgetting. Treplev's death neither makes her responsible nor retroactively requires her to stay. Source note retains 1978, works volume 13, both Russian sources and both acts.
- 13: Werner's interrupted good news, uncertain repayment, water flask and two rescues, Tellheim's advance during wartime collection and subsequent suspicion, pawned engagement ring, Minna's original reason for love and deliberate early meeting remain. Her false disinheritance is identified as an agreed deception known to the reader. His renewed borrowing occurs before royal vindication, and the letter's removal of real obstacles is retained. Gendered double standards do not vanish automatically; Werner is rejected for a separate anger; Franziska's anxiety and Minna's admission that the joke went too far remain. The returned ring is the pawned one she recovered, not the ring he had given her. Werner's later money is accepted as safekeeping, leaving a recognizable old habit rather than a complete moral conversion. No future difficulties are claimed resolved.
- 14: Fountain pen, £5,000 cheque, initial five-by-£1,000 condition and £10,000 total, Bodger's original name, fundraising strain and threatened closure remain. Baines's practical objection is taken seriously without declaring liquor or arms harmless. Bill's violence against Jenny and an older woman, refused compensation, redirected donation, coin on the drum and demand for his soul remain distinct. The father's claim of “buying” is not converted into legal ownership. Gratitude and public endorsement are distinguished; the fiancé's flag and trombone and the father's retreat from the silver badge remain. Barbara kisses Baines and recognizes her responsibility without joining the celebration. Peter's tea, bed and Barbara's fare, his pride, her arm gesture and request for conversation remain. The later factory decision does not exonerate the weapons business; food security enables refusal of religious gratitude.
- 15: Father in yard, wife on porch, dead birth mother, another woman's child, 1950s Pittsburgh, rubbish collection and racial barriers to baseball remain. Troy still does not disavow his earlier choice; accepting paternal responsibility does not entitle him to assign Rose's work. Rose accepts the infant before saying the two sentences and enters the house without returning the baby. Child care is neither marriage repair nor a test of her greatness. Two months later, supper on the stove remains actual labour, not proof that her refusal expired. Acceptance and refusal require continuing work; this choice is not prescribed to other betrayed partners. Seven-year-old Raynell's garden on the funeral morning is her own concern, not a guarantee of an unhurt childhood. Source note preserves the scene, two-month and seven-year references, ending disclosure, public English transcription and Pearson guide.

## Terminology and review limits

Japanese titles and names are kept consistent within each article. Source notes identify Chinese-original translated/paraphrased speech rather than claiming quotation from a Japanese edition. Literal Self-as-an-End is absent from these source bodies and was not introduced. No interpretive blocker found.

This is translation fidelity review against the supplied canonical essays, not fresh verification of the external dramatic editions. Independent cross-review is still required before acceptance.
