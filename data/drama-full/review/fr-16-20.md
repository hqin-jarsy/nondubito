## Full semantic review

### 16 — Les Euménides

All 17 paragraphs retain the difference between acquittal and innocence, the Furies’ selective blood-kin protection, Apollo’s diminishment of maternity and Athena’s paternal/male allegiance. Divine speech is not endorsed as truth. Their lost responsibility is treated seriously while the threat to uninvolved land, crops and children remains unjust. Repeated curses, tied votes, dwelling, honors, Zeus, key and thunderbolt retain the pressure inside persuasion. Concrete questions about residence, power and duration remain; acceptance does not prove the original verdict perfect. Blessings target the same lives previously threatened, and continuing obligations are reciprocal. The city’s outward war and those left outside remain explicit. Torch procession and underground residence are an enacted step rather than a guarantee for future generations; Orestes’ departure differs from the Furies’ continued presence. Source note retains approximate 734–1047, Smyth, Kosmos revision and arguments/promises/actions distinction. Self-review clarified the syntax of the key/thunderbolt sentence without changing it.

### 17 — L’Orage

All 19 paragraphs preserve window closure, calling her back, the apparently absent woman, two children, demoiselle versus servant’s daughter, expulsion, two different thirty-year histories, shirts/embroidery/repairs/photo, recognition before inquiry into motive and instigator. Memory and guilt are neither declared wholly false nor treated as a debt of consolation. Birthday/furniture/window details do not answer her current treatment. Cheque, tearing, single visit and future exclusion stay distinct; compensation is neither necessarily forgiveness nor refusal a compulsory virtue test. Her limited wish does not imply pressure-free choice. Zhou Ping strikes Dahai in the latter’s conflict with Zhou Puyuan; Shiping protects Dahai, cannot finish recognition and leaves. The sons’ agency and her choice between them prevent reducing her life to testing Puyuan’s feelings. Source note retains textbook excerpt, Act II, 2004 Selected Works, cuts/adaptations, subsequent father/son conflict and exclusion of the final act. Self-review explicitly named Zhou Puyuan in the conflict sentence to avoid an ambiguous father pronoun.

### 18 — Un inspecteur vous demande

All 21 paragraphs preserve the ring’s initial refusal, legitimate checking, Sheila’s own prior skepticism, local officer and police-chief call, and the difference between discrediting identity and erasing facts. Mrs Birling is not wrongly included in a collective prior repentance. Separate photograph viewings and Gerald’s not seeing it do not prove substitution or distinct women. Mimicry, movement toward door without exit, bedtime/fatigue explanations, and the ring’s renewed offer stay in order. Delay is not permanent refusal; sibling lucidity does not itself repair their wrongs. The final phone report retains death during transport and the inspector’s approach, while all earlier evidential doubts remain unresolved and the curtain falls before arrival. The victim is not merely an educational device. Source note retains classroom PDF, Ormiston, unspecified print source and condensed Chinese reformulations; source pages 53–65 remain. Self-review smoothed a repeated prétexte formulation.

### 19 — Le Canard sauvage

All 20 paragraphs preserve the distinction between reconsidering the method and ceasing to love the father, the earlier paternal rejection and Gina’s practical response, found-child speculation, gifted duck, and shared prayer for father and bird. Voluntary vocabulary does not erase pressure. Rising, surprise, usefulness question, grandfather assistance and secrecy from mother are retained. Gina remains imperfect without losing the value of her immediate care. Gregers turns objection into courage/character rather than examining the method; the father’s requested day or two contrasts with the denied space for the child’s overnight thought. The shot and initial false interpretation precede discovery. Hjalmar’s tenderness and renewed-life words are not verified knowledge of the child. Fatal injury, Relling’s confirmation and parents carrying her into her own room stay distinct. Later claims about Hjalmar remain predictions, not staged futures. Her worth is not contingent on adult growth; neither a single-cause explanation nor a defense of all concealment is introduced. Source note retains Marx Aveling, Gutenberg 73631, IV end/V, page markers 112–141, tragic outcome and no detailed method.

### 20 — La Tempête

All 17 paragraphs preserve hour, planned end, continued confinement, differentiated distress, tears/beard/winter eaves and Gonzalo’s earlier provisions and books from Prospero’s own library. Ariel remains a spirit, not a newly compassionate human. Prospero’s assessment of repentance is distinguished from Alonso’s actual apology/restoration and Antonio’s silence. Ariel’s own service, prior rescue, freedom promise and renewed-tree threat remain. Listening does not cancel coercion; magic staff/book actions remain intentions, not completed stage actions. Music, still-bound arrivals, gradual recovery and Prospero’s tears remain. Clothing, flower/flight song, praise, anticipated missing him and new ship errand preserve the gap between happiness and freedom. Final voyage work precedes release, without inventing either deception or an already shown free life. Caliban’s failed plot, cleaning order and separate future remain. The result is substantial but not universal equality. Source note retains Folger V.1/I.2 and excludes the epilogue from the island scene. Self-review explicitly identified Prospero as the books’ owner.
