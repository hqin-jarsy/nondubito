## Semantic and edition checks

### 11 — Coriolano

Preserved the opening disagreement rather than a uniform crowd, gratitude as a felt obligation rather than a legal prohibition, military service alongside contempt during food protests, individual and small-group requests, humble clothing and its removal, the wounds as private experience and claimed entitlement, and the difference between acknowledging past service and consenting to future government. Retained the tribunes' grounded warning and their manipulation together, the opportunity to withdraw support before formal confirmation, their proposed public blame narrative, and the important qualification that popular initiative is only partly manufactured. No person is reduced to a wholly sincere or wholly insincere spokesman. Source note retains Folger/MIT, II.3, voices rather than ballot papers, and the nonlegal nature of the opening claim. Self-review improved the idiom for addressing the people contemptuously without changing the action.

### 12 — La gaviota

Preserved window, glass door, garden steps, right-hand lock and left-hand chair; the two-year gap; Tréplev's earlier account as his perspective; the dead bird and Trigorin's unwritten story idea; Nina's repeated interrupted self-descriptions and forehead gestures; Turguénev, the surviving small stage, Yelets, third-class travel and winter contract. Hunger, water, refusal of dinner, hat, coat, horse-drawn carriage, eavesdropping and the keyhole remain. Her work is neither guaranteed success nor invalidated by continued love; religious endurance does not vindicate inflicted suffering; desire for fame is reordered rather than erased. Tréplev's more orderly reading of her is not accepted as the complete truth. Preserved her final recitation, embrace and glass-door exit; the chair's removal, stuffed bird, Trigorin's forgetting and Nina's absence from that revelation; subsequent death does not impose retroactive responsibility on her. Source note retains Russian acts II/IV, 1978 collected works, literary works volume 13, Wikisource/FEB, and story idea rather than completed fiction or premeditated harm.

Self-review explicitly identified Arkádina as Tréplev's mother, made the carriage horse-drawn, and avoided wording that could imply Nina had previously accepted a reciprocal romantic relationship with Tréplev.

### 13 — Minna von Barnhelm

Preserved the opening urgent loan before the retrospective refusal, repayment uncertainty, Werner's independent willingness, cantimplora and two rescues, wartime advance and suspicion of improper benefits, dismissal/injury/poverty and pawned engagement ring. Minna's attraction predates their meeting; concern for her reputation does not let Tellheim decide her contribution. The disinheritance is explicitly invented by Minna and Franziska; subsequent royal vindication is real. The reversed arguments do not instantly remove Tellheim's gender hierarchy. The joke escapes control, harms Werner, and is acknowledged as excessive by Minna. Kept the two rings distinct, recovery by Minna before the attempted breakup, actual placement on his finger, handshake and money accepted for safekeeping rather than a perfectly transformed character. Future challenges remain unshown. Bell, Dartmouth Annotext and III.7/late IV/V remain in the note.

Self-review rewrote an awkward double negative about his reasons for difficulty accepting aid; broadened his willingness to give beyond material things; avoided suggesting his considerate language was necessarily a sham.

### 14 — La comandante Bárbara

Preserved own fountain pen, pause before signing, £5,000 plus five prospective £1,000 donations to reach £10,000, Bodger's original name and alcohol business, Undershaft supplying the remaining half, and buying influence without legal ownership. Baines's genuine responsibility and instrumental fundraising are both retained; suspect provenance does not erase food or shelter. Bill's assaults, compensation refusal, gold coin on the drum, and distinctions between redress, stopping violence and conversion remain. Undershaft's arms-business argument is not repentance. Flag, trombone, public endorsement, kiss, silver badge, father retreating, visible return gift and Barbara left in the yard remain. Kept extended hand to Bill, Peter's unemployment, promised job search, tea/lodging/fare, taking his arm, reciprocal need and no religious assent requirement. Third-act factory choice neither cancels the earlier dilemma nor proves arms harmless. All three source labels and the full-act reading scope remain.

Self-review named Baines explicitly at the start of paragraph 10 and retained differing beliefs without unnecessarily strengthening them to direct opposition. The conventional Spanish work title and the corresponding comandante rank are used consistently.

### 15 — Vallas

Preserved yard/porch positions, mother's death, father's ongoing refusal to renounce his earlier choice, genuine but insufficient responsibility and Rose's separate say about scope and capacity. The action order is acceptance, receiving the baby, boundary statement, then entering the house. Care is neither marital forgiveness nor a proof of exceptional sacrifice, and the child is not an instrument of restoration. Feeding/sleep/illness, two-month later supper on the stove and church departure remain actual labor without undoing refusal. Continued cohabitation does not settle intimacy. No universal duty is imposed on betrayed partners to raise the child. Raynell's age seven, funeral morning, garden and waiting retain her independent concerns without guaranteeing an unscarred life. Source note retains II.3, later two-month/seven-year scenes, public English transcription and Pearson contextual guide.

Self-review corrected an ambiguous expression that could have meant receiving care to the explicit willingness to provide care, and improved the sentence about why supper resists a complete-break account.
