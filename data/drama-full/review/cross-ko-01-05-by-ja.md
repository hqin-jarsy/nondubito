## Findings requiring small corrections

Paragraph numbers below are one-based; body array indices are provided too. These findings refer to the text read during review and may be addressed subsequently by its owner.

1. **03, paragraph 9 (body[8]): bird-transmigration detail is ambiguous.** Chinese: “灵魂转生为鸟”. Korean: “영혼이 새로 환생한다는”. “새로” can be read as the adverb “anew,” leaving the concrete bird example unclear. Recommend “영혼이 새의 모습으로 환생한다는” or an equally explicit bird formulation. The remainder of the coercive-test argument is accurate. This correction should be made because the user requested preservation of concrete examples.
2. **01, paragraph 17 (body[16]): completeness qualifier is weakened.** Chinese: “对今后的人生，她并没有完整计划”. Korean: “앞으로의 삶 전체에 대한 계획은 없다.” This can suggest an absence of a plan for her life overall rather than absence of a complete plan. Recommended: “앞으로의 삶에 대한 완전한 계획은 없다.” The immediately preceding destination and the refusal to call Helmer permanently incapable of change are faithfully preserved.
3. **05, paragraph 3 (body[2]): belonging is replaced by biological relationship.** Chinese: “自己属于父亲”. Korean: “자신은 아버지의 아들이며”. This says “he is his father's son” rather than expressing the belonging or allegiance in Haemon's opening. In this essay the distinction matters because the next sentences analyze respect versus unconditional obedience. Recommend “자신은 아버지에게 속한 사람이며” or a natural equivalent preserving the expression of allegiance.

All three were immediately reported to the coordinator. No full paragraph omission, reversed principal claim, major speaker reassignment, or flattened ending was found.
