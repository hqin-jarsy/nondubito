## Semantic self-review

- **01, Une maison de poupée:** Preserved the second letter and Nora’s “and me?”, destruction of the bond, the real borrowing/forgery responsibility, Helmer’s unilateral restrictions, and the distinction between forgiving her and apologizing to her. Kept the dove/protector imagery, wife/child status, learning-to-please uncertainty, the non-symmetric responsibilities, religion/law, the unresolved responsibilities toward the three absent children, incomplete future plan, exchange of rings/keys, and the absence of a guaranteed happy future. Helmer’s affection is not rewritten as wholly false, and his promises are not treated as completed reform.
- **02, Tartuffe:** Preserved both the son’s earlier failed accusation and the wife’s discredited calm, the agreed stopping condition, coughing/licorice joke, two simultaneous demands for proof, and Elmire’s ironic request that Orgon hide again. The successful exposure does not recover property or papers; the royal intervention remains a separate external action. Orgon’s later ability to listen is left unguaranteed. Elmire’s performed assent is distinguished from Tartuffe’s deception without equating the two.
- **03, La Nuit des rois:** Preserved Malvolio’s unattractive vanity and past insults as well as the non-consent to confinement; robe/beard invisibility, the doctrinal test, two voices belonging to one person, the lamp/writing requests, and Toby’s self-interested wish to stop. The false accusation against Olivia is corrected within the story rather than treated as valid. Fabian’s wish to laugh it away, Feste’s old wound, the non-equivalence of insult and imprisonment, the risk of indiscriminate revenge, the detained captain, mediation proposed but not completed, and Feste’s song all remain. Other people’s happiness is not annulled. The Chinese says Olivia has promised marriage; retained that wording rather than silently importing the English context’s “has married.”
- **04, Oncle Vania:** Preserved concrete lamp/ink/accounts actions and Vania’s own request to work, rather than assigning that demand solely to Sonia. The professor’s scholarly worth remains unadjudicated, the missed shots still cause harm, and the return of the medication remains explicit. Preserved continuing remittances, Marina’s noodles/mealtimes, the horse/tea/alcohol/bread, unrequited love, Sonia’s physical posture and tears, and all final ambient sounds. Rest is explicitly after death, with God, angels and divine mercy retained; no promise of worldly success is substituted. Limited consolation does not settle justice. Revised one wording on review to avoid intensifying “this life is worth the effort” into the more existential “life worth living.” The material Eldritch/Gutenberg textual difference remains fully stated in the source note.
- **05, Antigone:** Preserved the two-option opening, recent civil war, the legitimate need for order, and the distinction between effective public decisions and obedience to injustice. Kept fearful public silence, trees and sailing imagery, the coryphaeus’s changing interventions, age/rank substitutions for reasons, the deserted-city retort, simultaneous personal obligations, Hémon’s threatening words and later accountability, and unequal means of harm. Antigone’s absence, Ismene’s reprieve, eventual but belated reversal, and the refusal to guarantee that a different conversational style would avert disaster all remain. Speaking time is distinguished from arguments being able to change a judgment.
