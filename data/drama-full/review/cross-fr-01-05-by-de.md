# Independent cross-review: French drama essays 01–05

Reviewer: German translation worker, reviewing French against the canonical Chinese source, not against the independent English articles or German wording.

## Coverage and method

Read every Chinese and French body paragraph in all five essays: 01 = 19, 02 = 20, 03 = 22, 04 = 22, 05 = 22; total 105 paragraph pairs. Also read all French titles, decks, source notes and source labels against the Chinese metadata. This is a full reading, not a sample. The initial combined output for 01–02 was truncated; the missing end of 01 and beginning of 02 were explicitly fetched and read separately. Completeness judgments below are based on semantic comparison, not length or paragraph-count proxies.

## Findings requiring or recommending precision edits

1. **01, paragraph 19, minor semantic omission.** Chinese “悔痛” includes remorse as well as pain; French currently has only “la douleur de l’homme resté dans la maison”. Recommend “la douleur et les remords de l’homme resté dans la maison”. The surrounding claim that neither hope nor suffering constitutes completed repair is otherwise preserved. Reported to coordinator immediately; not edited by this reviewer.
2. **04, paragraph 20, pronoun ambiguity in stage action.** Chinese explicitly places Sonia’s head on her uncle’s hand. French “la tête appuyée sur ses mains” may be read as Sonia’s own hands. Recommend “la tête appuyée sur les mains de son oncle” (or singular “la main de son oncle” to follow the Chinese wording closely). Kneeling, fatigue and her tears are present. Reported immediately; not edited by this reviewer.
3. **05, paragraph 15, low-priority clarity.** “elles plaident aussi pour lui, pour lui-même” renders “也为你、为我” but makes the two referents harder to distinguish in indirect speech. Recommend “elles plaident aussi pour son père, pour lui-même et pour les dieux d’en bas”. This is not a proven reversal of speakers; it removes avoidable ambiguity.
4. **02, paragraph 15, low-priority kinship clarity.** Following “Elmire a bien fait entendre la vérité à son mari”, “sa fille” could be read as Elmire’s biological daughter. Chinese simply says “女儿”. “la fille d’Orgon” would make the family relation unambiguous. The central claim correctly keeps the daughter as the person no longer pushed into the marriage.

At the final verification before this note was saved, findings 1–2 were still present in the French files. Coordinator has been informed; this report does not claim those changes have been applied. Findings 3–4 are optional clarity improvements, not release-blocking semantic failures.

## Complete-reading conclusions by essay

- **01 / Une maison de poupée:** All 19 paragraphs carry their Chinese arguments and scene details. The second letter and destroyed bond, Helmer’s initial restrictions, forgiveness versus apology, wife/child metaphor, change of clothes and seating, father/husband continuity, education proposal, acknowledged past kindness, Nora’s imagined miracle and non-symmetrical responsibility, maternal obligations and uncertainty, limited next-step plan, rings, keys and offstage door are present. The conditional judgments are retained; neither Nora’s future nor Helmer’s change is guaranteed. Only the remorse nuance above merits a precision addition.
- **02 / Tartuffe:** All 20 paragraphs are substantive translations. Stopping conditions, cough and liquorice, double demands for proof, delayed intervention, Elmire’s agency without a duty to stage exposure, permissible verification, irrevocable consequences of donation and papers, royal intervention, and the difference between protective deception and exploitation remain. Speakers and sequence are correct. No assertion that recognition itself restores property or resolves all relationships was introduced. Kinship wording can be clarified as noted.
- **03 / La Nuit des rois:** All 22 paragraphs preserve the contrast between collective celebration and Malvolio’s unfinished grievance. His vanity and earlier harshness are present without making imprisonment consensual. The fake letter, yellow stockings, crossed garters, false Topas, robe and beard, darkness, bird/soul test, alternating voices, appeal for writing materials, mistaken accusation of Olivia, confession, revenge threat, incomplete mediation and closing song remain. The translated account preserves the difference between stopping harm and understanding harm, between recognition and licensed retaliation, and between the play ending and the grievance ending. No actionable semantic omission found.
- **04 / Oncle Vania:** All 22 paragraphs retain the accounting work, professor’s proposal and its limits, Vania’s perspective rather than a definitive verdict on the professor, actual danger of gunfire, returned medication, Vania’s own wish to work, unchanged remittances, Marina’s meals and noodles, horse care, candle farewell, unrequited warmth, need for comfort, afterlife promise and limits of universalizing it. God, angels and divine mercy are not secularized. The source note faithfully retains the Eldritch/Russian versus Gutenberg 1756 payment-line difference. The final accounting metaphor is intact. One stage-action pronoun should be clarified.
- **05 / Antigone:** All 22 paragraphs preserve the opening forced choice, war and burial context, Haimon’s respect without unconditional obedience, the legitimate need for order, information silenced by power, tree and sailing comparisons, age/status shifts, the uninhabited-city retort, limited political claim, competing relationships, Haimon’s own dangerous anger and later responsibility, asymmetry in means of harm, spared Ismene, real but late change and the difference between speaking time and influence. No invented political system or guaranteed alternative outcome appears. One indirect-speech referent would benefit from clarification.

## Readability and overall assessment

The French is fluent, structurally clear and complete as essay prose. It preserves the Chinese’s carefully qualified reasoning rather than flattening it into moral slogans. A few literal conceptual expressions are visible, but they remain understandable and do not warrant wholesale rewriting. No substantive paragraph-level omission, character-speaker reversal, invented historical claim or systematic drift was found. Suggested edits are local precision fixes, not grounds to abridge or rebuild the translations.

No French file was modified. This reviewer separately applied the Korean reviewer’s four confirmed corrections to German 02–04 and recorded them in de-01-05.md; those edits are not part of the French review.
