## Semantic checks and concrete drift risks

### 11 — Coriolan

Preserved initial disagreement before the candidate enters, gratitude versus the ability to refuse, remembered grain disputes, colored thoughts, the humility garment and individual/small-group requests. The initial prohibition remains a citizen’s sense of gratitude, not law. Kept the military merits, question of the consulship’s price, request to ask kindly, alms, future friendship, reluctant wound display and simultaneous use of wounds as entitlement. Respect is distinguished from obligatory affection and false performance; the candidate’s dislike of hypocrisy is not treated as permission to dismiss the citizens.

Kept his thought of abandoning the office, half-finished ordeal, Senate confirmation, change of clothes, differentiated regret, tribunes’ valid question about future rule, and their deliberate provocation strategy. The separate paragraph about rewriting citizens’ original motives is retained (rather than collapsed as in the shorter English essay). Initial assent is influenced too; changed judgment through hearing others is not declared inauthentic. Preserved the tribunes’ admission that the reversal is partly the people’s own, each party’s continuing responsibilities, and the distinction between past gratitude and future government. Source note retains Folger/MIT, II.3, spoken voices rather than paper ballots, and non-legal initial refusal claim.

Self-review correction: replaced a potentially inverted French formulation in paragraph 20 with an explicit rejection of the idea that only answers uninfluenced by others count as personal. Smoothed a repeated disposition/disposée formulation without changing the responsibility argument.

### 12 — La Mouette

Preserved two-year interval, right-hand locked door/left-hand chair, garden glass door, adjacent dinner and continuing life, Treplev’s prior account to the doctor, dead child, uneven performances and warm letters signed as the bird. The dead bird and Trigorine’s note remain an idea of a story, not an already published story or premeditated injury. All recurrences of the mouette/actrice correction remain, along with forehead rubbing, Tourgueniev, remembered stage, handholding, third-class journey to Elets, winter engagement and strangers’ unwanted attentions.

Kept request to stay or accompany her, hat/coat, water, adjacent laughter and keyhole, technical acting difficulties, derision shaping self-perception, and the metaphor’s legitimate account of injury without letting it finish her life. Faith/endurance/cross language is not used to justify others inflicting suffering. Treplev’s overly completed picture of her and her remaining fame ambition both remain. Hunger, declined dinner, continuing love for Trigorine, secrecy request, actual departure plan, old dramatic lines and embrace remain. The doctor moving the chair, stuffed bird, forgotten commission and Nina’s absence from that revelation are explicit. The ending is not recast as a happy recovery, nor is Treplev’s death attributed to Nina’s refusal. Source note retains Russian II/IV, 1978 collected-works volume 13, Wikisource/ФЭБ and the limitation on the story idea.

Self-review corrections: explicitly named Treplev as Arkadina’s son and as the owner of the love Nina need not devalue; rendered the final embrace as se serre dans les bras rather than an ambiguous kiss.

### 13 — Minna von Barnhelm

Preserved urgent initial borrowing, withheld good news, no guarantee of repayment, earlier refusal for exactly that reason, Werner’s future, shared canteen and two rescues. The military contribution advanced from personal funds and subsequent suspicion of improper benefits remain; innocence and repayment are separate stakes. Minna loved the generosity before meeting him. Protection does not replace her answer, while wealth does not authorize her to command him and refusing an unwanted gift is not intrinsically wrong.

Kept the rehearsed deception, ring return, false disinheritance over the uncle’s marriage plan, new willingness to borrow/marry/depart/redeem the ring, royal vindication and repayment, and repetition of Tellheim’s words by Minna. His gendered objection does not instantly disappear. The ring misunderstanding, Werner’s new funds and renewed rejection, Franziska’s sustained anxiety, Minna’s admission of excess and Tellheim’s refusal to listen all remain. Both distinct rings, recovery from the innkeeper, putting the ring on his finger, later handshake and keeping money rather than admitting a loan remain. Reconciliation does not magically educate away his old character, and the future is not guaranteed. Source note retains Bell, Dartmouth German comparison, IV end/V, and III.7 for canteen/rescue conversation.

Self-review corrections: explicitly named Tellheim’s returning words and Minna’s stratagem to avoid pronoun ambiguity.

### 14 — La Commandante Barbara

Preserved personal fountain pen, five-thousand-pound cheque, table/chair action, sudden stop, ongoing fundraising and pennies, conditional five additional thousand-pound donors, ten-thousand total, Bodger’s name/spirits business and advertising. Mrs Baines’s practical counterargument and sincerity remain, without making suspect funds solve all donor responsibility.

Bill’s violence toward Jenny and the older woman, refused personal payment, request for compensation, refusal, proposed organizational gift, soul/conversion demand and coin on drum remain distinct from the larger cheque. Undershaft’s explicit horrors and peace-argument reversal, employers’ advantage from poor people’s reduced hostility, and Barbara’s own fundraising compromises remain. Kept public procession, flag/trombone, rank-based endorsement, kiss for Mrs Baines, insigne removed/pinned despite father’s retreat/displayed as return, prayer difficulty, music moving away and continuing mockery. Hand offered to Bill, Peter’s unemployment/age, promise of work, small sum’s three concrete uses, arm-taking and reciprocal conversation across beliefs remain. Act III arms-factory choice is retained without declaring arms harmless or small expenditures sufficient to replace the large donation. Source note preserves Act II sequence and reading through Act III’s end; three labels retain their order.

Self-review corrections: explicitly identified Bodger’s name in the thanks, made the skipped-meal consequence idiomatic, and avoided an ordre/ordre repetition. Work title uses the established French La Commandante Barbara; the Army rank in the body is major and the source label retains the original English title.

### 15 — Fences

Preserved night, yard/porch, delayed reception, dead biological mother, paternal responsibility alongside unresolved betrayal, 1950s Pittsburgh, rubbish work, racial barriers to baseball and Rose’s own domestic life. The two unfair forced interpretations of her response remain; urgent child needs do not settle the marriage. Troy’s genuine step toward responsibility does not entitle him to assign Rose’s labor. The order accept/carry/speak/enter is intact, and her refusal does not return the baby.

Care is not forgiveness or a token restoring the marriage, and admiration for absorbing injury does not make the child a test of sacrifice. Feeding/sleep/illness and labor after a spoken boundary remain. Two months later, church and supper on the stove neither cancel refusal nor cease to count as labor. Both acceptance and refusal require continuing life, not a permanently effective single declaration. The account is not imposed as a rule on other betrayed partners; capacity for long-term care remains a separate question. Seven-year-old Raynell’s flower garden on the funeral morning is kept without promising an unscarred future or concluding all family history. Source note retains II.3, two-month/seven-year subsequent scenes, ending, public transcription and Pearson context.

Self-review corrections: explicitly distinguished Troy’s fault from Rose’s generosity, clarified the departure from a total-rupture summary, and made the final transfer explicitly from Troy’s arms to Rose’s.
