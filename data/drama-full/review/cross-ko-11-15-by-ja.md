## Localized recommendations

1. **11 paragraph 5, rehearsal chronology.** Chinese “话还没有练完” means he has not finished rehearsing the words. Korean “말을 연습하기도 전에” means before even rehearsing them. Change to “말을 다 연습하기도 전에” to retain that the rehearsal is already underway but unfinished. The mockery of those who fled battle and Menenius's warning are otherwise preserved.
2. **15 paragraph 8, implicit observer.** Chinese “婴儿便不知不觉成了一件回礼” describes the unrecognized instrumentalization in our summary. Korean “아기는 자신도 모르게 답례품이 된다” can instead attach the lack of awareness to the infant herself. Prefer “아기는 어느새 답례품이 된다”. The remainder of the paragraph correctly distinguishes the child's claim to care from the father's desert of forgiveness, so this is a local ambiguity rather than an argumentative reversal.
3. **11 paragraph 24, plain language.** “이 구표 장면에서” is more opaque than necessary. Prefer “이렇게 표를 청하는 장면에서” or “이 표를 청하는 장면에서”. This is readability only, not a substantive mistranslation.

## Full semantic checks

### 11

Plural motives and disagreements remain before, during and after assent. The opening prohibition is felt gratitude, not law. Citizens ask about their future treatment while Coriolanus treats consent as a gate to an earned reward. Clothes, wounds, the friend's departure, awkward responses, remaining Senate confirmation and wish to change clothes remain. Objection to false performance is allowed without exonerating contempt. Tribunes identify a real danger and also manipulate the angry candidate, prescribe a retrospective story of the original vote, and admit the movement is only partly the people's own. Influence is not equated with invalid judgment. Votes/voices versus paper ballot and both editions remain in the source note.

### 12

Two years, the garden door, right lock and left chair, adjacent supper, laughter and keyhole, later doctor and preserved gull remain. Nina's repeated gull/actor corrections, remembered earlier play, third-class travel, Elets, winter engagement, unwanted attentions, technical difficulties and internalized contempt are all present. Faith and endurance do not justify others imposing suffering. Continuing ambition, hunger, declined food, love of Trigorin, secrecy and departure remain. Treplev's desire for reunion is not confused with Nina's work-bound future. His idealized view of her is qualified. She neither sees the stuffed bird nor hears the author's forgetting. His death does not establish her duty to accept his love or blame her for it. Russian sources, 1978 volume 13, and the notebook-idea qualification are complete.

### 13

The interrupted good news, uncertain repayment, water flask and two rescues remain. Wartime advance, suspicion, poverty, pawned ring and Minna's initial attraction are distinct. Her disinheritance is clearly a planned fiction; Tellheim's actual trouble is not made fictitious. His new willingness to borrow comes before vindication. Royal relief changes real conditions, but gendered expectations persist. The joke's emotional damage, Werner's unrelated rejection, Franziska's worry, Minna's admission of excess, and Tellheim's refusal to listen remain. The two rings are correctly distinguished; Minna returns the pawned one she recovered. Safekeeping preserves a residual habit after reconciliation rather than making both characters perfectly reformed. The future is not guaranteed.

### 14

The fountain pen and cheque, threatened closure, initial joy, five supplementary £1,000 donors and £10,000 total are preserved. Bodger's name changes the meaning of the news without erasing its material use. The father's proposed purchase is about influence, not necessarily legal ownership. Baines's counterargument, the real needs outside the door and mixed motives are taken seriously. Bill's violence, rejected victim compensation, coin on the drum and soul demand are not conflated. Donation, gratitude and public endorsement differ. The flag, trombone, kiss, silver badge, father's retreat and visible return remain. Refusal is not a comfortable victory. Tea, bed, fare, arm-in-arm sharing and requested conversation remain concrete. The later factory decision retains security that allows refusal, not an acquittal of the arms business.

### 15

Troy's and Rose's locations, birth mother's death, another woman's child, historical setting, racial barrier and both adults' labour remain. A sincere paternal step does not entitle Troy to Rose's work, and he still does not renounce his prior choice. Acceptance precedes refusal; she does not return the infant and does not promise renewed marriage. Care is neither a token of forgiveness nor a measure of heroic self-sacrifice. Its practical labour remains after boundaries are spoken. Two-month supper does not invalidate refusal, and refusal does not erase labour. The continuing need to hear both acceptance and refusal and the warning against prescribing Rose's choice to others remain. Raynell's age, garden and funeral morning give her a concern of her own without guaranteeing a painless future. The source note retains scene 3, subsequent two-month/seven-year life, ending, public transcript and Pearson guide.
