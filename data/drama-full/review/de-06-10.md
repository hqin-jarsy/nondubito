## Semantic checks and concrete risks

- 06: Kept the actor's competent but differing performance, the distinct meanings of laughter, the hat/black mourning clothing, refusal to record a line, the removal of the undressing demand versus an invented sympathetic question, and the director's legitimate need to select without endorsing every convenient deletion. Father, stepdaughter and mother retain conflicting claims; the mother's pain is not reduced to a harmless rehearsal. The accidentally fallen curtain remains a misheard instruction, not the end of the play. The source note explicitly selects the 1921 Bemporad text and excludes the 1925 revision. During self-review removed an unnecessary possessive assignment of the hat brim, avoided moralizing clothing appropriateness as Anstand, and made the scope of the final negation clearer.
- 07: Kept death and real return to life rather than misdiagnosis, the father's request for lethal testing, mirror/shadow examination, Chen Zuiliang's corrected report, the daughter's prior love rather than gratitude-bound marriage, medicinal wordplay, the mother's independent journey, and both imperial decrees. The first decree does not end the play or repair recognition automatically. Preserved divorce as the father's condition, Liu Mengmei's separate injuries, Du Liniang's use of rank without making rank the origin of love, collapse and paternal concern without exoneration, hand/shoulder/bowing actions, and the noncompetitive comparison with Nora. Self-review replaced Seelenwanderung with the roaming of her soul to avoid suggesting reincarnation and explicitly named the married pair in paragraph 12 to prevent an ambiguous antecedent. Edition catalogue, unknown date, reprint status, shelfmark and p0100–p0111 are retained. Romanization preserves publication and scene names without inventing a bibliographical German title.
- 08: Kept the real protection concern and the difference between delaying until departure and releasing her while departure is possible. The stranger's coercive certainty is not idealized as freedom. Wangel's love, grief and relinquishing control coexist; release is not a technique guaranteed to retain her. Ellida can choose to stay, disagree with his psychological explanation, and still must ask the children for acceptance. Legal divorce is explicitly not represented as completed. Self-review named the stranger explicitly in paragraph 9 and clarified the grammar of risking loss while ceasing to decide for her in paragraph 12. In paragraph 13 unecht preserves authenticity rather than moral correctness.
- 09: Kept Oronte's nonthreatening but loaded friendship invitation, the invitation to truth versus readiness for disappointment, Philinte's aside/audience distinction, Alceste's initial indirectness, the old Paris song and his own sensitivity, his overgeneralization about other admirers, and criticism that can be justified yet hurtful. Philinte's peacemaking is useful independently of the earlier flattery. The summons and offstage mediation reported in act IV remain separate from the first encounter. The concession respects the person without falsifying aesthetic judgment; the forced embrace is limited and does not erase later resentment. The three source locations and 1910 edition remain.
- 10: Kept the true family identity alongside the fabricated grievance, the early preference for force, the trust-based temporary custody of the bow, refusal before return, threatened coercion, the attempted shot and its prevention. The army needs the archer, not merely a detached weapon. Trust in Neoptolemos is not generalized to the army. Healing and the oracle remain significant, but do not answer the injured man's fears about living with his previous persecutors. The promise to take him home is accepted before divine assurance, reciprocal protection is offered after unconditional return, and Herakles' command—not a successful human persuasion—changes the route. Kept divine origin, mutual lions, reverence after conquest, farewell details and Johnston's bracketed Greek versus ordinary English line numbers. Self-review made explicit that the threatened capture concerns Philoktet and not the bow.
