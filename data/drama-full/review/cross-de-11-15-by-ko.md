## Precision corrections reported to coordinator

1. **15, paragraph 8 (body[7]):** “Auch muss Rose, um ihm nahe sein zu wollen, nicht zuerst glauben, das Handeln ihres Mannes sei annehmbar.” The preceding sentence mentions “der Vater”; “ihm” can therefore be read as the father rather than the child. The Chinese explicitly refers to closeness with the child. Use “um dem Kind nahe sein zu wollen” or an equivalent explicit reference to the child. This protects the central distinction between accepting the child and returning to the husband.
2. **14, paragraph 8 (body[7]):** “ihn von weiteren Verletzungen abzuhalten” is less explicit than the Chinese “使他停止伤人”. Suggested: “ihn davon abzuhalten, weiterhin andere zu verletzen”. Bill is the person to be stopped from hurting other people, not the person whose further injuries should be prevented. The wider paragraph supplies the right context already, but explicit agency avoids an unnecessary ambiguity.

These are targeted precision refinements, not missing paragraphs or changes to the argument. They were sent to the coordinator for the German translator to implement; this report does not claim that implementation has occurred.
