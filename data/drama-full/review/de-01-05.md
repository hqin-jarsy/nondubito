## Semantic checks

- 01: Preserved the difference between Helmer forgiving Nora and apologizing for his own actions; genuine care is not dismissed as fake. Nora does not possess a fully formed true self or a guaranteed future. Her imagined heroic husband is distinguished from the actual man without making responsibility symmetrical. The children’s unanswered future, conditional mutual change, ring/key exchange and final offstage door remain.
- 02: Preserved Elmire’s agreed stopping condition, the cough/sweetwood misunderstanding, both men’s demands for proof and Orgon’s late recognition. Recognition does not itself recover property or remove the risk attached to the secret papers. The royal intervention is a separate external act. Elmire’s protective deception is not equated with Tartuffe’s exploitation.
- 03: Preserved Malvolio’s earlier vanity and cruelty without treating captivity as chosen or proportional. The dark room, false Topas, soul-transmigration test, switching voices and demands for writing materials remain. Disclosure begins his inquiry rather than requiring shared laughter. The revenge threat is neither excused nor used to demand immediate forgiveness; proposed reconciliation remains incomplete.
- 04: Preserved Wanja’s own initiative to resume work, the distinction between a workable next moment and a just future, the danger of the shooting and removed medication, and the practical importance of Marina’s meals/noodles. Sonja’s rest remains explicitly after death, with God, angels and mercy retained rather than converted to secular success advice. The source-critical note about the omitted payment promise in Gutenberg 1756 is complete. During review, repaired an awkward German sentence about warmth in unrequited care and restored the final image of writing sorrow into the accounts.
- 05: Preserved the real need for public order without endorsing unconditional obedience. Haimon’s love informs and can bias his judgment; it does not invalidate his reasons. The tree/sail metaphors, shifting tests of age/loyalty/authority, threatening speech, asymmetric means of harm, spared Ismene and genuine but late reversal all remain. More speaking time is not confused with influence over judgment.
