Read all five works, authors, titles, decks, source notes, and all 11 source labels in order. This was a full read, not sampling. The independent English version was not used as the controlling text. No Korean files were edited by this reviewer.

## Corrections sent to coordinator

1. **06, paragraph 12:** 완전히 같을 수 없다고 포기할 수밖에 있는 것은 아니며 has a malformed 수밖에 construction. Use 포기할 수밖에 없는 것은 아니며, preserving that inability to be completely identical does not mean abandonment is the only possibility. This is a local grammatical correction, not a change in argument.
2. **07, paragraph 23:** 그녀는 남편 대신 맞았던 옛일을 없앨 수 없고 can attach 대신 to 맞았던, implying she was beaten instead of her husband. The Chinese says she cannot cancel her husband’s past beating on his behalf. Suggested: 그녀는 남편이 맞았던 옛일을 그를 대신해 없앨 수 없고. This is the important agency correction in this batch.
3. **08, paragraph 13 (precision refinement):** 선실은 비어 있으며 means the cabin is empty, whereas the source says the cabin is already reserved/prepared. Suggested: 선실은 이미 마련되어 있으며 or 그녀를 위한 선실은 이미 확보되어 있으며. Preserve an actual available arrangement rather than merely vacancy.

These were reported immediately to the coordinator for the Korean translator to implement; they were findings at review time, not claims that corrections had already been applied.
