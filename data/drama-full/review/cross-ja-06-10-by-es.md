## Localized recommendations

1. **09 paragraph 21, reluctant assent.** Chinese: “阿尔赛斯最后勉强同意说”. Japanese: “アルセストは最後に、ようやく次のように言うことに同意する”. “ようやく” conveys eventual assent but does not explicitly retain reluctance. Suggested: “アルセストは最後に、しぶしぶ次のように言うことに同意する”. Keep the sincere but awkward apology and unchanged aesthetic judgment that follow.
2. **10 paragraph 14, who made the promise.** Chinese refers to Neoptolemus's earlier promise to take Philoctetes home. Japanese “ピロクテテスは、以前自らした約束がもう一つあると念を押す” makes the reflexive “自ら” liable to attach to the current subject, Philoctetes. Suggested: “ピロクテテスは、若者自身が以前した約束がもう一つあると念を押す”. The following explanation already supports the correct reading, but naming the young man removes avoidable ambiguity.
3. **10 paragraph 17, participation in deciding.** Chinese: “持有本领的人有机会参与决定，自己想保护谁”. Japanese: “その能力を持つ人に、誰を守りたいかを決める機会がある”. Prefer “誰を守りたいか、その決定に加わる機会がある” to retain participation in a common undertaking rather than leaving only the broader act of deciding. This is a precision recommendation, not evidence that the paragraph actually argues for unilateral control.
