## Findings sent to coordinator

No omitted paragraph, summary substitution, core interpretive reversal or invented resolution was found. Two localized wording recommendations:

1. **17 source_note:** 전작의 마지막 막 is potentially read as “the previous work's last act,” whereas the Chinese says “the last act of the whole play.” Recommend 작품 전체의 마지막 막. The other edition restrictions are preserved: Act II excerpt, 2004 Cao Yu Selected Works, pedagogical excerpt with deletions/modifications, and Chinese quotations/English paraphrase based on it. This is a scope-label clarification rather than a missing source restriction.
2. **18 paragraph 1:** 가장 쉽게 원상태로 돌아가지 않는 is awkward and less exact than the intended “least easily restored.” Recommend 가장 원상태로 돌아가기 어려운. The paragraph as a whole correctly retains the raised ring, Sheila's nonacceptance and need for time; this is a local fluency/precision recommendation.

These were reported before writing this report. No fixes to Korean were made by the reviewer.

## Full-reading conclusions

### 16 — 자비로운 여신들

Equal votes release Orestes without establishing innocence of matricide. His thanks, pledge of lasting homeland/Athens friendship, departure and the Furies' continued presence remain. Their blood-kin distinction, Apollo's reduction of motherhood, Athena's paternal/male alignment and the status of these as character arguments rather than unchallengeable divine truth are explicit. The Furies' concern over the taboo is not erased, but harm to unrelated land, crops and children is also not justified. Repeated curse, insufficient first explanation, Zeus and thunderbolt-key threat, refusal to use the threat yet retaining pressure, residence versus attacks on citizens, detailed questions and promised ongoing worship remain. Neither agreement nor actual utility vindicates the verdict. Blessings concern the very lives previously targeted. Ongoing obligations bind both city and goddesses; dangerous power is not made decoration. External warfare and those outside the city remain beyond the peace settlement. Underground dwelling, torchlit escort, two distinct departures and absence of future guarantees are retained. Smyth, Kosmos Society and lines 734–1047 are complete.

### 17 — 뇌우

Window closure, visit to daughter, unfamiliar face, two children and mistaken belief in death remain. Calling her a young lady is corrected to a maid's daughter, and expulsion restores the agents of harm to the story. Thirty years of memory and thirty years of livelihood/childcare are distinct. Embroidery/mending, clothes not yet removed from storage, cabinet photograph, recognition followed by questions about motive and instigator are all retained. Genuine feeling, shame, fear and uncertainty are not reduced to a single diagnosis. Furniture, birthday and window habit show remembrance without answering the present obligation. The demand for injured-person consolation, coexistence of memory and suffering, cheque, future exclusion of the Lu family and possibility of compensation without forgiveness remain. Her limited wish to see the elder son without immediate recognition is neither inflated nor declared pressure-free. Zhou Ping injures Lu Dahai, she protects the younger son and leaves with unfinished recognition. Her memory does not exercise Zhou Puyuan's exclusionary power. Her own future and decisions remain beyond testing his character. The source's pedagogical and edition limits are all present, subject to the scope wording clarification above.

### 18 — 죄악의 집 / 검사관의 방문

Doubt itself is not condemned. Sheila questions identity before Gerald's new clues. The local officer inquiry and subsequent chief-of-police call are preserved as actual evidence with bounded implications. Mrs Birling's continuing self-justification prevents invented uniform repentance. Separate photograph viewing, Gerald's never seeing it, distinction between unverified identity and observed substitution, and uncertainty about whether the women are the same all remain. Continuing admissions about Gerald's summer are distinguished from blindly trusting coerced statements. Shared questioning does not require shared relief. The hospital result is time/place limited, absence of death is welcome without erasing earlier harm, and judgments may be withdrawn or suspended selectively. Mockery, movement toward the door without exit, fatigue explanation, ring presentation, provisional refusal and time for independent reconsideration remain. Sheila/Eric have not thereby paid for their own harm. Final reported death en route to hospital and inspector on the way arrive before curtain, without answering all earlier evidentiary doubts. The woman is not reduced to the family's moral lesson. PDF provenance, unspecified print basis, compressed Chinese paraphrase and pages 53–65 are retained.

### 19 — 들오리

The opening reconsideration concerns the method, not love for father. Disputed paternity, father's withdrawal, daughter's distress and Gina's practical priority remain. The found-child conjecture and gifted duck provide the child's own argument for nonbiological attachment. Prayer starts during father's illness and then includes the weak duck without dividing a finite stock of love. Standing, surprise, usefulness question, requested courage, assent, grandfather's assistance and secrecy remain in order. The proposal is called voluntary but pressure and mother's exclusion are not erased. Morning reconsideration is reclassified as deficient courage, keeping the proposal from scrutiny. Father's fatigue/weather/practical difficulties and one/two-day pause contrast with the child's denied pause. Shot, premature interpretation, father's emotion and renewed-life talk precede discovery. Fatal injury, physician's confirmation, Gina's wish not to expose the child to spectators and both parents carrying her to her own small room remain. Posthumous debate is not presented as known future. Neither parental transformation nor failure validates or wastes the child's life. The tragedy is expressly not reduced to one sentence's single effect or made a defence of all concealment. Source preserves Acts IV/V, Marx Aveling, Gutenberg 73631, page markers 112–141 and no detailed harm method.

### 20 — 태풍

Time inquiry, previously set finishing hour, confinement, three disordered men, grieving companions and Gonzalo's tears remain together as in Chinese. Winter-eaves image is not transformed into exculpation. Gonzalo's earlier supplies and books distinguish him from enemies. Ariel's nonhuman conditional and Prospero's judgment of penitence remain character statements. Ariel's freedom request, real rescue, debt argument and renewed confinement threat preserve unequal standing. Reporting suffering neither bargains for Ariel's freedom nor cancels prior threats. Release is not instantaneous: stated staff/book plans are distinguished from performed actions, men arrive still under spell, and recovery is gradual. Alonso's apology/restitution and Antonio's silence remain different. Forgiveness does not waive restitution. Dressing, song, praise, anticipated missing Ariel and renewed ship errand retain service amid promised freedom. Final sea task has a defined end, not automatically deception, but the voyage and subsequent free life are not staged. Caliban's failed violent plot and conditional cleanup remain separate from Ariel's promise. One effective compassionate intervention does not resolve every inequality. Both Folger scenes and exclusion of the Epilogue are retained.
