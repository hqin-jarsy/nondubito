## Required local corrections

1. **16, paragraph 13, polarity of reciprocal obligations.** Chinese says one cannot demand that one side never become angry while demanding nothing of the other. Japanese currently reads: 「一方にだけ永遠に怒らないことを求め、他方には何も求めずにはいられない」. The construction 「ずにはいられない」 means cannot help doing something, producing a reversed or confused assertion here. Suggested replacement: 「一方にだけ永遠に怒らないことを求め、他方には何も求めないわけにはいかない」. This preserves the source's prohibition of asymmetric demands.

2. **17, paragraph 13, compensation and forgiveness.** 「補償を受け取ることは、生まれつき許しを意味するわけではない」 mistranslates the Chinese 天然 as an attribute acquired at birth. Suggested: 「補償を受け取ることが、そのまま許しを意味するわけではない」. Compensation does not inherently/automatically mean forgiveness; birth has no role.

## Additional clarification

- **19, paragraph 3:** 「実の娘ではないのかと疑い」 risks being read as wondering whether she actually is his biological daughter. The intended suspicion is that she is not. Suggested unambiguous wording: 「ヘドヴィグが自分と血のつながった娘ではないのではないかと疑い」. Context supports the intended direction, but this central family fact deserves explicit syntax.
