## Local corrections requested

One-based paragraph numbers. Both points were sent to the coordinator as soon as identified; implementation and verification belong to the French owner/coordinator.

1. **08, paragraph 9: ambiguous speaker after retreat to the husband.** Chinese: “她向丈夫身边退去。他允许她回答，心中却仿佛已经知道回答该是什么。” The paragraph continues to describe the visitor's professed permission and predetermined expected answer. French: “Elle recule vers son mari. Il lui permet de répondre, tout en paraissant déjà savoir, en lui-même, quelle doit être sa réponse.” After “son mari”, the pronoun “Il” can wrongly assign this sentence to Wangel. Suggested local change: “Le visiteur lui permet de répondre…” This is a preventive antecedent clarification, not a finding that the whole paragraph misrepresents the scene.

2. **10, paragraph 23: accommodating response versus friendliness.** Chinese: “一次弥补有没有价值，不必只看受伤的人随后有没有变得好说话。” French: “La valeur d’une réparation n’a pas à se mesurer seulement à l’amabilité que manifeste ensuite la personne blessée.” “Amabilité” shifts the criterion toward friendliness or politeness. The Chinese concerns whether the injured person becomes easier to deal with/persuade, including the continued refusal in the next sentence. Suggested wording: “La valeur d’une réparation n’a pas à se mesurer seulement au fait que la personne blessée se montre ensuite plus accommodante.” The deck already uses “plus accommodant” correctly. This is a local conceptual precision, not an omitted argument.
