## Precision refinement reported to coordinator

- **19, paragraph 4 (body[3]), final clause:** “et elle l’aime tout autant” can be read as asserting equal intensity of affection for the duck and the father just mentioned. The Chinese “她照样那么喜欢它” says that she nevertheless loves the gifted bird so much, without comparing the degree of these two loves. Prefer “et elle l’aime pourtant tant” to preserve the point without an unintended quantitative comparison. The surrounding argument already preserves the coexistence of both affections. This is a small precision refinement, not a missing passage or a shift in the overall argument. Implementation remains with the French translator/coordinator.
