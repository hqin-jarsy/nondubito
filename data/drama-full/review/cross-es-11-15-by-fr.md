Colored thoughts and different reasons for identical assent remain. The humility garment, individual tongues/small groups, hostile rehearsal, first request, “amablemente,” alms, hat gestures, wound refusal and simultaneous invocation of wounds preserve the scene’s physical and verbal progression. The critique of performance does not excuse contempt, and public respect does not demand love for every stranger.

The citizens’ hope for friendship, candidate’s consideration of withdrawal, half-completed ordeal, Senate confirmation, urgent change of clothes and mixed later reactions remain. The tribunes identify a real risk while also planning to provoke anger and supply a convenient rewritten story about the first vote. The independent paragraph on rewriting individual motives is present; the first vote is not treated as uninfluenced authenticity and the second as pure manipulation. Their “en parte” and the people’s own feelings survive. The conclusion retains responsibilities for all three parties and the possibility of an unwelcome answer. Source note preserves Folger II.3/MIT comparison, voices rather than paper ballots, and gratitude judgment rather than a legal prohibition.

### 12 — La gaviota: all 24 paragraphs

The window knock, garden glass door, right-hand lock/left-hand chair, nearby dinner and two-year interval remain. Arkádina is explicitly Tréplev’s mother. His earlier account to the doctor, dead child, unsuccessful early acting, intermittent talent and intelligent warm letters retain their perspective as his account, not the final word on Nina. The dead gull and Trigorin’s note remain a story idea rather than a completed tale or predetermined plan to hurt her.

All changes between gaviota and actriz remain, including forehead rubbing, Tourgueniev, old stage, handholding, Yelets/third-class train/winter contract, unwanted attentions, hat/coat, request for water, laughter and keyhole. Work and Tréplev’s wish for her to stay/accompany her are distinct futures. Technique, contempt shaping self-perception, legitimate injury metaphor and its inability to exhaust her future remain. Religious faith/patience/cross language sustains her without justifying others’ power to inflict injury. His too-complete image of her, her continuing fame ambition, hunger and declined meal remain.

Her continuing love for Trigorin does not invalidate her work, and she leaves rather than asking him to choose her again. Old lines and embrace do not erase the old stage. The chair’s restoration, stuffed gull, forgotten commission and her absence from that revelation remain. Treplev’s death is not made her fault or evidence she was obliged to accept his love. The urgent loan before the good news is heard, uncertain repayment, earlier refusal for that very reason, Werner’s own resources, canteen and two rescues remain. Bell, Dartmouth and the separate III.7/IV-end/V scope survive in the source note.

### 15 — Vallas: all 17 paragraphs

The delayed reception, night/yard/porch, dead biological mother, father’s step toward responsibility and unresolved betrayal are present. Pittsburgh, racial limits on baseball, rubbish work and Rose’s own labor are kept, without making Troy’s injustice cancel her needs. The unfair pair of imposed interpretations remains: receiving the child need not restore him, and refusal should not require hurting a weaker person to prove injury. Urgency does not settle marriage, and Troy’s sincerity does not authorize assigning Rose’s contribution.

Acceptance, receiving the baby, the two sentences and entering the house occur in order. The child is not returned to emphasize refusal, and accepting her does not forgive Troy. The child is neither a reconciliation token nor a sacrifice test proving Rose’s virtue. Feeding/sleep/illness and subsequent labor remain. The two-month scene, church and supper do not erase either refusal or work. Neither care nor boundaries become once-for-all gestures. The example is not imposed on other betrayed people without asking who can sustain long-term care. Raynell at seven, funeral morning and garden preserve her independent concerns without guaranteeing no later injury. Source note keeps II.3, two months/seven years, ending, public English transcription and Pearson.
