# Independent Chinese–German cross-review: 21–23

Reviewer: Korean translation worker. Began only after the German author confirmed final corrections and self-review had been saved after reconnection. Read all 52 German paragraphs plus all metadata, source notes and ordered labels in three untruncated per-file displays, comparing to the complete canonical Chinese essays read during Korean translation. Not a sample. No German or shared-code file edited. This review concerns fidelity, not fresh external verification of the dramatic editions.

## Result
No missing paragraph, material abridgment, major argumentative reversal, fabricated reconciliation, or version conflation found. Prose is coherent and fluent overall. One small physical-detail correction is recommended:

- **23 paragraph 17:** Chinese “那双理头发的手已经停在那里” explicitly refers to a pair of hands. German “lag die Hand, die das Haar richtete, dort” reduces this to one hand. Prefer a plural formulation, for example: “Schon bevor der alte Mann erwachte, ruhten ihre Hände bereits dort, mit denen sie sein Haar gerichtet hatte.” This retains the source's later plural recollection without altering the earlier paragraph's singular hand description. Sent to author and coordinator; no body change made by reviewer.
