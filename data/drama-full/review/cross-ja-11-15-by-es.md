## Findings reported to the coordinator

1. **15, paragraph 14: clarify the subject of deserving love.** The ending “すべての人に愛される価値があると証明する責任” can mean that the injured person must prove that they deserve to be loved by everyone. Chinese instead says that the injured person is made responsible for proving that everyone deserves love. Recommend “誰もが愛されるに値すると証明する責任” or “すべての人が愛される価値を持つと証明する責任”. This is a grammatical-direction issue and should be corrected.

2. **14, paragraph 15: identify whose employment she promises to seek.** “彼女は仕事を探すと約束し” omits the explicit beneficiary in Chinese “她答应替他找工作”. In a passage about Barbara's own disrupted work, the Japanese can be understood as a promise to seek employment for herself. Recommend “彼女は彼のために仕事を探すと約束し”. The following money-counting action is correctly retained.

3. **12, paragraph 3: small precision recommendation.** “恋は不幸に終わり” adds an ending to Chinese “爱情不幸”. The later paragraphs correctly retain her continuing love, so this is not a reversal of the essay's argument, but a nonterminal phrasing such as “恋は不幸なものとなり” would adhere more closely to the control source without adding finality.

These are findings on the reviewed version, not a claim that fixes have been applied. No missing paragraph, summary substitution, other material reversal, or ungrounded resolution was found.

## Full-reading conclusions

### 11 — コリオレイナス

The opening gratitude debate, food-protest history and varied motives remain distinct. The crowd is never made uniformly approving or uniformly regretful. Individual/small-group requests, clothing, wounds, reluctance to perform affection, future friendship and earned reward all remain in their proper relation. Refusing to exhibit one's body is allowed without making past service a claim to unconditional future office. The tribunes' well-founded warning and their manipulative narrative coexist, including the genuine chance to withdraw support before confirmation, offered public blame, and the crucial “一部” qualification. Voices are not treated as paper ballots or a legally compulsory vote. Folger/MIT, II.3 and the gratitude/legal distinction are retained in the source note.

### 12 — かもめ

All door, window, garden, chair and keyhole actions are preserved, as are the two-year interval, initial account by Tréplev, mother's identity, dead child, early stage difficulties, bird, unwritten story idea, Turguénev memory and surviving small stage. Yelets, third-class travel, winter employment, unwanted attentiveness, tiredness, hunger, water, refused dinner and waiting horse carriage remain. Nina's work and Tréplev's hope for a renewed romance are not made the same future. Her repeated corrections do not become a tidy declaration; continuing love does not invalidate work, and religious endurance does not justify harm. His envy does not become an authoritative diagnosis that she is fully resolved. Her final words remain a recitation from the old play, followed by embrace and exit; she does not witness the stuffed bird or Trigorin's forgetting. The eventual death does not impose a duty to have stayed. Russian II/IV, 1978 volume 13, Wikisource/FEB and story idea versus finished story or planned harm all remain. Only the local finality nuance in paragraph 3 is noted above.

### 13 — ミンナ・フォン・バルンヘルム

The urgent opening loan is followed by the earlier refusal, not conflated with it. Uncertain repayment, Werner's own willingness, water bottle and two rescues, wartime advance, accusation, dismissal, wound, poverty and pawned ring remain. Minna's initial attraction and wish to share a future are not reduced to charity. The disinheritance is expressly an invented story that Tellheim believes; royal vindication is real. Mirroring his reasons does not erase his gender hierarchy, and the prank causes real anger and damaged trust, including Werner's repeated exclusion and Minna's admission of excess. Both rings and their different histories are distinguished. The final handshake and money held for safekeeping preserve old habits rather than an instant moral transformation. The unresolved future and existing uncle's consent remain. Bell, Dartmouth, III.7 and late IV/V are accurate against the Chinese note.

### 14 — バーバラ少佐

All sums and conditions remain: the original £5,000, five proposed £1,000 contributions, £10,000 total and the father's filling the entire remaining half. Bodger's identity, alcohol advertising and actual food/shelter needs remain. The text neither makes Baines wholly corrupt nor lets practical benefit settle every donor responsibility. Bill's two assaults, old woman's compensation, refused conversion substitute and gold on the drum are complete. Undershaft's professed arms-industry harm and peace argument are not repentance; buying influence is distinguished from legal ownership. Flags, trombone, publicity, kiss, badge, father's retreat, visible return gift and Barbara staying in the yard are present. Peter's pride, tea, shelter, fare, linked arms and reciprocal emotional need remain. Third-act factory work explicitly requires people with subsistence who can withhold gratitude, without declaring the arms business harmless. Her promised job search needs the beneficiary clarification above. Source coverage and all three labels are retained.

### 15 — フェンス

The opening yard/porch placement, child's deceased mother, father's genuine but limited assumption of responsibility and refusal to renounce his earlier choice remain. Rose's agreement, receipt of the child, words to Troy and entrance occur in the correct order. Welcoming the child does not restore marriage or prove the father's worthiness of forgiveness; praising sacrifice is not allowed to swallow the new relationship. Daily labor, feeding, sleep, illness, two months, church and supper on the stove remain without treating supper as withdrawn refusal. Continuing cohabitation does not eliminate the need to hear her boundary again. The text rejects imposing her path on every betrayed partner; its final sentence in paragraph 14 requires the subject clarification above. Raynell at seven, funeral morning, garden, growth and her own concerns remain without guaranteeing a life free of harm. II.3, later two-month/seven-year scenes, English transcription and Pearson are retained.

Their differences from the independent English labels are not translation errors. The series deck, text-versus-production warning, full-text link, navigation, source label, counts and language label are clear. “続きを読む” is a usable short further-reading link label; no blocking concern found.
