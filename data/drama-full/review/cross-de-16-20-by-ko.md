Reviewer: Korean translation worker. ## Result
No missing paragraph, material abridgment, invented resolution, major argumentative reversal, or edition conflation found. Two localized corrections are recommended before acceptance. The first removes ambiguity in the direction of an exchange; the second restores the precise limit of a stage action. No batch retranslation is needed.

1. **16 paragraph 11, exchange direction.** Chinese asks whether the goddesses have found a life to protect or received honour in exchange for ceasing pursuit: “还是以荣誉换取不再追究”. German “oder tauschen sie Ehre dafür ein, ihre Forderung nicht weiterzuverfolgen?” can suggest that the goddesses surrender honour to obtain non-pursuit, rather than receive honour for giving up pursuit. Prefer “oder erhalten sie Ehre dafür, dass sie ihre Forderung nicht weiterverfolgen?” This makes the contextual exchange unambiguous without resolving the essay's question.
2. **18 paragraph 18, arrival versus entry.** Chinese “新来者尚未进门，幕已经落下” says the newcomer has not entered when the curtain falls. German “Noch bevor der neue Besucher die Tür erreicht, fällt der Vorhang” adds that he has not reached the door. Prefer “Noch bevor der neue Besucher eintritt, fällt der Vorhang.” The subsequent distinction between renewed fear and still-unanswered evidentiary questions is otherwise intact.
