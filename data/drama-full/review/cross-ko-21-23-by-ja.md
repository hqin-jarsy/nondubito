# Independent cross-review: Korean drama essays 21–23

Reviewer: Japanese translation worker. Control: canonical Chinese body_zh and Chinese metadata in data/drama/21.json–23.json. English and Japanese were not substitute controls.

## Coverage and result

Read all 52 Korean paragraphs and all work/author/title/deck/source-note/ordered-label fields against the Chinese, not a sample. Displays of 21–22 and then 23 were untruncated. Rechecked all Chinese source notes and labels separately. JSON checks passed: exactly nine required fields, matching id/ko, Chinese/Korean body counts 15/15, 19/19, 18/18, and one source label in each.

No omissions, condensed replacement paragraphs, core interpretive reversal or invented resolution were found. All three are acceptable in substance. Two localized precision improvements in 21 were sent immediately to coordinator and Korean author:

1. Paragraph 14: 계속 보복했다면 … 처벌받았을 것 casts the conditional as a past counterfactual. Chinese says that if servants continue revenge they will also be punished, preserving the ongoing disciplinary constraint. Recommend 계속 보복한다면 … 처벌받을 것 (within the sentence). This is a tense/modal precision point; the Korean already preserves the presence of punishment and departure authority.
2. Paragraph 15: 끝까지 하려 했던 말 can suggest attempted rather than accomplished speech. Chinese says she insisted on finishing her words. Recommend 끝까지 해낸 말 or 끝까지 한 말 to retain completed speaking. Elsewhere the Korean correctly retains her full answer and longer/slower speech.

No Korean file was changed by the reviewer. Recommendations are not a claim that corrections have been applied. No build, commit or push.

## Complete-reading findings

### 21 — 노예들의 섬

Costume change precedes Cléanthis's understanding. Clothing-size joke and knee embrace remain. Two shipwrecked pairs, exchanges of names/clothes/conditions, corrective education, set term and Trivelin's control of departure remain. Servants repeat commanding power through compulsory courtship, not simply an improved life. Euphrosine's plea, dropped arms, silence, shared childhood, tears and beatings remain in sequence. Iphicrate's benefits/discipline defence is not accepted. Arlequin offers return or friendship before the master's admission of unworthiness. Renewed master-title and self-blame are not automatically endorsed with compassion.

The example is redirected from injured servant to masters. Cléanthis continues questioning unequal demands for virtue and is not labelled merely ill-tempered. Her tears do not replace her speech, nor Arlequin's announcement replace her answer. Euphrosine's admission precedes full release/return assent. Cléanthis retains the condition that future abuse is not her fault, without requiring infallibility before ceasing revenge. Wealth-sharing is only promised; ending talk of servitude may also silence old injury. Kneeling and hand-kissing keep unequal bodily positions visible. Trivelin's approval, disciplinary power and two-day return remain, with the tense recommendation above. Return voyage has not begun. Longer speech does not weaken the decision, with the completed-speech wording recommendation above. All source scene numbers, French provenance, Chinese/English paraphrase basis, exchanged-name identification and PDF 37–42 remain.

### 22 — 하찮은 여자

Addressed envelope, pending sealing and wax, four o'clock summons and marriage within days remain. The guests' praise, flowers/furnishings, secretary post, absent-father remark and ironic knowledge gap are complete. Mother's repeated interrupted attempts, own refusal, twenty-year debt framing and surname qualification remain character claims. Filial sacrifice and sympathy do not imply consent to his proposed repair.

Past desired marriage, departure on a promise, prebirth pleading, repeated delay, refusal, taking the child and changed present love remain. Today's refusal need not deny past injury. Personal-proposal conjecture, leave-after-wedding compromise and duty to other women shift pressure onto her. Religious obligation is interpreted differently by mother/son; false altar promises, hatred, son's humiliation and twenty-year concealment remain. Shame, closed door, pleasures avoided and nursing/church work as permitted spheres complicate the admired home. Her attachment need not be perfect before she can refuse. Greater sympathy does not immediately change Gerald's conclusion.

Hester's embrace and recall of the prior assault lead to kneeling, but not instant recovery. Demands for comfort and reverential martyr language remain qualified. Sharing wealth, correcting inherited-guilt judgment, inviting co-residence and accompanying departure provide concrete support; overseas justice remains hope. Illingworth enters despite refusal, initially offers property and half-year residence rather than marriage, sees the garden, then the undelivered and unshredded letter. Mother's return request, changed authorial intent and reader's attempt to revive superseded terms are explicit. Marriage is offered to obtain son, not made obligatory by arrival of the desired answer. Renewed promise and abandoned ambition do not override refusal. His glove strikes his face; she weeps afterward, so refusal is not effortless immunity. The young return unaware, Hester asks again to be daughter, mother asks if chosen, and they embrace toward garden. All edition limits remain: Act IV with II/III, Gutenberg 854, Methuen 1919 eighth edition, Chinese paraphrase/short self-translations and character-level surname/marriage statements.

### 23 — R.U.R.

Sunrise/book choice, moving to window, hand-in-hand arrival, sleeping elder, book/test-tube/microscope setting and no-touch warning remain. Robots' bodily vulnerability and decline are explicit; Alquist is a builder lacking the lost knowledge. Robot Helena is distinguished from the human namesake. Unfamiliar pains, fear of death, dream language, cottage, overgrown garden, dogs/puppies, licking hand, holding puppies and whole day together remain. Usefulness/beauty questions, different touch and hand lingering on hair precede the experiment; no complete understanding of love is imposed.

Laughing wakes elder, seeming human return, identity, shyness, approach, protective step and immediate dissection order remain in sequence. Earlier representatives' report of manufacturing failure and finite life, threat, earth-wide reward, trembling/killing objection, representative's hesitation/assent and aborted experiment remain. Past coercion does not excuse passing pressure onward. Primus first threatens elder, then extinction reminder prompts same-day-manufactured substitution. Genuine protection still does not ask Helena to consent to losing him. Numerical replaceability question, tears, counteroffer and blocked attempt maintain agency.

Alternating sacrifice keeps the demand alive; escalating death threat is described without added method and not romanticized as love's required depth. The final answer refuses both deaths rather than electing a substitute. Mutual belonging does not require obedience, since her objection changes his action. Shared near future cannot simply be appropriated for species future; formula and decline remain unresolved. Release and departure precede Adam/Eve naming. This particular English version ends there without displayed birth or proven reproduction. Hope does not appoint them compulsory world founders. Unpartnered or nonsacrificial lives are not disposable, nor successful release proof that coercion was necessary. Hair-tending preceded testing; garden and later life are not shown; unresolved book does not consume their day. Source retains Selver–Playfair, Gutenberg 59112, epilogue, original page marker 84 through end, exact curtain point and exclusion of other versions' final monologue.
