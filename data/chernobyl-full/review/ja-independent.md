# Independent Japanese full-series review

Reviewer: Korean translation worker, separate from the Japanese translator.

## Scope

Read every saved Japanese block in all six essays, in sequence, together with titles, decks and complete source notes. Compared the argument, examples, questions, transitions and revised factual claims against the Chinese snapshots plus all 184 entries in `approved-chinese.json`. This is a complete-prose review, not a sample or only a structural check.

## Findings requiring a small language fix

1. `02-lying-to-your-own.json`, title: current `彼らは見た。そして、見なかったことを求められた` is unnatural because the demanded action is missing. Use `彼らは見た。そして、見なかったことにするよう求められた`. This preserves the source meaning and reads as a complete Japanese demand.
2. `03-ninety-seconds.json`, block29: current emphasized clause `一人が上にいられるのは90秒を超えてはならない。` mixes a nominalized clause with a duration limit. Use `<strong>一人が上にいられる時間は90秒を超えてはならない。</strong>`. Keep the preceding non-emphasized text unchanged.

These are local fluency issues, not omissions or factual problems.

## Optional polish

- Essay06 block109: `私はこのドラマを読み` is a literal rendering of the Chinese but sounds like reading a script. `私はこのドラマを観て` is natural for the screen work discussed here.
- Essay06 blocks84/96/110: `版を聞いている` / `聞かされている版` / `整理された一つの版` are intelligible but mechanical. A consistent phrase such as `誰かが整理した形の物語` can retain the constructed-version idea with less stiffness. This is optional; do not let stylistic polishing inflate the prose.
