## Historical and technical boundaries

- IAEA INSAG-7, 1992: https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/Pub913e_web.pdf . Distinguishes the 1975 Leningrad event from the positive scram effect identified in 1983; describes control-rod design and failures of communication and corrective action. Institutional warnings do not prove Legasov’s personal knowledge of a specific flaw for eleven years.
- US NRC NUREG-1250: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-Y3_N88-PURL-gpo80236/pdf/GOVPUB-Y3_N88-PURL-gpo80236.pdf . Graphite displacer and lower-core water replacement clarify the short technical explanation.
- National Security Archive, July 3, 1986 Politburo record: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/19501/ocr . Documents Legasov’s acknowledgment of institutional oversight responsibility without supplying a personal eleven-year knowledge chronology.
- Adam Higginbotham interview: https://www.inverse.com/article/56132-chernobyl-hbo-true-story-miners-legasov-location . Early probable-cause recognition is not a completed investigation; miners’ work is documented but nakedness is not established by the evidence he reviewed. Character conversations and the inquiry plot are constructed.
- Swedish radiation authority Nordic report: https://www.stralsakerhetsmyndigheten.se/contentassets/3b0be724abfa418aa65dd78ea8cfac55/199608-contamination-and-radiation-exposure-evaluation-and-measures-in-the-nordic-countries-after-the-chernobyl-accident . Forsmark detection occurred on the morning of April 28, not a shift-end check.
- Shcherbina historical boundary: https://www.historyvshollywood.com/reelfaces/chernobyl/ . Radiation’s precise contribution to his death remains unestablished; no causal certainty is inferred from the dramatic juxtaposition.

The political provenance of the dosimeter’s chosen range is not established here. The essay retains its institutional reading while explicitly separating that interpretation from procurement history. Lyudmilla’s explanation is presented as oral testimony and dramatic characterization, not a verified fetal radiation-shield mechanism. The roof time limit is not presented as risk-free or as a uniform predictor of later illness.
