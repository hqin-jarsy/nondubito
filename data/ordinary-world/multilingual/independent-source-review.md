# Independent source review: Ordinary World

Reviewer: moon. Read-only source audit and independent EN/DE review, 2026-11-18. Chinese essays and former English versions were read in full. The old English versions were summaries and in episode 1 invented a geographic zoom instead of the steamed-bun scene. New translation review must preserve scenes and arguments, not merely paragraph counts.

## Confirmed original-text corrections

1. **Bun nicknames, episode 1.** Europe, Asia, Africa name wheat, maize, sorghum steamed buns. They are not a geographical sequence. Original volume 1 chapter 1, university-hosted text: https://www.ldzy.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/d9/85/aa860e6e486bb425305d33b6ed44/22172c53-a57d-4601-ac01-7e8f2cf3705d.pdf . Retain the essay’s interpretation of rank.
2. **Kiln chronology, episode 2.** Volume 2 chapter 10 opens in the first spring of the 1980s and describes building the kiln around Qingming. https://99csw.com/book/2002/56678.htm . Volume 3 chapter 53 explicitly dates the school ceremony to spring 1985 and reports Xiulian’s collapse and lung-cancer diagnosis. https://99csw.com/book/2002/56775.htm . Therefore more than ten years at this kiln is impossible; write years/about five years. The novel supplies a diagnosis, not a medical determination that kiln work caused it. Retain bodily cost as a literary juxtaposition, without inserting a clinical disclaimer into prose.
3. **Political marriage pressure, episode 3.** Volume 1 chapter 46 explicitly says Tian Fujun did not know the marriage’s background, thought the couple mutually willing, and became angry when Runye described Xu Guoqiang’s intervention. https://www.chinesepen.org/blog/archives/172187 . Do not include Fujun himself among the adults who pressured her for political advantage. Family pressure remains part of the argument.
4. **Xiaoxia’s real help, episodes 4–5.** Volume 2 chapter 52 shows her recommending Shaoping to Runye as a summer-camp counselor. Chapter 53 shows Shaoping actively asking for her help with recruitment, Xiaoxia invoking her father’s identity to secure favorable processing, and her proposing to ask him to transfer Shaoping out of the mine later. Her father does not know she is using his name. https://www.chinesepen.org/blog/archives/172374 . This disproves both never offered help and never relied on connections. Preserve the distinction between practical assistance and reducing a person to rank; do not make her a perfect exception to the hierarchy. Her support for books/conversation and her own vocation remain valid essay themes. A mere added in-this-reading cannot repair a contradicted factual assertion.
5. **Household registration, episode 4.** Same volume 2 chapter 53 says Shaoping already has registration at Yanggou. Volume 2 chapter 21 also states this: https://99csw.com/book/2002/56689.htm . He is not still registered in Shuangshui. Rural-origin miner is a safe natural formulation without an unnecessary later hukou claim.
6. **Wang Shicai’s rescue, episode 5.** Volume 3 chapter 17: a steel beam travels toward An Suozi; Wang redirects it toward himself and dies. Shaoping witnesses the rescue, rather than being the person saved. https://99csw.com/book/2002/56739.htm . Retain his witness to a worker saving a coworker, not an invented personal rescue debt.
7. **Jin Xiu and the city transfer, episodes 4–5.** Volume 3 chapter 54 separates Jin Xiu’s wish to work at the mine hospital after graduation from Lanxiang/Wu Zhongping’s proposed move to the provincial capital. https://99csw.com/book/2002/56776.htm . Do not equate Jin Xiu’s love with an offer to take him upward out of the mine. He declines two different proposals; their motives need not be identical. His return can still support the essay’s reading of a chosen life.
8. **Final scene, episode 5.** Same chapter: Shaoping returns to the mining area and sees Huiying, Mingming and the dog coming to greet him. No final Uncle Shaoping dialogue. Tell the actual scene without editorial remarks about an invented line.
9. **Lu Yao chronology, episode 5.** Completion 25 May 1988; death 17 November 1992 aged 42. Four years later, not three. https://cpc.people.com.cn/n/2013/0815/c87228-22567219.html . Writing to exhaustion can remain literary framing, not a proven medical cause of death.

Online novel transcriptions were read as primary text, with matching parallel passages checked where available; they are not represented as a facsimile of the user’s physical edition. Chapter numbers are preferable locators to scraped page paragraph order (99csw text is reordered by the site). No novel paragraphs are reproduced in this report.

## Interpretation boundary

Runye’s debt/claim structure after the crash and the claimed structural necessity of Xiaoxia’s death are the essay author’s readings. Preserve them as readings; do not replace them with a generic disclaimer or a different argument. Factual corrections must nevertheless remove false supports, especially the claim of total independence from connections. Xiulian’s natal-family poverty and Runye’s precise work location at the accident remain flagged, not independently established in this audit. No speculative corrections made on those points.

## Translation completeness checks

Episode 1 needs meals, shame, reading/Hao Hongmei, hard labor, the brothers’ contrasted imperatives and the final challenge to promotion within the same ranking. Episode 2 needs the substituted reason for refusing Runye, kiln collapse/recovery, illness, constrained choice, and Xiulian’s own home. Episode 3 needs giving as accumulating debt, the crash reading, comparisons with the three common interpretations and Xiulian. Episode 4 needs books, the appointment, three possible routes, independent journalism and the corrected mixed reality of practical help. Episode 5 needs the rescue, actual ending, return to the bun/ruler motif, coordinate argument and Lu Yao.
