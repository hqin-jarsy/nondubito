## Editorial review

Each foreign text was checked against all 156 Chinese units, including all 36 verse units, rather than sampled. Drafting was followed by full bilingual rereading and a separate full cross-review. English and Japanese also received additional direct source comparison during integration. Reported corrections were verified in the saved text before the final build.

The preceding four published chapters and their terminology records were consulted. The existing continuity registry is unchanged. Established English Hawkes-compatible names, the German project's Kuhn-derived mixed convention, French and Spanish project traditions, Japanese kanji forms and established Korean forms were retained. Newly needed names without dependable project or edition attestation remain conservative project choices; this review does not claim direct collation of every new name against the complete published translations.

Representative corrections:
- Pale-blue 月白 and scarlet wool broadcloth 猩猩毡, following earlier chapter notes.
- Adopted daughter, not stepdaughter; married women servants, not an invented younger age.
- Three total circuits of ancestral bows, not four; rush-cushion dust, not ash or bedding.
- Birth-date cards, fox-flank fur, layered-gauze flowers and the long charred lamp wick.
- No added foreign ruler, unstated textile motif, cotton padding or diagnosis.
- P112 expresses grief for companions awaiting the net, not a deliberate severing of those companions.
- The peace-marriage arrangement is the means of Tan-chun's escape, not an added aspiration to bring peace.
- Go vocabulary, musical gong/shang, li distances, tea names and ritual relationships remain distinct.

Unresolved details remain unresolved: the boat's earlier household, the unnamed Jiang, the bride, the distant smoke and its inhabitant, the faint painted figure and the spiny seafood. The lunar date and Qingming reference were not harmonized. Violent predictions remain the character's poem, not narrator-confirmed future events.

This is a full source-aligned editorial review, not certification by native professional editors or a claim that every literary choice is final beyond further author review.
