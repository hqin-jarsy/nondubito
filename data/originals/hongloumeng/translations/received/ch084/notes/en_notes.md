## Concrete revisions made during self-review and independent review

1. Restored the Palace message exactly as previously published: “Be guarded in speech and cautious in conduct.” No name for the consort was added.
2. Changed the opening from waiting for the hundredth day to waiting **until the hundred days were over**; the final marriage instruction still says **within** the hundred days.
3. Rendered 七七 as forty-nine days, with its seven-week ritual sense recorded in the glossary. The three-year deferral of the proposed concubinage remains distinct.
4. Corrected the box's 押 notation to **pawned**, not a signature. The preceding chapter explicitly says that chest remains unredeemed. The later prescription joke 画押 is **put your mark on**.
5. Removed an inserted “her face” from the old woman's coaxing. “If you find it awkward to say it yourself” leaves the interlocutor unstated, as in the source.
6. Faithful now asks permission to **give her answer after mourning**, rather than telling Lady Xing not to ask before then.
7. Removed the unsupported “like you” while the source query was open. After the author's explicit 2026-11-02 correction, translated 去给…做小老婆 as “go and become …'s concubine”; the query is now resolved.
8. Corrected **Silver / Jade**, **Gem / Jewel**, **Carmine / Crimson**, and **Joker / Xing-er** after actual evidence checks. Kept the existing Shan-jie house form.
9. Kept **two garments**: Grandmother Jia's lotus-mauve silk shirt (绢衫), and the figured-silk jacket (绫袄) fetched for Faithful. The dead hand grips the former. Neither was collapsed into a generic robe.
10. Corrected 蜜蜡 to **opaque amber**, not beeswax; distinguished the stored white lanterns' fresh paste traces from their later dirt/wear elsewhere.
11. Changed the unnamed merchant from an “employee” to a **business associate**, avoiding an unsupported employment relation. He remains distinct from Zhang De-hui.
12. Changed “with her back to the others” to **out of sight of the others** for 背着人, not inventing a precise stance.
13. Kept Doctor Hu's fate as conflicting reports; the incomplete prescription remains a fragment, not a newly complete clue.
14. Removed unsupported sitting posture for Bao-yu and a specified chair for Xue Pan; the source says only that the former was stunned and the latter sat heavily.
15. Restored the explicit sniff after bringing the incense cake to his nose; restored tables/stools rather than an over-generic furniture reference; rendered 粮米 as supplies of rice, not an extra separately invented cereal.
16. Kept Jia She’s 混账东西 as “good-for-nothing wretch”, avoiding a specifically gendered slur not present in that phrase. The explicit whore/pimp/bastard insults in Faithful’s speech remain unsoftened.
17. Preserved the direct address “Your Old Ladyship” in Faithful's final cry, without extra religious promise or approving narrator commentary.
