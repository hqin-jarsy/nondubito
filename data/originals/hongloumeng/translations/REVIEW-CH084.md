## Seven local foreign-text corrections

The original received files remain immutable. `corrections.json` applies each
replacement exactly once while rendering:

- German: `von den anderen abgewandt` → `von den anderen unbemerkt`.
- Japanese: `人に背を向けて` → `人目を避けて`.
- Korean: `사람들에게 등을 돌린 채` → `남들 눈에 띄지 않게`.
  These preserve 背着人 as concealment, without specifying an extra posture.
- English: `Everyone outside knows by now:` → `Word’s all over the place now:`.
- Spanish: `Fuera ya lo sabe todo el mundo:` → `Fuera ya corre la voz:`.
  Xue Pan is repeating a rumor; the narrator has not identified the bride.
- German: the plural dowry sedans become a singular sedan.
- German: `Kleider, Getreide und Reis` → `Kleider und Reisvorräte`, avoiding an
  unnecessary expansion of 粮米 into separately specified commodities.
