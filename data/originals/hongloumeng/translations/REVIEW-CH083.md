# Chapter 83: local final edit and publication

Publication date: 2026-10-24 (reader's local date; the supplied ZIP uses 20261025).

The author approved publication of the eight-language review package. Its text
files and editorial records are retained byte for byte under `received/ch083/`.
The source ZIP and individual files are hashed in `../publication.json`.
The Simplified author manuscript loses only its leading Markdown `# ` marker;
punctuation, paragraph spacing and terminal newline remain unchanged.

Local changes are explicit substitutions in `corrections.json`. Major names follow
the published chapters 81–82, not uncorrected incoming terminology tables. German
old-style transcriptions are aligned to the existing hybrid reader convention;
French named servants and titles, English Sunshine and Grandmother Jia, Spanish
Pinger and established place names, and Korean 희봉/노마님 remain continuous.
New minor names are project choices, not claims to have verified a published
translation. `continuity.json` records preferred and retired forms for future checks.

The English, French and Spanish secret message now makes its second affectionate
expression refer to a person, not merely the speaker's life. It does not name
Baoyu or add an explanation of a marriage. The two repetitions remain identical.
Japanese 蒲団 is changed to a sitting cushion, and one German sentence is made
idiomatic. No destination or fate is invented for Amber, no confession or motive
is added to Xifeng's gestures, and no legal emancipation is added to the servants'
departure. The Chinese “李贵／你妈” and the fictional hundred-day instruction remain.

Traditional Chinese uses the same conversion convention as chapters 81–82, with
context-sensitive corrections including 一發, 鬆, 準, 紙紮, 桌几 and 餘. This is a
script reading edition, not a Chinese prose rewrite. The closing couplet is set
apart in all eight editions, without changing its words.

Verification: 32 body units per language (the review package counts 33 blocks by
including the title), exact author-source hash, complete rendered texts, repeated
message, name-continuity checks, same-language chapter navigation and reciprocal
hreflang. Existing chapter 81–82 reading text and the 48 character essays are kept.
These are text and static publication checks, not a claim of native-editor
certification or newly completed visual browser QA.
