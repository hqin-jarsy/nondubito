# Episode 10 full-translation review

## Coverage and method

Translated the full Chinese episode into English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, and Korean. Each file has all 140 source blocks in the same order, including the title, short rhetorical beats, section separators, and italic source note. No summaries, omitted counterarguments, or merged paragraphs.

After saving all six files, reread every source block aligned with the saved target blocks in three language pairs (English/French, German/Spanish, Japanese/Korean). Then applied editorial corrections and reran exact tag/count, nonempty-block, emphasis-run, and Self-as-an-End parity checks. All passed. This is translation/editorial review, not independent verification against Sanskrit editions or a browser-layout review.

## Editorial findings

- Preserved the sequence from exclusion to clay image, solitary practice, the seven-arrow dog episode, Arjuna's complaint, the right-thumb demand, and the loss of former speed and skill rather than all ability.
- Kept the counterargument about using Drona's name/image without consent, and the distinction between acknowledging sources and conceding ownership.
- Preserved the double qualification of voluntary action: do not erase Ekalavya's agency, but consent does not exempt the demand from judgment. No endorsement of the mutilation was introduced.
- Kept the distinction between developing a pupil and guaranteeing superiority by suppressing third parties; also retained the dog-related responsibility discussion without making it a defense of Drona.
- Preserved the cautious final claim and the source note's refusal to identify ancient Nishada status with a modern category.
- Post-write corrections clarified the English/German rights-versus-responsibilities sentence; repaired a missing French human referent; clarified a Spanish pronoun; corrected Japanese wording for 'unrivaled' and a possessive construction; and polished two Korean constructions.
- Independent title review clarified English, French, and German to make surrendering one’s skill explicit, avoiding the possible reading of merely demanding a performance. Rechecked source/title meaning and all structural checks; refreshed hashes.
- No substantive Chinese source issue found in this episode. Chinese snapshots, HTML, renderer, and git were not edited.
