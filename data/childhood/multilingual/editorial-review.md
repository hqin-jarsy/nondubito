# Childhood: original English retained; factual and dependent-statement repair

## Actual material and contract

All four received Chinese essays and all four received independent English essays were read in full and preserved in `source-original.json`. Chinese contains 115/128/147/171 blocks (561 total, including headings and bibliography). English contains 29/30/33/33 blocks (125 total). The English already presents the principal argument and major scenes, sometimes with its own developed explanations. It has not been retranslated merely because its paragraph count differs from Chinese.

The corrected Chinese maintains all 561 positions. `../source-corrections.json` records 103 changed blocks, including repeated dependent statements in later conclusions, with before/after text, Traditional wording and reasons. The local index/card descriptions have a separate metadata record. English retains its original 125 positions and heading locations; each locally altered block is recorded in `../english-corrections.json`. New five-language editions cover the complete corrected Chinese, not an abridgment of the existing English.

## Factual changes and the argument they preserve

- The baby brother is born before the river journey and dies during it. Tsyganok is nineteen, explicitly in the original. Other unsupported precise childhood ages are not reconstructed from biographical arithmetic.
- Tsyganok explains his pity and his hope that a switch will break, allowing the child to be taken away while another is fetched. He shows his injured arms. Protection is therefore not inexplicable or intrinsically secret. The essays retain care without an exchange demand; they no longer use absence of a motive or impossibility of praise as its proof.
- Grandfather has heard a concrete alternative: the mother says Alyosha's father refused to beat him and forbade her to do so. He still privileges his own history. The intergenerational violence argument remains, without the false assertion that he never encountered a counterexample.
- Reading lessons use a new book and include enthusiasm, praise and shouting, not unqualified serenity. His 1812 story concerns French prisoners brought to Balakhna. Literacy, narrative and violence remain juxtaposed rather than balanced into a verdict.
- At the end Grandfather is alive, has money and lends it. Pleading poverty and borrowing from acquaintances are not proof of complete destitution or a narrated death. Tea may be measured into the same pot. The loss of family closeness remains an interpretation; it is not proved by inventing zero assets or the disappearance of every relative. Yakov's killing of his wife is not attributed to Grandfather.
- Grandfather's prayers include confession and requests for mercy, not a fabricated literal list of debtors and enemies. Aunt Natalya is the initial prayer instructor who refuses the child's questions; Grandfather orders the instruction. The account-book reading is identified as interpretation of his punitive order.
- Grandmother criticizes her husband and describes divine punishment as well as mercy. The child's experience of two religious worlds remains; absolute claims of no punishment or no criticism do not. The child performs his own comparison using conflicting examples, rather than receiving no material from anyone.
- Fists and kicks drive existing hairpins into Grandmother's scalp; a hairpin is not the weapon wielded by Grandfather, and broken fragments are not asserted. Her request not to tell the mother has a particular relational context and coexists with direct criticism. The essay does not newly praise enduring abuse.
- In the fire, the dangerous container holds sulfuric acid. Her shoulder blanket burns; she knows and calls for its removal. She explicitly says her burned hands hurt. Courage does not depend on invented insensibility.
- The cross accident is reported afterward, not witnessed by Alyosha. Grigory and Grandfather make accusations; their accusations are not equivalent to an omniscient certification of deliberate murder. Grandmother's actions after returning do not become an invented continuous embrace throughout the dying.
- Good Thing's verified lessons concern noticing inventions, unnecessary words, and recording Grandmother's stories. Unverified quoted instructions and a rent-debt pretext are removed. The adult narrator places him first among later remarkable encounters; a child does not predict that whole future. The two books connect thematically, not through identical final and opening sentences.

The original interpretive lines—refusing to sort a person into a final label, different conclusions drawn from pain, the household's incomplete domination, and gifts not measured by repayment—remain. This is not a new philosophical thesis substituted for the author's. Accessible original-text evidence and its limits are in the two independent source audits; no claim is made to have inspected every page of the author's named Chinese print edition.

## Reproduction and limitations

`prepare_childhood_sources.py` applies recorded Chinese/Traditional changes from the original snapshot. `prepare_childhood_english.py` applies local English corrections and records them, preserving the original structure. Neither preparer runs implicitly in the normal renderer. `build_childhood_languages.py` uses persistent JSON; English is checked against original English positions, the five new languages against corrected Chinese. `validate_childhood_languages.py` separately verifies both contracts, exact recorded English changes, rendered bodies, documented Chinese changes, Traditional mappings, 30 pages, links, analytics, canonicals and eight language alternatives.

Full independent language reviews, final structural output and browser/layout QA are separate records. A structural pass is not a native-speaker certificate. The source and body work does not change shared indexes, and no push is performed.
