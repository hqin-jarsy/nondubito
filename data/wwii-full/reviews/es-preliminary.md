Scope: all 253 Chinese source units and all corresponding Spanish draft slots read, including titles and decks. ## Overall result

The restoration preserves the full argument sequence, examples, paragraph/heading counts and order. Outside the explicitly pending slots, no whole-paragraph omission or major argumentative mistranslation was found. Spanish is generally natural, readable essay prose. Approved revisions concerning the armistice carriage, coercion at Weimar, individual liability before Nuremberg, the Declaration’s legal effects, and the prior history of November 1989 are represented. ## Actionable translation and prose issues (zero-based body indices)

1. **1-weimar, unit 30, small subject ambiguity:** “su esposa es algo regañona, pero bondadosa, y los fines de semana lleva a sus hijos...” makes the fishing-trip subject sound like the wife. The Chinese subject is Hans. Once the pending fictional-example amendment is approved, separate the clauses: “Su esposa es algo regañona, pero bondadosa. Los fines de semana, Hans lleva a sus hijos a pescar al lago.” Do not independently resolve the historical/fictional status here.
2. **3-four-systems, unit 8, small detail loss:** source 质量管控 is specifically quality control. “horarios, cuotas y controles” is too generic. Prefer “horarios, cuotas y controles de calidad”.
3. **3-four-systems, unit 41, awkward negation:** “la información «no del todo» procedía de una sola fuente” and its power counterpart are understandable but syntactically stiff. Prefer “la información no procedía enteramente de una sola fuente; el poder no circulaba exclusivamente de arriba abajo”, preserving the qualification.
4. **4-promise, unit 7, hanging number agreement:** “Vistos en la larga historia, cada uno...” opens plural and continues singular. Prefer “Visto en perspectiva histórica, cada uno de los principios...” or “Vistos en perspectiva histórica, los principios ... tuvieron...”.
5. **4-promise, unit 41, narrowed object:** “vieron esos derechos aplazados...” narrows the Chinese contrast to rights alone, whereas the paragraph contrasts both reconstruction/repair and recognition of rights. Prefer “vieron aplazadas o condicionadas la reparación y el reconocimiento de sus derechos, o fueron directamente ignorados”.
6. **5-cracks, unit 6, vague phrase:** “de la base inicial a la industrialización” does not naturally convey 从零到工业化. Prefer “de prácticamente cero a la industrialización”, preserving the source’s rhetorical formulation without claiming to verify it historically.

Items 1, 2 and 5 improve fidelity; items 3, 4 and 6 are small prose repairs. None requires restructuring the essay or cutting material.
