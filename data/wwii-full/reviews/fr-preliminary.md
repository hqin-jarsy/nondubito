## New actionable translation issues

1. **4-promise, unit 7 — grammatical agreement, fix before final approval.** “Replacés dans la longue histoire, chacun des principes établis à Nuremberg avait…” leaves plural “Replacés” attached to singular “chacun”. Suggested: “Replacé dans la longue histoire, chacun des principes établis à Nuremberg avait quelque chose de bouleversant.” Alternatively restructure to plural “les principes… avaient chacun…”.
2. **1-weimar, unit 10 — idiomatic completeness, recommended.** “des objets à surveiller, à punir et à traiter comme bon semble” is awkward without a personal complement. Suggested: “des objets à surveiller, à punir et dont on peut disposer à sa guise.” This preserves 随意处置 and the object/subject distinction.
3. **1-weimar, unit 30 — small lexical nuance, optional with approved Hans revision.** “sa femme est bavarde mais bienveillante” renders 唠叨 as merely talkative, losing the mildly nagging/reproachful nuance. “sa femme est un peu grondeuse mais bienveillante” is closer. This is separate from the pending fictional-status decision and should only be folded into the authorized rewrite of that unit.
4. **5-cracks, unit 17 — institutional terminology, recommended.** “le Sénat vota une condamnation de McCarthy” is understandable but “le Sénat vota une résolution de censure contre McCarthy” identifies the institutional act more precisely and avoids an implication of criminal conviction. The approved source already refers to Senate censure.

Minor stylistic preference only, not a defect requiring change: “boucles de retour” is consistent and understandable; “boucles de rétroaction” is the more established systems term. No need for a global replacement if the author prefers accessible language.
