## 图1 / Figure 1 — 戴公

原图1580 × 976，全幅等比例缩至1185 × 732。中央偏左为大“戴”小“公”；右邻“橫千萬字”，左邻“又云馳毫驟”，保留其他邻列。原摄影两端已有截字，未冒充完整全部诗文。

**English.** The large 戴, surname Dai, stands above the small 公, an honorific, left of centre. The adjacent right column is 橫千萬字; the adjacent left is 又云馳毫驟. The whole source photograph is retained. Cut-off edge characters are already present in the museum photograph.

## 图2 / Figure 2 — 國与魚牋

[本地图](images/02_early_columns.jpg) · [官方原图17010106](https://theme.npm.edu.tw/selection/att/collection/04001004/17010106.jpg)

原图1580 × 976，裁框[490,0,1340,976]，输出850 × 976。主体五列完整，最左端另有邻字边缘被截。右上“國”与左下“魚牋”是比较两列重心的锚点；未删除印章或接缝。

**English.** Five consecutive columns remain complete. The crop retains a partial additional neighbour at the far left. 國, completing “capital,” is at the upper right; 魚牋, writing paper, is at the lower left. These anchor the comparison of top-heavy and bottom-heavy columns.

## 图3 / Figure 3 — 目愚劣

[本地图](images/03_mu_yu_lie.jpg) · [官方原图17010117](https://theme.npm.edu.tw/selection/att/collection/04001004/17010117.jpg)

裁框[790,0,1430,976]，输出640 × 976。中央“目愚劣”三字，右邻“失聲看不及”五字，左邻“則有從父司”五字。观察“目”外围与内部留下的纸色；不能将该字在句中的用法释为名词“眼睛”。

**English.** The central three-character column 目愚劣, “describing my foolish, inferior self,” is flanked by two five-character columns. 目 functions as a verb here. Its broad outline surrounds a substantial opening of paper.

## 图5 / Figure 5 — 卷首与后世层次

[本地图](images/05_opening_layers.jpg) · [官方原图17010105](https://theme.npm.edu.tw/selection/att/collection/04001004/17010105.jpg)

全幅等比例缩至1185 × 732。纹样绫地左边起“懷素家長沙”。图仅含卷首部分，不能称前六列的完整照片。纹样处小字、朱印与正文墨迹分别讨论。正文用历史题跋中的补写记载交代传承，不依据图像直接鉴定补写者。

**English.** The cursive opening begins immediately left of the patterned silk. Small writing and red seals are later layers. The photograph shows only part of the opening, not all six replacement columns. The replacement account is attributed to recorded colophons rather than inferred from the photograph alone.
