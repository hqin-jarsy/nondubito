## English

The present cathedral combines work before and after the 1194 fire. The north and south spires have different histories. Flying buttresses do not replace all interior load-bearing elements.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
