## 实际图像与论证边界

正文只有正面附近的两张图像依据；树桩、手臂、腿、头部方向、卷发明暗和胸腹体积均可在这两图核对。无背面观察、无微距伤痕分析、无结构计算或现场触摸。未断言头手较大是透视补偿的已证动机。未断言某一种心情唯一成立。关于“力量尚未释放”“空间有方向”的表述是本文形式解读，并非馆方原文。

## English

Accademia, Florence, inventory 1076, white marble, 1501–1504. The museum currently gives 517 cm including the carved base. The NGA booklet discusses David–Apollo, a different work; only its figure 2 is a photograph of this David.

The linked institutional records supply identification and historical context. The close reading and optional SAE connections are the essay’s interpretation, not claims attributed to the artist.
