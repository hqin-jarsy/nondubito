## 3. 可选SAE练习 / Optional SAE exercise

Han Qin (秦汉), [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant, 2nd edition](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html), 2026. [DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19296710](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19296710). 原站署明 / Source license: CC BY 4.0.

本文仅使用美与知识正确性、个人偏好与可讨论判断根据之间的区分；具体两组叶形的练习是本文的应用，不是对卢梭意图的历史论断，也不是将此画归入某条射线的证明。本篇无需引用某一分篇射线。
The exercise uses distinctions between beauty and factual correctness, and between personal preference and discussable grounds of judgment. The comparison of two leaf groups is this essay's application, not a historical claim about Rousseau's intentions or proof that the painting belongs to a particular ray. No individual ray is assigned.

## 4. 解读范围 / Interpretive scope

清晰叶形、浅层遮挡、色点联系、动物接触及草叶遮挡均依图像实看。它们的空间与节奏效果是可讨论的解释。未推断颜料层、用笔工具、制作顺序、动物遭遇的前因后果或画家的心理动机；未把其他作品的标本、照片等来源移用于本画。
Visible leaf shapes, shallow overlaps, colour connections, animal contact and screening grasses were checked against the image. Their spatial and rhythmic effects remain interpretations. The essays do not reconstruct pigment layers, tools, painting sequence, a preceding or subsequent animal narrative, or the artist's psychological motive; no source belonging to another Rousseau painting is reassigned to this one.
