## English

The main dome was built in 1420–1436, above an earlier cathedral project. The pointed octagonal double shell is not a hemisphere. Work without full traditional centring did not mean construction without scaffolds, lifting machinery or temporary works.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
