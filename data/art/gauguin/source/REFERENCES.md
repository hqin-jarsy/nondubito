# 参考资料 / References

核验日期 / Checked: 2026-10-15

正文以对官方图像的独立观看为主。以下来源分别承担作品资料、历史背景、理论和图像许可的证据，不把通用生平、其他作品的研究或书目条目冒充本画的专属技术发现。

The essays are primarily independent visual readings of the museum image. The sources below have distinct roles: object facts, historical context, contemporary theory and image licensing. General biography and studies of other paintings are not presented as technical findings about this work.

## 作品 / The painting

1. Cleveland Museum of Art. [The Large Tree, 1891, accession 1975.263](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1975.263).
   - Artist, title, date, medium, unframed dimensions, credit line and inscriptions; also the museum’s account of the foreground sequence and shade/sunlight contrast.
   - 画家、题名、年代、媒材、未装框尺寸、收藏信息、题字；馆方关于前景横列与阴凉/日晒对照的说明。
   - The catalogue identifies four women in the foreground. The doorway contains an additional smaller figure visible in the image; the essays do not describe the entire painting as containing only four people.
   - 馆方的“四位女性”用于前景；正文另述门口较小的人物，不将全画人数限定为四。

2. Cleveland Museum of Art. [Official collections API record](https://openaccess-api.clevelandart.org/api/artworks/?accession_number=1975.263).
   - API artwork ID 148297. Confirms CC0, print-image URL and size listing, inscriptions “Te raau rahi” and “P Gauguin 91”, and museum metadata. Actual downloaded bytes and pixel dimensions are recorded in image_manifest.json.
   - Catalogue bibliography was consulted only as a bibliography. No unread item concerning photography or Puvis de Chavannes is treated as proof of a model for this painting. No work-specific pigment, X-ray or layer report is claimed.
   - The 1975 accession prefix and the record’s 1976 accession date are not used to infer an acquisition story; the essays give the accession number and credit line without assigning a donation year.

## 历史背景 / Historical context

3. Cindy Kang. [Paul Gauguin (1848–1903)](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/paul-gauguin-1848-1903). Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, March 2011.
   - Supports the limited contextual points used in the essay: the colonial pavilions of the 1889 Paris Exposition Universelle influenced Gauguin’s overseas expectations; his artistic ambitions remained directed towards the Parisian avant-garde.
   - It also discusses the conjunction of decorative pattern and figuration in Gauguin’s Tahitian work. The present essay develops its own image-specific analysis.
   - Its discussion of photographic sources for other paintings is not transferred to The Large Tree. No specific photograph, sitter identity, garment history or tree species is asserted here.

## 可选理论 / Optional theory

4. Han Qin (秦汉). [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant / SAE 判断力与美学 · 致敬康德](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html), second edition, 2026. [DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19296710](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19296710). CC BY 4.0.
   - The optional section uses §3.2, “The Internality of Context in Beauty / 美的背景内在性”, as a contemporary question about the viewer’s judgment.
   - It compares the pictorial invitation to approach with the distance maintained by the figures’ orientation and occlusion. Colonial context informs the question of whether visual access amounts to understanding the people depicted; it is not presented as proof of a documented target audience for this painting. This painting-specific exercise is the essay’s application, not a claim made by the original SAE text.
   - No numbered ray is assigned to the work, and no inference is made about the artist’s self-legislation, the figures’ inner states, or mutual recognition between them.
