The essay is an independent close reading. These sources support object information, history, and theory. Visual observations and interpretive claims are developed in the essays. Technical findings about the related night painting have not been transferred to the Winter Morning.

## 作品与官方图像 Object and official image

1. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. [Camille Pissarro, The Boulevard Montmartre on a Winter Morning, 1897](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437310). Object 60.174. Supports title, date, medium, dimensions, gift credit, Grand Hôtel de Russie viewpoint, and the fourteen Boulevard Montmartre / two Boulevard des Italiens paintings made between February and April 1897. The essays paraphrase the museum's account; the artist's quoted letters have not been reproduced.

2. The Met Collection API. [Object 437310](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/437310). Confirms the public-domain designation and the primaryImage endpoint. [Original-size public image](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ep/original/DP-21959-001.jpg), 4000 × 3230 pixels. This is the original-size file available through the public API, not a claim about the size of an archival master.

3. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. [Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access). Public-domain collection images made available under CC0. The full download is unchanged; crops are specifically identified in IMAGE_CREDITS.md.

## 大道与系列 The boulevard and series

4. National Gallery, London. [Camille Pissarro, The Boulevard Montmartre at Night, 1897, NG4119](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/camille-pissarro-the-boulevard-montmartre-at-night). Supports the distinction between the boulevard and the Montmartre hill district, the hotel viewpoint and series of varying conditions, and the night picture's differentiated artificial lights. Linked comparison only; no night-view image is reproduced. Its claims about repainting, completion over several sittings, and particular lamps refer to NG4119 and are not asserted for the Met canvas.

5. Ville de Paris, Observatoire parisien des mobilités. [Bulletin no. 128, fourth quarter 2021, page 4, Portrait de rue](https://cdn.paris.fr/paris/2022/04/15/ea8157484cbd27e87b85bc4b8d96a064.pdf#page=4). Dates completion of Boulevard Montmartre to 1705. This official street history prevents an inaccurate identification of this older boulevard as a newly cut Haussmann street. The PDF is linked, not included in the package.

## 可选理论 Optional theoretical reading

6. Han Qin (秦汉). [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant / SAE 判断力与美学 · 致敬康德](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html), second edition, 2026. [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19296710](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19296710), author's site marked CC BY 4.0. The optional section draws specifically on §3.2, the internality of context. The comparison of Pissarro's morning and night views is this essay's application, not a claim made by the artist or an art-historical proof of the philosophical framework. No individual aesthetic ray is assigned merely because the picture shows traffic.

## 证据边界 Scope of evidence

- 可见：构图、车流疏密、树枝/窗列关系、颜色邻接、笔触形状；这些均可在随包原图和局部上核对。
- 有馆方/官方来源：画名、年份、媒材、尺寸、收藏信息、旅馆视点、系列数量与月份、道路地理与历史。
- 本文解释：稳定视角如何容纳不等节拍，固定地点的反复描绘如何使城市时间成为绘画问题。
- 未声称：车辆精确速度、动作真实同时性、画家逐笔顺序或手速、作品在一个清晨完成、近时具体降雨、浅色路面就是积雪。

Visible relationships are distinguished from documented facts and the essay's interpretations. The text does not reconstruct exact traffic speeds, real simultaneity, stroke sequence, painting speed, a one-morning execution, a particular recent rainfall, or a blanket of snow.
