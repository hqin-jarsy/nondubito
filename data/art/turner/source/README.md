## 本篇的观看路线 / Reading route

从月光打开的水道走到右侧照明火光前的船工，再退回全图。文章讨论亮度与看清的差别、观看距离改变了什么、两种光怎样落在同一片水上，以及油画材料如何建立水光。历史背景紧随初看，随后回到图像中的具体关系。可选SAE部分包括临时遮挡与隔时复看两个观看实验。

Move from the moonlit channel toward the workers beside the fires, then return to the full painting. The essay considers brightness and visibility, the effects of viewing distance, two kinds of light on one body of water, and the paint that produces reflected light. Historical context follows the first visual encounter before the essay returns to specific painted relationships. Two optional SAE exercises use temporary occlusion and looking again after an interval.
