# 质量审核说明 / Quality review

版本 / Version: 0.1 · 2026-10-14

本包为八语完整作者审阅稿。审核包括实际看图、来源核对、各语言全文审读、跨语言意义核对和文件完整性检查。文字审读采用模型辅助方式；没有经过八语人类母语编辑终校。

This is a complete eight-language package for author review. Review covers actual image inspection, source checks, full-text reading in each language, cross-language semantic comparison, and file-integrity checks. Editorial reading was model-assisted; the package has not received final editing by human native-language editors in all eight languages.

## 作品与论证 / Painting and argument

已对照全图与三张局部，检查从月光水道到右侧人物、从局部回到全图、冷暖光的交接，以及水面颜料的具体描述。文章保持“亮度与看清有差别”这一观看主线，并将它作为本文提出的读法；没有以画家心理或现代立场替代画面论证。

The full image and three details were inspected against the descriptions of the moonlit channel, figures by the fires, changing viewing distance, warm and cool light, and paint in the water. The distinction between brightness and seeing clearly remains the essay's own reading, developed through visible relationships.

已核对1835年、希尔兹港区、委托和1834年威尼斯对幅；煤的转运方向为从低矮平底船越过较大海船船舷。近处火光统一按照明功能叙述，不把所有烟霭归为同一种燃料。右前方木料保留审慎描述，不确认为木筏。1835年评论明确经Hayes的1992年目录转引。材料段依据该目录的技术记录，未把水彩介入的推测写成确证。

Checks covered the date, Shields setting, commission, and 1834 Venetian companion; coal moves from low keels over the higher sides of larger seagoing vessels. Nearby fires are described by their illuminating function, without assigning every haze to one fuel. The foreground timber is not confidently classified as a raft. The 1835 criticism is explicitly reported through Hayes's 1992 catalogue. Material claims follow that catalogue; its tentative suggestion about watercolor is not converted into certainty.

## 八语全文 / All eight texts

简繁中文采用同一稿件。英德法西日韩保留完整观察、推理和结论，以各语言重新组织句法与表达。逐篇审读覆盖正文、可选SAE、图注、替代文字和资料说明，并核对各语的史实与论证没有被缩成摘要。

Simplified and Traditional Chinese share one text. The English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, and Korean versions preserve the full observations, reasoning, and conclusions while reworking syntax and phrasing for their readers. Full-text reading covered the main essays, optional SAE sections, captions, alternative text, and source notes, including checks for abridgment or loss of argument.

本轮修订包括：把“较高的船舱”明确为越过高船船舷；避免题名语序因语言不同产生矛盾；区分火光前可见轮廓与可辨面容；修正法西语中由“感受”自行解释理由的句法，日语注意力对象的意思，以及韩语助词、观察主体和带有秘密意味的“幽会”增义。也调整了少数直译感较强的句子。

Revisions clarified the movement over a ship's side, avoided title-order assumptions across languages, and retained the distinction between a visible outline and a legible face. Language edits addressed agency in French and Spanish sentences about explaining responses, the object of attention in Japanese, and Korean particles, the subject of observation, and an unintended suggestion of a secret meeting. Several literal-sounding phrases were also smoothed.

## SAE观看实验 / SAE viewing exercises

临时遮挡实验的提问与实际遮住的范围一致，不把剩余画面误称为只剩某一种光。隔时记忆实验依次为合上画面、等待片刻、写下记忆、重开核对。感知、调用经验辨认事物以及隔时记忆与预期之间的差别保留清楚。两个练习都回到可指认的图像关系，主体艺术导读可独立阅读。

Questions in the temporary-occlusion exercise match the regions actually covered, without claiming that one light source is all that remains. Delayed recall proceeds by closing the image, allowing an interval, recording remembered details, and reopening it to compare. Perceptual appearance, experience involved in recognition, and memory and anticipation remain distinct. Both exercises return to identifiable image relationships; the main art reading stands independently.

字数与校验值只用于发现遗漏和检查文件完整性，不作为文学质量或语言自然度的证明。数字复制品的亮度、颜色和放大尺度受显示条件影响；本次审读不替代原作观察或新的材料检测。

Counts and checksums help detect omissions and verify file integrity; they do not establish literary quality or natural language. Brightness, color, and enlargement depend on display conditions. This review does not replace examination of the original painting or new material analysis.
