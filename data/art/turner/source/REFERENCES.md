# 参考资料 / References

核查日期 / Checked: 2026-10-14

本篇以美国国家美术馆开放图像的独立细读为主体。关于亮度与看清的差别、观看距离如何改变人物的分量，以及全景与装煤现场之间的往返，是本文的解释。以下资料分别支持作品数据、转运工序、历史评论、绘画材料和可选理论阅读。

This essay is grounded in independent close looking at the National Gallery of Art's open-access image. Its discussion of brightness and visibility, viewing distance, and the movement between a harbor spectacle and a working site is an interpretation developed here. The sources below support object data, coal-transfer procedures, historical reception, painting materials, and the optional theoretical reading.

## 作品与历史 / Painting and history

1. [National Gallery of Art: Keelmen Heaving in Coals by Moonlight](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/1225-keelmen-heaving-coals-moonlight). 1835年，布面油画，92.3×122.8厘米，Widener Collection，1942.9.86。核对作品信息、展史、委托与对幅关系；同页音频说明由平底小船向海船装煤。/ Object data, exhibition history, commission, and companion painting checked. The audio transcript describes coal being heaved from flat-bottomed keels into seagoing colliers.

2. [National Gallery of Art: Sublime Light](https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/sublime-light-jmw-turner-keelmen), 2026-01-12. 用于希尔兹港区、麦康奈尔的纺织业背景、1834年威尼斯对幅，以及浪漫主义的简短介绍。两画对应英国兴起与威尼斯衰落的解释保持为可能性。正文不据此推定透纳的环境立场或为他宣布政治意图。/ Used for the Shields setting, McConnel's textile-business background, the 1834 Venetian companion, and brief Romantic context. The economic-rise-and-decline reading of the pair remains a possibility rather than a declaration of Turner's intention.

Used for the impasto of the moon and water highlights and the thin, fluid handling of the sky. The catalogue's tentative watercolor suggestion is not presented as a confirmed identification.

## 转运工序 / Coal handling

4. [National Coal Mining Museum: The Hidden Lives of the Coal Traders](https://www.ncm.org.uk/whats-on/the-hidden-lives-of-the-coal-traders/), “Along the Coaly Tyne.” 用于煤由河岸装入keel、沿河运往海运煤船、从低船向高船人力铲装的流程。本文不把行业一般知识当成每一个画中姿势的逐项鉴定。/ Used for coal moving from shore facilities into keels, downriver to colliers, and upward from low boats into larger ships. General transport history is not treated as an exact identification of every painted gesture.

5. [Tyne + Wear Building Preservation Trust: Keelmen's Hospital](https://keelmens.twbpt.org.uk/), “A Brief History.” 用于keelmen的职业与平底小船向深水段海船送煤的背景；医院及现代修复内容未用于正文。/ Used for the occupation of the keelmen and their flat-bottomed boats' role in transferring coal to seagoing ships in deeper water. Hospital and restoration history is outside the essay's scope.

## 可选SAE阅读 / Optional SAE reading

6. Han Qin / 秦汉, [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html), 2026, second edition. 用于将个人审美印象继续展开为可讨论的观看根据，并把不同“射线”作为问题的方向。/ Used as a framework for moving from personal response toward discussable grounds in looking, with the rays understood as directions of inquiry.

7. Han Qin / 秦汉, [The Beauty of Perception / 感知之美](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-8.html), 2026. 可选部分的临时遮挡实验追问亮暗、宽窄和方向如何在一次观看中呈现。/ The temporary-occlusion exercise asks how light, dark, width, and direction appear within an act of looking.

8. Han Qin / 秦汉, [The Beauty of Cognition / 认知之美](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-9.html), 2026. 可选部分的隔时复看实验追问记忆保留了什么，以及新观察如何改变期待。/ The delayed-return exercise asks what memory retains and how renewed observation alters anticipation.

这两项实验为本文设计的当代观看练习，不是对透纳本人理论意图的追认。理论链接用于继续阅读，正文导读不以读者预先接受该体系为前提。

These are present-day viewing exercises designed for this essay, not attributions of theoretical intention to Turner. The links offer further reading; the main essay does not require prior acceptance of this framework.
