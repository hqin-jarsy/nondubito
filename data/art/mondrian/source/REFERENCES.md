## 证据范围 Scope

视觉分析依据已实看官方全图及同源局部。本文不使用未获本件证据支持的黄金分割、固定色彩象征或数学最优解释；也不把其他蒙德里安作品的X光、颜料或改画报告转用于本件。关于线条停止、白区形状及色块比例的解释，是可回到图上讨论的细读判断。

Visual interpretations are based on the official image and its details. The essays do not claim a golden-ratio scheme, fixed symbolic meanings for the colors, mathematical optimality, or a reconstructed sequence of execution. Technical findings about other Mondrian works are not transferred to this painting.
