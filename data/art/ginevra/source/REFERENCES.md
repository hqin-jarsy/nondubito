# 第四篇参考资料 / Episode 04 references

核查日期 / Checked: 2026-10-14

八语文章共用以下资料。正文以独立看图与论证为主体；文献用于核实作品资料、传统解释与研究分歧。面貌、姓名与赞辞之间的关系，以及可选SAE观看练习，是本篇提出的读法。

The eight essays share these references. Independent close looking provides the main argument; the sources verify object information, historical interpretations and scholarly disagreements. The relationship between appearance, name and praise, and the optional SAE exercises, are the essay's readings.

## 正文资料 / Sources for the main essay

1. National Gallery of Art, **Ginevra de’ Benci [obverse]**, Leonardo da Vinci, collection record, 1967.6.1.a.
   https://www.nga.gov/artworks/50724-ginevra-de-benci-obverse
   - 采用馆藏媒介、年代、尺寸、收藏信息，以及导览中的肖像姿势语境。杜松的名称关联与后世蜡印也由此核对。
   - Used for medium, date, dimensions, collection, portrait-pose context, the juniper/name association and the later wax seal.
   - 现存原板38.1×37厘米；42.7×37厘米包括后加底边木条。网页简要介绍中的婚姻语气较确定，本文依详细研究保留委托问题的开放性。
   - The surviving original panel measures 38.1×37 cm; 42.7×37 cm includes a later strip. The short introduction's firm marriage wording is qualified in the essay in light of specialist discussion.

2. National Gallery of Art, **Wreath of Laurel, Palm, and Juniper with a Scroll inscribed Virtutem Forma Decorat [reverse]**, 1967.6.1.b.
   https://www.nga.gov/artworks/50725-wreath-laurel-palm-and-juniper-scroll-inscribed-virtutem-forma-decorat-reverse
   - 核对反面tempera分类、植物辨认、箴言、本博徽识与底层铭文。反面委托较有根据，正面委托仍未证实。媒介分类本身不是蛋黄黏结剂的化验结论。
   - Used for the tempera classification, plants, motto, Bembo's emblem and underlying inscription. Evidence for his reverse commission is stronger than for the obverse; the medium category alone is not a chemical identification of egg binder.
   - 拉丁文按原画辨读为VIRTVTEM FOR / MA DECORAT；通常拼写为VIRTUTEM FORMA DECORAT。引用的是历史画面上的短句，不是现代译文长段。
   - The Latin is read from the painting; the short quotation belongs to the historical artwork.

3. David Alan Brown, ed., **Virtue and Beauty: Leonardo’s Ginevra de’ Benci and Renaissance Portraits of Women** (National Gallery of Art, 2001). Entries 16 and 17, by David Alan Brown; printed pp.142, 145–146 and 148.
   https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/virtue-and-beauty-leonardos-ginevra-de-benci-and-renaissance-portraits-women
   - 研究中核读相关条目，并核看142、145页。用于身份与婚姻资料、仿斑岩底、礼赞与委托方案、现代手部重构的来源。分阶段完成等仍为研究方案。
   - Relevant entries were consulted, with pp.142 and 145 visually checked. Used for identity, marriage, the porphyry-like ground, praise and patronage hypotheses, and the provenance of the modern hand reconstruction. Staged execution remains a hypothesis.
   - 此处是2001年展览图录，不是2003年系统目录；原书PDF及重构图不随包分发。
   - This is the 2001 exhibition catalogue, not the 2003 systematic catalogue. Its PDF and reconstruction images are not redistributed.

4. Royal Collection Trust, **A study of a woman’s hands**, Leonardo da Vinci, RCIN 912558, c.1490.
   https://www.rct.uk/collection/912558/a-study-of-a-womans-hands
   - 研究中核到的现行官方说明将风格定于更晚时期，并提出与《抱银貂女子》早期构思有关的可能性；同纸上为两次交叠双手姿态的研究。这与常见吉内薇拉重构并不完全一致。
   - The current official account consulted in research dates the style later, proposes a possible link with an early conception of *Lady with an Ermine*, and describes two studies of crossed hands. It differs from the familiar Ginevra reconstruction.
   - 直接网页有时只显示网站界面；准备研究采用的完整官方索引说明已核对。该素描图像未加入本包。
   - Direct page access sometimes returns only site navigation; the full indexed official description was checked during preparatory research. The drawing is not reproduced here.

5. John K. Delaney, Kathryn A. Dooley, Lisha D. Glinsman, Paola Ricciardi and Barbara H. Berrie, **“Leonardo da Vinci’s Ginevra de’ Benci: The palette and further observations on underdrawing and undermodeling,”** Technè, Hors-série 2024, pp.76–85; online June 20, 2025.
   https://journals.openedition.org/techne/21418
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.4000/11v44
   - 正式HTML全文已核读；正文采用含铜绿色颜料褐变及杜松／天空边界修改的研究。技术图和假彩色合成不作为真色复原。
   - The published HTML was consulted. The essay uses findings on altered copper greens and revisions to juniper/sky boundaries. Technical images and false-colour composites are not treated as colour reconstructions.
   - 文本许可CC BY-NC-ND 4.0不等于附图一律开放；本包不复制研究图。
   - The text's CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licence does not establish the same rights for all illustrations; no research image is reproduced.

6. Barbara H. Berrie, **“Pigment Alteration and Color Change,”** Facture, vol.7, 2025, Ginevra discussion on printed p.20 and notes 61–62.
   https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/facture-vol-7-change/pigment-alteration-and-color-change
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.66322/kyoa2476
   - 相关段落及PDF页已核读、看图。用于脸部红色淀褪色的限制；没有可供本篇使用的精确失色百分比或已确证原色复原。
   - Relevant text and PDF page were consulted and visually checked. Used for the red-lake fading qualification; no exact percentage of loss or definitive colour reconstruction is claimed.

## 可选SAE资料 / Optional SAE reading

7. 秦汉 / Han Qin, **SAE判断力与美学 / SAE Judgment and Aesthetics**, second edition, 2026.
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html
   - 总体区分个人审美与判断可共同承认的根据；射线为提问方向。
   - General distinction between personal aesthetic response and grounds for shared acknowledgment; rays are directions of inquiry.

8. 秦汉 / Han Qin, **感知之美 / The Beauty of Perception**, 2026.
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-8.html
   - 用于放下识别用途后，比较皮肤、卷发、针叶仍然呈现的不同形状与边缘。
   - Applied to the appearances of skin, curls and needles when their identifying uses are temporarily set aside.

9. 秦汉 / Han Qin, **认知之美 / The Beauty of Cognition**, 2026.
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-9.html
   - 用于学到姓名关联后，记忆怎样进入下一次观看、期待又怎样受眼前细节修正。
   - Applied to how learning the name association affects a later look, and how visible details can revise expectations.

10. 秦汉 / Han Qin, **因果之美 / The Beauty of Causation**, 2026.
    https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-5.html
    - 用于观察裁切、变色及后加痕迹怎样使过去留在当前物件中。
    - Applied to how cutting, altered colour and later additions leave the past in the present object.
