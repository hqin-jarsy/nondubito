## 本篇 / This essay

本篇先看面孔、发卷与针叶，再沿杜松从正面的环境走向反面的姓名标志。面貌、姓名与赞辞相连，却不能彼此取代。全篇同时保留反面铭文的绘画性、委托问题的不同证据强度，以及颜色变化与被裁下缘带来的观看条件。

The essay begins with the face, curls and needles, then follows the juniper from the setting on the front to the naming emblem on the back. Appearance, name and praise are connected without becoming interchangeable. The reading also explores the painted form of the inscription, differing degrees of evidence about patronage, and the effects of altered colour and a cut-down panel.
