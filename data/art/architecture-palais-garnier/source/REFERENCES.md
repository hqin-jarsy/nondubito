## English

Garnier won the competition in 1861 and the opera house opened in January 1875. The 1877 painting is an interpreted social scene, not a crowd survey. Chagall’s ceiling dates to 1964.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
