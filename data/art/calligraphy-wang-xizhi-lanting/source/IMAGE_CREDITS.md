## 01 · 两个“之”与邻字

![第8至10列；中列为觀宇宙之大俯察品類之盛](images/01_two_zhi_context.jpg)

- 文件：`images/01_two_zhi_context.jpg`
- 源图范围：`2470,0,2980,1480`；输出510×1480。
- 右列第8列，从“是日也”开始；中列第9列为“觀宇宙之大俯察品類之盛”；左列第10列从“所以遊目”开始。
- 上“之”在“宙”“大”之间，下“之”在“類”“盛”之间。比较宽窄时仍看着邻字，不只看孤立轮廓。
- 对照：[官方PDF第5页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/01/03/u63b3a01212765.pdf#page=5)，刊页18。

English: columns 8–10. In the middle column, the upper 之 (“of,” in “the immensity of the universe”) lies between 宙 and 大. The lower 之 (“of,” in “the abundance of things”) lies between the dense 類 and the broad 盛. The right column begins 是日也 (“on this day”); the left begins 所以遊目 (“thus letting the eyes roam”). Neighbours remain visible for comparison.

## 02 · 开头四列

![开头四列及崇山旁补；右列暮春之初，第二列山陰之蘭亭](images/02_opening_context.jpg)

- 文件：`images/02_opening_context.jpg`
- 源图范围：`3380,0,4070,1480`；输出690×1480。
- 右起第1列的下部为“暮春之初”；第2列中部为“山陰之蘭亭”。
- 更左可见“崇山”以较小的字旁入“有”“峻”附近。该形态只按传本所见描述，不虚构补写现场。
- 对照：[官方PDF第3页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/01/03/u63b3a01212765.pdf#page=3)，刊页16。

English: the first four columns. In column 1, 暮春之初 means “the beginning of late spring.” In column 2, 山陰之蘭亭 means “the Orchid Pavilion of Shanyin.” Farther left, small 崇山 (“towering mountains”) sits beside the main line near 有 and 峻. Red seals are later collecting marks, not the original writing.

## 03 · “視聽之”怎样接到“娛”

![第10至12列，从右下的視聽之转到中上娛](images/03_column_crossing.jpg)

- 文件：`images/03_column_crossing.jpg`
- 源图范围：`2200,0,2695,1480`；输出495×1480。
- 右列第10列下端“視聽之”，转到中列第11列上端“娛”。
- 中列还含“夫人之相與”；这里“夫”是发语词，“人”是人，不把“夫人”合读成现代称谓。
- 左列第12列下端“一室之內”仍把“內”留在“之”下面，和“視聽之”须换列的情况不同。
- 对照：[官方PDF第3页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/01/03/u63b3a01212765.pdf#page=3)，刊页16。

English: columns 10–12. At the foot of the right column, 視聽之 (“of sight and hearing”) continues with 娛 (“delight”) at the top of the middle one. In 夫人之相與, 夫 is an introductory particle and 人 means “people.” Near the foot of the left column, 一室之內 (“within one room”) keeps 內 below 之. These are different kinds of continuation.

## 04 · 感慨段的四列

![第15至18列，保留老之將至、其所之既倦、感慨係之矣及俯仰之間](images/04_turning_context.jpg)

- 文件：`images/04_turning_context.jpg`
- 源图范围：`1380,0,1955,1480`；输出575×1480。
- 第16列即图中右起第2列，为“知老之將至及其所之既倦情”。同列可比较两个“之”。
- 左邻第17列中有“感慨係之矣”；更下的浓墨与小字是传本保留的修改形态，不为被遮部分猜字。
- 第18列即左列，从“欣俯仰之間”开始。“俯仰之間”的“之”仍有横向幅度和周围纸色。
- 对照：[官方PDF第4页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/01/03/u63b3a01212765.pdf#page=4)，刊页17。

English: columns 15–18. The second column from the right contains 知老之將至 (“know that old age is approaching”) above 及其所之既倦 (“when what one pursues has become wearisome”). The next includes 感慨係之矣 (“reflection follows”), with dense revision below. At the left, 俯仰之間 (“in the moment of looking down and up”) retains an open horizontal 之. The caption identifies visible forms without dating the revisions or assigning them a psychological cause.

## 05 · 最后五列与后来的读者

![末五列，從後之視今到後之覽者及有感於斯文](images/05_ending_context.jpg)

- 文件：`images/05_ending_context.jpg`
- 源图范围：`0,0,720,1480`；输出720×1480。
- 第24列即右列下方为“後之視今”；左邻第25列上部接“亦猶今之視昔”。
- 第27列即倒数第2列下端“後之覽”，跨到第28列上端“者”，然后向下至“有感於斯文”。
- 朱印及中部涂改保持原样。覆盖下的文字不在本包重建。
- 对照：[官方PDF第4页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/01/03/u63b3a01212765.pdf#page=4)，刊页17。

English: the final five columns, 24–28. 後之視今 (“later people looking at the present”) lies low in the right column; 亦猶今之視昔 (“as we now look at the past”) continues in the next. 後之覽 (“those later who read”) lies near the foot of the penultimate column; 者 continues it at the top of the final column, ending with 有感於斯文 (“be moved by these words”). Covered writing is not reconstructed. Later colophons do not appear in this image.
