## English

Utzon won the 1957 competition; construction began in 1959 and he departed in 1966. Hall, Todd and Littlemore continued the work before the 1973 opening. Shared spherical geometry does not imply identical roof profiles or substitute for structural verification.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
