## English

The Hall of Mirrors was built in 1678–1684. Official records describe 73 m, 17 window bays and 357 mirror panels. Its ceremonial arrangement and later display choices should not be confused with an unchanging seventeenth-century installation.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
