# 《祭侄文稿》图片说明与英文导览

核对日期2026年10月19日。七图共用于八语正文。来源均为国立故宫博物院《祭姪文稿》精赏相簿。

所有原书迹仅作矩形裁切和等比例缩小，不补笔、不去印、不改色、不锐化、不AI生成。标签仅添加在照片之外的索引底板。坐标以原图左上角为原点，xyxy为左、上、右、下边界。JPEG压缩和显示缩放不等于原件材质或尺寸。

All seven images are shared across languages. Original strokes are unchanged: rectangular cropping, proportional reduction and JPEG encoding only. Labels belong outside the photographs. No generative image editing, recolouring, seal removal or sharpening was used. Coordinates are pixels from the source image’s upper-left corner; xyxy lists left, top, right and bottom.

## 图1 季明与惟尔

![季明与惟尔](images/01_address_detail.jpg)

中列下部季明之靈曰；左列惟爾挺生；右邻祭告者和供品。

Address: find 季明, Jiming, in the lower middle column, then return to 惟爾, you, at the upper left. The right-hand column identifies the living officiant and his offerings.

## 图2 日期与官衔

![日期与官衔](images/02_opening_detail.jpg)

壬申下从父删除，第十三以小字旁入；左侧比较蒲州一列浓淡。

Opening: the date stands at right. Beneath 壬申 in the next column, small 第十三, thirteenth, enters beside cancelled words. Compare the pale upper strokes with the heavier 蒲州, Puzhou, near the bottom of the next column.

## 图3 家人的传言与战事

![家人的传言与战事](images/03_family_detail.jpg)

原仁兄愛我一列较淡，左邻歸止爰開土門较重，左侧父陷子死保留删字。

Family and warfare: locate the relatively pale 仁兄愛我, my elder brother loved me, just right of centre. Its left neighbour begins 歸止, returned. Farther left, the cancelled 擒 below 父 is distinct from the surviving 陷子死, was captured, the son died.

## 图4 第一次哀告

![第一次哀告](images/04_lament_detail.jpg)

中央荼毒以下念爾遘殘百身何贖；左邻嗚呼哀哉和吾承。

First lament: the middle column runs from 荼毒, devastation, down through 念爾, remembering you, to 百身何贖, a hundred lives cannot redeem you. The first 嗚呼哀哉, alas what grief, is at upper left; 吾承, I received, resumes the text below.

## 图5 安葬与结尾

![安葬与结尾](images/05_ending_detail.jpg)

中央震悼心顏；左邻魂而有知無嗟；最左久客、嗚呼哀哉、尚饗。上缘保留纸顶。

Ending: 震悼心顏, grief shakes heart and countenance, occupies the central column. To its left is 魂而有知無嗟, if the spirit knows, do not lament. The far-left column continues 久客, long sojourn, and reaches the final lament and offering formula. The top edge retains the top of the paper.
