## 阅读对象

本篇以国立故宫博物院藏颜真卿758年《祭姪文稿》的墨迹正文为对象。后世题跋、朱印与唐代正文分开。双图定位页保留照片重叠，索引保留官方相簿次序；两者都不是无缝全卷，不能据屏幕宽度量测原件。

文章从“季明之灵”转到“惟尔”，沿“仁兄爱我”“俾尔传言”“念尔”及“魂而有知”读祭文如何持续向具体亡者说话。常规书法细读可以独立阅读；SAE（Self-as-an-End）仅在可选附读出现。

## English

Extract the complete ZIP and open READ.html. Choose a language to read the full essay. All six non-Chinese editions include the complete Chinese requiem, a full translation and the full analysis. The five detail figures appear in every essay; two further navigation images are linked in the expandable notes. The files work offline except for external museum and reference links.

The subject is Yan Zhenqing’s 758 manuscript in the National Palace Museum, Taipei. Later inscriptions and red collectors’ seals are distinguished from his text. Two overlapping photographs are arranged for orientation, and eleven museum views are indexed separately. Neither image is a seamless or measurable reconstruction of the scroll.

The essay follows the change from naming Jiming to addressing “you,” then the family relations, lament and final arrangements for burial. The conventional close reading is self-contained. SAE, or Self-as-an-End, appears only in the optional further reading.

The package contains eight editable Markdown texts, eight HTML reading pages, a bilingual entrance, seven shared JPEGs, image and source notes, rights information and SHA-256 manifests. Image processing is restricted to crops, proportional reductions and arrangements with labels outside the photographs. No writing was generated or altered. The museum’s general open-information announcement is recorded separately from licences attached to its other download entries. No website has been published.

To verify extracted files, run a SHA-256 checker against SHA256SUMS.txt from this folder. The checksum file excludes itself. The ZIP is outside this folder and has a separately reported checksum.
