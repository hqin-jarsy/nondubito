中央荼毒以下念爾遘殘百身何贖；左邻嗚呼哀哉和吾承。

## 图5 安葬与结尾

![安葬与结尾](images/05_ending_detail.jpg)

中央震悼心顏；左邻魂而有知無嗟；最左久客、嗚呼哀哉、尚饗。上缘保留纸顶。

Ending: 震悼心顏, grief shakes heart and countenance, occupies the central column. To its left is 魂而有知無嗟, if the spirit knows, do not lament. The far-left column continues 久客, long sojourn, and reaches the final lament and offering formula. The top edge retains the top of the paper.
