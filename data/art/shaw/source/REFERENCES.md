## English

[Preliminary Study for The Shaw 54th Regiment Memorial: official collection record](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/102493-preliminary-study-shaw-54th-regiment-memorial).

The NGA record identifies the 1883 small plaster study, owned by the U.S. Department of the Interior, National Park Service, Saint-Gaudens National Historic Site. It is distinct from both the Boston bronze and the later full-size plaster. Kathryn Greenthal’s study discusses the seated viewer and bench; National Park Service sources provide the regiment’s history.

[Kathryn Greenthal, Engaging the Viewer, 2008, pp. 2 and 4](https://saint-gaudens.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Fall-20081.pdf).

[National Park Service: the memorial’s history](https://www.nps.gov/articles/the-robert-gould-shaw-memorial.htm).

[Expanding the Story](https://www.nps.gov/saga/learn/historyculture/expanding-the-story.htm).
