## English

Construction began in 1519 and continued under later rulers. The original architect’s identity is not settled; Leonardo-related comparisons are not treated as proven authorship. The two stair routes can share destinations without sharing their ascending flights.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
