# 参考资料

核查日期：2026-10-15。史实主要依据美国国家美术馆的对象记录及技术、历史研究。文中视觉分析由所附五图出发独立提出。

1. [作品记录与语音文字稿](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/110292-little-dancer-aged-fourteen)。对象、年代、尺寸、材料、展览史；Tara Hutton关于脚位与疲惫的个人专业解读。

2. [Technical Notes: Little Dancer Aged Fourteen](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/conservation-publication/technical-notes)。内部构造、蜡与实际衣物、真发，以及现存裙装的研究。

3. [Original State: Little Dancer Aged Fourteen](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/conservation-publication/original-state)。1881原貌资料不足、裙装与姿态争议、玻璃展柜。

4. [Iconographic Interpretations](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/iconographic-interpretations)。历史评论和后来的解释；评论者的偏见不能作为模特人格事实。

5. [Young Dancers in Nineteenth-Century Art](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/conservation-publication/young-dancers-nineteenth-century-art)。模特与年轻舞者的职业、社会背景。

6. [Edgar Degas Only Made One “Little Dancer.” And It’s Ours.](https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/edgar-degas-only-made-one-little-dancer-and-its-ours)。2023年馆方制作与保护介绍；原蜡塑与身后青铜铸件的区别。

7. [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html)。Han Qin（秦汉）的美学总论；可选部分借用个人审美与共同承认的区分。

8. [The Beauty of Perception / 感知之美](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-8.html)。延伸阅读：感知方向。本文材料替换比较是作者提出的思想实验，不作为该论文原有案例。

## English

Checked on 15 October 2026. Historical information comes chiefly from the National Gallery of Art’s collection record and technical and historical studies. The visual readings are the essay’s own proposals, based on the five accompanying photographs.

1. [Collection record and audio transcripts](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/110292-little-dancer-aged-fourteen): identification, date, dimensions, materials and exhibition history; dancer Tara Hutton’s professional interpretation of the position and apparent fatigue.

2. [Technical Notes](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/conservation-publication/technical-notes): internal construction, wax, real clothing and hair, and research on the surviving skirt.

3. [Original State](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/conservation-publication/original-state): incomplete evidence of the 1881 appearance, debates about the skirt and pose, and the glass display case.

4. [Iconographic Interpretations](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/iconographic-interpretations): contemporary criticism and later interpretations. Critics’ prejudices are not evidence of the model’s character.

5. [Young Dancers in Nineteenth-Century Art](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/conservation-publication/young-dancers-nineteenth-century-art): the model and the professional and social circumstances of young dancers.

6. [Edgar Degas Only Made One “Little Dancer.” And It’s Ours.](https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/edgar-degas-only-made-one-little-dancer-and-its-ours): the museum’s 2023 account of making and conserving the work, distinguishing the original wax from posthumous bronze casts.

7. [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html): Han Qin’s framework for aesthetics; further reading beyond the essay’s optional material comparison.

8. [The Beauty of Perception](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-8.html): further reading on perception. The imagined replacement of the skirt is this essay’s thought experiment, not an existing example from the paper.
