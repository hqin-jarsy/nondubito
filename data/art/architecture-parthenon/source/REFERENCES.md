## English

The temple building dates to 447–438 BCE and the pedimental sculptures were completed by 432 BCE. Eight and seventeen columns on the sides yield 46 distinct perimeter columns because the four corners are shared. Original sculptures and replacement casts are distinguished.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
