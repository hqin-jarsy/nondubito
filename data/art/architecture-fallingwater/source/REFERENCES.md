## English

The house was designed from 1935, occupied in 1937, with the guest house completed in 1939. Later structural strengthening used post-tensioning in 2001–2002. Photographic lightness is not a structural calculation.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
