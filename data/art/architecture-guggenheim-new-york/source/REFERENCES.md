## English

This is Wright’s New York museum, opened in October 1959. The essay distinguishes the design’s suggested visitor route from present exhibition rules, and later diagrams from the opening configuration.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
