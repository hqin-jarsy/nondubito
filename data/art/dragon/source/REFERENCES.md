# 《男孩与龙》参考资料

1. J. Paul Getty Museum，Boy with a Dragon，藏品号87.SA.42。[现行馆藏记录](https://www.getty.edu/art/collection/object/103RRC)；[馆方开放数据](https://data.getty.edu/museum/collection/object/4a337ed1-9a79-492b-9962-543c0ae2bb17)。作品名称、联合作者归属、约1617年、材料尺寸、巴贝里尼委托背景与幼年赫拉克勒斯传统的主要来源。
2. Getty，[本件作品IIIF清单](https://media.getty.edu/iiif/manifest/7e3f975f-34b0-46b5-b174-2d74f479cfc9)。收录本文所用正面偏俯视照片；包含图像像素、保护处理后拍摄标记与CC0许可。
3. The J. Paul Getty Museum Journal, vol.16, 1988，Acquisitions/1987，第184页，第85项Boy with a Dragon。[Getty电子出版物](https://www.getty.edu/publications/resources/virtuallibrary/0892361433.pdf)。基座底部通向龙嘴的孔道、可能作为室内喷泉的说明。该条目采用较早的单一作者归属与年代，本文仅采用其相关实物研究信息。
4. Masterpieces of the J. Paul Getty Museum: European Sculpture，第52页，第16项Boy with a Dragon。[Getty电子出版物](https://www.getty.edu/publications/resources/virtuallibrary/0892365137.pdf)。提供古代儿童与动物题材的比较，并显示“小赫拉克勒斯”这一识别在馆方不同时期的论述中有不同处理。本文采用与幼年赫拉克勒斯传统相联系的表述，不以一个神话名称替代形体分析。
5. Han Qin（秦汉），SAE Judgment and Aesthetics—In Tribute to Kant，2026。[美学总论](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html)。仅用于可选附读中关于判断与追问方向的理论入口。

检索与核对日期：2026年10月15日。本文所见实物图像限于第2项的一张照片。

## English

[Boy with a Dragon: official collection record](https://www.getty.edu/art/collection/object/103RRC).

Attribution and date follow the current Getty record: Pietro Bernini and Gian Lorenzo Bernini, about 1617. Earlier publications use different dates and attributions. The documented channel supports a possible fountain function; the essay does not reconstruct a proven original installation.

[Getty Museum Journal 16 (1988), acquisition entry, p. 184](https://www.getty.edu/publications/resources/virtuallibrary/0892361433.pdf).

[Han Qin, SAE Judgment and Aesthetics](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html) is optional philosophical reading, not a historical source about this object. The close reading and proposed interpretations belong to this essay.
