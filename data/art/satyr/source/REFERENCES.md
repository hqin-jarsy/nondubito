## 写作及证据边界

- 正文不直接引用研究文章的原句。馆藏主条目仅承担压缩的对象数据、功能与归属/材质说明；绝大部分篇幅为对已查看图片的独立视觉分析。
- 不把主条目的同页研究、查询参数不同的URL、2022年书本中的相同条目当成三个独立来源扩充转述额度。
- 同系列其他萨堤尔可能兼具墨水台、烛台功能，本文对象不据此改称墨水台或烛台。
- 本包没有依据照片认定头顶孔口的功能、内部储油通道、容量、实际燃烧时长、重心数值或具体原使用者。

## English

[Seated Satyr with Silvered Eyes: official collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/203914).

Read the artist field together with the scholarly discussion on the same page: the latter questions direct authorship by Riccio and discusses a later sculptor using his models. It also identifies brass where the basic material field says bronze; the essay uses the broader term copper alloy. The lamp’s documented function is distinct from hypothetical lighting conditions.

[Related Satyress: classical and Renaissance context](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/203913).

[Han Qin, SAE Judgment and Aesthetics](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html) is optional philosophical reading, not a historical source about this object. The close reading and proposed interpretations belong to this essay.
