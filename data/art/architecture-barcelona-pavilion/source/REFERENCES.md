## English

The foundation credits Mies van der Rohe and Lilly Reich. The 1929 pavilion was dismantled in 1930; the reconstructed building opened in 1986. Present stone patterns are not original surviving 1929 materials.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
