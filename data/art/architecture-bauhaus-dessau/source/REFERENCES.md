## English

The building opened in December 1926 and was designed by Gropius’s private office. Its curtain wall was damaged in 1945 and reconstructed using aluminium and glass in 1976; historical appearance does not establish original material continuity.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
