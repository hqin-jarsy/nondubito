# 第一篇参考资料

查阅日期：2026年10月14日。

## 作品与艺术史

1. National Gallery of Art，**Woman Holding a Balance**，现行馆藏记录。用于作者、约1664年、布面油画、现行画面与绷框尺寸、收藏信息及图像下载；也用于说明馆方的节制与反省读法、画框底边的局部错位。
   https://www.nga.gov/artworks/1236-woman-holding-balance

2. Arthur K. Wheelock Jr.，**Dutch Paintings of the Seventeenth Century: Woman Holding a Balance, c. 1664**，National Gallery of Art，2017年8月30日上线。用于旧题名、显微检查所支持的空秤盘、不同寓意解释与孕相争议。可定位到 Entry、Notes 1—12、Technical Summary。本文只选用必要事实，没有照译该文的心理与意图归因。
   https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/dutch-paintings-seventeenth-century-woman-holding-balance-c

3. Diane Richard，**Reconsidering Vermeer’s Perfectionism: Introducing an impetuous artist—and his studio**，National Gallery of Art，2022年10月7日。本文采用其中“Sometimes Vermeer changed his mind”段关于天平从斜置改为平行画面的技术发现。斜置指空间朝向，不等于两边重量不等。
   https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/reconsidering-vermeers-perfectionism

4. Diane Richard，**Who Is Vermeer? 10 facts about a famous but mysterious artist**，National Gallery of Art，2022年10月7日。用于背景段的代尔夫特生活背景与1650年代中期转向风俗画的时间。可定位到第1、3、6节。
   https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/who-vermeer

5. National Gallery of Art，**Vermeer and the Masters of Genre Painting: Inspiration and Rivalry**，2017—2018年展览介绍。用于背景段中十七世纪中叶荷兰室内风俗画的背景，以及画家之间在题材、构图与技法上的交流。不采用将整个社会概括为普遍繁荣的说法。背景段中关于首饰、《最后审判》与自省的过渡，是结合本幅画可见细节及第1项馆方说明作出的概括。
   https://www.nga.gov/exhibitions/vermeer-and-masters-genre-painting-inspiration-and-rivalry

## SAE可选小节

6. 秦汉，**SAE判断力与美学**，第二版，2026年。用于区分审美反应与可讨论的根据、射线作为提问方向而非作品等级。本文对维米尔的应用是本篇提出的当代解读。
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html

7. 秦汉，**感知之美**，2026年。用于把判断带回具体的显现、颜色、明暗与背景关系。
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-8.html

8. 秦汉，**认知之美**，2026年。用于观看者携带的旧经验、可能落空的期待及判断修正；不据此虚构画中人物的记忆。
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-9.html

9. 秦汉，**不疑之美**，2026年。用于说明“不疑”需要主体判断及其与他人的关系证据，不能由空盘或平静姿态推出。
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-12.html

## 可继续阅读

## 阅读边界

作品中可见的物件与画面关系、技术检查的发现、历史中的解释，以及本文提出的读法，证据性质不同。本文尽量在措辞中区分。女人的具体身份、是否怀孕、正在想什么，以及维米尔创作此画时全部的动机，均未作为已知事实处理。

## English reference guide

The eight essays use the same source links. Their visual arguments are independent readings, and the optional SAE application is a contemporary interpretation rather than a statement of Vermeer’s intentions.

1. **National Gallery of Art, Woman Holding a Balance.** Current collection record: artist, date, medium, painted-surface and stretcher dimensions, collection information and image; also the museum’s interpretation in terms of moderation and reflection and its discussion of the offset lower frame edge.
   https://www.nga.gov/artworks/1236-woman-holding-balance

2. **Arthur K. Wheelock Jr., Dutch Paintings of the Seventeenth Century: Woman Holding a Balance, c. 1664.** National Gallery of Art, online edition, published August 30, 2017. Used for historical titles, microscopic examination supporting the empty pans, alternative interpretations and the disputed suggestion of pregnancy. See Entry, Notes 1–12 and Technical Summary.
   https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/dutch-paintings-seventeenth-century-woman-holding-balance-c

3. **Diane Richard, Reconsidering Vermeer’s Perfectionism: Introducing an impetuous artist—and his studio.** National Gallery of Art, October 7, 2022. Used for the change from an oblique balance to one parallel to the picture plane. The change concerns spatial orientation, not a difference in weight between the pans.
   https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/reconsidering-vermeers-perfectionism

4. **Diane Richard, Who Is Vermeer? 10 facts about a famous but mysterious artist.** National Gallery of Art, October 7, 2022. Used for Delft and the turn toward genre subjects in the mid-1650s; see sections 1, 3 and 6.
   https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/who-vermeer

5. **National Gallery of Art, Vermeer and the Masters of Genre Painting: Inspiration and Rivalry.** Exhibition introduction, 2017–2018. Used for Dutch interior genre painting in the mid-seventeenth century and exchanges among painters in subject, composition and technique.
   https://www.nga.gov/exhibitions/vermeer-and-masters-genre-painting-inspiration-and-rivalry

6. **秦汉, SAE判断力与美学**, second edition, 2026. The general account used to distinguish aesthetic response from discussable grounds and to treat “rays” as directions of inquiry rather than rankings.
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html

7. **秦汉, 感知之美**, 2026. Used for attention to appearance, color, light, dark and background relationships.
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-8.html

8. **秦汉, 认知之美**, 2026. Used for a viewer’s past experience, possible disappointed expectations and revision of judgment.
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-9.html

9. **秦汉, 不疑之美**, 2026. Supports the limit on inferring SAE concepts from empty pans or a calm appearance alone.
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-12.html

10. **National Gallery of Art, Terms and Notices.** The open-access policy provides eligible artwork images under CC0; it does not imply museum endorsement of this essay.
    https://www.nga.gov/terms-and-notices

11. **National Gallery of Art, Free Images and Open Access.**
    https://www.nga.gov/artworks/free-images-and-open-access
