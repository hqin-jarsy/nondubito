## 解释范围

构图、色彩邻接、笔触方向、形体分配及本文的总体判断，是依据馆方数字图像的独立细读。作品制作顺序、工具、修复事实与历史关联，采用上述馆方研究并在正文相应位置链接。SAE部分是对该理论的当代运用，不作为伦勃朗采用该理论、人物心理状态或人格价值的历史证据。

Composition, color adjacency, stroke direction, distribution of form, and the essay's overall argument are independent observations of the museum reproduction. Claims about tools, sequence, conservation, and historical relationships are attributed to the museum research. The SAE section is a contemporary application, not historical evidence of the painter's theoretical intentions or the sitter's psychological or moral condition.
