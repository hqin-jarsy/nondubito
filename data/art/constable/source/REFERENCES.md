Observations of colour, light, overlap, scale, the fence’s turns and reflections come from inspecting the image. The claim that organized distances produce a feeling of nature is the essay’s interpretation. Dimensions, dates, the commission, canvas joins and correspondence are documented above. The essays do not infer wind speed, an exact time of day, a paint-layer sequence or a visually identifiable seam from the screen image.
