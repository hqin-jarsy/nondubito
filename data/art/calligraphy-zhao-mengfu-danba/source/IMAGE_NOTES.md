# 《胆巴碑》图片说明与导览

图像来自故宫博物院（北京）所藏《赵孟頫楷书帝师胆巴碑卷》，新00153386。对象是纸本墨迹，不是拓本。[馆方作品页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/subject_zhaomengfu/achievement/245821.html)；[官方高清缩放浏览](https://www.dpm.org.cn/dyx.html?path=/Uploads/tilegenerator/dest/files/image/8831/2017/0027/img0007.xml)。核对日期2026年10月19日。

七张JPEG由八语全文共用。正文五张局部各有本语图注；其余两张用于定位。每列从上向下、各列从右向左读。六段索引则按编号从上段向下段看，每段内部保留原来的方向。

## 图像加工边界

馆方高清浏览器以DeepZoom图块呈现一张完整的数字长卷。最高图层的图像尺寸为40335×1733，图块边长256、相邻图块重叠1像素。本次局部图取第15层（20168×867），定位图和索引取第14层（10084×434）。以原坐标拼回公开显示的图块，去掉重复的边缘像素；未对书迹作内容生成或重建。

五张细读图只在第15层已还原图像上矩形裁切，再以JPEG质量93、4:4:4编码保存。定位图等比例缩小；索引把第14层完整长图依阅读次序切成六段，逐段放置并在图外标数字及原图x坐标。末段宽度较短，靠右放置，左侧留的是索引画布，不冒称原作纸面。没有补笔、去印、改字、锐化、改色、透视变形、智能修复或AI生成书迹。

这是对馆方已呈现数字影像的查看与裁切，不是把不同版本拼成新书迹。像素尺寸不是实物尺寸；数字图中的纸色也不能越过摄影条件直接解释为纸质差异。

## English guide

All eight editions share seven images from the Palace Museum’s digital representation of the same ink-on-paper handscroll. Read each column downward and move between columns from right to left. The six-band index proceeds from the top band to the bottom; each band retains its own right-to-left order.

- [00 Overview](images/00_scroll_overview.jpg): the complete digital scroll reduced for orientation. Seal-script heading at right, regular-script inscription after it, later colophons at left. Use it to locate sections, not to inspect individual strokes.
- [Figure 1](images/01_title_transition.jpg): six columns of three large seal-script characters followed on the left by the regular-script title in three columns of eight, eight and three characters. The latter adds 之, “of.” A further column of authorial credentials is retained at the left.
- [Figure 2](images/02_signature_beginning.jpg): the three columns of credentials start about one character position below the adjacent heading and opening narrative. Find 集, start of Jixian; 夫, completing the title dafu; and 敕/勅, “imperial command,” at these lowered starts. 皇, “imperial,” begins the narrative at the higher level to their left.
- [Figure 3](images/03_temple_prose.jpg): the column 十三尺建大閣三重, “thirteen chi / build / great pavilion / three levels,” lies left of centre; the previous column ends 七, “seven,” completing “seventy-three chi.” The crop retains neighbours for comparisons of sparse and dense character structures. 銅, “copper,” 鑄, “cast,” 錢, “coin,” 以, “to/by,” and 太, part of Taizu’s name, occur together in a column farther right.
- [Figure 4](images/04_emperor_spacing.jpg): 國土保安靜, “the realm remain peaceful,” occupies five positions; 皇帝, “emperor,” occupies only two in the next column; 皇太后壽命等天地, “the empress dowager / lifespan equal heaven and earth,” fills eight in the next. The blank space beneath each remains visible.
- [Figure 5](images/05_end_date.jpg): end of the praise poem; separate 可盡, “can be exhausted”; Yanyou year three; a blank before 月, “month”; and separate 立石, “erect the stone.” Small later writing at left begins “Wuxing wrote this stele…,” Wuxing referring to Zhao Mengfu. It is a later reader’s colophon, not Zhao’s continuing text.
- [06 Six-band index](images/06_scroll_index.jpg): the full level-14 image cut into six consecutive bands. Numbers and x-coordinates are editorial labels outside the image, not historic writing. The final, shorter band is aligned right; its left margin is the index canvas. The bands show the complete sequence through the later colophons.

No calligraphy was generated or reconstructed by AI. Public museum tiles were assembled at their native coordinates, duplicate overlap pixels removed, and rectangular crops JPEG-encoded. The overview alone was proportionally reduced; the index rearranges consecutive rectangular bands for navigation. No strokes or seals were removed, no colour or sharpness altered, and no dimensions of the physical object inferred from pixel geometry.
