## 艺术史背景 / Art-historical context

4. James Voorhies, [Paul Cézanne (1839–1906)](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/paul-cezanne-1839-1906), The Metropolitan Museum of Art, October 2004. Used for Cézanne's participation in the Impressionist exhibitions, Pissarro's encouragement, and the development of adjacent color and tonal strokes as a way of building form. Its comments about perspective in other still lifes are not treated as technical findings about this painting.

5. National Gallery of Art, [Cézanne's Still Lifes](https://www.nga.gov/educational-resources/sense-place-france/cezannes-still-lifes), A Sense of Place: France. Used for repeated arrangements of everyday studio objects and sustained investigation of form and color. The article's more specific spatial discussion concerns another work, Still Life with Apples and Peaches; it is not imported into this essay. The surviving studio shown in its accompanying film is not identified here as the site of this arrangement.

## 可选理论 / Optional theoretical reading

6. Han Qin (秦汉), [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant / SAE 判断力与美学 · 致敬康德](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html), second edition as indicated on the source. [DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19296710](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19296710). The source site identifies its material as CC BY 4.0. The optional section draws on the distinction between personal aesthetic response and the grounds of a revisable aesthetic judgment. Its exercise asks how the cloth’s ornament can be read both as a pattern with its own rhythm and as a cue to folds and volume; this is the essay’s contemporary application, not an attribution to Cézanne and not an established art-historical explanation.

## 阅读与证据边界 / How evidence is used

馆藏资料支持身份、年代、尺寸、媒材、收藏与图像权利；传统细读主要来自对全图与局部的观察。边缘、笔向、冷暖、花纹和形体关系是本文可供核对的判断。玻璃中的暖色形被谨慎描述为透见与反光的线索，不替每一处指定唯一的光学来源。没有据数字图像推断具体颜料、实际作画顺序、单次作画或有意破坏透视。

The museum record establishes identification, dating, dimensions, medium, collection and image rights. Close reading rests principally on the full composition and its details. Claims about edges, mark direction, warm and cool colors, pattern and form remain open to checking. The warm shape within the carafe is treated as a cue for transparency and reflection without assigning every passage an exclusive optical cause. No pigment chemistry, actual sequence of execution, single-session history or intentional destruction of perspective is inferred from the digital image.
