# 欧阳询《九成宫醴泉铭》八语图文阅读包

中国书法细读06。题目：先看白字里的一块黑。

## 打开与阅读

完整解压后，打开READ.html或index.html，选择简体中文、繁體中文、English、Deutsch、Français、Español、日本語或한국어。正文、语言切换与配图均可离线使用；官方高清浏览与外部文献链接须联网。MD文件是可编辑原稿，HTML为本地阅读版。

八语都是完整文章，包含全部四张细读图、两段古文原文及完整译义、SAE可选附读、折叠文本与图像说明。外语保留所议汉字、释义、位置和阅读方向。七张图片由八篇共用；点击图片可单独打开。

## 本件与范围

唯一图像对象为北京故宫博物院藏宋拓李祺旧藏《九成宫醴泉铭》，藏品号新00044517。本篇细读馆方公开的三个不同对开，未声称覆盖全册26开，也没有混入其他拓本或今碑图。影像中的白字为凹刻经传拓而形成的纸色，深色主要为拓墨；这不是欧阳询亲笔纸本墨迹。

## English

Open READ.html after extracting the entire ZIP. It links to eight complete illustrated editions: Simplified and Traditional Chinese, English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Korean. Each non-Chinese edition retains the Chinese passages and translates them in full, with character meanings and directions for the visual discussion.

The seven shared images all depict one documented object, the Palace Museum’s Northern Song rubbing formerly owned by Li Qi, accession 新00044517. Four details are discussed in the text; three whole album openings help locate them. The essay does not claim to cover the entire twenty-six-opening album. It never mixes the chosen rubbing with another version or a photograph of the stone.

Local text, navigation and images work offline. External museum and reference links require internet access. This package has not been published online, and no separate written museum permission for public or commercial image reuse has been obtained. See LICENSE_AND_CREDITS before any publication.
