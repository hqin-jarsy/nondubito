## English

The Palladio Museum records design in 1566–1567 and construction in 1567–1605. The villa was habitable but unfinished in 1569. Published plans, the built dome and later decoration must be distinguished.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
