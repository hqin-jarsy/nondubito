## English

The Flavian building was inaugurated in 80 CE, with later work under Domitian. The original arena surface covered the underground preparation spaces. Social seating divisions and the violence of the spectacles are part of the historical account.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
