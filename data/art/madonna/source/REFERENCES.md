Lucretia Kargère and Adriana Rizzo. “Twelfth-Century French Polychrome Sculpture in The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Materials and Techniques.” Metropolitan Museum Studies in Art, Science, and Technology 1 (2010): 39–72。[馆方出版页及全文入口](https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications/metropolitan-museum-studies-in-art-science-and-technology)。重点参照页46—49（木材、组合结构、封闭腔室及彩绘保存），55—56（摩根圣母彩绘），63注6（缺失及现代补配）。本文所引为书上印刷页码。
3. Lucretia Kargère. [Two Sculptures in Deep Conversation at The Met Cloisters](https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/virgin-and-child-sculptures-met-cloisters-heavenly-bodies). The Metropolitan Museum of Art, 2018。两件奥弗涅圣母子的技法比较，支持蓝色外衣、红色衣袍、较深红色的基督披衣及袖边锡制浮雕的说明。比较文章不等于两件图像可以互换，本文所用照片均为16.32.194a, b。

5. Han Qin（秦汉）. [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html), 2026，第二版。仅用于可选附读中的构造与判断问题；不是本作史实或中世纪神学来源。

正文中的体积、方向、遮挡、形体层次及观看位置，是依据所附照片作出的形式分析。关于原始彩绘、构造和功能的历史判断，分别依据以上馆方记录和技术研究。

## English

[Virgin and Child in Majesty: official collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/463795).

The collection record and technical research distinguish surviving polychromy from exposed wood and later repairs. A cavity may have held relics; that possible use is not presented as confirmed contents. Missing hands, renewed throne elements and the construction of the figures matter to the reading.
