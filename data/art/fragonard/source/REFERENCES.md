# 第五篇参考资料

查阅日期：2026年10月14日。以下列出八语共用来源；正文的主要分析来自对馆方原图和局部的独立观察。

## 作品与绘画史

1. **National Gallery of Art, Young Girl Reading**，现行馆藏记录。用于作者、约1769年、媒介、81.1×64.8厘米画面尺寸、赠藏信息、藏品号1961.16.1及原图。采用现行定年，未沿用旧约1770年。
   https://www.nga.gov/artworks/46303-young-girl-reading

2. **Yuriko Jackall, Young Girl Reading, c. 1769**，National Gallery of Art，French Paintings of the Fifteenth through the Eighteenth Centuries在线目录，Entry及Technical Summary。用于褶领的笔杆划痕、底层人物的修正辨认，以及待旧漆干燥后重画的材料证据。“至少约六个月”是材料干燥推估，非档案所载日程。
   https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/french-paintings-fifteenth-through-eighteenth-centuries/french-paintings-fifteenth-through-eighteenth-centuries-young-girl-reading-c-1769

3. **National Gallery of Art, Fragonard: The Fantasy Figures**，2017年展览介绍；以及2017年7月11日展览新闻资料。用于幻想人物系列、2012年出现的肖像小稿，以及想象的“西班牙式”装束与舞台化装背景。没有据此将所有小稿附名者直接等同于确定的模特。
   https://www.nga.gov/exhibitions/fragonard-fantasy-figures
   https://www.nga.gov/press/jean-honore-fragonard-exhibition-unites-fantasy-figures-and-newly-discovered-drawing-national

4. **Alex Dean, The Mysterious Fantasy**，National Gallery of Art，2025年12月29日。用于与本画有关的小稿未附姓名这一细节；亦与技术研究交叉核查。本文没有照译这篇现代解说的结构、比喻或笔触论述，也未使用其中的现代彩色重构图。
   https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/mysterious-fantasy-fragonard-young-girl-reading

5. **John K. Delaney, Yuriko Jackall, Michael Swicklik等，What Lies Beneath**，The Analytical Scientist，2016年8月15日。研究者对成像方法及早期图像推定的第一手介绍，用于区分技术证据与彩色假设模拟。网页直接读取曾报错，研究阶段通过检索所回传的文章内容及同期纸刊交叉核查；没有声称阅览未能取得的完整原始期刊论文。
   https://theanalyticalscientist.com/issues/2016/articles/aug/what-lies-beneath/

- 2017年完整展览图录、2009年旧目录PDF及2015年Burlington原论文未获全文阅读，故不列为本文已阅读全文的来源。

## English reference guide

The eight essays share these sources. Most of their argument is based on independent observation of the painting. Technical and historical findings are kept distinct from the essay's interpretation and its optional contemporary SAE experiments.

1. **NGA collection record, Young Girl Reading.** Current metadata, date c. 1769, painted dimensions, gift, accession number, and image.
2. **Yuriko Jackall, NGA online catalogue, Young Girl Reading, c. 1769.** Entry and Technical Summary: incised collar details, the female figure beneath the final head, and evidence for repainting after drying. The interval is an estimate, not a documented schedule.
3. **NGA 2017 exhibition and press materials, Fragonard: The Fantasy Figures.** The group, portrait sketches, and fanciful “Spanish” costume in a theatrical or masquerade setting.
4. **Alex Dean, The Mysterious Fantasy, December 29, 2025.** The absence of a name on the associated sketch. Its exposition and modern reconstruction images are not reproduced.
5. **Delaney, Jackall, Swicklik and colleagues, What Lies Beneath, 2016.** Firsthand methodological explanation, used to distinguish imaging evidence from hypothetical color reconstruction.
6. **BnF, Lire au féminin, March 2021, p. 2.** General historical limits on access to reading, not a statistical account of France in 1769.
7. **Rousseau, Julie ou la nouvelle Héloïse, first preface.** One example of moral discourse around young women's novel-reading, not evidence of this book's genre.
8–10. **Han Qin, SAE Judgment and Aesthetics, The Beauty of Perception, and The Beauty of Cognition, 2026.** Theoretical directions for the optional section; the painting-specific experiments are the present essay's proposals.
11. **NGA Terms and Notices.** CC0 terms for the eligible artwork image, without implying museum endorsement.

The numbered entries above provide the direct URLs. The full 2017 exhibition catalogue, unavailable 2009 catalogue PDF, and original 2015 Burlington article were not read in full and are not represented as such. No research PDFs are included in this package.
