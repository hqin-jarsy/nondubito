# 第三篇参考资料 / Episode 03 references

核查日期 / Checked: 2026-10-14

八语文章共用以下资料。正文以直接看图与独立分析为主体，文献用于核对事实、传统释义及解释的历史条件。本文提出的观看位置读法及SAE应用是本篇的分析，不代表馆方或历史画家的表述。

The eight essays share these sources. Close observation and the essay's own argument provide the main text; the references support factual details, traditional iconography and historical context. The account of viewing positions and the SAE application are the essay's interpretations, not statements attributed to the museum or the painter.

## 正文采用 / Sources used

1. National Gallery of Art, **The Annunciation**, Jan van Eyck, collection record, 1937.1.39.
   https://www.nga.gov/artworks/46-annunciation
   - 作者、约1434–1436年、原木板后转画布、画面与现支撑体尺寸、收藏信息、作品沿革与开放图像。
   - Artist, approximate date, original panel and later canvas support, dimensions, collection, provenance and open image.
   - 馆方早期出处以“Possibly”限定。本文未将尚莫尔出处、具体委托或原三联画重建写成定论。
   - The early provenance is conditional; the essays do not assert a secure Champmol origin, patron or reconstructed triptych.

2. National Gallery of Art, **Stories and Symbols in Jan van Eyck's “The Annunciation”**, February 7, 2025.
   https://www.nga.gov/stories/articles/stories-and-symbols-jan-van-eycks-annunciation
   - 用于交叉核对铭文方向及其传统宗教解释、圣灵图像等。本包未照译该文的结构或整篇讲解。
   - Used to cross-check the inscriptions' direction and traditional religious reading, and Holy Spirit imagery. Its structure and explanatory text are not translated into this package.

3. John Oliver Hand and Martha Wolff, **Early Netherlandish Painting**, The Collections of the National Gallery of Art Systematic Catalogue (Washington and Cambridge, 1986). Jan van Eyck biography, p. 75; “The Annunciation,” entry by John Oliver Hand, pp. 76–86.
   https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/early-netherlandish-painting
   - 核对画家宫廷及其他委托背景、拉丁铭文、建筑形式、摩西壁画、参孙与大卫地砖，以及传统预表释义。原PDF已在研究阶段取得并核读相关页，本交付包不附该书。
   - Consulted for the artist's court and other commissions, inscriptions, architecture, Moses murals, Samson and David pavement scenes, and historical typological readings. Relevant catalogue pages were consulted; the copyrighted book is not included.
   - 该书所述保存状况属于其出版时期，本文不将其当作今日状态。本文没有使用未单独确认公开许可的红外或其他技术图像。
   - Its condition account is historical, not an account of today's surface. No infrared or other technical image with unverified reuse rights is reproduced.

4. **Luke 1:26–38**, New American Bible, United States Conference of Catholic Bishops.
   https://bible.usccb.org/bible/luke/1
   - 用于交代报喜情节，并区分经文与画中增添的建筑、服饰和书。正文概述情节；画上拉丁短句按历史图像辨读，不引用现代英文译本的长段文字。
   - Used for the narrative and to distinguish the scriptural account from the setting and objects in the painting. The essays paraphrase the episode; their short Latin quotations belong to the historical artwork.
