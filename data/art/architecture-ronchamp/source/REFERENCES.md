## English

The chapel was designed in 1950–1955 and inaugurated in June 1955. Roof support, apparent heaviness and the light gap are distinct issues. Chapel light towers are not the separate bell structure by Jean Prouvé, installed in 1975.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
