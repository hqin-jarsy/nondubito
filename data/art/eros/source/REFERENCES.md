## 事实与判断的边界

“把重量交给身外”“力量暂时不在行动”“羽翼也在休息”等为基于可见姿态的解释，不是古代原文引语或馆方定论。没有诊断睡眠的医学状态，没有推定梦境或人格。现有颜色与孔蚀属于今天照片所见，不能直接作为制作当时的肤色和表面外观。作者未署名；正文不归属于普拉克西特列斯或利西波斯。未采用未经核实的出土地传闻。

## English

[Bronze Statue of Eros Sleeping: official collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/254502).

The 2013 Met study distinguishes the Hellenistic bronze, an ancient repair between the legs, and the modern base. Reconstructions of missing bow, quiver and support derive from comparison, not from details fully preserved in these photographs.

[Sleeping Eros: technical research and reconstruction, 2013](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2013/sleeping-eros).

[Han Qin, SAE Judgment and Aesthetics](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html) is optional philosophical reading, not a historical source about this object. The close reading and proposed interpretations belong to this essay.
