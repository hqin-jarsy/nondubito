- SAE段落是可跳过的当代观看练习；古旧、磨损或拥有历史不会自动证明美。

The essays distinguish painted dimensions from dimensions including additions; freehand incision from impressed dots; a probable central-panel role from an uncertain reconstruction; general craft knowledge from object-specific technical evidence; traditional iconography from the essays’ own reading. ## 仍然保留的边界 / Remaining limits

The photograph fixes one illumination and cannot reproduce the original gold’s changing reflection. Visible cracks can be distinguished, but the photograph alone cannot date or explain each one. The original arrangement and location of the altarpiece remain unresolved.
