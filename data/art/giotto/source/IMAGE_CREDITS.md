## 复制品的边界 / Limits of the reproduction

照片记录一次照明条件下的金面。屏幕无法重现观众或光源移动时，原作真实金箔反光的变化。图像顶部拱形之外的白色为照片背景，不是画内的天空。

The photograph records the gold surface under one illumination. It cannot reproduce the changing reflection of the physical gold as a viewer or light moves. White outside the upper outline belongs to the photographic background, not to a painted sky.
