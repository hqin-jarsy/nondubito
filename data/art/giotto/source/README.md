# 西方绘画细读 09 · 乔托《圣母子》

八语完整阅读包 / Eight-language reading package

这篇从金底与身体各自的转向进入乔托《圣母子》：金箔接住画外的光，颜料描绘面容与衣褶上的光；花、指尖和薄布使圣像的庄严呈现具有可触的亲近感。传统细读独立完整，文末SAE阅读可跳过。

This essay explores the relationship between a reflective gold surface, modeled flesh and cloth, and the small exchanges of hands and a flower. The art-historical close reading stands on its own; the concluding SAE exercise is optional.
