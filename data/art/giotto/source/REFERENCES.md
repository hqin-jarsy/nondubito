# 参考资料 / References

The essays are principally independent close readings of the image. These sources support object data, technical and historical statements, material context, and the optional SAE exercise. Interpretive claims remain those of the essays.

## 作品及史料 / The work and its history

1. National Gallery of Art, [Giotto, Madonna and Child](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/397-madonna-and-child).
   - 当前归属乔托；约1310—1315年；杨木板蛋彩；绘画表面85.4×61.8厘米，含后加木条87.7×63.2×1.3厘米；Samuel H. Kress Collection，1939.1.256。
   - Object identification, collection data, dimensions, the white rose’s association with Marian purity, and the image’s public-domain designation.

2. Miklós Boskovits, [Italian Paintings of the Thirteenth and Fourteenth Centuries: Madonna and Child, c. 1310/1315](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/italian-paintings-thirteenth-and-fourteenth-centuries/italian-paintings-thirteenth-and-fourteenth-centuries-madonna-and-child-c-13101315), National Gallery of Art, online publication 2016-03-21.
   - 中央画板的可能归属、未定的同组侧板与安放地点、画板削薄与顶部修整、玫瑰图像学、肌肤底色与薄层画法。
   - The probable central-panel function; uncertainties about reconstruction and destination; alteration of the support; rose iconography; the technical account of flesh underpainting and thin paint layers.
   - 技术总结与注9须合读：复杂纹样徒手刻划，边缘点列为压点。本文不把它们一概写成用成形印模冲压的图案。
   - The technical summary and note 9 distinguish freely incised motifs from impressed dot borders. These are not collectively described as repeated stamped motifs.

## 工艺与用途 / Materials and function

3. Royal Collection Trust, [Making a Panel Painting](https://www.rct.uk/collection/stories/italian-altarpieces-in-the-royal-collection-1300-1500/making-a-panel-painting), in *Italian Altarpieces in the Royal Collection: 1300–1500*.
   - 木板准备、蛋彩、贴金、打磨、刻线与压点怎样处理表面；一般工艺背景，不作为乔托这件画每一道工序均经检测的证据。
   - General technical context for panel preparation, egg tempera, gilding, burnishing, and worked ornament. It is not a technical examination of this specific Giotto panel.

4. Royal Collection Trust, [Function](https://www.rct.uk/collection/stories/italian-altarpieces-in-the-royal-collection-1300-1500/function), in the same series.
   - 祭坛画的祈祷、礼仪和叙事用途。本文未据其他作品的观看环境，推定这件乔托画的确切烛光、距离或悬挂高度。
   - Context for devotion and the functions of altarpieces. No exact illumination, viewing distance, or installation height is reconstructed for the Giotto panel from this general account.

## SAE

5. Han Qin (秦汉), [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html).
   - 可选当代观看框架；区分个人趣味与关于可共同辨认根据的判断。
   - Optional contemporary framework, distinguishing personal response from a claim about what can be recognized in the work.

6. Han Qin (秦汉), [The Beauty of Causation — SAE Aesthetics, Ray 5](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-5.html).
   - 核心参照是过去怎样以痕迹与依赖关系留在后来之物中。本文的练习辨别制作痕与可见裂纹，不以“古老”“受损”或“有来历”自动证明美。
   - The essay concerns the legible persistence of earlier events in later things. Our exercise distinguishes ornamental production from the present crack network; age, damage, and historical interest do not automatically establish beauty.
