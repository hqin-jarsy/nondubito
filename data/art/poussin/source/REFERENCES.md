## 作品与历史 Object and history

1. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. [Nicolas Poussin, The Abduction of the Sabine Women, 46.160](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437329). Current object information and overview; Catalogue Entry by Mary Sprinson de Jesús, 2010, with provenance and bibliography. Primary source for the title, tentative date, medium, dimensions, foundation legend, cloak signal, Créquy and Richelieu provenance, and the discussion of compositional methods and sculptural/pictorial sources. 核对当前“probably 1633–34”、布面油画、154.6×209.9厘米；蜡人方法来自馆方概述，古今造型来源来自学术目录。正文没有把每一姿态说成出自某个已证实的蜡模型。

2. The Met Collection API. [Object 437329](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/437329). Structured object information, accession 46.160, primary image URL, and isPublicDomain=true. 用于基本元数据和官方开放图源核验。

3. Musée du Louvre. [Nicolas Poussin, L’enlèvement des Sabines, INV 7290; MR 2335](https://collections.louvre.fr/ark:/53355/cl010062438). The separate Louvre canvas is oil on canvas, 159 × 206 cm. Its display date is 1637–1638, while the detailed scholarly comment discusses the possibility that both treatments were made close together around 1634–1635. The present essay does not adjudicate this chronology or transfer the Louvre canvas’s restoration and material history to the Met painting. 卢浮宫页面题头与学术注释保留不同年代层次，本文不借此宣布两作确定的先后；纽约本作采用Met当前的约年。

版本辨识也对照了卢浮宫作品页的官方图像：卢浮宫版本左侧庙宇有显著三角山墙，右侧建筑沿纵深后退；纽约版本右侧较正面的三暗窗外墙与左侧高台形成本文讨论的布局。卢浮宫图像仅用于核对，未收入本包。

The official Louvre image was also checked: its prominent temple pediment at the left and receding architecture at the right distinguish it from the Met canvas’s broad, more frontal three-window wall and high left platform. The Louvre image was consulted for identification and is not included in the package.

The depicted episode belongs to Roman foundation legend. It should not be presented as an eyewitness reconstruction, consensual courtship, a wedding, or the later intervention of the Sabine women. The pictorial reading distinguishes seizure, flight, low-lying distress, and resistance; it does not infer that every male figure has the same role.

画中是建城传说里的掳夺，不能写成可靠现场纪实、自愿求偶、婚礼，或后来妇女介入停战的另一幕。图中的抓夺、逃避、受惊和抵挡分别阅读，不把所有男子赋予同一行动。

## 理论出处 Theoretical sources

4. Qin, Han (秦汉). [SAE Judgment and Aesthetics — In Tribute to Kant](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html), second edition, 2026. [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19296710](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19296710). Source for the framework’s distinction between personal response and a fallible aesthetic claim, and for its treatment of aesthetic rays as directions of judgment.

5. Qin, Han (秦汉). [The Beauty of Philosophy / 哲学之美](https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-aesthetics-ray-2.html), 2026. [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22985905](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22985905). In particular §§2–5 and 7: a distinction must open a question and remain examinable itself; classification, a question with its answer already built in, and a local distinction elevated into an unconditional judgment do not suffice. 原论文已读。本文借此提出“看清形式组织”与“赞同行为”的区分练习，随后指出两者在具体画面中仍会互相作用；没有宣称本作满足该射线全部条件。

The SAE papers are the philosophical work of Han Qin and carry CC BY 4.0. Their use here is a limited, explicitly contemporary application. It is not a reconstruction of Poussin’s thinking or a statement of established art-historical consensus.

SAE原论文署名秦汉，许可CC BY 4.0。本篇只作有限的当代应用，不把它写成普桑思想或艺术史既定共识。
