No resizing, interpolation, colour conversion, adjustment, sharpening, denoising, restoration, or generated content has been applied.

全图外黑边与画布窄边属于摄影记录，予以完整保留。局部用于检查相邻人物与空间关系，未把妇女仅裁成孤立身体片段。右侧局部保留黄甲男子举剑的整段、灰须男子的抵挡动作和蓝衣女子抓布的手；局部上边缘截到背景人物，完整关系仍可参看图1。

The black surround and narrow canvas edge belong to the photographic record and remain in the full file. The crops preserve neighbouring figures and spatial relationships rather than isolating fragments of the women’s bodies. The right-hand crop retains the swordsman’s raised weapon, the bearded man’s opposing arm, and the woman’s grip on the cloth. Its upper boundary cuts through background figures; Figure 1 supplies the complete scene.
