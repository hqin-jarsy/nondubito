## 古代文本 / Ancient text

4. 柏拉图 / Plato, [Phaedo, Jowett translation, Internet Classics Archive at MIT](https://classics.mit.edu/Plato/phaedo.html).

5. 柏拉图 / Plato, [Phaedo, Center for Hellenic Studies](https://chs.harvard.edu/primary-source/plato-phaedo-sb/). 带Stephanus段号的现代编辑版本，用于核对段落定位。正文各语均自主转述，没有复制现代中文译文或大段现代英译。/ A modern edited version with Stephanus section numbers, used to locate passages. All language versions paraphrase independently; no modern Chinese translation or substantial modern English passage is reproduced.

主要段落 / Principal passages:

- 57a–59c：转述框架、喜悲混合及柏拉图缺席 / the narrative frame, mixed emotions, and Plato's absence
- 60d–61b：梦、作诗与音乐 / the dream, poetry, and music
- 85e–86d、91c–95a：琴与和谐的反对意见及回应 / the lyre-and-harmony objection and the response
- 89d–91c：关心论证的真而不只赞同苏格拉底 / concern for truth rather than mere agreement with Socrates
- 117a–118a：递杯、饮毒、悲泣与死亡的先后 / the sequence of the cup, drinking, grief, and death

《斐多》是经过文学与哲学组织的古代文本；本文不把它当作现场录音。历史人物、文本角色和画中安排分别讨论。

The Phaedo is a literary and philosophical ancient text, not a recording of the event. The historical people, the dialogue's roles, and the painting's arrangement are kept distinct.
