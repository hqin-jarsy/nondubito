## 本篇的观看路线 / Reading route

从杯沿上方尚未接住的手开始，紧接古代事件与1787年的背景。三个主要部分分别追随接杯与说话的双重动作、周围人不同的身体反应，以及本来缺席的柏拉图为何被画入现场。可选SAE只在主体导读之后增加一个观看问题。

The essay begins with the hand that has not yet received the cup, then introduces the ancient event and the painting's setting in 1787. Three main movements follow the hands, the responses of the surrounding bodies, and the inclusion of the absent Plato. The optional SAE section adds a question after the main reading is complete.
