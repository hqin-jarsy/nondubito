## English

The surviving Roman building dates to the early second century CE. The approximate 43.3 m measurement concerns the interior diameter and total floor-to-apex height, not the height of the dome alone. The Soane drawings are later records, dated 1778.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
