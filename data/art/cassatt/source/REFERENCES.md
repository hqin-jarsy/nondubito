## 资料与细读的分工 / Facts and close looking

正文的大部分篇幅来自对本包全图与局部图的独立观察：背部的比例、手与桨的交叠、抱持姿态、衣纹、船舷弧线及色块与纵深的往返。历史事实只在需要处援引以上来源。文中的整体读法属于本篇作者的分析，不作为卡萨特的心理意图或馆方结论。

Most of the essay develops independent observations of the supplied image: the scale of the back, the overlaps around the hand and oar, the supported child, patterned clothing, curving gunwales, and the interaction of color shapes with depth. Historical sources are cited where needed. The essay’s central reading is an interpretation, not a statement of Cassatt’s intention or a museum endorsement. No modern source is reproduced or translated at length.
