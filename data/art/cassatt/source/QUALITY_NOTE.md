# 质量审核说明 / Quality note

版本：v0.1，作者审阅包。审核日期：2026年10月14日。

Version 0.1, prepared for author review. Review date: 14 October 2026.

## 全文与基线比较 / Full-text review and the series baseline

八种语言均已逐篇全文审读，结合全图与四张局部图核对，随后复核修订句及其相邻段落。另完整阅读《持天平的女人》v0.3中文基线，比较传统导读的深度、论点、图证与文章力度。质量判断以实际内容为依据；字数、文件数量和校验值另用于完整性检查。

本篇保留了与首篇相当的细读厚度：真实尺度、背部的比例和体积、观看位置、桨与横木、握持和抱持、头部朝向、衣纹与笔触、帆和船弧之间的水面形状、表面色块与纵深的关系，都有具体展开。文章围绕“共同维持有限船内空间”形成独立读法。四节各增加新的视觉依据；传统导读可独立阅读，可选SAE只延伸实际观看。结构和证据随本画展开，没有强行增加第五节或借用其他画作的技术材料。

All eight essays received full-text, model-assisted review against the complete image and four details, followed by checks of revised sentences in context. The Chinese text of the Vermeer v0.3 baseline was also read in full for comparison of depth, argument and visual evidence. Counts and checksums serve separate completeness checks.

This essay gives sustained attention to scale, bodily mass, viewpoint, oar and crosspiece, gripping and supporting arms, heads, patterned clothing, brush marks, the water shape between sail and gunwale, and the interaction of depth with broad color areas. Its four sections develop a distinct reading of bodies maintaining a shared, confined space. The main essay stands on its own; the optional SAE section extends concrete acts of looking.

## 已落实的实质修订 / Substantive revisions

- 将三顶帽子的关系明确为蓝色水面背景中的高低错开，消除人物隔水的误读。
- 英、德、日等语种明确“高起水面”指画面中的位置，避免被读成外海、海况或海平面变化。
- 法语把桨叶保持在“画外”这一可确认范围；袖口明确为衣料，不写成深色手腕。
- 孩子的重量统一落在当前被手臂托持的姿态上，避免推断其独坐能力。
- 日语明确眼睛是朝一侧看；韩语图注以头部朝向描述人物，保留被帽檐遮住的眼睛这一可见条件。
- 西语将前后关系明确为画面空间；八语传统结尾均落回观看如何容纳已在场的身体。

Revisions clarify the hats’ positions, distinguish a high placement of water in the composition from sea conditions, keep the unseen oar blade outside the frame without inventing its state, identify the dark cuff accurately, and describe the child’s present support without inferring physical ability. Japanese and Korean wording was refined around gaze and head direction, Spanish around spatial depth, and the conclusions around the viewer’s experience of bodily presence.

## 图像、资料与包级检查 / Images, sources and package checks

- 全图与馆方下载JPEG逐字节一致。
- 四张局部的解码像素与原图对应矩形区域一致，内嵌ICC色彩配置保留。
- 八份正文各含五幅图的本地图注及非空替代文字；来源URL集合一致，包内相对链接可解析。
- 简繁中文保持同稿；其他六语完整重写后分别全文检查，保留历史背景、全部核心细读与可选SAE练习。
- 研究目录PDF、网页截图和制作中间文件不收入交付包。图像登记、文件清单、SHA-256与ZIP完整性独立核验。

The original JPEG is byte-identical to the museum download. All four crops match their source pixel regions and retain the embedded ICC profile. Each language includes five localized captions and alternative texts; source-URL sets match and local links resolve. The Chinese versions preserve the same text, while the six rewrites retain the complete argument and optional exercises. Research PDFs, webpage captures and production intermediates are excluded. The image inventory, package manifest, checksums and ZIP integrity receive separate verification.

## 审阅范围与限度 / Scope and limits

审核结果支持作为完整作者审阅稿交付。本包为模型辅助制作和审读，尚未经八种语言的母语人工编辑终校，也未替代作者最终裁定。数字图像支持构图与可见笔迹的观察，不能替代面对原作或技术检测；本篇没有据屏幕颜色提出颜料化学判断。

The review supports delivery as a complete author-review package. Production and review were model-assisted; the texts have not received final editing by human native speakers in all eight languages and remain subject to the author’s judgment. Digital images support close looking at composition and visible marks, while differing from direct examination and technical analysis.
