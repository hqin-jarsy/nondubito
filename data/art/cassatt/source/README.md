## 本篇读法 / This essay’s reading

文章从右侧巨大的深蓝背部开始，沿握桨、抱持和垂下的双腿进入船内，再看面孔、衣纹以及海面、帆和船舷的关系。本文将画中的亲近感理解为几个人共同维持有限空间的身体事实：明亮颜色里仍有重量，靠得很近也需要留出位置。四节结构围绕本画展开。

The essay begins with the large dark-blue back, follows the grip, supporting arms and dangling legs, then considers the faces, clothing, sea, sail and gunwale. Its reading locates the picture’s intimacy in bodies sharing and sustaining a confined space. Bright color retains weight; proximity requires room. The four main sections grow from this particular painting.
