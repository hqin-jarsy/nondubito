# Episode 12 — Sources / 来源与版本核对

Accessed and read 2026-10-19 UTC. Image evidence was viewed, not inferred from metadata alone.

1. **Palace Museum, 《明拓鲁相韩勅造孔庙礼器碑册》**
   https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234635.html
   Establishes the Han stone's date (Yongshou 2, 156 CE), Han Chi's temple and ritual-equipment project, this album's Ming rubbing date, preservation of front/back/sides, Zhang Boying's label, and Wang Shu's assessment “瘦劲如铁，变化若龙”. The article quotes that brief historical criticism and tests it against the actual figures. Its location wording “今存山东曲阜孔庙” is not used as the current location without correction; see source 4.
2. **Palace Museum Digital Cultural Relics, accession 新00044512**
   https://digicol.dpm.org.cn/cultural/detail?id=f13cba4b1de349ca92bc1f3be913ecf3&source=6
   Confirms the specific Ming album and supplies the linked image list and official high-resolution viewers. The original stone and this paper object are consistently distinguished. The public image list contains 39 images; these list indices are not assigned original leaf numbers.
3. **Public image-list page**
   https://digicol.dpm.org.cn/cultural/listCulturalImage?id=f13cba4b1de349ca92bc1f3be913ecf3
   Source of public image identifiers and thumbnail URLs. Four normal public viewers were opened in a cloud browser and captured. Detailed coordinates, links, and limitations: IMAGE_NOTES.md.
4. **Agency for Cultural Affairs / Tokyo National Museum, 文化遺産オンライン《礼器碑》**
   https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/537055
   Its explanation locates the original stone at the Han and Wei Stone Inscriptions Exhibition Hall in Qufu, Shandong. This record's displayed rubbing is **not** the Palace Museum album reproduced here; only its statement about the original stone's present location is used.
5. **Du Zhiyong, 《礼器碑》及其刊定, 中国社会科学报／中国社会科学网, 2024-01-05**
   https://www.cssn.cn/skgz/bwyc/202401/t20240105_5725750.shtml
   Cross-checks that the stone moved from the eastern side hall of Qufu's Confucius Temple to the Han and Wei inscription hall, and supplies a contextual transcription of the ritual-equipment passage. This article's reconstructions of the sequence of carving and possible novelty of donor recording are interpretive proposals, not established facts adopted here.
6. **秦汉, 《木头里留着那一年的旱》, aesthetic ray 05**
   https://nondubito.net/essays/aesthetics/ray05.html?lang=zh
   English official page/title: https://nondubito.net/essays/aesthetics/en/ray05.html?lang=en — **The Wood Still Holds That Dry Year**. Read in full. Used only in the optional appendix: a surviving trace is not itself a complete causal story; additional comparison is required to assign a cause or date to damage. No personality judgment is derived from calligraphy.
7. **《SAE判断力与美学》**
   https://self-as-an-end.net/papers/sae-judgment-aesthetics.html
   Read for the causal ray and judgment framework. The appendix applies the distinction to the visible 王, irregular damage and album joins. The traditional formal analysis stands independently.

## Transcription and evidence boundaries

Quoted classical passages retain their complete quoted extent and receive full prose explanations in each language. Forms such as 韓勅, 禮, 器, 車 and 遲 remain visible alongside translated meanings. The donor numeral 二百 is not assigned an unverified coin denomination. The writer's name and number of hands are not established. No paper is dated to 156 CE. Cut-mounting and damage are not presented as voluntary brush actions.
