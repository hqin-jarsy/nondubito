## English

The main building was constructed in 532–537; after collapse in 558 the dome was rebuilt higher in 562. A 2019 museum brochure records a historical display state; mosque use resumed in 2020.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
