## English

The centre opened in 1977. Piano, Rogers and engineering teams including Arup contributed to the project. The text distinguishes structure, weather enclosure and services, and treats the 2030 renovation as a project rather than completed experience.

The linked institutional records provide historical and material context. Interpretive comparisons belong to the essay and are not claims about an architect’s unrecorded intentions.
