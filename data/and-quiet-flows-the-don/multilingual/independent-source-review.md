# And Quiet Flows the Don: independent source audit

Reviewer: moon. Scope: all five original Chinese articles and all five old English articles fully read. Chinese 106/106/126/118/124 = 580 blocks; old English 8 each = 40 blocks. Original novel checked at targeted passages, not read cover to cover in this audit. No article edits by reviewer.

## Evidence
- Russian novel, books III–IV, parts VI–VIII: https://lib.ru/PROZA/SHOLOHOW/tihijdon34.txt (1980 edition transcription; local read-only reference /tmp/don34.txt).
- Part IV chapter IV, original-novel excerpt with precise attribution in national examination: https://ance.gov.md/sites/default/files/12_llru_u_test_ss17.pdf . This directly says his heart hardened and the pain for other people felt at the beginning of war had gone. Accordingly a claim that he never became numb throughout seven years is contradicted, not merely unverified.
- Chinese ending, part VIII chapter XVIII: https://doosho.com/yi/219/8/18 . Daughter dies of diphtheria, not scarlet fever. Dunyasha is alive.

## Confirmed minimum repairs
1. EP1/4/5: replace never becoming accustomed / never crossing the line with an account that allows both hardening and renewed moral pain. Do not invent a permanently immaculate protagonist. Retain author's argument about political categories failing to exhaust a person.
2. VI.XXXIII–XXXIV: Petro and fellow prisoners emerge and cluster, not a documented execution queue. Petro appeals to Ivan Alexeyevich, his child's godfather; Koshevoi fires. Koshevoi's whole body trembles, not specifically a shaking gun hand. Following chapter reports eleven corpses, Petro plus ten others.
3. VI.LVI: Daria shoots captive Ivan; Antip then bayonets him. This verified scene must not silently become Grigory personally witnessing generic women killing with shovels and immediately leaving the Whites. That specific claim/causal connection was not established.
4. VII.XVI: Natalia tells Ilinichna she is pregnant and in her third month. Use pregnant again, not third pregnancy. Her decision follows learning of renewed infidelity; do not reduce it exclusively to absence of a doctor or a husband prevented from returning by war.
5. VIII.X: narrative explicitly describes grain requisition and requisition detachments in late 1920 and the unrest around them. Index English collectivizing power is misleading if read as later agricultural collectivization; coercive political power preserves the essay's broad meaning without that historical implication.
6. VIII.XVII: Aksinia is shot after a sentry challenges the fleeing pair and shots are fired at them. No warrant for nobody aimed at her. She dies shortly before dawn; Grigory buries her in morning daylight, not before dawn. The sword and his hands are used to dig.
7. VIII.XVIII: the expected May Day amnesty is deserters' hope, not guaranteed narratorial future. Grigory discards a rifle and Nagant revolver and pours away ammunition, not a pistol alone and bullets one by one. March ice remains on river. The boy recognizes him; the original does not give a joyful run toward him. Son reports aunt Dunya alive, sister dead, Mikhail away on service. End remains open, not certain future execution.
8. EP3 death arithmetic: both Petro and Aksinia die by gunfire, and Petro's death is an execution rather than battlefield fighting. Keep distinction between battlefield deaths and war's wider destruction; do not repair by saying only Petro was shot.
9. Dunyasha cares for the children, but cannot have raised both to adulthood since Poliushka dies as a child. Living sister also defeats the literal statement that son is his sole living relative. Son can remain the ending's concrete relation without erasing her.
10. EP2 illegal love remains central; blanket statements that every legitimate marriage has disappeared are unsafe given Dunyasha/Koshevoi. Likewise Grigory's military absence must not automatically become a voluntary abandonment of Aksinia.

## Boundaries / unresolved details
- First Austrian killing trauma is supported; vomiting specifically and repeatedly looking into every victim's face were not independently verified. Remove repetition/totality rather than fabricate scenes.
- Specific women-with-sticks/shovels scene and its supposed direct cause of leaving the Whites remain unverified. Broader political/military changes have multiple circumstances; do not reduce every change of side to one atrocity without evidence.
- Exact final age of Mishatka has not been independently pinned down in this audit; three years old is unsafe. Say young son without making up a replacement age.
- Last words' cold modifies sun in the Russian sentence. Translate or paraphrase transparently rather than inventing an exact quotation from a named Chinese translation.
- Some individual medical explanations and Ilinichna's dream interlocutor still require precise localization; avoid categorical additions.
- Author's ethical reading, refusal to reduce characters to factions, private attachment versus social legitimacy, and irrecoverable household losses should stay. Corrections do not authorize a new counter-essay.

## Old English completeness
All forty existing body blocks read. They carry the five main interpretive arcs but omit much Chinese concrete evidence (named deaths, prisoner's dialogue, family relationships, qualifications). They are not complete translations. Expand missing scenes and factual supports while retaining readable independent English structure; equal block counts are not the acceptance criterion.

## Revised-source full read, subsequent primary check
All 580 candidate revised Chinese blocks read in full (106/106/126/118/124), not merely the correction list. Remaining local inconsistency: EP3 block29 still scarlet fever, whereas surrounding source correctly says diphtheria. Submitted one consolidated correction list to editor.

Important new evidence: parts VIII.VIII–IX (same lib.ru original-novel transcription). Grigory reports for registration and returns, later showing endorsed military papers to Koshevoi. He moves to Aksinia with the children. Dunyasha offers that she and her husband will move out; he permits them to remain until spring. Brother and sister explicitly preserve their bond. She urges him to marry Aksinia and reports their late mother's approval; he jokes about inviting his sister to the wedding. Thus an immediate literal expulsion from the family home followed by the final sibling bond being severed is wrong. Political hostility, uncertainty and later arrest danger can support the author's reading that home no longer offers security without falsifying these scenes. Likewise absolutely no one ever blessing the love must become its long lack of institutional recognition, not the absence of every later family blessing. Parent notified because multiple interpretive dependency sentences need narrow repair, not wholesale thematic replacement.

Other consolidated items: remove abandonment as the cause of Aksinia's isolation while Grigory serves; do not erase Natalia's agency in abortion merely to describe constrained choice; limit all legitimate marriages failed claims because Dunyasha/Koshevoi survive as a married pair; describe amnesty as hoped for. Ilinichna dream's supposed daughter-in-law addressee was not verified, and neither daughter-in-law is alive at her death: prefer she spoke of dreaming of her son without inventing an addressee. No claim of full original-novel verification is made.
