## Meaning and readability
- 01 retains the distinction between prudential reciprocity and limiting present power, including the self-defense qualification. The lion's laughter, the mouse's teeth, and the unseen future net remain central rather than being replaced with abstract summary.
- 02 retains the distinctions between present loss, later consequences, action now and a final life judgment. All warnings against cruel consolation, manufactured injury and evasion of responsibility remain. “How do you know?” stays deliberately open rather than becoming a promise of good fortune.
- 03 retains both neglect and over-intervention as mistakes. Practice, requirements and supervision are not flattened into an unconditional hands-off principle. The tired farmer and visible-height joke remain.
- 04 keeps the mark accurate while distinguishing its changing reference. The counterexample of a sword tied to the boat and the qualifications about promises and stable rules remain.
- 05 retains learning from genuine success, causal control versus receiving a result, and justified waiting versus an unfalsifiable method. The final second-rabbit line remains a punch line.
