## Findings and action

The sample retains the source distinctions between evidence and inference, assistance and entitlement to govern, altered wishes and material conditions, and mutual aid and the conditions that make aid necessary. It preserves the approved missing-negation correction in 06, source notes, emphasis and internal line breaks.

Two Korean grammar refinements were confirmed against the Chinese and incorporated: 16 `[114]` explicitly names colliding with the earthen pot before breaking it; 19 `[134]` names people as the ones whose judgments change. Neither alters the source meaning.

The apparent conflict between a direct imperative in 20 `[20]` and the denial of a direct command in 20 `[90]` is preserved for the author's decision, not silently converted into a claim about physical coercion. The city-mouse story in 09 was read with its intervening tasting passage, rather than treated as a demonstrated source contradiction. No other material mistranslation was found in this sample.

## Author-approved follow-up, 2026-11-08

The source-20 issue described above is historical. The author subsequently approved replacing 20 `[90]` with `他们没有强行让婆罗门放下羊。`. Simplified and Traditional Chinese and all six translations now express lack of coercion, while the earlier imperative at `[20]` and all other prose remain unchanged.
