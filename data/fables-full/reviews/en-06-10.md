## Semantic and literary review

- 07 preserves effectiveness, gentleness and legitimacy as different judgments. It does not turn all indirect influence into coercion; proportionality, refusal, transparency and supportive arrangements all remain.
- 08 preserves the boy's responsibility and the villagers' reasonable distrust, without making credibility determine truth. The verification alternatives, costs of false alarms, repair of trust and collective consequences remain in full.
- 09 preserves both mice's possible choices, limits arising from shared costs, and the value of trying a path and returning. Neither country life nor town life is made universally superior.
- 10 retains the crow's initial caution, her own agency, the fox's test design and all examples of identity-based pressure. The final “So?” pause and cheese/praise contrast remain sharp rather than moralizing.
