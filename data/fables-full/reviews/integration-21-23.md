# Additional integration review: 21–23

Eleven source paragraphs were compared across English, Japanese, French, German
and Spanish (55 target-paragraph checks): 21[44,61,125], 22[57,69,153],
23[58,74,79,97,136]. These cover responsibility for requesting authority without
consenting to predation, withdrawal of authorization, fair allocation without
justifying the underlying harm, and valuing continuing care without manufacturing
dependence or requiring impossible independence.

All selected qualifications are present. Spanish 21[61] was changed from
“dejarse devorar” to the passive “ser devoradas” so that being eaten does not
suggest voluntary submission. The Chinese is unchanged. No other new semantic
issue arose in this sample.

Together the five integration batches sample 69 source paragraphs in each of
five foreign languages (345 target-paragraph comparisons), plus the first thirty
translated titles. This does not claim independent review of all sentences.
