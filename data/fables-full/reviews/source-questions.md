# Chinese source questions and decisions

## 06: missing negation, corrected

The opening fox quotation said `看看百兽见到我敢不敢逃` (source text index 3).
A single missing 不 was restored: `看看百兽见到我敢不敢不逃`.
The same correction is applied to Simplified and Traditional Chinese and the six
translations. This is within the author's permission for small Chinese fixes;
no larger argument is rewritten. Original page snapshots and the original 06
source JSON remain intact. Exact record: `../source-corrections.json`.

The underlying phrase is `观百兽之见我而敢不走乎` in *Strategies of the Warring
States*, Chu I. Independently opened and checked on 2026-11-08:
https://www.shidianguji.com/book/SK0818/chapter/1m10mt143cz6i

## 09: narrative ellipsis and the city-life metaphor, no Chinese edit

Opening text 6 interrupts the country mouse just as it is about to taste the
food; text 30 explicitly says it has eaten food and escaped the cat and dog.
The opening does not narrate every intervening action, so this is not an
established factual contradiction. In text 81, `尝过以后离开` sits among paragraphs
about experiencing a way of life before deciding. English and French were
clarified to keep that broader experience/taste-of-city-life scope. No eating
scene was added and no source correction is proposed.

## 20: direct command versus coercion, approved and resolved

The original opening at text 20 says “你怎么把一头驴扛在肩上？快把它放下，别让更多人看见。” The later text 90 originally said `他们没有直接命令婆罗门放下羊。` This denied a direct command despite the earlier imperative.

The author explicitly approved the exact replacement on 2026-11-08: `他们没有强行让婆罗门放下羊。` This is a substantive distinction between a command and coercion, adopted only after that approval. It is now synchronized in Traditional Chinese and all six translations.

The surrounding statements remain unchanged: the Brahmin himself finally discards the goat, but performing the act himself does not mean the conditions for that act were not deliberately arranged by others. The earlier imperative also remains unchanged. All original snapshots and `source-original/20.json` preserve the prior reading. Exact changes and review: `approved-20-correction.md`.

The issue was identified internally in the text; this correction does not claim an external primary-source audit.
