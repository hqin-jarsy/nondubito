## Semantic review by essay

### 11: The ant and the cicada

Preserved both sides of the argument: preparation and material support matter, but saving food does not grant authority to judge every other form of life. Responsibility for an earlier choice remains distinct from the question of help. The missing facts about food reserves, winter, alternative sources, and previous contribution remain missing, rather than being invented in either animal's favor. Conditional assistance, resource limits, unequal starting conditions, and the possibility that the cicada really was shortsighted are all retained.

Kept the opening cicada distinct from the later violin-playing grasshopper. Used `매미` and `메뚜기` rather than silently replacing the later insect with the familiar Korean fable's katydid. Retained the source note's explanation of the later work/play framing.

### 12: Lord She and the dragon

Retained the outer Zizhang/Lord Ai frame, the seven-day wait, and the lack of basic courtesy. Fear of a real dragon is not translated as proof that all prior affection was fraudulent. The right to set boundaries and refuse remains explicit. The central demand is willingness to encounter and assess an actual other, not unconditional acceptance of danger, advice, or disagreement. The examples concerning independence, caregivers, poverty, young people, and tradition all remain.

### 13: The fish in the rut

Retained both narrative levels, the loan of grain, the promise of three hundred gold, the promised river, and the urgency of survival. In particular, 13[28] deliberately remains `물고기가 죽고 나면 삼백 금도 쓸모없다.` The source switches narrative levels there; the fish was not silently replaced with Zhuang Zhou.

Preserved the quantities: one dou and one sheng are `한 말 한 되`; the later one-dou examples remain `한 말`. No cup, bowl, or modern-volume conversion replaces them. Kept the distinction between emergency aid and structural reform, the recipient's participation in defining help, the giver's limits, and the refusal to turn rescue into ownership of the recipient's future. The critique of delayed procedures does not become a demand to abolish procedure.

### 14: Learning to walk in Handan

Preserved the value of imitation and the legitimate temporary instability involved in learning. The essay does not become an instruction to remain unchanged. Kept its tests of transfer, adaptation, reversibility, and independent judgment, along with the original Zhuangzi frame involving Gongsun Long and Wei Mou. Neither the teacher nor the learner is assigned invented blame. The final distinction remains between acquiring an ability and maintaining an appearance.

### 15: The bloodied mongoose

Kept the mongoose, black snake, water jar, absent husband, and mother's existing suspicion. The article still takes protective responsibility and real danger seriously. It does not demand perfect information, emotionlessness, or endless waiting. At the same time, the very short available check and the irreversible killing remain central. Strengthened the Korean at 15[27] to express that the act cannot be undone, rather than merely being difficult to undo.

Retained the separation of observation from interpretation, the burden on the party with power when the other cannot speak, and the distinct roles of final action, earlier care arrangements, resource pressure, and missing information. The mother's direct responsibility is not diluted into equal blame. The concluding tension between sometimes acting quickly and sometimes protecting the facts is intact.

## Readability revisions made during the comparative reread

- Smoothed 11's distinction between sustaining life and making life worth continuing, and repaired the awkward phrasing about judging a resource-holder's refusal.
- Replaced repeated stiff uses of `애호` in 12 with ordinary Korean expressions for liking something, without changing the argument or the controlled-image metaphor.
- Removed `물 한 모금` from 13's draft title because it could suggest drinking rather than the fish's need for water as its living medium. The final title uses `지금 건네는 물 조금`. Clarified the risk involved in timely judgment and corrected Korean spacing.
- Restored the explicit “sometimes” qualification in 14's title, improved the wording of “learning from others,” removed ambiguous subjects around imitation and teaching, and retained the distinction between old capacities being unusable and being disallowed.
- Clarified 15's irreversible action, the possibility of several interpretations of one sign, and the sentence about a serious consequence having more causes than the final blow. Polished particles and spacing without removing the rhetorical short paragraphs.

## Source concerns and limits

The cross-level fish/gold sentence in 13 is conspicuous but intentional in this translation, as requested. The difference between 11's cicada opening and the later grasshopper version is likewise retained, not harmonized. No other source issue required a Chinese change. Ancient source editions and external factual claims were not independently re-researched for this translation task; the existing source notes were translated faithfully.
