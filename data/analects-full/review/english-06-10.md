# English 06–10: semantic and editorial review

Read all five Chinese originals and their v0.2 editorial notes before translating. Manually reviewed the completed prose against scene order, each argument, quotations, name identities, comparative strength, and final questions. Word counts including metadata: 06 1023; 07 938; 08 1064; 09 1124; 10 1044.

- 06: Included both courtyard encounters, Chen Kang's three discoveries, the teacher's no-secrets declaration, both funerals, inner versus outer coffin, teaching-lineage knowledge as property, recorders, and the later courtyard allusion. Boyu/Kong Li and Qiu/Confucius are explicit. Preserved the strongest-evidence assertion and cannot-give/no-asset distinction. The allusion's irony remains the author's reading; no extra claim that everyone using the idiom believed in secret transmission.
- 07: Preserved the dismissive ox-cleaver joke, exact direction of Ziyou's correction using the teacher's own principles, all three rejected alternatives to public acknowledgment, the author's pride interpretation, Duke Zhao and Zai Yu episodes, Zigong's eclipse image, and final challenge. Yan/Yan Yan/Ziyou and Qiu/Confucius clarified. Distinguishes admission from apology. No attribution of inferred pride to a direct ancient record.
- 08: Preserved all three of Zai Wo's arguments, both permissions, reason/order distinction, after-departure dissatisfaction, love directed toward parents in the source's interpretation, coercive-law conclusion, and persuasion boundary. Explicitly prevents Yu from being mistaken for Confucius's first-person pronoun. Replaced erroneous character count with “requirement.” No conversion to thirty-six months, no new dynasty or statute. Alternative interpretations of permission/love remain unresolved rather than replacing the author.
- 09: Retained sole-firsthand-record assertion, all quote clauses and their successive analysis, cannot-stop/dare-not-stop distinction, no-path argument, student's gain versus teacher's regret, posthumous title versus ongoing growth, poverty/joy distinction, and closing question. Yan Yuan/Yan Hui identified. “Sage Restorer” does not become Mencius's “Second Sage.” Cultural learning and ritual remain two actions upon the learner.
- 10: Retained all behavioral examples, recorder's negative focus, instantaneous persons-before-property priority, camera analogy and complete/partial interior gradient, medicine/elders/community examples, both ritual judgments, later removal of judgment, and final camera question. “Few words” avoids the erroneous eleven-character count. Qiu identified. Historical assertions about horse value and tasting a medicine remain the essay's account without adding numerical or medical claims. Strong “cannot record” assertion preserved; complete interior capture versus partial traces made explicit, as the source's own gradient requires.

Quotations are fresh translations of the Chinese, with no modern translator attribution. Each original DOI, chapter list, CC BY 4.0, and overview retained. No external historical verification is claimed by this translation review.
