# English 41–45: semantic review

Reviewed full Chinese manuscripts and all five associated v0.2 notes.zh.md files, then checked the complete English texts against the source arguments, scenes, quotations, endings and references. These are full new translations, not expansions of the old English summaries. No modern translator is credited for independently translated classical quotations.

- 41: Checked both opening quotations, the three adversity scenes, three targeted criticisms, distinction between opposition and non-use as evidence, possible misuse versus preemptive condemnation, and all three closing criteria. Tuo and Chao of Song are people; gu is explained as a vessel. Source says “ten” essays but counts five opposed plus four unlike him; retained the numbers and did not invent a tenth classification. “Earlier thirty-one essays” is likewise retained rather than silently renumbered.
- 42: Checked Yan Hui/Yan Yuan identity, Yan Lu as father, Li as Confucius's son, coffin versus outer coffin, the refusal and actual disobedience. All four later “lavish burial” analogies remain analogies, not additional funerals. Preserved the strong claim that the passage proves both overriding reverence and actual honest recording, plus the teacher/student distinction in who is taken as the end.
- 43: Checked Zhongni as Confucius, the four defenses and their respective interlocutors, wall/gate, sun/moon and ladder images, and especially all four paired governing actions: teacher supports, people stand; teacher guides, people proceed; teacher reassures, people come; teacher stirs, people act harmoniously. The author's claim “not one act of making a sage” and his youth/later-life chronology remain authorial interpretation, not additions presented as independently verified history. No supernatural event added; eclipse and public correction argument retained.
- 44: Checked both favorable examples before the critique, all five clusters (filial devotion, Way, ren, self-examination, taking up the teacher's words), exact force of “and nothing more,” and the difference between not acting outside office and not thinking beyond station. Ren has an ethical gloss; zhong/shu is rendered as doing one's utmost and putting oneself in another's place. Retained speculative “perhaps” for why Song thinkers chose this lineage. Retained the categorical final “none of his sayings,” despite its tension with 45; did not silently add “directly” or narrow its scope.
- 45: Checked twenty-five passages in Book Nineteen; the complete friendship exchange and both one-sided risks; the recorder's refusal to adjudicate; craftsmen/exemplary person parallel; permission for minor deviations and the risk of abusing that permission. Retained Zengzi's compassion saying as usable evidence, preserving rather than repairing the 44/45 tension. Final criterion is structure rather than speaker's name.

Readability check: paragraphs preserve the Chinese argumentative sequence, quotes remain distinct from exposition, aliases are explained when needed, and metaphors are intelligible without a Chinese background. Source DOI, CC BY 4.0 and all passage numbers were compared to each manuscript. Source notes translate the five-volume titles descriptively; title consistency can be harmonized across the full project without changing the articles.

Open author queries: 41's nine-versus-ten count; 44/45's scope inconsistency. Neither blocks a faithful translation, and neither has been silently corrected.
