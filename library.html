权利论
Rights Theory

"权利"常常被裹挟在恩赐、许可、律法条文，或者"权力愿意留给你的余地"这些说法里，很少有人问：权利本身究竟是什么。这个系列从清理这些误认开始，逐步搭建一套结构性的权利理论——权利来自一个以自身为目的的主体如何为自己立法，从最基础的生命与身体层面的权利，一路展开成一整条通向承认性权利的谱系。

Rights are usually wrapped in language borrowed from gift-giving, permission, statute, or "whatever space power is willing to leave you" — rarely does anyone ask what a right actually is. This series begins by clearing away those misidentifications, then builds a structural theory from the ground up: rights arise from how a being that is an end in itself legislates for itself, unfolding from the most basic rights of life and body into a full spectrum reaching toward recognition-based rights.

战争论
War Theory

战争是目的王国必须处理、却最不希望发生的那件事。对它保持沉默，等于把这个场域整个让给别的话语去主宰。而一旦要说，就得先说清楚一件事：这个系列真正的主角不是战争，是涵育——主体性在时间中持续深化、互相生长的那个过程。战争在这张地图上位置很低，它是涵育被打断时不得不启用的兜底。正篇七篇（八种语言）之后是兵法接口九篇（英／简／繁）：让 14DD 那一侧的战争哲学自己说话——康德，克劳塞维茨与《论持久战》，老子与《司马法》与《墨子》，正义战争论传统与卢梭，孙子，甘地，新渡户——问它们与 SAE 在哪里同行，在哪里分开，在哪里必须批评，在哪里说不上话，最后结账。

War is the thing the Kingdom of Ends must handle and least wants to happen. Staying silent about it hands the whole field over to other discourses. And once you do speak, one thing has to be settled first: the protagonist of this series is not war but cultivation — the process by which subjectivity deepens and grows into other subjectivity over time. War sits very low on that map. It is the backstop invoked only when cultivation has been broken. After the seven (in eight languages) come the nine interface essays (English / 中文 / 繁體): the war philosophy of the other side speaking for itself — Kant; Clausewitz and On Protracted War; Laozi, the Sima Fa and the Mozi; the just war tradition and Rousseau; Sunzi; Gandhi; Nitobe — and then the reckoning.
