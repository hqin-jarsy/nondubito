权力论 · Shi
Power Theory · Shi

权力不是恶，也不是德——它是结构。这个系列从一个不寻常的地方出发：不问权力对不对，问权力是什么。当两个相互承认彼此主体性的人处在不对等的位置上，会涌现出什么？这个不对等从哪里来，以什么形态运作，遇到什么限度，走向哪两条截然不同的路，又如何消解？

Power is neither evil nor virtue — it is structure. This series begins somewhere unusual: not asking whether power is justified, but asking what power is. When two people who recognize each other as subjects occupy unequal positions, what emerges? Where does that asymmetry come from, what shapes does it take, where does it hit its limits, what two very different paths can it follow, and how does it dissolve?

权利论
Rights Theory

"权利"常常被裹挟在恩赐、许可、律法条文，或者"权力愿意留给你的余地"这些说法里，很少有人问：权利本身究竟是什么。这个系列从清理这些误认开始，逐步搭建一套结构性的权利理论——权利来自一个以自身为目的的主体如何为自己立法，从最基础的生命与身体层面的权利，一路展开成一整条通向承认性权利的谱系。

Rights are usually wrapped in language borrowed from gift-giving, permission, statute, or "whatever space power is willing to leave you" — rarely does anyone ask what a right actually is. This series begins by clearing away those misidentifications, then builds a structural theory from the ground up: rights arise from how a being that is an end in itself legislates for itself, unfolding from the most basic rights of life and body into a full spectrum reaching toward recognition-based rights.

战争论
War Theory

战争是目的王国必须处理、却最不希望发生的那件事。对它保持沉默，等于把这个场域整个让给别的话语去主宰。而一旦要说，就得先说清楚一件事：这个系列真正的主角不是战争，是涵育——主体性在时间中持续深化、互相生长的那个过程。战争在这张地图上位置很低，它是涵育被打断时不得不启用的兜底。

War is the thing the Kingdom of Ends must handle and least wants to happen. Staying silent about it hands the whole field over to other discourses. And once you do speak, one thing has to be settled first: the protagonist of this series is not war but cultivation — the process by which subjectivity deepens and grows into other subjectivity over time. War sits very low on that map. It is the backstop invoked only when cultivation has been broken.

道德律
The Moral Law

道德律系列从一个不寻常的地方出发：不问善恶，问结构。当多个真正意义上的自我立法主体共处一个共同体时，他们之间会涌现出什么样的形态？这个问题的答案不是一套从外部施加的规则，而是一组构成性条件——你在这个模式下运作，就必然如此。

The Moral Law series begins somewhere unusual: not from good and evil, but from structure. When multiple genuine self-legislating subjects share a community, what form naturally emerges among them? The answer is not a set of rules imposed from outside, but a set of constitutive conditions — operate within this mode, and these conditions necessarily hold.
